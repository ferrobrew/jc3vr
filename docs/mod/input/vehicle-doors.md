# Vehicle doors by hand

An empty hand opens a vehicle's doors as a real hand does, though more forgivingly: a grip anywhere on a closed door takes hold of it (on its handle when the hand is near the handle), a pull outward undoes the latch, and the door then swings on its hinge with the hand. Let go and a side door swings on, damped, and a lid stays up where it is left; shove it shut and it latches. Hoods, trunks, and the other hatches a vehicle's door controller keeps work the same way. The code is `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/doors/`, with the grip and tear handling in `physical_hands/pickup/` and the seated path in `physical_hands/seated.rs`; the engine side is in [vehicle-doors.md](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md). It is always on under the controller scheme and builds on the pickup and the hand bodies ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)). Its tuning is `crates/jc3vr-physical/tuning/doors.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)).

The grapple snatch pulls a closed door open from range through the same reach and unlatch, with the grapple's aim point standing in for the palm ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md#car-doors)).

## Finding a door

Every door of every vehicle near the player is offered by its handle (`doors/scan.rs`). The vehicles scanned are those the game's target system keeps within `scan.near_m` of a free palm, and, for each hand, the vehicles its reach rays hit that tick, since the target system does not keep every vehicle and a parked civilian car can be missing from it.

A door's handle comes from the door handle table when its part is in it (`doors::geometry::door_handle`). The table is `tools/door_handles/door_handles.json`, embedded at build time (`doors/table.rs`) and keyed by the part's name hash, which the live part's entry in the parts resource carries ([`Part::m_name`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_name)); every variant of a model shares its parts, so one entry serves them all. Each entry is a point and an outer-face normal in the part's space. The tool places a front door's handle where the player's entry animation has its hand when it opens that door (grade `clip`, or `clip_doubtful` when the hand is far off the door), and every other door's from its box with the hinge at its constraint point (grade `hinge`); [`tools/door_handles/README.md`](../../../tools/door_handles/README.md) has how, and the engine side is in [vehicle-doors.md, "Entering through a door"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#entering-through-a-door). Every grade is used.

A door the table lacks (a DLC vehicle, or a part whose hash is not in it) takes its handle from its part's model box (`DoorBox::handle`): the door is a panel, its thinnest extent through it, the hinge along one edge, and the handle on the outer face at the opposite edge, `handle.inset` of the door's run in from it and halfway along it. The hinge edge is read from the part's origin when that lies within `handle.pivot_near_end` of one end; otherwise from the kind of door: a side door hinges at its front, a hood at its back, and a trunk, rear door, or hatch at its top. A door part's origin is the vehicle's rather than its hinge, so on the shipped vehicles the kind decides. The first time a model's doors are scanned, the log has `physical hands: doors: door handles:` with each door's source and grade.

A palm within `grip.handle_reach_m` of a handle is offered that door, marked green on the handle ([grip.md](grip.md#markers)). A shut door is merged into its vehicle's body, and a grip on it needs that body to prove the vehicle alive while the hand holds the latch, so a short cast from the palm to the handle, or failing that across the face at the handle, finds it; an open door's own body is taken directly. Each door near a palm is noted with its slot, whether it is shut, its handle's distance and source, and why it was not offered, and the notes are logged with the hand's next grip press ([grip.md](grip.md#readouts-and-logs)).

The pickup's reach rays ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)) also ask about every hit on a vehicle or a loosened vehicle part before anything else, so a player standing on a car can still open its door:

- A hit on a loosened part's object ([`VehiclePartGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject)) that is one of its vehicle's doors is an open door, taken where the ray hit it.
- A hit on the vehicle's own body evaluates each door's box in world space ([vehicle-doors.md, "The loose door"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#the-loose-door)). The pure `doors::acquisition::panel` returns the reached point, the outward direction, and the body: `PanelBody::LatchedHandle` for a closed door the palm reaches within `grip.box_reach_m`, taken at the reached point, or at the handle when the palm is within the handle reach; or `PanelBody::OpenPanel` for an open door's own body. A grip may land away from the handle because the table's handles are where the entry animation's hand goes, which on some vehicles is not where the drawn handle is (a fighter's canopy glass is well away from its table handle).

Acquisition needs an actual ray hit inside the door's model bounds; a nearby box alone is not contact. Window glass never acquires a car door, and queries continue past panes the glass feature has hidden without ignoring the rest of the vehicle, so an empty window opening supplies no door. The known aircraft canopies (the fighter, the fighter-bomber, the vintage fighter, and the floatplane), identified by their part entries in the handle table, probe the canopy surface independently of the estimated handle, so an intact canopy pane can supply a grip at the actual hit point. A grip on an inner door panel does not snap to the exterior handle.

A door wins over the climb's vehicle hold wherever it is reached, hoods and trunks included: a hand that closes on a door most likely means to open it, and a hand bracing on a door opens it only by pulling outward past the unlatch distance. A hit on a part object that is not a door, such as a piece broken off, is taken as any loose object. A hit on the vehicle's own body that reaches no door takes nothing: a vehicle is never carried (`bodies::classify` refuses it), so a grip that misses the door's reach does not lift the car.

## The latch

A grip on a closed door holds the hand on the grab point, carried with the vehicle (`doors::Latch::handle_palm`): the hand's body is held there as a climbing hand's is held on the wall ([hand-climbing.md](hand-climbing.md#gripping)), and the hand is drawn there. The latch holds until the hand has pulled `grip.unlatch_pull_m` outward from where it took the door, so the gap the hand reached across never counts as a pull; outward is from the vehicle's origin toward the door's centre. Pushing in or sliding along the door never opens it. A door that is already unlatched when the grip looks (by another hand, an animation, or the AI) gives at once.

When the latch gives, the hand plays `Event::Unlatch` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)), and the door is loosened the game's own way without its motor (`doors::engine::unlatch`): [`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) puts the vehicle's body in the world and the vehicle in the update, so that the door controller keeps running for this door, the opening request it leaves is withdrawn at once so that no motor turns the hinge, and [`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart) loosens the part onto its hinge, keeping its constraint. The door's entry is marked open, as the controller marks a door it spawns.

The hand then takes the door's own body with the grab point in the palm, so the door comes to the hand by the gap and the pull. An offset carried in the palm's frame would swing with every turn of the wrist, often off the door's arc where the door cannot follow, while a point in the palm can only be missed by as far as the hand leaves the arc. The vehicle body captured at the handle need not survive the unlatch as the vehicle's own body (the reach may have hit another body the vehicle owns, and the loosened part may change it), so the vehicle is proven either by that captured body or by the loosened part's own reference to its vehicle, and the grip keeps the vehicle's current body for later checks.

## Seated doors and palm pressure

The seated path acts only while the player occupies a seat, and only on the occupied vehicle's attached doors. It uses contact and reach rays rather than the ordinary hand bodies, and door grips use the same latch and carry. Generic pickups, character grabs, climbing, chassis shoves, and player-push locomotion stay off. Entering, leaving, changing vehicles, and teleporting clear the previous interaction and pressure history, and a torn-off part is released.

An ungripped palm pressing a closed door's inner solid panel outward releases its latch after the same outward movement as a grip (`grip.unlatch_pull_m`), measured in the vehicle frame from first contact, so existing penetration and the vehicle's own motion do not spend it. A missed palm probe keeps the progress for up to `pressure.contact_grace_s` only while a separate short ray still proves the same closed solid panel and the palm stays within `pressure.gap_drift_m` of its last contact; no movement during the gap counts. Glass, an obstruction, a changed target, withdrawal, or invalid tracking clears the gesture, and pressure probes stop at hidden glass rather than continuing to a farther panel, so the grace never bridges an empty window or canopy opening. The contact query allows a wider follow-through (`pressure.reach_m`) than a new grip. An inward push on a closed outer face or a slide along a panel does not unlatch it.

First valid inner-panel contact plays one light tick, and a successful unlatch plays the latch pulse; generic palm-touch feedback is suppressed for that hand while the gesture owns it. Opening pressure applies a bounded impulse to the loosened door's body, and continued pressure on an open panel swings it either way, including shutting a canopy, without a grip. Striking glass belongs to the [glass feature](breaking-glass.md).

## Swinging and shutting

From then on the hand holds the door's own body as any loose object: through a joint to the hand's body ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#holding-through-a-joint)), or carried otherwise. The hinge constraint removes whatever the hand asks that it forbids, so moving the hand swings the door about its hinge and nothing else.

The game leaves a loose door on a bare hinge with no friction, and its latch merges a door that swings shut within [`DoorInfo::LATCH_ANGLE_DEG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_ANGLE_DEG) of shut faster than [`DoorInfo::LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S), a speed any rebound off the open stop, the player's capsule, or a lid's own weight reaches ([vehicle-doors.md, "The latch"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#the-latch)). So every door the hands opened, by hand or by the snatch, is registered at the unlatch (`doors/hinge/mod.rs`) and has the controller's latch switched off: it is marked kept open ([`DoorInfo::FLAG_KEEP_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)) with its kept angle at the hinge's largest, which skips the latch and never asks the motor to reopen it. Each palm tick the mod then decides for it:

- A door a hand holds never latches.
- A free side door is damped (`hinge.damping_per_s`), so a door thrown open settles rather than bouncing back off its stop.
- A free lid (the hood, the trunk, a rear door, the hatches, and the known aircraft canopies) above `hinge.strut_fraction` of its travel is held where it is against its weight, as a gas strut holds it; below that it falls freely.
- A free door latches only when it swings shut within the latch angle faster than `hinge.latch_speed_deg_s`, or rests within that angle for `hinge.settle_s`. It latches through the game's own merge ([`PartInstance::MergePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-MergePart), then [`Vehicle::OnFinishedClosingDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OnFinishedClosingDoor)), logging `a free door latched` with the cause. Merging destroys the door's body, so a hand on it loses the hold.

Damping removes only motion relative to the chassis, and a supported lid keeps the chassis's carrier motion and compensates gravity in world space, so support never fights the vehicle's motion. A known aircraft canopy that settles far enough open holds its pose relative to the chassis (`hinge.canopy`), correcting small drift so it does not creep toward the face. Gripping it, a palm push that opens it, or a clear closing movement yields that support until the motion settles, after which it captures the new pose if the canopy is still open enough.

A door the game asks to open or close (an entry or exit animation, the AI, a script) leaves the registry with the controller's latch restored, as does one that latched or broke off by other means. The registry runs on the game thread before the hand-mode gate, including while seated, and when the hands stand down (the gamepad scheme, or out of play) every door gets the native latch back.

## Tearing an open door

An open grip must first settle: its lag (how far the grab point is from where the hand would have it), the lag's direction, and the relative separating motion must hold within `tearing.settle_speed_mps` for `tearing.settle_s`. Gestures are measured from that settled position, in the vehicle frame, so a heavy door hanging a few centimetres off the hand still settles, and the opening pull, which moves the door, cannot tear it.

After settling, three gestures tear (`doors/tearing/mod.rs`; the native hinge has no force-break threshold):

- A yank: the lag opens `tearing.yank_lag_m` above the settled lag while the hand pulls away at `yank_speed_mps` or more. A held door offers the hand no resistance to yank against, so this is the pull a player makes on a door that does not give.
- A hard pull: the lag holds `wrench_lag_m` above the settled lag for `wrench_s`, short of the distance at which the hand's joint lets go.
- A push at the opening limit: a sustained grip load (`push_load`) and `push_lag_m` of further outward displacement from a fresh settled position at the hinge's open limit for `push_hold_s`. The opening direction comes from the grip point's observed motion while the hinge angle changes.

A yank or a hard pull whose lag runs mostly (`free_swing_share`) along a swing the hinge leaves free does not count, since the door would swing with the hand there and the lag is a heavy door catching up; a door at rest has no observed swing, so any hard pull on it counts. Free swing clears tear intent. Arriving at or leaving the open stop, or turning the pull by more than `direction_cos`, requires fresh settling, and an opening-aligned effort at the stop uses only the push gate, so reaching the stop in one continuous opening movement does not tear the door. A wrist adjustment's same-direction lag is indistinguishable from a pull, so these gates reduce accidental tearing without proving intent.

The pickup retains the vehicle, part index, native object, and body identity through opening, and processes each body once even with two hands. Before requesting damage it checks current ownership, vehicle and part protection, authored detachability, body-preserving break behaviour, a valid non-root part, representable health, and that the vehicle is outside its parallel update. Only then does it call [`PartInstance::DamagePart_ToBreakOff`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-DamagePart_ToBreakOff) with player attribution and forced damage, which bypasses the authored healthy-part restriction but not actual invulnerability. Locked and occupied vehicles have no extra mod-side immunity.

Confirmed detachment turns every matching held door into an ordinary prop, keeping the same native body, grab frame, and hand joint. The missing-door state comes from the game's break notification. A closed or replaced native body ends the old hold rather than rebinding it.

## When the grip lets go

A grip on a latched door lets go with the grip, when the hand stands down, when the vehicle is gone (its body no longer belongs to it), when the door broke off, and when the latch did not give the door a body. A latch that gave a door whose body cannot be read within `grip.body_wait_s`, or whose identity no longer matches the vehicle's door, lets go too. A hold of an open door that ends other than by the grip opening logs `physical hands: pickup: a hold was lost` with the reason ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)).

## Threading

The doors are found, gripped, and unlatched from the palms' tick (the player aim control's post-simulation update), on the game thread outside the physics step, as the door controller's own post-simulation update does it. The hand's place on the latch reaches the hand bodies' pre-simulation step through their own leaf lock, as the holds do. Every read of a vehicle's doors first checks that the vehicle is alive, through the body the reach found it on.

## Readouts and logs

The debug UI's Characters and doors feature opens with the `Vehicle doors` status row (under `Hands in the world`), then says which door each hand reaches and whether a hand grips a latch, counts confirmed and refused tears, and lists each hand's pressure state (the door slot, the outward displacement against the required one, and the result).

At info level, under `physical hands: doors:`, the log has `a hand gripped a latched door`, `the latch gave, and the hand holds the door`, `a grip on a latched door was lost` with the reason, the latch-gave failures (`the latch gave, but the door's own body could not be read`, `the latch gave, but the opened door's identity changed`), `a hand asked to tear a door` with the gesture and its measures, and then `a tear was refused` with the reason or `the game was asked to tear a door off` with the result. Pressure logs `door pressure: opened`, and `door pressure: native failure` once per unchanged failure on the same target.
