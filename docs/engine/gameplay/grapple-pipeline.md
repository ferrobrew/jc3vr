# The grapple pipeline

The grapple's hook zips the player to a surface or object, tethers two things together, or reels in. The weapon side of the shared aim state is in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md). The chief defs are [`PlayerAimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl), [`GrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook), [`WireEnd`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd), and [`HookTargetInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo).

## Targeting

The grapple casts no ray of its own. [`PlayerAimControl::UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) casts one ray per sim tick for every aim consumer of the local player, built from [`GameCameraManager::GetCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetCameraMatrix) ([aim-pipeline.md, "The raycast"](aim-pipeline.md#the-raycast-updatedirectaim)) through [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay), and hands the hit to [`PlayerAimControl::CheckDirectTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-CheckDirectTargets). That fills, per target type ([`TargetType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType); melee is skipped), the parallel arrays [`m_DirectTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectTargets), [`m_DirectHits`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectHits), [`m_DirectPositions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectPositions), [`m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos), [`m_IsInRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_IsInRange), and [`m_MaxAimDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_MaxAimDistance). Guns use the [`TARGET_WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_WEAPONS) slot and the grapple [`TARGET_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_GRAPPLE), so a change to the camera matrix moves both aims.

For the grapple, `CheckDirectTargets` also keeps a last-valid cache ([`m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO), [`m_HasLastValidGrapple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_HasLastValidGrapple), [`m_DirectGrapplePosIsGrapplable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectGrapplePosIsGrapplable)), so a momentarily lost target persists. A surface hit fills the slot with the ray hit; for entity and dual-tether targets, `m_Target[TARGET_GRAPPLE]` and `m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO` come from the grapple fitness and target scoring. The reads in `UpdateGrappleReticle` and `NReelInAimIK::Update` confirm the layout of `m_AimPos`, [`m_LastAimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_LastAimPos), and [`m_IsGrappleUsingDirectPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_IsGrappleUsingDirectPos).

The slot's live readers:

- [`GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition) returns a pointer to `m_AimPos[TARGET_GRAPPLE]`, for the aim blend states and the fire-input predicate (`CGrapplingHook::GetFireInputEnabled`, mislabelled `CInventory::UpdateWeapons_Serial`).
- [`GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO) and [`GetCurrentGrappleTargetWeakGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetCurrentGrappleTargetWeakGO) return the cached object, gated on `m_HasLastValidGrapple && m_IsInRange[TARGET_GRAPPLE]`.
- [`IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid) re-validates the cached aim by calling `GetCameraMatrix` itself.
- [`NStateTask_InAirGrappleFireMotionTask::OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_InAirGrappleFireMotionTask##func-OnEnter) calls `GetCameraMatrix` for the camera's position alone: for a target nearly straight above or below, it pushes the character horizontally toward the camera for a moment, and otherwise damps its horizontal speed. Its direction comes from the latched target.
- The [grapple reticle](#the-grapple-reticle).

### Latching the target

On a fire, [`GrapplingHook::SetUpTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-SetUpTarget) (static, `(CPlayer* player, CWireEnd::SHookTargetInfo& out, CTarget::ETargetType type)`) reads [`Player::m_AimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimControl)'s `m_AimPos[type]` and `m_Target[type]`, transforms the aim position into the target's local space, and writes a `HookTargetInfo`, falling back to `GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO` without a direct `CTarget` (for `TARGET_GRAPPLE` only). Its setup overloads are [`SetupTargetFromTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo##func-SetupTargetFromTarget) (`CTarget*`, `CVector3f local`) and [`SetupTargetFromGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo##func-SetupTargetFromGameObject) (`CGameObject*`, `CVector3f local`), which writes only the object, the local position, and a character's bone, leaving the world position, the in-range flag, and a non-character's bone to its caller.

The fire and the fire arm IK read the latched target's current world position, [`GrapplingHook::GetLastGrappleTargetPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetLastGrappleTargetPos), so a later change to the slot does not move the hook's target, while the reticle shows the live slot. A fire during a reel, allowed with the re-reel upgrade, latches the same way ([`FireHookFromReelingIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-FireHookFromReelingIn): `SetUpTarget` on the grapple slot, then the fire act), on the press edge or a press still queued within its window, not a release ([`OnPreSimUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-OnPreSimUpdate)).

## Fire and attach

The hook flies a short, animation-gated flight to the latched target:

1. [`TryFireHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-TryFireHook) takes the fire direction from the `HookTargetInfo` and calls the animation fire.
2. [`DoActFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-DoActFire) (static, `(const CVector3f& direction, boost::shared_ptr<CCharacter>&, bool dual_tether, bool reel)`) picks a directional act (`ACT_GRAPPLE_FIRE_*`, or `ACT_GRAPPLE_TETHER_FIRE_*` for a dual tether) and queues it each tick until `CCharacter::IsGrappleFiring` goes true. It creates no wire, and its callbacks (`FireHookQueueActCallback`, `FireDualTetherQueueActCallback`, `FireHookGrappleReelQueueActCallback`) only notify.
3. On the animation's release track message, [`OnPostCamUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-OnPostCamUpdate) reads the hand and shoulder positions, creates the wire ([`CreateNewWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CreateNewWire)), pins the near end to the device, and sets the far end extending at a tuning speed from the left shoulder toward the target ([`WireEnd::SetExtend`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-SetExtend), `(const CVector3f& start, float speed, SHookTargetInfo&, bool ghost)`).
4. On arrival or a hit, [`WireEnd::Attach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-Attach) builds a `CGrapplePointProxy` and marks the end attached. An end attaches to a proxy, never a bare point ([`AttachToProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-AttachToProxy), [`AttachToCharacterBone`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-AttachToCharacterBone) `(shared_ptr<CCharacter>&, int bone, const CMatrix4f& localOffset)`), so each end of a tether is an entity, a bone, or a world proxy.

A [`GrapplingHookWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire)'s [`m_WireEnds`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_WireEnds) are the hook end, which every fire extends, and the device end, which [`AttachLocalWireEndToDevice`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-AttachLocalWireEndToDevice) attaches through `CGrapplingHookWire::AttachEndToCharacterBone`. Their states are [`WireEnd::m_WireEndState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_WireEndState) and the wire's [`m_WireState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_WireState).

### The wire's near end

The device end hangs, at a small fixed offset, from a `CCharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy` on the bone [`SafeBoneIndex::GRAPPLE_DEVICE_ATTACH_BONE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-GRAPPLE_DEVICE_ATTACH_BONE) names, `LeftForeArmRoll`, the forearm twist bone under `LeftForeArm`, not the hand. The proxy reads the live bone matrix, so the end follows the forearm's pose, IK included, when the wire updates: in the sim from `OnPostCamUpdate`, after pose finalization, keeping each end as a previous and current pair that the render update interpolates by the frame's factor before rebuilding the spline. The drawn near end therefore moves at the sim rate.

### The fire gate

The hook fires only while [`GetFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetFireInputEnabled) holds (tested by the fire readiness and `OnPreSimUpdate`). It starts from [`m_GrappleFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrappleFireInputEnabled), which `NStateTask_DisableGrappleUseTask` drives: a state carrying it clears the flag on entry (optionally retracting the active wire and detaching a reeled-in attachment first), sets it within the task's named segment, and sets it on leaving. The base graph carries the task in every vehicle seat, the grapple fire states, landings and stumbles, and many traversal transitions; the seats' copies name no segment and do not retract, so a wire already out survives boarding. The fire is also refused for a holder that is not the player, through a sniper scope, without the grappling hook ability, under the parachute without the slingshot ability, in cinematic mode, in the wingsuit with the aim point too far behind the facing, when the inventory's animation forbids it, and under a blocking state bit.

### The grapple in a vehicle seat

A seat takes no grapple fire, and the slot is not refreshed there: in a recorded session it kept its boarding target for the whole ride (which test skips it is unverified). The vehicle grapple, [`VehicleGrappleHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::VehicleGrappleHook), is embedded in the hook but unreachable: the hook calls its [`PreSimUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::VehicleGrappleHook##func-PreSimUpdate) only under a static switch this build compiles out. Riding a vehicle, it fires on a `FIRE_GRAPPLE` press through `FireHookCustom` at the aim's target position, with no game object or bone and ignoring the vehicle, and once the press is released and the far end holds a proxy, calls [`TetherActiveWireToVehicleCOM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-TetherActiveWireToVehicleCOM). That intact function moves the near end from the holder's bone to a vehicle point proxy at the vehicle's centre of mass, moves the wire into the tether list, and returns the hook to inactive: an ordinary tether, which the release and squeeze act on.

## Traversal, tether, and retract

The hook's [`GrapplingHookState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState) ([`GHS_INACTIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_INACTIVE), `GHS_REELING_IN`, `GHS_REELED_ATTACHED`, `GHS_REELED_HANG`, `GHS_REELED_UPSIDEDOWN`, `GHS_REELED_STUNT`, `GHS_CUSTOM_ACTIVE_WIRE`) is [`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_State), set by `SetState`. The character's hook is [`Inventory::m_GrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_GrapplingHook) in [`Character::m_Inventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_Inventory), returned by [`Character::GetGrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetGrapplingHook). `OnPreSimUpdate` and `OnPostCamUpdate` read the input from a `CActionMap` ([input.md](input.md)):

- [`FIRE_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-FIRE_GRAPPLE): a press with no wire latches the aim and queues the fire before a tap and a hold are told apart. Held past [`GrappleTuning::m_HoldTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_HoldTime), it sets [`m_CachedDualTetherFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_CachedDualTetherFire), and the release fires a dual tether's second end through `SetExtend` (`OnDualTetherAttach`; at most the upgrade-gated `GetMaxDualTethers`, breaking the oldest beyond, `BreakOldestDualTetherIfNeeded`). Any other release, a tap let go before the hook arrived included, reels in: `SetState(GHS_REELING_IN)`, `QueueAct(ACT_PRE_REEL)`, then the reel tasks (`NStateTask_ReelIn`, `StartReelAttach`, `StartReelHangAtPosition`, `StartReelUpsideDown`) end in a `GHS_REELED_*` state.
- [`RETRACT_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-RETRACT_GRAPPLE): [`RetractActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-RetractActiveWire) destroys the constraint and detaches the far end, which [cancels a reel](#the-reels-endings). Nothing pulls a light object to the player: the function named `CGrapplingHook::HandleGrapplePull` only calls `RetractActiveWire`, sets a state, and queues an act.
- [`RELEASE_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-RELEASE_GRAPPLE) releases the tethers (`ReleaseAllDualTethers`, `DetachAllDualTetheredWiresAttachedToGameObject`).
- [`PUSH_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-PUSH_GRAPPLE), [`REELED_IN_JUMP_ACTION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-REELED_IN_JUMP_ACTION), and [`REELED_IN_RELEASE_ACTION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-REELED_IN_RELEASE_ACTION) drive the reeled states.

[`FireHookCustom`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-FireHookCustom) fires a wire, a ghost one if asked, at a given target info with no fire act, only with no active wire and the hook inactive; it ignores a pending normal fire and works with tethers out, since they are not the active wire. Its `GHS_CUSTOM_ACTIVE_WIRE` ignores fire, reel, and tether input but not the release. [`CancelActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CancelActiveWire) ends the active wire and the state, which the next `OnPreSimUpdate` returns to inactive.

## Wire constraints

[`GrapplingHookWire::ShouldUseConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-ShouldUseConstraint) (mislabelled `CWireEnd::GetAttachmentObjectInfo`) rejects an invalid wire or end or a missing proxy, and needs both proxies' constraint hints to permit a constraint and one to request a physical one. [`GenericObjectPointProxy::GetConstraintHint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GenericObjectPointProxy##func-GetConstraintHint) gives none for a weapon in a reel-without-attachment mode and a virtual or physical hint for a rigid object by its motion type, so an attachment need not mean a native rope.

[`CreateConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-CreateConstraint) (mislabelled `NPfxVehicle::SStaticWheelFrictionData::addFrictionConstraint`; the function labelled as wire creation does the tether squeeze) takes both proxy bodies and pivots, requires distinct valid bodies, builds the spring and linear-motor data, and calls `hknpWorld::createConstraint`. A new attachment snapshots its length into [`m_MaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_MaxLength), a wire neither active nor an attachment gets a safety buffer, and the spring length subtracts the requested squeeze. [`GrapplingHookWire::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-Update) advances the ends, evaluates the gate, creates or updates the constraint, and updates tension and rendering; [`UpdateWireTension`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-UpdateWireTension) can break the line and tells both proxies the tension. The character-bone proxy's [`GetVisualPositionAndNormalWS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy##func-GetVisualPositionAndNormalWS) and [`GetVisualTransformWS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy##func-GetVisualTransformWS) compose the attachment's local transform with the bone and world transforms.

## Weapons and the grapple

The grapple never hooks a weapon, and only its rays keep it from finding one.

- The aim ray (on [`RAYCAST_EVERYTHING_PHYSICAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-RAYCAST_EVERYTHING_PHYSICAL)) and a non-ghost end's casts in flight ([`WireEnd::TryCollide`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-TryCollide), same layer) never hit a weapon's body, since [`WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-WEAPONS) does not collide with that layer ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md)). No query layer meets that layer's set plus `WEAPONS`: [`VEHICLE_RAYCAST`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-VEHICLE_RAYCAST) meets `WEAPONS`, the world, the character proxies, the projectiles, and the vehicle layers, but not the ragdoll proxies.
- Everything after the ray takes a weapon. `CheckDirectTargets` puts a held weapon's holder in its place and keeps a dropped one as the slot's object with no target; its [`GetGrapplingMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGrapplingModeWeapon), [`E_GM_ReelInNoAttach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::GrapplingMode##variant-E_GM_ReelInNoAttach), counts as grapplable, so [`UpdateLastValidGrappleTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateLastValidGrappleTarget) caches it and `SetUpTarget` latches it. [`WireEnd::SetupProxyForAttachment`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-SetupProxyForAttachment)'s weapon branch builds the same object point proxy as any loose object's, and nothing in the attach reads the no-attach mode. A non-ghost hook at a latched, visible weapon would attach to whatever lies under or behind it and reel there (inferred from the code path).
- `CheckDirectTargets` picks per slot, by [`m_RayTypeSetting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_RayTypeSetting), the collector's first holey hit ([`PlayerAimRaycastCollector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastCollector)), the query's closest hit ([`CastRayInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfo)), or the collector's minimum-distance hit; a holey slot with no holey hit falls back to the closest.
- A ghost wire ([`m_GhostWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_GhostWire)) casts nothing in flight and passes through walls. Within a step of its goal, which follows the target object, it casts twice with a filter that accepts only that object ([`WireEndCastFilter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEndCastFilter)); when both miss, as on a weapon, it attaches to the object itself ([`ForceAttachToTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-ForceAttachToTarget)) without the impact effect. Past `m_MaxLength` the end retracts.

Mislabels named in their defs: [`GetTuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetTuning) and [`GetActiveWireAttachmentObjectInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetActiveWireAttachmentObjectInfo) are swapped, [`GetFireDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetFireDirection) and [`GetDualTetherFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetDualTetherFireInputEnabled) bear unrelated names, and the sphere cast in [`WireEnd::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-Update) is named as a debug line.

## The hook's sounds and effects

The hook is an attached effect container ([`m_AttachedEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_AttachedEffects)) loaded with its properties from the device's (`editor/entities/jc_props/grappling_device/grappling_device_jc3_default.epe`), which holds six usages: the four below, `GrappleFire` ([`GRAPPLE_FIRE_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##const-GRAPPLE_FIRE_USAGE)), a sound with the muzzle effect `effects/jc_weapons/grapple/grapple_fire.effc`, and `GrapplePreFire` ([`GRAPPLE_PRE_FIRE_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##const-GRAPPLE_PRE_FIRE_USAGE)), a sound alone.

- The fire animation's track messages (`CGrapplingHook::OnTrackMessage`) play the one-off usage their hash names (`CGrapplingHook::PlayEffectOnMessage`, which refuses a looping one). No code names `GrappleFire` or `GrapplePreFire`, so only track messages play them, and a custom fire, having no fire act, plays neither. Which animation sends `GrapplePreFire`, and when, is unverified: the patch archive's grapple clips carry neither hash, and the base archives' clips are unnamed in the file lists.
- The reel's loop, the one looping usage ([`m_ReelingInEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ReelingInEffectUsage), `ReelingIn`), plays from [`UpdateLoopingEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-UpdateLoopingEffects) in the post-camera update while the hook is [`GHS_REELING_IN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_REELING_IN) and stops softly otherwise, acting only on a change of [`m_ActiveLoopingEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveLoopingEffects); it never plays for a custom wire.
- The one-offs resolved by name are the retract ([`m_RetractingActiveWireEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_RetractingActiveWireEffectUsage), `Retracting`), a fire with no target ([`m_GrappleFireFailEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrappleFireFailEffectUsage), `GrappleFail`), and the release from a reeled attachment (`ReleaseFromAttached`), each through [`AttachedEffectContainer::ActivateAttachedEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::attached_effects::AttachedEffectContainer##func-ActivateAttachedEffect); [`FindAttachedEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::attached_effects::AttachedEffectContainer##func-FindAttachedEffectUsage) finds any usage by its name's hash.
- The impact, [`WireEnd::PlayImpactEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-PlayImpactEffect), plays from the end's own cast hit with its material, so `ForceAttachToTarget` plays none.

The wire's own container loops follow its state, for a custom wire too: a flight loop while an end extends, and the tethers' stress, retraction, and active loops (`CGrapplingHookWire::UpdateLoopingEffects`, unverified).

## The reel

### The reel's velocity

[`NStateTask_ReelIn::UpdateReelVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-UpdateReelVelocity) (mislabelled `DoPreReelBlockCheck`) advances the character's velocity once per tick in the reel task ([`NStateTask_ReelIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn)) and per predicted step in `SimulateImmediateTrajectory`, from the [`GrappleTuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning) it is passed:

- It accelerates along the line by [`m_ReelInAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInAccel), or [`m_RetractReelInAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_RetractReelInAccel) while the control parameters hold the retract context flag and the character is beyond [`m_ReelInDragMaxDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInDragMaxDistance). The wind-up, collision avoidance, and vehicle entries add their own pushes.
- Beyond that distance [`m_ReelInPerpDrag`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInPerpDrag) drags the velocity across the line; within it the velocity is turned onto the line, and a step past the target is cut to land on it.
- [`m_ReelInClampAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInClampAccel) holds the speed along the line between [`m_ReelInSpeedMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInSpeedMin) and [`m_ReelInSpeedMax`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInSpeedMax), or [`m_RetractReelInSpeedMax`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_RetractReelInSpeedMax) under the boost: [`GetRetractAcceleratesReelIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetRetractAcceleratesReelIn) (the hook's flag and the retract boost upgrade) with the retract held ([`m_TimeHoldingRetract`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_TimeHoldingRetract)) past the threshold in that field's def. The boost has no input of its own. `NStateTask_ReelIn::GetMaxSpeed` makes the same choice but has no callers.
- The reel arrives within [`m_ReelInArrivedDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInArrivedDistance) of the target.

### The reel's motion

[`NStateTask_ReelIn::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-Update) advances the character's velocity and hands it over with [`UpdateMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-UpdateMotion), which turns the character toward the reel, sets the task-defined motion state, flags the proxy's input velocity as game controlled ([`CharacterInput::gameControledVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-gameControledVelocity)), and sets the wanted world velocity. That is the whole motion: neither the reel nor the prediction adds gravity. That the proxy's flying state moves at the wanted velocity unchanged is unverified.

The reel works on the character's velocity less its inherited velocity and adds that back afterwards. The inherited velocity blends by a smoothstep over the reel's time from the velocity carried in toward the far end's attach-point velocity ([`GrapplePointProxy::GetObjectAttachPointVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##vfunc-GetObjectAttachPointVelocity): a dynamic body's velocity at the point, zero for static geometry), so a reel onto a moving vehicle chases it.

### The far end's proxy

An end's proxy ([`WireEnd::m_PointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_PointProxy)) is a [`GrapplePointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy) whose virtual interface answers for a character, a vehicle, static geometry, or another object ([`m_AttachmentObjectInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##field-m_AttachmentObjectInfo)): the attach point's position, normal, and velocity, the object's mass and game object, and the body a constraint there would join ([`GetRigidBodyForConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##vfunc-GetRigidBodyForConstraint)), a dynamic object's own or none.

The active wire never holds a physics constraint: its device end's [`CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy) is built virtual only with a hint that permits none, so `ShouldUseConstraint` refuses it, and `AttachLocalWireEndToDevice` returns an end it finds physicalized to virtual. The reel moves the character by velocity alone.

The hang is the game's one rope swing with a body: the hook's [`m_GrapplingHangProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrapplingHangProxy), a free capsule placed at the hang and given the character's velocity on activation, which (unverified) requests an active constraint, so the attachment wire's native spring joins it to the far end's body. Only the reel's arrival into a hang enters it.

### The reel's endings

Nothing in the task times the reel out or watches its progress, so a velocity that does not close the distance keeps it running. It ends:

- Cancelled by `Update` (the grapple cancel act, the character righted, the active wire retracted) for a non-positive time step, a far end not attached or a hook out of `GHS_REELING_IN`, or no reel target or direction; a retract's detach is answered this way on the next update. One world update after the cancel, the character's whole velocity is scaled to about a tenth on all three axes in one tick under the cancel's state (measured on a recorded take; which state or task does it is unverified). A canopy opened out of the reel leaves by another path and keeps the velocity.
- Finished by [`FinishReel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-FinishReel) on arrival, tested by the far end's kind: a character, the distance left after the tick's travel along the line within the kick distance; a vehicle, [`CheckReelToVehicleRelease`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-CheckReelToVehicleRelease) (near or past the entry point, or a predicted pass through a sphere around it); anything else, facing along the line within `m_ReelInArrivedDistance`, or a distance less the tick's travel under a small buffer. The travel is the whole speed with a floor, not the speed along the line, and for the last kind the finishing tick does not hand over its velocity. A static or generic far end ends in the attachment its setup chose: a perch, a hang, on a ceiling the upside-down attach ([`StartReelUpsideDown`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-StartReelUpsideDown), which clears the velocities and queues the act that holds the character inverted against the surface), or else a floor landing or a cancel into the fall. Neither step changes the hook's state there, so an upside-down start that does nothing leaves the reel running to arrive again next tick.
- Resolved by [`CheckReelCollisions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CheckReelCollisions) in the post-simulation update: past the opening jump, with the proxy touching something and the hips closing on the target slower than a low speed for longer than a short time, the reel is dropped on walkable ground or for a custom attachment, otherwise reattached to a nearby foot support or floor, or dropped if none is found.
- Replaced by a [fire from the reel](#latching-the-target), or retracted by a cancel click (`OnPreSimUpdate`).

## The tether squeeze

[`UpdateDualTetherHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-UpdateDualTetherHandling) runs every tick, reading `RETRACT_GRAPPLE`'s analog value and `RELEASE_GRAPPLE`'s click, counting `m_TimeHoldingRetract` while the value is positive and [`m_TimeSinceRetractHeld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_TimeSinceRetractHeld) while it is zero. A click releases the tethers. Otherwise the squeeze is the cube of the value while the hook is not initialising, reeling, or holding a custom wire, the character can retract, and the player has the tether retract ability, scaled (unless a game setting says otherwise) by a ramp of the hold time from a fraction to all of it (times in the def). [`SqueezeDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-SqueezeDualTethers) rebuilds each wire's constraint shorter, smooths its squeeze factor toward the value, and sets [`m_IsSqueezingDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_IsSqueezingDualTethers) while it is positive. The squeeze is a rate: the tethers shorten while it is held and stay put at zero.

The tethers live in a list apart from [`m_ActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveWire). [`GetNumDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetNumDualTethers) counts those neither invalid nor broken with neither end retracting, [`HasValidTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-HasValidTethers) says whether one has both ends attached, and [`GetHighestWireTension`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetHighestWireTension) gives the counted wires' highest tension fraction.

### The game's own rumble for the line

`CPlayer::UpdateCameraShakeRumble` picks one camera shake and rumble state per update from the hook: the reel with or without the boost, the parachute and wingsuit with or without their retract boosts, free fall, and, while the tethers are squeezed and valid, one of three squeeze states by `GetHighestWireTension` (near zero, moderate, and above the threshold in its def). The hang swing's stick impulses are under [`NStateTask_GrapplingHangTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_GrapplingHangTask##func-Update).
