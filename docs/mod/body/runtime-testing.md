# Integrated runtime testing

Compiler checks and pure tests do not cover the native callback schedule, the model factories, the live physics response, or headset comfort. This is the order in which the paths that need a live game are exercised together in one session; each path's behaviour is in its own doc.

1. On foot with the grounded rig ([rig.md](rig.md)): confirm the Body fit readout advances. Walk, bend, crouch, turn, and reach: a bend stays a lean and a walk commits a step. Test slopes, stairs, edges, and moving platforms, then toggle body follow (`steps.enabled`) off and check that steps stop.
2. The body estimator ([body-estimator.md](body-estimator.md#in-the-game)), with weights from `scripts/egoposer_weights.sh`: the readout says `running` within seconds and the log names the weights. A glance swings the green floor arrow part way and the magenta one barely; a held quarter turn brings the legs round smoothly onto the green arrow. Recenter, snap, and smooth turn without either arrow jumping. Off foot only the green arrows draw; under the gamepad scheme neither does. Renaming the weights away stops the payload with a message naming the path.
3. The head on the neck pivot ([head-and-body.md](head-and-body.md)): with the `Body rig` visualisation on, look level, down, and well to each side. The pivot sprite sits on Rico's neck in the mirror and shadow, the head neither trails nor floats, and the chest stays out of the lower view while leaning.
4. Takeoff, landing, recovery, floor pickup, and hanging, twice each: head motion stays independent.
5. The parachute handles ([canopy-target-contract.md](../input/canopy-target-contract.md)): take, steer, release, and retake; the markers match the handles' rest.
6. Reload ([reload.md](../input/reload.md)): pull, abort, and reinsert a magazine and check the chamber and magazine counts; repeat the button, flick, and belt paths, including weapon changes mid-operation; rack slides, bolts, and pumps repeatedly.
7. Grenades ([grenades.md](../input/grenades.md)): draw into both hands at zero stock, transfer, stow, drop, recover, and throw; use a grenade shelf across its streaming range. No stock changes and no fuse on inert items.
8. Paired racks ([paired-rack-weapons.md](../input/paired-rack-weapons.md)): take each member in either order with the other hand busy and holsters full; ammunition is conserved across leaving, returning, and a reset.
9. Human hulls ([human-hull-restoration.md](human-hull-restoration.md)): inject with NPCs nearby and stream more in, then exercise standing, crouching, swimming, and the wingsuit. Request restoration in the open and beside an obstruction; a deferred request restores no body partially. Eject and reinject.
10. Record full-rate tracking with joints throughout ([recorder.md](../testing/recorder.md)); check the drop counters, the shutdown recovery, and that `tracking-synth dump` reads the file.
