# Grapple snatch targets

What the grapple snatch ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md)) takes, and how each kind of target is freed, hooked, and finished. The common path (the fire, the pull, and the catch) is in [grapple-snatch.md, "The pull and the catch"](grapple-snatch.md#the-pull-and-the-catch), and the swing of a heavy object or a body in [grapple-snatch-swing.md](grapple-snatch-swing.md).

| Kind | Freeing | Hook | Pull | Finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon on the ground | none | ghost fire at the weapon | the line through the hold solve | the catch, then `pickup::take` |
| Weapon in a character's hands | the steal's drop and stagger | ghost fire once it has bodies | as on the ground | as on the ground |
| Weapon holstered on a character | the slot's weapon dropped | as on the ground | as on the ground | as on the ground |
| Gun on a rack or in a locker | dropped where it hangs or lies | as on the ground | as on the ground | as on the ground |
| Gun on a rack with unlimited stock | the rack asked for a copy, dropped where the rack's weapon hangs | once the copy lies in the world | as on the ground | as on the ground |
| Pistol of a rack's pair | the aimed pistol claimed from the pair and dropped where it hung | once the pistol lies in the world | as on the ground | as on the ground |
| Loose object a hand can carry | turned loose at the bite if placed | ghost fire at the object | as on the ground | the catch, then the hand's ordinary hold |
| Grenade lying in the world | none | ghost fire at the grenade | as on the ground | the catch, then the grenade taken into the hand |
| Placed C4 charge | its body put into the simulation | ghost fire at the charge's body | as on the ground | the catch, then the hand's take of a placed charge |
| Heavy loose object, up to the ceiling | none | ghost fire at the object | none: the held line swings it | the grip's release lets it fly |
| Ragdolling character, up to the ceiling | none | ghost fire at the bone nearest the body hit | none: the held line swings it | the grip's release lets it fly |
| Car door | the doors' unlatch once the hook is on | fire at the vehicle at the handle | an outward impulse on the door's body | the wire cancelled; the door swings |

A disarmed character staggers and reacts as to the steal in reach: it draws another weapon, fights unarmed, or goes for one lying in the world. A gun torn or blown off its mount is dropped and snatched as any weapon: the hands' table holsters a heavy weapon the pickup leaves dropped, so it can take one from the world.

## Guns on characters

A gun in a character's hands goes through the steal's selection change, as the steal in reach does. A holstered gun (the pistol, two-handed, and special slots in the holstered state) is dropped as the player's own replaced weapon is ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop), then [`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon)) without a selection change, so the character keeps what it wields and the stagger is its whole reaction. In both cases the take's rules are checked before the gun leaves the character. A hand within reach takes a holstered gun directly through the same release ([physical-weapons.md, "Taking a gun from a character"](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-gun-from-a-character)).

## Racks and lockers

A rack's weapon has no body, the aim never hits it, and the game offers it only through the player's valid interaction entries, which hold only what is within reach. So the mod notes the weapons a placement rule holds out as the game renders them (`hold::rack::remote` in `jc3vr-weapons`, from the weapon render detour), each by a weak reference: a weapon in the rack state, and a weapon lying where its rule set it down (a locker's), known by its rule reference ([`WeaponBase::m_SpawnedFromRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SpawnedFromRule)) with no holder. The render pass only notes; everything that changes a weapon runs in the input tick. The snatch offers the noted weapons whose gun shows near its ray, each gun of a rack's pistol pair on its own, within the floor's widening cone and with the same line-of-sight cast; one already lying in the world with its body is the floor's candidate instead.

Firing frees the weapon through the hands' take from a source ([physical-weapons.md, "Taking a weapon from a source"](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-weapon-from-a-source)), after the same admission a hand's take makes, so the only difference from a hand's grab is the delivery:

- A rack that hands its own weapon over has [`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart) called for the player, which has no distance test: the weapon enters the picking-up state and sends its picked-up event, which tells its rack, so a finite rack is left as empty as a hand leaves it. The weapon then drops into the world where it hangs ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop), whose world addition places each body at its gun's model frame). A locker's weapon drops the same way.
- A rack with unlimited stock ([`WeaponBase::m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup) in the rack state) is asked for a copy as a hand's take asks (`hold::rack::copy`, [physical-weapons.md, "Taking a weapon off a rack"](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-weapon-off-a-rack)): the rack's placement rule hears of the one pickup, the rack's weapon is hidden, and the copy spawns a few frames later where the rack's weapon hangs. For the snatch it is then shown and dropped into the world there, and the snatch waits for it (the phase reads freeing, up to the hold tuning's `hold.rack.copy_wait_ms`) before the hook flies. The rack's weapon fades back in a moment later, restocked. A locker weapon with unlimited stock is not offered, since its copy would be dropped inside the bodies of the weapon still lying there; a hand in reach takes its copy.
- A rack's pistol pair gives up the pistol aimed at, or the other while the aimed one has already left ([paired-rack-weapons.md, "From range"](paired-rack-weapons.md#from-range)): the snatch observes the pair as a hand's reach does, so its staged pistol is asked for, and once it is staged the pistol is claimed from the pair, shown alone, and dropped into the world where it hung. The claim is settled at once as a take that left: a finite pair's first claim commits its ammunition split, so a missed catch leaves one loose single pistol holding its share, and the other pistol stays on the rack with the rest.

## Loose objects

A loose object is offered by the hand's own classification of what it reaches ([physical-hands.md, "Picking things up"](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up), `physical_hands::remote::loose_mass`), so whatever a hand would take as a loose object the snatch takes too: not a character, a vehicle or a part still on it, or a weapon, with a finite mass. That includes a door torn off a car and a placed object at rest whose body is not yet dynamic, such as a crate or a tyre set in the world. A loose object whose dynamic body lies near the aim's line is a candidate within the floor's widening cone, found by its box (`remote::dynamic_near`); one the player's other hand holds is not. A placed object at rest has no dynamic body to find this way, so it is offered under the aim's exact hit, stays where it was placed until the hook bites, and is then turned loose as the game turns one loose (`snatch::pull::loosen`, [havok-bodies.md, "Placed bodies becoming dynamic"](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#placed-bodies-becoming-dynamic)).

It is offered as an object when one hand could lift it, and as a heavy object up to the swing's ceiling otherwise. On the catch the wire ends and the object is handed to the hand's ordinary hold through the body's hand-over seam (`held::handover`, which the physical hands fill with `physical_hands::remote::hold_remote`), taken at its surface on the line from the palm to its centre: the hold's own solve takes its momentum within the hand's strength, as for an object taken in reach, and the grip's release lets go of it, thrown with the hand's motion.

## Grenades

A grenade lying inert in the world (dropped with its pin in, and still the loadout's) is offered as a grenade (`OfferKind::Grenade`), a candidate off the ray within the floor's widening cone (`grenade::inert_lying`), anchored by its own body rather than by the surface cast that checks a loose object is in sight, since a cast toward so small a body rarely meets it. On the catch the grenade is handed to the weapon handling, which takes it into the hand through the loadout at the next input tick, as a hand closing on it would ([grenades.md, "Dropped grenades"](grenades.md#dropped-grenades)). A grenade a character drops is normally made one of the mod's own as it drops, and is snatched the same way ([grenades.md, "Grenades lying in the world"](grenades.md#grenades-lying-in-the-world)).

A hand grenade the game left lying in the world as its own pickup (a native grenade weapon, which the game collects by proximity only while the grenade reserve has room) is offered as `OfferKind::WorldGrenade`, only for a finite one, not [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup). The pickup refuses it, since the game never picks a hand grenade up, so the line yanks the native grenade's own body in, and the catch takes that grenade into the hand at the next input tick (`grenade::take_world_pickup`), the same take a free hand closing on it makes. A missed catch leaves it where it falls. A grenade whose pin is out is live and not a snatch target, and a grenade on a display (a locker's or a rack's) stays where it is for a hand in reach ([grenades.md, "Locker sources"](grenades.md#locker-sources)).

## Placed charges

A C4 charge the player placed is offered as a charge (`OfferKind::PlacedCharge`), among the charges the C4 weapon last listed (`c4::placed::charges`), within the floor's widening cone and line-of-sight cast. The yank puts the charge's existing native body into the simulation through [`PlantedExplosive::DropExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-DropExplosive), and the hook takes hold of that body. The body the drop restores comes off its surface slowly, so the charge's stall window is the usual one times `charge_stall_scale`.

The charge stays on the weapon's active list during travel, so it remains counted and available to the detonator. A miss or cancellation leaves that original live charge in the world. Only a successful catch requests the same take as a grip on a placed charge (`c4::placed::take`): the weapon's next serial update primes a replacement in the catching hand before removing the original from its active list and removing its actor, and a failed replacement leaves the original intact. Flight and the queued take keep the charge's pool slot and allocation identity but no strong pooled-object reference, and the serial update validates the identity against the current live-list entry before removal, so a charge that disappears or whose allocation is recycled is refused without removing the replacement or priming a charge. A busy weapon or a planting refusal also leaves the original in the world and buzzes the hand.

## Car doors

A closed door of the vehicle under the aim whose handle area is within `door_reach_m` of the aim's point is the target, found by the doors' own reach ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md)) with the aim's point standing in for the palm; a door is a large surface the aim lands on directly, so no cone is needed. A vehicle moving faster than `door_max_vehicle_speed_mps`, read from the body the aim hit, is refused. The yank fires the hook at the vehicle, latched at the handle in the vehicle's frame. Once it holds, the door's latch is undone as a hand's pull undoes it, the door's own body is pushed outward at the handle by its mass times `door_swing_mps`, and the wire ends. The door swings; nothing comes into the hand.
