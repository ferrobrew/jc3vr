# Weapon equip, pickup, and drop

How a weapon object moves between the world, the inventory, the hands, and the body. Picking a weapon up through the use input is in [item-pickup.md](item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon), the magazine and the reserve in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md), the attach bones in [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md), the weapon's user in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-weapons-user), and what keeps a weapon alive, the spawn reset, the gives, and the save in [weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md).

## One object for its whole life

A weapon is one [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase) whether it lies in the world, is held, or is holstered. The character's inventory holds one weapon per slot ([`Inventory::m_Weapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Weapons)), and the weapon's state ([`WeaponBase::m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)) says where it is. The magazine ([`m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo)) travels with the object; the reserve belongs to the character.

The state setter ([`WeaponBase::SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState)) keeps the weapon's bodies in the physics world ([`WeaponBase::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-AddToWorld)) only in the dropped, mounted, and turret states, and wakes them for dropped. Every other state, holstered and wielded included, takes them out ([`RemoveFromWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-RemoveFromWorld)), as does a turret becoming dropped.

Each gun has its own body ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)). Out of the world it keeps its id, its shape ([`hknpBody::m_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_shape)), and its motion, mass included. Putting it back ([`WeaponData::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-AddToWorld)) keeps its collision system group or takes a fresh one, sets its layer for the weapon's state, makes it dynamic, and places it at the gun's world matrix ([`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)), so the body's frame is the gun's model frame.

## The dual-wield weapon

A weapon of the dual-wield slot ([`E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD)) is one object with two guns, one per pistol ([`WeaponBase::m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData)). The attach-bone table puts the first gun in the right hand when wielding or dual-wielding and on the right hip when holstered, and the second in the left hand only when dual-wielding and on the left hip otherwise ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)). Everything else is the object's and so shared: the state, the magazine, the fire interval and trigger state, the reload, the holder, and the drop. How the two guns take turns firing is in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md#which-gun-fires).

The state decides how many pistols the weapon shows and fires. The equip dual-wields the slot for the player and for a character whose blackboard holds [`WeaponBase::DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##const-DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE), and wields it for any other character. In the wielding state a dual-wield weapon is one pistol:

- the visibility's base implementation ([`WeaponBase::SetVisibleBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetVisibleBase)) hides its guns past the first whenever it shows the weapon. A gun is shown and hidden through its model instance's status bits ([`ModelInstance::m_Flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##field-m_Flags)), and a model part such as a slide shows only while its own gun's model does ([`WeaponComponentBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-SetVisible));
- the tail of the shot keeps the next gun ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)) on the first, and entering the wielding state resets it there.

The same base implementation hides the extra guns of a non-player character's dual-wield weapon in every state when the character lacks the blackboard value. A player's dual-wield weapon, holstered or dual-wielded, shows both guns and fires them in turn. Nothing applies the rule between calls, so a weapon whose state changes keeps its guns' visibility until the next call; the animation's state tasks set the wielding state without one ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)).

In the world a dual-wield weapon is one pistol: the drop virtual hides every gun past the first, as for any weapon of several guns, and `AddToWorld` puts in only the first gun's body and takes the second's out, so a dropped pair falls, lies, and is picked up as one pistol. Picked up by the player, it is dual-wielded again with both guns shown. Whether the player's animation accepts the dual-wield weapon in the wielding state outside the states whose tasks set it is unverified.

## Completing a pickup

[`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem) takes the character's game-object subobject ([`Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##const-GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET)) and ignores any other activator. It:

1. unequips the wielded weapon, which goes to its holster;
2. puts this weapon into the inventory with the equipping mode ([below](#the-inventorys-add));
3. selects the weapon's slot, adds its stored ammunition ([`m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo)) to the reserve, and plays the pickup vocal;
4. clears the stored ammunition.

It does not refill the magazine and plays no animation act. On land, [`PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart) first puts the weapon in the picking-up state with its bodies out of the world, and the pickup track message calls `PickupItem` later. A swimming character's pickup calls it directly on a weapon still in the dropped state, with no `PickupStart`, after dropping the weapon already in that slot, and then queues `ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON_PARTIAL` (see [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)).

Two kinds of weapon are not put into the inventory by it, though both still lose their stored ammunition to it. A hand grenade is left to `PickupStart`, which adds its stored rounds to the reserve and despawns it whatever its infinite-pickup setting: it hides, leaves the physics world, is handed to the spawn system's despawn, and sends its picked-up event, so a grenade on a locker or rack never hands out a copy. A weapon with [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup) set gives the character a copy from `PickupStart`, spawned from the weapon's spawn tag ([Racks](#racks)).

## The inventory's add

Every way a weapon enters a slot ends in [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon). It reads the incoming weapon's slot ([`m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)); another weapon in that slot is dropped when the inventory's per-slot drop flag is set and otherwise removed, which despawns it (a mounted gun is only released), so a slot emptied beforehand with `RemoveWeapon` ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#into-and-out-of-a-slot-without-a-drop)) loses nothing. It then stores the weapon and runs its pickup virtual, and with an equipping mode ([`EquipMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::EquipMode)) unequips the wielded weapon and equips the new one. The hand-grenade, planted-explosive, and beacon slots are started using at once ([`StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing)), since those weapons are in use while holstered.

The pickup virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup)) on its own takes a weapon into a character's keeping without any slot: it sets the holder, puts the weapon in the holstered state (its bodies out of the world), disables its pickup interaction and hint, and for the locally controlled character sets the avatar ready for combat and shows the weapon in its slot's arm of the weapon selection ([`HUDUI::OnPickupWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-OnPickupWeapon)). [`HUDUI::InitWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-InitWeapons) rebuilds the selection from the slots.

The add has three callers:

- [`Character::PutWeaponIntoInventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PutWeaponIntoInventory), a direct call, used by the completed pickup, a mounted gun's `StartUsing`, the weapon's track-message handling, and the supply drop's broken cargo. Pickups from the ground, racks, and characters, and the supply drops, all arrive this way.
- [`Character::PickUpWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PickUpWeapon), which fills the magazine to the tuned size ([`WeaponBase::GetMagazineSize`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetMagazineSize)), adds the weapon, and with [`E_EQUIP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::EquipMode##variant-E_EQUIP) selects the slot ([`Character::SelectWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SelectWeapon)) for the dual-wield, two-handed, special, heavy, and FOW slots. The respawn hand-over and the spawn-and-equip callback call it ([the give](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-give)).
- The character's looting of its children (`CCharacter::LootChildren`).

## Equip and unequip

The equip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip)) shows the weapon, runs its components' equip, selects its slot, starts using it, puts the player's avatar into combat, and sets the state to wielding, or to dual-wielding for the player's dual-wield slot. It queues no act: the draw animation comes from the character's draw and the slot select, so a weapon equipped through the virtual alone is in the hands at once without a draw having played.

The unequip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip)) cancels the holder's reload, sets the state to holstered, and stops using the weapon, playing no holster act. A mounted gun keeps its state, and a heavy weapon is instead dropped through the inventory's drop of the heavy slot, whatever that slot holds, so a heavy weapon outside the slots is neither dropped nor holstered.

Both virtuals have base implementations ([`OnEquipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnEquipBase) and [`OnUnequipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnUnequipBase)); the mortar, the rocket launcher, the planted explosive, and the fire-leech launcher override the equip, and the sun gun overrides the unequip. Neither checks the inventory's draw and holster permissions ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing) and [`GetAnimationAllowsHolstering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsHolstering)).

## Who equips and unequips

Every equip the game makes runs the equip virtual, and every unequip the unequip virtual:

- Equip: the inventory's add with an equipping mode; the draw clip's equip track message ([`WeaponBase::HandleTrackMessage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HandleTrackMessage)); the `NWeaponEquipTask` state task ([`NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter)), which with nothing wielded either switches to a weapon with ammunition or, instant, equips the first valid of the two-handed, dual-wield, and special slots directly, and which no state of Rico's graphs carries; the exit of the weapon-state task ([`NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit)), which restores a wielding or dual-wielding state through the equip; and the deferred equip.
- Unequip: the `NWeaponUnequipTask` state task ([below](#vehicles-and-traversal)); the character's neutral state ([`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState)); the holster clip's unequip track message; the inventory's add when it equips another weapon; the pickup; the respawn hand-over ([`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned)); the despawn ([`WeaponBase::Despawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Despawn)); the drop virtual; and the deferred unequip.

`ForceNeutralState` drops the heavy weapon, stops using the planted explosive, unequips the wielded weapon, and cancels a primed grenade. It runs on every revive (so every respawn), at the start of every teleport ([`UpdateTeleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world##func-UpdateTeleport) and [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstant`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayerInstant)), on the vehicle exit's upward teleport, from the god mode and the walk-on-water mode, when a scripted sequence takes the character ([`Character::SetupForSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetupForSequence)), when the character is enabled, from the network, from the AI's mission-critical teleport, and from the player's ragdoll constraint fix-up.

Every automatic unequip and the inventory's weapon tick start from the wielded weapon, [`Character::GetWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWieldingWeapon) ([weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-selected-slot)), so a weapon wielded in a slot that is not selected is invisible to them.

## Deferred operations

A weapon can queue an equip, an unequip, a start-using, a stop-using, or a drop for its next pre-simulation update. Each request sets a flag ([`m_DeferredEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquip), [`m_DeferredUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUnequip), [`m_DeferredStartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStartUsing), [`m_DeferredStopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStopUsing), or [`m_DeferredDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredDrop), with the character in [`m_DeferredEquiper`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquiper) or [`m_DeferredUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUser)) and registers the weapon for the update ([`m_RequiresPreSimUpdates`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RequiresPreSimUpdates)). The requesters are few:

- [`EquipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-EquipDeferred): only the AI's equip action, so the game never defers an equip of the player's weapon;
- [`UnequipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UnequipDeferred): the AI's unequip and switch actions, the non-player branch of `NWeaponUnequipTask`, and the graph script's `SpawnWeapons` node, which is the only one to defer the player's unequip;
- [`StartUsingDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsingDeferred): only the mounted-weapon state task's entry (its stop-using counterpart is unverified);
- [`DropDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DropDeferred): only the inventory's drop.

The pre-simulation update ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePreSim)) runs, for any weapon with or without a slot, the per-gun update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)), the components', the deferred recoil impulse, and the deferred weapon-fire event ([weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-gunshot-the-ai-hears)), then the queued operations in this order: the drop runs the drop virtual; the unequip runs the unequip virtual and skips the guns' render interpolation; the equip runs the equip virtual with the deferred equipper and ends the update; a start-using and a stop-using queued together cancel each other; a start-using alone, for a weapon without a user, unequips the deferred user's wielded weapon and then starts using; and a stop-using alone runs the [`StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing) virtual on a weapon that has a user. While the weapon has a user, the update keeps the guns' animation at full detail while the user's [`m_LodFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_LodFlags) are clear, and drops it beyond a middle distance from the camera otherwise.

## Dropping

The inventory's drop of a slot ([`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon)) clears the slot, stops using the weapon when it is wielding or dual-wielding, and defers the drop; with its second argument set it then selects another weapon when the dropped slot was selected. The drop virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop)), which the deferred drop runs, unequips a weapon still wielded by a user (leaving the player's precision aim), sets the dropped state, adds the bodies to the world and wakes them, gives them a velocity, clears the holder, enables the pickup interaction again, and hides every gun past the first. The velocity depends on the state the weapon is in once unequipped:

- holstered, with a character holder: the holder's velocity plus its forward direction scaled by the slot (forward for the two-handed slot, backward for the special slot, nothing for the others);
- still wielding or dual-wielding (a heavy weapon, which stopping does not holster): the first gun's measured velocity and angular velocity ([`WeaponData::m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) and `m_MeasuredAngularVelocity`), set on its body.

Since the inventory's drop stops using the weapon first, a held weapon other than a heavy one is dropped as if from its holster, with the holder's motion and not the hand's.

A swimming character's pickup calls the drop virtual on the slot's occupant directly, which drops it at once with the slot still holding it. The deferral reads the weapon's state when the inventory's drop runs, not when the drop is carried out: a weapon already in the dropped state is left unmarked. So a weapon dropped through its virtual and then let go of by the inventory is dropped once, even if something picks it up in between.

## Racks

A weapon on a rack is placed by a weapon placement rule ([`WeaponPlacementRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule)). Once the rule's weapon has spawned, the rule hands it the rule's infinite-pickup setting ([`WeaponBase::m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup)) and a weak reference to the rule ([`WeaponBase::m_SpawnedFromRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SpawnedFromRule)), which stays on the weapon after it leaves the rule's spot, and, for a rack, puts it in the rack state ([`E_WS_WEAPON_RACK`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WEAPON_RACK)), which keeps no body in the physics world and takes the gun's world matrix from the rule. A rule that places its weapon invisible hides it through the visibility virtual ([`WeaponBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetVisible)). A rack that does not use the ordinary pickup disables the weapon's own pickup interaction and offers it through an interaction-graph node that stands for the rule.

The player's interaction user proxy ([`Character::m_InteractionUserProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_InteractionUserProxy)) lists the entries of nearby interaction graphs the player could take ([`InteractionUserProxy::m_ValidEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##field-m_ValidEntries)). The weapon an entry offers is its graph's context's parent object when that is a weapon ([`InteractionContext::m_Parent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionContext##field-m_Parent)), and otherwise, when the entry's node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex)) stands for a weapon placement rule, the weapon the rule spawned ([`WeaponPlacementRule::GetSpawnedObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule##vfunc-GetSpawnedObject)). A swimming character's pickup walks the entries this way, and so does the player's weapon readout (unverified).

An entry becomes valid only while its graph's conditions pass: the proxy works through the entries it is validating ([`InteractionUserProxy::m_PotentialEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##field-m_PotentialEntries)) in [`ProcessPotentialEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##func-ProcessPotentialEntries), flagged meanwhile by `m_IsValidatingEntry`. The pickup condition [`InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory##func-Evaluate) finds the weapon the first entry offers in the same two ways and fails when the character's inventory holds a weapon in that weapon's slot ([`WeaponBase::m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)) of the same kind ([`WeaponBase::m_WeaponId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponId)). A character carrying a weapon is therefore not offered another of its kind, on a rack or in the world, while a weapon of another kind in the same slot is offered and replaces the occupant. Which graphs use the condition is read from the behaviour (a rack's remaining weapon left the valid entries while one of its kind was carried, and came back when it was dropped), not from the graph data.

On land, the interaction's execution starts the pickup (`PickupStart`) and the pickup animation's track message completes it on the same weapon, found from the interaction in the same two ways (unverified). Starting the pickup of a rack weapon puts it in the picking-up state and sends the rule's picked-up event. A rack with unlimited stock hands out copies: its weapon has infinite pickup, and starting the pickup asks the inventory for a new weapon of the same spawn tag, with a magazine of the tuned size, equipped on arrival and dropping the weapon it replaces ([`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon), [the give](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-give)); the copy spawns a few frames later, and the rack's own weapon stays where it is.

## The drop's callers

[`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon) is called by the character's drop of every slot ([`Character::DropAllWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-DropAllWeapons), from the script's drop-all event and the player's reset to the default loadout), the character's destruction and death ([`Character::OnDestroyed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-OnDestroyed): its best weapons through [`Inventory::DropBestWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropBestWeapon), and its hand grenade when a random draw falls below [`m_DropGrenadeChance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_DropGrenadeChance), for a character other than the local player; the avatar's death handling drops nothing), the slot select, the neutral state, the `NDropWeaponStateTask` state task, the inventory's add replacing an occupant with the drop-old flag, the inventory's weapon handling (a heavy weapon on the holster or cancel input), the unequip of a heavy weapon and the sun gun's unequip, the single-use rocket launchers after their shot, and the network. The player's update also drops an empty Bavarium power-core weapon (unverified).

## Vehicles and traversal

Which weapons the inventory's tick updates in a vehicle, on a motorcycle, and in the boost wingsuit is in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-shooting-permission). The graph's base layer puts weapons away through state tasks (the states named are Rico's base graph's):

- `NWeaponUnequipTask` ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter)) acts on the wielded weapon alone. Its instant variant unequips the player's weapon at once (another character's through the deferred unequip) on entry to every vehicle entry, seat, and seat change, the motorcycle, pogo stick, and cow rides, the mounted-gun pickups and seats, the wingsuit openings, boosts, rolls, evades, and flinches, free fall and fast fall, the underwater dives, the hit-reaction flights, the ragdoll, the get-up, dying, and the guitar-case exit. Its non-instant variant queues the holster act on the mounted-gun and cow-ride entries, the crawl, and the surface-swim starts. Either way the player returns to normal aim and the avatar's mental-state timer is reset.
- `NDropWeaponStateTask` ([`NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) is configured for the heavy slot and drops its weapon whatever its state, holstered included, on every grapple and tether fire, grenade throw, fall, free fall and fast fall, parachute and wingsuit opening, reel-in to a fall, vehicle entry, surface swim, crawl, and hit-reaction flight and stumble.
- `NStopUsingWeaponStateTask` ([`NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) stops using the selected slot's weapon in the ragdoll, crash, dying, drowning, and get-up states.
- `NWeaponHideInVehicleTask` ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter)), two per seat state, turns a configured slot's holstered weapon holstered-invisible when the vehicle sets [`Vehicle::m_HideCharacterWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_HideCharacterWeapons), and its exit ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit)) shows it again. It touches only holstered slot weapons.

The vehicle exit's upward teleport also forces the neutral state. The parachute has no holster in code (only the auto-aim tuning and the statistics read it), and the grapple hang only affects the inventory permissions and the weapon's precision camera hint (unverified); any holster there comes from the graph's tasks and acts. The wingsuit's guns are placed from the holder's bones and the flight direction ([`WeaponData::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)). Nothing in these paths moves a weapon held outside the slots.

## A character that loses its gun

The game takes a gun from a living character only by dropping it: the character's death drops its best weapon through the inventory, and an animation state task can drop a configured slot. There is no disarm as such, and a hit reaction below the knockdown threshold staggers without a ragdoll ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#the-hit-reaction)). Once a gun leaves its hands, a character gets the ordinary handling of a character with nothing wielded:

- the inventory's drop with its selection on selects another weapon the character carries when the dropped slot was the selected one;
- the weapon-equip state task, when its character wields nothing, switches to the first weapon that still has ammunition ([`Inventory::TryToSwitchToValidWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-TryToSwitchToValidWeapon));
- the AI's perception keeps a sense of weapons lying in the world whose pickup interaction is live, sorted by distance, and the behaviour trees can reserve one for a character to fetch (unverified, unbound).
