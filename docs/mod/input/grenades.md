# Grenades, C4, and the detonator

Under the controller scheme the explosives are objects in the hands rather than throw animations. A grenade from the chest pouch is the game's own grenade from the draw to the detonation, inert until its pin is pulled; a C4 charge from its pouch is the game's own charge, thrown or pressed onto a surface by hand; and the detonator is held like a gun. Under the gamepad scheme none of this runs and the game's own throw and planting work as always.

The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/{grenade,c4}/`, with the pouch returns in `pouch_return.rs`, the fingers' opening in `opening.rs`, and the pouches' models in `pouch_models/`. The tuning is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/grenade.toml` and `c4.toml`, with the pouches in `hold.toml` and the opening thresholds in `handling.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). The engine side is [aim-pipeline.md, "Grenades"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#grenades) and [planted-explosives.md](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md).

## The pouches

The grenade pouch and the C4 pouch sit on the front of the chest, the C4 pouch on the side the grenade pouch leaves (`volumes::grenade_pouch`, `volumes::c4_pouch`; placement in [weapon-handling.md](weapon-handling.md#pouches)). Only a free hand takes from the chest. The grenade pouch is offered while the stock has a grenade; the C4 pouch while a charge may be placed or no hand holds the detonator.

A hand lets go of an explosive when its fingers open, not when its squeeze relaxes (`weapons::opening`): a throwing hand relaxes well before the fingers leave the handle, and a force-sensing grip's press ends before the throw's peak, which would drop the grenade at the feet. The fingers are read through the grip's value, with hysteresis. While a grenade is in a hand, the game's `THROW_GRENADE` is held back.

### The pouches' models

The pouches have no model of their own in the game, so the mod draws the game's own models on them (`pouch_models/`): a grenade (the grenade weapon's [`m_PhysicalProjectileModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PhysicalProjectileModel)), the C4 charge (the planted explosive weapon's [`m_InactiveModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_InactiveModelInstance), its in-hand model despite the name), the detonator once no charge may be placed, and the held gun's spare magazine ([`WeaponData::m_MagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineObject)) on the magazine pouch. Each is drawn only while its pouch has something to give, so what the player sees is what a hand reaching there takes.

Each is an instance the mod owns, from [`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromModel) and holding its own model reference, so a magazine already made stays drawn while the game respawns a gun's spare prop. Instances are made, hidden, and released only in the input tick's game stage, and released when the source changes, the weapon handling stands down, outside gameplay, and at unload.

Each model's placement (the hold tuning's `pouch_models`, given for the right side and mirrored) is stored relative to the bone its pouch rides, and once per rendered frame, before the engine's render update culls, its matrix is pushed on that bone as the body is drawn, carried by the character's world transform interpolated at the frame's fraction ([render-time-anchoring.md](../rendering/render-time-anchoring.md)). The magazine's placement offset also moves the pouch's grab centre (`pouch_models::display_centre`).

### The magazine in the hand

While a hand holds a round from the magazine pouch, a second instance of the gun's spare magazine model is drawn there at the reload's held pose (`pouch_models::hand_tick`, `reload::held_round_matrix`), separate from the gun, so the old magazine stays in the gun until it leaves ([reload.md](reload.md)). The shotguns and revolvers have no spare magazine, so their rounds are drawn with the casing their ejection effect throws, from the game's model cache by path hash ([`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromHash), which loads nothing), and a revolver's belt pouch draws a ring of casings as a speedloader (`pouch_models/speedloader.rs`).

## Safe items

Taking a grenade from the pouch creates a loadout item with a native [`Grenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade) of its own (`grenade::safe`, `grenade::native`), made by the grenade weapon's own factory, [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::PrimeGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-PrimeGrenade). Its claim on the weapon, [`m_PrimedGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PrimedGrenade), is released at once, so any number of items can exist. The factory's own round deduction ([`ConsumeAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ConsumeAmmo)) is suppressed for that call.

A drawn grenade is inert. [`Explosive::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdatePostSim) ticks the fuse only while [`Grenade::m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer) holds, the switch the beacon weapon clears. The draw clears it, and clears [`m_CanUseUpgrades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_CanUseUpgrades) too, since the contact upgrades can zero the timer regardless; the upgrade flag is put back when a live grenade leaves through the launch. Only the pin raises the switch, so a grenade held, stowed, or dropped with its pin in never starts its fuse. While inert, the AI's grenade awareness ignores it (`grenade::native::ai`).

The controller holds only weak references, so an actor the pool reclaims retires its item. Allocation must happen in the input tick's game stage: registered then, the grenade first updates after the ammunition manager has made its physics body; registered earlier, its post-simulation update runs without a body and faults ([`Grenade::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-UpdatePostSim)). During a draw the allocator's reclaim argument is forced false, so a full pool refuses the draw rather than deleting another grenade. A safe grenade moves between the hands and the hip holsters keeping its actor, each move checked by the loadout table ([two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md#props-and-explosives-in-the-table)).

## Stock

The stock (`grenade::stock`) is the game's own count: the grenade weapon's [`m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo) plus the inventory's reserve ([`Inventory::m_Ammunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Ammunition)), capped by [`m_MaxAmmunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_MaxAmmunition). A draw makes the item first and only then takes one grenade through the game's setters, so a refused allocation costs nothing. Under unlimited ammunition (the rule [reload.md](reload.md) reads) the draw is free.

Restocking is physical only. While the controller scheme runs, the game's grenade pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart)) credits nothing to the local player, and a grenade a hand takes from the world has its rounds emptied first.

## Putting it back

The chest pouches take back what they give (`weapons::pouch_return`). A press counts when the hand holding the item enters the pouch's return volume from outside, after leaving it since the draw or the last press, so a draw never puts its item straight back. A grenade with its pin in retires into the stock (`Event::PouchReturn`); a full stock refuses with `Event::PouchFull` and a dry-fire click, and a live grenade never goes back. A C4 charge is despawned ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::DespawnPrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-DespawnPrimedExplosive)); a held charge is primed but never placed, so it never counted against the limit.

## In the hand

A held explosive is placed by a hold (`weapons::in_hand::HoldPlacement`): its model's origin and axes in the hand's grip frame, given for the right hand and mirrored, each derived from the model's geometry, since the grip frame's origin is the centre of the closed hand. Once per rendered frame its transforms are set to the hold on the drawn hand's grip (`grenade::safe::render_frame`, or a detour on the explosive's render update for a caught grenade and a charge), or it would trail the hand by a tick or two. The other hand takes a held explosive by closing on it ([grip.md](grip.md)).

## The pin

Three things pull the pin (`grenade::pin`, logged with `by`):

- a trigger press on the holding hand;
- the other hand's squeeze on the pin, offered to a free hand as a grip target at the top of the fuse head (`GrabVolume::GrenadePin`, a red ring labelled "pull pin") ranked over the grenade, while a squeeze elsewhere on the grenade takes it into that hand;
- the teeth: with the other hand occupied, holding the pin at the mouth for a short dwell.

Pulling the pin raises `m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer` and pulses the hand (`Event::GrenadePin`); nothing puts it back, and a grenade held too long explodes in the hand. The pin comes out only once the grenade has its physics body, at the end of the tick it is drawn in. A live grenade can pass to the other hand but cannot be holstered or returned.

The game shows a burning fuse only through a thrown grenade's trail, which its own update drives from its timers ([aim-pipeline.md, "The burning fuse"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#the-burning-fuse)). The game's prime gives a grenade no trail resources, so the pin first sets them up from the weapon's player trail ([`Explosive::SetupEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetupEffects) with [`m_TrailEffectPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_TrailEffectPlayer)) and then starts it ([`Explosive::PlayTrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PlayTrailEffect)). From the pin to the detonation, for a pouch grenade or a caught one, a ring at the hand and a haptic click (`Event::GrenadeWarning`) quicken and redden with the share of the fuse burnt (`grenade::fuse_urgency`).

## Releases

Opening the fingers releases the grenade with the motion every let-go takes (`grenade::throw_release`; [physics-substrate.md, "Release velocities"](../body/physics-substrate.md#release-velocities)), linear and angular, from the item's own centre. The pin, not the hand's speed, decides what the release is.

A held grenade's body is not in the simulation; only the engine's launch puts it there. [`Explosive::UpdatePostSimFromManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdatePostSimFromManager) applies a pending launch at the end of the tick, setting the body's velocities from [`m_InitialAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_InitialAngularVelocity) and [`m_DeferredInitialVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredInitialVelocity) and disabling collision with the thrower, so a grenade takes its release through those fields and moves one tick later.

- A grenade with its pin in leaves inert: the release queues the pose in [`m_DeferredPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredPhysicsTransform) and writes the deferred launch fields, without the launch's trail. It lies in the world as the loadout's item.
- A live grenade is re-attached to `m_PrimedGrenade` and fired through [`WeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Projectile), so the engine's launch owns the effects, with the launch hook replacing its start and motion; the grenade weapon's override never deducts ammunition. When the launch cannot go ahead ([`Inventory::m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades) off), it falls back to the inert path with the fuse running. The item retires once the grenade is in the simulation, since taking it up as an item would give it a fresh inert actor and lose its fuse.

Every release waits for the physics body, since the deferred launch dereferences it unchecked, and retires nothing until the launch has landed.

### Dropped grenades

A dropped safe grenade keeps its table entry and can be taken again by a free hand closing on it (`GrabVolume::LiveGrenade`, reached from the controller's grip where the drawn hand cannot reach; [grip.md](grip.md#resolving-a-hand)) or by the grapple snatch ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md)). Its landed body is simulating, so taking it up gives it a fresh inert actor and removes the landed one, the only case where an item changes actors.

## Grenades lying in the world

A finite native hand grenade lying in the world (not [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup)) is one grenade. A free hand's take (`grenade::take_world_pickup`) admits a safe item first and only then consumes the native grenade, rounds emptied and despawned, so one native grenade becomes exactly one item; the snatch's catch makes the same take ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md#the-pull-and-the-catch)).

A character's drop makes the grenade a safe item from the start (`grenade::world`). Every drop of a hand grenade reaches [`WeaponBase::OnDropBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnDropBase), since the grenade weapon has no drop of its own and the game's drops (a death's [`m_DropGrenadeChance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_DropGrenadeChance), the drop state task) all go through [`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon). The drop runs in the world update, where nothing may be spawned, so the detour notes it and the next game stage spawns a safe item at the grenade's pose and motion (`safe::spawn_in_world`), retrying briefly on a full pool. Grenades dropped before the mod loaded, under the gamepad scheme, or from untraced placement and loot paths stay native and are taken as above.

## Locker sources

A free hand can take a grenade from a native display or a static collection display (`grenade::locker`), its nearest within reach. Completion rechecks the same source, admits a safe item, and only then removes the grenade, so a changed source refuses rather than picking another. A native display, a hand grenade in a dropped or rack state, is consumed through the game's own pickup with its rounds emptied (`stock::consume_through_pickup`), so it does everything the pickup does but credit.

A static collection display is a grenade model instance in one of the Guardia outposts' model collections, which the game never picks up. The adapter accepts only an allowlist of collection paths with expected instance counts (`locker::format`), captured as they load ([`Location::LoadModelCollection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/location::Location##func-LoadModelCollection), with full bounds checks) and discovered among already loaded locations (`collection::discovery`). The registry keeps copied records, never pointers, and drops a collection's records in the [`ModelCollection::Destruct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelCollection##func-Destruct) detour. A take hides the instance by clearing [`VISIBLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceFlags##flag-VISIBLE) and `FLAG_ENABLED`, as the game's own hide does, until the location reloads.

## Live grenades

A free hand catches a live grenade near the player and throws it on (`grenade::catch`). The detour on [`Grenade::PropelToward`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-PropelToward) notes every launched grenade with its body ([`Explosive::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_PfxInstance)), revalidated each tick. Each within range is a `GrabVolume::LiveGrenade` for each free hand; the player's own waits a moment after launch. Once caught, each tick sets the body's velocity to carry it to the hand's hold, so it keeps its own physics and its fuse runs on; opening the fingers throws it through the owned-body setter.

## The beacon

The primed beacon of the selected beacon weapon is held in the dominant hand at the grenade's hold, and the game's own fire of it held back (`grenade::beacon`). Closing and opening the grip throws it through the weapon's own launch with the hand's release. A thrown beacon is not caught.

## C4

A grip press at the C4 pouch primes a charge in that hand. The game's planting mode (equip, a camera-aimed ghost, an animation) is bypassed: before [`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial), which runs every frame whatever is wielded, the mod calls [`PrimeExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-PrimeExplosive) and puts back the weapon state priming changes. Priming consumes nothing; the only limit is how many charges may be placed. At the limit ([`CanPlantExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-CanPlantExplosive)) the pouch gives the detonator instead. A grab is refused while [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives) is off. The weapon wheel's explosives enter the planting mode, which the mod leaves with [`Character::HolsterWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HolsterWeapon) and then primes a charge in a free hand (`c4::wheel`).

Opening the fingers throws the charge (`c4::stage`). Once it has a body, [`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile) makes it live and counted, and the mod drops it as the game drops a charge under the parachute: moved to the grip, [`PlantedExplosive::DropExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-DropExplosive), then the release motion. The charge's own collision handler sticks it to what it lands on. A release at the limit takes the charge back rather than throw one nothing would detonate.

### C4 pressed onto a surface

A held charge out of the pouch for a moment casts six short rays from its centre along the grip frame's axes each aim update, excluding the player (`c4::stick`). A surface in contact is pressed onto: the next weapon update makes the charge live, stands it off the surface along the normal, drops it, and pushes it on, so the game's collision handler sticks it exactly as a thrown charge sticks (`Event::C4Stick`). The same rays reach further to mark where it would stick (`GrabVolume::ChargeSurface`).

### Taking a placed charge back

Each charge on [`m_ActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_ActiveExplosives) near the player is a grab target for a free hand (`c4::placed`, `GrabVolume::PlacedCharge`). The weapon's next serial update primes the replacement in the hand first and only then removes the placed one ([`RemoveFromActiveList`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-RemoveFromActiveList), [`Explosive::RemoveSelf`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-RemoveSelf)), so a take works at the limit. The taken charge sticks to nothing until its probes have once found every surface clear. The planting permission does not refuse a take, since it is off while the grapple is busy at a snatch's catch.

### The detonator

The detonator is held like a gun and its trigger detonates the placed charges (`c4::detonator`). There is one, so a hand that takes it takes it from the other; opening the hand returns it to the chest, never the world. Its model is an instance the mod owns of the C4 weapon's base model ([`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance)), the trigger the game shows in Rico's hand during its detonate act ([planted-explosives.md, "Detonation"](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md#detonation)); while that act plays, its detonator is placed at the pressing hand. A hand holding an unthrown charge detonates with its trigger too. The press that brought the detonator into the hand never detonates.

### Detonation

A trigger press requests a detonation: the C4 weapon's serial update raises [`WeaponInput::E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES) for its own update only, until [`m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput) rises or a timeout, so the game detonates exactly as its own input would, under [`GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives). A press the game would not act on, or with none placed, buzzes. The detonate act schedules the charges partway through its animation, so while charges are in flight a press buzzes rather than queue a second request that could set off a charge placed just after.

## Diagnostics

Every grenade whose pin the player pulls is followed in the log until it detonates (`grenade::fuse_log`, info level, `controllers` target): the pin, the release, the contact trigger's arming, its contacts ([`Grenade::OnCollision`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-OnCollision)), and the detonation ([`Grenade::Detonate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-Detonate)) with its cause. The debug UI's Weapons > Grenades, C4, and the detonator page shows the stages, fuse, stock, catches, placed charges, and detonator; the Inventory and body slots page reads out the pouch models.

Not yet checked in the headset: pool pressure, whether the allowlisted collections cover every grenade shelf, and whether a velocity-driven caught grenade holds steady.
