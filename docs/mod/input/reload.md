# The true reload

Under the controller scheme the game's timed reload is replaced by one done with the hands: the magazine is ejected with a button or a flick of the gun, a fresh one is taken from a pouch on the belt and pushed into the gun's own magazine well, and a gun that ran dry is chambered by working its slide, charging handle, bolt, or pump. A gun can also be reloaded in one hand by pushing its well down onto the pouch. The code is in `payload/src/controllers/weapons/reload/`, the settings are `controllers.weapons.reload`, and the engine side is in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ammo-and-reload.md). Nothing here has been run in the headset yet; see "Unverified" at the end.

## Which weapons

A weapon is reloaded by hand when it is wielded in the hands, has a magazine limit, is not one of the weapons whose reload has no animation, and is of a class with something to load: the pistols, revolvers, SMGs, rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles, grenade and rocket launchers, and the mortar (`plan.rs`). The minigun, the mounted and vehicle guns, and the special weapons keep the game's reload, as does every weapon with the reload off (`enabled`).

What a weapon loads and how its action is worked come from the wells table (see "Wells"), or from its class when the table has no entry for it:

| Load | Weapons | Eject | Load | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Magazine | pistols, SMGs, rifles, sniper rifles, grenade launchers, the Hydra's rocket pod | yes | a whole magazine | slide, charging handle, bolt, or none |
| Shell | shotguns | no | one shell per push | the pump of the pump-action shotgun, otherwise none |
| Rocket | rocket launchers, the mortar | no | one round into the muzzle | none |
| Cylinder | revolvers | yes (the spent cylinder) | a whole cylinder | none |

## Stopping the game's reload

Every animated reload the game starts goes through one call, [`Character::StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-StartReloadingWieldingWeapon): the reload button, the automatic reload of an empty or magazine-less gun, the predicted reload while idle, a reserve refilled by a pickup, an animation's request, and the launchers' and sniper rifles' own requests. A detour on it refuses the start for the local player while the input tick reloads either hand's weapon by hand (`gate.rs`). No reload act is queued, so the game's reload state never begins and nothing is left to clean up. The input tick publishes each hand's weapon it reloads with a time stamp; if the tick stops for half a second, the detour stops refusing and the game reloads as usual. Switching the reload off mid-reload leaves a magazine-less gun to the game, which reloads it.

With `button` set to `Native`, a start while the game's reload input is held goes through: the reload button plays the game's timed reload, as an accessibility fallback, while the pouch, the flick, and the belt still work. With `Eject` (the default) the button ejects instead.

## Eject

A tap of the semantic `reload` action (the off-hand's lower face button on foot, in the default layout) ejects the magazine of the dominant hand's gun, or of the other hand's while the dominant hand holds no gun. The same button held is the game's holster, so only a press released within `tap_s` ejects. With `flick_eject` (off by default, since the button already ejects and a fast turn of the gun hand in play reads as a flick too easily), a gun hand turning faster than `flick_speed_dps` ejects that gun's magazine, at most once per `flick_cooldown_s`; the speed is the change in the hand's orientation between two input ticks (`eject.rs`).

The eject is the game's own magazine drop, run on the input tick: [`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) drops the gun's spare magazine prop from the magazine part (hiding the part) or from the gun itself, with the gun's velocity, so a flicked gun throws the magazine; [`WeaponData::SpawnMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-SpawnMagazineObject) asks for the next spare; and the weapon's magazine-out flag ([`WeaponBase::m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine)) is set, which the game itself reads to refuse fire. A weapon with no magazine prop drops nothing. With the magazines in the world off, the rounds left in the ejected magazine stay counted in the weapon until the next magazine seats, when the game's reload returns them to the reserve, as the game's own reload does; nothing is lost and there is no extra round in the chamber. With them on (the default), the rounds leave the gun with the magazine (see "Magazines in the world").

## Physical magazine removal

Physical extraction is disabled at the engine boundary. [`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) requires the physics world's write mark. The input tick does not establish that authority, and no verified write guard or writable serial executor is installed for this path. `engine::physical_detach_available` therefore returns false and the provider withholds `GrabVolume::InsertedMagazine`. The legacy button and belt input calls share an unresolved write-authority risk; they do not justify enabling another caller.

The prepared provider in `reload/extraction.rs` requires a tracked free hand, an inserted detachable magazine, a table-defined well, a measured gun frame, world conversion, and a live tagged spare prop. Shell-fed pumps, revolver cylinders, muzzle-loaded rockets, and fallback wrist wells are excluded. Both `reload.extraction.enabled` and `reload.loose.enabled` must be enabled after a verified executor is installed.

A fresh grip claim on that target starts a pull. No release button or additional latch is required. The hand must travel outward along the well's axis by `reload.extraction.pull_m`. Movement is measured relative to the moving gun, so translating or rotating the gun with the hand does not complete the pull. The grab radius and sideways tolerance have separate settings. The target centre lies outside the well along its axis by `reload.loose.half_length_m`, converted from model lengths to the player's scale. This is a configured approximation of the magazine centre, not a measured magazine hull.

Before detachment, opening the grip, losing hand tracking or world conversion, changing the gun or world scale, moving beyond the sideways tolerance, or disabling extraction cancels the pull. Cancellation leaves the magazine inserted, with its ammunition, chamber readiness, and action travel unchanged. An existing brace or action hold prevents acquisition. For a new grip inside the magazine target, `reload::prioritize_extraction` removes overlapping brace, gun-transfer, and action targets before ordinary nearest-target resolution. An active pull owns the hand exclusively and blocks the gun from firing. Bracing outside the magazine target and deliberate grip transfer retain their existing paths.

Pull completion submits a value request through `DetachEngine`; it does not call the engine from input. A later result distinguishes `NoThrow`, `DroppedUnretained`, and `Retained`. A false engine throw requests no spare and changes no ammunition, magazine-out flag, readiness, or visibility. A successful unretained drop leaves rounds in the engine's native accounting without a reserve refund. A retained result takes the exact recorded prop into the pulling hand and starts insertion with `InsertionSource::RetainedMagazine`. Retention capture matches both the expected gun and spare, so an unrelated reentrant throw cannot replace the result. The engine wrapper carries raw addresses, with no exclusive owner borrow across throw or spawn callbacks. The chamber record remains readiness only, not a separate counted round.

An active or pending pull blocks belt processing for that gun and resets an already-started belt push. The block persists through the result or cancellation tick. Physical/world magazines do not use the pouch ammunition timeout while held. Grip release and tracking loss still release them, and the registry keeps its held entry beyond the world retention lifetime. Pouch ammunition retains its existing timeout.

The shared grab-volume registration, hand-anchored placement, finish-outcome registration, and priority call are applied. The input owner calls `reload::cancel_extraction_on_standdown` on entry to UI. That operation clears only the extraction claim and pending request, preserves unrelated holds, and requires neutral grip before a fresh claim. The production mailbox has no admission opener or native consumer. The uninstalled native detour is removed until callback-entry exclusion through hook removal is established. Isolated mailbox tests retain the execution and completion model without installing a hook.

The current workspace test run passes the state-orchestration, engine-outcome, pull, priority, insertion, and source-lifetime cases. A first deferred carry after world pickup retains the acquired Holding state, hand association, and take count. Recovery seats and transfers rounds once; release clears the hold without transferring rounds. Passing these tests does not enable physical extraction. The engine execution boundary, legacy input write authority, native prop lifetime, and live all-state behavior remain open.

## Load

The pouch (`weapons.pouches.magazine`) is one universal pouch at the front of the belt on the side of the hand without the gun, so that hand reaches it without crossing the body, whichever hand the gun rides ([controllers.md](controllers.md#sockets-pouches-the-foregrip-and-the-action-runs-tier) places it). It hands out whatever the gun in the other hand takes, and it is a grab volume for the hand without a gun while a reloaded weapon is held. A grab with no ammunition left (and no unlimited ammunition) gives nothing and a faint pulse.

The hand holds the round with its top `held_top_m` ahead of the closed fist, pointing the way the fist points (`insert.rs`). The top catches the well when it comes within `capture_radius_m` of the well's mouth, turned no more than `capture_angle_deg` from the well's axis, and moving into the gun. A caught round slides only along the axis and seats `seat_depth_m` past the mouth; pulled back out it is held again. Letting go drops it, unless it is more than half seated, which seats it, and a round not seated within `timeout_s` is dropped. A magazine is taken only by a gun whose magazine is out; a shell or a rocket by a gun that is not full.

A seated magazine, cylinder, or rocket loads through the weapon's own reload ([`WeaponBase::Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Reload)), which keeps every rule of the game's: the reserve, unlimited ammunition, the upgrades, the rocket on the launcher, the magazine-out flag, and the HUD. A shell adds one round through [`WeaponBase::SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo) and takes it from the reserve through [`Inventory::SetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-SetAmmoCount), unless the ammunition is unlimited. Pickups, crates, and cheats only touch the reserve, so they keep working; the game's reload-on-refill is one of the starts the detour refuses.

While a round is in hand, the gun's well is a touch target, marked gold at its mouth on the gun ([grip.md](grip.md#markers)): the round goes in as the hand brings it there, without a squeeze. A weapon whose magazine is a separate model part shows the part again in the hand that took it, drawn at the hand's grip by the late update of the gun's parts, and hides it if the round is dropped with the gun's magazine still out; a seated magazine shows the part back in the gun ([`WeaponComponentBase::Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-Enable)). Pistols and SMGs draw their magazine as part of the body, so nothing shows in the hand.

## Wells

The well's mouth and axis are in the gun's model frame, from the wells table (`tools/sights/wells.json`, derived from the game's models by `tools/sights/extract_wells.py`; see [the tool's README](../../../tools/sights/README.md#magazine-wells)). A weapon with a separate magazine part has its well where the part enters the gun; pistols and SMGs, whose magazine is part of the body mesh, have it at the bottom of the grip. The gun's frame is carried to the hand by measuring the gun's world matrix against the wrist target its placement followed, as the foregrip does (`frames.rs`); the measurement needs the wrist at full weight and is kept until the next one. A weapon the table lacks (or grades as failed) has its well at `well_offset_m` in the holding wrist's frame, its axis toward the wrist.

## Magazines in the world

With `loose.enabled` (on; in the debug UI's Controllers tab, under "Weapons"), the mod can retain dropped magazine props for pickup (`loose.rs`). The detour on [`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) reserves a fixed accounting slot for the exact gun and spare before the native call. The store has 32 slots shared by pending reservations, executing drops, unread results, held magazines, and world records. It has no overflow collection. Each committed record has an opaque generation-bearing handle, so an old sighting or delayed result cannot acquire a replacement that reuses the same native addresses.

- An admitted manual eject transfers the gun's remaining rounds into its retained prop and clears the gun's count only after retention succeeds. At capacity, the manual eject is refused before native mutation; ammunition, visibility, readiness, and magazine state remain unchanged.
- An automatic local-player drop can be admitted as an empty magazine because native reload retains its ammunition accounting. An already empty NPC magazine can also be admitted. At capacity, automatic calls run the original game path unchanged, and their props remain untracked and are not offered as mod ammunition.
- Nonempty NPC pickup remains an engineering blocker, not an approved feature removal. The native throw does not debit the weapon's rounds, and a finite native reload returns the old count to reserve. The old detour copied that count into a world record without removing it from native accounting. No verified discard or ownership-transfer boundary makes that import safe. Those calls therefore remain native and untracked until such a boundary is established; the mod does not clear NPC ammunition or invent a native prop-retirement call.

A record is offered only while its committed handle is current, its body has no observed ownership loss, and a current pose read succeeds. A body ID and owner pointer do not prove a native lifetime across reuse. While a gun's magazine is out, each usable retained magazine of the gun's tag within `loose.range_m` (2 m) is a grab volume for the hand without a gun (`GrabVolume::WorldMagazine`, radius `loose.radius_m`, 0.1 m). A grip press there takes the nearest as the round in hand (`reload: picked a magazine up from the world`, with its rounds), with no pouch needed and whatever the reserve: the magazine prop itself is carried to the hand each tick by its body's velocities, turned so its top points out of the hand, its centre `loose.half_length_m` behind the held top, so the magazine the player sees in the hand is the one from the ground (a gun's magazine part does not show in the hand meanwhile). It is inserted into the well as a pouch magazine is. Seated, it loads its own rounds rather than a reload from the reserve: the rounds still counted in the gun go back to the reserve, the gun's magazine is set to the magazine's rounds (up to its size), the magazine-out flag clears, the part shows, and the prop is disabled ([`GameObject::Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-Enable)), which takes it out of the world (`reload: seated a magazine from the world`). Let go of before it seats, it falls from the hand with the motion the carry gave it and stays in the world to be picked up again.

The physical hands can also pick a magazine up as any light loose object ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)) when the gun does not want one; the magazine's grab volume wins over the hands' pickup while it does. Only usable, unheld, unleased empty records expire after `loose.keep_s` (300 s). Nonempty records never expire to make room. Maintenance scans the fixed store before reload eligibility checks, without requiring sightings, while the controller input tick runs. Unavailable physics, a non-dynamic body, or bad motion preserves the record and its capacity. A missing or reowned body also preserves accounting and permanently quarantines pickup and seating, since the current API provides no lifetime generation. A later matching body ID cannot restore it. Pose observations from sightings, carry, and seating, and carry velocity-write results all update the matching committed generation immediately. Temporary failures defer the insertion transaction and preserve its hand association and counters. Recovery retries the operation; no ammunition moves until seating commits. Grip release still clears the held input association, including during a deferred or quarantined operation. Confirmed destruction cleanup requires a verified lifetime or world-generation invalidation source, which is not integrated. Retained or quarantined records can therefore keep the store full; manual drops remain refused and automatic drops remain native and untracked in that state. The debug UI's Status tab shows `MAGAZINES` with the counts, and the scenario signals `magazines.dropped`, `magazines.picked_up`, and `magazines.seated` count them (`testing/scenarios/magazines_in_the_world.toml`).

## The belt reload

Pushing a gun's well down onto the pouch reloads it in one hand (`belt.rs`), for every weapon reloaded by hand, whether or not a hand is free. The mouth has to enter the pouch moving down faster than `belt.min_push_speed_mps`, with the gun upright enough that its insertion axis leans no more than `belt.max_tilt_deg` from straight up, and then go `belt.depth_m` into it. An entry that misses any of these does nothing until the mouth leaves the pouch, and the belt waits `belt.after_draw_s` after a draw, so drawing from the hip holster beside the pouch never reloads. A magazine still in the gun drops as the fresh one goes in, so no eject is needed first. A belt reload works the action as well. The gun cannot fire from the push until the mouth leaves the pouch. A shotgun takes one shell per push.

The current wrist hint is limited to the dominant hand's eligible belt reload. The reload owner captures the insertion, extraction, draw-delay, well, and pouch facts after its steps, then reads the current gun's final ammunition state after weapon finish. A same-tick offer reaches the wrist only while the final gun claims still match and that physical hand is ready. The hint describes the push motion before it is performed; the existing speed, tilt, depth, and leave-before-retry checks still determine whether a push reloads. Pouch display models and native reload-button permission do not establish belt eligibility.

## The chamber

The game has no chambered round; a gun fires while its magazine has rounds. The chamber is the mod's (`chamber.rs`). With `chamber` on, a gun that fires its last round has an empty chamber, and a gun that is reloaded while its chamber is empty stays empty until its action is worked: the trigger only clicks. A tactical reload, of a gun with rounds left, keeps the round in the chamber. There is no slide-release button; the belt reload chambers by itself.

The action is worked with the hand without a gun. With `action.physical` on (the default), the action is a part that follows the hand through its travel (see "The physical action"). Off, it is a gesture:

- A slide or a charging handle: a grip press at the handle (a grip target while the chamber waits), a pull back along the gun's axis to `action.pull_fraction` of its travel, and a release or a push back home.
- A bolt: the same, but it has to be pushed back home.
- A pump: the hand already on the foregrip slides back and forward along the barrel.

The handle's place and travel come from the wells table; a weapon whose handle the table does not place is never held for its chamber.

With `manual_action` on, a bolt-action rifle and a pump-action shotgun empty their chamber after every shot, whether or not `chamber` is on, and the game's own cycling is suppressed (see "The bolt and the pump"). A worked action loads the chamber when the magazine is in and holds a round. With the action a gesture, while a pistol's chamber is empty its slide is drawn back, and while it is worked it follows the hand: the game moves a slide back along its own axis after each shot and all the way while the magazine is empty ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength)), and the late update of the gun's parts adds the rest (`render.rs`, through `hands/parts.rs`).

A gun held by its chamber, by a magazine out, or by the belt cannot fire: the input tick keeps its trigger from the game and plays `Event::DryFire` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)) at the pull's press edge, and the ammunition check clears the gun's shot as well against a buffered one (`empty.rs`). With the dual-wield split a held pistol's trigger is kept from the split, so the other pistol still fires.

## The bolt and the pump

The sniper rifle keeps its own bolt lock ([`SniperWeapon::m_HasFiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_HasFiredThisInput)), set by each shot, which blocks fire until the bolt animation runs. With `manual_action`, the rifle's update runs with its bolt-action setting cleared, so the game never queues the bolt act, and the lock keeps the rifle from firing until the player works the bolt; the worked bolt clears the lock. A reload by hand does not raise the reloading state that would clear the lock, so a rifle reloaded after running dry still needs its bolt worked.

The pump-action shotgun's pump is queued on every shot by its do-act component ([`DoActWeaponComponent::Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-Fire_Start)), on the holder and on the gun's own skeleton. With `manual_action` the component's fire start is skipped for the local player, and nothing in the game stops the next shot, so the chamber does. The worked pump plays the gun's pump clip ([`WeaponData::DoAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-DoAct)).

## The physical action

With `action.physical` on, each gun's action is a part on its travel (`travel.rs`, worked in `action.rs`): a place from home to the rear as a share of its travel, and for a bolt a turn up as a share of its lift. Each gun keeps its own, remembered with its chamber across weapon changes.

- Taking it. The handle is the grab volume `GrabVolume::WeaponAction` at the handle's place, which rides the part, so a slide held back is taken where it is. It is placed for the hand without a gun while the chamber waits for the action or the part is out of battery, so a bolt left open can always be closed; a gun whose chamber is loaded and whose action is closed offers nothing to take. The pump is taken by the hand on the foregrip, and only while its chamber waits: a fired pump-action unlocks its pump, a loaded one holds it forward. The volume is the action's region of the gun's grab regions (`regions::action_volume`, [physical-guns.md, "Grabbing a gun anywhere"](physical-guns.md#grabbing-a-gun-anywhere)), which the reload places while the chamber waits; at any other time a hand on the action braces the gun there.
- Following the hand. Held, the part follows the hand along the gun's axis from wherever along it the hand took hold, between home and the rear: it never jumps to the hand, and the hand can pull no further than the travel. A hand that strays more than `action.release_distance_m` off the axis, or lets go of its grip (of the foregrip, for the pump), lets go of the part.
- Let go. A slide or a charging handle runs home on its spring, over `action.spring_s` from the rear. A pump stays where it is let go, and settles home once forward of `action.return_fraction`. A bolt stays where it is.
- The bolt. A bolt is turned up about its axis (`action.bolt_lift_deg`, the handle `action.bolt_radius_m` from the axis, on the side the table places it) before it slides; it counts as up past `action.lift_fraction` of the turn. It cannot be turned down until it is home, and a bolt not turned down is out of battery.
- The rack. The part reaching `action.pull_fraction` of its travel is the rack (`Event::Rack` on the working hand). Coming home after one, forward of `action.return_fraction` (and for a bolt, turned down), cycles the action: the chamber loads from the magazine when it is in and holds a round, the bolt rifle's lock clears, and `Event::Chambered` plays on the gun hand. The gun's own pump or bolt clip is not played, since the hand moved the part already. A short pull runs home without cycling.
- The slide's catch. With `action.lock_back` on, the shot that empties a pistol's magazine leaves its slide caught at the rear, as does a weapon first seen with an empty magazine, and a slide pulled to the rear and let go over a magazine that is in and empty catches there too. A caught slide stays back through an eject and a fresh magazine; taking it frees it from the catch, and letting go runs it home, which chambers from the new magazine (a rack). With no magazine in, the slide does not catch. The game holds a pistol's slide back on its own while the magazine is empty, so a slide that the travel has home is drawn home against that hold. There is no slide-release button: every button of the gun hand is taken on foot, so the slide is released by a rack, and the belt reload closes the action by itself.
- Out of battery. A gun whose part is back past `action.return_fraction`, or whose bolt is turned up, does not fire, whatever its chamber.

The part is drawn where the travel has it:

- A separate part (the pistols' slides, and the U-SV 45's `ejection_port`): the late update of the gun's parts moves it back along its own axis by its share of the game's own travel for it ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength)), less what the game holds it back by on an empty magazine ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo)), so the game's kick after a shot still shows. The U-SV 45's part is a cover beside the bore, so it slides with the bolt's travel but does not turn.
- A bone of the gun's skeleton (the U-Rys 96's pump, [`WeaponData::PUMP_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData), and the CS111 Archangel's and the Final Argument's bolt, [`WeaponData::SLIDER_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)): a post-call detour on the gun's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)) moves the bone's model-space joint back along the model's +Z by its share of the table's travel and turns it about the model's Z by the bolt's lift, over the pose the gun's clips gave it (`bones.rs`). The update runs on the simulation's workers for every character's guns; anything but the reloaded weapons' guns is turned away before a lock. A part at home is left to the clips.
- Nothing (the SMGs' and rifles' charging handles, part of the body mesh): the travel, its stops, and its rack act, and nothing on the gun moves.

While the pump is back, the foregrip moves back along the barrel with it (`foregrip::set_pump_slide`), so the drawn off-hand rides the pump. With the hand bodies and `controllers.physical_hands.bodies.guns.enabled` on, the off-hand on the foregrip is drawn at its body, joined to the gun body where it took the foregrip, so the drawn hand stays at the brace while the pump moves.

The log has, at info level, `reload: the action cycled` with the action, the gun hand, and whether a round was chambered; `reload: the last shot locked the slide back`; `reload: the slide caught at the rear on an empty magazine`; and `reload: took the slide off its catch`. At debug level, `reload: took the action's handle`. The debug UI's Status tab shows `LEFT ACTION` and `RIGHT ACTION` under `WEAPONS IN HAND`: the action and where its part is (`HOME`, `BACK 40%`, `UP 100%, BACK 30%`, or `LOCKED BACK`), `HELD` while a hand holds it, the chamber, and the actions worked. The scenario signals are `actions.worked`, `actions.locked_back`, `actions.held`, and `actions.travel` (`testing/scenarios/physical_actions.toml`).

## Each hand's gun

Every gun in a hand is reloaded on its own, whichever hand holds it and whether the game wields it or it is armed in its hand ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The per-hand tick"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-per-hand-tick)): its plan, its well, its magazine, its chamber, and its action are its own weapon's. The shots of each weapon are counted against it, each hand's gun is held from firing on its own weapon's state, and the game's own reload is stopped while either weapon is reloaded by hand. A tap of the reload button ejects the dominant hand's gun. The pouch is placed only for a hand holding nothing, so with a gun in each hand no round is taken by hand: a flick of a hand ejects its gun, and the belt push loads it.

A single pistol ([physical-weapons.md, "Separate pistols"](physical-weapons.md#separate-pistols)) is a weapon of its own with one gun, and is reloaded as any other: its magazine is its own, half the tuned size rounded up, which the reload reads as its capacity, and its reserve is the character's. The game's own reload fills it to the tuned size, so a load through the game's reload is brought down to the half at once, the rounds over it going back to the reserve. The game's pair, while the pistols are not made separate, is one weapon of one magazine with a gun in each hand: each hand's eject drops that gun's magazine prop and takes the pair's magazine out, and a load fills the pair.

A weapon is remembered by its item in the loadout table when the table holds it, and otherwise by its object (`records.rs`), so its chambers and its actions outlive the spawn reset's replacement of the weapon object, as they outlive a weapon change. A gun's chamber and action are kept by the gun's place in its weapon rather than by the hand, so a weapon handed to the other hand keeps them.

## Haptics

Each step plays a cue of the weapons category ([haptics.md](haptics.md)): the eject on the gun hand (`Event::Eject`), a round taken from the pouch (`Event::PouchTake`), an empty pouch (`Event::PouchEmpty`), a round caught by the well and a seat on both hands (`Event::WellCatch`, `Event::MagazineSeat`), a belt seat on the gun hand (`Event::BeltSeat`), a full pull of the action on the working hand (`Event::Rack`), the physical action's part meeting a stop (home, a bolt turned all the way up or down) on the working hand, or on the gun hand for a slide run home by its spring or caught at the rear (`Event::ActionDetent`), and a chambered round on the gun hand (`Event::Chambered`). The dry fire is `Event::DryFire`.

## The readout

The Controllers tab's weapon section shows each reloaded weapon's name hash, its load and action, whether its well came from the table, the magazine and the reserve (the pair's once), each gun hand's chamber, belt push, fire hold, and turning speed, the round in hand and its distance from the mouth, each gun hand's physical action (how far back, a bolt's turn, held, locked back, racked) or the gesture's work, the recent reload events, and the counters (ejects, seats, belt reloads, actions worked, slides locked back, dry fires, empty pouch grabs, and the game's reload starts refused). The physical action's settings are beside the gesture's.

## In the headset: magazines in the world

1. With a rifle drawn and a few rounds fired, tap the reload button: the magazine drops, the HUD reads zero, and `MAGAZINES` counts one in the world. Take a fresh magazine from the pouch, seat it, then fire the rifle dry and eject that one too.
2. Reach down to the first magazine on the ground with the free hand: `reload: picked a magazine up from the world` with the rounds it held. The magazine rises into the hand, top out, and stays there as the hand moves. Seat it: `reload: seated a magazine from the world`, the magazine on the ground is gone, and the HUD shows the rounds it held. Check the reserve did not change beyond the rounds the gun still held.
3. Pick the empty one up, seat it: the gun reads zero and the chamber asks for the action.
4. Pick one up and open the hand away from the gun: it falls and can be picked up again.
5. Observe an empty NPC magazine drop with free capacity, then pick it up with a compatible gun: it seats empty. Observe a nonempty NPC drop separately: it must stay native and untracked while the ammunition-transfer blocker remains unresolved. Do not treat this as acceptance of removing nonempty NPC pickup.
6. Leave an unheld empty magazine for longer than `loose.keep_s`: its record expires after a successful maintenance read. Nonempty, held, and unread-result records remain accounted. A temporarily unavailable body must not lower the count or free capacity.
7. Fill the fixed store with retained nonempty magazines. A further manual eject must leave ammunition and native state unchanged. An automatic drop must follow the native path without adding a tracked record or offering new mod ammunition. Seat a tracked magazine to free capacity, then verify that a later safe automatic drop can be admitted.

## In the headset: the physical action

With `reload.chamber`, `reload.manual_action`, and `reload.action.physical` on, `RIGHT ACTION` on the debug UI's Status tab open, and the hand bodies off for the first pass:

1. Draw a pistol and fire it dry: the last shot leaves the slide back, `reload: the last shot locked the slide back`, and `RIGHT ACTION` reads `SLIDE: LOCKED BACK, CHAMBER EMPTY`. The trigger only clicks.
2. Eject and seat a fresh magazine from the pouch: the slide stays back. Reach to the slide's rear with the left hand and press the grip: `reload: took the slide off its catch`, `HELD`. Ease the slide forward a little and back: it follows the hand and stops at the rear, with a click at the rear. Let go: it slams home with a tick in the gun hand, `reload: the action cycled` with `chambered=true`, a click on the gun hand, and the pistol fires.
3. Take the slide again with the gun loaded: nothing is taken (the action is closed and chambered). Fire it to a few rounds, then grab the slide: still nothing.
4. Fire it dry again, eject, and with no magazine in take the slide, pull it to the rear, and let go: it runs home without catching, and the chamber stays empty. Seat a magazine with the slide home; rack it (pull to the rear, let go): chambered.
5. Fire it dry, take the slide, and pull it only halfway before letting go: it runs home, nothing is chambered, and `RIGHT ACTION` reads `HOME`. Pull it all the way and let go: chambered.
6. Watch the slide while doing the above: it is drawn where the hand has it, never jumps to the hand when taken, and does not run past the rear. With the magazine empty and the slide home in the travel, it is drawn home, not held back by the game.
7. Draw the pump shotgun and fire once: the pump unlocks. With the left hand on the foregrip, pull back: the pump (and the drawn left hand) slides back along the barrel, with a click at the rear; push forward: `reload: the action cycled`, a tick, and the shotgun fires again. Let go of the foregrip halfway back: the pump stays there and the gun does not fire; take the foregrip again and push it home.
8. With the shotgun loaded, pull back on the foregrip: the pump does not move.
9. Draw the CS111 Archangel (or the Final Argument) and fire once. Take the bolt's handle on the right of the receiver: turn it up (a tick at the top), pull it back (a click at the rear), push it forward, and turn it down (a tick): `chambered=true`. Check that the bolt is drawn turning and sliding with the hand, and that it cannot be pulled back before it is turned up, or turned down before it is home. Let go with the bolt open: it stays open, the rifle does not fire, and the handle can be taken again to close it.
10. Draw the U-SV 45 and fire once: work its bolt the same way. Its moving cover beside the bore slides with the travel but does not turn.
11. Draw an assault rifle and fire it dry: after a fresh magazine, take the charging handle on the right of the receiver, pull it back, and let go: it chambers. Nothing on the rifle moves.
12. Do 2 and 7 again with the hand bodies and `physical_hands.bodies.guns.enabled` on: the slide and the bolt work as before; the pump moves but the drawn left hand stays at the brace.
13. Switch `reload.action.physical` off: the action is the gesture again (a pull to the pull share and a release), and the slide is drawn back while the chamber is empty.
