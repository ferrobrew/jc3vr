# Parachute locomotion and steering

How the game moves and steers a parachuting character. The steering helper mixes the camera input matrix into the steering, so the canopy turns toward where the camera points, not only where the stick points. Opening the canopy from a jump or a fall is in [wingsuit.md](wingsuit.md#opening-from-the-air).

## The state task

Parachuting runs on its own movement state task, [`NStateTask_MovementParachuteTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NStateTask_MovementParachuteTask##func-Update), which reads the movement effectors and calls free functions of the `NParachuteMovement` namespace (the [`input::parachute`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute) module): [`UpdateParachutePhysics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-UpdateParachutePhysics), [`GetParachuteSteeringValues`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-GetParachuteSteeringValues), [`UpdateParachutingTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-UpdateParachutingTransforms), [`ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities), and [`ApplyAnimationVelocityWithSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-ApplyAnimationVelocityWithSteering). It then handles the cancel input that closes the canopy. The parachute does not use the fall family's [`UpdateFallSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::locomotion##func-UpdateFallSteering) or the camera look-steer [`NAirMovement_UpdateStdLookSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-NAirMovement_UpdateStdLookSteering); it is a separate movement core. Its tuning block, [`CharacterAirMovementSettings`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::CharacterAirMovementSettings) (the `m_LookSteer*`, `m_XInputYawPitchRollAmount`, and spring fields), is also embedded in the wingsuit and freefall settings.

## The stick's vertical axis

The task builds the input vector from the action map's move half-axes: across is [`MOVE_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-MOVE_RIGHT) minus `MOVE_LEFT`, along is `MOVE_BACKWARD` minus [`MOVE_FORWARD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-MOVE_FORWARD), so a stick pulled back is positive, and passes it to `UpdateParachutePhysics` as a `CVector2f`. Both axes steer through the input mixes below. The along axis is also the target of the air-control lift and drag amounts, which follow it through `m_LiftInputSpring` and `m_DragInputSpring`; `ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities` then takes the lift from the three-entry `m_Lift` (the middle entry at rest, blending toward the last with the stick back and the first with it forward) and the drag from `m_DragX`, `m_DragY`, and `m_DragZ` the same way. Pulling back slows the canopy only as far as the shipped tuning makes the last entries a higher lift and drag; the code path is established, the shipped values are not read.

## The steering helper

`UpdateParachutePhysics` calls `GetParachuteSteeringValues` each frame for three outputs: `steering_values_out` (the yaw, pitch, and roll mix), `wanted_char_yawpitchroll_out` (the desired orientation), and `look_steer_out` (the camera-relative look steer). The steering sums two mixes:

- The stick: `input->x` and `input->y` through `m_XInputYawPitchRollAmount` and `m_YInputYawPitchRollAmount`, present even with no camera.
- The look steer, for the local player only (`character->m_IsPlayer`; an NPC's canopy steers from the stick alone). The helper decomposes the camera input matrix ([`GameCameraManager::GetInputMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetInputMatrix)) to Euler angles and takes the yaw and pitch deltas to the wanted orientation (`m_SteeringProxy`), shaped by `m_LookSteerDeadZone`, `m_LookSteerMaxYaw`, and `m_LookSteerExponential` into `look_steer_out`. That is mixed into all three steering axes through `m_XLookYawPitchRollAmount` and `m_YLookYawPitchRollAmount` (the `v63/v61/v62` sum at the helper's tail), and `UpdateParachutePhysics` uses it as the yaw and pitch spring inputs (`v254.x` to `m_RotateCharYawToVelocity`, `v254.y` to `m_RotateCharPitchToVelocity`) and in the slingshot and animation-input blend (`1.0 - look_steer_out.x`). The canopy therefore follows the camera once its relative yaw passes the deadzone, with or without stick input.

The helper also computes a velocity-alignment yaw: above `m_VelocityAlignMinSpeed` of horizontal speed, the wanted yaw is driven toward the velocity's heading through the `SCharacterSpring` `m_RotateCharYawToVelocity`, blended by `m_VelocitySteeringBlendScale`, which keeps the canopy squared to its travel when not steered.

## Orientation

`UpdateParachutePhysics` applies the heading through the proxy matrices of the character's [`ParachuteActionParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::ParachuteActionParams): `m_PivoProxy` (the suspension pivot), `m_CharacterProxy`, `m_ParachuteProxy`, and the blended `m_SteeringProxy` between them. `UpdateParachutingTransforms` copies the steering matrix into `m_SteeringProxy`, converts it to a quaternion, and drives the rig's swing, and the character's world orientation follows it; `ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities` and `ApplyAnimationVelocityWithSteering` compute the velocity response.

## The slingshot

While the grappling hook reels in under the canopy ([`GHS_REELING_IN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_REELING_IN)), `UpdateParachutePhysics` runs the slingshot. It reads the line's direction each update ([`GrapplingHook::GetReelingInDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetReelingInDirection)) and weights all it adds by how far the line points along the facing, clamped to zero for a line beside or behind: an acceleration along the line (`m_SlingshotDirectAccel`), one along the facing (`m_SlingshotForwardAccel`), a raised top speed (`m_SlingshotMaxSpeedAdjust`), and a drag adjustment, each blended toward the retract boost's values (`m_RetractSlingshotSettings`) while the retract is held and the hook allows the boost.

A line beside the character therefore adds almost no speed, and the slingshot does not turn the canopy toward it: the yaw toward the line (the `m_RotateCharYawToSlingshot` spring) sits behind a static switch the game never sets, and behind the look-steer yaw or the stick's across axis passing a small threshold (in `UpdateParachutePhysics`'s def). What turns the canopy toward a line is the look steer, since the player looks where they fire, and the turn is also what brings the pull to bear.

The update breaks the slingshot, retracting the active wire and queuing `ACT_GRAPPLE_CANCEL`, once the reel is within `m_SlingshotBreakDistance`, once the line falls further behind the facing than the break yaw (widening from `m_SlingshotBreakYawStart` to `m_SlingshotBreakYawEnd` over `m_SlingshotBreakYawBlendTime` of reeling), or once the character pitches past `m_SlingshotBreakPitch`. The shipped values are not read.

## The openings out of a reel

A canopy opened during a grapple reel enters one of two openings, `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_LONG` (from `S_GRAPPLE_REEL_IN` within its `SEG_USE_CUSTOM_TO_PARA_TRANSITION` segment) or `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_SHORT` (from the reel's other states, the pre-reels, and the reel's landings), on `ACT_TRIGGER_PARACHUTE` above the reel-to-parachute speed. Both clips roll Rico over into the harness. Each state queues its opening act on the canopy object on entry, and leaves for `S_JC_PARACHUTE_IDLE` once its custom lift segment ends, its clip finishes, or a fire lands within its interrupt segment; `ACT_FORCE_PARACHUTE_IDLE` takes it there early only within `SEG_CAN_ABORT_TRANSITION`.

These act transitions do not pass through [`StateMachineInstance::SetStateInternal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::StateMachineInstance##func-SetStateInternal), whose only callers are `AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`, `AnimationRuleSystem::DoExternalAction`, a debugger callback, and a thunk; the machine's event handling sets the state with its own inlined copy. [`AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::AnimationRuleSystem##func-SetStateById) moves a machine to any state of its definition through it, whatever the current state's transitions allow.

## The handle props

The canopy's rig has a handle on each side on its own cord, three bones a side: the handle (`RightHandle`, `LeftHandle`), its rotation bone (`RightHandleRotate`, `LeftHandleRotate`), and the link the cord hangs from (`RightLink`, `LeftLink`). The handles are not attached to the character's bones. [`NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones), which tracks the rig to its character every update, places them for the player: a placed handle's joint takes the world position of the character's second hand-attach bone (`RightHandAttach2`, `LeftHandAttach2`) in the rig's model space, the rotation bone takes that bone's turn, and the link is drawn a little toward the handle while the handle is further from it than in the bind pose, so the handles are drawn in Rico's hands. An unplaced handle sits on its link. The right handle is not placed while the character wields a weapon or plays `SEG_NO_RIGHT_HANDLE_PLACEMENT`, and the left one while the grapple fires or its hook is out or during `SEG_NO_LEFT_HANDLE_PLACEMENT`; a flag in the parachute's properties suspends both unless `SEG_FORCE_HANDLE_PLACEMENT_OVERRIDE` plays.

The [`NParachuteHandleIKTask`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NParachuteHandleIKTask) state task separately pulls the wrists toward the links through the `SECONDARY` HumanIK pass's blackboard keys, weighted by its blend-in and blend-out segments.
