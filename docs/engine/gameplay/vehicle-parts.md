# Vehicle parts

A vehicle is built from separately modelled parts (the body, doors, wheels, mirrors, steering wheel, and so on) that can deform, loosen, and break off. The doors are in [vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md).

## The parts resource

Each vehicle entity embeds a `<model>_vehicle_parts.vmodc`, an ADF `VehicleParts` resource whose `parts` array holds one [`Part`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part) per piece: its `name`, `damage_type`, `behavior`, `model`, and `model_swap` as ADF hash strings (a string pointer with its `hashlittle` hash, [`AdfHashString`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::AdfHashString)), its own and its parent's index, and its `parent_space_offset`, the pivot in its parent's space. The resource's `hinged_parts`, `rotating_parts`, and `skinned_parts` name the parts the engine animates, and `door_parts` the door parts, whose behaviours (`FrontLeftDoor`, `Hood`, `Trunk`, and so on) match the door slots' names; that the door controller fills its slots by behaviour is unverified.

Part names follow `<model>_<piece>`. The name identifies a part; the damage type, chosen for the damage taken, does not (some steering wheels use `Land_Generic` or `Boat_Body`, and the modern mini's and old moped's mirrors `Land_Steering_Wheel`).

A part's mesh is authored in its own space, about the pivot its offset places. Most parts, the doors, hoods, and trunks included, have an identity offset under the body, so their meshes are in the vehicle's space (+X right, +Y up, -Z forward, the driver on -X in a car); a loose door hangs from its prototype constraint, whose point is also a deform point. A hinged part the engine turns, such as a handlebar, has its hinge pivot as its offset.

Each entity also embeds a `<model>_vehicle_deform.vmodc`, an ADF `DeformPoints` resource of named, typed transforms in the vehicle's space, each with its source part and the part it is skinned to, moved with the deformation. Among them are the seats the interaction graph's nodes name ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-interaction-graph-in-the-entity)), each loose part's constraint (type `Constraint`, named after the part's `..._CON` node), lights, and stunt-area corners.

## The live parts

A vehicle's physics body, [`Vehicle::m_PfxVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_PfxVehicle), is a `CPfxVehicle`, whose `CPfxBreakable` base ([`PfxBreakable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable)) holds one [`PartInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance) per part entry, contiguous by part index; `GetPartByIndex` bounds the index by the resource's part count. The render and update passes walk the valid parts' list, `m_ValidPartIndices`.

A part instance points at its entry (`m_PartProperties`) and its parent instance (`m_ParentPart`). Its `m_RootSpaceTransform` maps the part's space into the body's root space: the parent space offset under the parent's root-space transform, moved further by a hinge's angle, a loosened part's swing, or deformation. Its model instance is `m_PartModelInstance`, and [`ModelInstance::GetModelSpaceBoundingBox`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-GetModelSpaceBoundingBox) gives the model's box in the part's space from the shared model resource. `BrokeOffOrAncestorBrokeOff` is true once the part, or any part it hangs from, has broken off.

## Drawing the parts

The vehicle keeps its world transform at both ends of the simulation step, `m_WorldMatrixT0` and `m_WorldMatrixT1`. Each render update, on the parallel update's worker threads, `CVehicle::UpdateRender_Parallel` interpolates them by the frame fraction into [`m_InterpolatedWorldMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_InterpolatedWorldMatrix), runs the procedurally animated parts and the vehicle's animation, and hands the transform to [`Vehicle::UpdateRender_ModelMatrices`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-UpdateRender_ModelMatrices). That walks the valid parts (`NPfxBreakable::ForEachValidRenderablePart`) and, for each that has a model, whose damage owner is this vehicle, and that has not broken off, sets the model matrix to the root-space transform times the vehicle's transform, the only input the drawn part takes from the part. A zero frame fraction, or a vehicle flagged to snap, initialises the model matrix instead of updating it. One part behaviour gets an extra scale from the vehicle's state before the multiply (its purpose is not traced). Afterwards the function copies the root-space transforms of parts that attached objects hang from into those attachments.

## Steering wheels

Almost every car and boat has a steering wheel part, `<model>_steeringwheel` or `<model>_steering_wheel`, except the modern bus and the old truck, whose wheels are part of another mesh, the tanks, which have none, and the spy speedboat, the medium old fishing boat, the heavy patrol boat, and the corvette.

Every steering wheel part has an identity offset under the body (the grand tourer's and the transport boat's under another part), so its box is in the vehicle's space: for a disc, the centre is the hub, half the width the rim's radius, and the depth against the height the face's tilt. Boxes of meshes that include the column or are not discs mislead: the circuit racer's and the ladbil's run far forward, the old bus's runs down to the floor, the old jeep's depth reads as a steep tilt, and the transport boat's ship's wheel spans its handles.

The game never turns a car's or a boat's steering wheel; the hands' grip is the driving animation's, one per animation set, with no hand IK.

## Hinged parts

For each part in `hinged_parts`, `CVehicle::OnModuleChanged_HingedParts` looks up the vehicle-common hinged-part properties by the part's behaviour, builds a `CHingedPart` driven by the property's target variable, and takes the hinge's frame and axis from the part's prototype constraint. Each post-simulation update, `CHingedPart::Update_PostSim` turns the target value, clamped to the hinge's range, into an angle and sets the part's root-space transform to the hinge's local turn under its parent's. `Update_PostSim` is read; the setup, including that a part without a constraint fails it, is unverified.

The vehicle-common properties include a `SteeringWheel` hinge driven by `steering_turn`, a `SteeringFork` hinge driven by `steering_angle_inverted`, `BoatEngine` driven by `steering_turn`, and the ailerons, rudders, flaps, and landing gear. The handlebars of the motorcycles, the ATV, the jetski, and the duck are `SteeringFork` hinged parts named `<model>_upper_steering`, pivoting on the steering head (the snowmobile's has no behaviour and is not hinged). A few cars give their steering wheel the `SteeringWheel` behaviour, but none lists it in `hinged_parts`, so it drives nothing. `steering_turn` is [`Vehicle::m_SteeringTurn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_SteeringTurn), the signed steering amount the controller last applied (which sign is right is not checked).

## Landing gear

The landing gear and its hatches move toward [`Vehicle::m_DesiredLandingGearState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_DesiredLandingGearState) in [`Vehicle::UpdateLandingGearVariables`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-UpdateLandingGearVariables): the hatches open before the gear comes down and close after it is up, and the gear's attached effects (its sounds) play as they open, close, and lock. Lowered gear is limited by the height above the ground, so it does not stand through a runway.

There is no player input for the gear: while the local player drives, the update chooses the desired state every call: down while the vehicle is low and slower than a deploy speed, unless airborne and tilted past its landing tilt, and up otherwise while airborne. The deploy speed moves between the take-off and landing speeds with the throttle ([`Vehicle::m_SteeringAcceleration`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_SteeringAcceleration)): braking raises it toward the landing speed at once, and thrust lowers it toward the take-off speed over a smoothing time. With another seat's occupant, or none, the desired state stays as last set, for example by the AI steering's deploy and retract.

## The vehicle type hash

[`Vehicle::m_VehicleTypeNameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleTypeNameHash) keys the vehicle's profile item and localised name. Each variant's entity property file carries a `name` (such as `v0405_car_mugello_moderncircuitracer_civilian_01`, or `..._generated` for the debug variants) and its `name_hash`; that the type hash is this `name_hash`, one per variant rather than per model, is unverified.
