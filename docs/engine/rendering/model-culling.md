# Model culling

How Just Cause 3 decides whether a model instance's geometry reaches a render pass. A model passes two independent gates, at different stages.

## 1. Instance-level BFBC cull (build phase)

[`ModelCollection::DoCulling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelCollection##func-DoCulling) (mislabelled `CAOVolumeManager::AddToRenderThread` in the IDB) runs the broad-phase (BFBC) scene cull over model instances against the occluder manager's `m_CullingCamera` (at [`OccluderCollectionManager::CULLING_CAMERA_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager::CULLING_CAMERA_OFFSET) in [`OccluderCollectionManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager)) and passes each survivor to `ShowUpdate` → `ModelInstance::AddToRender`.

## 2. Per-render-block frustum cull (draw-list build)

[`ModelInstance::AddToRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-AddToRender) (mislabelled `CModelInstance::AddToPass` in the IDB) builds the per-pass draw lists for an instance's render blocks. Its [`AddToRenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::AddToRenderContext)'s `m_Camera` is the camera manager's active camera ([`CameraManager::m_ActiveCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_manager::CameraManager##field-m_ActiveCamera) of the [`CameraManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_manager::CameraManager) singleton), then dispatches to `NModelSystem::ForEachRb<UseBBoxRBView, CoarsePreZ>` selected by two globals:

- [`use_bounding_box_model_rb_view`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::use_bounding_box_model_rb_view) (`g_UseBoundingBoxModelRBView`)
- [`enable_coarse_pre_z_models`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::enable_coarse_pre_z_models) (`g_EnableCoarsePreZModels`)

Both are statically enabled, so the game always runs the `<1,1>` instantiation ([`ModelInstance::ForEachRb_1_1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-ForEachRb_1_1); the `<0,1>` [`ForEachRb_0_1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-ForEachRb_0_1) and `<1,0>` [`ForEachRb_1_0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-ForEachRb_1_0) variants also test). Each calls `Camera::IsBoxVisible(context.m_Camera, blockWorldAABB)` per render block and, on failure, skips that block for every pass. Only the `<0,0>` variant ([`ForEachRb_0_0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-ForEachRb_0_0)) has no test, and it loses coarse pre-Z. The coarse pre-Z path's own thresholds, [`coarse_pre_z_models_distance_threshold`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::coarse_pre_z_models_distance_threshold) (against the projected `w`) and [`coarse_pre_z_models_area_threshold`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::coarse_pre_z_models_area_threshold) (a projected area), are not bounding-box thresholds.

[`Camera::IsBoxVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##func-IsBoxVisible) tests a world-space AABB against the camera's precomputed frustum planes ([`Camera::m_FrustumPlane`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_FrustumPlane) plus AAB acceleration data), rebuilt each frame by [`Camera::UpdateFrustum`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##func-UpdateFrustum) from the centre [`Camera::m_ViewProjection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_ViewProjection).

So a model's render blocks are re-culled against the active camera's frustum after the BFBC pass, a frustum distinct from the occluder-cull and terrain cameras; a view wider than it loses a large multi-block building's blocks at its edge even though the BFBC pass admitted the instance. Sprites (`CRenderBlockParticle`) and gathered lights take other paths, not `ForEachRb`.

## The same active-camera frustum elsewhere

`Camera::IsBoxVisible` against `m_ActiveCamera` also gates:

- `CModelInstanceManager::StartFade`: an instance not in view hides at once instead of fading.
- [`RoadMeshManager::UpdateAddToRenderer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::RoadMeshManager##func-UpdateAddToRenderer).
- [`LightManager::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-UpdateRender) / [`AddFarLightsToPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-AddFarLightsToPass) far-light cells.

## Related: spawn visibility

[`Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##func-Update) (on the [`SpawnSystem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem) singleton, whose BFBC state is [`m_SpawnBFBC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_SpawnBFBC), [`m_SpawnBFBCParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_SpawnBFBCParams), [`m_SpawnBFBCState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_SpawnBFBCState), [`m_CenterPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_CenterPos), [`m_ViewPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_ViewPos), and [`m_ViewDir`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##field-m_ViewDir)) calls [`GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager##func-GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime) at its tail with the camera manager's `m_ActiveCamera`, not the occluder manager's `m_CullingCamera`, and [`CheckInternal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnFactoryImpl##func-CheckInternal) uses the result as a "don't (de)spawn while visible" gate. Its budgets are characters and vehicles, so it governs their (de)spawns near the view edge, not buildings', and a view wider than the active camera's can see (de)spawns at its edge.

## Related: character occlusion

[`StartCommitAddRemove`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world_object_manager::GameWorldObjectManager##func-StartCommitAddRemove) (on the [`GameWorldObjectManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world_object_manager::GameWorldObjectManager) singleton) copies the camera manager's `m_ActiveCamera` into [`m_OcclusionCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world_object_manager::GameWorldObjectManager##field-m_OcclusionCamera) each frame and dispatches `SCharacterOcclusionHandler::ProcessCharacterOcclusion` ([`ProcessCharacterOcclusion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world_object_manager::GameWorldObjectManager##func-ProcessCharacterOcclusion)) as a CPU fragment, which runs `BFBCProcess` with `E_BFBC_PROCESSFUNCTION_FRUSTUM_CULL` over the active camera's frustum and hides the characters it rejects. A view offset from or wider than that camera can show characters the pass culls.

## IDB symbol caveats

Besides the two above, `DumpModelInstanceHistogram` in the IDB is a distance-streaming task, bound as [`ModelInstanceManager::TextureStreamingTask`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-TextureStreamingTask); the defs carry the correct names. These findings come from a decompilation sweep and are not runtime-verified; zeroing `use_bounding_box_model_rb_view` and `enable_coarse_pre_z_models` live would confirm the per-block cull.
