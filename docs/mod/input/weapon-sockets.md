# Weapon sockets

Every weapon on the body sits on a socket of the loadout table's (`grab::socket::Socket`): each hip and each shoulder, the shoulders being where the game docks its long guns across the back ([two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md)). What a grab at a socket does is the table's decision (`loadout::grab`): an empty hand draws the weapon on the socket, and a hand holding a weapon docks it on a free socket that fits it. Nothing selects a slot or asks the game to holster. This page covers what the hands see and feel at the sockets; the rest of the hand-facing weapon handling is in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md). The numbers are the hold tuning (`crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/hold.toml`, [tuning.md](../tuning.md)).

## The volumes

The sockets' volumes are seated from the table each input tick and reached by the hand as it is drawn (`weapons::docks`), so what the player sees on the body is what they grab, wherever the animation, the procedural torso, or the body rig's step drive puts the bone a socket places its weapon on:

- a socket holding a weapon has a volume on the weapon's grip ([`WeaponBase::GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition), or the gun's origin without one), for each hand that may draw it;
- a free socket that takes the weapon a hand holds has a volume where that weapon would sit there, for that hand: where the weapon's grip was on the socket's bone when it was last seen there, or else at the bone, moved by the socket's offset when the game does not dock the weapon's slot there itself;
- a socket that does not take the weapon a hand holds (taken, or too small for it) is marked for that hand with a small red disc while the hand is near, never taken, so the hand knows before it opens. A weapon without a slot docks nowhere (`hold::act::dockable`), so every socket refuses it.

The grip's place on its bone is taken from the gun's last placement and the bone's pose now, so a gun noted while the body moves fast sits slightly off; an offset longer than `docks.max_grip_offset_m` is taken for a bad read and not noted.

Each volume's reach is its kind's: `volumes.sockets.hip_radius_m` for the hips, wide enough for a hand that closes on the front of the hip rather than the pistol's grip behind it, and `volumes.sockets.shoulder_radius_m` for the shoulders. The shoulders are reached for docking at `volumes.back_reach_offset_m` from where the weapon would sit, lower and further out from the spine in the chest's frame (`volumes::reach_back_sockets`): a long gun on the back sits with its grip up behind the head, and a hand putting one away over the shoulder closes below and outside that. A draw is still reached on the weapon the player sees. The volumes on the parts of a long gun docked on the body ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)) are reached by the drawn hand too.

Seated (the seated posture, or a locked vehicle seat), the hips are not on the drawn thighs, which the seat's animation places away from the player's own. They hang at a waist below the player's neck pivot by the standing spine length, with the bind pose's holster offsets turned by the torso (`docks::seated_waist`), and `docks::place_waist` moves the holstered weapons there. The neck is the one the player had once their posture read seated (a locked seat with the player still standing uses the live neck), latched until they stand or the cockpit frame changes (a recenter, a follow bank, or the seat lock's yaw re-base, through `flight_pose::epoch`), so leaning to look at a thigh or reaching toward the knees does not carry the hips with the head, which would carry a docked gun out through a cabin wall.

## Drawing and docking

A draw and a dock take no act: the gun is the same object throughout, and slides between the socket and the hand over `hold.snap_s`, eased in and out (`hold::act`, with the pure parts in `hold::dock`). Since no draw or holster clip plays, the draw plays the gun's draw sound as it leaves the socket and the dock its holster sound as it lands there ([reload.md, "Sounds"](reload.md#sounds)).

A draw checks that the animation allows one ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing)), measures how the gun sits on the socket's bone (its placement relative to the bone's current pose), and tells the table, whose reconcile wields the weapon when the hand is now the primary ([`WeaponBase::OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip)) or arms it in the hand beside the other hand's weapon ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The per-hand tick"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-per-hand-tick)). Its gun rides the hand's attach bone at once; for the snap's duration the rigid grip places that bone part of the way from where the gun sat (the bone's current pose composed with the measured offset, so the start follows the body) toward the hand's grip (`hands::grip::slide_grip`).

A dock of the wielded weapon checks [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsHolstering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsHolstering). The gun slides from the hand to where it sat on the socket at its last draw off it, held back from firing meanwhile, and when the slide ends the table takes the dock, the wielded weapon is unequipped ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip)), and the reconcile wields the other hand's weapon when that hand is now the primary, or disarms a weapon armed in the docking hand. A weapon not drawn off that socket since the payload loaded has no measured pose there and docks at once without the slide. A dock by a grab and a dock by opening the grip over the socket both pulse the grab cue (`Event::VolumeGrab`) on the docking hand as the dock begins.

Each hand has its own slide, so both hands draw and dock at once. The rigid grip shows a slide only while the hand's controller weight is above zero; a hand the controllers do not drive shows the switch at once. The holstered scale of a long gun on the back, when it is not one, still pops at the switch.

A dock or an attracted weapon's travel begun by opening the grip is called off only by the grip pressed again (the derived press, `hold::act::pressed_grips`), which takes the gun back into the hand. A force-sensing grip (the Index's) lets go of its press while the fingers still rest round the handle for a few frames, and its squeeze value reads those fingers as a hold, so calling a dock off on the squeeze would drop the gun whenever a hand opens slowly.

## Attraction

A socket's reach is tight, so that a grab between two sockets takes the nearer one, but putting a weapon back must not need that precision. The shoulders are behind the head and out of sight, and a seated player's chair stands between the hand and the shoulder blade, so a hand opened over the shoulder lands well outside the shoulder's reach. Every free socket that takes the weapon a hand holds therefore also has an attraction around its volume's centre, which only a release consults, after the sockets' own reach has found nothing (`weapons::docks::attract`). The radii are `docks.attraction.shoulder_m` (about an arm's length, so a hand opened over the shoulder, beside the head, or behind it docks the long gun) and `docks.attraction.hip_m` (anywhere beside the thigh, short of the side of the chest).

Two limits keep a deliberate drop a drop:

- A shoulder attracts only a hand no more than `docks.attraction.back_front_m` in front of the line through Rico's shoulder joints and no more than `docks.attraction.back_floor_m` below it, so a weapon tossed down by the thigh is never drawn onto the back. A hip attracts only a hand no more than `docks.attraction.hip_front_m` in front of the line through his hip joints, so a weapon opened out over the chest or held out over the belly falls. The hip's limit is deep enough for a pistol turned down onto the front of the thigh, since relative to the hands the player's own hips sit a few centimetres ahead of Rico's.
- A hand moving fast in the last `docks.attraction.speed_window_s` before the release is throwing the weapon, which falls with the throw: faster than `docks.attraction.hip_max_speed_mps` (player-scale) for a hip, and `docks.attraction.back_max_speed_mps` for a shoulder, where a gun swung back over the shoulder leaves the hand quickly and the front limit, not the speed, keeps a throw out.

A long gun the other hand braces is not attracted: it stays in the bracing hand. Of the sockets that attract a release, the one the hand is deepest inside, as a share of its radius, takes it. A socket the other hand's weapon is already sliding or travelling to is left out of both the reach and the attraction, so two guns let go of together over one free shoulder go to two sockets.

The attracted weapon travels onto the socket along the dock's own slide, not in a jump: over the distance at `docks.attraction.travel_mps` on average, no quicker than `hold.snap_s` and no longer than `docks.attraction.max_travel_s`, eased in and out, held back from firing, and docked by the table when it arrives (`hold::act::attract`). A weapon drawn off that socket before travels to where it sat at that draw; one never drawn off it travels to where its grip goes on the socket, at the orientation it left the hand with, and takes the socket's own placement on arrival.

### The swap

A taken socket swaps (`attract::swap_attractors`, `loadout::grab::swaps`): a shoulder or a hip that holds another weapon, and would take the held one if it were free, attracts a release as a free socket of its kind does, with the same radius and limits, and is marked for the hand as a socket that takes the weapon rather than with the red disc. A release it draws in first lets the weapon docked there fall into the world (`hold::swap::eject`): it leaves the socket moving out from the body and a little up (`hold.swap.eject_mps`, `hold.swap.eject_lift`), so it falls clear of the player, and the table moves it from the socket to the world as the game's own drop does. The released weapon then docks on the freed socket as any attracted weapon does, and the docking hand feels a knock (`Event::SocketSwap`) instead of the dock's click. A free socket in reach takes the weapon before a taken one, however deep the hand is in the taken one's attraction. A swap that could not be done is logged with its reason, and the weapon falls as it would have.

### Feeling the attraction

The hand feels where the attraction is before it opens (`attract::ZoneWatch`, `Event::DockAttraction`): a hand holding a weapon that comes into a free socket's attraction, or a taken socket's that swaps, within its front limit, gets one tick, which says that letting go there docks it. That is how the shoulders, which cannot be seen, are found. The tick does not repeat while the hand stays, and comes again only once the hand has gone past the attraction's radius or its front limit by `docks.attraction.exit_margin_m` and come back, so a hand resting on the edge does not buzz; moving straight from one socket's attraction into another's ticks for the new one. The zone the hand is in is the socket a release there would take: a free socket before a taken one. A hand whose gun the other hand braces gets no tick. The tick does not read the hand's speed, so a hand swinging through the attraction ticks even though a throw from there would not dock.
