# Vehicle doors

How a vehicle's doors, hood, trunk, and hatches open, stay open, latch, and break off, and how a character enters through one. A door is a part of the vehicle's breakable body ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)), merged into the body while closed and hanging on a hinge as a body of its own while open; the other parts loosen and break off through the same calls.

## The door controller

The vehicle's door controller ([`Vehicle::m_VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleDoorController), a [`VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController)) maps each door slot ([`DoorSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorSlot): the four side doors, the centre doors, a turret door, the trunk, the hood, a rear door, a cargo bay door, a folding wing, and a missile hatch) to a vector of door entries ([`DoorInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)), since a slot's parts can open together. The `std::map` is a red-black tree: the controller holds the head node ([`m_DoorMapHead`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController##field-m_DoorMapHead)), whose parent link is the root and to which every leaf links back, and each [`DoorMapNode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorMapNode) carries its slot, the vector's bounds, and a flag set on the head only.

An entry names its part instance ([`m_PartInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PartInstance)), its [`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_State) and requested [`m_PendingState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PendingState) (each a [`DoorState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState); a pending [`DOOR_CLOSED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_CLOSED) means none, and a broken-off door is [`DOOR_MISSING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_MISSING)), the hinge's range from its powered controller, a smallest angle for a door held open, the latch's last measured angle, and flags.

## Opening and closing

[`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) puts the vehicle's body in the physics world and the vehicle in the update if needed, and sets each of the slot's entries pending [`DOOR_OPENING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPENING) with its flags cleared, or, to keep it open, sets [`DoorInfo::FLAG_KEEP_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo) and a smallest angle a little below the hinge's largest. [`Vehicle::CloseDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-CloseDoor) requests a close likewise. The callers are the entry and exit animations' track messages, the AI, scripts, the turret, the cargo bay, and the EMP state.

The vehicle's post-simulation update (`CVehicle::UpdatePostSim_Doors`, through `CVehicleDoorController::UpdateDoors`), on the game thread after the physics step, acts on requests. An opening loosens a part not broken off ([`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart) with [`EPER_PartOperated`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartEventReason##variant-EPER_PartOperated), keeping the constraint). Either request then goes to `CVehicleDoorController::StartDoorAnimation`, which converts the hinge to a powered one on first use (`CPfxBreakable::ConvertHingeToPowered`, with the door's default motor values), runs its motor at the speed that covers the hinge's range in the door's open or close duration, and clears the request.

Opening (`UpdateOpeningDoorInternal`) advances the motor's target angle each update until the door is within a small angle of the hinge's largest or has touched another body for a while; then the motor is disabled unless the door is kept open, the hinge's smallest angle is set where the door is, and the vehicle is told. Closing (`UpdateClosingDoorInternal`) turns the door's collision off and brings the target down, and at the hinge's smallest angle merges the part back (`CPartInstance::MergePart`) and closes the door.

## The latch

An open door with its motor off swings freely under collisions and the vehicle's motion, with no friction or spring, whether a finished opening or a direct `EnablePart` loosened it. Each update, for a free door hanging by its constraint ([`PartInstance::IsConstraintActive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-IsConstraintActive)) with a door object, the controller compares its hinge angle above the smallest ([`PfxBreakable::GetHingeAngleAboveMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetHingeAngleAboveMin): the absolute angle less the smaller absolute limit) with the last update's ([`m_PreviousAngle_Deg`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PreviousAngle_Deg)). A door within [`LATCH_ANGLE_DEG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_ANGLE_DEG) of shut and closing faster than [`LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S) (statics of the update) is merged ([`PartInstance::MergePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-MergePart)) and reported closed, and [`Vehicle::OnFinishedClosingDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OnFinishedClosingDoor) queues the slot's door-closed effect; one that drifts shut stays loose. The speed is low enough for a door rebounding off its open stop, or a lid falling under its own weight. The latch reads the part's constraint and door object, whatever the entry's state.

A kept-open door skips the latch. While open and touching nothing, it is reopened by its motor once the hinge's largest angle exceeds its angle above the smallest plus its kept angle ([`m_KeepOpenMinAngle_Rad`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_KeepOpenMinAngle_Rad)) by more than [`KEEP_OPEN_REOPEN_RAD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-KEEP_OPEN_REOPEN_RAD), so a kept angle at the hinge's largest leaves it swinging freely, never latching. An opening request clears the flags, so the next entry or exit animation returns the door to the latch.

## The loose door

`EnablePart` with the constraint kept runs at once on the calling thread. The part's owner spawns its physical part (for a vehicle, `CVehicle::SpawnPhysicalPart`): a `CPfxVehiclePart` body (a [`PfxRigidBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxRigidBody)) from the part's world-collision body, a separate raycast body, and the rigid object that owns and draws it, a [`VehiclePartGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject) ([`GetVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject##func-GetVehicle) finds its vehicle). Before `EnablePart` returns, `SpawnPhysicalPart` keeps the object in the vehicle's part collection, stores its weak reference in the matching `DoorInfo`, and sets [`DOOR_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPEN), as `CVehicleDoorController::SetDoorObject` does; the bodies exist only if creation succeeded. The prototype's constraint is then cloned to hang the part from its parent, the loosened event fires, and the part disables the merged world-collision and raycast shapes for itself and its children. A part already hanging is left alone, and one loosened without its constraint breaks off. A loose part's body is [`PartInstance::GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny), null while merged.

The loosened event's handler only processes damage effects and operated turret hinges. The next post-simulation update runs a motor only for a pending request, so otherwise an open hinged door takes the latch path through the published weak reference, or the keep-open path under its flag.

A closed door has no body, so a ray that hits it reports the vehicle. Its place is the model's box in the part's space, carried by the part's root-space transform and the vehicle's ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-live-parts)). A door part's parent space offset is the identity, so its origin is the vehicle's and says nothing about its hinge, which is the part's constraint, whose point the deform points carry as the door's `..._CON` entry ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-parts-resource)). [`PfxBreakable::GetConstraintInfoFromPrototype`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetConstraintInfoFromPrototype) reads an authored limited hinge's frame, axis, and signed angular limits by part-name hash, refusing absent parts, absent constraints, and other constraint kinds.

## Breaking off a door

[`PartInstance::DamagePart_ToBreakOff`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-DamagePart_ToBreakOff) applies enough neutral-flag damage to pass below zero health, leaving negative health alone. Ordinary damage admission checks the part's invulnerability, player-only vulnerability, and damage-flag exclusions; forced damage bypasses it and the healthy-body restriction ([`Part::m_prevent_breaking_while_alive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_prevent_breaking_while_alive)) in [`PartInstance::IsAllowedToBreakOffNow`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-IsAllowedToBreakOffNow), but [`Part::m_should_break`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_should_break) and the non-root requirement still apply. The final instant break refuses [`PartInstance::m_Invulnerable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##field-m_Invulnerable) itself and queues its non-thread-safe operation while [`PfxBreakable::m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##field-m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter) is positive. Vehicle damage admission blocks part damage under [`Vehicle::m_Invulnerable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_Invulnerable) or [`m_InJesusMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_InJesusMode), which a direct part call skips.

A part that disappears when broken ([`m_disappears_when_broken`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_disappears_when_broken)), or lacks an authored world-collision shape ([`m_has_world_collision_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_has_world_collision_shape)) outside the native special behaviour, is removed; otherwise an active hinge breaks and the loosened body stays. The broken-off notification marks the part broken for good and notifies listeners, the vehicle marks matching door entries missing and drops its part object, and the native material and collision-group changes still happen. `EnablePart` without the constraint skips the damage admission and authored breakability, so it is not damage.

## Entering through a door

A seated character is attached through the interaction graph ([`Character::IsAttachedToVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsAttachedToVehicle), [`GetVehiclePtr`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetVehiclePtr)): each [`VehicleSeat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleSeat) is a node, and its occupant the node's. The player enters with [`ENTER_VEHICLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-ENTER_VEHICLE) on foot, and the entry animation opens and closes the seat's door through the controller. The animation set has hijack acts and states (`ACT_GET_HIJACK`, `ACT_EXIT_VEHICLE_HIJACK`, `S_HIJACK_STRUGGLE_FAIL`), and the graph has conditions on a door's existence and closure and on a vehicle locked against hijacking. Which entry the action picks, and that it hijacks an occupied seat, is the gamepad behaviour, unverified in the code.

### The interaction graph in the entity

A vehicle's [`InteractionGraph`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph) comes from its entity property file: a `CInteractionContext` object holds a `CInteractionGraph` object whose children, each with its `_object_id`, are the nodes and entries, read by `CInteractionGraph::SetEntryProperties`, `SetNodeProperties`, `SetPointNodeProperties`, and `SetDeformablePointNodeProperties`. What matters:

- The graph's `modifier`, an animation modifier such as `MOD_LOW` or `MOD_HIGH` for its users; an entry or node can override it, as the rear seats' NPC entries do.
- An entry's `state` (`S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE`, `S_ENTER_PASSENGER_VEHICLE`, and the no-door, hijack, unlock, and stunt variants), act, `user_type` (the player, NPCs, or anyone), and `alignment_bone`, the bone the warp aligns (`Reference` on every vehicle entry).
- An entry's `entry_node`, the object id of its node, which becomes [`m_EntryNodeIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphEntry##field-m_EntryNodeIndex).
- An entry's identifier (`m_Identifier`, property name unknown): the door slot of its seat (the front left for the driver, the front right for the front passenger).
- A point node standing in for a deformable point node (`m_TempPretendImADeformablePoint`) and its deform point's name. Every seat node is one, placed not by its `world` matrix but by the vehicle's deform point of that name (`driver`, `passenger1`, and so on, in `<model>_vehicle_deform.vmodc`), deformation and all (`CVehicle::GetDeformableTransform_WorldTransform`).

### Entry clips and the open message

The entry's state and modifier pick an animation node: the player's `S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE` plays `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE_MOD_LOW` with `MOD_LOW` and `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE` without. The node names its clip, segments, and track messages, one of which opens the door as the hand reaches the handle: `open_door` and `quick_open_door` open the current entry's slot (its identifier), `open_door_with_identifier` the slot its argument names, so a clip can open another seat's door. The message handler calls `Vehicle::OpenDoor` (or `CVehicle::QuickOpenDoor`). The standard car entries open the door partway in; the NPC entry clips open it at their first frame, before a hand reaches it.

### The entry warp

An entry clip plays warped (`NInteractionGraphEntryTasks::NWarpedMotion`) to end in the seat whatever the vehicle. On entering the state, `OnEnterSetupMotionWarping` anchors the warp on the entry's alignment bone and aims it at the entry's node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex)), the goal, in graph space, set by one of three modes in the state's warp properties: the best point on the node (`CInteractionGraph::GetBestPointOnNodeLS`; a deformable point node's full transform), the closest point, or the node's centre at the character's current orientation. `NMotionWarpUtility` extracts the anchor's motion from the current time to the clip's end (less the track's early finish time, or to a named extraction segment's end) and spreads the correction landing it on the goal over the warp segments, `SEG_WARP_HORIZONTAL`, `SEG_WARP_VERTICAL`, and `SEG_WARP_ORIENTATION`, or one `SEG_WARP` for all three; outside its segment each part of the motion is the clip's own.

On the standard car entries, the horizontal and heading segments end at the open message and the vertical one starts after it, so at the open the character stands along and across the vehicle by the seat, less the clip's remaining motion, at the clip's own height over its starting ground. A clip serves a class of vehicles per modifier, so the hand meets the door where the class's reference vehicle had its handle, not necessarily this vehicle's.

## Unverified

- Which object a ray query reports for a loose door (its part object or the vehicle), and whether it hits the world-collision or raycast body.
- That the part's root-space transform follows a loose door's swing.
- How each class's hatches and folding wings hinge, and whether any opening slides.
- How the message handler picks the slot for `open_door`, `quick_open_door`, and `open_door_with_identifier`.
- Which goal mode each entry state's warp uses; one keeping the character's orientation would not turn it to the seat's heading.
- The entry clips' tracks' early finish times, which would end the extracted motion before the clip.
