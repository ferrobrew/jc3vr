# Parachute locomotion and steering

How the game moves and steers a parachuting character. The steering mixes in the camera input matrix, so the canopy turns toward where the camera points as well as where the stick points. Opening the canopy from a jump or a fall is in [wingsuit.md](wingsuit.md#opening-from-the-air).

## The state task

Parachuting runs on [`NStateTask_MovementParachuteTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NStateTask_MovementParachuteTask##func-Update), which reads the movement effectors, calls the `NParachuteMovement` free functions of the [`input::parachute`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute) module ([`UpdateParachutePhysics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-UpdateParachutePhysics), [`GetParachuteSteeringValues`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-GetParachuteSteeringValues), [`UpdateParachutingTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-UpdateParachutingTransforms), [`ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities), and [`ApplyAnimationVelocityWithSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-ApplyAnimationVelocityWithSteering)), and handles the cancel that closes the canopy. It is a movement core of its own, using neither the fall family's [`UpdateFallSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::locomotion##func-UpdateFallSteering) nor the look steer [`NAirMovement_UpdateStdLookSteering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-NAirMovement_UpdateStdLookSteering). Its tuning block, [`CharacterAirMovementSettings`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::CharacterAirMovementSettings) (the `m_LookSteer*`, `m_XInputYawPitchRollAmount`, and spring fields), is embedded in the wingsuit and freefall settings too.

## The stick's vertical axis

The task passes `UpdateParachutePhysics` a `CVector2f` of the move half-axes: across is [`MOVE_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-MOVE_RIGHT) minus `MOVE_LEFT`, along is `MOVE_BACKWARD` minus [`MOVE_FORWARD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-MOVE_FORWARD), positive with the stick back. Both axes steer through the input mixes below, and the along axis is also the target of the air-control lift and drag amounts, through `m_LiftInputSpring` and `m_DragInputSpring`. `ApplyConventionalParachuteVelocities` takes the lift from the three-entry `m_Lift` by that amount (the middle entry at rest, blending toward the last with the stick back and the first with it forward) and the drag from `m_DragX`, `m_DragY`, and `m_DragZ` alike. Pulling back slows the canopy only if the shipped tuning's last entries are a higher lift and drag; the code path is established, the values are not read.

## The steering helper

`UpdateParachutePhysics` calls `GetParachuteSteeringValues` each frame for `steering_values_out` (the yaw, pitch, and roll mix), `wanted_char_yawpitchroll_out` (the desired orientation), and `look_steer_out`. The steering sums two mixes:

- The stick, `input->x` and `input->y` through `m_XInputYawPitchRollAmount` and `m_YInputYawPitchRollAmount`, present even with no camera.
- The look steer, for the local player only (`character->m_IsPlayer`; an NPC steers by stick alone). The camera input matrix ([`GameCameraManager::GetInputMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetInputMatrix)) is decomposed to Euler angles, and its yaw and pitch deltas to the wanted orientation (`m_SteeringProxy`), shaped by `m_LookSteerDeadZone`, `m_LookSteerMaxYaw`, and `m_LookSteerExponential`, become `look_steer_out`. That joins all three steering axes through `m_XLookYawPitchRollAmount` and `m_YLookYawPitchRollAmount` (the `v63/v61/v62` sum at the helper's tail), drives the yaw and pitch springs in `UpdateParachutePhysics` (`v254.x` to `m_RotateCharYawToVelocity`, `v254.y` to `m_RotateCharPitchToVelocity`), and weights the slingshot and animation-input blend (`1.0 - look_steer_out.x`). Past the deadzone, the canopy follows the camera with or without stick input.

The helper also aligns to the velocity: above `m_VelocityAlignMinSpeed` of horizontal speed, the `SCharacterSpring` `m_RotateCharYawToVelocity` drives the wanted yaw toward the velocity's heading, blended by `m_VelocitySteeringBlendScale`, keeping an unsteered canopy squared to its travel.

## Orientation

`UpdateParachutePhysics` applies the heading through the proxy matrices of the character's [`ParachuteActionParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::ParachuteActionParams): `m_PivoProxy` (the suspension pivot), `m_CharacterProxy`, `m_ParachuteProxy`, and the blended `m_SteeringProxy`. `UpdateParachutingTransforms` copies the steering matrix into `m_SteeringProxy`, converts it to a quaternion, and drives the rig's swing, and the character's orientation follows it; the two velocity functions compute the velocity response.

## The slingshot

While the hook reels in under the canopy ([`GHS_REELING_IN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_REELING_IN)), `UpdateParachutePhysics` reads the line's direction ([`GrapplingHook::GetReelingInDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetReelingInDirection)) and weights everything it adds by how far the line points along the facing, zero for a line beside or behind: an acceleration along the line (`m_SlingshotDirectAccel`), one along the facing (`m_SlingshotForwardAccel`), a raised top speed (`m_SlingshotMaxSpeedAdjust`), and a drag adjustment, each blended toward the retract boost's (`m_RetractSlingshotSettings`) while the retract is held and the hook allows the boost.

A line beside the character adds almost no speed, and the slingshot does not turn toward it: the `m_RotateCharYawToSlingshot` spring sits behind a static switch the game never sets and behind the look-steer yaw or the stick's across axis passing a small threshold (in `UpdateParachutePhysics`'s def). The look steer turns the canopy toward a line, since the player looks where they fire, and the turn brings the pull to bear.

The update breaks the slingshot, retracting the wire and queuing `ACT_GRAPPLE_CANCEL`, within `m_SlingshotBreakDistance`, once the line falls further behind the facing than the break yaw (widening from `m_SlingshotBreakYawStart` to `m_SlingshotBreakYawEnd` over `m_SlingshotBreakYawBlendTime` of reeling), or past `m_SlingshotBreakPitch`. The shipped values are not read.

## The openings out of a reel

A canopy opened during a reel enters `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_LONG` (from `S_GRAPPLE_REEL_IN` within `SEG_USE_CUSTOM_TO_PARA_TRANSITION`) or `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_SHORT` (from the reel's other states, the pre-reels, and its landings), on `ACT_TRIGGER_PARACHUTE` above the reel-to-parachute speed. Both clips roll Rico into the harness. Each state queues its opening act on the canopy object on entry and leaves for `S_JC_PARACHUTE_IDLE` when its custom lift segment ends, its clip finishes, or a fire lands within its interrupt segment; `ACT_FORCE_PARACHUTE_IDLE` takes it there early only within `SEG_CAN_ABORT_TRANSITION`.

Act transitions like these set the state with the machine's own inlined copy of [`StateMachineInstance::SetStateInternal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::StateMachineInstance##func-SetStateInternal), whose only callers are `AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`, `AnimationRuleSystem::DoExternalAction`, a debugger callback, and a thunk. [`AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::AnimationRuleSystem##func-SetStateById) moves a machine to any state of its definition, whatever the current state's transitions allow.

## The handle props

The canopy's rig has a handle each side on its own cord, three bones a side: the handle (`RightHandle`, `LeftHandle`), its rotation bone (`RightHandleRotate`, `LeftHandleRotate`), and the link the cord hangs from (`RightLink`, `LeftLink`). The handles are not attached to the character's bones. [`NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones), which tracks the rig to its character every update, places them for the player: a placed handle's joint takes the world position of the character's second hand-attach bone (`RightHandAttach2`, `LeftHandAttach2`) in the rig's model space, the rotation bone takes that bone's turn, and the link is drawn a little toward the handle while it is further away than in the bind pose, so the handles sit in Rico's hands; an unplaced handle sits on its link. The right handle is unplaced while a weapon is wielded or during `SEG_NO_RIGHT_HANDLE_PLACEMENT`, the left while the grapple fires or its hook is out or during `SEG_NO_LEFT_HANDLE_PLACEMENT`, and a flag in the parachute's properties suspends both unless `SEG_FORCE_HANDLE_PLACEMENT_OVERRIDE` plays.

The [`NParachuteHandleIKTask`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NParachuteHandleIKTask) state task separately pulls the wrists toward the links through the `SECONDARY` HumanIK pass's blackboard keys, weighted by its blend-in and blend-out segments.
