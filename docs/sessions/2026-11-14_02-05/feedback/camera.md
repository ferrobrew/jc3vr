# Camera, view, and comfort: the 14 November 02:05 feedback

Written on 15 November from the take and the code as they were before the fixes listed in the [README](../README.md#the-feedback-item-by-item); file and line references are to that code. Scripts, dumps, and frame sheets it names were working files of the analysis and are not kept.

Times are the Rerun `t`. Data comes from the harvest arrays (`session1114/body/t14.npz`, the controller state's rendered camera, the final-pass pose, and the `body_state` events) reduced by scripts in `analysis/camera/` (`win.py` prints, per frame, the case and rule state, the camera's height over Rico's root, the distance and offset from the camera to Rico's drawn head bone in the camera frame, and the camera's and the headset's pitch). Unnamed rule-state hashes were resolved against `anim_graph/rico_base.graph.txt` with hashlittle (`hl.py`). Frames are in `analysis/camera/` (`strip_445.jpg`, `s565.jpg`, `s576.jpg`, `h_740.35.jpg`, `h_774.24.jpg`). One caveat: at speed (over about 15 m/s) the pose and camera samples are up to a frame apart, so the camera-to-head distance alternates by the frame's travel; the stationary numbers below are not affected.

Two mechanisms explain most of this area:

- `headpose::animation::CameraFollow` (`payload/src/headpose/animation.rs:80-125`, wired in `payload/src/hooks/character/body_ik/animation.rs:54-58`) turns the view with Rico's animated head frame, pitch and roll included, for 1.5 s after any change into or out of `Mode::Vehicle` or `Mode::Airborne`, through a 0.55 s low-pass. It came in with 6ac8a4c5 (9 November). The wingsuit's opening clips pitch Rico's head 60 to 80 degrees down, and this carries it into the view.
- 20b1ab41 (12 November, `Case::view_holds_root`, `payload/src/controllers/body/procedural/authority.rs:179-198`) holds the view at the neutral standing head over the root through ragdolls and get-ups as well as through jumps, falls, and landings. That is the change the reviewer asks to revert.

## Head pitch swings entering the wingsuit (295, 592.69, 757.5, 1176 s)

What happened. At every one, the camera pitches 60 to 80 degrees below the headset within 0.4 to 0.5 s of the suit opening and comes back over 1 to 2 s. At 295.2 to 295.6 the camera goes from level to −84 degrees while the headset goes from 0 to −22; at 592.14 to 592.52 from −25 to −77 (headset −21 to −15); at 756.8 to 758.3 it dips to −84, swings 30 degrees above the headset, then dips to −88; at 1175.67 to 1176.69 from −31 to −89 while the headset is at −18 to −10, and the reviewer then pitches their real head up to +20 degrees to find the horizon (1177.2 to 1178.4), with the camera still 25 degrees under it 2.7 s after the opening. The root's own pitch is 0 to +20 degrees throughout, so the pitch is not the root's.

Root cause. `CameraFollow` arms on the mode change into `Airborne(Wingsuit)` and the view slerps toward the animated head frame (`S_JC_WINGSUIT_OPEN_FROM_FALL` and the wingsuit idle hold the head pitched down into the dive). `xr::body_rotation_followed` composes the view on `Geometry::view_frame` = root × `camera_animation_rotation` (`payload/src/headpose/anchors.rs:66-77`), and in the wingsuit `camera_weight` is 0, so nothing levels it.

Fix. Drop `Airborne` from `camera_follow_state` (`headpose/animation.rs:81-86`), or at least the wingsuit and the parachute, so the view never takes the clip's head frame in flight; the wingsuit view then composes on the root alone, which keeps the bank roll the head-and-body doc wants (the bank rotates the root) and never the clip's pitch. If any pitch-follow is kept, it should be the twist about the root's up only (`synthetic_rotation` already separates heading). The test `the_view_follows_only_around_vehicle_and_airborne_transitions` changes with it. This also removes most of README new problem 3 (the 77 degree straightening at 593.1 when a crash hands the view from the pitched wingsuit view to the root-held upright one: the pitch it straightens is this follow's).

The speed loss at 295 is not the wingsuit's impulse. The reel ended on the tick the deploy gesture fired (gesture 294.759, speed 36.3 to 15.4 m/s between 294.756 and 294.782, every component scaled by 0.43, `the reel ends` logged at 294.826), and the suit opened later at 295.12 from 11 m/s and accelerated to 18 m/s. Every airborne reel ending in the take cuts the speed to 0.4 to 0.5 of itself (33.6, 173.1, 308.2, 311.9, 467.3, 789.2, 836.3, 1086.2, 1991.5, 2244.9 s, among others), except those that open the canopy (0.96 to 0.99). So the "impulse" is the reel's exit, which the grapple area owns (it is the same family as the swing-exit speed drop). For the reviewer's lerp: when the suit is requested during a reel or within a few ticks of its end, capture the velocity before the exit and, once `SB_IS_WINGSUIT` is set, hand it to the suit and let it decay to the suit's own speed over about 1.5 s. Where the exit scale comes from is not established (the engine doc says a cancel keeps the velocity, so the scale is in the arrival or finish path, unverified), and the wingsuit's velocity state is not bound in pyxis (`physics.pyxis` has only the `WINGSUITING` proxy state), so both need RE before the lerp can be written.

## Camera-body-head detachment on get-ups and landings

What happened. At every time listed, the view sits at standing height over the root while Rico's drawn head is 0.3 to 1.1 m away, below and in front:

| t | case / rule state | camera over root | camera to head (right, up, forward) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 297.0 | ragdoll, `S_FULL_RAGDOLL` | 1.74 m | 0.65 m (0, −0.65, 0) |
| 298.7 to 299.4 | `S_GETUP_POSEMATCHING` | 1.55 to 1.70 m | 0.58 to 0.79 m, below |
| 301.0 | getting up, `S_GETUP` | 1.49 m | 0.26 m |
| 490.5 | `S_GETUP_POSEMATCHING` | 1.50 m | 0.64 m (0, −0.28, +0.57) |
| 491.5 | `S_GETUP` | 1.51 m | 0.47 m |
| 597.3 | `S_GETUP_POSEMATCHING` | 1.51 m | 0.65 m |
| 755.4 | fall | 1.66 m | 0.39 m |
| 824.9 | movement task not running | 1.60 m | 0.32 m |
| 870.2 | on foot, `S_LAND_FAST_FALL` | 1.56 m | 0.84 m (−0.33, −0.35, +0.69) |
| 1382.8 | fall | 1.64 m | 0.47 m |
| 1806.9 to 1808.2 | `S_LAND_FAST_FALL` | 1.54 m | up to 0.97 m, 0.9 m in front |
| 2993.0 | ragdoll, `S_FLAILING_RAGDOLL` | 1.41 m | 1.14 m |
| 3824.8 | fall | 2.10 m | 1.03 m |

The orientation stays upright (0 to 1 degree from the headset), as 20b1ab41 intended; it is the position that leaves the body.

Root cause. 20b1ab41 added `Flight`, `HitReaction`, `AuthoredMotion`, `NoMovementTask`, `Ragdoll`, and `GettingUp` to `Case::view_holds_root`, and `Eligibility::root_fixed` (`payload/src/controllers/locomotion/view.rs:252-259`) hangs the camera from `stable_anchors`, which put the head straight above the root's XZ at neutral height (`view.rs:274-301`). A ragdoll's root is the physics body's, and a get-up's clip moves the head 0.3 to 0.6 m forward of the root and from the ground up, so the view floats above and behind the body for the 2 to 3 s a get-up lasts.

The roof-jump shift the 10 November feedback complained of (its 122 s and 580 s) was the short version of the same thing: a drop from a grapple hang moving the head 0.9 m in 0.3 s and a landing crouch dipping it 0.33 m. Both are transients well under a second, after which the body is back under the view. The get-ups and ragdolls are long and end with the body somewhere else. So the revert should split on duration and displacement, not restore the old animated head everywhere.

Fix proposal (files: `controllers/body/procedural/authority.rs`, `controllers/locomotion/view.rs`, `controllers/locomotion/anchor.rs`, `headpose/anchors.rs`):

1. Remove `Ragdoll` and `GettingUp` from `view_holds_root`, so they hang the view from the animated head again, but keep the view frame upright: today one weight (`Stabilized::weight`, published as `Geometry::camera_weight`) drives both the anchor position and the frame's turn toward the root's heading. Split it into a position weight and a frame weight, and give these two cases position weight 0 and frame weight 1, so a tilted ragdoll root still never tilts the view (that part of 20b1ab41 is good, and fixed the 100 degree tumble).
2. Follow the animated head through a critically damped follower on the anchor position in those two cases (about 0.25 to 0.35 s, with a speed cap of a few m/s), so the ragdoll's first 1.5 m fall is a quick ease rather than a drop, and the rise of the get-up is followed. This is the same `HeightFilter` idea extended to 3D.
3. Keep `Jump`, `Flight`, `HitReaction`, and the landing one-offs root-held, which is what fixed the roof jumps, but add an escape: if the drawn head stays more than about 0.45 m from the root-held head for longer than about 0.4 s (a landing that turns into a roll or a ragdoll, the 0.9 m `S_LAND_FAST_FALL` excursions at 870 and 1807), hand over to the followed animated head with a longer blend (`BLEND_S` is 0.25 s; 0.5 s here). A 0.3 s landing dip never reaches it; a get-up always does.
4. `AuthoredMotion` and `NoMovementTask` are mixed bags (824.9 is the latter at 0.32 m); the escape in 3 covers them without listing states.

Yaw on the get-up (490 to 492.5). Rico's root faces 25 to 30 degrees away from the view through and after the get-up (camera yaw minus root yaw about 153 to 160 degrees against 180 for aligned), and the 1806 landing turns the root 75 degrees in 0.2 s under the view (camera minus root from −104 to 180). The get-up's pose matching picks its facing from the ragdoll's orientation, so the body stands up facing wherever it fell. Per the "never reorient the player without a movement action" rule the view must not turn to it; the body should turn to the player instead. Proposal: when the get-up ends, steer the body to the torso estimate with a zero deadband for a moment, the way the boost take-off does with `xr::set_take_off_heading` (`payload/src/headpose/xr.rs:304-310`, consumed in `advance_body_yaw`, `xr.rs:219-231`). The reviewer's 1806 guess is right: during the fast fall Rico's root faced about 100 degrees from the view (the follow only runs on foot), and the landing then swings the body through the view; the same zero-deadband steer at touchdown helps there, and so would keeping the body's heading on the view's through falls.

1742.59 (entering the mounted gun) is a vehicle entry rather than a get-up: the drawn head is 0.17 m from the camera at that instant, and the detachment the reviewer saw belongs with the vehicle entry whips below (`Case::Vehicle` keeps the animated head and `CameraFollow` turns the view with the clip).

## The parachute roll at 262 s

What happened. The canopy at 261.5 rolled the view to 164 degrees with the harness filling the frames (261.7 to 263.8), as at 815.8 and 1814.7.

Root cause, revised from the harvest. The harvest (body.md, world.md) concluded the reel's end into `jump` made the game play an opening the hook does not watch. The `body_state` events say otherwise: the rule state from 261.458 to 262.437 is hash 2316017636, which is `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_LONG`, entered while the case was still the grapple traversal, and the same state plays at 814.77 to 815.71 and 1813.83 to 1814.81. The two level openings (1459, 2613) were `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_SHORT` (2105903187), which rolls less. So the hook watched the right states and never ran: no `canopy: skipped` line and no warning anywhere in the log.

Why: in the release IDB, `StateMachineInstance::SetStateInternal` (the address the detour binds in `animation/rule_system.pyxis`) has only four code callers: `AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`, `AnimationRuleSystem::DoExternalAction`, a function the IDB labels `CAFSMDebugger::OnSMEvalBegin`, and a nine-byte thunk. The dump's `CStateMachineInstance::HandleEvent`, `HandleExternalEvent`, `SetInitialState`, and `ResourceChanged` all call it, so in the release build it is inlined into the event path that ordinary act transitions (here `ACT_TRIGGER_PARACHUTE`) take, and the detour never sees them. The doc's claim that every state change goes through it (`docs/mod/body/canopy-opening.md`, `docs/engine/gameplay/parachute-locomotion.md`) is wrong for the release build and should be corrected. (The `OnSMEvalBegin` label is suspect: a debugger callback that sets state would be odd; worth checking which function that really is.)

Fix, at the animation level as asked. Do not depend on the transition path: in the `AnimationController::UpdateAnimations` detour (`payload/src/hooks/animation.rs:51-75`), before calling the original for the local controller, read the body machine's current state (`active_state_type_id`, same file) and, if it is one of the reel openings, move the machine on with `AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById` to `S_JC_PARACHUTE_IDLE`, exactly as `canopy::skip_reel_opening` does. The opening's `OnEnter` (which queues the canopy object's opening act) has already run by then, since entry happens in the rule system's update before evaluation; that needs confirming in IDA (the order of the rule system's update and `UpdateAnimations` in the character update), and if entry and evaluation share a tick with no gap the hook should instead go on the rule system's event dispatch, `HandleEvent` (bind it in pyxis) after the original. Keep the `SetStateInternal` detour for the `SetStateById` path or drop it. The open question for the reviewer: the openings from the wingsuit (`S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_WINGSUIT`, 1146, 1406, 2806) swing Rico's legs up through the view too; they could go into the same list.

## Clipping into the body and the camera underground

445.836 (underground). `S_JC_BOOST_CRASH_RECOVERY` at 445.68: the wingsuit view still hangs from the animated head, which slides prone along the road, and the camera is 0.24 to 0.31 m below the root (frame 445.60 shows the inside of the ground's edge, 445.84 the asphalt at the lens). Cause: the wingsuit case keeps the animated head with no floor clamp. Fix: clamp the camera's height to the ground under it plus a near-plane margin whenever it hangs from an animated head (the ground height is already in the controller state's `character.height`); the clamp belongs in `controllers/locomotion/anchor.rs` where the anchors are composed.

445.911 (inside the body). At 445.76 the case flips to a fall (root-held), and the anchor lifts from the prone head to standing height, 1.3 m in 0.2 s (camera over root 0.01, 0.35, 0.82, 1.29, 1.62 m at 445.76 to 445.95), sweeping up through Rico's torso: frame 445.91 is dark harness with desktop bleed-through. Cause: the handover blends position linearly between the animated and the root-held anchors over `BLEND_S` (0.25 s, `anchor.rs:260`), and the path between a prone head and a standing one runs through the body. Fix: with the get-up split above, a crash recovery would follow the animated head instead of jumping to standing height; generally, a handover whose endpoints differ by more than half a metre should go through the follower, not a lerp.

446.576 (body visible). The view is root-held 1.6 m over the root and the head is 0.11 m away, so the anchor is right; the teal shape at the bottom of the frame is Rico's own pack or shoulder in the `S_JC_BOOST_CRASH_RECOVERY_ONGROUND` crouch while the reviewer looks 28 degrees down. It is the same detachment as the get-ups (the clip's crouch under a standing view) and the get-up proposal covers it.

565 to 572. The case is `standing on a vehicle` throughout (`S_GRAPPLE_REEL_TO_STUNT_LAND`, `_P2`, `S_STUNT_FWD_STEP_DOWN_FRONT`, `S_RUN_START_0`, `S_FLICK_0` on the car roof), the camera is on the animated head (0.07 to 0.2 m from the head bone) at 0.76 to 1.5 m over the root, and the frames show the arm and the wingsuit fabric across the view at 568.0 and the body's dark fabric at 570.7. `StuntStanding` is not in `Case::game_poses_body` (`authority.rs:140-158`), so the 6 cm face clearance does not apply on a vehicle's roof, and the stunt clips crouch and step down. Fix: add `StuntStanding` to `game_poses_body` while a stunt clip other than the idle plays, or better, treat a vehicle roof like the ground: root-held with the get-up-style escape, since the player stands on their own feet there too. 569.8 is a white flash (the capture bleed-through, not the camera).

576 (death). `Dead` keeps the animated head (by design in 20b1ab41's table), and `S_DYING` rolls the view 70 degrees (camera roll 2.7 to 72 degrees over 575.2 to 578.4) and lowers it until it is 0.23 m below the root, with Rico's arm and pack filling the frames at 576.3 to 577.6. The reviewer likes falling over. Fix: keep following the dying head's position, but level the frame (the frame weight of the split above set to 1, or at least the roll removed), add the floor clamp, and raise the face clearance while dead. The general cure for all of these, if clearance tuning keeps failing, is to hide the player's own body draws within a few centimetres of the eye (the draw hooks already identify the body model's blocks by instance info, `docs/mod/body/head-and-body.md`), or to collapse the head, neck, and upper chest bones while the camera is inside their capsules.

1806. See the get-up section: the fast-fall landing turns the root 75 degrees and pushes the head 0.9 m in front of a root-held view. The reviewer's yaw-misalignment guess is right.

2806 (canopy from the wingsuit). `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_WINGSUIT` lifts Rico's head 0.6 to 0.9 m above the camera at 2806.04 to 2806.23 and keeps it 0.3 to 0.4 m above until the idle at 2807.9; in a normal canopy idle the head is 0.1 to 0.2 m from the camera (265, 1150, 2810). So the camera does not ride the opening's lift and stays in the chest while the arms reach up. The mechanism is not established: the parachute case keeps the animated head, so a 0.3 to 0.9 m lag should not happen; candidates are the eye arm being turned by the 0.1 s filtered `animation_rotation` while the clip swings the head from prone to upright, and the wingsuit-to-parachute mode change re-arming `CameraFollow`. This needs a replay of the anchor composition over 2805.8 to 2808 to settle. Note it does not conflict with the wingsuit item: the reviewer wants the position to follow the canopy's yank and the angle to stay the player's.

## Vehicle entry and exit whips (README new problem 2) and the step turn

What happened. Scooter entry at 182.75 (`S_ENTER_DRIVER_RIGHT_MOTORBIKE`): the camera yaws 56 degrees in 0.4 s (−108 to −164) with the camera within 3 to 9 degrees of the root throughout, pitches 19 degrees down, and rolls 6.6 degrees. Scooter exit at 251.77 (`S_ROLL_OUT_EXIT_LEFT_MOTORBIKE`): 66 degrees in 0.16 s. Helicopter at 3787.98 to 3788.54 (`S_ENTER_DRIVER_NO_DOOR_VEHICLE`, case `jump`, mode still on foot): 64 degrees in 0.32 s with the view staying on the root.

Root cause. The view rides the root as the entry or exit clip turns it: `advance_body_yaw` (`payload/src/headpose/xr.rs:146-169`) freezes `BodyFollow` whenever `sim`'s mode is not on foot, which makes the view `body × Ry(−frozen)` and carries every root turn into the view, and `CameraFollow` adds the clip's head pitch and roll for 1.5 s. The seat lock's eased re-base (b46cafdb) only acts once seated.

Fix, the binding step turn (view yaw steps toward the vehicle's forward every 0.5 s, up to 30 degrees a step). `BodyFollow` (`payload/src/headpose/follow.rs`) is already the tool: while following, the view's heading is held apart from the body's. Add a stepped mode: from the first tick of an entry or exit (a vehicle-attach state bit or `Case::Vehicle`; the helicopter entry at 3788 reads as `jump` on foot, so the trigger must come from the character's vehicle state, not the hand-over case) keep the follow running with the view's heading held, and every 0.5 s move it toward the body's heading by min(30 degrees, the remaining offset), as a hard step (no lerp). When the offset is under a few degrees and the seat is locked (or the exit has landed on foot), hand back: in a seat the existing `bank` folds the remainder into the cockpit baseline, and on foot the torso follow takes over. Remove `Mode::Vehicle` from `camera_follow_state` at the same time so the clip's head pitch and roll stop reaching the view. A snap step of up to 30 degrees should probably be drawn with the snap-turn fade the turn modes already have, which is the reviewer's call. The view-frame step interacts with the seat lock's 1 s eased re-base: the re-base should run after the steps finish, or be replaced by them.

## Loading screens, grab circles, and the stray cursor dot

Loading screens (582). The panel world-locks during loading: `hud/mod.rs:365` freezes it when `hooks::camera::vr_loading_view_active()` holds, by design for issue #27, so the player can look around it. With a loading screen (no world visible) the reviewer wants the HUD's lazy follow. Fix: drop `vr_loading_view_active()` from `freeze` and keep only `menu_world_lock()`; the render camera's head tracking around `LOADING_BASE` stays as it is. If some loading transitions show the world (the fast travel's fly-out), freeze only while the game's loading movie is actually up; which flag says that needs finding.

Grab circles in a loading screen (740.35). Frame 740.35 shows a large grip-target ring beside the loading panel. At 739.761 the fast travel's spawn reset rebound the loadout and the controls summary switched to `ON FOOT` while the hand-over case read `not in gameplay`: the grab tick (`controllers/grab/tick.rs:156-159`) submits markers whenever the bindings poll on foot, and the overlay's draw path (`controllers/overlay/mod.rs:94-110`) checks only the overlay switch. `producers::tick` gates on `hooks::in_gameplay()` but clears only its own seven sources (`producers.rs:62-66, 387-395`), not `GrabMarkers` or the other externally submitted ones. Fix: gate `overlay::draw` (or the store's read) on `hooks::in_gameplay()` for every source except the panel pointer, which fixes all grab circles and any other world marker during loads at once.

Cursor dot (774.24). Frames 740.35 and 774.24 both show a small white dot apart from the desktop's mouse cursor: on the loading panel at 740.35, and in the sky left of the mouse cursor at 774.24, after the map's fast travel (map use at 730 to 734). It is not the mod's egui panel dot (that is cleared by `reset_egui_pointer` and drawn only on the panel). The likeliest source is the game's own Scaleform mouse cursor, left visible because the map was torn down by the fast travel while `controllers::map` had switched the engine to mouse mode for the map's input (`controllers/map/mod.rs`, `hooks/ui/mouse.rs:256`). Hypothesis only; confirming it needs the UI manager's cursor visibility (not bound in pyxis) and a check that `map::cancel` or an equivalent runs when the map closes without its own exit path.

## Body yaw arrows

These are the debug UI's "Floor arrows" (`controllers.overlay.body_estimate`, `payload/src/controllers/config/overlay.rs:45`). c76e1fd6, which landed during this analysis, already flips the default to off. The session's settings snapshot (`session1114/body/settings.toml`) has `body_estimate = true`; if the reviewer's saved file carries that line it overrides the new default, so it should be removed from their file (or switched off once in the debug UI's Body fit feature).

## Conflicts and related items

- None of the proposals conflict with the binding answers. The wingsuit item (keep the head angle) and the 2806 item (follow the canopy's yank) agree once position and orientation are treated separately, which is the split proposed for the get-ups as well.
- The step turn on vehicle entry interacts with b46cafdb's seat-lock ease; one of the two should own the yaw.
- The reel exit's speed cut belongs to the grapple area (world/grapple), as does the swing-exit drop.
- The binding "airborne IK'd, landing animated" answer for the body is compatible with the root-held view on landings; the get-up follow concerns only the view.
