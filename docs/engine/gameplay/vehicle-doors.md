# Vehicle doors

How a vehicle's doors, hood, trunk, and hatches are kept, opened, held open, and latched shut. A door is a part of the vehicle's breakable body ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)): closed, it is merged into the body; open, it hangs on a hinge as a body of its own. A vehicle's other parts loosen and break off through the same calls.

## The door controller

Each vehicle has a door controller ([`Vehicle::m_VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleDoorController), a [`VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController)), which keeps the vehicle's doors in a `std::map` from the door slot ([`DoorSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorSlot): the four side doors, the centre doors, a turret door, the trunk, the hood, a rear door, a cargo bay door, a folding wing, and a missile hatch) to a vector of door entries ([`DoorInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)). A slot can hold several parts that open together. The map is the standard library's red-black tree: the controller holds its head node ([`m_DoorMapHead`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController##field-m_DoorMapHead)), whose parent link is the root and to which every leaf links back, and each node ([`DoorMapNode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorMapNode)) carries its slot and the vector's bounds, and a flag that is set on the head only.

A door entry names its part instance ([`DoorInfo::m_PartInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PartInstance)), its state and the state it is asked to move to ([`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_State), [`m_PendingState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PendingState), both a [`DoorState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState)), the hinge's range as its powered controller reports it, a smallest angle for a door held open, the last hinge angle the latch measured, and flags. A pending state of [`DOOR_CLOSED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_CLOSED) means that nothing is pending. A door whose part broke off is [`DOOR_MISSING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_MISSING).

## Opening and closing

[`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) asks every door of a slot to open: it puts the vehicle's physics body in the world if it is not, puts the vehicle in the update, and sets each entry's pending state to [`DOOR_OPENING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPENING) and clears its flags, or, asked to keep the door open, sets the keep-open flag ([`DoorInfo::FLAG_KEEP_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)) and a smallest angle a little below the hinge's largest. [`Vehicle::CloseDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-CloseDoor) asks for a close the same way. The callers are the character's entry and exit animations (through their track messages), the AI, scripts, the turret, the cargo bay, and the EMP state.

The controller acts on a request in the vehicle's post-simulation update (`CVehicle::UpdatePostSim_Doors`, through `CVehicleDoorController::UpdateDoors`), on the game thread after the physics step. For an opening, a part that has not broken off is loosened ([`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart) with [`EPER_PartOperated`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartEventReason##variant-EPER_PartOperated) and the constraint kept), and then for either request the door's animation starts (`CVehicleDoorController::StartDoorAnimation`): the hinge is converted to a powered one on its first use (`CPfxBreakable::ConvertHingeToPowered`, with the door's default motor values), its motor is enabled, and it turns at the speed that covers the hinge's range in the door's open or close duration. The pending state is then cleared.

While opening (`UpdateOpeningDoorInternal`), the motor's target angle advances each update. Once the door is within a small angle of the hinge's largest, or has been touching another body for a while, it is open: the motor is disabled unless the door is kept open, the hinge's smallest angle is set where the door is, and the vehicle is told the door finished opening. While closing (`UpdateClosingDoorInternal`), the door's collision is switched off, the target angle comes down, and at the hinge's smallest angle the part is merged back into the body (`CPartInstance::MergePart`) and the door is closed.

## The latch

An open door whose motor is off swings freely on its hinge, pushed by collisions and the vehicle's motion. Each update, for a free door that hangs by its constraint ([`PartInstance::IsConstraintActive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-IsConstraintActive)) and has a door object, the controller reads its hinge angle above the hinge's smallest ([`PfxBreakable::GetHingeAngleAboveMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetHingeAngleAboveMin)), and compares it with the angle it read the last update ([`DoorInfo::m_PreviousAngle_Deg`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PreviousAngle_Deg)). A door within a small angle of shut that swings shut faster than a threshold speed is merged into the body and reported closed: a door swung shut hard latches, and one that drifts shut slowly stays loose. A door kept open that is pushed back from its kept angle, and is touching nothing, is asked to open again instead. Both thresholds are statics of the update.

The door's state entry is only one reader of this: the latch acts on the part's constraint and door object, whatever the entry's state says.

## The loose door

Loosening a merged part ([`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart)) runs on the calling thread at once. With the constraint kept, the part's owner spawns its physical part, for a vehicle `CVehicle::SpawnPhysicalPart`: a vehicle-part body (`CPfxVehiclePart`, a [`PfxRigidBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxRigidBody)) made from the part's world-collision body, a separate raycast body, and the game object that owns and draws it, a [`VehiclePartGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject) (a rigid object). The part's constraint from the physics prototype is then cloned to hang it from its parent, and the part's loosened event fires. A part already hanging by its constraint is left alone; without the constraint kept, the part breaks off instead. A loose part's own body is [`PartInstance::GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny), null while the part is merged. The part object finds its vehicle again through [`VehiclePartGameObject::GetVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject##func-GetVehicle).

A closed door has no body of its own, so a ray that hits it reports the vehicle. Where a closed door is comes from its part: the model's box in the part's own space, carried by the part's root-space transform and the vehicle's transform ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-live-parts)). A door part's parent space offset is the identity, so its own space is the vehicle's and its origin says nothing about its hinge. The hinge is the part's constraint, whose point the vehicle's deform points carry as the door's `..._CON` entry ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-parts-resource)).

## Entering through a door

A character in a vehicle seat is attached to the vehicle through the interaction graph ([`Character::IsAttachedToVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsAttachedToVehicle), [`GetVehiclePtr`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetVehiclePtr)); each seat ([`VehicleSeat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleSeat)) is a node of that graph, and the occupant of a seat is the graph's occupant of its node. The player enters a vehicle with the vehicle-entry action ([`ENTER_VEHICLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-ENTER_VEHICLE)) on foot; the entry's animation opens and closes the seat's door through the door controller ([opening and closing](#opening-and-closing)). The animation set has hijack acts and states (`ACT_GET_HIJACK`, `ACT_EXIT_VEHICLE_HIJACK`, `S_HIJACK_STRUGGLE_FAIL`), and the interaction graph has conditions on a door's existence and closure and on a vehicle being locked against hijacking. Which entry the action picks, and that it hijacks an occupied seat, is unverified from the code; it is the game's behaviour on the gamepad.

### The interaction graph in the entity

A vehicle's interaction graph ([`InteractionGraph`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph)) is built from its entity property file: a `CInteractionContext` object holds a `CInteractionGraph` object, whose children are the graph's nodes and entries, each an object with its own `_object_id`. `CInteractionGraph::SetEntryProperties`, `SetNodeProperties`, `SetPointNodeProperties`, and `SetDeformablePointNodeProperties` read them. The properties that matter for an entry:

- The graph's `modifier`, an animation modifier such as `MOD_LOW` or `MOD_HIGH` that its users take; an entry or a node can carry its own `modifier`, as the rear seats' NPC entries do.
- An entry's `state`, the state it puts its user in (`S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE`, `S_ENTER_PASSENGER_VEHICLE`, the no-door, hijack, unlock, and stunt variants), its act, its `user_type` (the player, NPCs, or anyone), and its `alignment_bone`, the bone the entry's warp aligns (`Reference` on every vehicle entry).
- An entry's `entry_node`, the object id of the node it leads to, which becomes its [`m_EntryNodeIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphEntry##field-m_EntryNodeIndex).
- An entry's identifier (`m_Identifier`, a property whose name string is not known). On a vehicle it is the door slot of the seat the entry leads to: the driver's entry names the front left slot, the front passenger's the front right.
- A point node that stands in for a deformable point node (`m_TempPretendImADeformablePoint`), and the name of its deform point. Every seat node of a vehicle is one: the seat's place is not the node's own `world` matrix but the vehicle's deform point of that name (`driver`, `passenger1`, and so on, in `<model>_vehicle_deform.vmodc`). The node's world transform is the deform point's world transform (`CVehicle::GetDeformableTransform_WorldTransform`), deformation and all.

### Entry clips and the open message

The entry's state and modifier choose an animation node of the character's animation set: the player's `S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE` with `MOD_LOW` plays `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE_MOD_LOW`, and without a modifier `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE`. The node names its clip, the clip's segments, and its track messages. One message opens the door, and its time is the moment the hand is on the handle:

- `open_door` and `quick_open_door` open the slot of the character's current entry, its identifier.
- `open_door_with_identifier` opens the slot its argument names, so a clip can open a door other than its seat's.

The character's message handler calls [`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) (or `CVehicle::QuickOpenDoor`) with the slot. The standard car entries open their door partway into the clip; the NPC entry clips open it at their first frame, before any hand reaches it.

### The entry warp

An entry clip is played with a warp (`NInteractionGraphEntryTasks::NWarpedMotion`) so that it ends in the seat whatever the vehicle. On entering the state, `OnEnterSetupMotionWarping` takes the entry's alignment bone as the warp's anchor and the entry's node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex) of its entry node) as the destination, and sets the warp's goal from the node by one of three modes the state's warp properties choose: the best point on the node (`CInteractionGraph::GetBestPointOnNodeLS`), the closest point, or the node's centre with the character's current orientation. For a deformable point node the best point is the deform point's full transform. The goal is in the graph's space. `NMotionWarpUtility` then extracts the anchor's motion from the clip's current time to its end (less the track's early finish time, or to the end of a named extraction segment) and spreads the correction that lands the anchor on the goal over the clip's warp segments: `SEG_WARP_HORIZONTAL` for the horizontal position, `SEG_WARP_VERTICAL` for the height, `SEG_WARP_ORIENTATION` for the heading, or one `SEG_WARP` for all three. Outside its segment, each part of the motion is the clip's own.

On the standard car entry clips the horizontal and heading segments end at the open message and the vertical segment starts after it. So when the door opens the character is placed along and across the vehicle by the seat, less the clip's motion still to come, and at the clip's own height above the ground it started on. The clips are shared by a class of vehicles, one per modifier, so the hand meets the door where the class's reference vehicle had its handle, not necessarily where this vehicle's handle is modelled. `tools/door_handles` computes that point for every vehicle.

## Unverified

- That the loosened event sets the door's entry to [`DOOR_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPEN) and its door object, as `CVehicleDoorController::SetDoorObject` does; the spawn writes the door object's reference into the entry directly.
- Which object a ray query reports for a loose door: the part object that owns the part's body, or the vehicle; and whether the query hits the part's world-collision body or its raycast body.
- That the part's root-space transform follows a loose door's swing.
- How each vehicle class's hatches and folding wings hinge, and whether any opening is a slider rather than a hinge.
- How the character's message handler picks the slot for `open_door`, `quick_open_door`, and `open_door_with_identifier` was read in the symbol dump and not in the release IDB; the entry warp's setup and the best point on a deformable point node were read in both.
- Which of the three goal modes each entry state's warp uses; a state whose warp keeps the character's orientation would not turn it to the seat's heading.
- The early finish times of the entry clips' tracks, which would end the extracted motion before the clip does.
