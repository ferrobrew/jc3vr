# Grenades, C4, and the detonator

Under the controller scheme the explosives are objects in the hands rather than throw animations. A grenade from the chest pouch is the game's own grenade from the draw to the detonation, inert until its pin is pulled; a C4 charge from its pouch is the game's own charge, thrown or pressed onto a surface by hand; and the detonator is held like a gun. The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/grenade/` and `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/c4/`, with the pouches' presses in `pouch_return.rs` and the fingers' opening in `opening.rs`. The tuning is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/grenade.toml` (`GrenadeTuning`) and `c4.toml` (`C4Tuning`), with the pouches' volumes and returns in `hold.toml` and the opening thresholds in `handling.toml`'s `opening` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). The engine side is in [aim-pipeline.md, "Grenades"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#grenades) and [planted-explosives.md](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md). Under the gamepad scheme none of this runs and the game's own throw, planting, and pickups work as they always do.

## The pouches

The grenade pouch and the C4 pouch sit on the front of the chest, the C4 pouch on the side the grenade pouch leaves (`volumes::grenade_pouch`, `volumes::c4_pouch`). A hand holding anything takes nothing from the chest: with one hand occupied only the other may take, and with both occupied neither pouch is offered. The pouches are offered on foot, falling, under the parachute, and in a vehicle seat (`weapons::capabilities::Capabilities`, its `pouches`). The grenade pouch is offered, and its model drawn, only while the stock has a grenade to give ([Stock](#stock)); the C4 pouch while a charge may be placed or no hand holds the detonator ([The detonator](#the-detonator)).

A hand lets go of a grenade or a charge when its fingers open, not when its squeeze relaxes (`weapons::opening`). A throwing hand relaxes its squeeze well before the fingers leave the handle, and on a force-sensing grip the press ends before the throw's peak, which would drop the grenade at the feet. The fingers are read through the grip's value: on the Index controller the capacitive sense of the fingers round the handle, on a Touch controller the grip's pull. A hand counts as closed once the value passes `opening.closed` or the grip is pressed, and opens when it falls under `opening.open`; a controller whose value is not read opens with its grip's press. While a grenade is in a hand, the game's `THROW_GRENADE` is held back so that a wind-up of the game's own cannot take it.

## Safe items

Taking a grenade from the chest pouch creates an independent loadout item and, with it, a native [`Grenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade) of its own (`grenade::safe`, `grenade::native`). The grenade comes from the grenade weapon's own factory, [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::PrimeGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-PrimeGrenade): the engine's pooled allocation, constructor parameters, owner, weapon provenance, and ammunition-manager update registration are all the game's. Its one lasting claim on the weapon, [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::m_PrimedGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PrimedGrenade), is released as soon as the grenade exists, so the weapon keeps no reference to the item and any number of items can exist at once without contending for it. The draw plays `Event::GrenadePrime` on the drawing hand; a refused draw plays `Event::PouchRefused`.

The factory deducts a round through the weapon's [`ConsumeAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ConsumeAmmo) virtual, and that deduction alone is suppressed, on that thread and for that weapon, for the duration of the call. The pouch takes its grenade from the stock separately; every other way an item is made (a take from the world, a locker, the world's own replacement of a dropped grenade) leaves the stock as it is.

A drawn grenade is inert. A grenade's fuse counts down from the moment it spawns: its post-simulation update, [`Explosive::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdatePostSim), ticks [`Explosive::m_DetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DetonationTimer) only while [`Grenade::m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer) holds, the switch the game's beacon weapon clears to keep its beacons' timers still. The draw clears it, and clears [`Grenade::m_CanUseUpgrades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_CanUseUpgrades) too, because the contact upgrades can zero the timer without consulting the switch. Only pulling the pin raises the switch ([The pin](#the-pin)); nothing resets it or the timer, so a grenade held, stowed, thrown, or dropped with its pin in never starts its fuse, and a caught grenade's fuse is never restarted. The original upgrade flag is put back when a grenade whose pin is out leaves through the weapon's launch, and cleared again if the launch does not go through. Creation accepts only finite, positive start and remaining timers. While an item is inert, the AI's grenade awareness ([`SenseAwareness::GrenadeCheck`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ai::ai::SenseAwareness##func-GrenadeCheck)) ignores it (`grenade::native::ai`), so characters do not flee a grenade that cannot go off.

The controller holds only a weak reference to each item's actor, and a weak count check does not prevent forced pool erasure, so an item whose actor the pool reclaims retires from the loadout rather than being kept alive. Native accesses run on the game thread, in the input tick's game stage at the top of the world's pre-simulation queue and at the render boundary after the ammunition manager's post-simulation pass. An allocation has to come from that game stage: registered after the commit, the grenade is first updated in the next tick, after the ammunition manager has made its physics body, while registered before it, its post-simulation update would run in the same tick without a body and fault ([`Grenade::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-UpdatePostSim)). A draw or a pickup anywhere else is refused.

During a safe-item draw the allocator hook forces the factory's reclaim argument false under the allocator's critical section, so a full pool refuses the draw without deleting another item or a live grenade, and a refused allocation leaves no item, no model, and nothing in the hand. Held and stowed items get their transforms from the controller; world items keep the matrices the simulation writes. A safe grenade can move between the hands and the hip holsters and keeps its item and its actor throughout; the loadout table checks the expected item view before every move, so an occupied destination or a stale move leaves the item unchanged ([two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md)). An item whose pin is out cannot be holstered.

## Stock

The pouch draws from the game's own grenade count (`grenade::stock`): the hand-grenade weapon's [`m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo) plus the inventory's reserve of the weapon's ammunition kind ([`Inventory::m_Ammunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Ammunition)), the sum the HUD's grenade readout shows. Its capacity is the reserve's cap ([`Inventory::m_MaxAmmunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_MaxAmmunition)), which the game's grenade upgrades raise.

A pouch draw reads the stock first, and an empty stock refuses (`grenade: the pouch is empty`). The item is made next, and only then is one grenade taken, from the reserve while it has any and otherwise from the magazine, through [`Inventory::SetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-SetAmmoCount) or the weapon's [`SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo), which keep the HUD current; a refused allocation therefore takes nothing. Under the game's unlimited ammunition ([`Character::HasUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasUnlimitedAmmo), or the weapon tuning's [`m_unlimited_ammo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_unlimited_ammo), the rule [reload.md](reload.md) reads) the draw is free and the pouch is always offered.

Restocking is physical only. While the controller scheme runs, the game's own grenade pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart)), which both the proximity pickup ([`ShouldAutoPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ShouldAutoPickup)) and the use input reach, credits nothing to the local player, so walking up to a locker or over a dropped grenade credits nothing. A grenade the hands take from the world or a display has its rounds emptied first, so it credits nothing either. Other ways the game sets the count, such as a save's load, the default loadout on a respawn, or a script's give, still apply.

## Putting it back

The chest pouches take back what they give (`weapons::pouch_return`). A press counts when the hand holding the item enters the pouch's return volume from outside. A grenade has to go `pouch_return.grenade.depth_m` into the pouch, and its reach also rises `pouch_return.grenade.rise_m` above the pouch, since a hand bringing a grenade back stops high; a C4 charge counts up to `pouch_return.c4.return_margin_m` beyond its draw volume, without a push into the chest. An item drawn inside the volume counts only after the hand has left it by the item's `leave_margin_m`, and after a press, taken or refused, the hand has to leave the same way before pressing again, so a draw never puts its item straight back. The same item has to stay held across the approach.

A grenade with its pin in pressed onto the grenade pouch retires: its item leaves the loadout table, its actor leaves the pool, and the stock goes up by one through the game's setter (`Event::PouchReturn`). A grenade whose pin is out never goes back. A full stock refuses with a long buzz of its own (`Event::PouchFull`) and a click, the dry fire of the gun the other hand holds or else of a gun carried on the body (`reload::refusal_click`), and the grenade stays in the hand. Under unlimited ammunition a full stock still takes the grenade.

A C4 charge pressed onto the C4 pouch is taken back as a stand-down takes it back ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::DespawnPrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-DespawnPrimedExplosive), `Event::PouchReturn`). C4 has no stock, as in the game: the only limit is how many charges may be placed at once. A held charge is primed but not yet placed, and only the weapon's fire adds a charge to the placed ones ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile)), so a charge put back never held a place against the limit, is never counted by the detonation ([`NumActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-NumActiveExplosives)), and, removed from the pool, can never detonate. Only a charge the hand still holds goes back.

## In the hand

A held grenade and a held C4 charge are each placed in the hand by a hold (`weapons::in_hand::HoldPlacement`): the model's origin and axes in the hand's grip frame, given for the right hand and mirrored for the left. The grip frame's origin is the centre of the closed hand, about where Rico's palm is drawn, so a model centred there with the grip's own orientation sinks into the palm; each hold is derived from its model's geometry.

The grenade (`hold` in the grenade tuning) stands upright in the fist: its body runs up the finger tube with the fuse head out past the thumb, its lever lies against the palm, and its body sits one body radius out of the palm. The grenade palm pose closes the fingers round a bar of the body's diameter. The C4 charge (`hold` in the C4 tuning) is a flat square brick lying with its top face on the palm, so its sticking face looks out of the hand at whatever it is pressed onto; the surface probes start at the brick's middle (`CHARGE_CENTRE_M`, from the model). The charge pose bends the fingers over the brick's far edge onto its outer face, with the thumb along its side. The magazine's hold is in [reload.md](reload.md).

A held explosive is drawn where the hand is drawn. The transforms the simulation ticks give it come from the tick's tracked grip, and the engine's render update interpolates between the last two, so drawn from them alone it would trail the hand by one to two ticks and catch up when the hand stops. So once per rendered frame its transforms are set to its hold on the drawn hand's grip at the frame's interpolation fraction (`hands::late::drawn_hands`; `grenade::safe::render_frame` for a safe grenade, and a detour on the explosive's render update, `weapons::in_hand`, for a caught grenade and a charge; [render-time-anchoring.md](../rendering/render-time-anchoring.md)). A stowed grenade keeps the ticks' transforms.

The other hand takes a held grenade or charge from it by closing on it ([grip.md](grip.md)), and the loadout table logs the move as a handover; the throw then belongs to the hand that took it.

## The pin

Three things pull a held grenade's pin (`grenade::pin`), and the log names which (`by`: `the trigger`, `the other hand`, or `the teeth`):

- A trigger press on the hand holding it, whatever the other hand does.
- The other hand's squeeze on the pin. While one hand holds a grenade with its pin in and the other is free, the pin is offered to the free hand as a grip target (`GrabVolume::GrenadePin`) at the top of the fuse head, with a reach of `pin.radius_m`. Its marker is a red ring the size of that reach, shown always and labelled "pull pin" while the hand is at it. A squeeze anywhere else on the grenade, within the hold tuning's `hold.handle_radius_m`, takes the grenade into that hand instead. The pin ranks over the grenade (`Tier::Handle`), so a hand within the pin's reach takes the pin even when it is nearer the grenade's grip. The squeeze plays the pin's bump on both hands.
- The teeth. With the other hand occupied (holding something, closed on something, or untracked), bringing the grenade up so that its pin is within `pin.mouth_radius_m` of the mouth (`pin.mouth_in_head_m`, below and in front of the eyes) and holding it there for `pin.teeth_dwell_s` pulls the pin. A grenade only passing the face pulls nothing, and one left at the mouth pulls once per visit.

Pulling the pin raises [`Grenade::m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer), so the fuse counts down from that moment, and plays a bump on that hand (`Event::GrenadePin`). A grenade held too long explodes in the hand. The pin comes out only once the grenade has its physics body, which it gains at the end of the tick it is drawn in, so a press in that tick leaves the pin in. Nothing puts the pin back. A grenade whose pin is out can pass to the other hand but cannot be holstered or put back into the pouch.

A grenade whose pin is out looks and sounds live in the hand. The game shows a burning fuse only through a thrown grenade's trail, its effect and its sound, whose fuse parameter the grenade's own update drives from its timers ([aim-pipeline.md, "The burning fuse"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#the-burning-fuse)), and starts it at the throw. Pulling the pin starts that trail on the held grenade at once ([`Explosive::PlayTrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PlayTrailEffect)). The game's prime gives a grenade no trail resources, which its launch hands over just before it plays the trail, so the pin first gives the grenade the hand-grenade weapon's player trail effect and sound ([`Explosive::SetupEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetupEffects) with [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::m_TrailEffectPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_TrailEffectPlayer) and `m_TrailSoundPlayer`). A release through the weapon's launch restarts the trail, as the game's throw would; an inert release of a live grenade leaves it playing. A grenade whose pin is in never has a trail.

The fuse warning runs from the pin to the detonation, for a grenade from the pouch and a caught one alike: a ring at the hand and a haptic click (`Event::GrenadeWarning`) whose gap shrinks from `warning.interval_s[0]` to `[1]` as the fuse runs out, while the ring shifts from `warning.calm_rgba` to `warning.urgent_rgba`. Both follow the share of the fuse that has burnt (`grenade::fuse_urgency`), whatever the fuse's length. With grenades in both hands it follows the fuse nearest to running out.

## Releases

Opening the fingers releases the grenade, with the motion every hand-thrown item takes ([The throw's motion](#the-throws-motion)), and once the actor has its physics body the release goes through at once, in the input tick's game stage. The pin decides what the release is, whatever the hand's speed; there is no arming on a throw.

A grenade with its pin in leaves the hand inert. The release queues the captured pose in [`Explosive::m_DeferredPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredPhysicsTransform) and raises [`Explosive::m_DeferredSetPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredSetPhysicsTransform), which the ammunition update applies before the pending launch, and writes the deferred launch fields with the release motion ([`Explosive::m_InitialAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_InitialAngularVelocity), [`Explosive::m_RelativeVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_RelativeVelocity), [`Explosive::m_DeferredInitialVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredInitialVelocity), and [`Explosive::m_DeferredPropelTowards`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredPropelTowards)), so the body takes the release velocity on its next update, without the launch's trail effect. It lies in the world as the loadout's item ([Dropped grenades](#dropped-grenades)) and can never detonate.

A grenade whose pin is out is a live grenade. The release re-attaches it to [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::m_PrimedGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PrimedGrenade) and calls [`WeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Projectile), so the engine's own launch owns the effects and the slot bookkeeping, and the launch hook replaces the launch's start and motion with the captured transform and the hand's release. The grenade weapon's launch never reaches the ammunition deduction, because its `Fire_Projectile` override does not run the base implementation that makes it. When the launch cannot go ahead (the game's grenade permission, [`Inventory::m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades), is off, or the weapon already has a primed grenade), the release falls back to the inert path with the fuse still running. Either way the item and its table entry retire once the grenade is in the simulation: taking it up again as an item would give it a fresh, inert actor and lose its fuse, so a live grenade in the world is caught as any other is ([Live grenades](#live-grenades)).

Every release has to be a launch the engine applied. Both engine paths that put a grenade into the simulation need its physics body, and the deferred launch dereferences it unchecked, so a release waits until the body exists, proves that the launch landed, and only then retires anything. A release in the tick of the draw is serviced at the render boundary once the body exists; if the body has not appeared within the wait, the item stays in the hand and the request is abandoned. A loss of tracking or input releases the hand's grenade at rest by the same rules, inert while its pin is in.

### Dropped grenades

A dropped item keeps its table entry in the world and can be taken up again: a free hand closing on it takes it (a `GrabVolume::LiveGrenade` target of radius `catch.radius_m`, reached from the controller's grip where the drawn hand cannot reach it; [grip.md, "Resolving a hand"](grip.md#resolving-a-hand)), and so does the grapple's snatch ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md)), whose catch takes it into the hand at the next input tick. The physical hands never take a grenade as a loose object, so the loadout always owns it. The landed body is simulating, so the engine no longer follows its transform; taking the item up gives it a fresh inert actor in the hand and removes the landed one from the pool, once the pool has room for the replacement. That is the only case where an item changes actors. Stop, load, and reset callbacks clear pending releases and move old actors to a retirement list, which the render boundary removes without touching newly drawn items.

## The throw's motion

A grenade, a caught grenade, a C4 charge, and the beacon leave the hand the way a gun does when the hand lets go of it, and through the same code: `grenade::throw_release` takes the one release estimate every let-go takes ([physics-substrate.md, "Release velocities"](../body/physics-substrate.md#release-velocities); [physical-weapons.md, "Letting go"](physical-weapons.md#letting-go)), at the held item's own centre and from when the item came into that hand, linear and angular, so a flick of the wrist spins it. There is no separate throw window, scale, or cap.

Three differences remain. The first two are the engine's, and the third is the item's safety:

- A held grenade's body is not in the simulation, and only the engine's launch puts it there: [`Explosive::UpdatePostSimFromManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdatePostSimFromManager) applies a pending launch at the end of the tick, putting the body into the simulation, setting its angular velocity from [`Explosive::m_InitialAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_InitialAngularVelocity) and its linear velocity from [`Explosive::m_DeferredInitialVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredInitialVelocity), and disabling collision with the thrower. A grenade therefore takes its release through those fields instead of the owned-body setter a gun's body takes, and it first moves one tick after the release. The release's transform and the actor's current matrix are both set to the release pose, so the grenade waits where the hand let go of it. A caught grenade's body is already simulating, so it takes the owned-body setter as a gun does.
- The engine gives a grenade's body a high angular damping when it creates it, so a grenade's spin dies away within a fraction of a second.
- The fuse: only the pin starts it; a release never does.

A held gun is a body of its own joined to the hand bodies ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)). A held explosive has no such body: its own body follows the hand's transform until the release, and a hand holding a grenade, a charge, or the detonator is not free, so its palm does not act.

## Grenades lying in the world

A finite native hand grenade lying in the world (dropped, and not [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup)) is one grenade. A free hand closing on it takes that grenade (`grenade::take_world_pickup`): a safe item is admitted to the hand under the draw's allocation rules, and only then is the native grenade consumed, its rounds emptied so nothing can credit them, and despawned with its bodies out of the physics world. A refused admission leaves it lying, so one native grenade always becomes exactly one item. The grapple's snatch yanks the same native grenade's body to the hand, and its catch makes the same take ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md#the-pull-and-the-catch)). Once taken it is an ordinary safe item.

A character's drop makes such a grenade a safe item from the start (`grenade::world`). A detour of the drop's base implementation ([`WeaponBase::OnDropBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnDropBase)) notes each finite hand grenade dropped by a holder other than the player. The drop runs in the world update, where nothing may be spawned, so the next input tick's game stage makes a safe item lying in the world at the native grenade's pose, moving with its body's motion, under the draw's pool and stage rules (`safe::spawn_in_world`), and only then consumes the native grenade as a take does. A refused allocation is tried again for `world.max_retry_ticks` input ticks at most, then leaves the native grenade a pickup.

Every way a character drops its hand grenade reaches the detour. The grenade weapon has no drop of its own, so any drop through the weapon's drop virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop)) runs the base implementation, and the game's drops all go through the inventory's drop, which queues the virtual for the weapon's next pre-simulation update. A dying character ([`Character::OnDestroyed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-OnDestroyed)) drops its best weapon and, when a random draw falls below its [`m_DropGrenadeChance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_DropGrenadeChance), its hand grenade, both through [`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon). A knocked-down or tethered character drops only through the `NDropWeaponStateTask` state task, the same inventory drop. The mod's disarm and the grapple's snatch take only guns, so neither ever drops a grenade.

## Locker sources

A free hand can take a grenade from a native weapon display or a static collection display (`grenade::locker`). Each hand is offered its nearest display within `locker.reach_m`. Completion checks the same source and distance, admits a safe item to the hand under the draw's allocation rules, and only then removes that grenade from the display; a missing or changed source refuses the grab rather than selecting another, and a refused admission leaves the display as it was. Each grenade on a display is one real grenade: it stays where it is until a hand takes it, and the take leaves the stock, the other hand, and holstered items unchanged. A finite native grenade lying in the world is registered the same way but is not a display: the hand takes it as the grenade it is, and the snatch can reach it, where it leaves displays to the hand in reach.

A native display is a hand grenade in a dropped or rack state, registered from read-only observations of the weapon's state changes and render; the bounded registry keeps weak references and never changes native pickup eligibility. A take consumes the grenade by the game's own pickup with its rounds emptied first (`stock::consume_through_pickup`), so the pickup credits nothing and does everything else it does for a grenade: the weapon disables its pickup, hides, leaves the physics world, is handed to the spawn system's despawn, and sends its picked-up event. The game's pickup of a hand grenade never hands out a copy, whatever its `m_InfinitePickup`, so the grenade comes back exactly when the game would restock it: when the placement rule that put it there spawns its weapon again.

A static collection display is a grenade model instance in one of the Guardia outposts' static model collections, which the game never picks up or restocks. The adapter accepts only an allowlist of those collections' paths, each with its expected instance count (`locker::format`), and finds the grenade instances by the grenade model's resource name; a collection that does not match refuses registration rather than falling back to a wider search. It captures collections as they load ([`Location::LoadModelCollection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/location::Location##func-LoadModelCollection), parsing the dictionary with full bounds checks) and also discovers them on the input tick from the location system's loaded near locations (`collection::discovery`), so a location loaded before injection is found too. The registry keeps copied records only (the collection address, a generation, the instance index, and the instance and model identities), never a pointer for later dereference, and the [`ModelCollection::Destruct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelCollection##func-Destruct) detour removes a collection's records before it is destroyed. Offers, completion, and retirement share one lock, held through the safe draw, so address reuse or an unload invalidates an old offer.

A take from a static display hides the instance (`collection::hide`): under the registry's lock, the take checks that the entry is still the recorded instance drawing the grenade model and still shown, and clears [`VISIBLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceFlags##flag-VISIBLE) and [`FLAG_ENABLED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceFlags##flag-FLAG_ENABLED), as the game's own hide of an instance does. An entry that is no longer that instance refuses the take, and the admitted item is put away again. The instance stays hidden while its collection lives; when its location streams out and loads again, the collection makes it afresh, shown and a source again.

Leaving gameplay or losing input clears the hands' offers but keeps the static records, since a loaded location need not load again when gameplay resumes. Shutdown closes and clears both registries.

## Live grenades

A free hand catches a live grenade near the player and throws it on (`grenade::catch`). Every grenade launched, by the player or anyone else, passes through the grenade's launch ([`Grenade::PropelToward`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-PropelToward)), whose detour notes each one with its physics body ([`Explosive::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_PfxInstance)) and the body's owner. On the input tick each noted grenade's body is read through the hand bodies' ownership check (the body still exists, is dynamic, and is owned by the same object), which proves the grenade has not detonated or been removed; one that fails is forgotten.

While no grenade is caught, each live grenade within `catch.range_m` of the player is a grab volume (`GrabVolume::LiveGrenade`, radius `catch.radius_m`, larger than a pouch's since a flying grenade moves between ticks) for each free hand that holds no grenade. The player's own grenade waits `catch.own_grace_s` after its launch, so it is not taken back as it leaves. A grip press there catches the grenade nearest the hand (`Event::GrenadeCatch`). From then each input tick gives its body the velocity that carries it to the hand's hold over the tick, capped at `catch.max_carry_mps`, so it stays in the hand with its own physics and its fuse runs on, with the fuse warning on that hand. Opening the fingers throws it: its body takes the release motion through the owned-body setter. Nothing in the catch raises or resets the fuse, and a loss of input lets go of a caught grenade with its fuse unchanged.

## The beacon

The beacon is held and thrown by hand as a grenade is (`grenade::beacon`). The primed beacon of the local player's selected beacon weapon is held in the dominant hand at the grenade's hold, unless that hand holds a grenade, and the game's own fire of that beacon is held back. Closing the hand's grip and opening it again throws the beacon with the release every hand-thrown item takes: once its physics body exists, the weapon's own launch fires it with that release. The beacon leaves the hand at rest when the hand never closes on it within `beacon.grip_wait_ms`, its body does not appear within `beacon.body_wait_ms`, the hand loses tracking or input, the weapon is no longer selected, or the controller scheme ends. A thrown beacon is not caught.

## C4

A grip press at the C4 pouch primes a charge in whichever hand grabbed it (`Event::C4Prime`). The game's own planting goes through its planting mode, which equips the explosives slot, aims a placement ghost with the camera, and places the charge on the aimed surface with an animation; the mod bypasses all of it and never touches the selected slot. Before the C4 weapon's serial update ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial)), which the inventory runs every frame whatever is wielded, it calls the weapon's own prime ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::PrimeExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-PrimeExplosive)), which spawns the charge as the planting mode does. Priming moves the weapon to its wielding state, so the state it was in is put back at once. Priming consumes nothing, so the limit is the most charges that may be placed at once.

A grab while as many charges are placed as may be ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::CanPlantExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-CanPlantExplosive)) gives the detonator instead ([The detonator](#the-detonator)). A grab is refused while the game does not allow planting ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives)) and while the game's own planting holds the weapon, with `Event::PouchRefused` on the grabbing hand and an info-level log line with the reason. While a charge is held, the weapon's attachment update puts its in-hand model and its transform at the hand instead of the right-hand bone.

The weapon wheel's explosives put the game in its planting mode ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::m_IsInPlantingMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_IsInPlantingMode)), whose plant input the controller layout leaves unbound (`c4::wheel`). With a hand free, the dominant one first, the mod leaves the mode with the game's own holster ([`Character::HolsterWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HolsterWeapon)) and, once it has ended, primes a charge in that hand as the pouch does. A planting mode that has not ended within `wheel.leave_timeout_s`, or a hand that took something else meanwhile, gives no charge, and the log says so.

Opening the fingers throws the charge (`c4::stage`). Once the charge has its physics body, the weapon makes it live ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile)), which adds it to the placed charges the detonation reads, and the mod drops it from the hand the way the game drops a charge under the parachute: it moves the charge to the grip ([`Explosive::SetPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetPhysicsTransform)), drops it into the simulation ([`PlantedExplosive::DropExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-DropExplosive)), gives its body the release motion through the owned-body setter ([`PlantedExplosive::SetVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-SetVelocity), linear only, is the fallback when the body refuses it), plays its trail, and pulses the hand (`Event::C4Throw`). Under the parachute the weapon has already dropped it from the right-hand bone, so the mod only moves it to the hand and replaces its motion. The explosive's own collision handler sticks the charge to what it lands on. A release once the limit is reached takes the charge back rather than throwing one that nothing would detonate. When the throw stops acting, a held charge is taken back ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::DespawnPrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-DespawnPrimedExplosive)). A charge once thrown or stuck leaves its hand free.

### C4 pressed onto a surface

A held charge can be pressed onto a surface instead of thrown (`c4::stick`; the C4 tuning's `stick`). Once it has been out of the pouch for `stick.after_prime_s`, so the chest the pouch is on is never taken for a surface, each aim update casts six short rays from the charge's centre along the axes of the hand's grip frame, with the player's own character excluded. A surface within `stick.contact_m` is touching the charge, and the charge is pressed onto it.

The press is the throw's release with a different start and velocity. The next C4 weapon update makes the charge live as a throw does, moves it to `stick.standoff_m` off the surface along its normal, turned as the hand holds it, drops it into the simulation, and pushes it onto the surface at `stick.push_mps` plus the character's own velocity. The charge's own collision handler then sticks it where it touches, oriented by the surface and attached to the object it touched, exactly as a thrown charge sticks where it lands, so a charge pressed onto a car rides the car (`Event::C4Stick`). Opening the fingers before the charge touches anything throws it.

The same rays reach on to `stick.preview_m`, and the nearest surface within it is marked where the charge would stick: a red ring lying on the surface (`GrabVolume::ChargeSurface`, a marked target no squeeze takes; [grip.md](grip.md)).

### Taking a placed charge back

A charge already placed comes back by closing a free hand on it (`c4::placed`). Each charge on the weapon's live list ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::m_ActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_ActiveExplosives)) within `placed.offer_range_m` of the player is a grab volume for each free hand (`GrabVolume::PlacedCharge`, radius `placed.radius_m`, also reached from the controller's grip when the drawn hand cannot crouch to it), offered only while no charge is held or asked for, and a grip press takes the charge nearest the hand. The weapon's next serial update first primes the hand's replacement charge, and only after that succeeds does the placed one come off the live list ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::RemoveFromActiveList`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-RemoveFromActiveList), which drops its HUD hint and light) and get removed ([`Explosive::RemoveSelf`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-RemoveSelf)), so a take works at the limit ([planted-explosives.md](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md#the-weapon-and-its-charges)). The hand then holds a primed charge as a pouch grab leaves it, except that it sticks to nothing until the stick probes have once found every surface clear of it, so the surface it came off does not take it straight back.

A take is refused, the charge left placed, when the charge went off or was removed since the offer and while the game's own planting holds the weapon, with the refusal buzz and an info-level log line. The game's planting permission does not refuse a take, since a take plants nothing and the permission is off while the grapple is busy, as it is at a snatch's catch. The grapple's snatch pulls the original charge's body and requests the same take only on a successful catch; the original stays live and counted during the flight, and the queued take carries its allocation identity, which the weapon update checks against the live list before removing anything.

### The detonator

The detonator is held in a hand like a gun, and its trigger detonates the placed charges (`c4::detonator`). A grab at the C4 pouch while as many charges are placed as may be hands it over instead of a charge; the pouch is offered for it, and draws the detonator's model in place of the charge's, while no hand holds it ([pouch-models.md](pouch-models.md)). There is one detonator, so a hand that takes it takes it from the other. It sits upright in the fist by its grip (the C4 tuning's `detonator_hold`), and the hand takes the pistol-grip pose with the trigger finger following the trigger. Opening the hand puts it back on the chest with a click; it is never dropped into the world. While it is in a hand, that hand counts as holding something.

The model is an instance the mod owns of the C4 weapon's base model ([`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance)), which the weapon's entity names as its trigger: the model the game shows in Rico's hand during its detonate act ([planted-explosives.md, "Detonation"](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md#detonation)). The input tick shows it at the hand, and each rendered frame moves it onto the hand as the hand is drawn. While the game's own detonate act plays, its detonator is placed at the grip of the hand whose press asked for the detonation, by the same hold, rather than at the act's attach bone.

The trigger of a hand holding a charge it has not let go of detonates the placed charges too, and the charge stays in the hand; a hand that opens on the tick its trigger is pulled throws the charge instead. The trigger press that brought the detonator into the hand never detonates: the trigger has to let go first.

### Detonation

A detonation goes through the game's detonate input ([`WeaponInput::E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES)), so it detonates the placed charges exactly as the game's own does, under the game's permission ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives)). The dominant stick click's `DETONATE_EXPLOSIVE` reaches it through the game's own hold rule ([bindings.md](bindings.md)). A trigger press instead requests a detonation: the C4 weapon's serial update raises the detonate input for its own update only, putting the game's value back afterwards, until the weapon starts the detonation ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput) rises) or `detonation.timeout_s` passes.

A press with charges placed clicks both hands (`Event::DetonatorPress`). A press the game would not act on, its permission being off (dead, ragdolling, staggered, or on a live wire), is refused with a buzz and logged (`c4: the game does not allow detonating now`). A press with none placed buzzes the hand (`Event::DetonatorEmpty`). A detonation's charges stay placed for a while after it starts, because the detonate act schedules them partway through its animation and each then runs down its own timer, so from the start until the count of placed charges falls to zero, rises, or `detonation.in_flight_s` passes, the charges are in flight, and a press then buzzes rather than queueing a second request that could set off a charge placed just after. A detonation the weapon starts, from either source, plays a long thud on both hands (`Event::C4Detonate`).

## Diagnostics

Every grenade whose pin the player pulls is followed in the log until it detonates (`grenade::fuse_log`), at info level under the `controllers` target, without changing what the grenade does: the pin (`grenade: the pin is pulled, and the fuse runs`), the release (`grenade: a live grenade left the hand`, with its speed, path, and fuse), the contact trigger's arming (`grenade: the contact trigger changes`), the first contacts its collision handler ([`Grenade::OnCollision`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-OnCollision)) handles and any that zeroes the timer (`grenade: a live grenade touched something`), and the detonation ([`Grenade::Detonate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-Detonate), `grenade: a live grenade detonated`). The detonation's `cause` is `the contact trigger` when a contact zeroed a running timer, `the fuse` when the timer ran out on its own, and `other, with time left on the fuse` otherwise (damage, for instance), and its `relation` and distances say where it was relative to the hand that held or let go of it and to the head.

The debug UI's Weapons > Grenades, C4, and the detonator live status shows the grenade and C4 throws' stages, the fuse, the stock, the catches, the placed charges and the limit, the detonator, and the detonations.
