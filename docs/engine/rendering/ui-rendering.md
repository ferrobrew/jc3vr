# UI rendering

How the Scaleform UI is updated and captured on the game thread, drawn on the UI render worker, and composed in its target, plus the off-screen movies, display-info writes, and the HUD's markers and layout. Where the UI sits in the frame is [rendering §10](rendering.md#10-ui--hud-pipeline); the screens and the ActionScript-native traffic are in [pause-menu.md](../pause-menu.md#screens-and-the-root-movie); the runtime and loader are in [scaleform.md](../scaleform.md). The bindings are in [`ui::ui_manager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/ui::ui_manager) and [`ui::scaleform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/ui::scaleform).

## One movie

Every screen shares the root movie, [`UIManager::m_Movie`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_Movie), an instance of `ui/root.gfx` that loads `hud.gfx`, `pause.gfx`, and the rest as children. The UI is one display tree, captured once and drawn once per frame into one target; only the render-to-texture UIs are outside it ([Off-screen movies](#off-screen-movies)).

## The update side

`CUIManager::PreRender` runs on the game thread under [`m_DeferredRenderLock`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_DeferredRenderLock), gated on an unbound system-ready flag and the three render gates [`m_RenderReady`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_RenderReady), [`m_RenderActive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_RenderActive), and [`m_RenderingEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_RenderingEnabled). In order, it:

1. claims the capture thread ([`m_CurrentCaptureThread`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_CurrentCaptureThread) and the movie's own together);
2. flushes queued `root.OnVideoStop` invokes and calls [`UpdateCachedValues`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-UpdateCachedValues);
3. calls `Update` on every active registered screen, including [`HUDUI::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-Update) with its invokes and marker writes;
4. ages the image delegates and advances and processes the dying-movie list;
5. calls `CRenderToTextureUI::Update` on every render-to-texture UI;
6. calls [`MovieImpl::Advance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::MovieImpl##vfunc-Advance) with its own capture off, then [`MovieImpl::Capture`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::MovieImpl##vfunc-Capture), on the root movie;
7. updates the UI post effects.

Each capture therefore follows exactly one update of every screen, and [`MovieImpl::CaptureImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::MovieImpl##func-CaptureImpl) is the last point at which a display-tree write reaches this frame. `PreRender` runs once per render update: in gameplay, paused or not, `CUIManager::StartPrerender` queues it as a CPU fragment (`CUIManager_PrerenderProxy`) on the frame's fragment queue; the frontend and other states call it directly. Nothing in the frame's draw calls it.

## The capture and the snapshot

`Advance` runs ActionScript and mutates the tree; `Capture` publishes its changes as the pending snapshot. The render side takes the latest snapshot with [`RTHandle::NextCapture`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RTHandle##func-NextCapture) and never sees the live tree.

`Render::Context::Capture` mutates the active snapshot, the data every game-thread UI write touches, and nothing locks writers against it: the capture lock only orders `Capture` against `NextCapture`. The engine is safe because it captures only inside `PreRender`, on the thread that makes every UI write; a capture from another thread races them ([the issue #8 and #14 constraints](../../issues/08-14-hud-overlays-and-depth.md#constraints-established)). [`UIBase::InvokeImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_base::UIBase##func-InvokeImpl) runs ActionScript at once only on the thread in `m_CurrentCaptureThread` and queues otherwise, so moving capture ownership off the game thread turns every screen invoke into a delayed one.

## The render side

[`UIManager::StartRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##vfunc-StartRender) queues a CPU fragment whose body calls the [`Render`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##vfunc-Render) virtual; [`SyncRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##vfunc-SyncRender) waits for it, and [`Submit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##vfunc-Submit) takes the master graphics context lock and flushes the HAL.

[`UIManager::RenderImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-RenderImpl) takes the deferred render lock and checks the three render gates, stamps the render thread on the texture manager and the UI command queue and executes the queue, binds [`m_RenderBuffer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_RenderBuffer) with [`RenderHAL::SetRenderTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##vfunc-SetRenderTarget), and opens a frame and scene ([`BeginFrame`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##vfunc-BeginFrame), [`BeginScene`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##vfunc-BeginScene)). When `NextCapture` consumes a snapshot it calls [`RenderHAL::Draw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##func-Draw) on the render entry, then closes the scene and frame and pops the target. It never clears the target: the UI is drawn over the finished frame. The IDB mislabels this function `CRenderToTextureUI::Update` (it uses the manager's lock, HAL, movie, render buffer, and gates), and labels the real `CRenderToTextureUI::Update` `CUIManager::DrawOcclusion`; pyxis binds the first under its correct name.

## Off-screen movies

Each render-to-texture UI ([scaleform.md](../scaleform.md#standalone-movies-crendertotextureui)) owns a separate movie. `PreRender` advances it through `CRenderToTextureUI::Update`, which, when the UI's code interface is active, calls that screen's `Update`, makes the game thread the movie's capture thread, and advances the movie.

[`UIManager::RenderOffScreenTexturesImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-RenderOffScreenTexturesImpl) then, for each with an active code interface, takes capture ownership and captures the movie from the render side, builds a target from the UI's colour and depth surfaces ([`RenderHAL::CreateRenderTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##func-CreateRenderTarget)), pushes it with the movie's rectangle ([`PushRenderTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##vfunc-PushRenderTarget)), draws the capture in a scene, calls `Submit`, pops ([`PopRenderTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::RenderHAL##vfunc-PopRenderTarget)) and releases the target, so every off-screen draw is flushed before its target is popped.

`PreRender` also calls the per-movie update on an object held next to a render block, flags that block, and adds it to the render lists (`CRBILists::Add`), apparently a path for drawing a UI movie into the world; it is not bound or investigated.

## Display-info writes

[`ValueObjectInterface::SetDisplayInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::ValueObjectInterface##func-SetDisplayInfo) writes a display object's presentation state through a `GFx::Value` handle. [`DisplayInfo::VarsSet`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::DisplayInfo##field-VarsSet) selects the fields that apply: x, y, rotation, x scale, y scale, alpha, and visibility (only [`DisplayInfo::V_VISIBLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::DisplayInfo::V_VISIBLE) is bound; the others are verified). A position is snapped to twips and applied through the object's `SetMatrix`, which updates only its own render node; no ActionScript or AS3 setter runs. Text fields take a separate text-rectangle path and 3D-flagged objects another branch. The HUD places its markers this way every frame ([Points of interest](#points-of-interest)), as it does the weapon selection cross ([weapon-selection.md](../gameplay/weapon-selection.md#placement)).

## Points of interest

Every world-anchored HUD marker is a point of interest ([`POI`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::poi::POI), `CPOI`, a game object): mission objectives, waypoints, enemy and vehicle health markers, weapon pickup hints, active explosives, and the detector target. Missions, game systems (through `CHUDUI::RegisterPOI`), and the HUD's own pickup, explosive, and detector updates create them. Each has a [`POIRecord`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::POIRecord) in [`HUDUI::m_POIListHead`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##field-m_POIListHead) under [`m_POIListLock`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##field-m_POIListLock), since systems register from their own threads: a weak reference to the POI, an optional strong one that keeps it registered, the marker clip ([`m_Element`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::POIRecord##field-m_Element), a pooled `poi` clip taken on first update), and whether it is shown ([`m_Visible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::POIRecord##field-m_Visible)).

[`HUDUI::UpdatePOIs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-UpdatePOIs) walks the list under the lock once per HUD update, drops records whose POI is gone, skips invisible POIs, and for each live one calls [`POI::UpdatePOI`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::poi::POI##vfunc-UpdatePOI), which rebuilds [`m_Transform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::poi::POI##field-m_Transform) from the attached object. It projects that transform's translation through [`UIManager::Get2DInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-Get2DInfo) with the active camera's view-projection, its orthonormalised transform and field of view, a fixed margin, the POI's clamp flag, and its 2D offset. The distance range, the behind-the-camera test, and the vehicle, settlement, mission, and ROM filters decide visibility, and a change invokes the movie's `ShowPoi` or `HidePoi`. A shown marker gets its icon scale and alpha, distance text, edge-arrow state, flash, label, and progress bar through `UpdatePoi`, then its x and y through a display-info write. The one on-screen POI nearest the stage centre whose label box contains the centre shows its label ([`m_ShowingLabelPOIId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##field-m_ShowingLabelPOIId)). The `poi` clip lays out its children around its origin and never reads its own position.

[`Convert3DCoords`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-Convert3DCoords), called by `Get2DInfo`, maps NDC to stage coordinates. When the cached viewport ratio differs from the stage's ([`m_CachedStageRatio`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_CachedStageRatio)) it stretches the longer axis so the stage keeps its shape, and the frame extends past the stage on that axis (show-all scaling: content beyond the stage there is still visible). A point inside the safe area ([`m_SafeArea`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_SafeArea)) shrunk by the margin is on screen. Otherwise an unclamped marker is off screen in front or behind, and a clamped one, after a point outside the horizontal field of view is swung back toward the view, is clamped to the shrunk safe area with an edge code ([`ClampToScreen`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-ClampToScreen)).

[`ComputeSafeArea`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##func-ComputeSafeArea) derives the safe area from the stage, [`m_SafeAreaPercentage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::ui_manager::UIManager##field-m_SafeAreaPercentage), and the cached viewport size (not the movie viewport, so layout and render viewport can differ), and pushes it to every screen, whose movies anchor their edge containers to it. Where the cached viewport is relatively longer than the stage on an axis, the safe area widens on that axis by the extra extent visible at the stage's scale, moving the edge containers out beyond the stage.

Health: [`HUDUI::UpdateHealth`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-UpdateHealth) pushes [`Character::GetHealth`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##vfunc-GetHealth) (on the scale of [`m_MaxHealth`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_MaxHealth)) to the movie on change, which shows it only as the full-screen damage vignette and the reticle's low-health outline. The weapon readout: [`HUDUI::UpdateShowWeaponsAndGrenadesUiTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-UpdateShowWeaponsAndGrenadesUiTimer) holds [`m_ShowWeaponsUiTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##field-m_ShowWeaponsUiTimer) full while the character should show its weapon, counts it down otherwise, zeroes it on holstering, and shows and hides the readout on its edges; the grenade readout has its own timer.

## Weapon icons

The weapon icons are standalone textures, one per weapon id ([`WeaponBase::m_WeaponId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponId)): the movie's `GetWeaponIconName` builds `textures/ui/weapon_icons/<id>.ddsc` (or `<id>_sm.ddsc`, which not every weapon has) and its icon clips load it through `LoadIcon`. The selection cross, the weapon readout, the pickup panel (`MCI_weapon_pickup`, shown by `OnNewValidWeaponPickup` with a weapon and the one it would replace), and the pickup hint markers show them. The hand grenade and C4 have icons under their weapons' ids; the grenade readout uses vector clips instead. Each `.ddsc` is an `AVTX` header (DXGI format, size, mips) and a table of data streams, each with an offset, a size, and a source (the file or a companion high-resolution file); the weapon icons are one BC3 mip in the file, white on transparent, all one size.

The HUD keeps one pickup hint per weapon slot: the nearest valid weapon registered as a hint for that slot, shown for the pistol and two-handed slots only while the player's weapon there is low on ammunition (`CHUDUI::UpdateWeaponPickupHints`, `CHUDUI::IsValidWeaponPickup`, `CHUDUI::WeaponInSlotIsLowAmmo`, found by name; unbound and unverified).

## Colour, alpha, masks, and filters

The blend states are verified in the IDB (`Scaleform::Render::D3D1x::HAL::createBlendStates`, unbound); the rest follows Scaleform's D3D1x HAL source (`applyBlendModeImpl`).

- The HAL builds two blend states per blend mode and one for mask submission, from a per-mode table. The ordinary set draws normal content with `SRC_ALPHA, INV_SRC_ALPHA` on colour and `ONE, INV_SRC_ALPHA` on alpha. The second is the same table with a colour source factor of `SRC_ALPHA` replaced by `ONE`, for premultiplied content: render-target layers, filter results, and cached bitmaps. The mask state writes no colour.
- Over a target cleared to transparent black, ordinary content leaves `rgb = c * a` and `alpha = a`: the UI target holds premultiplied colour with coverage alpha, and a consumer compositing it must blend it as premultiplied (`ONE, INV_SRC_ALPHA` on colour).
- Masks are stencil-based: submitted with colour writes off, their rectangles cleared from the stencil afterwards (`PushMask_BeginSubmit`, `drawMaskClearRectangles`).
- A filtered subtree is drawn into a temporary target from the render buffer manager, filtered, cached while unchanged, and drawn back with the premultiplied states; its output extends past the clip's bounds by the filter radius (usual Scaleform behaviour, not traced in the IDB).

## The HUD movie's layout

`ui/hud.gfx`'s main timeline places ten top-level clips, listed on [`ScaleformInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::scaleform::ScaleformInfo): the six `MCI_safe_area_*` edge and corner groups; `MCI_safe_area_center`, holding `MCI_reticles` (the weapon and grapple reticles), `MCI_mech_reticle`, the pickups, the full-screen overlays, and the large button hint; `MCI_weapon_selection_wheel`; `MCI_hud`; and `MCI_poi_stage`. ActionScript creates everything else. The live marker pool is anonymous clips directly under the HUD clip, not under `MCI_poi_stage`.

`MCI_safe_area_bottom_right` holds, among others, the weapon readout (`MCI_weapons_parent`), the grenade and heat (`MCI_heat_container`) readouts, the notifications, the systemic button hints, the cutscene skip prompt, and the tether readout `MCI_dual_tether`: the tethers in use out of the grapple's maximum, written by `UpdateTetherCount`, and a timeline of states for a new tether, a broken one, retracting, and release. `ShowDualTetherUi` shows it when `UpdateWireData` sees a new tether, and it fades when every tether is gone. The movie also builds a list of attached tethers with tension bars in `MCI_dualtether_stage`, which its constructor hides and nothing shows again.

Every mask placement is inside a widget (the circular meters, the life bar, the timers, the chaos award, the mission objective); the main timeline places none. Hundreds of placements carry a filter, mostly glows, then blurs and drop shadows. A handful use a non-normal blend mode, and one is cached as a bitmap.
