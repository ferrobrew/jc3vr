# Visibility culling in VR

The engine culls against the centre camera: a single view with a symmetric projection, narrower than the two eyes' off-axis frusta combined. Each eye's frustum reaches further out and is shifted laterally, so geometry that an eye can see past the edge of the centre frustum is culled for both eyes. It shows as black voids and pop-in at the outer edges of each eye. The mod widens each of the engine's cull frusta to the binocular union, and leaves the per-eye render projections untouched. Every widen is VR-only: on flatscreen no union projection is published, and each hook passes through.

The engine has several independent visibility gates, which test against three different cameras: the occluder manager's cull camera, the camera manager's active camera, and the landscape system's terrain camera. They are documented in [`model-culling.md`](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md) and, for terrain, in [issue #40](../../issues/40-terrain-black-tiles.md). The hooks are in `payload/src/hooks/graphics_engine/scene/culling.rs` and `scene/terrain_cull.rs`, and the tuning constants are in those modules and in `payload/src/vr/frame.rs`, except the spawn widen and budget scale, which are on `StereoConfig` (`payload/src/stereo/config.rs`).

## The union projection

`begin_render_frame` (`payload/src/vr/frame.rs`) builds one cull projection per VR frame and publishes it through `cull_projection_standard()`. It takes the outermost tangent of the two eyes' fields of view on each side, scales each outward by `1 + CULL_FOV_PADDING` (`0.4`), and adds an eye-shift margin of half the IPD over the near plane. Each resulting half-angle is clamped to 85 degrees so that a wide-FOV headset cannot produce a degenerate frustum. The near and far planes come from the live active camera. The padding covers motion: the engine culls at a single interpolated pose, so under fast motion, especially flying, geometry can otherwise pop in at the edges before the cull catches up. It applies to the vertical axis too, which flying pitch shifts.

## The scene cull

The scene-cull widen covers the broad-phase BFBC scene cull that terrain, models, streaming, and AO volumes share ([model-culling.md, "1. Instance-level BFBC cull"](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md#1-instance-level-bfbc-cull-build-phase)). The `get_bfbc_frustum_params` detour on [`OccluderCollectionManager::GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager##func-GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime) identifies the main cull camera by its address (the manager's own `m_CullingCamera`, at [`OccluderCollectionManager::CULLING_CAMERA_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager::CULLING_CAMERA_OFFSET)) and writes the union projection over its `m_ProjectionF` before the engine builds the frustum. Shadow and reflection culls use other functions, and any other camera through this hook fails the identity check. The write is not restored: the cull camera is the occluder manager's own per-frame copy of the centre camera, not a render camera, and rewriting the same value is idempotent when parallel cull jobs re-enter.

The same hook applies two further main-view corrections:

- `CULL_SIZE_FOV_DEG` (`50`) sets the cull camera's `m_FOVT1`. BFBC's screen-space size cull drops an object whose angular size falls below `tan(fov/2)` times a minimum screen percentage. The mod injects a 90 degree camera FOV, where the flatscreen game runs about 50 degrees, so without this the size cull is about twice as aggressive in VR and drops small and distant geometry and vehicle sub-meshes. Only the size and AO-volume culls read `m_FOVT1` on this camera, so the frustum cull and LOD are unaffected. `0` would leave the field untouched.
- The hook sets [`BFBCFrustumCullParameters::m_FrustumCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::BFBCFrustumCullParameters##field-m_FrustumCount) to 1 in the built parameters, which leaves the camera frustum at index 0 and drops the occluder frustums. Software occlusion casts occluder silhouettes from the single centre viewpoint, which is wrong for both offset eyes: an eye can see past the edge of an occluder that hides geometry from the centre. The widen also re-includes edge occluders, which concentrates the loss at the periphery. The write is skipped if the count is outside `2..=33`, since such a value indicates that the parameter layout does not match the build. The cost is overdraw of geometry that the centre view would occlude.

## The spawn and character-occlusion frusta

`CSpawnSystem::Update` and the character-occlusion pass build their BFBC frusta from the camera manager's active camera, not from the cull camera ([model-culling.md, "Related: spawn visibility"](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md#related-spawn-visibility) and ["Related: character occlusion"](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md#related-character-occlusion)), so the scene-cull widen does not reach them. With `widen_spawn_cull` (on), the same `get_bfbc_frustum_params` detour recognizes the active camera, writes the union projection over its `m_ProjectionF` for the duration of the original call, and restores all 16 floats afterwards. The spawn system's "do not (de)spawn while visible" gate and the character-occlusion cull then cover both eyes. The size-cull and occlusion corrections above do not apply on this path.

`spawn_budget_scale` (default `2.0`) addresses a related symptom: when a spawn budget is exceeded the spawn system despawns the highest-ranked object, which under the wider VR view can remove vehicles and characters in front of the player. The `spawn_system_update` detour multiplies every budget entry once, on the first `Update`, through the patcher, so the change reverts on uninject. The value is read once and needs a restart to change.

## The per-render-block frustum cull

`CModelInstance::AddToRender` re-culls each render block against the active camera's precomputed frustum planes, after the BFBC pass has admitted the instance ([model-culling.md, "2. Per-render-block frustum cull"](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md#2-per-render-block-frustum-cull-draw-list-build)). Without a widen, large multi-block buildings pop out at the edges of the combined view even with the scene cull widened. The `camera_update_frustum` detour on `Camera::UpdateFrustum` widens it. For the active camera only, it calls the original a second time with `m_ViewProjection` temporarily set to `m_View * union`, then restores `m_ViewProjection`, so only the frustum planes widen. The same planes gate the instance fade, road meshes, and far lights ([model-culling.md, "The same active-camera frustum elsewhere"](../../engine/rendering/model-culling.md#the-same-active-camera-frustum-elsewhere)), so the widen also replaces the instant hide at the view edge with the normal fade, and keeps road meshes and far lights at the edges. The second call goes through the trampoline, so it does not re-enter the detour.

## Terrain

Terrain patches are selected by the landscape system against its own [`STerrainPatchSystem::m_TerrainCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::STerrainPatchSystem##field-m_TerrainCamera), a per-frame copy of the active camera, so none of the widens above affect terrain. The `terrain_patch_system_update` detour hooks [`TerrainPatchSystemUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/graphics_engine::render_engine##func-TerrainPatchSystemUpdate). After the original has refreshed the copy, it writes the union projection onto `m_TerrainCamera`, rebuilds its view-projection, and rebuilds its six frustum planes with `Camera::UpdateFrustum`. The copy overwrites the camera on every call, so the widen is reapplied every frame. This fixes patch holes at the outer edges and bottom of the view when flying.

The tessellated terrain also discards patches in its hull shader, with a back-patch cull and a frustum cull whose constants are baked once per frame from the render camera. In stereo, `terrain_patch_setup_constant_buffers` clears both enable flags around that bake. The mechanism and the reasoning are in [issue #40, "Recurrence under the single-pass collapse"](../../issues/40-terrain-black-tiles.md#recurrence-under-the-single-pass-collapse-the-hulls-view-dependent-culls).

## Interaction with the double-draw

Every widen runs once per frame and changes visibility only, not lighting or shadow content. The save/restore ledger in [`lighting-shadow-vr-interactions.md`](lighting-shadow-vr-interactions.md#saverestore-ledger--default-on-modifications) lists them for completeness. The sun-shadow cascade fit is widened separately, in `shadow_manager_update_render` (documented there).
