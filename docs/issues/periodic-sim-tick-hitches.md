# Periodic sim-tick hitches

Resolved: the cause is outside the game and the mod. Sessions hitched on a fixed wall-clock beat, a stall of about 300 ms twice a minute and roughly every other minute one of about 600 ms, inside the sim tick and outside every phase it times. This page says how to recognise the cause and what the mod's one remedy costs.

## The cause: the Steam client's link to a Steam Frame

The native Linux Steam client, with a Steam Frame headset paired through a USB Wi-Fi adapter and out of reach, tries to join the Frame's network every 61 s. Each attempt goes through NetworkManager, sits in configuration for about 25 s, fails, and drops back. The client handles the attempt and the failure on a thread that also answers its IPC, so for a few hundred milliseconds at each (about 600 ms at an attempt, which also starts a Wi-Fi scan, and about 300 ms at a failure) any call a game makes into it waits. Under Proton the game's `steam_api64.dll` reaches the native client through Proton's `lsteamclient` bridge and the client's IPC pipe, so the game thread's first Steam call in that stretch blocks. That gives two stalls per 61 s cycle, about 26 and 35 s apart, on wall-clock time even while the game runs in slow motion. Only the events that meet a Steam call from the game stall it.

To recognise it: the stalls sit in `sim_steam`, the Steam platform system's update ([frame-timing.md](../mod/performance/frame-timing.md)), and each writes `online: the Steam callbacks blocked the game thread` with its duration. Their times match the attempts and failures in the native client's `logs/remote_connections.txt` and `logs/client_networkmanager.txt` (local time, against the session log's UTC). The fix is the player's: unplug the adapter while the Frame is not in use, keep the Frame on and in range, or unpair it in the Steam client. Stalls that remain in `sim_steam` and do not fall on those events have another cause in the client, and timing the dispatched callbacks by id (which needs detours on Proton's `steamclient64.dll` exports) would be the next step.

The mod does not work around it. Moving `SteamAPI_RunCallbacks` to another thread would run game handlers off the game thread, drive the Steam API's dispatch and pipe from two threads, and only move the stall to the game thread's next Steam call. Deferring the callbacks cannot help either: the stretch cannot be seen before a call meets it, and predicting it from the beat would tie the mod to a schedule inside the Steam client.

## The remedy: the offline retry's logged-on check

Before the cause was found, the stalls sat in the online-suite manager, on its one untimed per-tick call. While the [`LoginManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginManager) is [`LoggedIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginState##variant-LoggedIn) in the [`Offline`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginMode##variant-Offline) mode and the online suite has not been reached, [`OnlineSuiteManager::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::OnlineSuiteManager##func-Update) asks [`AccountManager::IsConnectedToFirstPartyService`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::AccountManager##vfunc-IsConnectedToFirstPartyService) on every tick, before it looks at the clock, whether to retry the suite; the five-minute limit applies only to starting the retry. For Steam the check is [`AccountManagerSteam::IsConnectedToFirstPartyService`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::AccountManagerSteam##func-IsConnectedToFirstPartyService), which returns `ISteamUser::BLoggedOn`, a round trip into the Steam client of about 0.16 ms on every tick. Skipping it moved the stalls to `sim_steam` unchanged, which is how the cause was found, so the check had only been the first Steam call to meet them.

The skip stays, to save that round trip. A detour on the Steam account's check (`crates/jc3vr-gameplay/src/hooks/online.rs`) answers false without calling Steam when the call comes from the manager's retry, told apart by the game thread being inside the manager's update and outside the online suite's own. The switch is in the debug UI under Previews and performance, beside the frame breakdown, and is on by default (`online.skip_logged_on_check`). `sim_logged_on` times the call site either way.

The account manager's other callers keep calling Steam: the title screen's choice between an online and an offline login, the login-state change out of logged out, the reconnect from the pause menu and the comm link (`CAccountManager::TryReconnectOnline`), the Steam account's login completion, the Steam achievements' request for the statistics, and the pause menu's DLC store items. What the player loses is the automatic retry: a session that logged in offline, because the suite or the network was unreachable at the title screen or because the player chose offline, no longer probes the suite every five minutes, so it stays offline (without the Square Enix Membership rewards and the online statistics and brags) until the game restarts. Steam, the achievements, and the store are unaffected, and a session that logged in online never runs the check.
