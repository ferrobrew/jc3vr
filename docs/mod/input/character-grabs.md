# Taking hold of characters

An empty hand can take hold of a character. A grip on a standing character throws them into their ragdoll at once, and the hand holds the ragdoll by the bone nearest the palm, so they hang from the hand, to be dragged and thrown with the scoped ragdoll strength. The hold joins the hand's body to the bone, and the hold writes back the character's ragdolling timers every tick so the game's get-up does not stand them up out of the grip; let go and they lie where they fall, and get up the game's own way once they come to rest. A character the game protects, mission-critical or in a scripted sequence, is not taken at all. A body on the floor, knocked down or dead, is gripped by the limb nearest the palm and dragged or lifted with the whole body's weight. The grapple's snatch swings such a body on its line ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md#swinging-on-the-line)). A character seated in a vehicle is reached through an open door: grip them and yank, and the player pulls them out of the seat and takes it, the game's own hijack. Through a window the hands have brought down ([breaking-glass.md](breaking-glass.md)), a yank pulls them out of the seat as a ragdoll instead, to be dragged out through the opening. The code is `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/characters/` and the grips in `physical_hands/pickup/grips.rs`, on top of the pickup ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)) and the doors ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md)); the engine side is in [melee-and-damage.md](../../engine/gameplay/melee-and-damage.md#the-hit-reaction), [ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md), and [vehicle-doors.md](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#entering-through-a-door). All are always on under the controller scheme. None of it has yet been seen working in the headset.

## Reaching a character

The pickup's reach rays find characters as well as objects: a hit on a character carries the character and the body hit. A gun in the character's hands or holstered on its body is the weapons' own target and wins over the character whenever it is within its reach, however much nearer the character's capsule is ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-gun-from-a-characters-hands), [A holstered gun](physical-weapons.md#a-holstered-gun)); the hand takes the character only with the gun out of reach. What a hit is depends on the character:

- A character the game protects is never a target, whatever the hit ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#protected-characters), `physics::characters::protection`): one a mission marks as critical to it ([`m_IsMissionCriticalCharacter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_IsMissionCriticalCharacter)), one a scripted sequence drives ([`m_InSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_InSequence)), and the player. The grip's notes say why.
- A character on foot, hit on its proxy's capsule ([`PfxCharacterInstance::GetCharacterRigidBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-GetCharacterRigidBodyId)), is thrown into their ragdoll by the grip, or held on to (below).
- A character that ragdolls ([`Character::IsRagdolling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsRagdolling)), fallen, knocked down, or dead, is taken by a body of its ragdoll ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#the-ragdoll-instance)): the body the ray hit when it is one of the ragdoll's dynamic bodies, and otherwise the one whose centre is nearest the hit, since the ray may meet the capsule or the ragdoll's hit proxy instead (`characters::ragdoll`). It is held through the hand body's joint with the scoped ragdoll strength, so a body on the floor is dragged or lifted by the limb nearest the palm, the rest hanging from it, and thrown by letting go while swinging.
- A dead character that does not ragdoll yet, still in its death animation, is not taken, nor a hit on a body of a standing character's ragdoll, which its animation drives.
- A character attached to a vehicle ([`Character::IsAttachedToVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsAttachedToVehicle)) is a target only when one of that vehicle's doors stands open at least 25° ([`PfxBreakable::GetHingeAngleAboveMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetHingeAngleAboveMin)) with its box's centre within 1.2 m of the palm, or when the hand came in through a window of that vehicle the glass brought down ([through a broken window](#through-a-broken-window)). Intact glass is never a way in.

A ragdoll weighs as a whole. The hand holds one body, and the rest of the ragdoll hangs from it by the ragdoll's constraints. With the hand bodies on, the joint between the hand's body and the bone carries the bone alone (`pickup::tick::joined_mass_kg`): the hand's drive asks for the bone's momentum and holds up the bone's weight, within the hand's own strength, and the rest of the body's weight and inertia reach the joint through the ragdoll's constraints over the physics step, so the body hangs limp from the point gripped and a heavy one sags the hand a little. A drive that asks for the whole body's momentum and weight through the bone overshoots it several times over every step, so the bone rings faster each step until it passes the tear distance and the body is flung or dropped. The hold still records the whole ragdoll's mass (`characters::ragdoll::Ragdoll::mass_kg`) as the held mass, for whether the hands lift it and how it is thrown: a hand without a body drives the bone with its strength spread over the whole ragdoll's weight, with the scoped ragdoll strength (`characters::RAGDOLL_CARRY_STRENGTH`), and a ragdoll within that strength is thrown even while joined.

Whether the player's faction is hostile to the character ([`GetRelation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/faction##func-GetRelation)) is judged at the reach and logged; it decides nothing. The grip, the yank, the blows, and the shove treat every character alike ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#hits)), and so does the game's hit reaction, which does not weigh the faction ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#into-the-ragdoll)).

## Throwing a character into their ragdoll

A grip on a standing character's capsule throws them into their ragdoll as it takes hold (`characters::topples`), when the topple is on, the character is alive, and its state does not keep it out of its ragdoll ([`SB_SKIP_HITREACTION_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_SKIP_HITREACTION_RAGDOLL), under which the game itself never ragdolls a character). The throw is the character's own hit reaction ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)) from the grab point, as a blow without damage of 1.1 times (`characters::TOPPLE_STRENGTH`) the hand blows' knockdown, calibrated against the character's own knockdown threshold, so it falls just past the knockdown rather than flying. It goes along the hand's motion when the hand moves at least 1.5 m/s, and otherwise level toward the hand, so the character folds toward the palm (`characters::topple_direction`). Because it is the game's own knockdown, the game's ragdoll, AI, and get-up handle it as any knockdown: no state of the character is written.

The hand then waits for the ragdoll as after a yank (below): it stays at the grab point, following the falling body, and takes the bone of the ragdoll nearest the palm as soon as its reach finds one (`a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll`), holding it through the hand body's joint with the whole ragdoll's weight. The hit reaction returns without ragdolling in some states the topple does not see (on the 14 November take an ally holding her gun stayed standing through three topples, and fell once disarmed), so a character still standing 0.3 s into the wait has the ragdoll act queued directly, once: the hit reaction's own ragdoll act where the state takes it, else the full ragdoll (`physics::characters::retry_ragdoll`, logged as `the topple left the character standing; retried the ragdoll` with whether an act was queued). The character hangs from the hand by that bone. Letting go leaves the ragdoll to the game: once it comes to rest, or once the ragdoll's own timer runs out, the animation graph's get-up stands the character up, and its AI carries on ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#out-of-the-ragdoll)). A dead character stays down.

## Holding the get-up off

The get-up does not wait for the hand: on the 14 November take it fired about six seconds after a topple while two hands held the soldier, the ragdoll's bodies went back under the character's control, both joints ended (`the constraint or the object's body went away`), and she stood up out of the grip. The graph stands a character up on its ragdolling timers, the total time ragdolling and the time settled ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#out-of-the-ragdoll)), and a held body counts as settled once its limbs move together, which the damping of a held ragdoll within itself hastens. The game's cancel input keeps only the player's own ragdoll one, so the hold does not press it.

The timers sit in the character's control parameters ([`RagdollingControllerParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::RagdollingControllerParams)). So on each pickup tick while a hand holds a character by its ragdoll, once per character however many hands hold it, the hold writes the total ragdolling time below zero and the settled time to zero (`characters::get_up`, `pickup::held_ragdolls`). Below zero is the value the ragdolling state task keeps while the ragdoll's owner moves it, which the timer condition never holds on, whichever way its data compares; a settled time of zero keeps the settled condition from holding. The game's update between two pickup ticks advances them by one frame at most. Let go, and the timers run again from there. Whether the graph has a get-up path that reads neither timer is unverified.

Each hold of a ragdoll that ends is logged with what the character does next: `physical hands: characters: the game's get-up ended a hand's hold of a ragdoll` at warn level when it is getting up ([`SB_IS_GETTING_UP_FROM_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_GETTING_UP_FROM_RAGDOLL)) or already stands, and `a hand's hold of a ragdoll ended` otherwise, both with the time held, whether the hold wrote the timers, and the loss. The first write of a hold logs `holding a held character's ragdolling timers off its get-up`. A get-up warning with `timers_held=true` says the graph stands the character up by another path.

A character dragged, swung, and thrown this way can be hurt or killed by what its ragdoll hits, if the game's ragdoll impacts harm a ragdolling character as they appear to; the topple itself does no damage.

## Holding on and yanking

For a character whose state keeps it on its feet, a grip on a character's capsule holds the hand at the grab point, carried with the character's own frame, the same way a latched door holds it ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-latch)): a driven hand's body is held there, a probed hand drawn there. Pulling the controller 20 cm (`characters::YANK_M`) away from the grab point, in any direction, is a yank, and ends the grip.

A yank on a character on foot pushes them along the yank through their own hit reaction ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)), as a blow of 1.3 times (`characters::YANK_STRENGTH`) the hand blows' knockdown (`HAND_BODIES.hits.knockdown_impulse_ns`), calibrated against the character's own knockdown threshold as a blow is, and without damage. The default reaches past a knockdown, so a character falls as a ragdoll pushed along the yank, unless its own thresholds or state keep it standing. After a yank, the hand stays at the grab point for up to a second of game time (`characters::REGRIP_S`), so slow motion stretches it with the fall, following the falling body, and as soon as its reach finds a body of that character's ragdoll it takes hold of it (`a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll`); if none comes within reach in time it lets go.

Letting go of a character on foot, without a yank, with the hand moving at least 1.5 m/s (`characters::SHOVE_MIN_SPEED_MPS`), shoves them along the hand's motion: a blow without damage of an arm of `HAND_BODIES.hits.blow_mass_kg` at the hand's speed, under the same rules.

The reactions are queued from the palms' tick and dealt on the next frame tick, on the game thread outside the game's update, where the blows are dealt ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#threading)); the character is found again from the body the hand held, so a character freed since is never touched.

A yank on a seated character, through a door that is still open beside the hand, has the next input tick click the vehicle-entry action ([`ENTER_VEHICLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-ENTER_VEHICLE)), as the use button does on foot. The game then takes the player into the vehicle by the entry nearest the player, and an occupied seat is hijacked: the occupant is thrown out by the game's own animation. A yank after the door has swung shut does nothing but let go.

A mounted gun's gunner is not hijacked: the vehicle-entry action would seat the player in another seat and leave the gunner where they are. A yank on a character who mans a mounted gun, in a vehicle's gunner seat or on a standalone tripod ([`Character::IsInMountedGunState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInMountedGunState); the standing path's ragdoll never takes a tripod's gunner off the gun, as four yanks on one on the 14 November take showed), queues the full ragdoll ([`ACT_FULL_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable)) once the seat's state accepts it (`physics::characters::gunner_out`), since the hit reaction never ragdolls a character attached to a vehicle ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)). The gunner falls out of the seat where they sit, and the hand takes hold of the ragdoll as it does after any yank, to be carried or set down. A gunner the game protects, or whose seat takes no full ragdoll, stays, and the hand lets go; the log says which. Stepping into the emptied seat is in [vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md#the-gunner-seat).

## Through a broken window

A window the hands have shattered is hidden, not removed: its glass shapes still answer ray queries, and the physical hands' casts step past a pane the glass recorded as brought down ([breaking-glass.md](breaking-glass.md#bullets-through-a-pane-brought-down)). The glass has no collision with the hands either way, so a hand goes through intact and broken windows alike, and whether it came in through a broken one is judged by casting along its way in (`characters::pane`): from the eyes the camera renders from to the palm, the leg a hand already in the cabin crossed, and on from the palm to the point the reach found on the occupant (`physical_hands::query::crosses_hidden_pane`). A leg that passes a brought-down pane whose hit names the occupant's vehicle, before anything solid, is a way in. The casts run only once the glass has brought a pane down, and only for an enclosed occupant with no open door beside the hand, since a door is preferred. So, standing on the hood with the windshield shattered, a hand reaching down through it reaches the driver, and one reaching through the roof does not.

A grip taken this way keeps the seat for as long as the character keeps it in the same vehicle: the pane stays down, and the hand, now in the cabin, is not judged again. The readout says `the character sits behind a window the glass brought down`.

A yank on an occupant reached through a broken window does not hijack. It takes them out of the seat as a ragdoll where they sit, by the full ragdoll ([`ACT_FULL_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable)), queued once the seat's state accepts it, as for a mounted gun's gunner (`characters::unseat`, `physics::characters::seat_out`), and the hand takes hold of the ragdoll as after any yank, to drag through the opening: `physical hands: characters: a hand pulled an occupant out of the seat through a broken window`. An occupant the game protects, or whose seat takes no full ragdoll, stays, and the hand lets go: `an occupant reached through a broken window stays in the seat` with the reason. Which seat states besides a gunner's take the full ragdoll is unverified.

A punch through a broken window is treated as a punch on a motorcycle rider. The hand bodies' blows already strike a seated character they meet, but the game's hit reaction never ragdolls a character attached to a vehicle, so a knockdown on a rider queues the full ragdoll in its place. A punch on an enclosed occupant whose hand came in through a window of that vehicle the glass brought down, judged by the cast from the eyes to the hand at the contact, does the same (`physics::blows::exposed_seat`): a blow at knockdown strength that the game did not refuse knocks them out of the seat as a ragdoll, logged as `hand bodies: a punch through a broken window knocked an occupant out of the seat`, and the blow's line carries `rider_reaction_queued`.

## Getting in

With a door open, the use button enters the vehicle as it always does, by the entry nearest the player, so the side whose door the hand opened is the side the player gets in by. The entry plays the game's own door animation; a hand still holding that door lets go when the player sits down, since the hands stand down in a vehicle.

## When the grip lets go

A grip on a character lets go with the grip, with a yank (unless it waits for the ragdoll), when the hand stands down, and when the character's capsule no longer belongs to the character (gone or replaced). A hold on a ragdoll's body lets go as any hold does.

## Settings and readouts

Character grabs have no settings: the grab, the topple, the get-up hold-off, and the hijack are always on under the controller scheme. The yank, the regrip, the shove, and the hijack's door opening and reach are constants in `physical_hands::characters`. The debug UI's Characters and doors feature opens with the `CHARACTERS` status row (under `HANDS IN THE WORLD`), which counts the grips, the characters thrown down by a topple or a yank, the knockdowns of the yanks and shoves, the ragdolls dragged, and the occupants pulled out. Beneath it, the readout says when a hand reaches a seated or a standing character and behind which door.

The log has, at info level, `physical hands: characters: a hand took hold of a character` with whether they are seated, `a hand's grip threw a character into their ragdoll` with the hostility and the direction, `a hand yanked a character` with whether the player is hostile to them, `a hand threw a character` with whether it was a yank, the hostility, the share of a knockdown, and the reaction impulse given, `physical hands: characters: a hand took hold of a ragdoll` with the body, its own and the whole ragdoll's mass, the hostility, and whether it is joined, `a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll` with the body, the whole ragdoll's mass, and whether it is joined, `a hand's grip on a character could not be placed` with whether the body still belonged to the character, `no ragdoll came within the hand's reach after a yank`, `physical hands: hijack: a hand pulled an occupant out through the open door` with the door, its opening, and the yank's direction, and `the door beside the hand closed before the yank`.

## Unverified in the headset

- That the reach rays hit a character's capsule and its ragdoll's bodies, and that a ragdoll's bodies are owned by the character; if not, `a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll` never appears and the hand gives up after the regrip time. The one grip on a character in the session of 2 November found a character in reach and did not take hold, with no reason logged; the grip's refusal is now logged.
- That a settled corpse's bodies wake when the hand's joint or drive moves them.
- That a hand held at a standing character's grab point pushes against their capsule acceptably, and how the character's own capsule listener reacts to it.
- That the topple's strength knocks every ordinary character down without throwing them far, and that the hand's reach finds the falling ragdoll within the regrip time.
- Whether queuing the ragdoll again holds the get-up off while a hand holds the ragdoll ([holding the get-up off](#holding-the-get-up-off)): if not, the bone stops being dynamic, the hold ends, and `the game's get-up ended a hand's hold of a ragdoll` says so.
- That a character let go after a topple gets up and resumes what it was doing: a soldier back to fighting, a civilian back to walking or fleeing.
- Whether a ragdoll swung into walls is hurt or killed by the impacts.
- That a yank at the default strength knocks a soldier down.
- That the reach rays hit a seated character through an open door, and that a character in a vehicle reports itself attached to it.
- That a clicked vehicle entry by the open door hijacks the driver's seat, rather than entering a passenger seat or doing nothing, and how it behaves with the player's hand still holding the door.
- How a hand body held at a point inside the cabin behaves against the seats and the vehicle's body.
- That the use button's entry by an open door picks that door's seat.
- That a car seat's state takes the full ragdoll, so a yank or a punch through a broken window unseats the occupant, and that the ragdoll comes free of the cabin through the opening rather than catching on it.
- That a vehicle's glass hits name the vehicle as their object, as the bullet's glass handling assumes.

## Headset checklist

With `GRIP MARKERS` on:

1. Reach toward a standing soldier's collar: a violet marker. Grip: `a hand took hold of a character`, then `a hand's grip threw a character into their ragdoll` and `a hand threw a character` with a strength just above one. The soldier folds toward the hand, and `a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll` follows within a second: the soldier hangs from the hand by the collar or shoulder.
2. Drag the soldier a few steps, lift and swing, then let go while swinging: the body flies on, heavy. Watch it come to rest: the soldier gets up and fights again.
3. Grip a civilian the same way: they go down and hang from the hand too. Let go and step back: they get up and walk or flee.
4. Hold a toppled soldier up off the ground for ten seconds, then lay them down still in the hand for ten more: `holding a held character's ragdolling timers off its get-up`, and the soldier stays a ragdoll until let go, with no `the game's get-up ended a hand's hold of a ragdoll`. If the get-up still fires, note its `held_s` and `timers_held`. Then let go: `a hand's hold of a ragdoll ended` with `the character still ragdolls`, and the soldier gets up a few seconds later.
5. During a mission, reach toward a mission character (an escort, a rebel leader): no marker, and the grip's notes say `critical to a mission`. In a scripted scene, `in a scripted sequence`.
6. Beside a car stopped with a driver, open the driver's door by hand ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#headset-checklist)) past the hijack's opening. Reach in toward the driver's shoulder: a violet ring, and the readout says the character sits behind the front door. With the door shut, no ring.
7. Grip the driver, then yank back: `a hand pulled an occupant out through the open door`, the game's hijack plays, and the player ends in the driver's seat.
8. Open a door of an empty car by hand and press the use button: the player gets in on that side.
9. Knock a soldier down or kill one, and reach toward the body on the floor: the grip press's notes say `a character's ragdoll ... in reach`. Grip a wrist: `a hand took hold of a ragdoll` with a whole-ragdoll mass of several tens of kilograms, and the arm comes up first, the body dragging behind. Lift with one hand: the body barely leaves the floor; with both hands on it, it comes up further. Swing and let go: the body slides on rather than flying.
10. Grip a corpse at the ankle and drag it backward a few metres: it follows, heavy, without tearing from the hand.
11. Topple a soldier and hold them by the collar for five seconds while moving the hand slowly: the body hangs from the hand and follows it, limp, and is neither flung nor dropped. Read the `held` reports: the bone's speed stays near the hand's, with no growing back-and-forth, and no `torn from the hand` while the hand stays within reach.
12. Stand on the hood of a car with a driver, shatter the windshield by hand, and reach down through it toward the driver: a violet ring, and the readout says `the character sits behind a window the glass brought down`. With the windshield intact, no ring and the grip's notes say `seated with no open door or broken window beside the hand`. Grip and yank back: `a hand pulled an occupant out of the seat through a broken window`, the driver goes limp in the seat, and the hand takes the ragdoll; drag them out over the hood. Then punch a passenger through a shattered side window hard: `a punch through a broken window knocked an occupant out of the seat`.
