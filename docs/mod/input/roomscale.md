# Posture and roomscale

The player's body in the room: the body dimensions, the STAGE floor, the seated and standing postures, the head height remap, crouching, teleports and recenters, the head and comfort fades, and the seat transitions. The body following the head about the room is the body rig's ([rig.md](../body/rig.md)); this page is what surrounds it. The code is `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/roomscale/`, `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/body/`, and `crates/jc3vr-view/src/vr/stage.rs`, with the settings under `controllers.roomscale` and `controllers.body`.

These parts do not depend on the controller scheme, because a gamepad player standing in a room moves the head the same way. They act only while the headset owns the head pose. The head is the ground truth and the body follows it, and the camera is never derived from the body's motion, so the view stays one to one with the real head ([head-and-body.md](../body/head-and-body.md)).

## Body dimensions

The player's dimensions are the `[body]` section of the settings file ([settings.md](../settings.md)), in metres, written by hand or by the calibration on the pause menu's `VR: BODY` page ([pause-menu.md](../pause-menu.md#body-calibration)). A missing field takes the generic adult value (`BodyDimensions::GENERIC_ADULT`), an unknown one is logged and skipped, and dimensions that cannot describe a body (`BodyDimensions::validate`) reset to the generic adult's. The optional `seated_eye_height` is the head's height above the STAGE floor while the player sits upright; without it the key is left out of the file. Rico's proportions are an estimate from his stated height (`BodyDimensions::CHARACTER_ESTIMATE`), except his arms and shoulders, which the hands measure from his skeleton ([hands-and-aiming.md, "Arm reach"](hands-and-aiming.md#arm-reach)).

The calibration measures the standing (or seated) eye height and the distance between the controllers' grip poses in a T-pose. `controllers/body/calibration/derive.rs` derives the rest with the Drillis and Contini segment ratios: the height from the eye height; the arm span from the grip span plus the part of each hand beyond the grip; the shoulders and hands scaled with the height; and the upper arm and forearm splitting what the span leaves. Seated, the height is estimated from the arm span, which is about the height for most adults, and the player can correct it. An eye height outside a standing adult's range is refused, as is a span too short for the height or longer than any adult's. The values are validated before the file is written, through a temporary file so a failed write leaves the old one whole, and apply at once.

The remap is a set of pure functions: the hands' offsets from the shoulders scale by the ratio of the arms (`controllers.body.remap_hands`, on), and the eye height and vertical head travel by the ratio of the heights (`controllers.body.remap_head`, on).

## The STAGE floor

When the runtime offers a STAGE reference space, the session creates one beside LOCAL. Once per frame the mid-eye pose is located against STAGE, which gives the head's height above the floor, and the play area's bounds are read again on every reference-space change (logged as `stage: play area bounds` when they change). The eye views, the controllers, and the compositor layer stay in LOCAL, so a runtime without STAGE loses only the floor height.

A runtime may move the STAGE floor when it fires a reference-space change (Monado has dropped it by a third of a metre in a burst of them), which would put every standing measure off by as much. So for the view tuning's `stage.floor_watch_ms` after each change, a step in the head's height between two frames larger than `stage.max_head_step_m` (more than a head moves in a frame) is taken to be the floor's and taken back out of the height every consumer reads (`FloorWatch`), logged as `stage: the floor jumped across a reference space change`. The shift lasts for the session.

## Posture

The posture (`controllers.roomscale.posture`) is `Auto`, `Seated`, or `Standing` ([rig.md, "Posture"](../body/rig.md#posture)). Seated, the seated eye reference maps to Rico's standing eye height, the body follows the head only by the upper body, and a downward flick of the dominant stick toggles a crouch. Standing, the floor gives the true height, crouching is physical, and the body follows the head about the room.

OpenXR has no signal for a seated user, so `Auto` tells them apart by the head's height above the floor (`roomscale/posture.rs`, `PostureClassifier`), with thresholds between the seated eye height (the saved one, or the roomscale tuning's `posture.seated_eye_fraction` of the standing one) and the standing eye height. Until a head height is located the player counts as seated, because a seated treatment never forces a crouch, while a standing one turns a seated player into a crouching stander. The first located height and the height at a manual recenter classify at once against the midpoint. After that a seated player stands once the head stays above `posture.stand_fraction` of the way to the standing height for `posture.stand_after_s`, which a seated player cannot reach without standing up, and a standing player sits once the head stays below `posture.sit_fraction` for `posture.sit_after_s`, long so a crouch behind cover stays a physical crouch. The head must also stay steady within `posture.steady_band_m` and above the floor level (the seated eye height less `posture.floor_margin_fraction` of the standing one): a standing player whose head goes lower has got down onto the floor, and stays standing so the head keeps its true height. A crouch held steady longer than the wait is the classifier's known failure, which the explicit postures avoid. The controllers' heights are not used, because a crouching player's hands sit as low as a seated one's. Each change logs `roomscale: the automatic posture's detection changes`.

## Head height

Standing with a floor, the true eye height is remapped onto Rico by the ratio of the heights, and the view's vertical cockpit offset is the remapped height less Rico's standing eye height, which is where the animated head anchor already puts the eyes. The player's standing eye height in the remap is the one the body rig measures ([rig.md, "The pelvis solver"](../body/rig.md#the-pelvis-solver)), seeded at the calibration and held near it, because most players stand a few centimetres below their calibrated height once relaxed and would otherwise see from Rico's collar.

Seated with a floor, the head's travel from the seated eye reference (`roomscale/seated.rs`) scales by the height ratio. A single calibrated height belongs to one chair and one way of sitting, so the reference follows the player: it starts at the saved `seated_eye_height` (or the first seated frame's height), a manual recenter while seated captures it, and the adaptive baseline (the roomscale tuning's `seated` section) moves it slowly toward the median of a window of head heights whenever they differ by more than a deadband, so a lean or a duck held for less than half the window stays one to one. Without a floor, the baseline is the eye height at the last recenter. With `remap_head` off, the offset is the raw cockpit height.

The seated crouch drops the seated eyes by `posture.seated_crouch_drop_m`, only on foot and out of a vehicle seat: in a seat it would drop the view into the footwell, and under a canopy, in the wingsuit, or swimming into Rico's torso. It keeps its state for when Rico is back on foot, and acts only in the seated posture (including a seated `Auto`), because standing players crouch physically.

The camera height readout (`crates/jc3vr-gameplay/src/controllers/camera_height.rs`, at the top of Body/tracking > Posture) diagnoses a view that sits too low or too high: it records the camera's eye point and Rico's animated eye midpoint, both measured up from the character's root, beside the cockpit's vertical offset, the remap's inputs, the posture's decision, the seated reference, and the crouch states, and logs them at most every five seconds while Rico is on foot or seated. With a zero offset the two heights agree, so a low view with a small offset points at Rico's animated eyes, and a large negative one at the remap or the seated reference. It only reads.

## Crouching

The eyes' drop below Rico's standing eye height, after the remap, marks the character crouched once it passes the roomscale tuning's `crouch.enter_drop_m`, and standing once it is back above `crouch.exit_drop_m`. Standing on the ground the drop is the crouch the body rig measures, the head's drop that no bend at the waist explains, so a look at the floor or a lean over a table does not count ([rig.md, "Posture"](../body/rig.md#posture)).

While crouched, the simulation half requests the game's crouching proxy state ([`ProxyState::CROUCHING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::ProxyState##variant-CROUCHING)), whose shortened capsule every character has ([root-motion-and-proxy.md, "Capsule dimensions and runtime resize"](../../engine/character/root-motion-and-proxy.md#capsule-dimensions-and-runtime-resize)), through the pending proxy state ([`Character::m_PendingProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_PendingProxyState)), which the engine applies on the simulation thread. It asks only while the pending state is the default ([`Character::m_DefaultProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_DefaultProxyState)) or already the crouch, so the game's own states (swimming, the parachute, vehicles, ragdoll) win. When the head rises, the request is released only once a ray up to the top of a standing character finds headroom, and it is released at once when the locomotion task stops or the headset stops driving the view.

## The split between frame and tick

The frame half runs once per rendered VR frame, from the pose composition on the game thread: it resolves the posture, remaps the head height, hands the cockpit to the rig for its shift ([rig.md, "The bookkeeping"](../body/rig.md#the-bookkeeping)), tracks the seat phase, advances the fade, and publishes the posture, the crouch, and the seat phase. The simulation half is the rig's root hook ([rig.md, "Who moves the root"](../body/rig.md#who-moves-the-root)), which also steps the crouch capsule; the head probe runs on every simulation tick in every state ([rig.md, "Walls"](../body/rig.md#walls)).

## Teleports

A post-call detour on [`GameCameraManager::ResetCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-ResetCamera) counts a teleport, because the game resets the camera after every discontinuity it makes to the player (a teleport, a respawn, a load, a playlist commit). A move that bypasses the game world, such as a vehicle teleport carrying its driver, is caught by a per-tick test: a displacement further than the rig tuning's `root.warp_min_m` and than `root.warp_speed_factor` times what the proxy's speed covers in the tick. On a teleport the head probe forgets its last clear position and the rig starts its bookkeeping over ([rig.md, "Teleports and seat exits"](../body/rig.md#teleports-and-seat-exits)).

## Recenters

What a recenter re-bases is the rig's ([rig.md, "Recentering"](../body/rig.md#recentering)). A manual recenter (F7 or the stick-click chord, [bindings.md, "Chords"](bindings.md#chords)) also classifies the automatic posture at once and, while seated, captures the seated eye reference. The seat transitions request their own re-bases (`vr::request_seat_recenter`), which do neither, because they happen on the game's schedule rather than the player's.

## The head fade

`controllers.roomscale.head_fade.enabled` (on). On every simulation tick in gameplay, in every state, the rig's head probe casts from the last position the head held in the open to where it is now ([rig.md, "Walls"](../body/rig.md#walls)), and a hit means geometry lies between them; a second cast from the animated head, which the capsule keeps in the open, forgives a stale last position. The casts use the player aim's filter, because a plain query can hit the player's own ragdoll and hit-proxy bodies. The head's depth past the hit sets the fade, clear up to the rig tuning's `probe.head_fade_start_m` and black from `probe.head_fade_full_m`, and a terrain check adds the eyes' depth below the heightfield. Seated in a vehicle the fade is held clear, because the head leans into cockpit glass and cabin trim the probe reads as walls.

## The comfort fade

The head fade, the rig's push-back past its cap ([rig.md, "Walls"](../body/rig.md#walls)), and the seat transitions each set a target darkness. The level eases toward the darkest target at the roomscale tuning's `fade` rates, and the VR blit scales each eye's image by one minus the level as it copies the image into the swapchain. The engine's own screen fade is not used, because the game drives it for its own transitions and a second writer would fight them; the flat mirror is unchanged. A gap in the rendered frames resets the fade.

## Seat transitions

The game enters vehicles through animations in which Rico crosses metres of world, opens doors, and hijacks drivers, so under a head-anchored camera an entry is a forced camera movement that ends at an arbitrary yaw. The game's vehicle predicates do not separate an entry, a locked seat, and an exit, so the seat phase (entering, locked, shuffling, exiting) is classified from the body rule system's current state by name (`controllers::roomscale::seat`), hashed at startup with the engine's state-name hash. A state in none of the tables counts as out of the vehicle, and one seen while the character still has a vehicle is logged. The step drive is off in a seat. `controllers.roomscale.seat_transition` picks what each edge does:

- `Ride` (the default) lets the view ride the animations and, when the seat locks, re-bases only the cockpit's yaw, by the head's heading against the body at the latest follow bank ([rig.md, "The body turning under the view"](../body/rig.md#the-body-turning-under-the-view)), so a look off the body when it stopped following does not carry into the seat as a look off the vehicle's axis. The re-base is part of the entry, a movement the player took, turns by at most the yaw's turn angle, and eases over the view tuning's `recenter.seat_yaw_blend_s` as a run of small re-bases, so the hands and the cockpit controls turn with the view; a bank older than `recenter.seat_yaw_bank_window_ms` is not used.
- `Instant` darkens the view during the entry, clears it and re-bases fully at the lock, shows the exit live, and re-bases yaw and height once the character is out.
- `Fade` does the same, darkens the exit too, and holds the view dark for the roomscale tuning's `seat.fade_hold_s` after the lock and after the exit so the re-based view settles first.

A shuffle between seats stays locked and does not re-base. The re-base at the lock is needed because the seated head bone sits lower than the standing one and elsewhere, so the standing cockpit offset is wrong once seated.

Body/tracking > Posture shows the posture, the STAGE space and bounds, the head's height, the remap, the crouch states, the teleports, the head probe, the fade, and the seat phase, with a button that toggles the seated crouch.
