## New problems

1. Stows refused by the animation drop the gun. 88 `hold: the animation forbids it` refusals came in 14 bursts. In 11 of them the attraction drew the gun in, `Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsHolstering` refused the dock on every tick for 0.2 to 0.35 s, and the gun fell. One burst (t 3935.55 to 3935.69) docked after six refusals. Nine of the 11 were shoulder stows, two of them the Hydra to the left shoulder. In the previous take the refusal kept the gun in the hand (`StowFallback::Keep`) until the hip took it. The attraction's path (`hold::act::attract` refusing at `start_dock`) has no such keep, so the release retries and then lets go. The grips were fully open throughout (controller squeeze 0.0 at 2124.73 and 4214.55), so these weren't regrip cancels. What animation state forbids holstering isn't logged; nearby were a fired shot 1 to 4 s earlier (5 of 11), a gun taken or drawn 1.5 to 2 s earlier (3), or nothing (860.08, 1033.45, 2077.26).
2. The attraction starts and the gun still falls. At t 2099.72, 2169.87, 3218.65, 3322.19, 3509.34, and 3513.69 the socket drew the gun in (`drew in the weapon`, travel 0.16 to 0.34 s), and the gun was let go into the world 0.06 to 0.2 s later. No refusal was logged at info level. In four of them the controller grip was at 0.0 from before the release to after the drop (2169.87, 3218.65, 3322.19, 3513.69); at 3509.34 the left grip closed again to 0.78. The hand speeds at the release were 0.55 to 1.7 m/s (controller grip, the same measure as the docks'), inside the docks' own range. My suspicion is that `finish.rs` reruns `release()` once the snap is gone (`retry_release`), and a rerun that finds the hand outside the attraction lets go. Which step dropped them only logs at debug level, as in the previous take.
3. A refused planting loses the charge. At t 906.38, 1939.30, and 2100.35 a thrown or pressed charge got `c4 pouch outcome=refused: planting is not allowed now` and then left the loadout (`the object is gone`). Nothing was placed: the next detonator presses found no charges (1946.6 to 1949.7, 2106.8 to 2108.0). At 1939.3 the frames show the hand inside a parked jet's fuselage with a red ring. At t 1075.58, 1532.36, and 2933.90 the same refusal met a pouch press, and no charge came out, each 0.5 to 1.0 s after the stunt clip modifiers were restored (a landing).
4. Detonations that didn't happen. After the empty pouch handed out the detonator at t 574.29, ten presses logged `detonates charges=3` 0.33 s apart, none gated as "going off", and the charges on the jeep stayed intact in the frames through 577.2. Every other detonation in the take was followed by the "going off" gate on the next press. The player was dying at the time, which may be why the game didn't start the chain; the mod logged a detonation regardless.
5. Belt pushes with the magazine in did nothing. Nine `the Right gun's belt push could not drop its magazine` (t 1110.24 to 1118.80 five times, 2161.57, 2167.32, 3918.57, 4114.28). At 1110 the pistol was full or one round down. After the push the gun counted as "in the belt pouch", and three trigger pulls at 1121.18 to 1122.46 fired nothing for that reason.
6. Allied characters don't ragdoll but are logged as if they did (ragdoll check above, t 3256 to 3261).

Suspected:
- The extra detonator presses during the game's 1.2 s chain suggest the player can't tell the press worked. Each one buzzes "empty".
- The Hydra's second rocket stops the burst at the same point every time, with no game reload logged; the next step is to log what throws the pod.
