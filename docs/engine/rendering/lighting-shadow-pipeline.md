# JC3 per-frame sun-shadow and global-lighting pipeline

How the game computes and stages its per-frame sun-shadow and global-lighting state: the `cb0` GlobalConstants the material shaders read, where each value comes from, and which are selected by a frame counter or parity, the values that legitimately differ frame to frame. The pyxis defs hold every address and offset. `rendering §N` cites [`rendering.md`](rendering.md), the frame pipeline; the shader side is in [`shaders.md`](shaders.md).

## 1. The frame counters

Three engine-global counters and one per-context stamp select frame-varying state. The three counters live in [`render_frame_counters`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::render_frame_counters) (a [`RenderFrameCounters`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderFrameCounters)), advanced once per real frame in the [`GraphicsEngine::Draw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::GraphicsEngine##func-Draw) prologue (rendering §1.3):

| Counter | Advance | Selects |
|---|---|---|
| [`m_Counter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderFrameCounters##field-m_Counter) | `m_FrameIndex = m_Counter++` — post-increment each frame | The rolling counter, read whole: the shadow-cascade amortization (§3.1) and the light triple buffer (§6). |
| [`m_FrameIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderFrameCounters##field-m_FrameIndex) | takes the pre-increment `m_Counter` | The parity `m_FrameIndex & 1`: the shadow atlas double buffer (§3), the TAA jitter phase (rendering §5.7), and the shadow-matrix storage stride. |
| [`m_RingIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderFrameCounters##field-m_RingIndex) | `m_FrameIndex % 3` | The three-slot constant-buffer / light-lookup ring. |
| [`RenderContext::m_RenderFrameNo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_RenderFrameNo) | copied from `m_FrameIndex` in `SetupRenderContext` | The per-dispatch stamp the draw side keys shadow parity on; equal to `m_FrameIndex`. |

The shadow simulation writes its parity storage by `m_Counter & 1` (in `ShadowManager::UpdateRender`), and the draw side reads by `m_FrameIndex & 1`. They agree because the sim update runs in `CGameStateRun::UpdateShadows`, before the `Draw` prologue: at sim time `m_Counter == C`, and the prologue then sets `m_FrameIndex = C`.

[`RenderPass::SetupRenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_pass::RenderPass##func-SetupRenderContext) also stamps the context's ring indices: [`RenderContext::m_RenderFrameIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_RenderFrameIndex) (`m_FrameIndex % 3`) and [`RenderContext::m_PreviousRenderFrameIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_PreviousRenderFrameIndex) (`(m_FrameIndex + 2) % 3`). It then copies the live per-frame light and sun values into the context: the point and spot light counts, the light manager's global light direction and diffuse colour (`LightManager::m_GlobalLightDiffuse` into [`RenderContext::m_GlobalLightDiffuse`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_GlobalLightDiffuse)), the sun vector ([`RenderEngine::m_SunVector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_SunVector) into [`RenderContext::m_SunVector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_SunVector)), and the time-of-day light modulator ([`LightManager::m_TimeOfDayLightModulator`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##field-m_TimeOfDayLightModulator) into [`RenderContext::m_TimeOfDayEmissiveScale`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_TimeOfDayEmissiveScale)).

## 2. Cast of state owners

- [`LightManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager) (a singleton), the scene light gather: the global light's direction and diffuse colour, the environment and VFX light intensity scales, and the frame's visible point and spot lights in a buffered update list ([`LightManager::CopyLightsToUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-CopyLightsToUpdate), §6).
- [`ShadowManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager) (through [`GraphicsEngine::m_ShadowManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::GraphicsEngine##field-m_ShadowManager)), the cascaded sun shadows (§3), enabled by [`shadow_system_enabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::shadow_system_enabled).
- [`RenderEngine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine) (a singleton): the GlobalConstants staging blocks and the time-of-day sun and fog scalars.
- [`RenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext) ([`GraphicsEngine::m_RenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::GraphicsEngine##field-m_RenderContext)), the per-dispatch view state the passes fill and read.

## 3. Sun-shadow simulation (sim phase)

The sun shadows are a cascaded atlas ([`ShadowManager::m_AtlasTexture`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_AtlasTexture), a depth `Texture2DArray`), parity double-buffered: each shadow frustum's [`CascadeData::m_RenderPassSettings`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::CascadeData##field-m_RenderPassSettings) holds one entry per frame parity naming the atlas slices its passes draw into, so alternate frames render into (and the shaders sample) different slices of the array.

### 3.1 `SetActiveShadowPassCount`: the amortization schedule

[`ShadowManager::SetActiveShadowPassCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##func-SetActiveShadowPassCount) is called from `CGameStateRun::UpdateShadows` before the fit. It sets the active cascade and spot-shadow count and rebuilds the update schedule. Each cascade carries an update level in [`m_CascadeUpdateLevels`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_CascadeUpdateLevels) (by default the nearest every frame and each further one half as often), and refreshes this frame only when `((1 << level) - 1) & m_Counter == 0`, otherwise carrying its previous fit forward. The gate is on the whole `m_Counter`, so which cascades are fresh cycles with period `2^level`, independently of the parity double buffer.

### 3.2 `UpdateRender`: the per-frame fit

[`ShadowManager::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##func-UpdateRender) runs once per sim frame and writes parity `m_Counter & 1`. Against the active camera ([`CameraManager::m_ActiveCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_manager::CameraManager##field-m_ActiveCamera), whose `m_ProjectionF` gives the fit frustum), per scheduled cascade, it:

- computes the cascade's world frustum (`CFrustum::Compute`) and calls `UpdateCascade` to fit the slice;
- writes the **parity-indexed** cascade box params and per-cascade blend/scale into [`m_ShadowCascades[parity]`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_ShadowCascades), a pair of per-parity cascade reference vectors, and the **per-slice shadow projection matrices** into [`m_ShadowTextureMatrix[parity][slice]`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_ShadowTextureMatrix). The reference vectors are `m_ActiveTessellationOrigin` and `m_PendingTessellationOrigin`, which are not yet located;
- leaves unscheduled cascades (§3.1) with the prior parity's fit;
- finally regenerates the cull planes (`GenerateCullPlanes`).

The shadow manager thus holds two parity copies of every cascade's transform and box parameters: the half just written and the previous frame's, and an amortized cascade leaves even the fresh half with an older fit.

### 3.3 `CommitRenderPassSettings`: the per-dispatch gate

[`ShadowManager::CommitRenderPassSettings`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##func-CommitRenderPassSettings) runs per dispatch from [`GraphicsEngine::HandleDrawThreadTask`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::GraphicsEngine##func-HandleDrawThreadTask), gated by `shadow_system_enabled`, at parity `m_FrameIndex & 1`. It clears [`RenderPassState::m_Enabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_pass::RenderPassState##flag-m_Enabled) on every static/dynamic shadow pass, then re-enables the passes the schedule marked active this frame and re-points each enabled pass's render target at the parity's atlas slice, read from the cascade's `CascadeData::m_RenderPassSettings[parity]`.

### 3.4 `GetShadowFade` and `m_ShadowTransparency`

[`ShadowManager::GetShadowFade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##func-GetShadowFade) returns [`ShadowManager::m_ShadowTransparency`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_ShadowTransparency) when shadows are enabled, else one. It is a static configuration value, not a per-frame term: its only writer is `CShadowManager::SetShadowTransparency` (a one-line setter, not located), it is the debug console variable `"Dev|Graphics|Shadows|Transparency"` (zero from the constructor), and it does not track sun visibility. `SetGlobalShaderConstants` stages it as `1 - fade` and `fade` (§5.2).

### 3.5 The sun-grazing shadow swing

No sun-occlusion query feeds the shadow strength. The whole-terrain shadow swing when the sun grazes the horizon has two continuous causes:

1. The minimum sun-elevation clamp (§5.1) rotates the lighting sun direction up below a floor angle, so near the horizon a small change in the true direction makes a larger change in the clamped one.
2. The cascade re-fit (§3.2): turning the camera re-fits the scheduled cascades, and with the light direction nearly in the fit plane the basis is ill-conditioned, so the per-slice shadow matrices swing; the amortized cascades swing on their own frames.

## 4. Deferred lighting combine

[`RenderBlockDeferredLighting::DrawClustered`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockDeferredLighting##func-DrawClustered) (pass [`RP_DEFERRED_LIGHTS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_DEFERRED_LIGHTS)) does the clustered light assignment and the deferred combine into MainColor. It reads the render context's per-view constants: the light counts ([`RenderContext::m_ActivePointLightCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_ActivePointLightCount), [`RenderContext::m_ActiveSpotLightCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_ActiveSpotLightCount)), the camera position ([`RenderContext::m_RenderCameraPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_RenderCameraPosition)), and the projection scalars ([`RenderContext::CameraFOV`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-CameraFOV), [`RenderContext::m_DisplayHeight`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_DisplayHeight), [`RenderContext::m_DisplayRatio`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_DisplayRatio)). It binds the global CB ([`RenderContext::m_FPGlobals`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_FPGlobals) on fragment constant buffer 0) and calls `LightManager::SetupLightingTextures`. It selects no frame-indexed state itself; the sun and the cascade sampling ride the GlobalConstants (§5).

### 4.1 How the froxel grid is built

Local lights are assigned to a 64-pixel tile lattice over the framebuffer, `Tx = m_DisplayWidth / 64` by `Ty = m_DisplayHeight / 64` tiles by 64 depth slices, built by `DrawClustered` in two phases before the resolve.

**Phase 1, light assignment.** It binds [`RenderContext::m_LightAssignmentRenderSetup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_LightAssignmentRenderSetup), a target of one texel per tile arrayed over light chunks, then:

```c
Graphics::Clear(ctx, CLEAR_RENDERBUFFER_R | _G | _B | _A, {1,1,1,1}, ...);  // ClearRenderTargetView on every colour RT
Graphics::SetVertexProgramConstants(ctx, 2, 0, &view_rows, 4);   // lightassignment VS  cb2.ViewMatrix
Graphics::SetGeometryProgramConstants(ctx, 0, 0, M, 4);          // lightassignment GS  cb0.ProjMatrix
Graphics::SetFragmentProgramConstants(ctx, 1, 0, tile_bounds);   // lightassignment PS  cb1  (2 vec4s)
Graphics::SetBlendEnable(ctx, 1); SetBlendEquation(ctx, …); SetBlendFunc(ctx, …);  // a commutative blend
Graphics::DrawIndexedInstanced(ctx, nullptr, 240, point_light_count);  // sphere proxies
Graphics::DrawIndexedInstanced(ctx, nullptr, 48, spot_light_count);    // cone proxies
```

where `M = m_ProjectionF · T(+1,-1,0) · Scaling(Tx/ceil(Tx), Ty/ceil(Ty), 1) · T(-1,+1,0)`, the projection plus the NDC nudge that snaps the grid to whole tiles when the display size is not a multiple of 64; the clear flags are [`ClearFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::draw::ClearFlags).

The `cb2` view matrix comes from [`RenderContext::m_View`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_View) with its translation row forced to `(0,0,0,1)`, over light positions the CPU has already biased by `RenderContext::m_RenderCameraPosition` (the main render camera's world position, distinct from [`RenderContext::m_CameraPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_CameraPosition), which follows whichever camera the pass draws with). The light-assignment vertex shaders (`sh_0090` point, `sh_0092` spot) end with `add o0.xyz, r0.xyzx, cb2[3].xyzx`, so `cb2` row 3 is the translation the engine zeroes.

The geometry shader (`sh_1483`) emits `SV_RenderTargetArrayIndex` from each light's packed chunk index and passes the proxy triangle's three view-space vertices down as packed f16. The pixel shader (`sh_0572`) reconstructs them and tests them against the tile's frustum, derived affinely from its own pixel coordinate:

```
cbuffer PerFrameConstants {          // cb1 -- 8 floats
  float scale_x, bias_x0, bias_x1, dummy_x;
  float scale_y, bias_y0, bias_y1, dummy_y;
}
dcl_input_ps_siv linear noperspective v0.xy, position
mad r1.xz, v0.xxxx, cb1[0].xxxx, cb1[0].yyzy     // tile x max/min frustum bound
mad r4.xy, v0.yyyy, cb1[1].xxxx, cb1[1].yzyy     // tile y max/min frustum bound
```

`SV_Position` is the absolute target coordinate (rendering §4.3), so `v0.x` is the absolute tile index plus a half.

**Phase 2, the fill.** It binds [`RenderContext::m_LightAssignmentFillRenderSetup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_LightAssignmentFillRenderSetup) and, as a texture, phase 1's output ([`RenderContext::m_LightAssignmentTexture`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_LightAssignmentTexture)), uploads the four light-chunk counts on `cb1`, and issues `Graphics::Draw(ctx, nullptr, 192)`; its geometry shader (`sh_1484`) emits a fullscreen triangle per primitive with `SV_RenderTargetArrayIndex = SV_PrimitiveID`, one per depth slice. Each output texel reads only its own tile's chunk entries.

Then the resolve: `SetupLightingTextures`, `PerspectiveFovInverse` (rendering §4.1), and a fullscreen `Graphics::Draw(ctx, nullptr, 3)`.

So the phase-1 clear ignores the viewport and scissor and covers the whole target, the assignment blend is commutative (disjoint tile sets accumulate in any order), and the fill is per-tile-local.

### 4.3 The lighting frame constants are camera-relative

The local lights reach the shaders through the frame constant buffer `LightingFrameConsts` ([`RenderContext::m_LightBuffer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_LightBuffer), fragment constant buffer 3), which [`LightManager::SetupLightingData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-SetupLightingData) fills from the frame's light records. [`LightManager::ApplyDynamicLights`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-ApplyDynamicLights) calls it at the start of `SetGlobalShaderConstants` (§5), so the buffer is rebuilt once per dispatch.

Every position in it is relative to the render camera: each point light's first row of `PointLights` and each spot light's first row of `SpotLights` hold the light's world position minus `RenderContext::m_RenderCameraPosition`, and each projected spot light's `Projections` matrix is the offset world-view-projection of the light's frustum built with a world transform translated to the same camera position, so it maps a camera-relative position. The pixel shaders match: the deferred resolve subtracts its depth times the interpolated view ray (the pixel relative to the camera) from each light position and applies the projection to that same product, and the forward-lit families subtract the interpolated world position minus the `GlobalConstants` camera row. The rest of the buffer is world-space or camera-independent: the light-propagation-volume transforms map world positions into a volume placed around the camera, and the ground fill and the ambient scalars do not depend on the camera. The particle vertex and domain shaders light per vertex from the same buffer.

## 5. `SetGlobalShaderConstants`: the centerpiece

[`RenderEngine::SetGlobalShaderConstants`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-SetGlobalShaderConstants) runs per dispatch from `GraphicsEngine::HandleDrawThreadTask` after `SetRenderContextCamera`, only when the `LightManager` singleton exists. It calls `LightManager::ApplyDynamicLights` and [`LightManager::UpdateRenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##func-UpdateRenderContext), then assembles the `cb0` GlobalConstants into the staging blocks [`RenderEngine::m_VPGlobalConstData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_VPGlobalConstData) (vertex) and [`RenderEngine::m_FPGlobalConstData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_FPGlobalConstData) (fragment). Rows below are `cb0` rows of those blocks.

### 5.1 Sun / light direction (with the elevation clamp)

- Raw sun direction: [`LightManager::m_GlobalLightDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##field-m_GlobalLightDirection) → fragment row 3 `.xyz`.
- Elevation-clamped sun direction, fragment row 31 `.xyz`: with `e = dot(sunDir, up)` (`up` is [`vector3_up`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/types::math::vector3_up)), if `e < sinf(minElevation)`, where `minElevation` is [`RenderEngine::m_SunBacklightHeightClamp`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_SunBacklightHeightClamp), it rotates the direction up to the floor by `RotationAxis(cross(sunDir, up), -(minElevation - asin(e)))` (§3.5).
- Sun backlight and shadow colour products, fragment rows 29 to 33, from [`RenderEngine::m_SunBacklightColor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_SunBacklightColor) through [`RenderEngine::m_SunShadowSpecularMultiplier`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_SunShadowSpecularMultiplier), the time-of-day colours and multipliers the environment update sets.

### 5.2 Shadow terms (the parity surface)

- Horizon-map blend time, [`m_HorizonTime[m_RenderFrameNo & 1]`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_HorizonTime), to vertex row 4 `.w` with a copy in the fragment block; parity-indexed, and a blend time rather than an atlas slice base.
- Horizon-map occlusion scale: [`ShadowManager::m_HorizonScale`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_HorizonScale) when [`ShadowManager::m_EnableHorizonMapping`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_EnableHorizonMapping) is set, else `0` → vertex row 5 `.w`.
- Shadow fade (§3.4): `1 - fade` and `fade` at fragment row 9 `.xy`.
- The cascade block: a copy of the forward-material [`RenderContext::m_ShadowCascades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_ShadowCascades) (a [`ShadowCascades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::ShadowCascades): per-cascade scale/blend + offset/slice + box-test params) into fragment rows 45 onward, filled by `SetRenderContextCamera` from the shadow manager's parity storage.
- The per-slice projective shadow matrices, [`RenderContext::m_ShadowMatrices`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_ShadowMatrices) into fragment rows 63 to 94, likewise parity-filled.
- The active cascade count, [`ShadowManager::m_ActiveCascades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::shadow_manager::ShadowManager##field-m_ActiveCascades) into [`RenderContext::m_ActiveCascadeCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_ActiveCascadeCount), set by `SetRenderContextCamera` from the same parity storage.

### 5.3 Camera-derived constants

- The full, translation-bearing view-projection: the render camera's [`Camera::m_ViewProjectionF`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_ViewProjectionF) into vertex rows 0 to 3, for screen-space and non-geometry work (rendering §2.4).
- The camera world position, the translation row of the render camera's [`Camera::m_TransformF`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_TransformF) , into vertex rows 4 and 5 and fragment rows 4 to 6. The fragment cascade translation (row 48, §5.2) is baked relative to it. Which pixel shaders read these rows, and for what, is in [`shaders.md`](shaders.md#pixel-shaders-that-read-the-camera-position).
- The depth-unproject constants from the render camera: `1/m_Near` ([`Camera::m_Near`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_Near)) and `1/m_Far` ([`Camera::m_Far`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera::Camera##field-m_Far)), fragment row 9 `.z = 1/near − 1/far` and `.w = 1/far`.
- The inverse display size, `cb0[8].zw = (1/DisplayWidth, 1/DisplayHeight)`, from [`Graphics::GetDeviceInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/graphics_engine::device##func-GetDeviceInfo) (`Graphics::SDeviceInfo`), not from the bound render setup or the viewport: the shaders' one screen-size constant (rendering §4.3).

### 5.4 Wetness and fog

- The weather wetness block, `GetWetProperties(ctx)`, into fragment rows 40 to 43.
- The fog, through [`RenderEngine::SetFog`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-SetFog) at the tail: builds two fog layers from the render engine's time-of-day fog scalars. The fog colour goes to vertex rows 23 and 24 and fragment rows 27 and 28; the per-layer distance/density ramp (start/inv-range/height) goes to vertex rows 14 and 15 and fragment rows 14 and 15; and a normalized fog light direction goes to fragment row 44. The fog scalars on `RenderEngine` are not yet bound in the defs. Fog reads the render camera's height (the `.y` of `RenderContext::m_RenderCameraPosition`).

## 6. Light gather buffering

`LightManager::CopyLightsToUpdate` copies the frame's visible point and spot lights into the triple buffer of `m_FrameLightInfo[3]`, the [`LightManager::m_LightLookup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::LightManager##field-m_LightLookup), and `m_LightIndexBuffer[3]`, indexed off `m_Counter`, and configures the volumetric spot-light passes ([`enable_low_res_spot_light_volume`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::light_manager::enable_low_res_spot_light_volume)). The light list the deferred combine reads is thus one of three slots, chosen by the rolling counter.

## 7. What varies frame to frame

Of the values above, the horizon-map blend time, the cascade block, the per-slice shadow matrices, and the active cascade count switch on the parity `m_FrameIndex & 1` (equal to `m_RenderFrameNo & 1`); which cascades carry a fresh fit (§3.1) and the dynamic light list (§6) switch on the whole `m_Counter`; the TAA jitter phase follows the parity (rendering §5.7), and auto-exposure is frame-counted (rendering §5.4). The full view-projection and the camera position follow the render camera, so they differ between dispatches drawn from different cameras. Everything else (the sun direction and its clamp, the sun colours, the time-of-day modulator, the global light colour, the sun vector, the shadow fade and enable, the depth-unproject and display-size constants, the wetness, and the fog) is recomputed each frame from continuous inputs and is the same for every dispatch. A lighting or shadow flicker not explained by the exposure meter or the jitter therefore comes from a parity- or counter-indexed shadow value read at a parity or counter that disagrees with the half that was written.
