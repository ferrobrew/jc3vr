# G03 shadow body fit

`estimate/` is a pure shadow estimator over G01 `Observation`. It reuses the existing reference `Rig`, analytic two-bone solver, `FootPose`, swing arc, and torso heading helpers. No production authority, engine call, unsafe code, shared solver edit, observation edit, or camera writer is added. The coordinator owns the active test-only registration in `rig/mod.rs` and serialized central verification.

The required registration remains:


All review revision edits stay within `estimate/`. No Cargo, Rust, formatter, clippy, test, benchmark, stage, commit, definitions, or IDB operation runs in this provider task. Existing Rust LSP initialization failed through the recursive toolchain fallback; source navigation uses targeted reads. Compilation, lints, assertions, runtime cost, and headset behavior remain unverified. The coordinator owns serialized validation with aggregate `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=16` and test threads at most four.

## State and observation model

One committed simulation state contains intended stance, pelvis compensation, torso articulation, coordinate rates, virtual contacts, and an immutable snapshot. The stance supplies the root request through the measured neutral root-to-stance transform. Pelvis compensation is separate, so a rearward hip hinge does not itself request backward travel. Requests never credit the movement ledger. Completed gait travel uses the observed root pair after subtracting transport of the same support-local root anchor.

G01 composes raw tracking once through the corresponding current side. Each candidate supplies player shoulders and arm reach. Raw shoulder-to-grip displacement is remapped once to character arm reach, and the remapped result never becomes sensor evidence. Head/hand validity and measured flags are copied independently. Player hip height, segment splits, and eye-to-neck offset remain priors. Character dimensions derive from the validated reference `Rig` and its measured arm lengths.

The reduced model has independent intended stance X/Z/yaw, pelvis X/Z compensation and height, lower-torso bend, upper-torso bend, and chest twist. Two fixed-length torso segments and analytic fixed-length legs realize the pose. A continuous comfort cost couples hip/knee/ankle articulation, rearward pelvis placement, and approximate mass projection. No activity label, floor-reach gate, head-pitch hip command, or low-head timer selects a posture. Knee and pelvis support types do not exist in this model.

## Contact target and realization contract

The update first carries contacts, then fits immutable contact-topology alternatives. Each alternative starts from the original carried baseline. Candidate and exact contact set stay paired. Within absolute measurement tolerance, fewer releases take precedence; comparable residual fits also prefer retained contacts. A successful first single release never changes the baseline for the opposite single release.

The preliminary body supplies support-relative hip prediction for contact transitions. The previous hips are carried through the corresponding support delta before differencing. Support-only translation or yaw therefore supplies no local velocity lead. Intended stance is also carried through the root pair as a point, including rotation about the root rather than translation alone. Its world-planar X/Z correction-rate vector rotates through the same completed root rotation before prediction. Search directions, planar speed/acceleration envelopes, and planar continuity costs then use completed-root axes. Planar temporal intervals describe local displacement rather than absolute world coordinates. Pelvis-local compensation and articulation rates remain local and are not rotated as world vectors. Recenter and reseed retain explicit derivative resets.

Contact transitions precede the final numerical fit. Released legs use the candidate's unconstrained rest target during fitting. The published released foot is the realized ankle, never an old contact pose. Active contacts retain the final requested target through candidate evaluation; their residual is its distance from the realized ankle. After both legs realize, candidate validation checks the signed left-to-right separation of both realized ankles, including every active/released combination. A Released foot's stale retained pose never supplies the other side of this final check. Crossing or insufficient separation rejects the candidate, and topology fitting can explicitly relax the inferred active contact. The final publishing operation sets every foot position to that realized ankle. Body ankles, published feet, contact states, and residuals consequently describe the same final target selection. A lift at progress zero is fitted at its actual swing origin. Failed endpoint scheduling leaves a released, consistently realized foot.

`Support` is injected evidence for one named, generation-stamped, rigid upright finite horizontal patch. It is not a live terrain/vehicle-deck adapter. Planted feet retain local poses. Patch shrink removes an invalid planted hypothesis, and missing/replaced support releases it. The continuing support transform must match the prior committed current transform.

Both swing origin and endpoint are support-local. The entire arc is evaluated locally and transformed once, so translating or yawing support carries both endpoints. Before late commitment, endpoint position and yaw approach the desired local foothold at bounded speeds. The endpoint freezes once progress reaches the declared late-swing fraction. Advancing the clock to one does not plant the foot.

Touchdown requires current patch containment, signed left/right separation, reach reserve, hip flexion/abduction, knee and ankle limits, and foot-to-stance twist. Candidate fitting and endpoint validation share the full-leg articulation helper. A valid tentative plant enters the final fit; if that fit cannot retain it, topology relaxation explicitly removes it. Invalid touchdown releases the foot rather than planting an invented surface or stale endpoint. One foot swings at a time. The bounded model does not claim general terrain search or dynamic balance.

## Temporal viability and bounded recovery

Temporal bounds intersect anatomy, speed, acceleration, and stopping distance for every reduced coordinate. A candidate endpoint rate is the position difference over the simulation interval. Its braking distance must fit before either finite hard position bound. The resulting quadratic displacement interval prevents committing an outward rate that cannot stop before a joint limit. Empty temporal/anatomical intersections return `EmptyTemporalIntersection` instead of collapsing an inverted interval to an invalid endpoint.

`temporal::prepare` checks an incoming position/rate pair. A nonviable incoming rate or empty interval triggers `TemporalStatus::RateRecovery`: rates reset explicitly, the next candidate remains inside anatomy and a new viable envelope, and quality reports reduced authority. Independent coordinate viability does not prove that the next pose satisfies coupled leg geometry. If a continuing update exhausts its normal bounded search, `recover_constraints` evaluates the carried previous pose against four immutable contact-release alternatives. It carries support geometry without advancing swing progress. A valid result commits `TemporalStatus::ConstraintRecovery`, zero correction rates, and reduced authority. It can report current tracking residuals. Invalid prior geometry still returns `SearchExhausted` transactionally. Both recovery statuses explicitly waive acceleration continuity; normal commits retain hard speed, acceleration, and braking constraints.

Recenter preserves valid world contacts and resets derivative rates without gait travel. Calibration, world-scale, and authority changes require explicit reseeding. Teleport discards contacts and derivative history. Missing steps, stale or invalid observations, unsupported owners, and invalid roots/supports remain structured errors. Failed commits do not replace the last snapshot. Allowed simulation intervals remain positive and at most one tenth of a second.

Duplicate identity compares composition, provenance, focus, hand presence, validity/tracked flags, reported velocities, and valid pose components. Unavailable pose components are ignored. An exact retry with unavailable NaNs therefore returns the original snapshot. The stored raw observation is not normalized or rewritten. A changed valid head/hand component or provenance still fails the same-step retry.

## Search and confidence

Each fixed-budget search uses four generic seed families: predicted, neutral, reach-projected, and coupled torso/pelvis geometry. Every seed and coordinate trial uses the same temporal constraint calculation. A rejected predicted seed cannot prevent evaluation of a coupled feasible seed. The search still covers a finite represented family, not all possible body poses. `SearchExhausted` explicitly means that family was exhausted, not that geometric infeasibility was proved. A committed candidate reports either `Admissible` or `ResidualAfterBudget` separately from endpoint residuals and temporal recovery.

Tracking residuals are zero-cost inside declared absolute tolerances. Anatomy and finite segment geometry remain hard. Continuity, coupled comfort, retained support, and transport costs resolve the remaining freedom. Stance-heading departure from completed root facing and carried stance heading has a normalized secondary cost, including initialization. This is a held-heading prior, independent of head and wrist axes. Tracking and neck-limit residuals still take precedence, and chest twist remains available before stance transport. Search ties are deterministic. The analytic leg solve retains its extension reserve and branch seed. Degenerate segment geometry is rejected by anatomy validation rather than hidden by a fallback height.

Yaw ambiguity probes the reported chest heading, which is stance yaw plus chest twist. Each offset tests twist-only, shared stance/twist, and stance-only allocations against absolute measurement tolerance. Continuity cost does not count as measurement evidence. The fixed coverage can demonstrate ambiguity but cannot establish uniqueness. Yaw confidence therefore stays `Low`, including when every probe fails. Lower-body confidence also stays `Low`; contact persistence, dwell, and repeated samples do not measure physical feet or waist pose.

## Static work bound

One seed evaluates its initial candidate and twelve sweeps over both directions of nine coordinates: 217 evaluations. Four seeds make 868 evaluations per search. Each topology pass evaluates at most retained, left-release, right-release, and both-release alternatives. Two passes surround contact transition selection. At most four fixed-pose recovery evaluations, one final realization, and twelve heading probes give at most 6,961 candidate evaluations per accepted update. Each candidate uses at most two analytic leg solves. At most ten additional endpoint checks run, for a total bound of 13,932 analytic leg solves. Recovery during the preliminary pass skips transitions and the final search, so recovery never adds another pair of topology passes. Early anatomical rejection can perform less work. No convergence loop, history-sized work, scene query, worker, or wall-clock deadline is added.

This is a static work bound, not a runtime benchmark or a frame-time safety claim. The count increases to cover recovery seeds, independent topology alternatives, and final realization. EB profiling and the live fit/gate budget remain required before G09 activation.
