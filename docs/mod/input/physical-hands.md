# Physical hands

Each empty hand is a body in the game's physics world, driven toward its controller within the hand's strength: it stops at walls and is drawn there, shoves what it meets, strikes characters, and pushes the player away from what does not give. A grip near a loose object holds it where the hand touched it, and a grip near a weapon lying in the world collects the weapon. An object the grapple snatches ([snatch.md](../grapple/snatch.md#targets)) is handed to the same hold on arrival (`physical_hands::remote`). A hand whose body does not act is probed with ray casts instead ([probes](#probes)).

The code is `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/` (the hand bodies in `physics/`), tuned in `crates/jc3vr-physical/tuning/` (`physics.toml`, `hands.toml`, `pickup.toml`, and the `vault` section of `climb.toml`). The engine side is in [havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md), [melee-and-damage.md](../../engine/gameplay/melee-and-damage.md), and [item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md). How a grip picks its target is in [grip.md](grip.md), the contact cues (touch, take, let go, and the pressure and load levels) in [haptics.md](haptics.md), what a held object's contacts do in [impacts.md](impacts.md), and the diagnostic views in [hand-views.md](hand-views.md). The one setting is `controllers.physical_hands.bodies.strength` (the pause menu's `HAND STRENGTH`), which scales the bodies' and the holds' strength; the rest is always on under the controller scheme.

## Hand bodies

A hand body is a capsule along the fingers, centred on the palm, made as the grappling hook's hang proxy makes its free capsule ([havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#making-a-free-dynamic-body)): a dynamic body in a [`PfxRigidBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxRigidBody) with a null owner, gravity off, never sleeping. It is on the [`WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-WEAPONS) layer in the player's system group with a zero subsystem id, so it meets the world, props, characters, and vehicles but never the player's capsule or the other hand, and the aim's rays pass through it. A player in system group zero gets no hand bodies.

### The drive

The pre-simulation callback ([`PfxPreSimulationCallback`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxPreSimulationCallback)) gives each acting body the velocities that close a share of its error to the goal (the palm led to the end of the step) in one step, with the change of velocity capped by the hand's strength ([`hknpWorld::setBodyVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyVelocity), `physics/drive.rs`). The strength is the caps, not the mass: the solver shares a contact's impulse by mass, so a light hand meets a light prop without throwing it, yet shoves a heavy one within its strength over a few steps. The speed cap is relative to whatever carries the player (the cockpit frame's world velocity is fed forward), so a hand keeps up with a fast platform while a tracking glitch is still capped; the carrier's lead counts steps, not time, or a frame with no step would make the hand snap back and forth.

A rig jump ([physics-substrate.md, "The rig's jumps"](../body/physics-substrate.md#the-rigs-jumps)) moves every palm without the hands moving, so the next step places each body at its palm at rest with its contacts muted briefly, and the turn strikes nothing. A body far behind its palm is put back only when a ray to the goal is clear, so a hand pulled through a wall keeps pressing at the surface; a hand holding through a joint is never put back.

### Drawing the hand at its body

Each step measures where the body ended against where the controller's palm was predicted (not the goal, so a hand a grip pins to a wall is drawn on the wall), as a shift and a turn about the palm; a small turn is ignored so a free hand keeps the controller's rotation. The hand's cockpit poses are moved by that offset, which updates at the simulation rate and is added to the latest controller pose at the render rate, so a free hand keeps the controller's latency ([render-time-anchoring.md](../rendering/render-time-anchoring.md#moving-frame-sampling)).

### Pushing the player

A hand held back lags its goal, and the lag pushes the player the other way, up to the hand's strength, through the on-foot locomotion task's wanted velocity ([pushing off surfaces](#pushing-off-surfaces)); a mostly vertical lag vaults instead.

### Pushing up

A press down on an upward-facing static surface lifts the body, a jump assist onto a ledge or low wall (`physical_hands/vault.rs`), for a hand that neither grips nor holds; the stronger hand's lift wins. A press near the feet lifts nothing (a hand reaching for a magazine on the ground rests there), and the lift starts only while the proxy stood that step and never lifts a player moving down, because while falling the rig carries both goals down faster than the bodies follow and the hands read as pressing on nothing. The lift is set after the proxy updates, as the climb's velocity is, and while a pressing hand reaches the world the proxy is written as supported ([hand-climbing.md](hand-climbing.md#keeping-the-game-on-foot)), so the game keeps the player on foot and the stick carries them over the top. A grip on the surface climbs it instead ([hand-climbing.md](hand-climbing.md)).

### Hits

Each hand body's wrapper gets a copy of its virtual table whose contact-impulse slot is the mod's, everything else the engine's own, with contact events on ([`PfxRigidBody::SetContactImpulseEventActive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxRigidBody##vfunc-SetContactImpulseEventActive), [havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#contact-impulse-events)). Contacts arrive inside the step and are only queued (`physics/contact.rs`); the next frame tick acts on them (`physics/blows.rs`). The hand plays `Event::HandStrike` by its momentum. A contact with a character is a blow only when the hand swung into it, judged by its speed toward the chest less the player's own ([`Character::m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity)) per real second, so a resting hand, a character walking into it, or a slow-motion reach strikes nothing; a hand going for the character's gun strikes only at a solid punch's speed, and not while its grip closes on the gun ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-gun-from-a-character)).

The blow is an arm's momentum at the hand's share of the closing speed (the solver's impulse grows with the hand body's mass, so it is not used), calibrated against the character's own thresholds ([`m_HitReaction_ImpulseStumbleThreshold`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_HitReaction_ImpulseStumbleThreshold), [`m_HitReaction_ImpulseRagdollThreshold`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_HitReaction_ImpulseRagdollThreshold)) so tuned impulses land just past a stagger or reach a knockdown, and delivered through [`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction) ([melee-and-damage.md](../../engine/gameplay/melee-and-damage.md#the-hit-reaction)), the character found again from the body so a freed one is never touched. A hard blow also deals the player's [`m_MeleeDamage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_MeleeDamage) (so the hammer upgrades count) as a melee damage message, which runs the same reaction, so the character reacts once. Every character is struck alike whatever its faction, and cooldowns make one swing one blow. There is no melee gesture: a blow is only ever a hand body's contact.

### Lifecycle

A hand acts on foot, on the ground or falling (so a flung hand can catch a ledge), not on the grapple, while tracked and holding nothing but what its body holds through a joint, a gun included ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)). A hand that takes a grenade or carries what it grabbed is parked (taken out of the simulation) at once, so it does not stand frozen as an obstacle; one that stops acting otherwise is held briefly, then parked. A driven hand does not probe or shove, and casts its reach from where its body was, so it reaches what it touches, not what lies beyond a wall.

Both bodies are destroyed on leaving gameplay, on a change of the player's system group (a new character after a death), and on unload. A body whose slot no longer holds it is forgotten without touching the engine; its wrapper may still point at the mod's virtual-table copy, so forgetting one pins the payload's image for the rest of the run. The callback is registered from the frame tick, never while the engine walks callbacks, and removed by the shutdown cleanup. The bodies keep no Havok-space positions, so a world recentring needs nothing of them.

## Probes

A palm whose body does not act is probed with the aim's filtered ray query ([`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay)), which never hits the player; the game's box overlap query is unused because its collector owns Havok heap memory. Six points on the palm box are each cast from where they last were in the open, and a hit presses the point in by its depth. The rendered hand is moved out of the surface by the deepest press, while the probes keep the real hand. Palms pass through characters. Seated, the palms reach only the vehicle's doors ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#seated-doors-and-palm-pressure)).

## Pushing off surfaces

A palm pressed into a mostly vertical surface pushes the player along its normal in proportion to the press. The push, from the probes or the bodies, joins the on-foot locomotion task's wanted velocity after the stick's ([rig.md, "Who moves the root"](../body/rig.md#who-moves-the-root), [physics-substrate.md](../body/physics-substrate.md#the-wanted-velocity)), so the view moves with the body, and only on foot.

## Shoving objects

A probed palm pressed into a dynamic body asks its point under the palm to move at least as fast as the hand, applied at the contact ([`hknpWorld::applyBodyImpulseAt`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-applyBodyImpulseAt), [item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md#a-body-in-the-havok-world)), so an off-centre push turns it. How much it gave scales the push off it: a light crate slides away, a crate against a wall pushes like the wall. A fast first contact knocks it with a share of [`Player::m_MeleeImpulse`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_MeleeImpulse).

## Picking things up

An acting empty hand casts five short rays each tick from the back of the hand through the palm (out of the palm, along the fingers, between, and out of either edge), and offers the nearest hit it takes to the grip (`GrabVolume::Pickup`), where it beats a surface to climb:

- a weapon lying loose ([`WeaponBase::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-TYPE_ID) in [`E_WS_DROPPED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_DROPPED));
- any other dynamic body of finite mass, including a part off its vehicle (`doors::engine::broken_off`; one still attached is the doors', [vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md));
- a placed rigid object not yet dynamic, turned loose by the grip through [`RigidObject::MakeDynamic`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##func-MakeDynamic) as a knock would ([havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#placed-bodies-becoming-dynamic)). A static one is world, climbed.

The same rays find characters ([character-grabs.md](character-grabs.md#reaching-a-character)) and doors. Vehicles and the body the player stands on are never targets. Each hit's refusal reason is logged with the next grip press ([grip.md](grip.md#readouts-and-logs)).

A weapon grab takes the weapon straight into the hand ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md#picking-a-weapon-up-into-the-hand)), or for one the direct pickup refuses clicks [`USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-USE_ITEM) so the game picks it up its own way ([item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon)), through the pickup sink the weapon handling registers in the body crate (`controllers::held::pickup`). Under the controller scheme the hands are the only way to pick a weapon up (`weapons::hold::button_pickup`): the pickup panel is cleared in place of [`Player::UpdateWeaponHUD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateWeaponHUD), and the use-item weapon input reads zero during [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling) outside the fallback click; the use action itself stays, since it enters vehicles.

### Holding

Without a joint, a held object stays dynamic and is carried by a pair of soft constraints solved each tick (`hold.rs`), the grab point following the palm and the body turning with it, capped by the hand's strength (`HandStrength`) and starting from where gravity will have it, so the pull holds it up. Under slow motion the hold runs in real time ([`Clock::m_UpdateSpeed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/clock::Clock##field-m_UpdateSpeed)) while gravity stays the world's. From the caps: one hand lifts what weighs less than its pull, two twice that, heavier drags on the ground, and a long object held at one end droops. What the hands can lift takes on the character's change of velocity, so a jump carries a held tyre.

A hold lets go when the object is caught rather than slow (`grasp::Strain`): the grab point lags past a break distance and stops closing (a crate snagged on a wall), or past the tear distance at once, judged against the hand's own motion so a door held from a fast car stays held. A body freed or reused is let go, and holds drop without a throw when the palms stand down; a game menu keeps them.

### Holding through a joint

A driven hand holds through a fixed constraint to the object's body ([havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#joining-bodies-with-a-fixed-constraint)), made by the next step in their current pose and pulled within the budget a carried hold would have (`HoldTarget::hand_force_n`); two hands' pulls add, and release leaves the object with the hand's velocity. The step owns the joint (`physics/joint.rs`), ending it on release, tear, stand-down, leaving gameplay, or when the constraint slot no longer holds it. The world's constraint holds the only reference to its heap data, and the world does not end a constraint whose body is destroyed, so the next step must notice and drop the hold.

A ragdoll or articulated object hangs most of its mass on its own constraints, which the drive does not see, so it is driven softly (`HoldTarget::constrained`), or the body orbits the palm. Two hands on one assembly hold it by ball-and-socket points (`JointKind::Point`, [`hkpBallAndSocketConstraintData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/havok::hkpBallAndSocketConstraintData)), since two fixed poses rarely match what the assembly allows and fight through the solver. With two hands on any object, the wrists are weakened so the grab points set its line.

### Articulated objects

A dynamic body joined to a non-hand body by an enabled [`hknpConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpConstraint) in [`hknpWorld::m_constraintManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_constraintManager), such as a gate or hatch, is held the same way and the step removes what its joint forbids (`physical_hands/articulated.rs`, [item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md#joined-bodies)); its constraint carries its weight. Its assembly (dynamic bodies joined transitively, stopping at fixed or keyframed ones) is damped toward its mean velocity by impulses that sum to nothing, since the hand drives only one body and the rest would ring against it.

### The player's own body

The held object's centre is kept out of the torso (`PlayerKeepOut`: the procedural torso's chest and belly capsules, [procedural-body.md](../body/procedural-body.md#the-arms), or a cylinder), and its bodies stop colliding with the player's through the pair filter until it comes clear after release (`physical_hands::carry_collision`), leaving out any body a vehicle owns, since a stunt area puts the proxy in the vehicle's group. Because the keep-out moves only the centre, two keyframed capsules follow the drawn chest and belly (`physics::drawn_body`), in the player's group so they never touch the player or hands and never push; a held prop meets them. Such contacts give no feedback, and an object whose box stays near them counts as resting against the torso (a resting contact is reported only as it starts), so the strain does not count its lag as caught. The other palm's probes pass through what the hands hold, so a free hand never pushes off it.

### Letting go and throwing

The last hand to let go of an object one hand lifts throws it through the release a gun or grenade takes ([physics-substrate.md, "Release velocities"](../body/physics-substrate.md#release-velocities)); a heavier object keeps the hold's velocity.

## Threading

The bodies are made, driven, and destroyed only in the pre-simulation callback and the shutdown cleanup, both on the game thread outside the step. The callback begins each step on the mod's step clock (`jc3vr_core::published::Step`), by which step-stamped publications age, so a hitch that holds up the simulation never parks a hand or tears a gun's joints. The contact handler runs inside the step and only queues. Blows are dealt from the frame tick, since from the callback a blow could ragdoll a character whose own callback would register while the engine walks callbacks.

The aim control's post-simulation update, which may run on a worker, only notes that a palms' tick is due (`tick::after_aim`). The tick runs on the game thread at the start of the render update (`tick::main_thread_tick`, from the [`Game::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::Game##func-UpdateRender) hook), before the VR frame waits on the compositor, and only when the thread's role ([threading.md](../threading.md#the-guard)) is the game thread; the device poll runs on a worker ([input.md](../../engine/gameplay/input.md#where-the-device-poll-runs)). Grabs read on the input tick ([bindings.md](bindings.md#injection-timing)) are acted on at the next palms' tick.

## Interaction evidence

`pickup::diagnostics::status()` keeps bounded evidence of each hand's grip acquisition (the input observation, the resolver's candidates, the reach scan with each branch's result, the latches, the routed grabs, the last press, and recent requests), keyed by input sequence, grip episode, and request ordinal, with the simulation's result recorded against the key. A missing scan means no evidence, not an observed absence. Recordings carry each stage as a `grip_acquisition` event ([recorder.md](../testing/recorder.md#what-a-recording-holds)).
