# Planted explosives (C4)

How the C4 weapon places charges and detonates them. The grenade's side is in [aim-pipeline.md, "Grenades"](aim-pipeline.md#grenades), the slot selection in [weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md), and the default buttons in [input.md](input.md#the-default-gamepad-keymap). Native functions named here without a link are unbound but verified in the IDB.

## The weapon and its charges

The C4 weapon ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon)) sits in the [`E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE) slot and owns the character's placed charges: a live list ([`m_ActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_ActiveExplosives), each entry the charge and the index of the weapon light marking it), which the HUD's charge hints and the detonation read, capped by [`MaxActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-MaxActiveExplosives), one of three tuned values by the explosives-capacity upgrades. [`CanPlantExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-CanPlantExplosive) compares the cap with [`NumActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-NumActiveExplosives). A charge consumes no stock: the cap is the only limit, freed by detonating or removing the charges.

[`RemoveAllExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-RemoveAllExplosives) takes every placed charge away without detonating it (unregistering its HUD hint, removing it with [`Explosive::RemoveSelf`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-RemoveSelf), and turning off its light), then queues a deferred stop-using, which leaves the planting mode if the weapon is in it, so a charge primed outside the mode stays. The game calls it when a character is disabled, when a mission activates or resets, when a ROM starts, and from the player's event handler. [`RemoveFromActiveList`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-RemoveFromActiveList) unregisters one charge's hint, turns off its light, erases its entry, and refreshes the HUD's count, leaving the charge in the world. A charge's detonation and destructor call it, but the destructor skips it for a charge `RemoveSelf` already marked removed (before erasing it from the ammunition pool), so a charge removed that way without first leaving the list keeps its entry, hint, and light.

The inventory runs the weapon's serial update ([`UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial)) every frame whatever the character wields ([`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Serial)), so detonation works with a gun in hand. Its attachment pass ([`UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)) also runs every frame and puts a primed charge's in-hand model on the right-hand bone.

A charge ([`PlantedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive)) is an explosive with two models: the in-hand model, shown while primed and placed at [`m_InactiveModelWorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##field-m_InactiveModelWorldMatrixT1), and the live model at the explosive's own transform, which [`Activate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-Activate) shows instead. Like a grenade, it gets its body ([`Explosive::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_PfxInstance)) on its first post-simulation update after spawning.

## Planting

Planting needs the weapon equipped. Selecting the explosives slot ([weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-decision)) equips it in its planting mode ([`m_IsInPlantingMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_IsInPlantingMode)): the camera aims a placement ghost at the surface ahead, and an animation track message primes a charge. With the planted-explosives ability, the weapon input task throws a grenade only on a `THROW_GRENADE` released within the hold threshold.

- [`PrimeExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-PrimeExplosive) puts the weapon in [`E_WS_WIELDING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WIELDING), hides it, spawns a charge from the ammunition pool at its shot matrix, and stores it in [`m_PrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_PrimedExplosive), checking neither the mode nor the cap.
- The weapon input task sets [`E_INPUT_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES) while planting on a `PLANT_EXPLOSIVE` released within the hold threshold, which reaches `CPlantedExplosiveWeapon::Fire_Start`. That does nothing at the cap, while already throwing or re-priming, or outside the mode; a target out of range shows the HUD's out-of-range notice, and with no primed charge the weapon is holstered. Otherwise `CPlantedExplosive::PlaceExplosiveAtPosition` casts a short ray at the aimed surface, puts the charge on it by the surface normal, and attaches it to the object hit, and the weapon queues a throw act picked by the target's height, so the charge is in place before the act plays.
- The act's track message calls [`Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile): it removes a charge stuck to a vehicle whose bavarium shield is up, activates the charge, adds heat, runs [`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase), adds the charge to the live list (which ignores one beyond the cap), and moves it from `m_PrimedExplosive` to [`m_LastPlantedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_LastPlantedExplosive). A track message with no primed charge left queues the re-prime act, or leaves the mode at the cap.
- Leaving the mode (the weapon's `StopUsing`) removes a primed charge ([`DespawnPrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-DespawnPrimedExplosive)). Outside the mode the serial update neither primes nor removes a charge.

[`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives) is recomputed every frame: off without the planted-explosives ability, with a heavy weapon wielded, while a camera hint of the wielded weapon is active, while the grapple is busy, and while [`SB_DISABLE_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES) is set.

### The drop under the parachute

Under a deployed parachute ([`Character::IsUsingParachute`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsUsingParachute)), `Fire_Projectile` instead moves the charge to the right-hand bone ([`Explosive::SetPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetPhysicsTransform)), drops it into the simulation ([`PlantedExplosive::DropExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-DropExplosive), which makes its body dynamic and keeps it from colliding with the thrower), gives it the character's [`m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) plus a small downward speed ([`PlantedExplosive::SetVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-SetVelocity)), and plays its trail ([`Explosive::PlayTrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PlayTrailEffect)).

### Sticking

The charge keeps the base explosive's collision handler, which the grenade overrides: on a collision it moves the charge to the contact point, oriented by the contact normal, and attaches it to the object hit (a vehicle's part on a vehicle), or plays its stick effect against the world. A dropped charge therefore sticks where it lands.

## Detonation

The detonation reads [`E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES) in [`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs), which the player's weapon input task writes every frame, planting or not. Under the gamepad rules ([`EPCUseGamepad`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/settings_manager::SettingType##variant-EPCUseGamepad)) it is set once `DETONATE_EXPLOSIVE` is held past the hold threshold, which is why the gamepad's RB throws a grenade on a tap and detonates on a hold; under the keyboard rules a press of `DETONATE_EXPLOSIVE_TAP` sets it too.

The serial update reads the input from its holder, not from the input struct it is passed. While the input is positive, the inventory allows triggering explosives ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives): off without the ability, with a heavy weapon wielded, while the grapple is busy, and while [`SB_DISABLE_TRIGGER_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_TRIGGER_EXPLOSIVES) is set), the character is not already detonating ([`Character::IsDetonatingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsDetonatingExplosives)), and the weapon has not acted on this input ([`m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput)), it queues the upper-body detonate act if the character can take it, or else cancels a reload. A queued act sets the latch and adds heat when charges are live, and the latch clears once the input is zero, so a held input detonates once. The act's track message schedules every live charge outside its safety range (`CPlantedExplosive::ScheduleForDetonation`, skipping a charge that fails `CPlantedExplosive::IsOutsideSafetyRange`, whose measure is unverified) and tells the HUD; a charge too close stays placed. The detonation depends on neither the selected slot, the planting mode, nor the charges' count: with none live the act still plays.

The detonator Rico holds during the act is the weapon's own model, visible only while the act plays: the weapon's entity gives its weapon data the trigger model as the base model, loaded from its properties (`CWeaponData::SetPropertiesDeferred`) into [`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance).
