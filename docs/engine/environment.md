# Time of day and weather

The time of day and the weather are engine singletons: [`WorldTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WorldTime) for the clock, and the live [`Weather`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::Weather), reached through the `LandscapeManager` singleton's `m_Atmosphere` and `Atmosphere::GetWeather`. `DayCycle::Apply` is the engine's own driver of both.

## Time of day

[`WorldTime::SetTimeOfDay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WorldTime##func-SetTimeOfDay) takes hours, wraps them to a day, fires the per-hour event, and clamps against `m_PauseTimeOfDay`. Writing `m_CurrentTimeOfDay` directly skips the clamp and the event. The render engine copies `m_CurrentTimeOfDay` out each frame, so a change reaches the lighting on its own. [`WorldTime::m_SpeedMultiplicator`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WorldTime##field-m_SpeedMultiplicator) scales the day cycle, and zero freezes it.

## Weather

The weather is not a preset enum but a continuous model of scalars on `Weather`. The direct writers are [`Weather::SetSeverity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::Weather##func-SetSeverity) (its clear-to-storm range is documented on [`Weather::m_Severity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::Weather##field-m_Severity)), `UpdateSeverityTarget`, which moves toward a target smoothly, and `SetWeatherTime`, beside the `m_SnowRatio` and `m_RainIntensity` scalars.

`WeatherController` pulls those scalars toward its own targets every frame, so a direct write does not hold. What sets the targets is the named events the controller subscribes in [`WeatherController::Init`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController##func-Init): [`EVENT_SUNNY`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController::EVENT_SUNNY), [`EVENT_RAIN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController::EVENT_RAIN), [`EVENT_SNOW`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController::EVENT_SNOW), [`EVENT_RESTORE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController::EVENT_RESTORE), and [`EVENT_INSTANT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/environment::WeatherController::EVENT_INSTANT), with `cloud_base` and `cloud_height`. Each event's doc comment gives the severity, rain intensity, and snow ratio it pins. They carry no payload and are sent by name through the static overload `NEvent::CSendEvent::SendMsg(const char*, void*)` with a null payload, the idiom `CDialogue::Play` uses to fire `on_play_dialogue`.
