# Weapon handling

The parts of the controller weapon handling that sit between the hands and the weapon pages: the pouches on the body, the foregrip, a held gun's weight, recoil, the action-runs tier, the fire input with empty hands, the empty weapon, and how grenades and C4 meet the hands. The weapons themselves as physical objects in either hand are [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md) and [physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md), the loadout is [two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md), the reload is [reload.md](reload.md), grenades are [grenades.md](grenades.md), and what a grip takes is [grip.md](grip.md). The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/`, tuned by `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/hold.toml` and `handling.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). Everything here acts only while the bindings inject, under the controller scheme.

The weapon handling runs on the input tick in two halves around the binding tables. The first half reads the wielded weapon and offers its grip targets (the sockets, the pouches, a held gun's parts, and the rest listed in [grip.md, "Targets"](grip.md#targets)); the grip model picks what each hand takes; the second half acts on the grabs and holds the fire inputs back during a weapon action. A gun stays in the hand while its grip is held, and nothing else draws or docks a weapon.

## Pouches

The body volumes ride Rico's skeleton, the body the player sees, so what the player sees on the body is where they reach ([rig.md, "The body's volumes on Rico's skeleton"](../body/rig.md#the-bodys-volumes-on-ricos-skeleton)). The sockets are reached on their weapons ([physical-weapons.md, "The sockets"](physical-weapons.md#the-sockets)). Each pouch (`volumes.pouches` in the hold tuning) sits at its offset from a landmark of the skeleton:

- the grenade and C4 pouches on the chest's front, below the shoulder joints by `volumes.chest_pouch_drop_fraction` of the torso, out from the midline by `volumes.chest_pouch_out_fraction` of the shoulder width, and `volumes.chest_front_m` in front of them. The grenade pouch is on the dominant hand's side and the C4 pouch on the other, whichever hand holds a gun, so they do not change sides with each handover; the C4 pouch sits nearer the midline, since a hand reaching across the body stops short of the far shoulder.
- the magazine pouch on the belt's front, above the hip joints by `volumes.belt_rise_fraction` of the torso and `volumes.belly_front_m` in front of them: on the midline with one gun or none, so the free hand and the gun's push onto it ([reload.md, "The belt reload"](reload.md#the-belt-reload)) both reach it without crossing the body, and with a gun in each hand one on each gun's side (`volumes.belt_out_fraction`). It is laid out around the gun hand (the hand holding a gun, or the one that last held one, or the dominant hand), because a single gun can sit in either hand.

While Rico moves on foot the hip joints the belt is built on are low-passed over about half a stride (`volumes::StrideFilter`, `volumes.stride`), because the gait swings and bobs the pelvis every stride and sprinting grabs at the belt missed. Looking down bends Rico's chest forward while the player's barely moves, so the chest pouches ride the chest turned back toward upright by the look's share (from `volumes.look_down_start_rad` to `volumes.look_down_full_rad`), only while the spine is near upright (`volumes.bend_full_rad` to `volumes.bend_none_rad`), so a body lying in a skydive keeps them on its chest.

Each pouch has a reach margin (`volumes.pouches.reach_margin_m`), a fallback taken only when nothing else is in reach, because a hand feeling for a pouch lands a little off it. A pouch is placed only while its feature acts and it has something to give. A unit test holds every volume clear of every other, and every pouch clear of every socket, on several body sizes, turned, and with either hand dominant. Every socket and pouch is marked on the body ([grip.md, "Markers"](grip.md#markers)), and a grip press that takes nothing is logged with the nearest target it missed. The pouch models are [pouch-models.md](pouch-models.md).

## The foregrip

A long gun held in one hand while the other is free has a foregrip ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The gun in either hand"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-gun-in-either-hand)). The free hand's grab volume (`foregrip.radius_m`) is around the weapon's own grip point for that hand ([`WeaponBase::GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition)), or `foregrip.fallback_distance_m` along the barrel for a weapon without one; a hand on any other part of a physical gun braces it there ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)). The weapon's geometry in the holding wrist's frame is measured on the input tick from the gun's last placement, only when its wrist target was reached at full weight, and frozen for a hold.

While held, the holding hand's poses turn about its grip so the foregrip lies on the line from the holding hand to the support hand, and the support hand's poses move onto the foregrip, keeping the controller's rotation. The turn is the shortest rotation that carries the grip-to-foregrip direction onto that line, so the roll stays the holding hand's. It stops at `foregrip.max_turn_deg`, past which the support hand falls away and the hold lets go at the release distance, so a hand crossing behind cannot spin the gun toward the player; below `foregrip.min_span_m` the line is too short to aim along and the pose fades out over twice that. The pose is applied to the cockpit-frame hands every hand subsystem starts from, so the IK, the late update, the sight line, and the overlay see the same pose. The engine's own off-hand grip IK stays suppressed ([hands-and-aiming.md, "The secondary hand IK"](hands-and-aiming.md#the-secondary-hand-ik)). Engaging and releasing blend over `foregrip.blend_s`.

The hold lasts while the support hand's grip is held, and ends when it opens, when the hand moves further than `foregrip.release_distance_m` from the foregrip (measured with the holding hand fully turned, so only what the turn and a slide along the gun cannot take up counts), when a physical gun's brace breaks ([physical-guns.md, "The seat and the joints"](physical-guns.md#the-seat-and-the-joints)), when the weapon leaves the hands, or when a hand loses tracking.

## The one-handed weight

Held in one hand, a long gun is awkward, so the player braces it. The class comes from [`WeaponBase::m_Class`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Class): pistols, revolvers, and SMGs are sidearms; assault rifles, shotguns, and sniper rifles are rifles; launchers, miniguns, mortars, the mounted-calibre guns, and the special weapons are heavy. An unset class counts as a rifle in a two-handed slot and a sidearm otherwise. Each class has its tuning (`heft.sidearm`, `heft.rifle`, `heft.heavy`), and each hand's gun its own weight, spring, and freeze (`controllers::weapons::heft`).

The weight is applied to the active hands after the foregrip pose and before the recoil, so the IK, the late update, the sight line, and the overlay carry it, and a shot goes where the drooping gun points. The barrel (the wrist frame's fingers axis) pitches down by `sag_deg` plus `reach_sag_deg` as the hand moves from `heft.reach_near_m` to `heft.reach_far_m` from the head, scaled by how level it is; it sways in pitch and yaw on two incommensurate waves (`sway_deg` at `heft.sway_hz`), never rolling; and the result follows on a damped rotational spring (`lag_stiffness`, `lag_damping_ratio`), so the gun trails a fast turn and settles. A one-handed shot's kick is multiplied by `kick_scale`, and a detour on [`WeaponBase::GetCurrentScatterValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetCurrentScatterValue) multiplies the player's scatter by `spread_scale`. The foregrip's weight scales it all down to nothing once engaged.

The volumes on a held gun (the foregrip, the well, the action) are placed on the gun as shown, with its two-handed pose and weight. While a round for the reload is in hand, the gun in the other hand freezes, and while the support hand is in the foregrip's volume the gun it closes on freezes, keeping its turn without lag or sway, so the well and the foregrip move rigidly with the hand.

## The two-handed weight

Braced, a long gun still handles as a heavy object rather than a pointer: it follows the hands on a critically damped rotational spring (`heft.braced.<class>.stiffness`, for `smg`, `rifle`, and `heavy`; pistols and revolvers carry none) and sags a little (`sag_deg`). A swing at a steady speed trails by about twice the speed over the square root of the stiffness. `heft.braced.strength` scales it all, and the whole turn is limited to `heft.braced.max_angle_deg`. The support hand is turned with the gun about the holding grip, so the gun drives both hands. As the grip engages the two weights blend by its weight, the springs by their natural frequencies.

## Recoil

A detour on [`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) reads the change in the weapon's per-tick recoil ([`m_RecoilPitch`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilPitch), [`m_RecoilYaw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilYaw)) across each shot of the player's gun and kicks a per-hand damped spring by it, about the controller's grip: a pitch of the barrel, a yaw about the axis up the handle, and a shift back along the barrel (the handling tuning's `recoil` section, scaled by `controllers.weapons.recoil.kick_scale`, the pause menu's `RECOIL STRENGTH`). The spring's offset is applied after the weight, so everything that reads the hands carries it, and the aim follows the rendered gun: a player who holds still sees it climb, and one who pulls against the climb keeps it on target. Under the foregrip the kick is smaller and the spring stiffer (`recoil.two_hand_kick_scale`, `recoil.two_hand_stiffness_scale`). The game's scatter stays on top. Recoil and the fire rumble follow the gun that fired, read from its attach bone at the per-shot seam.

While the guns aim by hand, a replacing detour on [`WeaponCameraModifier::ProcessCameraContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::weapon_camera_modifier::WeaponCameraModifier##func-ProcessCameraContext) writes the camera transform without turning the boom by the recoil or running the snap-aim assist, because the boom's turn persists into the camera's yaw and pitch, and the camera belongs to the headset.

## The action-runs tier

A reload, a draw, or a holster ([`SB_IS_RELOADING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_RELOADING), [`SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON), [`SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON)) is the weapon-action state, which the hand tiers assign the action-runs tier ([hands-and-aiming.md, "Hand tiers"](hands-and-aiming.md#hand-tiers)): the game plays the action while the hand stays on its controller. The fire inputs are dropped from the tables' output while the state holds and the hand holding the weapon is in that tier, so a trigger pull does not fire during the action.

## Fire with empty hands

The game draws the selected weapon when the fire input arrives with nothing wielded or during a holster, and when the precision-aim input arrives with nothing wielded ([`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)). Under the controller scheme a weapon comes from a socket, the weapon wheel, or a slot select instead, so for the length of that call the player's fire and precision-aim entries of [`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs) read zero whenever they would draw, and are put back afterwards for everything that reads them later in the tick (`controllers::weapons::unarmed_fire`, in the weapon handling's detour in `controllers::aim_state`). For the same reason, while a grenade or a charge is in a hand the fire input is held back unless the other hand holds a gun.

## The empty weapon

The game switches to another weapon when the trigger of an empty weapon that cannot reload is pulled, which would take a gun out of the hand that drew it ([weapon-selection.md, "The switch away from an empty weapon"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-selection.md#the-switch-away-from-an-empty-weapon)). A detour on [`WeaponBase::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase) marks the check of the player's weapon on its thread, and a detour on [`Inventory::TryToSwitchToValidWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-TryToSwitchToValidWeapon) reports, inside a marked check, that no weapon qualified (`controllers::weapons::empty`). The game then plays the out-of-ammunition notice and effect, with no shot, and the hand pulses (`Event::DryFire`). The switch's other caller, the weapon-equip state task, runs outside a check and is unaffected, as are manual selection and scripted switches.

## Grenades and C4

Grenades and C4 come from their pouches into a hand and leave it by opening the fingers ([grip.md, "Letting go"](grip.md#letting-go)); nothing throws them from a button, and the game's own planting mode, which aims a ghost with the camera, is bypassed. While one hand holds a gun only the other may take an explosive, so one is never primed in the gun's hand, and while a grenade or a charge is in a hand the fire input is held back unless the other hand holds a gun (see "Fire with empty hands"). The pin, the fuse, the throw's motion, catching, putting an item back, the beacon, C4 pressed onto a surface, taking a placed charge back, the detonator, and the detonation are [grenades.md](grenades.md); the engine side is [planted-explosives.md](../../engine/gameplay/planted-explosives.md).

## Melee

The game's melee input is on no binding and no gesture starts it, because the game's melee lunges the body toward its target and plays its own attack, taking the body from the player. An empty palm meets the world ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)) but passes through characters.
