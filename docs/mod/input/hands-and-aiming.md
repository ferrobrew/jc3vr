# Hands and aiming

The controller-driven hands and what aims by them. Everything here acts only under the controller scheme ([controllers.md, "Scheme selection"](controllers.md#scheme-selection)). The code is in `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/` (`aim_state.rs`, `hands/`, `fingers/`, `aim/`, `overlay/`), the scopes in `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/scope/`, and the render passes in `crates/jc3vr-render/src/controllers/`.

## The aim-state pin and the mental-state cuts

The aim state ([aim-state.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-state.md)) switches the full-body states, strafing, the arm aim IK, and the jump and hang facing. The mod pins it clear for the local player: pinned set, the mental state would stay in combat and the aim IK and facing would need cutting one by one, while pinned clear, firing still starts combat and walking does not. The cost is the laser sight's beam, which draws only while weapon aim is set.

- [`StateBitFlag::SB_NO_WEAPON_AIM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_NO_WEAPON_AIM) is set around the player's aim-input function, its only reader, so [`AimState::m_AimingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AimState##flag-m_AimingWeapon) stays clear; fire gating reads neither. [`AimState::m_AimingGrapple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AimState##flag-m_AimingGrapple) is cleared after the grapple's pre-simulation update, so a tether does not turn the body.
- The player's `NAimIKTask`, `NRightArmAimIK`, `NGrapplingFireArmIK`, and `NReelInAimIK` return early while the wrist IK owns their hands.
- [`ControlParameters::m_AimTargetWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::ControlParameters##field-m_AimTargetWeapons) is held ahead of the head along the body's forward, so no residual aim blend turns the body.
- The wall block that [`WallPenetrationWeaponComponent::UpdateWeapon_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WallPenetrationWeaponComponent##func-UpdateWeapon_Serial) computes from the animated barrel is cleared, because the rendered muzzle is at the controller.
- The mental state keeps running for AI perception, but [`StateBitFlag::SB_DISABLE_MENTAL_STATE_DRAW_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_MENTAL_STATE_DRAW_WEAPON) is set before each [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling) (which clears it every tick) to stop the automatic draw. The automatic holsters are dropped through the act arbiter (`controllers::acts`), and a detour on [`Character::HolsterWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HolsterWeapon) lets the player's own holster pass.

The body turns and moves through the orientation and displacement evaluators instead ([input.md, "Tapping the move input"](input.md#tapping-the-move-input)).

## The wrist targets

The engine side is [humanik.md](../../engine/character/humanik.md) and [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../../engine/character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md). A wrist target is the grip pose in the cockpit frame times the grip offset (`controllers.hands.grip`, mirrored for the left), remapped about the shoulders ("Arm reach"), and carried into the world through the head's transform, so the hands and the view agree. It is queued on HumanIK's `MAIN` pass at the entry of [`Character::UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel), at the `UPPER_BODY` step, scaled by the hand's tier weight, as a rotation from the animated wrist frame built from the knuckles. The engine refreshes the wielded weapon from the attach bone after the solve, so the gun follows within the tick. Under the procedural torso the targets go at the solve gate with elbow hints ([procedural-body.md, "The arms"](../body/procedural-body.md#the-arms)).

### The rigid grip

The attach bone is animated, so on a tracked wrist the gun would turn over in a still hand ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../../engine/character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-guns-pose-in-the-hand-is-animated)). A pre-call step in [`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms) (`hands::grip`) writes each driven hand's attach bones to the wrist composed with the hand's bind-pose grip ([`AnimationController::GetBindPoseBoneMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetBindPoseBoneMatrix)), blended by the controller weight, after both IK solves, so the muzzle effects, the physics proxy, the shot origin, and the sight line follow the solved wrist. The right hand's bind-pose grip holds a gun backwards, so it can take the left's mirrored ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md#the-right-hands-grip)). The gun-in-hand watch (`controllers::weapons::grip_watch`) warns when a gun snaps or sweeps in the hand.

### The secondary hand IK

[`Character::UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass) runs after the `MAIN` solve on blackboard weight keys that animation tasks write (the grapple fire arm, the parachute handles, the off-hand grip), some inside the pose-finalization pass ([animation-layers.md](../../engine/character/animation-layers.md#state-tasks-inside-the-pose-finalization-pass)). At its entry each driven hand's present key is zeroed (a missing one is never created), and so is the wrist's translation reach, because the pass drops a stale target only at zero reach ([humanik.md](../../engine/character/humanik.md#target-entry-lifetime-and-the-is_valid-flag)). While the off-hand grip runs for the other hand, a driven wrist gets a weak `SECONDARY` target at its `MAIN` one, or the grip's solve drags it off the controller.

## Hand tiers

Each scripted state takes one of four tiers (`hands::tiers`, `HandTierTable::PLAN`), classified once per input tick from the mode snapshot:

- Free: the hand follows its controller (on foot, swimming, seated, under the parachute).
- Action runs: the game plays a reload, draw, or holster while the hand stays on its controller ([weapon-handling.md](weapon-handling.md#the-action-runs-tier)).
- Anchored: the hand is attached to a world point and its movement drives the game: the grapple reel and hang, and a mounted gun ([vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md#mounted-guns)), classified before the vehicle states since a gunner seat would read as an entry.
- Yielded: the animation owns the hand, blending over `tiers.yield_blend_s` and `tiers.return_blend_s`: ledge grabs, climbs, vaults, the helicopter hang ([stunt-positions.md](stunt-positions.md)), vehicle entry and exit, melee, ragdoll, cutscenes, and the wingsuit, whose membrane is skinned to the arms (they still steer, [flight.md](flight.md)).

On the grapple the anchored hand keeps its controller position and points at the wire ([`GrapplingHook::GetActiveWireAttachmentGraphicsWorldPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetActiveWireAttachmentGraphicsWorldPosition)), and its displacement pushes the hang ([grapple.md, "The game's grapple hang"](../grapple/grapple.md#the-games-grapple-hang)). Only the drawn hand turns, so a re-fire goes where the controller points. A grapple hand that holds anything stays free.

## Arm reach

A fully extended player arm must ask for a fully extended Rico arm, about the joint HumanIK solves from (the hands tuning's `reach` section).

- The measurement (`hands/rig/`): Rico's arm, measured at the pre-solve seam from the bones HumanIK drives, whose shoulder is the upper-arm joint, not the collar ([humanik.md](../../engine/character/humanik.md#effector-id-model)).
- The remap: the wrist's offset from the player's shoulder, scaled by Rico's arm over the player's (`BodyDimensions::arm_reach`), placed about Rico's shoulder. Rico's shoulders sit where his reference pose holds them (`body::CHARACTER_EYES_TO_RIGHT_SHOULDER`), never at the animated ones, which swing each stride and pump a rifle in and out of the hands. On the ground both follow the body rig's shoulder estimate, so a crouch or lean carries the hands.
- The reach limit (`hands::reach::ReachLimit`) holds each wrist within Rico's arm. It sits in `hands::active_hands_raw`, which every hand subsystem starts from; only the crouched floor reach reads the controller before it.
- The pull ramps the wrist target's pull as the reach nears full, so the shoulder and chest follow; zero under the procedural torso.
- The detach: a shortfall the solve leaves shows at render time as the hand and gun leaving the arm toward the controller, up to a cap, with the forearm stretched. The shot stays on the sim-rate transform.

## The late update

The engine's interpolation leaves the rendered hands one to two sim ticks behind, so `hands/late/` corrects them each rendered frame: the latest controller pose, at the render-time head, times the inverse of the last two ticks' IK targets interpolated by the engine's fraction. A post-call detour on [`WeaponBase::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateRender) moves a held gun by the correction, and the palette edit moves the hand and finger bones by the same, so they never separate; the gun's model parts ([`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-UpdateRender)) ride the hand they are on (`hands::parts`). The muzzle flash, the shot origin, and the wire's near end stay at sim rate. The presentation layer re-solves the arms to the corrected hands ([presentation-layer.md](../rendering/presentation-layer.md)).

## Finger curls

Each finger of a driven hand gets a curl and a splay (`fingers/`, tuned by `crates/jc3vr-body/tuning/fingers.toml`). With `XR_EXT_hand_tracking` joints the curl is the finger's measured flexion; otherwise it is estimated from what the fingers rest on: the thumb on the stick and buttons, the index on the trigger, the rest on the grip value.

The pose is absolute: each joint's bend is measured from a straight finger and replaces the animated bend, because the free-hand animation already curls the fingers and a bend added on top drives them into the palm. The straight finger is built from the animated palm, since the defs bind no reference pose, and every axis comes from the hand's geometry, so the hands mirror with no per-side sign. The fingers are written once per sim tick in the post-call detour on [`Character::UpdatePropEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePropEffects), with [`AnimationController::SetJoint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-SetJoint) over the finalized pose ([skeleton.md](../../engine/character/skeleton.md)); the late update carries them with the hand. A hand that holds something takes its grip pose ([grip.md, "Grip poses"](grip.md#grip-poses)).

## Gun aim

The engine side is [aim-pipeline.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md). The aim runs around [`PlayerAimControl::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdatePostSim): before it the hands are composed and the grapple ray handed over; after it the hand rays are cast ([`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) with [`PlayerAimRaycastFilter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastFilter)) and the aim points published for the per-weapon update that follows on the same thread. The grenade throw and the melee target follow the hands too.

Each gun aims along its own sight line, from its corrected transform, fire point, and sight correction. The hit becomes that hand's aim point, never nearer than `ray.min_target_distance_m`, because the game fires along the barrel for a point near the muzzle. [`WeaponBase::UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase) is handed the point for the gun about to fire ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)), the only seam that gives two guns two directions. The character-level point follows the dominant hand, because without it the game aims a fixed distance ahead of the body ([aim-pipeline.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#from-aim-control-to-the-character-cplayerupdatepostcamera)). Per-hand aim assist (`controllers.aim.assist_strength`, off by default) scores targets with [`PlayerAimControl::GetFitnessScore`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetFitnessScore) around the hand's sight line.

## Sight lines

A weapon's sight correction moves and turns the sight line in the fire point's frame (`aim/sights/`), looked up by the weapon's name hash: the settings' entry (`controllers.aim.sights`), then the embedded `tools/sights/sights.json` ([README](../../../tools/sights/README.md)), then none. Only the table's `scoped`, `pistol`, and `tuned` grades are used, because iron sights with rails or carry handles do not derive reliably. The sight tuning tool (Weapons > Aim, sights, and scopes) places a marker ahead of the head, solves the offset that puts the sight line through it when the player lines up and captures, and copies the result as a `tuned` JSON line.

## The world-space overlay

The overlay draws sprites and lines into the scene per eye, for readouts that need their own depth. It draws at the end of [`RenderEngine::PostDraw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-PostDraw), after the post chain and the upscaler, so it keeps its colours through exposure and does not smear under temporal reconstruction.

Each producer owns a retained slot that its submission replaces and that expires on its own, behind a leaf lock any thread may take. A hand-anchored point is placed from the render-time pose, and a once-a-tick point can be interpolated between ticks (`Anchor::Ticked`) to ride with the view from a moving vehicle. The producers include the grapple highlight, the body rig's diagnostics ([rig.md](../body/rig.md#settings-and-diagnostics)), the docked guns, the grip markers ([grip.md, "Markers"](grip.md#markers)), and the debug panel's pointer. The primitives are resolved against the cyclopean eye so the eyes fuse, made relative to the frame origin because absolute `f32` world-to-clip jitters at the game's coordinates, and sorted far to near, because the overlay writes no depth and tests against the main depth texture instead.

### The game's reticles

The game's weapon and grapple reticles mark the gaze's aim ([aim-pipeline.md, "The reticles"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#the-reticles)), which nothing aims along under hand aim. `hud/game_reticle.rs` hides the grapple reticle under the controller scheme and the weapon reticle and hit marker wherever the hands aim the weapon, through the full-screen overlay suppression's clip handles (`hud/scaleform.rs`). Where they show, the HUD atlas draws them at the aim point ([ui-decoupling.md](../rendering/ui-decoupling.md#the-atlas-as-built)).

## Scopes

Precision aim zooms the camera and, on a sniper rifle, hides the character and its hands ([aim-pipeline.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#precision-aim-and-sniper-zoom)). A detour on [`Player::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-SetAimMode), every entry's path, refuses it under the controller scheme, and one on [`Player::UpdateAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateAimMode) leaves one entered earlier.

A scoped weapon (the sniper class of [`WeaponBase::m_Class`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Class)) gets a magnified lens instead, drawn on the eye close behind its eyepiece. The lens resamples a rendered eye image rather than rendering again, mapping a tangent `t` off the sight line to `t / zoom` at the aim point's distance. It samples the other eye's image, because the sighting eye's own has the scope model over the magnified region; in two-pass stereo that can be the previous frame's.
