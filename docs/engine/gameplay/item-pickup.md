# Item pickup and loose bodies

What the player can pick up, how the game picks up a weapon lying in the world, and what a loose object's body is to the Havok world: its motion, its owner, how its velocity is set and an impulse moves it, and how bodies are joined to one another. Hitting and pushing a body is in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md).

## What lies in the world

A weapon in the world (dropped by a character, broken out of a supply drop's cargo, or placed) is a [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase). A weapon is a physics game object of its own, not a rigid object: lying in the world it has its own body, and a physics query that hits it reports the weapon. Its state ([`WeaponBase::m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)) is [`E_WS_DROPPED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_DROPPED) while it lies loose and [`E_WS_PICKING_UP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_PICKING_UP) while it is being picked up. The run-time type test tells it apart ([`WeaponBase::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-TYPE_ID), through [`GameObject::IsType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-IsType) as in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#hit-objects-and-run-time-types)).

A dropped weapon stays until the spawn system's despawn ranking removes it, which depends on its distance from the player and the view, not on time; a weapon anything holds a strong reference to is never removed that way ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md)).

A loose prop (a crate, a barrel, a gas canister, a piece of debris) is a [`RigidObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject), a physics game object with one rigid body that may be static, fixed, keyframed, or dynamic ([`RigidObject::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##func-TYPE_ID)). Characters, vehicles, and weapons are not rigid objects.

A collectible (`CCollectibleObject`) is a plain game object with no physics instance: it is collected through its own collect event, which scripts and interactions send. A physics query never reports one. The other "use" interactables (tombs, tapes, and the like) are interaction graphs entered through the use input, not bodies either.

## Picking up a weapon

Ammunition needs no input. Each weapon in the world carries a pickup interaction; when the player comes within it, the interaction's activation collects what the game collects without asking: a hand grenade is taken outright while the stock is below its maximum, and a weapon whose ammunition type the player already carries gives up its stored ammunition to the inventory, which the HUD shows as an ammunition pickup.

The weapon itself takes the use input. The player's weapon input task sets [`E_INPUT_USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_USE_ITEM) on a click of the [`USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-USE_ITEM) action. The inventory's weapon handling ([`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)) acts on it while the character may pick up ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPickup): the animation's permission, [`Inventory::m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup), with [`SB_DISABLE_PICKUP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_PICKUP) clear). On land it queues the full-body pickup act (`ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON`). The weapon's pickup interaction starts the pickup when it executes ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart): the weapon leaves the physics world), and the weapon's pickup track message completes it ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem): the wielded weapon is unequipped, this one goes into the inventory and is equipped, its slot is selected, its stored ammunition is added, and the character says its pickup line). A swimming character picks up the first weapon among its interaction proxy's valid entries at once and plays the partial pickup act instead.

What completing the pickup does to the weapon, and how a weapon is dropped, is in [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md).

The game, not the input, chooses the weapon: the one among the interaction proxy's valid entries. Which entry wins when several weapons are in range, and how the pickup interaction's execution follows the act, are unverified.

## A body in the Havok world

The Havok world ([`hknpWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld)) keeps its bodies in one array indexed by body id ([`hknpWorld::m_bodies`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodies)), up to the highest id it has allocated ([`hknpWorld::m_peakBodyIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_peakBodyIndex)). A body ([`hknpBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody)) is moved by a motion ([`hknpBody::m_motionId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_motionId) into [`hknpWorld::m_motions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_motions)), and only a dynamic body ([`hknpBody::IS_DYNAMIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::IS_DYNAMIC) in [`m_flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_flags)) is moved by velocities and impulses. When a body is destroyed its id is freed and later handed to a new body; the freed slot's motion id is invalid ([`hknpBody::INVALID_MOTION_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::INVALID_MOTION_ID)) until then. A body id alone therefore does not name an object across frames.

The motion ([`hknpMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion)) holds the centre of mass and the orientation of the motion's frame, the inverse inertia and the inverse mass as Havok half floats ([`hknpMotion::m_inverseInertia`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion##field-m_inverseInertia); a zero inverse mass is an infinite mass), the linear velocity in world space, and the angular velocity in the motion's frame. The motion's frame is the body's principal frame: the inverse inertia is three values, one per axis of that frame, and the world-space inverse inertia is that diagonal turned by the motion's orientation. Several bodies attached to one another share one motion, and with it one centre of mass and one set of mass properties. Positions are Havok space: the game-space position less [`PhysicsSystem::m_AccumulatedShift`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##field-m_AccumulatedShift), as in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#pushing-a-body).

A body's owner is one of its properties. The body manager keeps per-property buffers indexed by body id ([`hknpWorld::m_bodyProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodyProperties), looked up by key with [`hknpBodyPropertyMap::getWithDefault`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBodyPropertyMap##func-getWithDefault)); a body has a property when its bit is set in the buffer's occupancy. The engine's instance property ([`hknpWorld::BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld::BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE)) holds the body's [`PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance), and the instance's owner ([`PfxInstance::m_GameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance##field-m_GameObject)) is the physics game object a ray query reports for the body ([`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) reads it this way).

## Setting a body's velocity

[`hknpWorld::setBodyVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyVelocity) sets a dynamic body's linear and angular velocity, both given in world space, and wakes the body if it sleeps; [`setBodyLinearVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyLinearVelocity) and [`setBodyAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyAngularVelocity) set one each. Like [`applyBodyImpulseAt`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-applyBodyImpulseAt), they leave a body that is not dynamic alone. The disassembler labels `setBodyVelocity` as a getter; the body writes both velocities.

An impulse at a point ([`hknpWorld::applyBodyImpulseAt`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-applyBodyImpulseAt)) is the same arithmetic a rigid-body solver does: the linear velocity gains the impulse times the inverse mass, and the angular velocity gains the impulse's moment about the centre of mass, turned into the motion's frame and scaled axis by axis by the inverse inertia. Everything an impulse at a point does to a body can therefore be predicted from the motion alone.

The velocity setters change the motion directly and are not deferred, so they belong outside the physics step, where the engine's own impulses and queries run; that no step overlaps the player's aim update is unverified (see [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#threading)).

## Joined bodies

A body may be joined to another by a Havok constraint, which the physics step resolves together with the body's contacts: whatever velocity the body is given before the step, the step removes what the constraint forbids. The game joins bodies in three ways.

- A constraint placed in the world, [`Constraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint) (`CConstraint`, told apart by [`Constraint::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint##func-TYPE_ID)), is a game object of its own that names two objects by object id and joins their bodies once both exist. Its data picks the kind of joint: fixed, deformable fixed, ball and socket, stiff spring, hinge, limited hinge (with angular limits and friction), ragdoll, wheel, or prismatic, optionally driven by a position, spring-damper, or velocity motor and optionally breakable past a force. The joined objects are found by object id, not by type, so any object with a body can be joined; which props in the shipped world are placed with one (gates, hatches, signs, and the like), and that they are rigid objects, is unverified.
- A vehicle's parts ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)) carry their own constraints from the physics prototype: a part attached with a constraint (`CPartInstance::AttachPartWithConstraint`, unbound) hangs from its parent by it, a loosened door or hood swings on its hinge, and the constraint breaks when the part breaks off (`CPartInstance::BreakPartConstraint`, unbound). A merged part has no body of its own, so a physics query on it reports the vehicle; a loosened one has its own body, owned by a vehicle-part object ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-loose-door)).
- The grappling hook's wire, the magnet weapon's gravity field, and a ragdoll's joints make constraints at run time.

A collision filter keeps joined bodies from colliding with each other, and a group filter gives each body a system group whose members never collide; `hknpWorld::setBodyCollisionFilterInfo` (unbound) changes a body's filter word on the live world. The filter word's layout is unverified.
