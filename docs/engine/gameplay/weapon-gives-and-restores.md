# Weapon gives, removals, and restores

How the game puts weapons into the player's slots on its own and takes them out again: the give through the respawn table, the marked and immediate removals, and the three caches that missions, the graph script, and the challenges use to take the player's weapons away and give them back. What keeps a weapon object alive, the spawn reset, and the save are in [weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md); the pickup, the equip, and the drop in [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md).

## Every way into a slot

Every way a weapon enters a slot ends in the inventory's add, [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon). It has three callers:

- [`Character::PutWeaponIntoInventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PutWeaponIntoInventory), a direct call of the add, which the completed pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem)), a mounted gun's [`StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing), the weapon's track-message handling, and the supply drop's broken cargo use;
- [`Character::PickUpWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PickUpWeapon), which fills the magazine to the tuned size ([`WeaponBase::GetMagazineSize`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetMagazineSize)), adds the weapon, and with [`E_EQUIP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::EquipMode##variant-E_EQUIP) selects the slot for the dual-wield, two-handed, special, heavy, and FOW slots ([`Character::SelectWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SelectWeapon)); the respawn hand-over and the spawn-and-equip callback call it;
- the character's looting of its children (`CCharacter::LootChildren`).

So the add is the one entry to the slots, and the gives below are the subset of it that arrives through the respawn table.

## The give

Every weapon the game gives the player takes one chain: missions and their checkpoint restores, the challenges, the graph script's `SpawnWeapons` node, the `equip weapon` script and console event, a rack's infinite copies, the respawn after a spawn reset, and the game-completion reward.

1. [`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon) writes the slot's entry of the respawn table: the type ([`m_WeaponToSpawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawn)), the rounds ([`m_WeaponToSpawnAmmunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnAmmunition)), whether rounds beyond the magazine go to the reserve ([`m_WeaponToSpawnExtraAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnExtraAmmo)), whether the occupant is dropped rather than despawned ([`m_WeaponToSpawnDropOld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnDropOld)), and, when the give starts equipped, the equipped slot ([`m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon)).
2. [`Inventory::RequestWeaponSpawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RequestWeaponSpawn) asks the spawn system for the entry's type unless a spawn is already pending for the slot ([`m_WeaponPendingSpawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponPendingSpawn)). The disassembler swaps the names of this function and the grappling device's request ([`RequestSpawnGrapplingDevice`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RequestSpawnGrapplingDevice)).
3. The spawn system calls back [`InventoryWeaponSpawnCallback`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/character::inventory##func-InventoryWeaponSpawnCallback), which despawns a spawned weapon that is not a profile item and hands any other to the inventory.
4. [`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned) checks the weapon's spawn tag index against its slot's entry (the slot being the weapon's [`m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)). On a match it clears the slot's removal mark, picks the equip mode (equipped for the entry's equipped slot, after unequipping the wielded weapon; equipped only into the selected slot when no slot is named; not equipped otherwise), and passes the weapon to `PickUpWeapon`. It then sets the magazine from the entry, moves the overflow into the reserve when the entry asks, applies the character's visibility to every slot weapon, and clears the entry. On a mismatch it removes the slot's occupant, or despawns the newcomer when the slot is empty.
5. `PickUpWeapon` adds the weapon, whose add drops or despawns the slot's previous occupant by the entry's drop-old flag.

An ordinary world weapon never passes through the callback or `WeaponSpawned`: only respawn-table entries do. Among those, the respawn after a spawn reset is the one requested by the post-reset event's handler ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-spawn-reset-and-the-respawn-table)); every other arrival is a give. Pickups from the ground, racks, and characters arrive through `PickupItem` (or `StartUsing` for a mounted gun), and the supply drops through their cargo, all through `PutWeaponIntoInventory`.

[`Inventory::RespawnPlayerGear`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RespawnPlayerGear) re-requests every respawn entry not already pending, and the grappling device; the player's post-physics update calls it once the save data has loaded. [`RespawnPlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RespawnPlayerWeapon) requests the table with the two-handed slot as the equipped slot.

## Removals

A removal is marked and carried out later, or done at once.

- [`Inventory::ForceRemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-ForceRemoveWeapon) marks a slot ([`m_WeaponToRemove`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToRemove)), optionally emptying the weapon's magazine and its type's reserve and clearing the slot's respawn entry; [`ForceRemoveAllWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-ForceRemoveAllWeapons) marks every slot, and [`CancelRemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-CancelRemoveWeapon) clears a mark.
- The inventory's pre-physics update ([`Inventory::UpdatePrePhysics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdatePrePhysics)) carries out each mark through [`Internal_RemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-Internal_RemoveWeapon): a mounted gun is stopped and released, and any other occupant is despawned.
- [`Inventory::DespawnWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DespawnWeapon) empties a slot and despawns its weapon at once; the challenges call it directly, past the marks.
- The weapon's despawn ([`WeaponBase::Despawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Despawn)) unequips it, hides it, puts its bodies into the physics world, sets its spawn ranking so the spawn system's despawn takes it, and stops its fire. The object lives on, hidden and in the world, while something else holds a strong reference to it.

The other ways out of a slot are the drop ([`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon), in [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md#dropping)) and the plain removal ([`Inventory::RemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RemoveWeapon), in [weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#into-and-out-of-a-slot-without-a-drop)). The character's damage handling marks every slot and clears the inventory for one instant-removal damage flag (unverified: read from the symbol dump).

## The mission manager's checkpoint cache

The mission manager ([`MissionManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager)) snapshots the player's slots at every mission checkpoint. Saving a restart point ([`SaveRestartPointToMap`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##func-SaveRestartPointToMap)) while a mission is active runs [`CacheWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##func-CacheWeapons), which records each of the eight slots ([`m_CachedWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##field-m_CachedWeapons), one [`WeaponCacheEntry`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graph_script::WeaponCacheEntry) per slot: the type, the reserve of its ammunition type, and the magazine), counting a mounted weapon as empty. It removes nothing.

[`RestoreCachedWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##func-RestoreCachedWeapons) replays the snapshot. Its one caller is the restart from a restart point ([`GoToRestartPoint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##func-GoToRestartPoint)), right after the player's respawn, while a mission is active: a death, a mission failure, or a checkpoint restart. Per entry, the reserve of the slot's current weapon's type is zeroed; a weapon of the cached type keeps its object and gets the cached reserve and magazine; another type, or an empty slot, gets a give (the reserve and the magazine as its rounds, not equipped, overflow to the reserve, the occupant despawned); and a cached empty slot that now holds a weapon is marked for removal.

## The graph script's cache

The graph script node `SpawnWeapons` ([`NGSONodes_SpawnWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/graph_script##func-NGSONodes_SpawnWeapons)) strips, gives, caches, and restores a character's weapons with a [`NodeWeaponCache`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graph_script::NodeWeaponCache). Its fields are a removal type (only replace, remove where the field is empty, or remove all and replace), a weapon and ammunition for each of the dual-wield, two-handed, special, hand-grenade, and planted-explosive slots, the equipped weapon, and the cache.

- On its main input it clears the cache, records every slot ([`CacheAllWeaponSlots`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graph_script::NodeWeaponCache##func-CacheAllWeaponSlots), which also forces the slots out for the remove-all type), and returns the player to normal aim. Then per field it marks the slot for removal for an empty field under remove-where-empty or under remove-all, keeps a weapon of the field's type (setting its reserve and cancelling its removal), and otherwise gives the field's weapon, equipped for the equipped field's slot. With no equipped field it unequips the wielded weapon through the deferred unequip ([`WeaponBase::UnequipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UnequipDeferred)) and relaxes the character; with an equipped field and nothing to give it selects that slot.
- A second input only caches.
- A third input restores: a return to normal aim, [`NodeWeaponCache::RestoreWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graph_script::NodeWeaponCache##func-RestoreWeapons), and a refresh of the HUD's weapons ([`HUDUI::InitWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-InitWeapons)).
- Its update waits until no slot's spawn is pending.

`CacheAllWeaponSlots` is called only from this node, and removes only for the remove-all type; the remove-where-empty strips go through `ForceRemoveWeapon` directly.

The give-back is one function, `RestoreWeapons`. It follows the mission manager's rules but skips the heavy slot and never removes the planted explosive. The disassembler mislabels it as the cache's lookup, and gives its name to an unrelated player-save-data store helper.

## The challenges

The challenges keep their own record of the loadout in the challenge manager and the challenge object.

- Starting a challenge ([`ROMManager::StartROM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMManager##func-StartROM)) whose spawn point overrides the loadout records each slot's type and total ammunition, marks every slot for removal, and gives the spawn point's weapons, equipped for the dual-wield and two-handed slots and dropping the occupants. Aborting it ([`AbortROM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMManager##func-AbortROM)) with that record marks every slot again and gives the recorded weapons back the same way.
- The challenge object's initialisation ([`ROMObject::PostInit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMObject##func-PostInit)), unless restarting or retrying, records every slot's type and magazine and every reserve; it then despawns at once each slot the challenge's loadout permission does not allow (the grenade and planted-explosive slots, and each of the dual-wield, two-handed, and special slots whose profile item fails the challenge's mask), and gives the weapons the player picked in the challenge's loadout menu.
- [`ROMObject::RestoreWeaponsAndAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMObject##func-RestoreWeaponsAndAmmo) empties every slot and gives each slot recorded with a weapon its recorded type and magazine, then restores every reserve but the beacons' and flares', and requests an autosave. The challenge object's destruction calls it to despawn the slots and give at once; for one kind of challenge the destruction instead marks the challenge manager, and the load state's reset step calls it to take the slots' weapons out without despawning them and to write only the respawn table, which the spawn reset then spawns.

Starting, retrying, restarting, and aborting a challenge each teleport with the load state, so each also passes through the spawn reset.

## What a restore does to a slot

All three restores compare by spawn tag index, slot by slot, and read nothing but the eight slots and the reserves. A slot holding a different type than the cache is replaced by a give that despawns the occupant rather than dropping it; an empty slot is given its weapon; a slot that holds a weapon where the cache had none is marked for removal, which despawns its weapon at the next pre-physics update; and a slot of the cached type keeps its object, with the cached reserve and magazine. Every restore first zeroes the reserve of each occupied slot's ammunition type, so a reserve is reset to what the cache recorded for the slot's weapon. A weapon held outside the slots is neither removed, despawned, nor duplicated by any of them.
