# Weapon equip, pickup, and drop

How a weapon object moves between the world, the inventory, the hands, and the body: what completing a pickup does, what equipping and unequipping do, and how a weapon is dropped. Picking a weapon up through the use input is in [item-pickup.md](item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon), the magazine and the reserve in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md), and the attach bones in [hands-locomotion-and-state.md](../character/hands-locomotion-and-state.md), what keeps a weapon alive, the spawn reset, and the save in [weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md), and the game's gives and removals in [weapon-gives-and-restores.md](weapon-gives-and-restores.md).

## One object for its whole life

A weapon is one [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase) whether it lies in the world, is held, or is holstered. The character's inventory holds one weapon per slot ([`Inventory::m_Weapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Weapons)), and the weapon's state ([`WeaponBase::m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)) says where it is. The magazine ([`m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo)) belongs to the object and travels with it; the reserve belongs to the character.

The weapon's state setter ([`WeaponBase::SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState)) keeps its bodies in the physics world ([`WeaponBase::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-AddToWorld)) only in the dropped, mounted, and turret states, and wakes them for dropped. Every other state, holstered and wielded included, takes them out ([`RemoveFromWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-RemoveFromWorld)), as does a turret becoming dropped.

Each gun has a body of its own ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)). Taken out of the world, it keeps its id, its shape ([`hknpBody::m_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_shape)), and its motion, mass included. Putting it back ([`WeaponData::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-AddToWorld)) keeps its collision system group or takes a fresh one, sets its layer for the weapon's state, makes it dynamic, and places it at the gun's world matrix ([`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)), so the body's frame is the gun's model frame and its shape is given in that frame.

## The dual-wield weapon

A weapon of the dual-wield slot ([`E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD)) is one object with two guns, one per pistol ([`WeaponBase::m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData)). The attach-bone table puts the first gun in the right hand when wielding or dual-wielding and on the right hip when holstered, and the second in the left hand only when dual-wielding and on the left hip otherwise ([hands-locomotion-and-state.md](../character/hands-locomotion-and-state.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)). Everything else is the object's and so shared: the state, the magazine, the fire interval and trigger state, the reload, the holder, and the drop.

The state decides how many pistols the weapon shows and fires. The equip dual-wields the slot for the player and for a character whose blackboard holds [`WeaponBase::DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##const-DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE), and wields it for any other character. A dual-wield weapon in the wielding state is one pistol:

- the visibility's base implementation ([`WeaponBase::SetVisibleBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetVisibleBase)) hides its guns past the first whenever it shows the weapon. A gun is shown and hidden through its model instance's status bits ([`ModelInstance::m_Flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##field-m_Flags)), and a model part, such as a pistol's slide, shows only while its own gun's model does ([`WeaponComponentBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-SetVisible)), so a hidden gun's parts hide with it;
- the tail of the shot keeps the next gun ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)) on the first, where any other weapon advances it after every shot, and entering the wielding state resets it there.

The same base implementation hides the guns past the first of a non-player character's dual-wield weapon in every state when the character lacks the blackboard value. A player's dual-wield weapon, holstered or dual-wielded, shows both guns and fires them in turn. Nothing applies the rule between calls, so a weapon whose state changes keeps its guns' visibility from the last call until the next; the animation's state tasks set the wielding state without a call ([hands-locomotion-and-state.md](../character/hands-locomotion-and-state.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)).

A dual-wield weapon in the world is one pistol. The drop virtual hides the models of every gun past the first, as it does for any weapon of several guns, and putting the bodies into the world puts in only the first gun's and takes the second's out ([`WeaponBase::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-AddToWorld)), so a dropped pair falls, lies, and is picked up as one pistol. Picked up by the player, it is dual-wielded again and the equip shows both guns. Whether the player's animation accepts the dual-wield weapon held in the wielding state outside the states whose tasks set it is unverified.

## Completing a pickup

[`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem) takes the character's game-object subobject ([`Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##const-GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET)) and ignores any other activator. It:

1. unequips the wielded weapon, which goes to its holster;
2. puts this weapon into the inventory with the equip mode that equips it. The inventory's add runs the weapon's pickup virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup): the holder is set, the state becomes holstered, so its bodies leave the physics world, the pickup interaction is disabled, and the HUD shows the pickup) and then its equip virtual;
3. selects the weapon's slot and adds its stored ammunition ([`m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo)) to the reserve, and plays the pickup vocal;
4. clears the stored ammunition.

It does not refill the magazine, and it plays no animation act. It accepts a weapon in the dropped state with its bodies in the world: a swimming character's pickup calls it on such a weapon directly, with no prior [`PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart), after dropping the weapon already in that slot, and then queues `ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON_PARTIAL` (see [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)). On land, `PickupStart` first puts the weapon in the picking-up state with its bodies out of the world, and the pickup track message calls `PickupItem` later.

Two kinds of weapon are not put into the inventory by it. A hand grenade is left to `PickupStart`, which adds its rounds to the reserve and despawns it. A weapon with [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup) set gives the character a copy from `PickupStart` (spawned from the weapon's spawn tag), and `PickupItem` adds nothing. Both still lose their stored ammunition to `PickupItem`.

## The inventory's add

The inventory's add reads the incoming weapon's slot ([`m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)). When the slot already holds another weapon, that weapon is dropped when the inventory's per-slot drop flag is set, and otherwise removed, which despawns it (a mounted gun is only released). Then the new weapon is stored and picked up, and with the equipping mode the wielded weapon is unequipped and the new one equipped. The grenade, planted-explosive, and beacon slots are put into use at once. The add is [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon), and its counterpart that takes a weapon out of its slot without dropping it is `RemoveWeapon` ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#into-and-out-of-a-slot-without-a-drop)).

## Equip and unequip

The equip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip)) shows the weapon, runs its components' equip, selects its slot, starts using it (its user becomes the character), puts the player's avatar into combat, and sets the state to wielding, or to dual-wielding for the player's dual-wield slot ([below](#the-dual-wield-weapon)). It queues no act: the draw animation comes from the character's draw and the slot-select path, not from the equip. So a weapon equipped through it alone is in the hands at once, with the character's animation never having played a draw.

The unequip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip)) cancels the holder's reload, sets the state to holstered (a heavy weapon is dropped through the inventory instead, and a mounted gun keeps its state), and stops using the weapon. It plays no holster act either. The heavy weapon's drop is the inventory's drop of the heavy slot, whatever the slot holds: a heavy weapon outside the slots is neither dropped nor holstered by it, its state left as it was.

Both virtuals have base implementations ([`OnEquipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnEquipBase) and [`OnUnequipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnUnequipBase)); the mortar, the rocket launcher, the planted explosive, and the fire-leech launcher override the equip, and the sun gun overrides the unequip.

Whether the animation allows a draw or a holster is the inventory's own permission ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing) and [`GetAnimationAllowsHolstering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsHolstering)); the virtuals do not check it.

## Who equips and unequips

Every equip the game makes runs the equip virtual, and every unequip the unequip virtual, so the two virtuals are the choke points:

- Equip: the inventory's add with an equipping mode ([`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon), [below](#the-inventorys-add)); the draw clip's equip track message ([`WeaponBase::HandleTrackMessage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HandleTrackMessage)); the `NWeaponEquipTask` state task ([`NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter)), which with nothing wielded either switches to a weapon with ammunition or, instant, selects the first valid of the two-handed, dual-wield, and special slots and equips it directly, and which no state of Rico's graphs carries; the exit of the weapon-state task ([`NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit)), which restores a wielding or dual-wielding state through the equip; and the deferred equip, which only the AI queues.
- Unequip: the `NWeaponUnequipTask` state task ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter), [below](#vehicles-and-traversal)); the character's neutral state ([`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState)); the holster clip's unequip track message; the inventory's add when it equips another weapon; the pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem)); the respawn hand-over ([`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned)); the despawn ([`WeaponBase::Despawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Despawn)); the drop virtual; and the deferred unequip.

`ForceNeutralState` drops the heavy weapon, stops using the planted explosive, unequips the wielded weapon, and cancels a primed grenade. It runs on every revive (so every respawn), at the start of every teleport ([`UpdateTeleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world##func-UpdateTeleport) and [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstant`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayerInstant)), on the vehicle exit's upward teleport, from the god mode and the walk-on-water mode, when a scripted sequence takes the character ([`Character::SetupForSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetupForSequence)), when the character is enabled, from the network, from the AI's mission-critical teleport, and from the player's ragdoll constraint fix-up.

The weapon the game treats as wielded is [`Character::GetWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWieldingWeapon): the selected slot's weapon in the wielding, dual-wielding, or mounted state, or the planted explosive while planting. Every automatic unequip and the inventory's weapon tick start from it, so a weapon wielded in a slot that is not selected is invisible to them. The selection and the draw are in [weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-selected-slot).

## Deferred operations

A weapon can queue an equip, an unequip, a start-using, a stop-using, or a drop for its next pre-simulation update. Each request sets a flag on the weapon ([`m_DeferredEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquip), [`m_DeferredUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUnequip), [`m_DeferredStartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStartUsing), [`m_DeferredStopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStopUsing), and [`m_DeferredDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredDrop), with the character in [`m_DeferredEquiper`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquiper) or [`m_DeferredUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUser)) and registers the weapon for the update ([`m_RequiresPreSimUpdates`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RequiresPreSimUpdates)). The requesters are few:

- [`EquipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-EquipDeferred): only the AI's equip action, so the game never defers an equip of the player's weapon;
- [`UnequipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UnequipDeferred): the AI's unequip and switch actions, the non-player branch of `NWeaponUnequipTask`, and the graph script's `SpawnWeapons` node, which is the only one to defer the player's unequip;
- [`StartUsingDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsingDeferred): only the mounted-weapon state task's entry (its stop-using counterpart is in the symbol dump only and unverified);
- [`DropDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DropDeferred): only the inventory's drop.

The weapon's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePreSim)) carries them out after its per-gun and component updates, the deferred recoil impulse, and the deferred weapon-fire event, in this order: the drop runs the drop virtual; the unequip runs the unequip virtual and skips the guns' render interpolation; the equip runs the equip virtual and ends the update; a start-using and a stop-using queued together cancel each other; a start-using alone, for a weapon without a user, unequips the deferred user's wielded weapon and then starts using; and a stop-using alone stops a weapon that has a user.

## Start using

[`WeaponBase::StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing) makes a character the weapon's user ([`m_User`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_User), with [`m_IsUserLocalPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserLocalPlayer) and [`m_IsUserPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserPlayer)), adds the weapon's camera hint for the locally controlled character, and hands the player's aim mode to the weapon. It does not change the weapon's state. The equip calls it, and the inventory's add calls it for the hand grenade, the planted explosive, and the beacon, which are in use while holstered. The weapon's per-tick update from its user ([`UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase)) checks no state either: it stores the aim point, updates the gun at the weapon's next gun with the inputs it is handed, and updates every gun's attached effects.

## Stop using

The stop-using virtual ([`WeaponBase::StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing), base [`StopUsingBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StopUsingBase)) ends the weapon's fire and effects, removes its camera hints for a local user, clears its user, and, when the weapon is wielding or dual-wielding and not in the heavy slot, holsters it. It writes the holstered state straight into the weapon's state rather than through the state setter, so the setter's physics-world and visibility changes do not run. A mounted gun stopped this way also leaves the inventory, which then selects another weapon.

## Dropping

The inventory's drop of a slot ([`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon)) clears the slot, stops using the weapon when it is wielding or dual-wielding, and defers the drop to the weapon's next pre-simulation update; with its second argument set it then selects another weapon when the dropped slot was selected. The weapon's drop virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop)), which the deferred drop runs, unequips a weapon still wielded by a user (leaving the player's precision aim), sets the dropped state, adds the bodies to the world and wakes them, gives them a velocity, clears the holder, enables the pickup interaction again, and hides every gun past the first. The velocity depends on the state the weapon was in once unequipped:

- holstered, with a character holder: the holder's velocity plus its forward direction scaled by the slot (forward for the two-handed slot, backward for the special slot, nothing for the others);
- still wielding or dual-wielding (a heavy weapon, which stopping does not holster): the first gun's measured velocity and angular velocity, set on its body ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)) ([`WeaponData::m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) and `m_MeasuredAngularVelocity`).

Since the inventory's drop stops using the weapon first, a held weapon other than a heavy one is dropped as if from its holster, with the holder's motion and not the hand's. A swimming character's pickup calls the drop virtual on the slot's occupant directly, which drops it at once with the slot still holding it. The deferral ([`WeaponBase::DropDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DropDeferred), [above](#deferred-operations)) does nothing to a weapon already in the dropped state, so a weapon dropped that way and then removed through the inventory's drop is dropped once.

The deferral reads the weapon's state when the inventory's drop runs, not when the drop is carried out: a weapon not yet dropped is marked for the drop and added to the pre-simulation updates, and a weapon already dropped is left unmarked. So a weapon dropped through its drop virtual and then let go of by the inventory is not dropped a second time later, even when something picks it up in between.

## Racks

A weapon on a rack is placed by a weapon placement rule ([`WeaponPlacementRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule)). Once the rule's weapon has spawned, the rule hands it the rule's infinite-pickup setting ([`WeaponBase::m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup)) and, for a rack, puts it in the rack state ([`E_WS_WEAPON_RACK`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WEAPON_RACK)), which keeps no body in the physics world and takes the gun's world matrix from the rule. A rack that does not use the ordinary pickup disables the weapon's own pickup interaction, and the weapon is offered through a node of an interaction graph that stands for the rule instead.

The player's interaction user proxy ([`Character::m_InteractionUserProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_InteractionUserProxy)) lists the entries of nearby interaction graphs the player could take ([`InteractionUserProxy::m_ValidEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##field-m_ValidEntries)). The weapon an entry offers is its graph's context's parent object when that is a weapon ([`InteractionContext::m_Parent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionContext##field-m_Parent)), and otherwise, when the entry's node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex)) stands for a weapon placement rule, the weapon the rule spawned ([`WeaponPlacementRule::GetSpawnedObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule##vfunc-GetSpawnedObject)). A swimming character's pickup walks the entries this way, and so does the player's weapon readout (unverified).

On land, the interaction's execution starts the pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart)) and the pickup animation's track message completes it, on the same weapon, through the character's track-message handling, which finds the weapon from the interaction in the same two ways (unverified). Starting the pickup of a rack weapon puts it in the picking-up state and sends the rule's picked-up event. A rack with unlimited stock hands out copies: its weapon has infinite pickup, and starting the pickup asks the character's inventory for a new weapon of the same spawn tag, with a magazine of the weapon's tuned size, to be equipped on arrival and to drop the weapon it replaces ([`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon), [weapon-gives-and-restores.md](weapon-gives-and-restores.md#the-give)); the copy spawns a few frames later. The rack's own weapon stays where it is.

A weapon is shown or hidden as a whole through its visibility virtual ([`WeaponBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetVisible)), which a placement rule uses for a weapon it places invisible.

## The drop's callers

[`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon) is called by the character's drop of every slot ([`Character::DropAllWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-DropAllWeapons), from the script's drop-all event and the player's reset to the default loadout), the character's destruction and death (its best weapon), the slot select, the neutral state, the `NDropWeaponStateTask` state task ([below](#vehicles-and-traversal)), the inventory's add when it replaces an occupant with the drop-old flag, the inventory's weapon handling (a heavy weapon on the holster or cancel input), the unequip of a heavy weapon and the sun gun's unequip, the single-use rocket launchers after their shot, and the network. The player's update also drops an empty Bavarium power-core weapon (unverified: read from the symbol dump).

## Vehicles and traversal

The inventory's weapon tick ([`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel) and its serial counterpart [`UpdateWeapons_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Serial)) picks which weapons are updated. In the mounted-gun state only the heavy slot's gun is. For the player, riding a motorcycle updates the wielded weapon but not the grenade, and any other riding state ([`Character::IsInRidingInVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInRidingInVehicleState), which covers the seats) updates neither. With a boost wingsuit in use (the character has one, [`Character::HasBoostWingsuit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasBoostWingsuit), and flies the wingsuit) the wingsuit's own guns are updated instead, each with its own fire input, the grenade input is zeroed, and the wielded weapon is not updated; otherwise the wielded weapon is updated and the wingsuit's guns are stopped.

The graph's base layer puts weapons away through state tasks (the states named are Rico's base graph's, from its decoded task lists):

- `NWeaponUnequipTask` ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter)) acts on the wielded weapon alone. Its instant variant unequips the player's weapon at once (another character's through the deferred unequip) on entry to every vehicle entry, seat, and seat change, the motorcycle, pogo stick, and cow rides, the mounted-gun pickups and seats, the wingsuit openings, boosts, rolls, evades, and flinches, free fall and fast fall, the underwater dives, the hit-reaction flights, the ragdoll, the get-up, dying, and the guitar-case exit. Its non-instant variant queues the holster act on the mounted-gun and cow-ride entries, the crawl, and the surface-swim starts. Either way the player returns to normal aim and the avatar's mental-state timer is reset.
- `NDropWeaponStateTask` ([`NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) is configured for the heavy slot and drops its weapon whatever its state, holstered included, on every grapple and tether fire, grenade throw, fall, free fall and fast fall, parachute and wingsuit opening, reel-in to a fall, vehicle entry, surface swim, crawl, and hit-reaction flight and stumble.
- `NStopUsingWeaponStateTask` ([`NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) stops using the selected slot's weapon in the ragdoll, crash, dying, drowning, and get-up states.
- `NWeaponHideInVehicleTask` ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter)), two per seat state, hides a configured slot's holstered weapon (holstered becomes holstered-invisible) when the vehicle sets [`Vehicle::m_HideCharacterWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_HideCharacterWeapons), and its exit ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit)) shows it again. It touches only holstered slot weapons.

The vehicle exit's upward teleport also forces the neutral state, which unequips. The parachute has no holster in code (only the auto-aim tuning and the statistics read it), and the grapple hang only affects the inventory permissions and the weapon's precision camera hint (unverified: read from the symbol dump); any holster there comes from the graph's tasks and acts. The wingsuit's guns are placed from the holder's bones and the flight direction ([`WeaponData::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)). Nothing in these paths moves a weapon held outside the slots.

## A character that loses its gun

The game takes a gun from a living character only by dropping it: the character's death drops its best weapon through the inventory, and an animation state task can drop a configured slot. There is no disarm as such. What a character does once a gun leaves its hands is the game's ordinary handling of a character with nothing wielded:

- the inventory's drop with its selection on selects another weapon the character carries when the dropped slot was the selected one;
- the weapon-equip state task, when its character wields nothing, switches to the first weapon that still has ammunition ([`Inventory::TryToSwitchToValidWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-TryToSwitchToValidWeapon));
- the AI's perception keeps a sense of weapons lying in the world whose pickup interaction is live, sorted by distance, and the behaviour trees can reserve one of them for a character to fetch (unverified: read from the symbol dump, unbound).

A hit reaction ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)) of an impulse between the character's stumble and knockdown thresholds staggers it without a ragdoll.

A character is held alive across frames through its weak reference to itself ([`Character::m_WeakThis`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeakThis)): a copy of it keeps only the control block, and locking it ([`WeakPtrGameObject::Lock`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/types::shared_ptr::WeakPtrGameObject##func-Lock)) yields a strong reference only while the character has not been destroyed, which holds it until released ([`ReleaseSharedPtrGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/types::shared_ptr##func-ReleaseSharedPtrGameObject)).
