# The pouches' models

The grenade, C4, and magazine pouches on Rico's body have no model of their own in the game, so the mod draws the game's own models on them: a grenade on the grenade pouch, the C4 charge as it shows in the hand on the C4 pouch (or the detonator once no charge is left to give), and the held gun's magazine on the magazine pouch. A round a hand takes from the magazine pouch is drawn in that hand the same way. What the player sees on the body is what a hand reaching there takes ([grip.md](grip.md); the pouches' placement is in [controllers.md](controllers.md#sockets-pouches-the-foregrip-and-the-action-runs-tier)). The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/pouch_models/`, and the debug UI's Weapons > Inventory and body slots page reads out which models are drawn, how many instances were made, and how many frames drew one.

## The models

Each model is an instance the mod owns, made from a model the game already holds for the item's lifetime, through [`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromModel) (`pouch_models/owned.rs`):

- the grenade: the hand grenade slot's weapon's projectile model ([`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::m_PhysicalProjectileModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PhysicalProjectileModel));
- the C4 charge: the planted explosive slot's weapon's in-hand model ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::m_InactiveModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_InactiveModelInstance), the model despite its name);
- the C4 detonator: the same weapon's trigger model, the base model of its first gun ([`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance), `c4::detonator::trigger_model`), which the held detonator is drawn with too ([grenades.md, "The detonator"](grenades.md#the-detonator));
- the magazine: the model of the reloaded gun's spare magazine prop ([`WeaponData::m_MagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineObject), [`RigidObject::m_Model`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##field-m_Model)).

Each instance holds its own reference to its model ([`ModelInstance::m_spModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##field-m_spModel)) and is added to the world, where the engine's dynamic cull draws it like any other model, casting a shadow, with no physics and no game object behind it. Its clip-on-distance flag is cleared, as the game does for the explosives a character holds.

The game drops a gun's spare magazine prop with each magazine it drops and respawns it a while later. Because the instance keeps its own reference, a belt or hand magazine already made for a weapon stays drawn through that gap, and the other one can be made from its reference (`pouch_models::owned::magazine_source`). Only before either has been made for the weapon is there no magazine to draw. Running out of stock hides the instance, and a different weapon releases it, so a missing prop never hides a stocked pouch and a stale model never stands in for a pouch with nothing to give.

## Where they are

A model is drawn while its pouch has something to give, wherever the pouch is placed and whichever hands are free (`begin::targets`, `shown_props`): the grenade while there is a grenade in stock; the C4 charge while another charge may be placed; the detonator once no charge may be placed and no hand holds it; and the magazine while the true reload acts on the held weapon and there is a round in reserve or unlimited ammunition ([reload.md, "Load"](reload.md#load)). The chest's explosives socket draws nothing, since the charge it selects is already drawn on the C4 pouch.

Each sits at its pouch's centre on the bone the pouch rides (the sternum for the grenade and the C4, the hips for the magazine), moved and turned by its prop's placement (`Prop::placement`: `offset_m` in the bone's axes and `rotation_deg` about X, then Y, then Z), given for the right side and mirrored across the body's midline for the left. The placements are the [hold tuning](../tuning.md)'s `pouch_models.model`, for the grenade, the magazine, and the detonator, which stands up the chest along its own length, and `pouch_models.charge`, which pitches the charge down a quarter turn so its face-up in-hand model lies flat on the chest, face forward. The magazine's placement offset also moves the pouch's grab and belt-push centre (`pouch_models::display_centre`), so the player reaches for the model they see.

## The magazine in the hand

While a hand holds a round taken from the magazine pouch, the reload asks for it to be drawn (`pouch_models::hand_tick`, from the reload's input tick), and a second instance of the gun's spare magazine model is shown, separate from the gun, so the old magazine stays drawn in the gun until it leaves ([reload.md, "What the hand shows"](reload.md#what-the-hand-shows)). Pistols and SMGs, whose magazine is part of the gun's body, get one this way too. Once per rendered frame it is placed where the reload holds the round, at the frame's interpolation fraction on the hand as it is drawn (`reload::held_round_matrix`). It is hidden once the hand lets go or the magazine seats, and on any tick the reload does not ask for it. A magazine picked up from the world needs none: it is its own object in the hand.

The model's own frame is the frame the game drops the spare from: the gun's magazine part's for a gun that has one (the spare is the same model as the part), and the gun's own for the pistols and SMGs. The wells table gives each spare model's top in that frame (`spare_top_m`) and leaves it out where the spare does not depict the magazine a hand loads (the UPU-210, whose spare is the empty cartridge) or the gun has none. Those guns are drawn by the reload with the gun's own round part instead. A held gun whose first gun has no magazine prop ([`WeaponData::NO_MAGAZINE_UNIQUE_TAG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-NO_MAGAZINE_UNIQUE_TAG)) logs `pouch models: the held gun has no spare magazine model to draw in the hand` once per weapon type.

The shotguns and the revolvers have no spare magazine prop and no round part, so their rounds are drawn with the casing their casing-ejection effect throws out: one shell for a shotgun, and six pistol casings in a ring for a revolver's cylinder, one instance per casing. The wells table names the model by its `.lod` path (`round_model`), and the instances come from the game's model cache under that path's hash ([`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromHash)), which loads nothing: the gun's entity carries the model for its effect, so it is cached while the gun is loaded. A model not in the cache draws nothing and logs one line per model. The magazine pouch draws no shell. A revolver's belt pouch draws a speedloader, its own group of casings standing mouths up in their ring with their mouths at the pouch's centre, one group on each revolver's pouch with a revolver in each hand (`pouch_models/speedloader.rs`, [reload.md, "Revolvers"](reload.md#revolvers)).

From the game's data, the guns without a spare magazine prop are the Balloon Head, the revolvers, the shotguns, and every launcher but the Bildt Negotiator, the UPU-210, and the Hydra (whose spare is its pod). The CS9 shares the Wraith 2000's magazine model, and the Capstone A-92 the Urga Vdova's. The Automat 12 borrows the UP-61's spare magazine prop, and once the game has spawned it, the belt and the hand draw it as any gun's ([reload.md, "What the hand shows"](reload.md#what-the-hand-shows)).

## Riding the body

On the input tick each model's placement is computed from the same body-relative pouch the grab uses and stored relative to its bone (the hips or the sternum), with the character's world transform. Once per rendered frame, before the engine's render update culls and draws (from `Game::UpdateRender`, after the late update's snapshot), each shown model's matrix is pushed ([`ModelInstance::UpdateMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-UpdateMatrix)) on that bone as the body is posed now, carried by the character's world transform interpolated between its last two ticks' ([`Character::m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) and [`m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)) at the frame's interpolation fraction, so it rides the body as the body is drawn ([render-time-anchoring.md](../rendering/render-time-anchoring.md)). The bone-local placement is not interpolated a second time. When the bone cannot be read, the model falls back to its placement in the character's model space interpolated between the last two ticks'. A model shown afresh starts at its new place rather than sweeping from its last. The copied placements are also published to the body mirror (`pouch_models/attachments/`), which compares the input observation with the render one.

## Lifetime

The instances are made, shown, hidden, and released in the input tick's game stage, on the game thread before the world update and outside the engine's input job, as the engine requires. A model whose pouch has nothing to give is hidden ([`ModelInstanceManager::HideModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-HideModelInstance)) and kept for when it comes back. An instance is released ([`ModelInstanceManager::AddToDeleteQueue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-AddToDeleteQueue)) when its source model or weapon changes, when the local player changes, when the weapon handling stands down, outside gameplay (where the world the instance lives in may be torn down), and when the payload unloads.

## Outstanding checks

None of this has been run in the headset. The open questions:

- whether the instances are safe across a save load beyond being released outside gameplay, and whether texture streaming covers them;
- whether the models' own origins put them on the pouches with a zero placement, or each needs an offset and turn;
- whether the casing models are in the model cache while their guns are held;
- whether pushing the matrix before the engine's render update is late enough in every frame for the cull to see it;
- whether the placements ride correctly on a moving platform and in a moving vehicle seat, where root sampling and the hands' late update are shared with the moving-platform correction.
