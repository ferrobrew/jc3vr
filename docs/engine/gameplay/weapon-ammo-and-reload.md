# Weapon ammunition and reload

The animation drives every reload: the reload act, the reload clip's track messages, and the reload state's state bits. The weapon's files and model parts are in [weapon-data.md](weapon-data.md), the automatic switch away from an empty weapon in [weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-switch-away-from-an-empty-weapon), and the rest of the fire path in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md).

## Where the counts live

A weapon holds only its magazine, [`WeaponBase::m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo), one per weapon object, so the dual-wield pistols share it. The capacity is the tuning's [`m_magazine_size`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_magazine_size); a negative size means no limit and no reload. There is no chambered round, and nothing tells a reload from empty from one with rounds left.

[`Inventory::m_Ammunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Ammunition) holds one count per ammunition kind, capped by [`m_MaxAmmunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_MaxAmmunition), which the ammunition upgrades raise. The weapon's kind is [`WeaponBase::m_AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AmmunitionType) (an [`AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::AmmunitionType) from the weapon's properties), so a kind's weapons share one reserve. The inventory is embedded in the character ([`Character::m_Inventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_Inventory)).

[`Inventory::GetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAmmoCount) reads a reserve, [`SetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-SetAmmoCount) writes one capped at the maximum, and [`GiveMaxAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GiveMaxAmmo) fills them all. A reserve refilled from empty through either reloads the wielded weapon of that kind if its magazine is empty (a hand-grenade refill reloads the hand-grenade weapon directly).

## Reload kinds

[`WeaponBase::m_ReloadType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReloadType) comes from the weapon's `reload_type` property, not its class:

- [`E_RELOAD_MAGAZINE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_MAGAZINE) refills the whole magazine at once: nearly every hand weapon, including the rocket launchers, the mortar, and the semi-automatic and sawn-off shotguns.
- [`E_RELOAD_BULLET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_BULLET) adds one round per refill and keeps the reload going while [`m_KeepReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_KeepReloading) is set. Among hand weapons only the U-Rys 96 pump shotgun uses it.
- [`E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION) is a timer with no act: the guitar-case launcher, the missile strike, and the EMP.

## Starting a reload

Every animated reload starts in [`Character::StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-StartReloadingWieldingWeapon), which queues `UB_ACT_RELOAD` only when [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsReloading) holds (the reload permission and a clear `SB_DISABLE_RELOAD`), the detonate input is zero, and the wielded weapon's [`CanReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-CanReload) holds. `CanReload` wants a limited kind, a magazine below its limit, and unlimited ammunition or a reserve (god mode always has one). The accepted act's callback runs [`StartReloadSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StartReloadSequence), which plays the reload effect and, for a bullet reload, sets `m_KeepReloading`; no rounds move yet. The reload state raises `SB_IS_RELOADING`, read by [`Character::IsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsReloading).

Its callers, every way the game reloads:

- the reload input, in [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling);
- the ammunition check (below), for an empty magazine, a magazine that is out, and the predicted reload;
- a reserve refilled from empty, through `SetAmmoCount`, `GiveMaxAmmo`, `CInventory::GiveMaxAmmoToAllWeaponInInventory`, `CInventory::SetMaxBeaconsByLevel`, and `CInventory::SetMaxFlaresByLevel`;
- the two reload-request track messages (below);
- the rocket launcher, when firing an unloaded launcher ([`RPGWeapon::Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-Fire_Start)) and for a holder that is not a player ([`RPGWeapon::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered));
- the sniper rifle's per-tick update, after a shot that emptied the magazine.

[`Character::CancelReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CancelReload) queues `UB_ACT_CANCEL_RELOAD` while `SB_IS_RELOADING` is set and clears the reload permission. [`WeaponBase::ReloadAnimationFinished`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ReloadAnimationFinished), called by the reload state's `NWeaponReloadFinishedTask` on exit, clears `m_KeepReloading` and [`m_IsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsReloading).

## Refilling the magazine

The rounds move in the [`Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Reload) virtual, base [`WeaponBase::ReloadBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ReloadBase). It refuses a weapon without a holder, of the unlimited kind, or without a magazine limit. Otherwise it clears [`m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine); a full magazine, or an empty reserve without unlimited ammunition, then ends it, clearing `m_KeepReloading` and returning false.

Otherwise the new count is the magazine size, or the old count plus the reserve if less (unlimited ammunition counting as a full reserve); a bullet reload adds one round and sets `m_KeepReloading` while still short. The count goes through [`SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo), and the reserve becomes the old count plus the reserve minus the new count, so a reload with rounds left loses nothing; unlimited ammunition leaves the reserve untouched. It clears `m_KeepReloading` on a full magazine and `m_IsReloading` always, and returns true. It never touches the model parts: a dropped magazine's part stays hidden until the clip's enable-model message.

The rocket launcher's override, [`RPGWeapon::Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-Reload), loads the rocket ([below](#launchers)); the mortar's override also wraps the base.

An `E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION` weapon needs no act: where the ammunition check would start a reload it loads [`m_ReloadTimeLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReloadTimeLeft) from the tuning's [`m_reload_time_scale`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_reload_time_scale) and sets `m_IsReloading`. [`UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel) counts it down, and the check blocks the shot meanwhile and calls `Reload` at zero. It is the only kind that times its own reload.

## The ammunition check

[`WeaponBase::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase) runs every tick for the gun being updated, whatever the trigger, from `UpdateWeapon_Parallel` with the tick's fire decision, which it may clear. What it reads and requests is the user's, not the weapon's. In order:

1. A finished no-animation reload timer calls `Reload`; a running one clears the shot.
2. A requested shot is cleared while the user reloads ([`Character::IsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsReloading)), draws, or holsters ([`Character::IsDrawingOrHolsteringWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsDrawingOrHolsteringWeapon)). These are character state bits, so they block every weapon the character uses.
3. For a player, with a limited kind and a magazine limit, an empty magazine with no reload running either starts a reload (when `CanReload` and the reload permission hold), or, when the weapon cannot reload and a shot was requested, tries the automatic switch and plays the out-of-ammunition feedback ([weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-switch-away-from-an-empty-weapon)). Either way the shot is cleared.
4. While `m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine` is set, it clears the shot, calls `StartReloadingWieldingWeapon` on every tick, and does nothing else.
5. Otherwise, a weapon that does not reload by magazine (the pump shotgun and the timed weapons), outside the hand-grenade, planted-explosive, and beacon slots, calls [`Character::CancelReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CancelReload) on its user on every tick its `m_KeepReloading` is clear, so updated beside the wielded weapon it cancels that weapon's reload every tick. The shot is cleared while the user has firing blocked ([`Character::IsNoFiringSet`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsNoFiringSet)). The predicted reload starts for a local user's idle weapon below [`m_PredictedReloadAmmunitionLimit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PredictedReloadAmmunitionLimit) once [`m_PredictedReloadTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PredictedReloadTime) has passed with no fire or input, when `CanReload` and [`Character::IsPredictedReloadingAllowed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsPredictedReloadingAllowed) hold (a player not in precision aim, plus a condition on the player's aim control whose meaning is unverified).

Every reload the check starts goes through `StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`, so a weapon used without being wielded requests the wielded weapon's reload, not its own, even with its trigger released.

## Track messages

The character's message poll, in its serial post-physics pass, hands each [`AnimationMessageData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::message::AnimationMessageData) to the character, which forwards the weapon messages to [`WeaponBase::HandleTrackMessage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HandleTrackMessage). The reload messages are constants on [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase), named for what the handler does (their names are not known):

- `TRACK_MESSAGE_THROW_AWAY_MAGAZINE` drops the magazine (below). Its arguments are a part name hash, a gun index, and a flag that keeps the magazine-out state clear; it does nothing while the magazine is out.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_RELOAD` calls `Reload`.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_ENABLE_MODEL_ATTACHEMENT` shows or hides every model part whose name hash is its first argument, by its second; reload clips use it to show the magazine part again.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_START_RELOAD` and `TRACK_MESSAGE_START_RELOAD_IF_EMPTY` call `StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`, the second only with an empty magazine.

## The guns' sounds

A gun's one-off sounds are attached-effect usages on its own container ([`WeaponData::m_AttachedEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_AttachedEffects)), from the effects block of the weapon's `.epe`. Each entry has a name, a usage hash (what code and track messages look up), an optional FMOD event GUID, and an optional particle effect, and the name can differ from the usage (the U-Rys 96's `PumpBackward` and the rifles' `CockBackward` are both the usage `CockBack`). Code plays only a few usages by a fixed hash: [`StartReloadSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StartReloadSequence) plays [`WeaponData::RELOAD_START_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) on every gun, the sniper's per-tick update plays [`WeaponData::BOLT_ACTION_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) as it starts the bolt act, the reload check plays the reload's end and the dry-fire click, the pickup's start plays `Pickup`, and the equip and the drop play usages no hand-held gun's effects carry. Every other usage is played from a clip, by a `HandleTrackMessage` message naming the usage and the gun.

What the hand-held guns' effect files carry, matched to event names through the FMOD strings bank:

- Every gun carries the draw and holster sounds ([`WeaponData::DRAW_WEAPON_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData), [`WeaponData::HOLSTER_WEAPON_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)), named `Draw` and `Holster` or `DrawWeapon` and `HolsterWeapon`, played by the clips.
- The reload's sound is one event per gun, `<gun>_reload`, covering magazine out, magazine in, and the action. Most guns carry it as `ReloadStart`; the four assault rifles carry it as [`WeaponData::RELOAD_REMOVE_MAGAZINE_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) instead, played where their clips take the magazine out. Only the shotguns that load shell by shell, the Bildt Negotiator, and the launchers loaded a round at a time carry a separate insert sound ([`WeaponData::RELOAD_INSERT_AMMO_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) and, for the first two, a reload end.
- Only the U-Rys 96 carries both halves of its action, as [`WeaponData::COCK_BACK_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) and [`WeaponData::COCK_FORWARD_USAGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) (`pump_back` and `pump_forward`). The CS111 Archangel and the Final Argument carry only the first, their bolt's `cock`. No pistol, SMG, rifle, or the U-SV 45 carries a sound of its slide, charging handle, or bolt; the bank's `slide_action` event for the U-Pozhar 98 is named by no effect entry.
- The Archangel's and the Final Argument's casing ejection is the bolt-action usage, a soundless particle effect, so their casings fly with the bolt act, not the shot. The revolvers carry `RevShellsBreach`, a soundless particle effect for the shells leaving the cylinder, and no sound of the cylinder apart from the whole reload.
- The magazine's landing is not the gun's: the bank's `magazine_drops` events are most likely the dropped magazine prop's impact sounds, through the rigid-object effect settings its entity names (unverified).

## The magazine drop and the spare prop

Each gun ([`WeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) keeps a spare magazine prop, [`m_MagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineObject), a disabled rigid object. [`WeaponData::SpawnMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-SpawnMagazineObject) requests it from the spawn system by the gun's [`m_MagazineUniqueTag`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineUniqueTag), a spawn tag from the weapon's properties; the spawn completes later and leaves the prop disabled. A gun with no tag never gets a spare. The weapon's post-initialization requests the first.

[`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) drops it, taking the gun as `this`, the holder, and a part name hash:

- With a part hash, it looks through the weapon's [`m_WeaponComponents`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponComponents) for a model part ([`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent)) whose [`m_NameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_NameHash) matches, takes the part's world matrix as the drop transform, and hides the part with its `Enable` virtual. The magazine part is `model.magazine`. With no matching part it drops nothing and fails.
- With no hash, or that of `no_magazine_model`, it drops from the gun's world matrix and hides nothing, as for the pistols and SMGs, whose magazines are in the body mesh.

It then makes the spare dynamic at the drop transform, puts it in the holder's collision system group, gives it the gun's [`m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) with any upward part removed and the gun's angular velocity, sets its body material, enables it, adds it to the update, and lets go, so a flicked gun throws its magazine. The spare's body is reached through [`PhysicsGameObject::GetIPfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsGameObject##vfunc-GetIPfxInstance), and the enabling is [`GameObject::Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-Enable), whose rigid-object override adds the body to the physics world on enabling and removes it on disabling. The dropped prop is an ordinary rigid object; the gun keeps counting its rounds until the next reload returns them to the reserve.

The track message wraps the throw: when it succeeds it calls `SpawnMagazineObject` for the next spare and sets `m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`, the magazine-out state: the ammunition check holds fire and asks for a reload while it is set, `CWeaponHasMagazineInsertedCondition` reads it, and only `Reload` clears it.

A gun's parts are in `m_WeaponComponents` (shared pointers to [`WeaponComponentBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase)); an entry is a model part when its [`IsType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-IsType) accepts [`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-TYPE_ID), which also accepts the barrel recoil and barrel spin parts derived from it; the magazine drop additionally requires [`m_Type`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##field-m_Type) to be the plain model-attachment kind. A part's [`m_Enabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##field-m_Enabled) is its shown state, set by [`Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-Enable).

## Unlimited ammunition

[`WeaponBase::HasUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HasUnlimitedAmmo) asks the user ([`m_User`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_User)) first: true when [`Character::HasUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasUnlimitedAmmo) holds, that is when the user has [`Character::m_UnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_UnlimitedAmmo) (the unlimited-ammunition option, set through [`Character::SetUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetUnlimitedAmmo)), is not the local player, or is in the challenge-island game mode. Otherwise, or with no user, it is the tuning's [`m_unlimited_ammo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_unlimited_ammo).

Unlimited ammunition makes only the reload free. Each shot still lowers the magazine through [`ConsumeAmmoBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ConsumeAmmoBase), which does not consult it: a weapon with a magazine size loses rounds from it, never below zero, and only one without draws from the reserve. It still runs dry and reloads, but the reload leaves the reserve alone. A weapon of the [`AMMO_UNLIMITED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::AmmunitionType##variant-AMMO_UNLIMITED) kind, or with no magazine limit, never reloads at all.

## The HUD

The HUD's readout is pushed, not polled: `SetMagazineAmmo` pushes the magazine count (`CHUDUI::OnSetSelectedMagazineAmmo`) for the local player's selected weapon, `SetAmmoCount` pushes the reserve (`CHUDUI::OnSetSelectedInventoryAmmo`) when it is the selected weapon's kind, and `Reload` reports the reload (`CHUDUI::OnReloadSelectedWeapon`). A write that bypasses these leaves the readout stale.

## Threads and phases

The track messages, and with them the animated `Reload` and the magazine drop, run in the holder's serial post-physics pass (`CCharacter::UpdatePassPostPhysics_Serial`), on the game update thread after the physics step; the no-animation `Reload` runs from the ammunition check in the parallel update. The magazine drop writes rigid-body state, allowed only on the thread holding the physics world's write mark.

## Launchers

No launcher has a cocking or cycling step: with rounds and the magazine-out flag clear, it fires through the ordinary ammunition check.

A weapon whose tuning has a burst count fires the burst from one pull: [`WeaponBase::Fire_StartBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_StartBase) loads [`WeaponData::m_BurstShotsLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BurstShotsLeft), and the parallel update fires while it is non-zero, whatever the trigger. `ConsumeAmmoBase` counts each shot against the burst first and, unless the weapon's burst consumes ammunition per shot, lowers the magazine only at the burst's last shot. A burst that stops early (the ammunition check refusing a shot, or the reload sequence clearing the bursts) leaves its round counted in the magazine; the rocket launcher's unequip finishes the count for a burst left running (unverified). The Capstone Hydra (`w144_rpg_capstone_hydra`) has a magazine of one, its pod, and a tuning burst count of five (from its `.wtunec`, an ADF file whose `WeaponTuning` instance holds `magazine_size`, `burst_count`, and `unlimited_ammo`); whether its burst consumes per shot is not checked.

- The rocket launchers are `CRPGWeapon`s ([`RPGWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon)), which fire only with the rocket loaded ([`m_AttachedState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##field-m_AttachedState)). `RPGWeapon::Reload` loads it when the magazine is not empty, and the launcher's per-tick update loads and shows it whenever the magazine holds rounds, so any refill brings it back. The rocket model is the `model.rpg` part ([`RPG_NAME_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent)), which that update shows on loading and, with [`m_RPGHideModelWhenOutOfAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##field-m_RPGHideModelWhenOutOfAmmo), hides once the launcher is empty; it leaves an empty launcher's unloaded rocket part alone.
- The UPU-210 (`w123_grenade_launcher_upu_210`) is a `CMissileWeaponBase` in its `.epe`, not a rocket launcher. Its fire start has no loaded state, refusing only below a minimum height for a vehicle holder (unverified). Its entity has two model parts, `model.cartridge_empty` and `model.cartridge_full` ([`CARTRIDGE_FULL_NAME_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent) and `CARTRIDGE_EMPTY_NAME_HASH`), both at the gun's origin with no rotation. No code names either; only the part-hash track messages can toggle them.

## Sniper bolt and shotgun pump

Neither cycle moves ammunition.

A bolt-action rifle ([`SniperWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon) with [`m_BoltAction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltAction), from the weapon's properties) locks after each shot: its [`Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile) sets [`m_HasFiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_HasFiredThisInput) for a player user ([`WeaponBase::m_IsUserPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserPlayer)), which makes the rifle's fire start and ammunition check do nothing. Its per-tick update ([`SniperWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel)) waits for trigger release and, in precision aim, for [`m_BoltActionDelayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltActionDelayer). Then, with rounds left, it plays the bolt-action effect and queues `UB_ACT_SNIPER_BOLT_ACTION` every tick until the bolt state raises `SB_IS_RELOADING`; empty, it starts a reload. The update clears the lock only while the holder reloads or has no ammunition, so the bolt state's `SB_IS_RELOADING` releases it. With [`m_BoltActionAutoZoomOut`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltActionAutoZoomOut) the cycle takes the local player out of precision aim and back afterwards ([`m_ShouldgoBackToPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_ShouldgoBackToPrecisionAim)), as the rifle's [`StartReloadSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-StartReloadSequence) does for a reload. Which rifles carry a clip of their own for the bolt act is unverified.

The pump shotgun's pump is a [`DoActWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent). Its [`Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-Fire_Start), run for each shot by `Fire_StartBase`, plays [`m_FireStartAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##field-m_FireStartAct) when [`ShouldActActivate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-ShouldActActivate) allows it: on the component's skeletal object if it has one, otherwise on every gun's own animated model ([`WeaponData::DoAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-DoAct)) with [`m_SendActToWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##field-m_SendActToWeapon), otherwise on the holder. The U-Rys 96's property file has two, one aimed at the holder and one at the gun. The acts gate nothing: no pump state blocks the next shot.

A pistol slide is a [`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent). Its placement ([`UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)) moves the part back along its own forward axis by up to [`m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength) after each shot. With [`m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo), a player's wielded weapon holds the slide fully back while the magazine is empty: the slide lock-back. A barrel recoil part is told from a plain model part by its vtable pointer ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::VFTABLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent)), by `m_Type`, or by its own type id.

### How each action is modelled

Read from the entity files and models with `tools/sights/`, the hand weapons' actions are modelled three ways:

- A separate part: the pistols' slides are barrel recoil parts, one per gun of a dual-wielded pair, and the U-SV 45 sniper rifle's moving piece is a barrel recoil part named `ejection_port`, a cover on the right of the receiver beside the bore rather than a bolt on it.
- A bone of the gun's own skeleton: the U-Rys 96, the CS111 Archangel, and the Final Argument have animated models whose skeletons hold a `Reference` root and one other bone, the pump ([`WeaponData::PUMP_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) on the shotgun and the bolt ([`WeaponData::SLIDER_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) on the rifles, moved by their pump and bolt-action clips. A gun's animated model ([`WeaponData::m_AnimatedModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_AnimatedModel)) is posed in the gun's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)), which advances its clips and computes the model-space pose that the controller's joint accessors read and write and the render phase skins. The Urga Vdova 89 and the U-Rys 12 have skeletons too, with a `Chamber` and a `Reload` bone.
- Nothing: the SMGs' and rifles' charging handles are part of the body mesh and do not move.

Which way a bone moves, and where a bolt bone's origin lies relative to the bolt's axis, have not been read.
