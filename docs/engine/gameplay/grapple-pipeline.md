# The grapple pipeline

The player fires the grapple's hook at a surface or object to zip toward it or to tether two things together, and can reel in. This page covers targeting, the hook's flight and attach, the modes, the wire constraints, the sounds, the arm IK and reticle, the reel, and the tether squeeze. The weapon side of the same aim state is in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md). The chief defs are [`PlayerAimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl), [`GrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook), [`WireEnd`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd), and [`HookTargetInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo).

## Targeting

The grapple casts no ray of its own. [`PlayerAimControl::UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) runs one physics ray per sim tick for every aim consumer of the local player: it builds the ray from [`GameCameraManager::GetCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetCameraMatrix) ([aim-pipeline.md, "The raycast"](aim-pipeline.md#the-raycast-updatedirectaim)), calls [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) once, and hands the hit to [`PlayerAimControl::CheckDirectTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-CheckDirectTargets). That loops over the target types ([`TargetType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType), listed in "The aim target" in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md); melee is skipped here) and fills, per type, the parallel arrays [`m_DirectTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectTargets), [`m_DirectHits`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectHits), [`m_DirectPositions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectPositions), [`m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos), [`m_IsInRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_IsInRange), and [`m_MaxAimDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_MaxAimDistance). Weapons use the [`TARGET_WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_WEAPONS) slot and the grapple the [`TARGET_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_GRAPPLE) slot, separated by index rather than by caller, so a change to the camera matrix moves both aims together.

For the grapple, `CheckDirectTargets` also keeps a last-valid cache ([`m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO), [`m_HasLastValidGrapple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_HasLastValidGrapple), and [`m_DirectGrapplePosIsGrapplable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectGrapplePosIsGrapplable)), so a momentarily lost target persists. For a surface hit the slot holds the ray hit; for entity attach and dual-tether targets, `m_Target[TARGET_GRAPPLE]` and `m_LastHitGrappleTargetGO` come from the grapple fitness and target scoring. The reads in `UpdateGrappleReticle` and `NReelInAimIK::Update` confirm the layout of `m_AimPos`, [`m_LastAimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_LastAimPos), and [`m_IsGrappleUsingDirectPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_IsGrappleUsingDirectPos).

The live readers of the slot:

- [`GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition) returns a pointer to `m_AimPos[TARGET_GRAPPLE]`. The aim blend states and the fire-input predicate (`CGrapplingHook::GetFireInputEnabled`, which the disassembler mislabels `CInventory::UpdateWeapons_Serial`) call it.
- [`GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO) and [`GetCurrentGrappleTargetWeakGO`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetCurrentGrappleTargetWeakGO) return the cached game object, gated on `m_HasLastValidGrapple && m_IsInRange[TARGET_GRAPPLE]`.
- [`IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid) re-validates the cached aim by calling `GetCameraMatrix` itself.
- [`NStateTask_InAirGrappleFireMotionTask::OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_InAirGrappleFireMotionTask##func-OnEnter) also calls `GetCameraMatrix`, for the camera's position only, and only for a target nearly straight above or below the character: it then pushes the character horizontally toward the camera for a moment, and otherwise damps its horizontal speed. Its direction comes from the latched target.
- The grapple reticle ([below](#the-grapple-reticle)).

### Latching the target

When the hook fires, [`GrapplingHook::SetUpTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-SetUpTarget) (static, `(CPlayer* player, CWireEnd::SHookTargetInfo& out, CTarget::ETargetType type)`) reads the player's aim control ([`Player::m_AimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimControl)), then `m_AimPos[type]` and `m_Target[type]`, transforms the aim position into the target object's local space, and writes a `HookTargetInfo`. With no direct `CTarget` it falls back to `GetCurrentGrappleTargetGO`, for `TARGET_GRAPPLE` only. The target info has two setup overloads, [`SetupTargetFromTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo##func-SetupTargetFromTarget) (`CTarget*`, `CVector3f local`) and [`SetupTargetFromGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::HookTargetInfo##func-SetupTargetFromGameObject) (`CGameObject*`, `CVector3f local`); the second writes only the object, the local position, and for a character the bone, and its caller sets the world position, the in-range flag, and a non-character's bone.

[`GrapplingHook::GetLastGrappleTargetPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetLastGrappleTargetPos) returns the latched target's current world position, and the fire and the fire arm IK read it rather than the live slot, so a change to the slot after `SetUpTarget` does not move the hook's target, while the reticle shows the live slot.

A fire during a reel, allowed once the re-reel upgrade is bought, latches the slot like any other ([`GrapplingHook::FireHookFromReelingIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-FireHookFromReelingIn): `SetUpTarget` on the grapple slot, then the fire act toward it). [`GrapplingHook::OnPreSimUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-OnPreSimUpdate) reads the fire input's press edge for it, or a press still queued within its window, not a release.

## Fire and attach

The hook makes a short, animation-gated flight from the hand to the latched target:

1. [`GrapplingHook::TryFireHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-TryFireHook) computes the fire direction from the `HookTargetInfo` and calls the animation fire.
2. [`GrapplingHook::DoActFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-DoActFire) (static, `(const CVector3f& direction, boost::shared_ptr<CCharacter>&, bool dual_tether, bool reel)`) takes a direction, picks a directional fire act (`ACT_GRAPPLE_FIRE_*`, or `ACT_GRAPPLE_TETHER_FIRE_*` for a dual tether), and `QueueAct`s it, re-issuing it each tick until `CCharacter::IsGrappleFiring` goes true. It creates no wire, and its callbacks (`FireHookQueueActCallback`, `FireDualTetherQueueActCallback`, `FireHookGrappleReelQueueActCallback`) only notify.
3. On the fire animation's release track message, [`GrapplingHook::OnPostCamUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-OnPostCamUpdate) reads the hand and shoulder positions, creates the wire ([`GrapplingHook::CreateNewWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CreateNewWire)), pins the near end to the device, and starts the far end extending at a tuning speed toward the target with [`WireEnd::SetExtend`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-SetExtend) (`(const CVector3f& start, float speed, SHookTargetInfo&, bool ghost)`). The flight starts at the left shoulder, not at the device.
4. On reaching or hitting, [`WireEnd::Attach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-Attach) builds a `CGrapplePointProxy` and moves the end to attached. An end attaches to a proxy, never a bare coordinate ([`WireEnd::AttachToProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-AttachToProxy), [`WireEnd::AttachToCharacterBone`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-AttachToCharacterBone) `(shared_ptr<CCharacter>&, int bone, const CMatrix4f& localOffset)`), so a tether is an entity, bone, or world proxy per end.

A [`GrapplingHookWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire) holds two ends in [`m_WireEnds`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_WireEnds): the first is the hook end, which every fire starts extending, and the second the device end, which [`GrapplingHook::AttachLocalWireEndToDevice`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-AttachLocalWireEndToDevice) attaches through `CGrapplingHookWire::AttachEndToCharacterBone`. An end's state is [`WireEnd::m_WireEndState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_WireEndState), the wire's [`m_WireState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_WireState).

### The wire's near end

The device end attaches through a `CCharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy`, at a small fixed local offset, to the bone [`SafeBoneIndex::GRAPPLE_DEVICE_ATTACH_BONE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-GRAPPLE_DEVICE_ATTACH_BONE) names: `LeftForeArmRoll`, the left forearm twist bone (a child of `LeftForeArm`), not the hand. The proxy reads the live bone matrix when evaluated, so the end follows whatever pose the forearm has, IK included, when the wire updates. The wire updates in the sim from `OnPostCamUpdate`, after pose finalization, and stores each end as a previous and current pair; its render update interpolates the pairs by the frame's interpolation factor and rebuilds the spline, so the rendered near end moves at the sim rate.

### The fire gate

The hook fires only while [`GrapplingHook::GetFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetFireInputEnabled) holds (the readiness to fire and `OnPreSimUpdate` test it). It starts from [`m_GrappleFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrappleFireInputEnabled), which the graph drives through `NStateTask_DisableGrappleUseTask`: a state carrying the task clears the flag on entry, optionally retracting the active wire and detaching a reeled-in attachment first, sets it while the animation is within the task's named segment, and sets it on leaving. The base graph carries the task in every vehicle seat (driver, rider, and passenger), the grapple fire states, landings and stumbles, and many traversal transitions; the seats' copies name no segment and do not retract, so a wire already out survives boarding. The fire is also refused when the holder is not the player, while the player aims down a sniper scope, without the grappling hook ability, under the parachute without the slingshot ability, in cinematic mode, in the wingsuit with the aim point too far behind the facing, when the inventory's animation forbids a grapple fire, or under a blocking state bit.

### The grapple in a vehicle seat

A seat takes no grapple fire (above), and the grapple slot is not refreshed there: in a recorded session it kept its boarding target for the whole ride. Which test skips the targeting is unverified. A vehicle grapple exists but is unreachable: [`VehicleGrappleHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::VehicleGrappleHook), embedded in the hook, whose update ([`VehicleGrappleHook::PreSimUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::VehicleGrappleHook##func-PreSimUpdate)) the hook's pre-simulation update calls only under a static switch this build compiles out. While the holder rides a vehicle, it fires on a press of `FIRE_GRAPPLE` through `FireHookCustom` at the aim's target position, ignoring the vehicle, with no game object and no bone; once the press is released and the far end holds a proxy, it calls [`GrapplingHook::TetherActiveWireToVehicleCOM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-TetherActiveWireToVehicleCOM). That intact function re-attaches the near end from the holder's bone to a vehicle point proxy at the vehicle's centre of mass, moves the wire into the tether list, and returns the hook to inactive, leaving an ordinary tether between what the hook hit and the vehicle, which the tether release and squeeze act on like any other.

## Traversal, tether, and retract

The hook's state is a [`GrapplingHookState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState) ([`GHS_INACTIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_INACTIVE), `GHS_REELING_IN`, `GHS_REELED_ATTACHED`, `GHS_REELED_HANG`, `GHS_REELED_UPSIDEDOWN`, `GHS_REELED_STUNT`, and `GHS_CUSTOM_ACTIVE_WIRE`) in [`GrapplingHook::m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_State), set by `SetState`. The character's hook is [`Inventory::m_GrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_GrapplingHook) in its embedded [`Character::m_Inventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_Inventory), returned by [`Character::GetGrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetGrapplingHook). `OnPreSimUpdate` and `OnPostCamUpdate` dispatch the input from a `CActionMap` ([input.md](input.md)):

- [`FIRE_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-FIRE_GRAPPLE) picks the mode. A press with no wire latches the aim and queues the fire at once, before a tap and a hold are told apart. Held past [`GrappleTuning::m_HoldTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_HoldTime), it sets [`m_CachedDualTetherFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_CachedDualTetherFire), whose release fires the tether's second end through `SetExtend`: a dual tether, whose far end dual-attaches (`OnDualTetherAttach`), up to the upgrade-gated `GetMaxDualTethers`, the oldest broken when over budget (`BreakOldestDualTetherIfNeeded`). Any other release, a tap let go before the hook arrived included, reels in: `SetState(GHS_REELING_IN)` and `QueueAct(ACT_PRE_REEL)`, and the reel tasks (`NStateTask_ReelIn`, `StartReelAttach`, `StartReelHangAtPosition`, and `StartReelUpsideDown`) finish into a `GHS_REELED_*` state.
- [`RETRACT_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-RETRACT_GRAPPLE) retracts: [`GrapplingHook::RetractActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-RetractActiveWire) destroys the constraint and detaches the far end, which a reel answers by cancelling itself ([the reel's endings](#the-reels-endings)). There is no pull of a light object toward the player: the function the disassembler names `CGrapplingHook::HandleGrapplePull` calls `RetractActiveWire`, then sets a state and queues an act.
- [`RELEASE_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-RELEASE_GRAPPLE) releases the tethers (`ReleaseAllDualTethers`, `DetachAllDualTetheredWiresAttachedToGameObject`).
- [`PUSH_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-PUSH_GRAPPLE), [`REELED_IN_JUMP_ACTION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-REELED_IN_JUMP_ACTION), and [`REELED_IN_RELEASE_ACTION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-REELED_IN_RELEASE_ACTION) drive the reeled states.

[`GrapplingHook::FireHookCustom`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-FireHookCustom) fires a wire at a given target info, as a ghost wire when asked, with no fire act, only with no active wire and the hook inactive. It does not test a pending normal fire, and finished tethers are not the active wire, so it works with tethers attached. It enters `GHS_CUSTOM_ACTIVE_WIRE`, where the fire, reel, and tether input does nothing (the tether release still releases). [`GrapplingHook::CancelActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CancelActiveWire) ends the active wire and leaves the state, which the next `OnPreSimUpdate` returns to inactive. The vehicle grapple (above) is built on this path.

## Wire constraints

[`GrapplingHookWire::ShouldUseConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-ShouldUseConstraint) (mislabelled `CWireEnd::GetAttachmentObjectInfo`) rejects an invalid wire, an invalid end, or a missing end proxy, and reads both proxies' constraint hints: both must permit a constraint, and at least one must request a physical one. [`GenericObjectPointProxy::GetConstraintHint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GenericObjectPointProxy##func-GetConstraintHint) returns none for a weapon in a reel-without-attachment mode, and a virtual or physical hint for a rigid object by its motion type, so an attachment does not imply a native rope.

[`GrapplingHookWire::CreateConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-CreateConstraint) (mislabelled `NPfxVehicle::SStaticWheelFrictionData::addFrictionConstraint`, while the function labelled as wire creation does the tether squeeze) takes both proxy bodies and local pivots, requires distinct valid bodies, builds the spring and linear-motor data, and calls `hknpWorld::createConstraint`. A new attachment snapshots the current length into [`m_MaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##field-m_MaxLength); a wire that is neither active nor an attachment wire gets a safety buffer on top, and the spring length subtracts the requested squeeze.

[`GrapplingHookWire::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-Update) advances the ends, evaluates the gate, creates or updates the constraint, and updates tension and rendering. [`GrapplingHookWire::UpdateWireTension`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookWire##func-UpdateWireTension) can break the line and notifies both proxies of the tension. On the character-bone proxy, [`GetVisualPositionAndNormalWS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy##func-GetVisualPositionAndNormalWS) and [`GetVisualTransformWS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy##func-GetVisualTransformWS) compose the attachment's local transform with the bone and world transforms.

## Weapons and the grapple

The grapple never hooks a weapon, and only its rays keep it from finding one.

- The aim ray (one `CastRay` on [`RAYCAST_EVERYTHING_PHYSICAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-RAYCAST_EVERYTHING_PHYSICAL)) and a non-ghost end's casts in flight ([`WireEnd::TryCollide`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-TryCollide), on the same layer) never hit a weapon's body: [`WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-WEAPONS) does not collide with that layer ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md)). No query layer meets that layer's set plus `WEAPONS`: [`VEHICLE_RAYCAST`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxLayer##variant-VEHICLE_RAYCAST) meets `WEAPONS`, the world, the character proxies, the projectiles, and the vehicle layers, but not the ragdoll proxies.
- Everything after the ray supports a weapon. `CheckDirectTargets` puts a held weapon's holder in its place and keeps a dropped one as the slot's game object with no target. The weapon's [`GetGrapplingMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGrapplingModeWeapon) is [`E_GM_ReelInNoAttach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::GrapplingMode##variant-E_GM_ReelInNoAttach), which counts as grapplable, so [`PlayerAimControl::UpdateLastValidGrappleTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateLastValidGrappleTarget) caches it and `SetUpTarget` latches it. [`WireEnd::SetupProxyForAttachment`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-SetupProxyForAttachment) has a weapon branch that builds the same object point proxy as for any loose object, and nothing in the attach reads the no-attach mode. With a weapon latched and the aim seeing it, a non-ghost hook would attach to whatever lies under or behind the gun and reel there (inferred from the code path).
- `CheckDirectTargets` chooses per slot, by [`m_RayTypeSetting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_RayTypeSetting), among the collector's first holey hit ([`PlayerAimRaycastCollector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastCollector)), the query's closest hit in its [`CastRayInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfo), and the collector's minimum-distance hit. - A ghost wire ([`WireEnd::m_GhostWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_GhostWire)) makes no cast in flight and passes through walls. The goal follows the object, and past the wire's `m_MaxLength` the end retracts.

Several functions here carry wrong names in the disassembly, each named in its def: [`GrapplingHook::GetTuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetTuning) and [`GetActiveWireAttachmentObjectInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetActiveWireAttachmentObjectInfo) are swapped, [`GetFireDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetFireDirection) and [`GetDualTetherFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetDualTetherFireInputEnabled) bear unrelated names, and the sphere cast inside [`WireEnd::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-Update) is named as a debug line.

## The hook's sounds and effects

The hook is an attached effect container ([`GrapplingHook::m_AttachedEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_AttachedEffects)), loaded from the grapple device's container (`editor/entities/jc_props/grappling_device/grappling_device_jc3_default.epe`) when its properties are set. No code names `GrappleFire` or `GrapplePreFire`, so they play only from track messages, and `FireHookCustom` queues no fire act, so a custom fire plays neither. Which animation sends `GrapplePreFire`, and when, is unverified: the patch archive's grapple clips carry neither hash, and the base archives' clips are not named in the file lists.
- The reel's loop is the one looping usage ([`m_ReelingInEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ReelingInEffectUsage), `ReelingIn`). [`GrapplingHook::UpdateLoopingEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-UpdateLoopingEffects), from the post-camera update, plays it while the hook is [`GHS_REELING_IN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_REELING_IN) and stops it softly otherwise, acting only on a change of [`m_ActiveLoopingEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveLoopingEffects). - The one-off usages resolved by name are the active wire retracting ([`m_RetractingActiveWireEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_RetractingActiveWireEffectUsage), `Retracting`), a fire that finds no target ([`m_GrappleFireFailEffectUsage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrappleFireFailEffectUsage), `GrappleFail`), and the release from a reeled attachment (`ReleaseFromAttached`). - The impact is [`WireEnd::PlayImpactEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##func-PlayImpactEffect), played from the end's own cast hit with that hit's material, so a ghost end's `ForceAttachToTarget` plays none.

The wire is a container of its own, whose looping usages follow its state, and play for a custom wire too: a flight loop while an end extends, and the tethers' stress, retraction, and active loops (from `CGrapplingHookWire::UpdateLoopingEffects`, unverified).

## The reel

### The reel's velocity

The reel task ([`NStateTask_ReelIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn)) advances the character's velocity once per tick through [`NStateTask_ReelIn::UpdateReelVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-UpdateReelVelocity) (mislabelled `NStateTask_ReelIn::DoPreReelBlockCheck`), which the trajectory prediction (`SimulateImmediateTrajectory`) also calls per predicted step. Its speeds and accelerations come from the [`GrappleTuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning) passed to it, not from the hook:

- The velocity accelerates along the line by [`m_ReelInAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInAccel), or by [`m_RetractReelInAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_RetractReelInAccel) while the character's control parameters hold the retract context flag and the character is farther than [`m_ReelInDragMaxDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInDragMaxDistance) from the target. The wind-up, collision avoidance, and vehicle entries add their own pushes.
- Beyond that distance, the velocity across the line is dragged by [`m_ReelInPerpDrag`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInPerpDrag); within it, the velocity is turned onto the line, and a step that would pass the target is cut to land on it.
- [`m_ReelInClampAccel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInClampAccel) holds the speed along the line between [`m_ReelInSpeedMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInSpeedMin) and the top speed: [`m_ReelInSpeedMax`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInSpeedMax), or [`m_RetractReelInSpeedMax`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_RetractReelInSpeedMax) under the reel boost, when [`GrapplingHook::GetRetractAcceleratesReelIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetRetractAcceleratesReelIn) holds (the hook's own flag and the retract reel-in boost upgrade) and the retract has been held ([`GrapplingHook::m_TimeHoldingRetract`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_TimeHoldingRetract)) past the threshold that field's def gives. The boost is therefore the retract held during a reel, with no input of its own. `NStateTask_ReelIn::GetMaxSpeed` computes the same choice but has no callers; the update inlines it.
- The reel arrives within [`m_ReelInArrivedDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrappleTuning##field-m_ReelInArrivedDistance) of the target.

### The reel's motion

[`NStateTask_ReelIn::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-Update) reads the character's velocity, advances it with `UpdateReelVelocity`, and hands it to the character with [`NStateTask_ReelIn::UpdateMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-UpdateMotion), which turns the character toward the reel's direction, sets the task-defined motion state, flags the proxy's input velocity as game controlled ([`CharacterInput::gameControledVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-gameControledVelocity)), and sets the wanted world velocity. That velocity is the character's whole motion for the step: `UpdateReelVelocity` adds no gravity, and the prediction integrates it without gravity too. That the proxy's flying state then moves at the wanted velocity as it stands is unverified.

The reel works on the character's velocity less the velocity it inherited, and adds the inherited velocity back after its terms. The inherited velocity blends, by a smoothstep over the reel's running time, from the velocity carried into the reel toward the velocity of the far end's attach point ([`GrapplePointProxy::GetObjectAttachPointVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##vfunc-GetObjectAttachPointVelocity)): a dynamic object's body velocity at the point, or zero for static geometry. A reel onto a moving vehicle therefore chases it.

### The far end's proxy

A wire end attaches to a proxy ([`WireEnd::m_PointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::wire_end::WireEnd##field-m_PointProxy)), a [`GrapplePointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy) whose virtual interface answers for the attached thing whatever its kind ([`m_AttachmentObjectInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##field-m_AttachmentObjectInfo): a character, a vehicle, static geometry, or another object): the attach point's world position and normal, its velocity, the object's mass, the game object, and the body a wire constraint at that end would join ([`GetRigidBodyForConstraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::GrapplePointProxy##vfunc-GetRigidBodyForConstraint)), a dynamic object's own body or none.

The active wire never holds a physics constraint. Its device end is a [`CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grapple_point_proxy::CharacterBoneAttachedPointProxy) built virtual only, whose hint permits no constraint, so `ShouldUseConstraint` refuses it whatever the far end is, and `AttachLocalWireEndToDevice` turns an end it finds physicalized back to virtual. The reel moves the character through its velocity alone.

The hang is the game's one rope swing with a body. Its proxy, the hook's [`m_GrapplingHangProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_GrapplingHangProxy), is a free capsule body placed at the hang and given the character's velocity on activation, and (unverified) it requests an active constraint, so the attachment wire's native spring joins it to the far end's body. Only the reel's arrival into a hang enters it.

### The reel's endings

Nothing inside the task times the reel out or watches its progress, so a velocity that does not close the distance keeps it running. It ends in these ways:

- Cancelled by `Update` (the grapple cancel act, the character righted, the active wire retracted) when the context's time step is not positive, when the active wire's far end is not attached or the hook has left `GHS_REELING_IN`, or when the hook gives no reel target or direction. `RetractActiveWire` detaches the far end, so a retract during the reel is answered by this cancel on the task's next update. The character does not keep its velocity: one world update after the cancel, its whole velocity is scaled to about a tenth on all three axes in a single tick, under the cancel's state (measured on a recorded take); which state or task applies the scale is unverified. A canopy opened out of the reel leaves by another path and keeps the velocity.
- Finished by [`NStateTask_ReelIn::FinishReel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-FinishReel) on arrival, tested by the far end's kind: for a character, the distance left after the tick's travel along the line within the tuning's kick distance; for a vehicle, [`NStateTask_ReelIn::CheckReelToVehicleRelease`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_ReelIn##func-CheckReelToVehicleRelease) (near the entry point, past it, or a predicted pass through a sphere around it); for anything else, facing along the line within `m_ReelInArrivedDistance`, or a distance less the tick's travel under a small buffer. The travel is the whole speed, with a floor, not the speed along the line, and for the last kind the finishing tick does not hand its velocity to the character. A static or generic far end ends in the attachment its setup chose for the surface: a perch, a hang, or on a ceiling the upside-down attach ([`GrapplingHook::StartReelUpsideDown`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-StartReelUpsideDown), which clears the character's velocities and queues the act whose states hold it inverted against the surface), or else a floor landing or a cancel into the fall. Neither the arrival nor the upside-down start changes the hook's state on that path, so an arrival whose upside-down start does nothing leaves the reel running, and it arrives again next tick.
- Resolved by the hook's stuck check, [`GrapplingHook::CheckReelCollisions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-CheckReelCollisions), from the post-simulation update: past the reel's opening jump, while the proxy touches something and the hips close on the target slower than a low speed for longer than a short time, the reel is dropped on walkable ground or for a custom attachment, reattached to a nearby foot support or floor otherwise, or dropped when none is found.
- Replaced by a fire from the reel ([above](#latching-the-target)), or retracted by a cancel click (`OnPreSimUpdate`).

## The tether squeeze

The retract input's analog value squeezes the tethers. [`GrapplingHook::UpdateDualTetherHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-UpdateDualTetherHandling) runs every tick with the hook's action map, reading `RETRACT_GRAPPLE`'s value and `RELEASE_GRAPPLE`'s click, and counting `m_TimeHoldingRetract` while the value is above zero and [`m_TimeSinceRetractHeld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_TimeSinceRetractHeld) while it is zero. A release click releases the tethers. Otherwise the squeeze value is the cube of the retract value while the hook is not initialising, reeling in, or holding a custom wire, the character can retract the grapple, and the player has the tether retract ability, and zero otherwise. Unless a game setting selects otherwise, a ramp of the hold time scales it from a fraction to all of it (the times are in the function's def). [`GrapplingHook::SqueezeDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-SqueezeDualTethers) then rebuilds each wire's constraint toward a shorter length, smooths each wire's squeeze factor toward the value, and sets [`m_IsSqueezingDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_IsSqueezingDualTethers) while it is above zero. The squeeze is a rate: the tethers shorten while it is held and stay put when it drops to zero.

The tethers live in the hook's tether list, apart from [`m_ActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveWire). [`GetNumDualTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetNumDualTethers) counts the list's wires that are neither invalid nor broken and have neither end retracting, [`HasValidTethers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-HasValidTethers) says whether one has both ends attached, and [`GetHighestWireTension`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetHighestWireTension) gives the highest tension fraction among the counted wires.

### The game's own rumble for the line

`CPlayer::UpdateCameraShakeRumble` picks one camera shake and rumble state per update from the hook: the reel with or without the boost (the retract held and `GetRetractAcceleratesReelIn`), the parachute and wingsuit with or without their retract boosts, free fall, and, while the tethers are squeezed and valid, one of three squeeze states by `GetHighestWireTension`: near zero, moderate, and above a threshold given in that function's def. The hang swing's stick impulses are described under [`NStateTask_GrapplingHangTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::NStateTask_GrapplingHangTask##func-Update).
