# Weapons in vehicles

The driver's built-in weapons aim by the dominant hand, the hands fire the guns they hold from a passenger or stunt seat, and a mounted gun is manned with both hands on its handles. The code is in `crates/jc3vr-vehicles/src/vehicles/`: `driving/aim.rs`, `passenger.rs`, and `mounted/`. The mounted guns' tuning is the `mounted` and `gunner_seat` sections of the vehicles' [tuning file](../tuning.md).

## The driver's weapons

Every built-in weapon aims from [`Vehicle::m_AimingTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_AimingTargetPosition), which the player's post-camera update sets through [`Vehicle::SetAimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-SetAimTargetPosition). Under the controller scheme a pre-call detour substitutes the dominant hand's aim point for the vehicle the local player drives, so AI-driven vehicles are untouched; with no hand aim the gaze point passes through. The motorcycle's and jetski's personal weapon is a wielded weapon, which the gun aim already covers.

## Passenger and stunt seats

In a passenger seat or on a stunt position, a gun in the hand aims along its sight line and fires from its trigger or its hand's upper face button ([bindings.md, "Vehicles"](bindings.md#vehicles), [hands-and-aiming.md, "Gun aim"](hands-and-aiming.md#gun-aim)); in the driver's seat the triggers are pedals, so only the face button fires. Outside the motorcycle the inventory's weapon update skips the wielded weapon in a seat ([engine: weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md)), so the mod's per-hand tick arms and fires the guns ([two-handed-inventory.md, "Traversal"](two-handed-inventory.md#traversal)), as in the boost wingsuit.

The seated upper body aims through the vehicle aim blend ([`VehicleAimBlendState::UpdateSamplers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::VehicleAimBlendState##func-UpdateSamplers)) toward [`PlayerAimControl::m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos), which the game sets from the gaze and would twist a passenger looking ahead toward the windscreen while they point out the side window. So in a non-driver seat (`jc3vr_body::controllers::facts::seat::rides_in_seat`) and off a mounted gun, the weapon slot is set to the gun hand's aim point after the aim control's post-simulation update, and the game carries it on to the body. The Status page has a PASSENGER AIM row.

## Mounted guns

A mounted gun, standalone or in a gunner seat, aims as a gun on a pivot does: at the grab a reference is taken from the hands' midpoint, the lever to the pivot, and the barrel, and from then on the barrel points from the hands through the pivot. The aim drives the mount's motors, which are disabled only while a hand holds the gun's own body. A gun gripped by its body, holder, or handle is driven without the use prompt: the runtime gives it a temporary shooter, fires it on the trigger, and tears it off on a sustained pull. A standalone gun's use prompt is hidden (`mounted::runtime::hide_standalone_prompts`), because the native use is hard to leave; a vehicle's gun keeps its prompt for the gunner seat.

### Taking the handles

Each hand takes its own handle like a physical control (`GrabVolume::MountedHandle`), and a handle within reach ranks over every other grip target ([grip.md](grip.md)), so a hand takes the handle rather than the gun's body. A change of hands takes a new reference, so nothing jumps. With both hands off, the gun keeps its last direction in the body frame, so a gunner can let go to throw a grenade and find the gun where they left it. A hand on a handle leaves the physical hands' palms (`physical_hands::free_hands`, through `jc3vr_body::controllers::facts::mounted`), so its hand body does not fight the gun.

### Recoil in the handles

Each shot kicks a stiff damped spring back along the barrel and down, moving the drawn handles, and plays `Event::MountedKick` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)); the kick is capped at a few shots' worth, so a minigun shakes the handles rather than driving them off. The aim is left alone, since the game's gun takes its own recoil. Shots arrive through `mounted::recoil::on_shot` on the weapons crate's seam on [`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) ([weapon-handling.md, "Recoil"](weapon-handling.md#recoil)).

### Tearing a gun off

A sustained pull past the handle's reach (`control::Tear`) tears the gun off (`runtime::tear_off`). The game's detach ([`WeaponBase::DetachMountedGun`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DetachMountedGun)) removes only the attach constraint and leaves the mount's hinges enabled, which would keep the gun on its tripod; the game's own tear equips the gun out of the physics world, where the hinges never act. So the runtime takes the attach constraint and hinges first, since the drop clears the weapon's references to them, and disables them afterwards ([`WeaponBase::m_ConstraintAttach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ConstraintAttach), `mounted::native::free_from_mount`). The gun goes into the hand through the loadout table ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md)) or stays where it fell with its pickup enabled ([`WeaponBase::PickupEnable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupEnable)). The game's use-item action is no fallback, since it picks the nearest pickup rather than the torn gun.

A standalone gun comes off only where [`WeaponBase::m_MountedCanBeDetached`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedCanBeDetached) allows; only the tripod minigun (`w201_minigun_mount`) sets it, through its entity file (read from the data, not traced in the code). A vehicle re-places each gun in [`Vehicle::m_MountedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_MountedWeapon) on its mount every update ([`Vehicle::UpdatePostParts_WeaponsAttachment_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-UpdatePostParts_WeaponsAttachment_Parallel)), so tearing a vehicle's gun also drops it and clears its entry as the vehicle's explosion does (`mounted::vehicle_hold`), outside the vehicle's parallel update ([`Vehicle::m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter)). From the gunner seat the pull first asks for the game's exit from the seat, then tears the gun into the hand.

## The gunner seat

A vehicle's empty gunner seat is entered by stepping into it (`mounted/gunner_seat.rs`): a head held just behind an unmanned gun's handles, while the game offers the seat ([`NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask::HasAvailableGunnerDestination`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask##func-HasAvailableGunnerDestination)), holds the vehicle-entry action, which the game takes past its hold time as the gunner seat ([`NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask##func-Update)). A gunner pulled out ([character-grabs.md](character-grabs.md#holding-on-and-yanking)) or shot frees the gun.

Manning a gun fades the local user out ([`Character::FadeCharacterInOut`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-FadeCharacterInOut)) for the turret camera, which in the headset would hide Rico's hands, so `mounted::input_tick::keep_gunner_visible` fades him back in. A vehicle gun turns on two powered hinges toward a desired yaw and pitch, from the look input when [`WeaponBase::m_MountedInputIsControlledByWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedInputIsControlledByWeapon) is set and from the aim point otherwise ([`WeaponBase::UpdateMountedInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateMountedInput)). Such a gun would ignore the hands, so while they hold the handles `mounted::turret` refuses the look-input path and the hinges turn toward the hands' point at the gun's own speeds and limits. The driving view moves the gunner's view back from the handles ([steering-wheel.md, "The driving view"](steering-wheel.md#the-driving-view)).
