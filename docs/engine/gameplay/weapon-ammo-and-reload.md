# Weapon ammunition and reload

How a weapon's rounds are counted, how the game refills a magazine, and what a reload does to the weapon's model parts. Everything here is driven by the character's animation: the reload act, the reload clip's track messages, and the reload state's state bits. The weapon entity's files and its model parts are described in [weapon-data.md](weapon-data.md), and the automatic switch away from an empty weapon in [weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md).

## Where the counts live

A weapon holds only its magazine. [`WeaponBase::m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo) is one count per weapon object, so the two guns of the dual-wield pistols share it. The capacity is the tuning's [`m_magazine_size`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_magazine_size); a negative size means the weapon has no magazine limit and never reloads. There is no chambered round: a full magazine is the magazine size, and nothing tells a reload from empty apart from a reload with rounds left.

The reserve belongs to the holder, not the weapon. [`Inventory::m_Ammunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Ammunition) holds one count per ammunition kind, capped by [`Inventory::m_MaxAmmunition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_MaxAmmunition), which the ammunition upgrades raise. The weapon's kind is [`WeaponBase::m_AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AmmunitionType), an [`AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::AmmunitionType) set from the weapon's properties, so every weapon of a kind draws on the same reserve. The inventory is embedded in the character ([`Character::m_Inventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_Inventory)).

[`Inventory::GetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAmmoCount) reads a reserve. [`Inventory::SetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-SetAmmoCount) writes one, capped at the maximum, and [`Inventory::GiveMaxAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GiveMaxAmmo) fills them all. Both have a side effect: a reserve refilled from empty starts a reload of the wielded weapon of that kind when its magazine is empty, so picking up ammunition for an empty gun reloads it. A refill of hand grenades from empty reloads the hand-grenade weapon directly.

## Reload kinds

[`WeaponBase::m_ReloadType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReloadType) comes from the weapon's `reload_type` property, not from its class:

- [`E_RELOAD_MAGAZINE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_MAGAZINE) refills the whole magazine in one step. Nearly every hand weapon uses it, the rocket launchers, the mortar, and the semi-automatic and sawn-off shotguns included.
- [`E_RELOAD_BULLET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_BULLET) adds one round per refill and keeps the reload going while [`WeaponBase::m_KeepReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_KeepReloading) is set. Among the hand weapons only the pump shotgun, the U-Rys 96, uses it.
- [`E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION) is a timer with no act. The guitar-case launcher, the missile strike, and the EMP use it.

## Starting a reload

Every animated reload starts in [`Character::StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-StartReloadingWieldingWeapon). It queues the upper-body reload act, `UB_ACT_RELOAD`, only when [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsReloading) holds (the inventory's reload permission and a clear `SB_DISABLE_RELOAD`), the detonate input is zero, and the wielded weapon's [`CanReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-CanReload) holds. `CanReload` wants a limited ammunition kind, a magazine limit, a magazine below it, and either unlimited ammunition or a reserve (a holder in god mode always counts as having one). When the act is accepted its callback runs the weapon's [`StartReloadSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StartReloadSequence), which plays the reload effect and, for a bullet reload, sets `m_KeepReloading`. No rounds move yet. The reload state raises `SB_IS_RELOADING`, which [`Character::IsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsReloading) reads.

The callers, which together are every way the game reloads:

- the reload input, in [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling);
- the ammunition check, [`WeaponBase::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase), in three places: the automatic reload of a player's empty magazine, every tick while the magazine is out, and the predicted reload of the local player's idle, partly empty weapon;
- a reserve refilled from empty, through `SetAmmoCount`, `GiveMaxAmmo`, `CInventory::GiveMaxAmmoToAllWeaponInInventory`, `CInventory::SetMaxBeaconsByLevel`, and `CInventory::SetMaxFlaresByLevel`;
- the two reload-request track messages (below);
- the rocket launcher, when firing an unloaded launcher ([`RPGWeapon::Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-Fire_Start)) and for a holder that is not a player ([`RPGWeapon::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered));
- the sniper rifle's per-tick update, after a shot that emptied the magazine.

[`Character::CancelReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CancelReload) queues `UB_ACT_CANCEL_RELOAD` while `SB_IS_RELOADING` is set and clears the reload permission. [`WeaponBase::ReloadAnimationFinished`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ReloadAnimationFinished), which the reload state's `NWeaponReloadFinishedTask` calls on exit, clears `m_KeepReloading` and [`m_IsReloading`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsReloading).

## Refilling the magazine

The rounds move in the [`Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Reload) virtual, whose base implementation is [`WeaponBase::ReloadBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ReloadBase). It refuses a weapon without a holder, of the unlimited kind, or without a magazine limit. Otherwise it clears [`m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine) first, whether or not it refills anything. A full magazine, or an empty reserve without unlimited ammunition, ends there: it clears `m_KeepReloading` and returns false.

Otherwise the new count is the magazine size, or the old count plus the reserve when that is less. With unlimited ammunition the reserve counts as a full magazine. A bullet reload adds one round instead and sets `m_KeepReloading` while the magazine is still short of full. The count goes through [`SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo), which updates the HUD. The reserve is written back as the old count plus the reserve minus the new count, so rounds left in the old magazine return to the reserve and a reload with rounds left loses nothing. With unlimited ammunition the reserve is not touched. It finally clears `m_KeepReloading` on a full magazine and `m_IsReloading` always, and returns true.

`Reload` never touches the weapon's model parts. A magazine part hidden by the magazine drop stays hidden until the reload clip shows it again with its enable-model message.

The rocket launcher's override, [`RPGWeapon::Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##func-Reload), puts the rocket in the launcher ([`m_AttachedState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::RPGWeapon##field-m_AttachedState)) when the magazine is not empty. The launcher's per-tick update also shows and loads the rocket whenever the magazine holds rounds and the rocket is not loaded, so any refill brings it back. The launcher fires only with the rocket loaded. The mortar's override also wraps the base.

An [`E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ReloadType##variant-E_RELOAD_NO_ANIMATION) weapon needs no act. When the ammunition check would start a reload it instead loads [`m_ReloadTimeLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReloadTimeLeft) from the tuning's [`m_reload_time_scale`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_reload_time_scale) and sets `m_IsReloading`. [`UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel) counts it down, the check blocks the shot while it runs, and the check calls `Reload` when it runs out.

## The ammunition check

[`WeaponBase::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase) runs for each gun from [`UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel) with the tick's fire decision, which it may clear. In order:

1. A finished no-animation reload timer calls `Reload`; a running one clears the shot.
2. A requested shot is cleared while the holder reloads, draws, or holsters.
3. For a player, with a limited kind and a magazine limit, an empty magazine with no reload running either starts a reload (when `CanReload` and the reload permission hold), or, when the weapon cannot reload and the shot was requested, tries the automatic switch to another weapon and plays the out-of-ammunition feedback at the pull's press edge. Either way the shot is cleared. An empty magazine with a reserve left therefore never fires: it reloads, and it does not switch.
4. While [`m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine) is set, it clears the shot and calls `StartReloadingWieldingWeapon` on every tick, and does nothing else.
5. Otherwise, for a weapon that does not reload by magazine, outside the hand-grenade, planted-explosive, and beacon slots, it cancels the holder's reload while `m_KeepReloading` is clear; it clears the shot while the holder's no-firing state is set; and it starts the predicted reload of the local player's idle weapon below [`m_PredictedReloadAmmunitionLimit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PredictedReloadAmmunitionLimit) once [`m_PredictedReloadTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PredictedReloadTime) has passed without input.

## Track messages

The reload clip drives the reload through track messages. The character's message poll, in its serial post-physics pass, hands each [`AnimationMessageData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::message::AnimationMessageData) to the character, which forwards the weapon messages to [`WeaponBase::HandleTrackMessage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HandleTrackMessage). The reload messages are named constants on [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase):

- `TRACK_MESSAGE_THROW_AWAY_MAGAZINE` drops the magazine (below). Its arguments are a part name hash, a gun index, and a flag that keeps the magazine-out state clear. It does nothing while the magazine is already out.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_RELOAD` calls `Reload`.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_ENABLE_MODEL_ATTACHEMENT` shows or hides every model part whose name hash is its first argument, by its second argument. Reload clips use it to show the magazine part again.
- `TRACK_MESSAGE_START_RELOAD` and `TRACK_MESSAGE_START_RELOAD_IF_EMPTY` call `StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`, the second only with an empty magazine.

The names of the messages are not known; the constants are named for what the handler does.

## The magazine drop and the spare prop

Each gun ([`WeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) keeps a spare magazine prop, [`m_MagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineObject), a disabled rigid object. [`WeaponData::SpawnMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-SpawnMagazineObject) requests it from the spawn system by the gun's [`m_MagazineUniqueTag`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineUniqueTag), a spawn tag from the weapon's properties; the spawn completes later and leaves the prop disabled. A gun without a magazine prop has no tag and never gets a spare. The weapon's post-initialization requests the first spare.

[`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) drops it. Its `this` is the gun, and it takes the holder and a part name hash:

- With a part hash, it looks through the weapon's [`m_WeaponComponents`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponComponents) for a model part ([`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent)) whose [`m_NameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_NameHash) matches, takes the part's world matrix as the drop transform, and hides the part with its `Enable` virtual. The magazine part is `model.magazine`. With no matching part it drops nothing and fails.
- With no hash, or the hash of `no_magazine_model`, it drops from the gun's own world matrix and hides nothing. A gun whose magazine is part of its body mesh, such as the pistols and submachine guns, has no part to hide.

It then makes the spare dynamic at the drop transform, puts it in the holder's collision system group so it does not hit the holder, gives it the gun's [`m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) with any upward part removed and the gun's angular velocity, sets its body material, enables it, adds it to the update, and lets go of it. A flicked gun throws its magazine. The spare's body is reached through the rigid object's [`PhysicsGameObject::GetIPfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsGameObject##vfunc-GetIPfxInstance), its one body's wrapper, and the enabling is the game object's [`GameObject::Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-Enable), whose rigid-object override adds the body to the physics world on enabling and removes it on disabling. Once dropped, the prop is an ordinary rigid object in the world: nothing records the rounds the magazine held, and the gun keeps counting them until its next reload returns them to the reserve.

The track message wraps the throw: when the throw succeeds it calls `SpawnMagazineObject` for the next spare and sets `m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`. The flag is the magazine-out state. The ammunition check keeps the weapon from firing and keeps asking for a reload while it is set, the animation condition `CWeaponHasMagazineInsertedCondition` reads it, and only `Reload` clears it.

To find a gun's parts, walk `m_WeaponComponents` (shared pointers to [`WeaponComponentBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase)). The game takes an entry as a model part when its [`IsType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-IsType) accepts [`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-TYPE_ID), which also accepts the barrel recoil and barrel spin parts derived from it. The magazine drop additionally requires [`m_Type`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##field-m_Type) to be the plain model-attachment kind. A part's [`m_Enabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##field-m_Enabled) says whether it is shown, and its [`Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-Enable) shows or hides it.

## Unlimited ammunition

[`WeaponBase::HasUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HasUnlimitedAmmo) is true when the holder has [`Character::m_UnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_UnlimitedAmmo), when the holder is not a player, during the challenge-island game mode, or when the tuning's [`m_unlimited_ammo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_unlimited_ammo) is set. Such a weapon still has a magazine and still reloads, but the reload fills it without touching the reserve. A weapon of the [`AMMO_UNLIMITED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::AmmunitionType##variant-AMMO_UNLIMITED) kind, or with no magazine limit, is different: it never reloads at all.

## The HUD

The HUD's ammunition readout is pushed, not polled. [`SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo) pushes the magazine count (`CHUDUI::OnSetSelectedMagazineAmmo`) for the local player's selected weapon, `SetAmmoCount` pushes the reserve (`CHUDUI::OnSetSelectedInventoryAmmo`) when it is the selected weapon's kind, and `Reload` tells the HUD a reload happened (`CHUDUI::OnReloadSelectedWeapon`). A write to the counts that bypasses these leaves the readout stale.

## Threads and phases

The track messages, and with them the animated `Reload` and the magazine drop, run in the holder's serial post-physics pass (`CCharacter::UpdatePassPostPhysics_Serial`), on the game update thread after the physics step. The no-animation reload's `Reload` runs from the ammunition check inside the weapon's parallel update. The magazine drop writes rigid-body state through the physics world, which the physics system allows only on the thread holding the world's write mark.

## Sniper bolt and shotgun pump

Neither cycle moves ammunition; both are animation.

A bolt-action rifle ([`SniperWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon) with [`m_BoltAction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltAction), from the weapon's properties) locks after each shot. Its [`Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile) sets [`m_HasFiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_HasFiredThisInput) for a player user ([`WeaponBase::m_IsUserPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserPlayer)). While the lock is set the rifle's fire start and ammunition check do nothing, so it neither fires nor reloads. Its per-tick update ([`SniperWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel)) waits for the trigger to be released and, in precision aim, for [`m_BoltActionDelayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltActionDelayer). Then, with rounds in the magazine, it plays the bolt-action effect and queues `UB_ACT_SNIPER_BOLT_ACTION` on every tick until the bolt state raises `SB_IS_RELOADING`; with an empty magazine it starts a reload instead. The update clears the lock only while the holder reloads or has no ammunition at all, so the bolt state's `SB_IS_RELOADING` is what releases it. With [`m_BoltActionAutoZoomOut`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_BoltActionAutoZoomOut) the cycle takes the local player out of precision aim and back afterwards ([`m_ShouldgoBackToPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_ShouldgoBackToPrecisionAim)), as the rifle's [`StartReloadSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-StartReloadSequence) does for a reload. Which rifles carry a clip of their own for the bolt act is unverified.

The pump shotgun's pump is a [`DoActWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent). Its [`Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-Fire_Start), run for each shot by [`WeaponBase::Fire_StartBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_StartBase), plays [`m_FireStartAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##field-m_FireStartAct) when [`ShouldActActivate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-ShouldActActivate) allows it: on the component's skeletal object if it has one, otherwise on every gun's own animated model ([`WeaponData::DoAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-DoAct)) with [`m_SendActToWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##field-m_SendActToWeapon), otherwise on the holder. The U-Rys 96's property file has two, one aimed at the holder and one at the gun. The acts are queued and gate nothing: no state of the pump blocks the next shot.

A pistol slide is a [`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent). Its placement ([`UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)) moves the part back along its own forward axis by up to [`m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength) after each shot and returns it. With [`m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo), a player's wielded weapon holds the slide fully back while the magazine is empty, which is the slide lock-back. A barrel recoil part is told from a plain model part by its vtable pointer ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::VFTABLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent)), by `m_Type`, or by its own type id.

### How each action is modelled

The hand weapons' actions are modelled in three ways, read from the weapons' entity files and models with the tools in `tools/sights/`:

- A separate part. The pistols' slides are barrel recoil parts, one per gun of a dual-wielded pair, and the U-SV 45 sniper rifle's moving piece is a barrel recoil part named `ejection_port`, a cover on the right of the receiver beside the bore rather than a bolt on it.
- A bone of the gun's own skeleton. The U-Rys 96 pump shotgun, the CS111 Archangel, and the Final Argument have animated models whose skeletons hold a `Reference` root and one other bone: the pump ([`WeaponData::PUMP_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) on the shotgun, and the bolt ([`WeaponData::SLIDER_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)) on the two rifles, which their pump and bolt-action clips move. A gun's animated model ([`WeaponData::m_AnimatedModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_AnimatedModel)) is posed in the gun's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)), which advances its clips and then computes the model-space pose that the joint accessors of its animation controller read and write, and the render phase skins that pose. The Urga Vdova 89 and the U-Rys 12 have skeletons too, with a `Chamber` and a `Reload` bone.
- Nothing. The SMGs' and rifles' charging handles are part of the body mesh and do not move.

Which way a skeleton's bone moves, and where a bolt bone's origin lies relative to the bolt's axis, have not been read from the skeletons or the clips.
