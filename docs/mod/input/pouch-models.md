# The pouches' models

The grenade, C4, and magazine pouches on Rico's body have no model of their own in the game, so the mod draws the game's own models on them: a grenade on the grenade pouch, the C4 charge as it shows in the hand on the C4 pouch, and the held gun's magazine on the magazine pouch. A magazine a hand takes from the pouch is drawn in that hand the same way. What the player sees on the body is what a hand reaching there takes ([grip.md](grip.md), [controllers.md, "Sockets, pouches, the foregrip, and the action-runs tier"](controllers.md#sockets-pouches-the-foregrip-and-the-action-runs-tier)). The code is `payload/src/controllers/weapons/pouch_models.rs`, and the setting is `weapons.pouches.models.enabled` (on), in the debug UI's Weapons > Inventory and body slots.

None of this has been run in the headset.

## The models

Each model is an instance the mod owns, made from a model the game already holds for the item's lifetime, through [`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromModel):

- the grenade: the hand grenade slot's weapon's projectile model ([`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::m_PhysicalProjectileModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PhysicalProjectileModel));
- the C4 charge: the planted explosive slot's weapon's in-hand model ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon::m_InactiveModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_InactiveModelInstance), the model despite its name);
- the magazine: the model of the reloaded gun's spare magazine prop ([`WeaponData::m_MagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MagazineObject), [`RigidObject::m_Model`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##field-m_Model)), which the game drops and respawns a while after each magazine it drops. Each instance holds its own reference to the model ([`ModelInstance::m_spModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##field-m_spModel)), so once the belt's or the hand's magazine has been made for a weapon, it stays drawn while the prop is absent, and the other one can be made from its reference (`pouch_models::owned::magazine_source`). Only before either has been made for the weapon is there no magazine to draw.

The instance holds its own reference to the model and is added to the world, where the engine's dynamic cull draws it like any other model, casting a shadow, with no physics and no game object behind it. Its clip on distance is cleared, as the game does for the explosives a character holds.

## Where they are

A model is drawn while its pouch has something to give, wherever the pouch is placed and whichever hands are free: the grenade while there is a grenade in stock and the grenade throw is on, the C4 charge while another charge may be placed and the C4 throw is on, and the magazine while the true reload acts on the held weapon and there is a round in reserve. It sits at its pouch's centre, turned with the girdle the pouch rides (the chest for the grenade and the C4, the pelvis for the magazine), moved and turned by its prop's placement (`Prop::placement` in `controllers::weapons::pouch_models`: `offset_m` in the girdle's axes and `rotation_deg` about X, then Y, then Z), given for the right side and mirrored across the body's midline for the left. The grenade's and the magazine's placement (`MODEL_PLACEMENT`) is zero, which leaves them at their pouches' centres. The C4 charge's (`CHARGE_PLACEMENT`) pitches it down a quarter turn about the girdle's X: its in-hand model lies face up, so square to the girdle it stood straight out of the chest, and pitched it lies flat on the chest with its face forward. The debug UI shows which models are drawn, including the magazine in the hand, how many instances were made, and how many frames drew one.

The chest's explosives holster draws nothing: the charge it selects is already drawn on the C4 pouch.

## The magazine in the hand

While a hand holds a magazine taken from the pouch, the true reload asks for it to be drawn (`pouch_models::hand_tick`, from the reload's input tick), and a second instance of the gun's spare magazine model is shown for it, separate from the gun, so the gun keeps its own magazine part and the old magazine stays drawn in it until it leaves the gun ([reload.md, "Load"](reload.md#load)). Pistols and SMGs, whose magazine is part of the gun's body, get one in the hand this way too. Once per rendered frame it is placed where the reload holds the round, at the frame's interpolation fraction on the hand as it is drawn, the same pose the gun's own magazine part was moved to before (`reload::held_round_matrix`). It is hidden once the hand lets go or the magazine seats, and on any tick the reload does not ask for it. A magazine from the world needs none: it is its own object in the hand.

The model's own frame is the frame the game drops the spare from: the gun's magazine part's, for a gun that has one (the spare is the same model as the part), and the gun's own, for the pistols and SMGs. The wells table gives the top of each spare model, centred across it, in that frame (`spare_top_m`, from `tools/sights/extract_wells.py`), and leaves it out where the spare does not depict the magazine a hand loads (the UPU-210, whose spare is the empty cartridge) or the gun has none. Those guns, and every gun with the setting off, keep the reload's old drawing of the round in the hand with the gun's own part. A held gun whose first gun has no magazine prop at all ([`WeaponData::NO_MAGAZINE_UNIQUE_TAG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-NO_MAGAZINE_UNIQUE_TAG)) logs one line per weapon type when a magazine is taken for it.

The shotguns and the revolvers have no spare magazine prop and no round part, so their rounds in the hand are drawn with a stand-in, the casing model the gun's casing-ejection effect throws out ([reload.md, "Load"](reload.md#load)): one shell for a shotgun, and six pistol casings in a ring for a revolver's cylinder, one instance per casing, each pushed at its own offset round the group's axis. The wells table names the model by its `.lod` path (`round_model`), and the instances are made from the game's model cache under that path's hash ([`ModelInstanceManager::CreateInstanceFromHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-CreateInstanceFromHash)), which loads nothing: the gun's entity carries the model for its effect, so it is cached while the gun is loaded. The first casing comes from the cache and the rest from its reference. A model not in the cache draws nothing and logs one line per model. These instances are never the belt's: the magazine pouch draws no shell.

From the game's data, the guns without a spare magazine prop are the Balloon Head pistol, the revolvers, the shotguns, and every launcher but the CS Bildt Negotiator and the UPU-210 (and the Hydra, whose spare is its rocket pod). The CS9 SMG shares the Wraith 2000's magazine model, and the Capstone A-92 the Urga Vdova's. The Automat 12, whose magazine is part of its body, borrows the UP-61's spare magazine prop, and once the game has spawned it, the belt and the hand draw it as any gun's ([reload.md, "Load"](reload.md#load)).

## Riding the body

On the input tick, each model's placement is carried into the character's model space and kept with the last tick's. Once per rendered frame, before the engine's render update culls and draws the frame (from `Game::UpdateRender`, after the late update's snapshot), each shown model's matrix is pushed ([`ModelInstance::UpdateMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-UpdateMatrix)) as its placement between the last two ticks', carried by the character's world transform between its last two ticks' ([`Character::m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) and [`m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)), at the frame's interpolation fraction, so it rides the body as the body is drawn ([render-time-anchoring.md](../rendering/render-time-anchoring.md)). A model shown afresh starts at its new place rather than sweeping from its last.

## Lifetime

The instances are made, shown, hidden, and released in the input tick's game stage, on the game thread before the world update and outside the engine's input job, as the engine requires ([controllers.md](controllers.md#bindings-and-injection)). A model whose pouch has nothing to give is hidden ([`ModelInstanceManager::HideModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-HideModelInstance)) and kept for when it comes back. An instance is released ([`ModelInstanceManager::AddToDeleteQueue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstanceManager##func-AddToDeleteQueue)) when its source model or weapon changes (another gun's magazine), when the weapon handling stands down, outside gameplay (where the world the instance lives in may be torn down), with the setting off, and when the payload unloads.

## In the headset

1. Stand with grenades and C4 in stock and look down at the chest: a grenade sits on the dominant hand's side and a C4 charge on the other, and they stay there when a gun passes to the other hand, on the pouches' markers. Turn, crouch, bend, and walk: they stay on the body.
2. Draw a rifle with reserve ammunition and look at the belt: its magazine sits at the middle of the belt's front. Draw a pistol: the magazine changes to the pistol's.
3. Throw every grenade: the grenade on the chest goes, and comes back with a grenade picked up.
4. Open the parachute, fall, and drive: the models stay on the body without lagging it.
5. Check that each model sits naturally on its pouch, and note the offset and turn any model needs.
6. Load a save and quit to the menu and back: the models come back, and the log has no errors.
7. Take a magazine from the pouch for a rifle with its magazine still in, then for a pistol and an SMG: a magazine shows in the hand each time while the belt's stays drawn, and the gun keeps its own. Eject and take one at once, before the gun's spare respawns: both the belt's and the hand's are still drawn. The full list is in [reload.md, "In the headset: the magazine in the hand"](reload.md#in-the-headset-the-magazine-in-the-hand).
8. Take a shell for a shotgun and a cylinder for a revolver: a shell, and a ring of six casings, show in the hand, and the belt draws nothing for either.

## Unverified

- Whether the instances are safe across a save load beyond being released outside gameplay, and whether texture streaming covers them.
- Whether the models' own origins put them on the pouches with a zero placement, or need a default offset and turn per model.
- Whether the casing models are in the model cache under their `.lod` paths' hashes while their guns are held.
- Whether pushing the matrix before the engine's render update is late enough in every frame for the cull to see it.
