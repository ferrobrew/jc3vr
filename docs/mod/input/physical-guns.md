# Physical guns

A gun in a hand is a body of its own in the physics world, joined to the hand bodies that hold it ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#hand-bodies)). Its weight hangs from the hands and drags them by its mass, it collides along its whole length, a two-handed hold is two joints, and swung into a character it strikes as the hands do. Each hand is drawn at its body and the gun at its body.

The code is split three ways. The gun bodies are the hand bodies' own, in `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/physics/guns/`. The guns in the hands, the shots that kick them, and what each step reports back are a store in the held-item facts, `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/held/guns/`, which also carries the weapon handling's support and weight targets, registered at install, for the hand bodies' drive. The weapon handling publishes into that store from `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/gun_bodies/`. The engine side is in [havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md) and [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#one-object-for-its-whole-life). The tuning is the physics tuning's `hand_bodies.guns` (`crates/jc3vr-physical/tuning/physics.toml`; [tuning.md](../tuning.md)).

A held gun has no body, and the kinematic weight ([`heft`](../../../crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/heft/mod.rs)), the synthetic recoil offset, and the wall probe ([physical-weapons.md, "Guns against walls"](physical-weapons.md#guns-against-walls)) act on its hand instead, in three cases: while its hand's body is parked (a vehicle seat, a swim, the wingsuit, the parachute, a lost controller), while no gun body has been made yet, and for a heavy weapon whose own body is in the world ([The lifecycle](#the-lifecycle)). Gun bodies are allowed on foot on the ground and falling, including during the grapple's traversal, where an empty palm does not act.

## Which body

Each gun has a body of its own ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)), in the world only while the weapon lies dropped or is mounted. Reusing it while the gun is held would mean fighting the weapon's state changes, which add it to and take it out of the world, and joining the hands to a body the game destroys with the weapon whenever it likes; the world does not end a constraint when one of its bodies is destroyed ([havok-bodies.md](../../engine/physics/havok-bodies.md#joining-bodies-with-a-fixed-constraint)). So the gun body is the mod's own, made the way the hand bodies are, with the gun's own shape ([`hknpBody::m_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_shape), which the new body takes a reference of its own to) and a mass taken from the gun's own body. Its joints join two bodies of the mod's own, so nothing the mod holds names a body the game can free.

The own body's mass is the weapon's pickup's, far heavier than a gun: against a hand body, a gun that heavy swings the hand rather than the other way round, and the gun flails. So the mass is held between `min_mass_kg` and the weight class's `max_mass_by_class_kg`, the dual-wield pair's two guns share their weapon's mass, and a gun whose own body gives no finite mass takes the class's `fallback_mass_kg` (`guns::math::gun_mass`). Its gravity stays on.

It is on the weapons layer in the player's collision group, as the hand bodies are: it meets the static world, terrain, props, debris, character capsules, ragdolls, and vehicles, and not the player, the hands, other weapons-layer bodies, or the aim's ray query, so it never blocks a shot.

## The seat and the joints

The weapon handling's input tick publishes each hand's gun (`gun_bodies::read_holds`): the weapon, the gun, its own body's id and wrapper, the weight class, and the bracing hand's contact. A gun counts only while the rigid grip has pinned it to that hand within the hold tuning's `gun_bodies.pinned_lately_ms` and no slide (a draw, a holster, a take) is moving it. The hand holding a gun then acts, so its body is driven toward its controller with the hand's full strength, against the hand's mass and the gun body's together. A loose object's smaller holding budget would let a rifle outweigh what the hand can turn.

The next pre-simulation step checks that the gun's own body still belongs to the gun (its slot's engine instance is the gun's wrapper, which the gun's destruction clears) and is out of the world, reads the gun's last placement ([`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1), the frame the gun's own shape is in), and takes the gun's seat on the hand from it: the placement in the frame of the controller's palm, which the hand's body is driven to. The placement is the attach bone's, which the rigid grip pins to the hand, so the seat is where the grip puts the gun in the palm.

The gun waits, with no body, while it is not in the hand yet or the hand's body is not on its palm yet:

- A seat further out than `max_grip_seat_m` is not in the hand (`GunError::NotInHand`). A gun sliding onto the grip from another part may sit as far out as its muzzle, held between `max_grip_seat_m` and `max_carry_reach_m`.
- A hand body further from its palm than `settled_shift_m` or `settled_turn_rad` is unsettled (`GunError::HandUnsettled`). The seat is taken from the palm, not from the body, so a body still catching up with its palm would otherwise hold the gun as far off the hand as it lagged, for as long as it was held.

The gun body is then made on its seat on the hand's body, moving with it, and joined to it there with a fixed constraint. It is checked to be in the player's collision system group; one that is not is destroyed and the gun waits.

### The brace

While the other hand holds the gun's foregrip or another support region ([Grabbing a gun anywhere](#grabbing-a-gun-anywhere), `weapons::foregrip`), the physical target uses the selected region's point, converted from the holding wrist's frame into the gun's frame, with the world scale applied once. The supporting palm keeps the tracked wrist orientation and the actual grip-to-palm displacement. Before making the second fixed constraint, the step places that palm's body on the selected point of the actual gun body, because a fixed constraint preserves whatever error it is made with.

The brace stores the palm frame in the gun its joint was made with, and is remade when the wanted frame has moved past `brace.distance_m` or turned past `brace.angle_rad` from that frame, and no sooner than `brace.min_interval_s` after it was made. Measuring from the joint's frame rather than the last step's means aim jitter never remakes it and a slow slide still does. A remake that moves the palm less than `quiet_rebrace_m` along the gun does not mute the palm's contacts. Releasing the support hold removes the brace; a released blend keeps no physical joint. Short hand spans fade the holder's steering only, so an engaged support hand still reaches its selected point.

The bracing hand rides the gun. While the brace stands, each step gives the bracing hand's body the velocity it has as part of the gun body (the gun's velocity at the hand's centre of mass, and the gun's turn), after the gun has been given its own, so the holding hand, the gun, and the bracing hand move as one rigid body whatever the bracing hand's own drive asked. Only the holding hand's drive steers, toward the two-handed pose, which already turns the gun onto the line between the controllers and carries the braced weight; the holding hand's drive carries the bracing hand's mass along with the gun's. Two drives that each close their whole error in a step, joined through one rigid gun, would pull it between them on alternate steps whenever the controllers disagreed with the gun's length or the support wrist turned. When a gun body strays and is put back on its seat, its bracing hand is put back on the gun with it. A bracing hand's distance from its controller neither pushes the player nor presses the hand.

The drawn bracing hand rides the holding hand's correction rather than its own (`physics::render::apply_offsets`). The two-handed pose puts the bracing hand on its point of the gun as the holding hand places it, and each hand body's drawn offset is what holds its body back from where its controller published it. The holding hand's offset moves the gun it holds, so the bracing hand takes that offset, turned about the holding palm, and stays on the gun as drawn. Its own body's offset is left out: a bracing hand whose body is not joined to the gun yet presses against the gun body and would be drawn held off the gun by it.

The brace breaks when the bracing hand has left the gun. The drawn bracing hand is on the gun wherever its controller is, so the step measures the tracked palm, before the two-handed pose moves it onto the gun, against where the brace holds the palm on the gun body. Further than `brace.break_distance_m` (at the world scale) for `brace.break_steps` steps in a row, the brace's joint ends and is not made again, and the foregrip hold lets go with `left the gun` at the next input tick (`held::guns::take_support_break`). Without the break, nothing measures the tracked hand against the physical gun: the foregrip's own release measures against a holding hand turned onto the off-hand and lets the hand slide along the handguard and barrel, so a hand the brace joint did not hold could keep the hold engaged while drawn well off the gun.

### Keeping the gun on its seat

Each step gives the gun body the velocity it has as part of the holding hand's body (the hand's velocity at the gun's centre of mass, and the hand's turn), so the two move as one rigid body and the joint holds the gun on its seat rather than catching it up a step late. What the gun meets still stops it within the step, and a shot's kick is given after. Each step also measures how far the gun body strays from its seat (the deviation, in the gun's own frame). A body past the drawn clamps (`drawn_max_shift_m` and `drawn_max_turn_deg`) is steered back to them within the step: the velocity that carries its deviation to the clamped one is added to the velocity the hand carries it with (`guns::math::clamp_velocity`), so the solver still stops it against what it meets, and a shot's muzzle rise lifts the body no further than the render shows. A gun body that strays past `stray_distance_m` or `stray_turn_deg` all the same, as when it is pressed into a wall, or whose hand's body was put somewhere rather than driven there (added, put back on its controller, or carried by a snap turn), is put back on its seat at once.

## Drawing the gun and the hands

The hands are drawn at their bodies as every hand body is ([physical-hands.md, "Drawing the hand at its body"](physical-hands.md#drawing-the-hand-at-its-body)), with no turn deadband for a hand on a gun, so a gun turned by the other hand's pull is drawn turned. The rigid grip ([`crate::controllers::hands::grip`](../../../crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/hands/grip.rs)) pins the gun's attach bone to the drawn wrist at the canonical grip, then carries it by the gun body's deviation from its seat, held to the same drawn clamps the body is held to, so the drawn gun is where the gun body is and the two agree. Everything that derives from the gun's placement follows it: the muzzle effects and the shot origin, the gun aim's sight line, the reload's magazine wells, and the render-time late update.

The support and class-weight composition (`gun_bodies::apply`, shared through `guns::targets`) supplies both the physical palm targets and the reference hands the drawn offsets are applied to, so the gun stays near-rigid on a holding hand that droops under one-handed weight and steadies with support. The physical offset measures only the body's remaining deviation from that reference: the weight response is not applied again after it, and the gun body's gravity is the simulated load rather than a second sag. The hand composition's `keep_physical` stage puts the physical gun hands back after the synthetic recoil and the wall probe, which the body replaces. Aiming down the sights still turns the drawn gun onto the eye, and the weight's wider one-handed spread still acts.

## Weight, collision, and strikes

The gun's weight hangs from the joints and its inertia rides the hands: the hand driving it carries the gun's mass, so a heavier gun lags a fast swing a little more, and a braced gun carries the bracing hand's mass with it. The droop and trail that make a one-handed long gun feel heavy are the class weight in the target ([`heft`](../../../crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/heft/mod.rs)), which the body follows; the body's own mass is held low enough that it never swings the hand. Pushed into a wall, the gun stops along its whole length, a side brushing a wall included, and the hand behind it lags, which pushes the player away as a hand pressed into a wall does. A gun pressed into a crate shoves it.

The gun body's contacts are listened for as a hand body's are ([physical-hands.md, "Hits"](physical-hands.md#hits)), and each counts as its holding hand's. A contact with a body in the player's collision group is none: the gun body never collides with them, and a hand touching the gun it holds is not a strike. A contact pulses the hand by the arm's mass alone at the hand's speed, not the gun's, and at most once every `contact_pulse_interval_s` per hand, since a gun rubbing along a wall touches it on every step and the gun's mass would saturate the pulse into a constant buzz. A gun swung into a character is a blow, its impulse the momentum of the arm (`hand_bodies.hits.blow_mass_kg`) and the gun together at the gun's speed, so a rifle butt strikes harder than a fist at the same speed. One swing through a character is one blow for the hand and its gun together.

## Shots

Each shot of a gun whose body the hands hold kicks that body: an impulse back along the barrel of the class's `recoil_impulse_ns`, and `muzzle_rise` of that upward across the barrel, given at the muzzle, so the muzzle rises about the gun's centre of mass. The barrel is the fire point's axis, and the up is the world's up across it as the gun was held when the body was made. The kick pulls the hands with it within their strength, and a braced gun kicks less. The shots are queued from the per-shot seam (`weapons::fire`, `held::guns::queue_shot`) and given at the next step.

A gun pointed at the player's own body hits it. The game's bullets ignore the bodies their holder names ([`WeaponBase::GetHolderInformation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetHolderInformation)), so a character never hits itself; under the controller scheme, `weapons::self_hits` takes the local player's character out of that list for the shots of the local player's bullet weapons ([`BulletWeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BulletWeaponBase##func-Fire_Projectile)). The hit then takes the game's own damage path at full strength, since a character skips its same-faction filter for damage the local player deals ([melee-and-damage.md](../../engine/gameplay/melee-and-damage.md#factions)). A vehicle holder's list (the vehicle and its driver) and the holder query's other callers are unchanged, and gamepad play keeps the game's behaviour.

## The lifecycle

The gun body is made, kept, and destroyed only in the pre-simulation step, on the game thread outside the physics step, like the hand bodies. Its joints always go before either body they join (`guns::step::destroy_slot`: the brace, then the holder's joint, then the body):

- The gun body goes when the gun leaves the hand (holstered, docked, dropped, handed over, moved between slots or onto a socket, sliding, or no longer published within the freshness tuning's `physics.stale_steps` physics steps, which a hitch of the simulation tick does not count toward), when the hand starts or stops holding it by a part other than its grip, when its own body is back in the world (a drop or a mount since the input tick), when the gun is gone, when its joint went away, and when the player's collision group changes.
- Every path that stops a hand's body first destroys the gun hanging from it and ends the brace it holds (`guns::step::drop_hand`): the hand parked or held in place, its body forgotten or destroyed (the game leaving gameplay for a load or the menu, a new character after a death), and the shutdown cleanup.

The weapon itself is never touched by the gun body: if the game destroys the weapon before the next step finds it gone, the gun body is a body of the mod's own with the mod's own joints, destroyed at that step.

A heavy weapon the table carries is holstered when it is taken, which takes its own body out of the world as for any carried weapon ([two-handed-inventory.md, "Heavy weapons"](two-handed-inventory.md#heavy-weapons)), so in a hand it gets a gun body like any other gun, with the heavy class's mass cap, fallback mass, and recoil impulse. A heavy weapon the game wields itself keeps its own body in the world while it is wielded; the own-body check refuses it (`GunError::OwnBodyInWorld`), so it gets no gun body and keeps the kinematic weight and recoil.

## Grabbing a gun anywhere

A hand holding a gun on its own always holds it by its pistol grip, ready to shoot. Any other hold (the stock, the handguard, the barrel, the magazine, the action) is support for a gun the other hand holds by its grip. A second hand braces a long gun (the two-handed, special, and heavy slots) wherever it closes on it; a pistol keeps its grip. A brace needs the other hand free, so only the long gun in a hand whose other hand is free is braced ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The gun in either hand"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-gun-in-either-hand)). The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/regions/`, and its tuning is the hold tuning's `regions` and `regions.shape` (`crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/hold.toml`).

### The regions

Each gun's regions are worked out in its own frame, the frame of [`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1) (+Y up, -Z toward the muzzle, its origin the attach point in the palm of the hand on the pistol grip), from:

- the model's box: the first gun's model instance ([`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance)), whose model space is that frame ([`ModelInstance::GetModelSpaceBoundingBox`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-GetModelSpaceBoundingBox));
- the muzzle: the first gun's current fire point;
- the foregrip: the other hand's grip point ([`WeaponBase::GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition)), when the gun has one;
- the magazine well and the action's handle: the wells table's ([reload.md](reload.md)), when its entry is trusted.

A hand touches the gun when its grip point is within `regions.reach_m` of the box (`regions::classify`). It is then in the nearest of the parts with a point of their own, within each one's size: the pistol grip (within `regions.grip_radius_m` of the line from the attach point down the grip), the action (a sphere at its handle), and on a long gun the magazine (the length a magazine hangs below its well). Otherwise it is in a region along the bore: the stock behind the grip, the handguard (the receiver and on to past the foregrip, or most of the way to the muzzle without one), and the barrel out to the muzzle. The point the hand closes on, the anchor, is the part's own point (the attach point for the grip), or for the stock, the handguard, and the barrel, the point nearest the hand on the line the region runs along, never past the gun's ends.

### What each hand does

- The hand on the grip holds the gun and is the only hand that fires it.
- A second hand on any other part braces the gun there: the foregrip hold takes the anchor in place of the gun's own foregrip point, so the two-handed pose puts the hand where it closed on the gun, the gun body's brace joint joins it there, and the fingers take that part's pose. A brace ahead of the grip (the handguard, the barrel, the magazine, or the action) steers the gun as the foregrip does; a hand on the stock holds the gun without turning it. While the hand stays on the handguard or the barrel, the brace slides along them with it.
- With `controllers.weapons.hold.grip_to_hold` (on), the holding hand letting go while the other braces passes the gun to the bracing hand rather than dropping it, wherever that hand braced. The bracing hand slides onto the grip over `regions.grip_slide_ms` (`regions::grip_from_brace`): the gun glides through the hand with an eased start and stop and turns from the way the hand braced it onto the canonical grip, so it ends aligned to shoot, and from then that hand holds it at its grip and fires it. The rigid grip pins the sliding gun so that the point the slide has reached sits in the palm (`held::slide_offset`). The turn is measured on the hand-tracking wrist frame, and the pin moves it onto the wrist bone's own frame, which the skeleton orients differently on each side (`held::slide_on_bone`). The gun body is seated again once the slide has ended.

A lone hand that takes a free gun takes it by its grip, wherever on the gun it closed: a direct pickup ([physical-weapons.md, "Picking a weapon up into the hand"](physical-weapons.md#picking-a-weapon-up-into-the-hand)) slides the gun from where it lay into the palm by its grip; a long gun docked on a shoulder has a volume on whatever part a free hand touches, which draws it as the shoulder's holster does; and a handover, a gun taken out of a character's hands or off a rack, and a gun caught from the snatch ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md)) are all taken by the grip.

A trigger press on a gun the hand braces, or one the mod's fire path holds back (the controller untracked, a mode without firing, a dock's slide, a reload, a draw, or a holster playing, or the true reload holding the gun), gives the dead click of a dry fire (`DryFire`, [haptics.md](haptics.md)) and one log line naming the reason. That refusal (`weapons::finish::refused_fire`) is the one place that decides the click for a gun the mod fires; for a gun the true reload holds, the reason is what the reload checked ([reload.md](reload.md), `weapons::reload::fire_hold`).

The fingers take each region's pose ([grip-poses.md](grip-poses.md)): the pistol grip on the grip, the foregrip's wrap on the handguard, a wrap round a thin bar on the barrel and a thicker one on the stock, a cup under the magazine, and a pinch on the action.

The debug UI has the gun bodies under the Physical hands feature's diagnostics (the `Left gun body` and `Right gun body` status rows) and the regions under Weapons > Hold, transfer, and grab regions, with each hand's region and role in the Status page's `Weapons in hand` section (`Left hand on gun`, `Right hand on gun`).

## Unverified

- What masses the game gives its guns' own bodies while the weapon is held; the class fallbacks are guesses, and the readout's mass source says which applied.
- That the gun's own shape, given in the gun's model frame, lines up with the drawn gun in the headset.
- Whether the solver holds a rigid chain of three bodies with a fixed joint at each link (holding hand, gun, bracing hand) steady, and whether the gun jitters against the world.
- Whether the break distance is right: a smaller one lets go while the support hand steers the gun quickly, since the braced weight trails a quick swing at the handguard; a larger one leaves the drawn hand on the gun further from the controller.
- Whether the fire point's axis is the barrel for every gun (a gun whose fire point is turned kicks off its barrel).
- Whether the model's box fits each gun closely enough for its regions: a box that takes in a scope, a bipod, or an underbarrel launcher moves the handguard and the barrel's line.
