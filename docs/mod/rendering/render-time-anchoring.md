# Render-time anchoring

The simulation ticks at 30 Hz and the engine draws each object between its last two ticks' cached matrices, by the frame's interpolation fraction. The headset's view, the hands drawn by the late update (`payload/src/controllers/hands/late/`), and the upper body the presentation layer re-poses ([presentation-layer.md](presentation-layer.md)) are placed from the latest tracking at render time instead. Anything the mod places from a tick's data and draws relative to the player therefore has to be anchored to a render-time pose, or it is drawn where the player was up to a tick ago. At walking pace that is a few centimetres; in a car at 114 km/h it is about a metre a tick, and a falling or wingsuiting player outruns it as fast.

## The rule

Anything drawn relative to the player is anchored at render time:

- an overlay primitive placed in the cockpit frame, or from a point that rides with the player, is anchored to the head (`Anchor::Head`, an offset in world axes from the head's render-time position) or to a hand (`Anchor::Hand`, an offset in one of the hand's poses as it is drawn at render time, `late::drawn_hands`, which stays on the hand where the hand falls short of the controller). `Anchor::World` is for points that lie in the world: a grapple target, a gun's hit point, a pickup on the ground;
- a model the mod holds in a hand is re-pushed at render time from the hand's render-time pose or correction, after the engine's own render update has pushed its interpolated matrix;
- a model the mod places on the body keeps its interpolation: both of its cached matrices are written on the tick, so the engine draws it between them with the body it rides.

## Where it applies

Overlay markers and readouts:

- The grip targets' markers (`grab/markers.rs`, [grip.md](../input/grip.md#markers)): a marker in the cockpit frame (the body's sockets and pouches, the vehicle's controls and buttons, the parachute's handles) is anchored to the head; a marker on a held gun (its grip, foregrip, action, and well) to the holding hand's grip; and only a marker on what lies in the world (an object, a weapon, a door, a character, a surface to climb) is a world anchor.
- The grab view (`physical_hands/grab_view/`, [grab-view.md](../input/grab-view.md#anchoring)): its palm markers and the hands' ends of its lines are anchored to the hands, a target's end where the target's grip marker is, its reach rays to the head, and its door handles, candidates, and the physical hands' refusals to the world.
- The wingsuit guide is anchored to the hands.
- The physical hands' palm boxes, their press rings and shove lines, and the line from a held object's grab point to where the hand would have it (`physical_hands/draw.rs`) are anchored to the head.
- The body mirror's figure is anchored to the head. The grenade fuse ring, the wrist detonator, the second weapon's magazine pips, the ghost hands, the gizmos, and the debug panel's pointer are anchored to the hands. The gun's laser starts at the hand and ends at its hit, a world point.

Models in a hand:

- A held gun's model and its separate parts are re-pushed by the late update with the holding hand's correction ([`WeaponBase::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateRender) and the parts' render update, detoured).
- The grenade primed from the pouch, a live grenade a hand caught, and the C4 charge primed from the pouch are re-pushed at the grip of the hand as it is drawn (`hands/held.rs`), through post-call detours on [`Explosive::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdateRender) (the grenade's model, [`m_ModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_ModelInstance)) and [`PlantedExplosive::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-UpdateRender) (the charge's in-hand model, [`m_InactiveModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##field-m_InactiveModelInstance)). The throws still place each on the tick, which moves its physics and its effects; each registers what its hand holds every tick, clears the registration when it lets go or throws, and a registration not refreshed lapses after a little more than a tick. The caught grenade is carried to the hand by its physics velocity, which trails the hand further, so drawing it at the hand matters most there.

Models on the body:

- The weapons the VR inventory carries beyond the slots are placed on their sockets on the tick through the weapon's own placement, which writes both cached matrices. A weapon comes into the carry set with its render interpolation skipped, so that it does not streak from the slot's bone to its socket, and that skip lasts until [`WeaponBase::UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose) clears it, which only the inventory's slots receive. Left set, the placement would copy the new matrix into [`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) every tick, so a carried weapon would be drawn un-interpolated, a tick ahead of the body: the rifle pops at speed, and a pistol floats below a falling player. So the carry set ends each pass for its weapons as the game does for the slots' (`inventory::carry::end_pass`, from the hold's detour of [`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose)), clearing the guns' [`m_SkipRenderInterpolation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_SkipRenderInterpolation) and their model parts'.
- A slot's weapon on the body, the snap between a dock and the hand, and the slides of a draw and a holster are placed by the game's placement or the rigid grip's pin, so they interpolate with the body; the part of a snap in the hand rides the late update's correction.
- The pouches' models (a grenade, a C4 charge, and the held gun's magazine, [pouch-models.md](../input/pouch-models.md)) are instances the mod owns, with no cached matrices of their own. Each is placed on the tick in the character's model space, and pushed once per rendered frame as that placement between the last two ticks', carried by the character's world transform between its last two ticks', at the frame's interpolation fraction, so it interpolates with the body it rides.

The headset checklist: drive at speed and fall with a rifle and a pistol carried on the body and watch them stay on their sockets; hold a primed grenade, a caught grenade, and a C4 charge while moving the hand quickly and while driving, and check that each stays in the hand; and drive with the vehicle controls' markers, the reload well's, and the pouches' showing, and check that they stay on the wheel, the gun, and the body.
