# The procedural torso outside standing play

Outside standing play the IK body stands down and the animation carries the hips and legs: the seat's pose, the swim, the parachute harness, and the wingsuit's flight pose are authored, and they are what the vehicle, the water, and the air move. The procedural torso ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md)) still poses the spine, neck, and clavicles in most of these states, as its [hand-over table](procedural-body.md#the-hand-over-table) says. The code is in `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/body/procedural/`.

The torso is solved as on foot ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md#the-torso-on-the-animations-hips)) with two differences. The clip's forward lean is kept, so a bike's lean into a turn and a driver's reach stay and the player's motion adds to them. And the rig has a chest heading only while Rico is grounded ([rig.md](rig.md#the-yaw)), so the chest keeps the clip's facing. The clavicles and the wrist targets work as on foot ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md#the-arms)), but the elbow hints, which point down and back from the chest, are queued only while the written chest is near upright. The view's anchor here is the animated head captured before the gate writes the pose ([head-and-body.md](head-and-body.md)), so the solve reaches for the clip's head moved by the player's tracked motion, with no feedback from the written spine.

Each state has a status row in the debug UI's Body fit live status with the torso's weight and the neck's distance from its target.

## Seated in a vehicle

Seated and riding ([`Character::IsInRidingInVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInRidingInVehicleState), as the hand tiers classify it), the torso is procedural on the seat's hips and the legs keep the seat's clip, which holds the feet on the pedals and moves them with the vehicle. Entries, exits, seat switches, and mounted guns are the vehicle's animation, and the torso blends between the two. The arms are on the wheel, the bars, or a held control ([steering-wheel.md](../input/steering-wheel.md)). The driving view's correction moves the head the solve reaches for as it moves the view ([head-and-body.md](head-and-body.md)). The row is `Seated torso`.

## Standing on a roof

Standing in a vehicle's stunt area ([`Character::IsInStuntArea`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInStuntArea); [stunt-positions.md](../input/stunt-positions.md)) is the case `standing on a vehicle`. The graph has no standing stunt state, so the animation is the on-foot one, carried by the vehicle's interaction node. The case is separate from on foot because the view hangs from the clip's head, not the root. The animation set's `STUNT_COMBAT_MODIFIER` and `STUNT_NONCOMBAT_MODIFIER` would select stunt clip variants inside the on-foot states ([animation-graph.md, "The on-foot idle"](../../engine/character/animation-graph.md#the-on-foot-idle)), so `hooks::character::modifiers` drops them for the local player there; the engine sets frame-bound modifiers again every tick, so nothing is left behind. A stable hang from a helicopter ([`Character::IsStuntHanging`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsStuntHanging)) is its own case with the torso procedural on the animation's hips. The row is `Roof torso`.

## The firm ragdoll

While the grapple lock holds the line and under the canopy, Rico's hips and legs hang from the physics (`controllers::body::firm_ragdoll`), because the animated legs ignore the swing and hang stiff under the canopy. The game's ragdoll presets that keep the upper body keyframed and drive the hips and legs toward the animated pose with constraint motors do this without the character ragdolling ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md)). On the line the mode writes the grapple hang's preset id with its sync flag, as the game's canopy update writes its own; under the canopy it keeps the canopy's. Each tick it writes the ragdoll's blend weight just before the post-physics pass blends the simulated pose in, ramping over `firm_ragdoll.blend_in_s` and `blend_out_s`, and once out it restores the preset it found. The blend lands before the HumanIK solve, so the head, hands, and chest stay the mod's.

The ragdoll's pre-physics step takes its muscle target from the animated pose, so a detour on it hands the motors a target composed from the drawn body instead ([ik-body.md](ik-body.md#the-firm-ragdolls-muscle-target)). Whether the motors hold the hips close enough for the holsters is unverified.

In `grapple line locked` without the firm ragdoll, the gate eases each leg bone toward the reference pose and hangs the thighs on a damped pendulum from the root (`leg_swing`).

## The wingsuit

In the wingsuit and the skydive ([`SB_IS_FREE_FALLING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_FREE_FALLING), which the game presents belly down like the suit) the torso keeps the flight pose, but Rico's arms follow the player's. The hand tier stays yielded ([hands-and-aiming.md](../input/hands-and-aiming.md#hand-tiers)), because the player stands upright while Rico flies prone, so each arm is carried over as the arm steering reads it ([flight.md](../input/flight.md#wingsuit-arm-steering)) in a frame that keeps the vertical and turns with the heading (`solver::wings`):

- The player's wrist is measured from the player's estimated shoulder in the frame of the player's heading. In flight that heading comes from the arms (`procedural::wings::WingHeading`): the line from the left wrist to the right when they are spread, otherwise a follower of the head that ignores glances (`wings.follow`). The same follower gives the torso heading to every airborne shoulder estimate (the hands, the arm remap, and the ripcord; [rig.md](rig.md#the-yaw)), so a glance turns none of them.
- That reach is scaled from the player's T-pose arm (`BodyDimensions::shoulder_to_wrist`) to Rico's ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md#the-rig)) and placed from Rico's animated shoulder in the frame of his flight heading (the hips-to-neck line laid flat, or the root's forward in a straight dive). The wrist orientation turns by the same turn between headings.

So a spread is a spread, a hand lowered for a bank goes toward the ground, and the wrist tilt that pitches the suit turns Rico's wrists. The targets are queued once the gate lets go of its state (`hands::ik::queue_model_wrists`), with no pull, at a weight that eases in with both controllers read and holds the last targets while easing out. Arms held far from the wing pose fold or stretch the skinned membrane. The row is `Wingsuit arms`.

## The parachute

Under the parachute the torso is procedural on the harness's hips and the legs keep the harness clip. The hand tier is free, so a hand follows its controller or holds the handle it took ([hands-and-aiming.md](../input/hands-and-aiming.md#hand-tiers)). The canopy is a separate model; whether its lines ride the harness bones the solve moves is not established, so a big lean may part them a little. The row is `Parachute body`.

## The grapple's wall attach

A grapple that reels Rico to a wall can leave him slumped against it in the body rule state `S_GRAPPLE_ATTACH_0`. None of the grapple predicates report it ([`Character::IsGrappleHanging`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsGrappleHanging), [`Character::IsStuntTraversing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsStuntTraversing)), so the gate reads the state by name (`procedural::state::grapple_attached`) and poses the torso on the clip's reclining hips, the twist and look-down shrinking with the spine's tilt as in the water. The view hangs from the animated head with the face clearance ([locomotion.md](locomotion.md#the-steady-view)).

## Swimming

Swimming, the torso and the arms are procedural and the legs keep the swim's kick. The swim lies Rico nearly flat, so the chest's twist and look-down pitch fade out as the spine lies down (a heading is a turn about the vertical, which a flat spine has no part in), while the spine still swings toward the head and the neck takes its share of the turn. The swimming hand tier is free (`HandTierTable::PLAN`), so a stroke the player mimes is the stroke Rico's arms make. The movement is in [swimming.md](swimming.md). The row is `Swim torso`.

## The body mirror

In the headset Rico's body is mostly out of sight, so the body mirror (`controllers.overlay.body_mirror`, off by default; the `Body mirror` switch in the debug UI's visualization drawer) draws his pose for checking alignment (`controllers::body::mirror`). It draws a stick figure rather than rendering the scene again, since a second camera would double the scene's cost. At the end of the pose-finalization pass, after the head override and the twist, it reads the figure's bones from the finalized pose, reflects them across a plane `overlay.body_mirror_distance_m` ahead of Rico's facing, and draws them through the world-space overlay ([hands-and-aiming.md](../input/hands-and-aiming.md#the-world-space-overlay)) with rings at the reflected head and wrist targets. A ring away from its bone is a visible misalignment. The primitives are anchored to the head so the figure keeps up at speed.
