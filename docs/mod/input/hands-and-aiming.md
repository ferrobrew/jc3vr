# Hands and aiming

The controller-driven hands and what aims by them: the aim-state pin that frees the body from the game's aim switch, the wrist targets and the rigid grip, the hand tiers, the arm reach, the render-time correction, the finger curls, the gun and grapple aim, the sight lines, the world-space overlay, and the scopes. Everything here acts only under the controller scheme ([controllers.md, "Scheme selection"](controllers.md#scheme-selection)). The code is in `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/` (`aim_state.rs`, `hands/`, `fingers/`, `aim/`, `overlay/`), the scopes in `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/scope/`, and the render passes in `crates/jc3vr-render/src/controllers/`.

## The aim-state pin and the mental-state cuts

The aim state ([aim-state.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-state.md)) selects between the no-aim and aim families of full-body states, switches locomotion between run-and-steer and aim-relative strafing, runs the arm aim IK, and changes jump and hang facing. The mod pins it clear for the local player rather than set, for three reasons: the player's mental state goes to combat whenever weapon aim is set, so a set pin would keep the player permanently in combat (changing how AI and civilians react, and keeping the relaxed walk from playing); the aim family brings the arm aim IK, the head look-at switch-off, a spine blend toward the aim point, and aim-specific jump facing, each of which would need cutting; and with the pin clear, firing still moves the mental state into combat, so firing starts combat and walking does not. The cost is the laser-sight component's beam, which draws only while weapon aim is set. The weapons' aim timer is left alone, because NPC weapons share it.

- The weapon pin: [`StateBitFlag::SB_NO_WEAPON_AIM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_NO_WEAPON_AIM) is set for the duration of the player's aim-input function, the only code that reads it, and restored afterwards. [`AimState::m_AimingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AimState##flag-m_AimingWeapon) then stays clear. The trigger still fires, because fire gating reads neither.
- The grapple pin: [`AimState::m_AimingGrapple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AimState##flag-m_AimingGrapple), which the weapon pin does not reach, is cleared after the grapple's pre-simulation update, so on a held tether the body does not turn to face the hook.
- The arm aim IK skip: the player's `NAimIKTask`, `NRightArmAimIK`, `NGrapplingFireArmIK`, and `NReelInAimIK` updates return early while controller wrist IK owns the hands they move (a fresh focused controller frame, usable grip poses, and positive tier weights; the right-arm task needs the right hand, the grapple-fire task the left, the shared tasks both).
- The neutral aim point: the body's aim point on the control-parameters blackboard ([`ControlParameters::m_AimTargetWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::ControlParameters##field-m_AimTargetWeapons)) is held the aim tuning's `neutral.distance_m` ahead of the head along the body's forward, so no residual aim blend turns the body or twists the spine. The character-level point follows the gun aim (see "Gun aim").
- The wall-block clear: after [`WallPenetrationWeaponComponent::UpdateWeapon_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WallPenetrationWeaponComponent##func-UpdateWeapon_Serial), the block it computes from where the animation would put the barrel is cleared on the player's held weapon, because the rendered muzzle is at the controller.
- The mental-state cuts: the mental state keeps running, because AI perception reads it, but its effects on the player's body go. [`StateBitFlag::SB_DISABLE_MENTAL_STATE_DRAW_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_MENTAL_STATE_DRAW_WEAPON) is set before each of the player's [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling) calls, because the inventory clears it every tick, which stops the automatic draw. The game's two automatic holsters are dropped through the act arbiter (`controllers::acts`), and a detour on [`Character::HolsterWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HolsterWeapon) marks the player's deliberate holster, which takes effect through the same relaxed branch, so it passes.

The locomotion tasks read the same flags to choose run-and-steer or strafe; the mod leaves them and turns and moves the body through the orientation and displacement evaluators instead ([input.md, "Tapping the move input"](input.md#tapping-the-move-input)).

## The wrist targets

The engine side is [humanik.md](../../engine/character/humanik.md) and [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../../engine/character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md). Each tracked hand's wrist target is composed, in pure functions, from the grip pose in the cockpit frame; times the grip offset (`controllers.hands.grip`, in the right hand's grip frame and mirrored for the left), giving the wrist frame (origin at the wrist joint, -Z toward the fingers, +Y out of the back of the hand); then the body remap about the shoulders (see "Arm reach"); then into the world through the head's transform, the composition the head pose uses, so the hands and the view agree.

Each wrist is queued on HumanIK's `MAIN` pass at the entry of [`Character::UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel), the seam the head and body IK use. The rotation target is the delta from the animated wrist frame, built from the animated knuckles, to the target, so it does not depend on how the hand bone is oriented at rest. Both targets go at the `UPPER_BODY` step with the hands tuning's `ik.wrist_reach_t` and `ik.wrist_reach_r` scaled by the hand's tier weight, re-queued every tick. The wielded weapon rides the hand's attach bone, and the engine refreshes the weapon's cached transform from it after the `MAIN` solve, so the gun follows the wrist within the tick. While the procedural torso poses the torso, the targets are queued at the HumanIK solve gate instead, with elbow hints ([procedural-body.md, "The arms"](../body/procedural-body.md#the-arms)).

### The rigid grip

The attach bone is animated, so the clips playing decide how the gun sits in the hand ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md, "The gun's pose in the hand is animated"](../../engine/character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-guns-pose-in-the-hand-is-animated)). With the wrist on a controller that reads as the gun turning over in a still hand. So a pre-call step in [`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms) writes the attach bone of each gun of the wielded weapon, for each hand with a controller weight above zero, to the hand's wrist joint composed with the hand's canonical grip (its primary attach bone relative to its wrist in the bind pose, [`AnimationController::GetBindPoseBoneMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetBindPoseBoneMatrix)), blended from the animated pose by that weight (`controllers::hands::grip`). The placement runs after the state tasks and after both IK solves, so the last run places the gun on the solved wrist, and everything that reads the gun's cached transform (the muzzle effects, the model parts, the physics proxy, the shot origin, the sight line) follows it. Both of a hand's attach points take the same grip, so a task moving the gun between them does not turn it. A gun on a holster or back bone, and a hand the controllers do not drive, are left to the game. The right hand's canonical grip holds a gun backwards, so the right hand can take the left's grip mirrored ([physical-weapons.md, "The right hand's grip"](physical-weapons.md#the-right-hands-grip)).

The gun-in-hand watch (`controllers::weapons::grip_watch`) checks the result: each input tick it compares the gun's rotation on the solved wrist, and relative to the wrist's target, with the previous tick's (the handling tuning's `grip_watch.flip_deg` is a snap) and with the rotation it had when the hand took it (`grip_watch.sweep_deg`, held, is a sweep), and warns with the bone, weapon state, animated deviation, solve miss, tier, and animation state, at most once a second per hand. With the grip pinned it should stay quiet; Weapons > Hold, transfer, and grab regions shows its readings.

### The secondary hand IK

The game's secondary hand IK ([`Character::UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass)) runs after the `MAIN` solve and can move the wrists again. It is driven by blackboard weight keys that animation tasks write (the grapple fire arm IK and the parachute handle IK per wrist, the weapon's off-hand grip), some inside the pose-finalization pass ([animation-layers.md](../../engine/character/animation-layers.md#state-tasks-inside-the-pose-finalization-pass)), so the switch acts at the `SECONDARY` pass's own entry: a present, positive key is zeroed for each controller-driven hand, and a key the game did not write is never created. The same entry zeroes the driven wrist's translation reach, because a `SECONDARY` target queued before the hand was driven would outlive its key: the pass removes an old entry only once its reach is zero, and the `MAIN` target keeps the reach up ([humanik.md](../../engine/character/humanik.md#target-entry-lifetime-and-the-is_valid-flag)). While the off-hand grip still runs for the other hand, the driven wrist gets a weak `SECONDARY` target at its `MAIN` target (the hands tuning's `ik.secondary_hold_reach`), or the grip's solve pulls the body and the released wrist rides off the controller. The weapon-agnostic body also switches the off-hand grip off for a hand the controllers do not drive ([unarmed-body.md](../body/unarmed-body.md)).

## Hand tiers

Each scripted state that wants the hands takes one of four tiers (`controllers::hands::tiers`, fixed at `HandTierTable::PLAN`), classified once per input tick from the mode detector's snapshot and a few state predicates:

- Free: the hand follows its controller.
- Action runs: the game plays the action (a reload's timer and the weapon model's magazine and bolt animation, a draw, a holster) while the hand stays on its controller; the fire inputs are held back meanwhile ([weapon-handling.md, "The action-runs tier"](weapon-handling.md#the-action-runs-tier)).
- Anchored: the hand is attached to a world point and its movement drives the game. Built for the grapple and the mounted guns.
- Yielded: the animation owns the hand, blending out over the hands tuning's `tiers.yield_blend_s` and back over `tiers.return_blend_s`. A state assigned the anchored tier where it is not built yields.

| State | Tier |
|---|---|
| On foot, swimming, a vehicle seat, the parachute | Free (the parachute's handles and the physical vehicle controls anchor a holding hand within it, because a hand holds them only while the player grips) |
| A reload, a draw, a holster | Action runs |
| Grapple reel and hang (`GHS_REELING_IN`, `GHS_REELED_*`) | Anchored |
| A mounted gun or gunner seat (classified before the vehicle states, since a gunner seat would read as an entry) | Anchored: the hands on the handles aim it ([vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md#mounted-guns)) |
| Ledge grabs, climbs, vaults, the stunt step-up and step-down, hanging from a helicopter ([stunt-positions.md](stunt-positions.md)) | Yielded |
| Vehicle entry, exit, and hijack | Yielded |
| Wingsuit | Yielded: the membrane is skinned between the arms and the body; the arms still steer ([flight.md](flight.md)) and the procedural torso carries them onto the flight pose ([procedural-states.md](../body/procedural-states.md#the-wingsuit)) |
| Interactions, melee, ragdoll, knockdown, death, cutscenes | Yielded |

In the anchored grapple states the grapple hand keeps its controller position and turns its pointing axis to the active wire's attach point ([`GrapplingHook::GetActiveWireAttachmentGraphicsWorldPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetActiveWireAttachmentGraphicsWorldPosition)), and while hanging its horizontal displacement pushes the hang ([physical-grapple.md, "The hang swing"](physical-grapple.md#the-hang-swing)). Only the drawn hand turns: the aim pose stays on the controller, so a re-fire from the reel goes where the controller points. A grapple hand that holds anything (a gun, a prop, a grenade, a charge) stays free, so what it holds stays where the controller puts it.

## Arm reach

A fully extended player arm must ask for a fully extended character arm about the joint HumanIK actually reaches from. Tuned by the hands tuning's `reach` section:

- The measurement (`controllers/hands/rig.rs`): at the pre-solve seam the hands measure Rico's upper arm and forearm from the bones HumanIK drives for the shoulder, elbow, and wrist effectors, found through the solver's node-and-bone pairs. HumanIK's shoulder node is the upper-arm joint, not the collar ([humanik.md, "Effector-id model"](../../engine/character/humanik.md#effector-id-model)), so the chain measured is the one the wrist is solved along. The lengths settle and then hold, since the rig carries no scale.
- The remap: the wrist's offset from the player's estimated shoulder, scaled by Rico's measured shoulder-to-wrist length over the player's reach (`BodyDimensions::arm_reach`, the segment tables' length for the height the arm span stands for: a straight arm forward or hanging down, the commonest poses), placed about Rico's shoulder. Both shoulders hang from the level eye point under the camera, turned by the torso's heading. The player's sit at their body dimensions' generic proportions (`body::shoulder_positions`); Rico's sit where his skeleton's reference pose holds them from his eyes (`body::CHARACTER_EYES_TO_RIGHT_SHOULDER`), and never follow the animation, since a run swings the animated shoulders forward and back each stride and a remap from them pumped a rifle in and out of the hands. Without a measurement the ratio is the arm spans' (`ArmRemap::estimated`), applied only with `controllers.body.remap_hands`. On the ground the player's shoulder moves to the body rig's estimate of it and Rico's by the same move scaled, so a crouch or a lean carries the hands. The wingsuit's arms keep the T-pose length, since they spread as the span was measured.
- The reach limit (`hands::reach::ReachLimit`) holds each remapped wrist within Rico's measured arm of the remap's shoulder, moving a wrist past it onto the sphere along the line from the shoulder. It sits in the composition every hand subsystem starts from (`hands::active_hands_raw`), so the hand body, the wrist target, the grab volumes, the holds, and the guns all start from a hand the arm can reach, and a controller held past reach leaves the hand at full extension along the line to it. Only the crouched floor reach reads the controller before the limit (`hands::controller_grips`).
- The pull: each wrist's position target carries a pull that ramps by a smoothstep from `pull_start` of Rico's arm length to `pull_max` at `pull_full`, over a floor of `ik.wrist_pull`, so the shoulder and chest follow a reach the arm alone cannot make; the body IK's shoulder pin yields by the same ramp. On the procedural torso the pull is zero, because the torso is the player's.
- The detach: with the reach limited, a solve shortfall comes only from a body that moved within a tick or a solve that did not converge. At render time the correction takes its reference from the target moved back along the shortfall beyond `detach_threshold_m`, up to `detach_cap_m`, so the rendered hand and gun leave the arm toward the controller by up to the cap, and the forearm stretches along its axis to stay attached (`max_forearm_stretch`). The shot origin, the muzzle flash, and the sight line stay on the sim-rate transform.

Body/tracking > Hand presentation shows the measured arms, the ratio, the extension, the pull, the shortfall, and the detach.

## The late update

The sim places the hands at the sim tick, and the engine's interpolation adds one to two ticks of lag, so the rendered hands and weapon are corrected at render time. Once per rendered frame, after the VR pose is published, the late update takes the latest controller poses with the render-time head pose pair and the targets the last two ticks' IK was asked to reach; each hand's correction is the latest pose interpolated between the head pair's sides by the engine's interpolation fraction, times the inverse of the two ticks' targets interpolated the same way (the grip offset cancels). The correction limit checks the physical wrist displacement and the shortest relative rotation, and a partial weight interpolates the reference wrist toward the latest before deriving the correction, so a turning wrist does not swing its pivot about the world origin.

A post-call detour on [`WeaponBase::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateRender) re-pushes the model matrix of a gun the player holds in a hand attach bone as the correction times the engine's interpolated matrix, and the character's palette edit moves the hand and finger bones by the same correction, so the gun and the hand never separate. A gun's separate model parts (a magazine, a slide, a minigun's barrel) are components with their own render update ([`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-UpdateRender)), re-pushed by the correction of the hand they ride (`controllers::hands::parts`); a part an animation task put on a holder's bone (a magazine carried during a reload) is recorded at its placement and rides that hand. The muzzle flash, the attached effects, the shot origin, and the grapple wire's near end stay on the sim-rate transform. The presentation layer re-solves the arms from the shoulders to the corrected hands every frame ([presentation-layer.md](../rendering/presentation-layer.md)).

## Finger curls

Each finger of a driven hand gets a curl from 0 (straight) to 1 (a fist), and each finger but the thumb a splay (`crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/fingers/`, tuned by `crates/jc3vr-body/tuning/fingers.toml`). A hand is curled only while its wrist target was queued this tick.

- From the hand joints, when the orientations they need are valid: every `XR_EXT_hand_tracking` joint has -Z along its bone and +Y out of the back of the hand on both hands, so a joint's flexion is its rotation relative to its parent about the parent's -X, and a knuckle's splay the rotation about +Y. A curl is the sum of a finger's flexions over their sum at a full fist (`tracked`).
- Otherwise, estimated from the inputs the fingers rest on (`estimate`): the thumb bends onto the face when it touches the stick, the trackpad, or a face button, further while it presses; the index finger rests on the trigger and closes with the pull; the other three follow the grip value on the Index (its force carrying them to a fist) and the grip's pull on a Touch. The estimate has no splay.

The curls follow their source with a time constant (`curls.smoothing_s`). The pose is absolute: each joint's bend is measured from a straight finger and replaces the animated bend, because the game's free-hand animation already holds the fingers curled and a bend added on top pushed a full curl into the palm. The straight finger is built from the animated palm (a finger's open direction runs from the wrist to its knuckle, and the open thumb lies in the palm's plane, `curls.thumb_spread_deg` from the fingers), since the defs bind no reference pose. A joint bends by its finger's range (`curls.curl_ranges` of `curls.finger_bend_deg` or `curls.thumb_bend_deg`) plus `curls.rest_offset_deg`, toward the palm about the axis square to the open direction and the palm's normal, and splays about the axis square to the open direction and the thumb side. Each bone turns by the shortest arc from its animated direction, so its animated roll is kept, and the tier weight is the share of that arc taken. Every axis comes from the hand's geometry, so the left hand mirrors the right with no per-side sign.

The fingers are written once per sim tick at the end of the pose-finalization pass, in the post-call detour on [`Character::UpdatePropEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePropEffects), with [`AnimationController::SetJoint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-SetJoint) over the finalized pose ([skeleton.md](../../engine/character/skeleton.md)), on Rico's `{Side}Hand{Thumb,Index,Middle,Ring,Pinky}1-3` bones found by name, each finger read before any is written and written root outward. The sim rate is enough because the late update moves the finger bones with the corrected hand. A hand that holds something takes that thing's grip pose instead, blended by how firmly it grips ([grip-poses.md](grip-poses.md)); a pose adds a hook (the bend moved between the knuckle and the outer joints) and the thumb's opposition, which measured curls lack.

## Gun aim

The engine side is [aim-pipeline.md](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md). Everything runs from the player aim control's post-simulation update ([`PlayerAimControl::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdatePostSim)), where the game casts its own aim ray: before it, the hands are composed from the latest snapshot and the grapple ray is handed to the grapple's hooks; after it, the hand rays are cast and the guns' aim points published for the per-weapon update, which runs right after on the same thread. The hands are composed and cast whenever the scheme is in effect, because the grenade throw and the melee target follow them too. The casts use the engine's closest-hit query ([`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay)) with the player aim's own filter ([`PlayerAimRaycastFilter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastFilter)), which rejects every physics instance of the character, its ragdoll and hit proxies, and held weapons.

Each gun of the wielded weapon aims along its own sight line: the gun's transform carried to the controller by the hand's late-update correction, composed with the gun's current fire point (whose -Z is the barrel) and the weapon's sight correction (see "Sight lines"). The line is cast up to the aim tuning's `ray.length_m`, and its hit, or a far point, becomes that hand's aim point, never nearer than `ray.min_target_distance_m`, because the game fires along the barrel for an aim point near the muzzle. The per-weapon base update ([`WeaponBase::UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase)) is handed that point for the gun that can fire in that call ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)); it is the only seam that gives two guns two directions, since the character-level point fans out to every held weapon. The character-level point still follows the dominant hand's point every frame, because without the game's setter the frame sets no point and the game aims every weapon a fixed distance ahead of the body ([aim-pipeline.md, "From aim control to the character"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#from-aim-control-to-the-character-cplayerupdatepostcamera)). A long gun raised to the eye is lined up with the eye ([long-gun-poses.md, "Aiming down the sights"](long-gun-poses.md#aiming-down-the-sights)). Out of the driver's seat the gun aim also drives the seated body's aim ([vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md)).

The per-slot game objects are weak pointers: moving one out and back is a raw copy with the source zeroed, leaving its weak count unchanged, and clearing one goes through the engine's assignment, which releases the reference.

Per-hand aim assist (`controllers.aim.assist_strength`, zero by default) scores, for each gun hand, the target sets the aim control's last pass included with the game's own weapon fitness ([`PlayerAimControl::GetFitnessScore`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetFitnessScore)), with the camera getters returning the hand's sight line so the fitness cone is the hand's. The best live hostile target ahead of the hand, within reach and with a clear line to within `assist.body_sight_tolerance_m` of its body, turns the hand's aim point toward it by the strength. The game's own aim assist stays on the gaze.

## Grapple aim

The engine side is [grapple-pipeline.md](../../engine/gameplay/grapple-pipeline.md). The grapple slot ([`TargetType::TARGET_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_GRAPPLE)) of the aim control follows the off-hand, from a ray starting at the aim tuning's `ray.grapple_origin_m` in the off-hand's wrist frame. Every part of the aim control that fills the grapple slot takes its ray from [`PlayerAimControl::GetAdjustedCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetAdjustedCameraMatrix) or [`GameCameraManager::GetCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetCameraMatrix); while a thread-local override is set, both return a matrix built from the grapple ray, so the engine fills the slot with its own filter, range, holey-geometry handling, and target resolution:

- [`PlayerAimControl::UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) runs twice: under the override, from which only the grapple slot is kept, and without it, which refills every other slot from the camera.
- The grapple target scoring ([`PlayerAimControl::SetTargetGrappleFitness`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-SetTargetGrappleFitness)) and the last-valid cache's re-validation ([`PlayerAimControl::IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-IsLastValidGrappleAimPosStillValid)) run under the override, so attachment and tethering pick their target the game's way, around the hand's ray.
- The visibility check ([`PlayerAimControl::IsTargetVisibleCast`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-IsTargetVisibleCast)) does not go through the engine, which keeps one scheduled raycast per target and answers every target type from it, so a request from the hand would leak into the camera-based types. The grapple's check is a synchronous cast from the hand ray's origin to the target's body, ignoring the player and the player's vehicle.

The alternatives fail: casting a ray in a post-hook and writing the slots by hand would reproduce the game's targeting; overwriting only the latched target at fire time leaves every reader of the live slot on the gaze; and telling the getter's callers apart does not work, because one raycast inside `UpdateDirectAim` serves the weapon and grapple slots together.

The grapple's aim assist (`controllers::aim::assist::grapple_assist`) turns the grapple ray before the aim update, so the targeting above runs along the turned ray. It acts only when the hand's own ray hits no object, which keeps the game's own target. It walks the target system's characters, vehicles, and objects within the aim tuning's `assist.grapple_cone_deg` of the ray and `assist.max_distance_m`, asks the game's grapple scoring whether each is attachable, in range, and not what the wire holds, and takes the one nearest the ray in angle with a clear line from the gauntlet. A grapplable surface is not searched, because the last-valid cache already holds a surface point while the ray grazes off it.

The grapple readout (`controllers::aim::GrappleAim`) is the slot after the game's targeting only while the game takes a grapple fire ([`GrapplingHook::GetFireInputEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetFireInputEnabled)); otherwise the slot is stale (in a seat it would hold the boarding point for the whole ride) and is reported as not current. In a vehicle seat the aim casts the off-hand's ray itself ([physical-grapple.md](physical-grapple.md#the-grapple-in-a-vehicle-seat)). The grapple's button, the yank, and the line's pull are [physical-grapple.md](physical-grapple.md).

## Sight lines

A weapon's sight correction moves the sight line's origin from the fire point and turns it, in the fire point's frame (`crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/aim/sights/`). It is looked up by the weapon's name hash: the settings' per-weapon entry (`controllers.aim.sights`, up to `SightTable::CAPACITY` entries), then the embedded table, then none, which leaves the line on the fire point.

The embedded table is `tools/sights/sights.json`, derived offline from the weapon models above their fire points ([`tools/sights/README.md`](../../../tools/sights/README.md); [weapon-data.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-data.md) has the files it reads) and compiled in. Scoped sights and pistols derive reliably; iron sights with rails, muzzle devices, or carry handles do not. Only the `scoped`, `pistol`, and `tuned` grades are used; `iron_uncertain` and `failed` leave the weapon on its fire point.

The sight tuning tool (`controllers.aim.sight_tuning.enabled`, off) under Weapons > Aim, sights, and scopes is the only editor of the settings' table. "Line up" places a marker the aim tuning's `sight_tool.marker_distance_m` ahead of the head, which the overlay draws with a dot where the dominant hand's sight line passes closest. The player lines the sights up on it and captures, with the button or, on foot, by holding both grips and pressing the dominant A (while the tool is armed, the dominant grip withholds the off-hand's grapple and both grips withhold the jump). The next aim update solves the offset that makes the sight line, at the weapon's current angles, pass through the marker, and refuses when no gun is in the dominant hand, the marker is not ahead of the muzzle, or the origin would be more than `sight_tool.max_offset_m` from it. Drag values adjust the entry finely, and "Copy as JSON" gives a `tuned` line for `tools/sights/sights.json`, whose extractor keeps tuned entries.

## The world-space overlay

The overlay draws coloured, alpha-blended sprites and thick lines into the scene per eye, for readouts that need their own depths, which the floating HUD panel cannot give. It draws at the end of [`RenderEngine::PostDraw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-PostDraw), on each eye's final back buffer after the post chain and the upscaler and before the HUD panel, so the readouts keep their colours through exposure changes and do not smear under temporal reconstruction. It works with and without single-pass stereo and acts only under the controller scheme.

Each producer owns a retained slot that its submission replaces wholesale, and the renderer draws a slot until its time to live runs out, so a producer that stops submitting disappears on its own. The store's lock is a leaf, so any thread may submit. Out of gameplay only the debug panel's pointer is drawn. A position is in world space, anchored to a hand, or offset from the head; an anchored one is placed from the render-time pose, so a hand-attached end follows the controller at frame rate whatever rate its producer runs at. A point measured once a sim tick can be placed between its last two ticks' positions by the engine's sub-frame fraction (`Anchor::Ticked`), so from a moving vehicle it rides with the interpolated view instead of jumping each tick.

The producers: the grapple highlight (a ring on a valid, in-range hook point, with a centre dot for a tetherable target and an outer ring when the grapple assist turned the ray onto it), the body rig's pivots and rings (`controllers.overlay.body_rig`, on; [rig.md](../body/rig.md#settings-and-diagnostics)), the body mirror (`body_mirror`, off; [procedural-states.md](../body/procedural-states.md#the-body-mirror)), the body estimate (`body_estimate`, off), the docked guns (`docked_guns`, on), the sight tool's marker, the grip targets' markers (`markers`, on; [grip.md, "Markers"](grip.md#markers)), and the debug panel's controller pointer ([hud.md](../hud.md)).

The frame's primitives are resolved against the cyclopean eye, so both eyes draw identical geometry and the pair fuses, made relative to the frame's origin, and sorted far to near, because the overlay writes no depth. Each eye projects them through a translation-free view-projection, because at the game's world coordinates an absolute `f32` world-to-clip transform jitters. The renderer uploads them once and issues one instanced draw per eye (`crates/jc3vr-render/shaders/overlay_vs.hlsl` and `overlay_ps.hlsl`); no depth target is bound, and the pixel shader tests against the main depth texture with the controls tuning's `overlay.depth_tolerance_m` or `overlay.depth_tolerance_rel` of the view distance. The draw restores the pipeline state it changes, except the viewport, which every mod draw at this seam sets.

### The game's reticles

The game's weapon reticle sits at the screen's centre and its grapple reticle at the gaze's grapple aim ([aim-pipeline.md, "The reticles"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#the-reticles)), so under hand aim both mark a point nothing aims at. `hud/game_reticle.rs` hides them by the detected mode: the grapple reticle everywhere under the controller scheme, since the overlay's highlight marks the grapple's target; the weapon reticle on foot, swimming, falling, in the wingsuit, and under the parachute whether armed or not (so it does not show between hand-aim results), in the driver's seat, at a mounted gun while its point comes from the hands, and in a passenger seat or under a menu while the aim's latest results, at most a quarter second old, aimed the weapon by hand. The hit marker plays at the weapon reticle, so it hides with it. The groups are held invisible at the HUD's capture seam through the clip handles of the full-screen overlay suppression (`hud/scaleform.rs`), which absorb the movie rewriting their visibility. Hiding them loses the target tint, the out-of-range dimming, the spread, and the attached-wire marker; the highlight covers only the in-range cue. Where the reticles show, the HUD atlas draws them at the aim point ([ui-decoupling.md](../rendering/ui-decoupling.md#the-atlas-as-built)).

## Scopes

The engine side is [aim-pipeline.md, "Precision aim and sniper zoom"](../../engine/gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#precision-aim-and-sniper-zoom). Precision aim adds a camera zoom hint, and on a sniper rifle shows the 2D sniper overlay and hides the character, which hides the hands. Every entry into it goes through [`Player::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-SetAimMode) (the precision-aim input, the sniper's return after a bolt cycle, the guided launcher's shot), so a detour there refuses it under the controller scheme, and a post-call detour on [`Player::UpdateAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateAimMode) leaves a precision aim entered before the scheme took over.

A scoped weapon (the sniper class of [`WeaponBase::m_Class`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Class), in either hand) shows a magnified image in a lens on the scope instead. There is one lens: with a scoped gun in each hand, the one raised to the eye, or else the one whose eyepiece is nearest an eye. The sight line is kept in the holding wrist's frame and placed from the render-time wrist, so the lens follows the controller at frame rate. The eyepiece is on the sight line the handling tuning's `scope.lens.eyepiece_back_m` behind its origin; a weapon with no embedded sight entry has its line along the barrel, below the scope, and the readout says so. While an eye is within `scope.lens.max_eye_distance_m` of the eyepiece and behind it, that eye sights, and the lens is drawn on it as a disc facing back along the sight line; the other eye sees the game's scope model.

The lens resamples a rendered eye image rather than rendering the scene again. Each dispatch copies its eye's back buffer at the end of `PostDraw`, before the overlay, with the camera that rendered it; after the overlay the sighting eye draws the lens (`crates/jc3vr-render/shaders/scope_lens_vs.hlsl` and `scope_lens_ps.hlsl`), mapping a point seen at a tangent `t` off the sight line to `t / zoom` off it, at the aim point's distance, so the image is right at the target. The source is the other eye's image, because the sighting eye's own has the scope's model in front of exactly the magnified region; in two-pass stereo, when the first eye sights, that is the previous frame's second eye, so the lens lags by a frame there. A narrow-field third scene render would show true parallax but is not built.

Weapons > Aim, sights, and scopes shows the precision-aim refusals, the scoped weapon and its hand, the eyepiece's distance, and the lens's state.
