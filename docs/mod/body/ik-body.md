# The IK body

While Rico stands on foot, his lower body is posed from the learned body estimate rather than the game's idle clip: the pelvis goes where forward kinematics on the estimate puts the player's, the feet stay planted on the ground through HumanIK and move only by explicit steps, and the root follows the floor point under the pelvis. In the air the pelvis hangs under the head with the legs relaxed toward a light air pose. Hanging from the hands, each foot braces on the face in front of it or hangs relaxed. On the walk the clip keeps the body, with a share of the IK pose turning its pelvis. Everything else keeps what it had: the game's clips, the procedural torso, the arms, and the head.

The pure parts are in the `body-rig` crate (`fk`, `contact`, `brace`, `ik_weight`), and the game-facing parts in `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/body/ik_body/`. Its settings are `controllers.body.ik`: the firm ragdoll's target switch and the contacts' tuning; the rest of its tuning is fixed in code. The design it implements, and the pitfalls of the procedural legs removed on 2 November that it is built to avoid, are in the 14 November session's feedback (`docs/sessions/2026-11-14_02-05/feedback/body.md`, "The IK-first on-foot body: a design").

## The FK pelvis

The estimator ([body-estimator.md](body-estimator.md)) gives the global rotation of each of the 22 SMPL joints in the cockpit frame, but no translation: the network places the body by aligning its head joint with the tracked head. The pelvis is therefore found by forward kinematics down from the head (`body_rig::fk`). On an SMPL skeleton whose offsets are the neutral template's, scaled so the chain from the pelvis to the head is as long as the rig's spine from the waist pivot to the neck pivot and the legs stand the ankles at their height under hips at the waist pivot's height, the pelvis is the tracked neck pivot less the chain under the estimated spine and neck rotations. The hips hang from the pelvis, the knees from the hips, and the ankles from the knees under the estimated hip and knee rotations. A player who bends over gets a pelvis behind and below the head; one who crouches gets one lower under it.

The root's frame half advances it once per rendered frame in the rig's room frame, before the grounded rig (`ik_body::fk`), and publishes the body relative to the neck pivot. A critically damped spring with the smoothing time `fk.smooth_s` of the IK body's [tuning](../tuning.md) follows each point, which takes out the estimate's jitter between windows. When the estimate goes stale (older than half a second) the points ease over `fk.fallback_blend_s` to a fallback, the grounded rig's waist pivot with the legs hanging straight, so nothing snaps; the published source reads `estimate`, `blending`, or `fallback`. The IK takes the standing body only while the source is the estimate.

## Seeding the pose

The procedural body's solve gate ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md)) writes Rico's pose before HumanIK solves. The IK body works there in two halves (`ik_body::gate`, `ik_body::effectors`).

Before the torso is solved, the FK body is carried into Rico's model space: the player's offsets from the neck pivot turned from the room frame into the model's by the view's rotation against the character's, scaled by Rico's spine over the player's for the pelvis and the hips and by Rico's legs over the player's for the knees and the ankles, and hung from the neck pivot the torso's solve reaches for. The hand-over works with positions, not rotations, so no bone's rest frame has to be known: the clip's pelvis frame is read from its upper legs and its first spine bone, the FK pelvis's from its hips and first spine joint, and the clip's hips are turned by the difference between the two and moved onto the FK pelvis, carrying the whole posture with them (`ik_body::seed`). Where a planted foot would be further from its hip than the leg reaches, the pelvis comes down until it is in reach, at most the tuning's `gate.max_pelvis_drop_m`. The torso's anchored solve then runs on this seeded posture rather than the clip's, so the spine bends from the player's pelvis toward the player's neck and the clip's forward lean is gone with its hips.

After the pose is written, HumanIK's targets hold it through the solve. The hips get a position target at the seeded hips with the tuning's `effectors.hips` gains and a zero rotation offset at full rotation reach and resistance (`effectors.hips_rotation_reach` and `effectors.hips_resist`). [`HumanIK::UpdateEffectorsFromTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-UpdateEffectorsFromTargets) applies a rotation target as an offset onto the pose as it stands before the solve, which is the seeded pose, so a zero offset holds the seeded pelvis's orientation. Each foot with a contact gets an ankle target and a toe target ahead of it along the foot's heading, with the `effectors.foot` gains, whose pull is nothing, so reaching a foot moves only its leg; each knee gets a weak hint along the estimate's thigh (`effectors.knee_hint`). The effectors are found from Rico's bones through [`HumanIK::GetEffectorIdFromBoneIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-GetEffectorIdFromBoneIndex).

The game's locomotion IK task ([`NLocoIKTask_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/animation::arm_aim_ik##func-NLocoIKTask_Update)) queues hip and foot targets every on-foot tick before the gate. A pass holds one target per effector, and a later queue overwrites the earlier one in place ([`HumanIK::AddEffectorTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-AddEffectorTargetPosition)), so each IK target replaces the game's: at full weight it is the IK's, and at a partial weight it is the game's own target (or the bone where the game queued none) mixed toward the IK's, with the reach, pull, rotation reach, and resistance mixed the same way and the game's rotation offset scaled by what is left. The targets are queued without the blend-out flag, so on the first tick the IK stops queuing one it is dropped rather than left steering while its reach decays, and the game's task takes the effector back with its next target.

## Feet

Each foot is planted, swinging, or unsupported (`body_rig::contact`). A planted foot is a pose in its support's frame with the support's identity, so it stays where it is however the pelvis moves; a swinging foot has a planned landing, an arc `contact.arc_m` (7 cm) high at its middle, and a duration; an unsupported foot is left to the clip and the game's foot IK. An unsupported foot is planted where the clip has it, on the ground under it, which is how the contacts are acquired when the IK takes the standing body and after a step that found no ground.

A planted foot is released into a step only by a rule, evaluated in priority order:

- out of reach: the FK hip, counted `contact.reach_drop_m` (10 cm) lower for the pelvis drop the gate may use, is further from the ankle than `contact.reach_share` (1.02) of the leg's straight length;
- balance: the centre of mass, `contact.com_neck_share` (0.3) of the way from the pelvis to the neck, is further than `contact.com_margin_m` (15 cm) outside the support polygon of both feet's heels and toes;
- the root carried: something other than the player (the root's travel the FK pelvis did not make) has moved the root more than `contact.root_tolerance_m` (0.2 m) since the last landing;
- twist: the pelvis's heading is further than `contact.twist_deg` (65 degrees) from the feet's, and has stayed so for `contact.twist_hold_s` (0.8 s), so a glance that turns the hips moves no foot;
- tidy: once the body is at rest, a foot further than `contact.tidy_m` (30 cm) or `contact.tidy_deg` (25 degrees) off its stance spot for `contact.tidy_s` (4 s).

The thresholds come from the offline replay below.

One foot swings at a time, and when both want to step the one that did not step last goes. A twist to the left opens with the left foot. A step after any rule but the tidy is followed at once by the other foot if it is left off its stance spot by half the tidy's thresholds, so a body turn is two steps. A step takes `contact.step_s` (0.3 s) and lands at its stance spot under the FK pelvis, the procedural tuning's `stance.width_m` across from its partner and turned out by its `stance.toe_out_deg` (the square stance's values), led along the pelvis's travel by `contact.lead` (0.5) of the swing. The landing's ground is probed: the gate does not cast, so each foot's probe is posted to a mailbox and cast from the player aim control's post-simulation update, where the head probe casts (`ik_body::probe`), and the answer reaches the contact model a tick or two later, well within the swing. A step whose answer has not come by its landing waits up to `contact.landing_wait_s` (0.2 s). A probe that finds no ground within `probe.below_m` below leaves the foot unsupported, never planted.

When the movement is a walk rather than a step, the contacts are dropped and the legs handed to the game's walk: both feet out of reach at once while the pelvis moves faster than `contact.walk_speed_mps` (0.8 m/s), a step longer than `contact.max_step_m` (0.6 m), or the planted foot's hip carried more than `contact.outrun_m` (0.1 m) past its reach while the other swings. The IK then stays on the walk until Rico has stood still for the tuning's `gate.resume_s` with the grounded rig's step drive at rest.

## Moving supports

A probe names the body it lands on as the foot's support (`ik_body::support`). A static body is the world, whose frame is the identity. A body that moves (a vehicle's deck, a keyframed platform, a loose crate) is its own support, named by its id in the Havok world and the lifetime stamp the hand climbing keeps for it ([hand-climbing.md](../input/hand-climbing.md#moving-bodies)), so a freed body whose id is reused is a different support and the foot on it is lost. The gate does not read the physics world, so the probe's update snapshots each moving support the gate has asked about in the last two seconds, its transform from the body's slot and its centre of mass and velocities from its motion, the same reads the climb's moving grips make. The gate carries the snapshot forward to its own moment by the body's linear velocity and its spin about its centre of mass, at most a tenth of a second; a snapshot older than a quarter of a second, or a body that has changed hands, stands for no support, and its planted foot is unsupported (`a foot's support is gone`).

Foot probes ignore the character alone. The vehicle carrying the character remains queryable, so a truck bed or boat deck can supply the first support contact.

A planted foot is held in its support's frame and rebuilt in the world each gate, so a deck carries it, and a swing is held in the frame of the support it left, so a step taken on a moving deck lands on the deck. The body standing on the support is carried too, so the rules read the body's own travel against it (`body_rig::contact::SupportFrame::carry`): the pelvis's velocity, which the walk's speed and a step's lead read, is its velocity on the deck, not the deck's, and the root's drift for the root rule is the root's travel the pelvis did not make once the deck's motion has been taken out of both. The feet's targets are queued in Rico's model space at the gate, which the render interpolates like the rest of the pose, so nothing is rebuilt at render time.

## The offline replay

The design's first stage replays the FK pelvis and the contact model over a recorded take's estimate before a headset session: `tracking-synth rig-eval --weights <egoposer.safetensors> --ik [--contact <json>]... <recording> <out.csv>` ([recorder.md](../testing/recorder.md)), in `tools/tracking-synth/src/rig_eval/ik/`. It recomputes the estimate from the recording's raw head and grip poses at the game's cadence, follows it with the FK pelvis, and runs the contact model wherever the IK body would own the standing body: grounded on foot, the move stick at rest, and the estimate fresh; anything else drops the contacts, as does a manual or seat recenter. The ground is flat, every probe answers at once, the root follows the FK pelvis's floor point exactly (so the root rule never fires), and the feet are acquired at their stance spots. It logs, for each contact tuning, the standing time and its spells, the steps per standing minute and the rule behind each, the hand-overs, how often and how long a planted foot stood out of reach even with the pelvis lowered by `gate.max_pelvis_drop_m`, the drop the feet needed (the ninetieth percentile and the largest), the twist of the pelvis over the planted feet (its median, ninetieth, and ninety-ninth percentiles, and the share of the time past `twist_deg`), and the glances (head turns faster than 60 degrees a second of at least 15 degrees, while standing) with the steps released during one or within a second after.

With the first defaults nearly every step was the reach rule: a player standing tall stands the FK hips a centimetre or two past the legs' straight length, and the estimate's pelvis bobs with the head, so the hip left its reach on every bob although the gate's pelvis drop keeps the drawn foot planted. Counting the drop in the reach (`reach_drop_m`) takes those out; the steps left were the balance, the twist, and the tidy, each followed by the other foot, and the later rows widen those until the take gives about four steps a standing minute, one for every six glances. No planted foot needed more drop than the gate allows at any setting (the largest 18 cm, the ninetieth percentile 6 cm), and the walk took the legs in none of the tuned rows. The cost of the wide twist is a pelvis turned up to 65 degrees over the feet for most of a second before they turn.

## The root

While the IK owns the standing body (its pelvis position's weight past one half), the grounded rig's drive follows the floor point under the FK pelvis rather than committing steps after the neck ([rig.md](rig.md#steps)): the rig's step phase reads `following`, the gap is the pelvis's to that point, and the drive closes it at the gap over `step.follow_response_s` (0.12 s), capped at the drive's top speed, with no walk on the move stick. A bend moves the FK pelvis little in the room, so it makes no root motion; a physical step moves it, the root follows, and the feet step for themselves. The ledger, the cockpit shift, the push-back, and the recenter's snap are as before. The step commit, the lean, the settle, and the idle drift take the root back whenever the IK does not own it: without an estimate, in gamepad-only play, on the walk, and during the blend.

## Per state

The state comes from the procedural body's hand-over case and the body's animation state, and each state asks for a weight on each channel (`body_rig::ik_weight`): the pelvis's position, the pelvis's orientation, the spine, the planted feet, and the airborne legs. The weights move toward the state's at a constant rate, so every hand-over takes the tuning's `gate.blend_s` whichever way it goes, and a partial weight mixes local rotations rather than model-space positions, so no segment shortens mid-blend.

| State | When | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| idle | on foot, standing still, in one of the standing idles, with a fresh estimate | all but the airborne legs at 1 |
| walk | on foot with the stick or the game's walk moving Rico, or after a hand-over to the walk | the pelvis's orientation and the spine at `gate.walk_weight` |
| clip | on foot outside the standing idles (a landing, a turn, a weapon's act), an authored motion, a hit reaction, or a fall the torso has handed back | none |
| airborne | a jump, or a fall while the torso is still the player's | the pelvis, the spine, and the airborne legs at 1 |
| climb | climbing with the hands | the feet (the braces) and the airborne legs (the hanging legs) at 1 |
| unchanged | every other case, and whenever the IK stands down | none |

On the walk the clip's hips turn `gate.walk_weight` of the way to the FK pelvis's orientation (yaw, pitch, and roll) about their own position, and the torso's solve on them carries the spine; the clip's feet keep the game's foot IK. The jump's takeoff is the clip's until the hand-over case reads a jump, which it does once the body leaves the ground, and the airborne body blends in from there. A landing reads as on foot in a landing state, so it is the clip's, and the contacts are acquired afresh where the feet stand once the state returns to a standing idle. In the air each ankle is drawn `gate.air_relax` of the way from the estimate's ankle toward a light air pose under its hip (the knee a little bent and forward), with the `effectors.air_foot` gains.

The IK stands down, and says why in the readout and the log, when it is switched off, the procedural torso is off (nothing would write the seeded pose), under the gamepad scheme (the estimator does not run, so gamepad-only play is unchanged), while the firm ragdoll owns the legs on the grapple line or under the canopy ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md)), without a fresh estimate, and in every case the table leaves to the game: seats, the parachute, the wingsuit, swimming, traversals, ragdolls, get-ups, and the grapple's ledge climb. Hand climbing is a state of its own (below), and it needs no fresh estimate. A teleport or a recenter drops the contacts; they are acquired again on the next standing tick.

While the IK owns the standing body the square stance yields the feet to it by the feet's weight, and stays as the standing body's feet whenever the IK does not have them ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md#the-stance)). The act filter keeps the game's turn-in-place acts dropped while the IK owns the standing body, so a body turn is the twist rule's turn steps rather than a clip's turn ([locomotion.md](locomotion.md#the-turning-animations)).

## Feet on the face

On the 14 November take the player hand-climbed a rock face for 40 seconds with Rico's legs in the idle clip standing in the air, a boot dangling off the face below the view (the session's feedback, `docs/sessions/2026-11-14_02-05/feedback/body.md`, "1313 s"). While the hands climb ([hand-climbing.md](../input/hand-climbing.md#the-body)), the IK takes the legs in the `climb` state, with the pelvis, the spine, and the torso left to the procedural body on the clip's hips (`ik_body::climb`, `body_rig::brace`).

Each tick each foot asks for two casts: from its hip along Rico's facing and from its ankle forward and down, each a share of the leg's length out (`climb.hip_cast_share` and `climb.ankle_cast_share`). They are cast where the feet's ground probes cast and answered a tick or two later (`ik_body::brace`), and the nearest hit on a face that looks back along its cast is the foot's candidate, with its support named as a probe names one. A candidate becomes the foot's brace when the face looks toward the hip (the cosine between its normal and the line to the hip at least `brace.min_facing`) and the ankle, stood off the face by its height along the normal, is within `brace.acquire_share` of the leg's length from the hip and beyond `brace.min_share`. The sole lies against the face: the toe runs up the face's slope (along the facing laid on a level face), and the knee is hinted `brace.knee_m` toward the face from the line between the hip and the ankle. The ankle, the toe, and the knee get HumanIK targets with the feet's reach and pull.

A brace is held in its support's frame, so the foot stays put on the face while the body climbs past it, and a moving body carries it. Once the hip is further than `brace.reach_share` of the leg's length from it, the foot lets go: it moves to the latest candidate on an arc `brace.arc_m` off the face over `brace.move_s`, or, with none in reach, fades out of the brace over the same time and hangs in the air pose, hung from Rico's own hips. A braced foot's targets mix from the hanging pose toward the brace by the brace's share, so a foot taking or leaving the face moves rather than jumps. The braces last through the blend out of the climb, and the climb's end lets go of both.

## The firm ragdoll's muscle target

On the grapple line and under the canopy the firm ragdoll owns the legs ([procedural-states.md](procedural-states.md#the-firm-ragdoll)), and its pre-physics step drives its keyframed upper body and its motor-driven hips and legs toward the producer's current pose, which is this tick's raw animation ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md)): the bodies chase the clip, not the drawn body. The IK body composes the pose to feed it instead (`firm_ragdoll::target`), from the producer's previous pose, which then holds the last tick's final pose, and the IK body's legs:

- every bone above the hips is held where the last tick drew it, the HumanIK solve included, so the keyframed arms are where the drawn arms are;
- the hips hang from that drawn spine as the clip relates the two, and the pelvis and the legs keep the clip's local rotations;
- each leg is then aimed along the IK body's: its thigh turned onto the IK thigh, the thigh twisted so the knee bends in the IK leg's plane, and its shin turned onto the IK shin, the foot carried with it. The IK legs are the air pose the airborne body gives them, which the gate publishes with the bones while the firm ragdoll owns the legs (`firm_ragdoll::publish_source`); without a fresh estimate the legs stay the clip's.

The previous pose's own hips and legs are the blended physics, so a target taken from them would chase itself and the motors would lose their restoring pull; that is why the lower body comes from the clip and the IK legs, and only the bones above the hips from the drawn pose.

The target is handed over as a stand-in pose producer (`firm_ragdoll::stand_in`): the game's current pose's header with its arrays pointed at the mod's own storage, flagged so that nothing frees it, holding the target's local and model-space transforms with no bone marked stale and both spaces marked in sync. The previous pose is read as Havok's own sync would read it, in a copy: a bone flagged with a stale model-space transform ([`HKA_POSE_F_BONE_MODEL_DIRTY`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::skeleton::HKA_POSE_F_BONE_MODEL_DIRTY)) is composed from its local transform. The ragdoll's step reads only the producer's current pose, so the game's producer, which the post-physics blend, the solve, and the skinning go on using, is untouched. `firm_ragdoll::feed` returns the producer to hand the step: the stand-in while the firm mode is in on the local player and the source is fresh, the game's own otherwise.

A detour on the ragdoll's pre-physics step ([`RagdollInstance::PrePhysicsUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ragdoll::RagdollInstance##func-PrePhysicsUpdate)) hands it over for the local player's instance and passes every other character's producer through. The readout shows how many targets were composed and refused, whether the legs came from the IK body, how far the upper body moved from the clip's, and how far each thigh turned, and the first target of each spell is logged at info level (`firm ragdoll: the muscle target is the drawn body`). The switch is `IK pose as the firm ragdoll's target` (`ragdoll_target`, on).

## Settings and readout

The debug UI's Body fit settings hold the switch `IK pose as the firm ragdoll's target` (`ragdoll_target`, on) and an `IK body` section with the contacts' tuning: the step's duration and lift, the release thresholds, and the longest step and the walk's speed. The IK body itself has no switch, and the walk's share, the blend, the resume time, the pelvis's drop, HumanIK's gains, the air pose's share, the pelvis's smoothing, and the braces' tuning are fixed in code. The Body fit readout shows the state and why the IK stands down, where the pelvis comes from, each channel's weight and how many targets the gate queued, each foot's state, the planted count, the steps and their rate over the last minute, the hand-overs and the missed probes, the last step's foot and reason, how far the seeded hips stand from the clip's, how far the pelvis was lowered, and whether the feet stand on the world or a moving body, how often they have come to stand on one, and how many moving supports are tracked, and while climbing whether each foot is braced or hanging and how many braces have been taken.

## Logs and the recording

At info level, under the `ik body` source, the log reports each change of state (`takes the standing body`, `takes the airborne body`, `blends onto the walk`, `stands down`, with the reason and the case), each step (`a foot steps`, with the foot, the rule, the step's length, and the estimate's weight), each hand-over to the walk (with the reason and the pelvis's speed), each landing without ground and each lost support, each change of the feet's support between the world and a moving body (`the feet's support changes`), each brace taken, moved, or let go while climbing (`a foot braces on the face`, `a foot moves to a new brace`, `a foot lets go of the face`, with the foot), and the contacts dropped at a teleport or a recenter. Every ten seconds of a standing spell it logs `ik body: standing` with each foot's state, the planted count, the spell's steps and rate, the feet's weight, the hips' move from the clip's, the pelvis's drop, the targets queued, the estimate's weight, whether the feet stand on a moving body, and the pelvis's speed on its support, and each spell ends with `ik body: the standing body ends`, its length, steps, rate, hand-overs, and largest drop. Each climb ends with `ik body: the climb's feet`, its length, the braces taken, and the share of it each foot was braced. Landings and acquisitions are at debug level.

The report lines are noted in the recording as diagnostic events, and the rig's sample names the drive's phase, which reads `following` on every frame the IK owns the root, so a recording carries the IK's transitions and steps with no change to the format. The final pose the recording samples shows where the solve put the feet against the steps' landings.

## Unverified

Nothing here has run in the game yet. How HumanIK answers a held pelvis and two planted feet (the controlled stand) is the first unknown: whether the zero rotation offset with full resistance holds the pelvis while the feet reach, whether the torso written before the solve stays where the anchored solve put it, and whether the knee hints keep the knees forward. The FK skeleton's proportions, Rico's ankle height over the ground (read from his reference pose's ankle over its toe, plus a generic toe joint height), the effectors the bone lookup finds for the hips and the knees, and the steps' timing and gains are first estimates. The release thresholds are tuned on one take's replay, which leaves out the root, the clip's feet, and the ground.

A moving support's snapshot is carried forward by its velocities from where the probes cast to the gate; whether that keeps a planted foot on a fast vehicle's deck without a visible lag is unverified, as is whether the deck's own body is the one the probe's cast reports for a vehicle's bed. The braces' casts, reaches, and knee hint are first estimates, and whether HumanIK reaches a sole flat against a vertical face is unknown. The muscle target's stand-in fills only the bound part of the pose header and zeroes the rest; whether the ragdoll's mapping reads anything further, and that the producer's previous pose's model-space transforms are current when the pre-physics step runs, are to be checked against the release IDB before the step is bound.

## Headset checks

1. Standing still, the body mirror on: the readout reads `idle`, both feet `planted`, and eight targets queued. Rico's pelvis sits under the player's, his feet stay put on the ground, and his torso is not pulled about. If the hips or the torso fight, note the log's `hips_moved_m` and try `Hips resist` and `Hips turn reach` at lower values, then `Feet pull` above zero.
2. Bending over to look down and crouching: no foot steps, the pelvis goes back and down, the knees bend forward. If the feet float or sink, report the readout's pelvis drop; if the knees bend backward or sideways, raise `Knee hints`.
3. Glancing about and turning the hips a little: no foot steps. Turning the body past about 65 degrees and holding it for most of a second: two turn steps, the left foot first for a turn to the left, and no game turn-in-place clip.
4. A physical step aside, forward, and back: one or two steps, feet alternating, landing square under the pelvis; the root follows without a slide. Record the steps per minute standing from the readout and the standing log: the replay of the 14 November take predicts about four a minute, one for every six glances. If the hips turn too far over the feet before they step, lower `Twist past`; if the feet shuffle on glances, raise it or the hold.
5. Walking off physically and with the stick: the readout reads `walk` with a hand-over or `walking`, the game's walk plays, and stopping brings the feet back in planted with no snap.
6. Steps and slopes: steps up and down a kerb land on its height; a step over a ledge leaves the foot unsupported (`a step's landing has no ground` in the log) rather than planted in the air.
7. A jump and a fall: the takeoff and the landing are the clip's, the pelvis hangs under the head in the air, the legs relax, and the standing body comes back after the landing.
