# Terrain rendering

Which render blocks draw the terrain, how its patches are culled and submitted, what its hull shaders discard, and how the GPU builds the detail rock skin. The cull cameras it shares with the rest of the scene are in [model-culling.md](model-culling.md); the bindings are chiefly in the pyxis module [`patch_system`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/patch_system) and on [`RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch).

## Which blocks draw the visible terrain

The visible terrain is the volumetric-patch system, `CRenderBlockTerrainPatch` (the `VolumetricTerrainPatch` block type), plus the detail skin, `CTerrainRenderBlockDetail` (the `TerrainDetail` type), the rock of cliff walls, cave ceilings, and the near field. The base volumetric terrain, `CRenderBlockTerrain`, is dormant in the shipped world: its hull-clip selection, [`RenderBlockTerrain::HullClipType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTerrain##func-HullClipType), and its per-patch fragment, [`TerrainPatchUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/patch_system##func-TerrainPatchUpdate), are never called. The live per-patch fragment is [`TerrainPatchSystemFragment`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/patch_system##func-TerrainPatchSystemFragment)(the fragment-name table pairs names and functions across rows, not within them).

The distant terrain is the `VolumetricTerrainPatch` type throughout, handing over to other types as the camera approaches; distant trees (`TreeImpostor`), `TerrainForest`, and `Occluder` are likewise far by type, not by distance. The `TERRAIN_APPLY_*` pass categories, from [`RenderPassId::RP_TERRAIN_APPLY_NEAR_DETAILED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_TERRAIN_APPLY_NEAR_DETAILED) to `RP_TERRAIN_APPLY_FAR`, hold no passes. The `BASEMESH_*_COLOR` passes hold patch blocks but process patch space rather than draw the G-buffer.

The block-type registry, [`RenderBlockTypeRegistry`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderBlockTypeRegistry) (`CRenderBlockFactory`'s vector), holds every type. Each type's `Enable`, `Disable`, and [`IsEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderBlockTypeBase##vfunc-IsEnabled) are stubbed to constants; an `IsEnabled` that returns false removes every block of its type.

## Culling and submission

The landscape manager, [`LandscapeManager::UpdatePatchSystem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/patch_system::LandscapeManager##func-UpdatePatchSystem), assembles up to [`PatchViewDesc::MAX_FRUSTUMS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/patch_system::PatchViewDesc::MAX_FRUSTUMS) cull frusta and runs a BFBC bounding-box cull of each patch against each, writing one bit per frustum into [`PatchHeader::m_VisibilityBits`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/patch_system::PatchHeader##field-m_VisibilityBits). The frusta are named by [`PatchFrustumId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/patch_system::PatchFrustumId): the occluder manager's main cull camera, the shadow cascades, the two reflective-shadow frusta, and [`FRUSTUM_ID_DEPTH_PREPASS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/patch_system::PatchFrustumId##variant-FRUSTUM_ID_DEPTH_PREPASS), a second run of the main cull camera. Both main-camera entries share one cached parameter set ([`OccluderCollectionManager::GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::OccluderCollectionManager##func-GetBFBCFrustumParamsForCameraAndTime), keyed by camera and time), so their bits cannot disagree. The planes are extracted from the view and projection ([`BFBCCalculateCameraFrustum`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/graphics_engine::bfbc##func-BFBCCalculateCameraFrustum)) for any projection whose clip volume is `0 ≤ z ≤ w`.

The fragment adds each patch's block to one pass per set bit (colour, shadow cascade, reflective shadow, depth prepass), with quadrant sub-blocks for the near passes at the finest LOD. The terrain block's Z-family entry points do not draw the prepass ([`RenderBlockTerrain::DrawZ`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTerrain##func-DrawZ) is empty): the prepass runs the same tessellation pipeline as the colour pass. The detail skin's blocks are submitted for the finest-LOD patches in the camera's quadrant, by position, not visibility bit.

The landscape system's GPU stages read their own camera, [`STerrainPatchSystem::m_TerrainCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::STerrainPatchSystem##field-m_TerrainCamera), which [`TerrainPatchSystemUpdate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/graphics_engine::render_engine##func-TerrainPatchSystemUpdate) refreshes from the active camera every frame.

## The hull's discards

The terrain hull shaders (`volumetricterrainpow2hull*` in `Shaders_F`) can discard a whole patch in the patch-constant phase by zeroing its tessellation factors, for three independent reasons:

1. Back-patch cull: all three control-point normals face away from one view direction by more than [`m_BackPatchCullThreshold`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##field-m_BackPatchCullThreshold). The direction is the render camera's forward axis, not the view vector to the patch, so a patch at the edge of a wide view that faces the eye can fail it. Only the reflection passes enable it, and only with [`m_EnableBackPatchCulling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##field-m_EnableBackPatchCulling) set.
2. Frustum cull: each control point, expanded by the patch radius along its normal, is projected through the render context's [`m_OffsetViewProjection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::graphics_engine::RenderContext##field-m_OffsetViewProjection), and the patch is discarded when all are outside one clip plane. The patch type enables it with [`m_EnableFrustumPatchCulling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##field-m_EnableFrustumPatchCulling), except in the shadow cascades and the near-to-colour basemesh passes, but the patch system's own hull programs test the frustum regardless of the flag.
3. LOD clip: for a tile coarser than the finest LOD, four taps of the global terrain visibility mask at the patch's corners discard it when a finer tile has fully faded in over its footprint, keyed on streaming residency, not the view. The patch type's [`Setup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##func-Setup) selects it inline from [`m_HullProgramHolders`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##field-m_HullProgramHolders): the near basemesh passes bind the near holder, the other tessellating passes the LOD-clip holder, and `m_DisableLodClipping` is compiled out. (The dormant base type selects the same clip through [`TerrainHullClipType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::TerrainHullClipType).)

The constants for the first two are baked by [`RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch::SetupConstantBuffers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainPatch##func-SetupConstantBuffers) (and, for the base type, [`RenderBlockTypeTerrain::SetupConstantBuffers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrain##func-SetupConstantBuffers)) once per frame per constant-buffer slot, cached against the frame number in `m_WasCBApplied`, so every view drawn in a frame is culled against the first bake's camera. A patch the hull discards draws in no pass, and the LOD clip removes the coarser tile over its footprint, so nothing fills the hole: the pixels keep the far depth and resolve dark.

## The detail skin

[`RenderBlockTerrainDetail::Draw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTerrainDetail##func-Draw) is an indexed instanced indirect draw from the tile's arguments slot (by [`m_DetailPatchIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTerrainDetail##field-m_DetailPatchIndex) and [`m_DetailLod`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTerrainDetail##field-m_DetailLod)) in [`RenderBlockTypeTerrainSetup::m_DetailDrawList`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_block::RenderBlockTypeTerrainSetup##field-m_DetailDrawList). GPU compute writes those arguments: the detail setup blocks dispatch three indirect stages in [`RP_TERRAINPATCH_SETUP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_TERRAINPATCH_SETUP), [`RP_TERRAINPATCH_MORPH_TARGET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_TERRAINPATCH_MORPH_TARGET), and [`RP_TERRAINPATCH_ENUMERATION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_TERRAINPATCH_ENUMERATION), all on the terrain camera.

The draw-list builder keeps a quad inside all six frustum planes of the terrain camera (uploaded into the frustum-plane rows of [`RenderEngine::m_VPGlobalConstData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##field-m_VPGlobalConstData) by [`RenderEngine::SetGlobalShaderProgramCameraConstants`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-SetGlobalShaderProgramCameraConstants)), within a shader-set distance of the camera (its radius plus 64 world units for the high-detail bucket, plus 128 for the detail bucket), and, with the camera above water, no more than 80 units below sea level. The planes are its only view-direction input.

The kept quads' vertices, indices, and texels come from fixed-size buffers through global atomic cursors with no capacity check. When the kept set outgrows them, Direct3D 11 drops the writes past the end, and the tiles that lost the allocation get empty draw arguments and render black; which tiles lose follows allocation order, not screen position, and changes in steps as the view turns. The buffer sizes are [`TerrainDetailBudgetValues`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::TerrainDetailBudgetValues), immediates in `CRenderBlockTerrainDetailSetup::Create` and `CRenderBlockTerrainSetup::Create` at [`TerrainDetailBudgetPatchSites`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::TerrainDetailBudgetPatchSites); they fit the stock field of view, and a wider frustum admits more quads than they hold. The setup types' `Recreate` does nothing, so new sizes take effect only when the types are destroyed and re-created.
