# Periodic sim-tick hitches

Resolved: the cause is outside the game and the mod. Sessions hitched on a fixed wall-clock beat, about 300 ms twice a minute and roughly every other minute about 600 ms, inside the sim tick and outside every timed phase.

## The cause: the Steam client's link to a Steam Frame

The native Linux Steam client, with a Steam Frame paired through a USB Wi-Fi adapter and out of reach, tries to join the Frame's network every 61 s through NetworkManager, and the attempt fails about 25 s later. The client handles the attempt (about 600 ms, with a Wi-Fi scan) and the failure (about 300 ms) on the thread that also answers its IPC. Under Proton, `steam_api64.dll` reaches the native client through `lsteamclient` and the client's IPC pipe, so the game thread's first Steam call in either stretch blocks. The stalls follow wall-clock time even while the game runs in slow motion.

To recognise it: the stalls sit in `sim_steam`, the Steam platform system's update ([frame-timing.md](../mod/performance/frame-timing.md)), each logs `online: the Steam callbacks blocked the game thread` with its duration, and their times match the attempts and failures in the client's `logs/remote_connections.txt` and `logs/client_networkmanager.txt` (local time, against the session log's UTC). The fix is the player's: unplug the adapter while the Frame is not in use, keep the Frame on and in range, or unpair it. Stalls in `sim_steam` that do not match those events have another cause; timing the dispatched callbacks by id, through detours on Proton's `steamclient64.dll` exports, is the next step for those.

The mod does not work around it. Running `SteamAPI_RunCallbacks` on another thread would run game handlers off the game thread, drive the Steam pipe from two threads, and only move the stall to the game thread's next Steam call. Deferring the callbacks cannot help, because the stretch is not visible before a call meets it.

## The remedy: the offline retry's logged-on check

While the [`LoginManager`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginManager) is [`LoggedIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginState##variant-LoggedIn) in the [`Offline`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::LoginMode##variant-Offline) mode, [`OnlineSuiteManager::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::OnlineSuiteManager##func-Update) asks [`AccountManager::IsConnectedToFirstPartyService`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::AccountManager##vfunc-IsConnectedToFirstPartyService) on every tick, before it checks the five-minute retry clock. For Steam that is [`AccountManagerSteam::IsConnectedToFirstPartyService`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/online::AccountManagerSteam##func-IsConnectedToFirstPartyService), which calls `ISteamUser::BLoggedOn`, a round trip into the Steam client on every tick. This check was the first Steam call to meet the stalls, which is why they first appeared in the online-suite manager.

The mod skips it to save the round trip. A detour on the Steam account's check (`crates/jc3vr-gameplay/src/hooks/online.rs`) answers false without calling Steam when the game thread is inside the manager's update and outside the online suite's own. The switch (`skip_logged_on_check`, on by default) is in the debug UI beside the frame breakdown, and `sim_logged_on` times the call site. The account manager's other callers (the title screen's login choice, the reconnect from the pause menu and the comm link, the login completion, the achievements, and the DLC store) still call Steam. The cost to the player is the automatic retry: a session that logged in offline stays offline, without the Square Enix Membership rewards and online statistics, until the game restarts.
