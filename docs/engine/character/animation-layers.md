# Animation layers and the pose job

A character's animation is a stack of layers, each a state machine with clips, blended into a local-space pose by the pose producer; rule systems, state tasks, and IK also contribute to the final pose. Related: [animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md) (the state machine format, the modifiers that pick the base layer's clips, and the weapon animation), [humanik.md](humanik.md) (the IK passes), and [skeleton.md](skeleton.md) (the pose store).

## The layers

[`Character::m_AnimatedModel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AnimatedModel) holds up to seven layers. `CCharacter::InitAnimationModel` creates layer *i* from the *i*-th state-machine file of the character's construction info, names it by `CCharacter::s_LayerNames` (mirrored by [`AnimationLayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer)), and stops at the first missing file. Rico ships four graphs, in `animations/jc_statemachine/main_characters/rico/` (`patch_win64/game1`):

| Layer | Rico's graph | What it plays |
|---|---|---|
| [`MAINBODY`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer##variant-MAINBODY) | `rico_base` | the full body: locomotion, stunts, the grapple, the parachute, vehicles, with weapon-modifier clip variants on nearly every node |
| [`SYNCED_PARTIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer##variant-SYNCED_PARTIAL) | `rico_partial_sync` | upper-body partials kept in step with the base: the turns toward the aim target (`S_PARTIAL_AIM_TARGET_*`), and idle, move, and fall partials |
| [`GLOBAL_PARTIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer##variant-GLOBAL_PARTIAL) | `rico_partial_global` | upper-body one-offs: draw, holster, switch, reload, shotgun pump, sniper bolt, pickup, grenade throws, explosives, and the parachute's arm states (`S_UB_*`, `S_JC_UB_*`) |
| [`SYNCED_ADDITIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer##variant-SYNCED_ADDITIVE) | `rico_additive_sync` | additives on the base: leans, starts and stops, landings, the aim-focus additives, the per-gun recoil ([animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md), "Recoil and the vertical aim blend"), and the grapple, grenade, and launcher fire additives |

Each layer is one rule system, [`AnimatedModel::m_RuleSystems`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimatedModel##field-m_RuleSystems) at the layer's index, and one body part, [`AnimationController::m_BodyParts`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##field-m_BodyParts) at the same index. A body part ([`AnimatedBodyPart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::AnimatedBodyPart)) owns the layer's clip driver ([`AnimationDriverTransition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::AnimationDriverTransition)), which follows the rule system's state, starts and blends its clips, and owns the layer's blender ([`AnimationDriverTransition::m_Blender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::AnimationDriverTransition##field-m_Blender)). `CAnimationControl::AddAnimatedBodyPart` pushes the body part and adds its blender to the controller's pose producer, so the blenders are in layer order.

## The pose job

`Character::UpdatePassAnimations_Parallel` either evaluates the pose through [`AnimationController::UpdateAnimations`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-UpdateAnimations) or only advances time through [`AnimationController::UpdateAnimationsTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-UpdateAnimationsTime); the two are alternatives, not consecutive phases. Both return without producer work when the controller is not initialized. The callback also has eligibility checks that can skip animation work, and picks the bone count for pose evaluation by the character's level of detail.

`UpdateAnimations` forwards the delta time and bone count to [`PoseProducer::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::PoseProducer##func-Update), which exchanges the previous and current pose buffers, advances blender times, and calls [`PoseProducer::UpdateBlendersPoseInRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::PoseProducer##func-UpdateBlendersPoseInRange) synchronously. The range operation blends into temporary transform and float buffers, copies the selected transforms and float slots into the current pose, and frees the buffers before the controller call returns; no deferred producer job is submitted. This does not establish that every later pose-finalization invocation has a fresh full-skeleton pose. `UpdateAnimationsTime` runs the producer's blender-time range operation over all blenders and neither evaluates nor copies the pose. Zero delta time holds clip time on either path; rule-state changes, blend weights, IK, and other pose writers can still change the final pose.

The range operation combines the layers in order:

1. The first blender samples its clips over the skeleton's reference pose ([`Blender::UpdatePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::Blender##func-UpdatePose)) and also writes its result as the pre-additive pose.
2. Each later blender for which [`Blender::HasSamplers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::Blender##func-HasSamplers) holds runs `Blender::UpdatePose` with the previous output as its reference and a fresh output buffer. A partial layer's clips carry per-bone weights, so it overlays only the bones it animates, and where its total weight falls below the skeleton's reference threshold the reference shows through. An additive layer adds onto the reference.
3. A blender without samplers is skipped and the pose below passes on unchanged. Since `UpdatePose` writes only its outputs, copying a layer's reference to its output would give the same result.

## What a layer does besides its pose

The state machine, its state tasks, the driver's clip selection and times, the track messages, the segment queries, and the animation-finished checks run from the driver and the rule system, not from the blended pose. The reload refill and the equip and unequip on draw and holster are track messages on their clips ([animation-graph.md, "The upper-body actions"](animation-graph.md#the-upper-body-actions)); the base layer's `NWeaponUnequipTask` ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter)) unequips on entering its states ([animation-graph.md, "The base layer's weapon tasks"](animation-graph.md#the-base-layers-weapon-tasks)); and `NSetInventoryPermissions` tasks, some gated on segments, block firing during the actions ([aim-state.md, "Fire gating"](../gameplay/aim-state.md#fire-gating)).

The engine's level of detail (`CCharacter::UpdateLOD`) turns a distant character's partial and additive layers off through `CAnimationControl::SetInactiveBodyPart`, which resets the layer driver's clips every update, so their track messages and segments stop too.

## State tasks inside the pose-finalization pass

[`Character::UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel) begins by running the current states' tasks registered in its state-task range, [`Character::FINALIZE_POSE_STATE_TASK_RANGE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character::FINALIZE_POSE_STATE_TASK_RANGE), through [`Character::UpdateStateTasksInRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateStateTasksInRange). Each update pass of a character runs its own range, and every task type is registered in exactly one. The aim IK, look-at IK, locomotion IK, grapple-fire arm IK, and weapon off-hand IK tasks are in the finalize range, so their effector targets and blackboard keys are written after the pass's entry and before the IK solve, and a key zeroed at the pass's entry is written again by the time the solve reads it. The off-hand task and the parachute handle task, which feed the `SECONDARY` hand pass, are in [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md, "The secondary hand pass"](weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-secondary-hand-pass).
