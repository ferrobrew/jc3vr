# The true reload

Under the controller scheme the game's timed reload is replaced by one done with the hands: the old magazine is knocked out with the fresh one in the other hand, ejected with a button or a flick of the gun, or pulled out by hand, a fresh one is taken from a pouch on the belt and pushed into the gun's own magazine well, and a gun that ran dry is chambered by working its slide, charging handle, bolt, or pump. A gun can also be reloaded in one hand by pushing its well down onto the pouch. The code is in `payload/src/controllers/weapons/reload/`, the settings are `controllers.weapons.reload`, the fixed tuning is `RELOAD` in `controllers::weapons::reload`, and the engine side is in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ammo-and-reload.md). Nothing here has been run in the headset yet; see "Unverified" at the end.

## Which weapons

A weapon is reloaded by hand when it is wielded in the hands, has a magazine limit, is not one of the weapons whose reload has no animation, and is of a class with something to load: the pistols, revolvers, SMGs, rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles, grenade and rocket launchers, and the mortar (`plan.rs`). The minigun, the mounted and vehicle guns, and the special weapons keep the game's reload, as does every weapon with the reload off (`enabled`).

What a weapon loads and how its action is worked come from the wells table (see "Wells"), or from its class when the table has no entry for it:

| Load | Weapons | Eject | Load | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Magazine | pistols, SMGs, rifles, sniper rifles, grenade launchers, the Hydra's rocket pod | yes | a whole magazine | slide, charging handle, bolt, or none |
| Shell | shotguns | no | one shell per push | the pump of the pump-action shotgun, otherwise none |
| Rocket | rocket launchers, the mortar | no | one round into the muzzle | none |
| Cylinder | revolvers | yes (the spent cylinder) | a whole cylinder | none |

## Launchers

The grenade launchers, the rocket launchers, and the mortar are launchers (`ReloadPlan::launcher`, from the weapon's class). None has an action to work: once its round seats it fires, and the game has no cocking step for any of them ([weapon-ammo-and-reload.md, "Launchers"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#launchers)). A launcher's well is large and sits far from the hand on the grip, so its round catches at `RELOAD.launcher_capture_radius_m` and `RELOAD.launcher_capture_angle_deg` instead of a gun's radius and angle; the gold ring at the mouth is drawn at that radius.

- The UPU-210 loads a cartridge of grenades, a magazine whose part (`model.cartridge_full`) enters the underside of the receiver just ahead of the grip, pushed up. It is ejected and pulled out like a magazine, and the cartridge from the pouch or the ground is drawn in the hand.
- A rocket launcher takes one rocket into the front of its tube, pushed back toward the shooter, the rocket's rear end first. The rocket (`model.rpg`) is drawn in the hand while it is held; let go before it seats, it goes back to whatever the launcher shows on its own, which is nothing while the launcher is empty.
- The Hydra's pod goes into the rear of its housing, pushed forward.

A seated round that leaves the launcher able to fire plays the ready cue (see "Haptics"); there is no other step.

## Stopping the game's reload

Every animated reload the game starts goes through one call, [`Character::StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-StartReloadingWieldingWeapon): the reload button, the automatic reload of an empty or magazine-less gun, the predicted reload while idle, a reserve refilled by a pickup, an animation's request, and the launchers' and sniper rifles' own requests. A detour on it refuses the start for the local player while the input tick reloads either hand's weapon by hand (`gate.rs`). No reload act is queued, so the game's reload state never begins and nothing is left to clean up. The input tick publishes each hand's weapon it reloads with a time stamp; if the tick stops for half a second, the detour stops refusing and the game reloads as usual. Switching the reload off mid-reload leaves a magazine-less gun to the game, which reloads it.

The reload button ejects (see "Eject"); a start of the game's own timed reload is refused while the weapon is reloaded by hand.

## Unlimited ammunition

With unlimited ammunition the magazine never empties, so reloading is optional (`reload/infinite.rs`). The game's own unlimited ammunition only makes the reload free: each shot still empties the magazine, and the game's reload, which the stop above refuses, would refill it ([weapon-ammo-and-reload.md, "Unlimited ammunition"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#unlimited-ammunition)). Instead, while a magazine is in a gun reloaded by hand:

- each shot puts its round back at once, at the per-shot seam, so the magazine is full again before the gun's next ammunition check and a pistol's slide never locks back;
- each input tick tops up a magazine that came in short, before the chamber partition reads it, so a gun taken from a character, whose magazine can be empty, is adopted full with a round chambered and fires at once.

The chamber is left alone: a bolt or a pump is still worked after every shot, and an action that is open or empty is still worked to chamber a round. The magazine can still be ejected or pulled, the pouch still gives a magazine at any time, and whatever magazine seats, from the pouch or the world, is full from then on. A magazine dropped while the ammunition is unlimited carries no rounds of its own into the world: it is filled when it seats, and none of the rounds the unlimited ammunition made reach a later finite load through it. It also ages out of the world store as an empty magazine does. A launcher of one round is loaded again after each shot.

Whether the ammunition is unlimited is the game's rule read for the local player (`engine::unlimited`): [`Character::HasUnlimitedAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasUnlimitedAmmo) of the player, which follows the game's unlimited-ammunition option, or the weapon's tuning's [`m_unlimited_ammo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_unlimited_ammo). The weapon's own query reads the weapon's user, which for a gun taken from another character or one only armed in a hand need not be the player, and answers true for a user that is not the player. The same rule decides whether the pouch has a round to give and whether a seat or a shell draws on the reserve, so it holds for both hands, a gun in each hand, the game's pistol pair and the separate pistols (each filled to its own half magazine), the launchers, and magazine pulls and inserts alike.

## Eject

A tap of the semantic `reload` action (the off-hand's lower face button on foot, in the default layout) ejects the magazine of the driest gun in either hand: the one with the fewest rounds in its magazine, the dominant hand's on a tie (`eject::tap_target`). A gun whose magazine is already out, or whose eject is still queued, is passed over, so a second tap ejects the other hand's gun and a double tap empties both. The same button held is the game's holster, so only a press released within `RELOAD.tap_s` ejects. With `flick_eject` (off by default, since the button already ejects and a fast turn of the gun hand in play reads as a flick too easily), a gun hand turning faster than `RELOAD.flick_speed_dps` ejects that gun's magazine, at most once per `RELOAD.flick_cooldown_s`; the speed is the change in the hand's orientation between two input ticks (`eject.rs`).

The input tick queues the eject for the local character's next native serial post-physics callback. The consumer resolves the current owned weapon, gun, and spare before calling the game's magazine drop: [`WeaponData::ThrowAwayMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-ThrowAwayMagazineObject) drops the gun's spare magazine prop from the magazine part (hiding the part) or from the gun itself, with the gun's velocity, so a flicked gun throws the magazine; [`WeaponData::SpawnMagazineObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-SpawnMagazineObject) asks for the next spare; and the weapon's magazine-out flag ([`WeaponBase::m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponIsWithoutMagazine)) is set, which the game itself reads to refuse fire. A weapon with no magazine prop drops nothing. With the magazines in the world off, the rounds left in the ejected magazine stay counted in the weapon until the next magazine seats, when the game's reload returns them to the reserve, as the game's own reload does; the existing live chamber remains part of the native loaded total, while the absent magazine cannot feed another round. With them on (the default), the rounds leave the gun with the magazine (see "Magazines in the world").

## Physical magazine removal

Physical extraction executes at [`Character::UpdatePassPostPhysics_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassPostPhysics_Serial), before the original callback polls native animation messages. The engine registers this callback in its nonparallel post-physics update range. The inspected native route has no explicit write-mark pair, so the mod does not call an unverified marker API. The input tick only submits a request. `engine::physical_detach_available` reflects installation and admission of this callback.

The provider in `reload/extraction.rs` requires a tracked free hand, an inserted detachable magazine, a table-defined well, a measured gun frame, world conversion, and a live tagged spare prop. Shell-fed pumps, revolver cylinders, muzzle-loaded rockets, and fallback wrist wells are excluded. Both `reload.extraction.enabled` and `reload.loose.enabled` must be enabled.

A fresh grip claim on that target starts a pull, as does a claim anywhere in the magazine's zone (see "The magazine's zone"). No release button or additional latch is required. The hand must travel outward along the well's axis by `reload.extraction.pull_m`. Movement is measured relative to the moving gun, so translating or rotating the gun with the hand does not complete the pull. The grab radius and sideways tolerance have separate settings. The target centre lies outside the well along its axis by `RELOAD.loose.half_length_m`, converted from model lengths to the player's scale. This is a fixed approximation of the magazine centre, not a measured magazine hull.

Before detachment, opening the grip, losing hand tracking or world conversion, changing the gun or world scale, moving beyond the sideways tolerance, or disabling extraction cancels the pull. Cancellation leaves the magazine inserted, with its ammunition, chamber readiness, and action travel unchanged. An existing brace or action hold prevents acquisition. For a new grip inside the magazine target, `reload::prioritize_extraction` removes overlapping brace, gun-transfer, and action targets before ordinary nearest-target resolution. An active pull owns the hand exclusively and blocks the gun from firing. Bracing outside the magazine target and deliberate grip transfer retain their existing paths.

Pull completion submits a value request through `DetachEngine`; it does not call the engine from input. A later result distinguishes `NoThrow`, `DroppedUnretained`, and `Retained`. A false engine throw requests no spare and changes no ammunition, magazine-out flag, readiness, or visibility. A successful unretained drop leaves rounds in the engine's native accounting without a reserve refund. A retained result takes the exact recorded prop into the pulling hand and starts insertion with `InsertionSource::RetainedMagazine`. Retention capture matches both the expected gun and spare, so an unrelated reentrant throw cannot replace the result. The engine wrapper carries raw addresses, with no exclusive owner borrow across throw or spawn callbacks. The ammunition ledger partitions the native loaded total into magazine-owned and chamber-owned rounds. A retained drop transfers only the magazine-owned rounds; an existing live chamber stays counted on the weapon.

An active or pending pull blocks belt processing for that gun and resets an already-started belt push. The block persists through the result or cancellation tick. Physical/world magazines do not use the pouch ammunition timeout while held. Grip release and tracking loss still release them, and the registry keeps its held entry beyond the world retention lifetime. Pouch ammunition retains its existing timeout.

The mailbox in `reload/serial.rs` holds at most one operation per gun hand, including an unread result. Requests contain copied character, weapon, gun, and spare identities, not borrowed pointers. The callback resolves the current local character's inventory or the existing strong-owned carry set before dereferencing the weapon. It rechecks the world, holder, gun index, spare identity, magazine state, controller focus, tracking freshness, reload permission, and the pulling hand's grip. A canceled or changed request does not fall back to input execution. Completion consumes the request once. The magazine-out flag is committed before the original native message poll, so its throw-away branch rejects a duplicate detach.

The input owner calls `reload::cancel_extraction_on_standdown` on a mode boundary. This clears magazine claims and queued ejects, preserves unrelated holds, and requires neutral grip before a fresh extraction claim. Canceling delivery after detachment leaves the retained magazine and its rounds in the world. Button, flick, knock, and belt drops use the same callback; a belt swap loads only after the drop result returns. `ReloadStatus::serial` reports installation, admission, pending/executing/completed slots, callback and execution counts, cancellation, and the last refusal. The source tests cover pull cancellation, insertion handoff, retention, and once-only queue transitions. Native prop lifetime, hook removal, and live all-state behavior still require runtime verification.

### The magazine's zone

Which part of the held gun the free hand is at decides what its grip takes (`reload/zones.rs`). The handover takes the gun by its pistol grip within a wide radius, wide enough to reach the bottom of a pistol's grip, where its magazine is, so a hand going for the magazine there took the gun instead. The space under the magazine well is therefore the magazine's zone: a cylinder down the well's axis from `ZONE.lip_m` (15 mm) inside the mouth to `ZONE.length_m` (0.24 m) below it, `ZONE.radius_m` (0.06 m) from the axis, in model metres at the player's scale. It exists while the gun has a magazine in a well the wells table places on the gun. A free hand in it is at the magazine: the handover is not offered to it, and the magazine target is in its reach wherever in the zone the hand is, so its grip starts the pull. Only a grab at the pistol grip above the well, or elsewhere in the handover's reach, hands the gun over. Without a magazine target (the physical pull unavailable for the gun, or a hand that has not yet opened its grip after a pull), a grab in the zone takes nothing rather than the gun. The grab view draws the zones on the held gun (see [grab-view.md](grab-view.md)).

## Load

The pouch (`POUCHES.magazine` in `controllers::weapons::volumes`) is one universal pouch at the front of the belt on the side of the hand without the gun, so that hand reaches it without crossing the body, whichever hand the gun rides ([controllers.md](controllers.md#sockets-pouches-the-foregrip-and-the-action-runs-tier) places it). It hands out whatever the gun in the other hand takes, and it is a grab volume for the hand without a gun while a reloaded weapon is held. A gun that loads a magazine or a cylinder takes one at any time, whether its own is in or out, though it seats only once the well is empty (see "Knocking the old magazine out"); a shotgun takes a shell and a launcher a rocket only while it has room for one. A grab with no ammunition left (and no unlimited ammunition) gives nothing and a faint pulse. While the pouch is not a grab target, the grip press log's notes say why (`magazine pouch: not offered, the gun is full` for a full shotgun or launcher, or no free hand, no gun reloaded by hand, no rounds left, a round already in hand, or no skeleton to place it on).

The hand holds the round as a magazine is held for loading: its feed end up the finger tube with its top `RELOAD.held_top_m` ahead of the closed fist, pointing the way the fist points, its back in the palm and its front (the gun's forward direction) out of it, and its centre line `RELOAD.held_palm_gap_m` out of the palm, since the closed fist's centre is about where Rico's palm is drawn (`insert.rs`, `render.rs`). A round that goes in along the gun's length (a rocket) turns its top out of the palm instead. The insertion check reads the top from the same line as the drawn round. The top catches the well when it comes within `RELOAD.capture_radius_m` (0.08 m: a little more than a magazine's own width, since nothing stops the hand at the well as a real gun would, and the hand and the well each carry a centimetre or two of tracking and placement error) of the well's mouth, turned no more than `RELOAD.capture_angle_deg` from the well's axis, and moving into the gun relative to the well. Consecutive top-to-well samples include hand rotation and gun movement. Shared transport does not count as insertion. A segment that crosses the mouth between ticks can catch, so a fast axial push does not skip the capture sphere. A caught round's visible part or retained body follows the well's axis at its measured depth. It seats `RELOAD.seat_depth_m` past the mouth only while lateral and angular capture remain valid. Sideways escape and withdrawal release the capture before the seat check. Letting go seats a round that remains captured and more than half seated. A round let go of with its top within `FLOAT.radius_m` (0.15 m, about a hand's breadth; twice the capture radius for a well whose capture radius is larger) of the mouth, short of the mouth's plane by more than the capture radius beside the gun, and turned no more than `FLOAT.angle_deg` (70°) from the axis floats in (`insert::Float`): its top travels from where the hand left it onto the axis without passing the mouth, then down the axis to the seat depth, turning onto the axis as it goes, eased in and out over the distance at `FLOAT.speed_mps` (0.8 m/s) within `FLOAT.min_s` and `FLOAT.max_s` (0.08 to 0.3 s), and seats when it arrives. It catches the well as it starts, with the catch's pulse, and drops if the gun stops taking it on the way. A magazine from the world is carried along the float by its own body, as it is carried to the hand, and a pouch round's part is drawn along it on the gun. Anything else let go of drops. A pouch round not captured within `RELOAD.timeout_s` drops. A magazine can be taken from the pouch only while its gun's magazine is out; a shell or a rocket requires a gun that is not full. The stocked belt source remains visible independently of this hand-take gate. [Body attachment interaction](attachments.md) describes the display offset, posture transport, and remaining runtime checks.

A seated magazine, cylinder, or rocket loads through the weapon's own reload ([`WeaponBase::Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Reload)), which keeps every rule of the game's: the reserve, unlimited ammunition, the upgrades, the rocket on the launcher, the magazine-out flag, and the HUD. A shell adds one round through [`WeaponBase::SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo) and takes it from the reserve through [`Inventory::SetAmmoCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-SetAmmoCount), unless the ammunition is unlimited. Pickups, crates, and cheats only touch the reserve, so they keep working; the game's reload-on-refill is one of the starts the detour refuses.

While a round is in hand, the gun's well is a touch target, marked gold at its mouth on the gun ([grip.md](grip.md#markers)): the round goes in as the hand brings it there, without a squeeze. The wrist summary shows `Push the Round into the Gold Ring on the Gun` on the hand holding it. A pouch magazine in the hand is drawn with the gun's spare magazine model: a second instance of the model the belt pouch draws ([pouch-models.md](pouch-models.md#the-magazine-in-the-hand)), separate from the gun, so the gun keeps its own magazine part, and the old magazine stays drawn in the gun until it is knocked, ejected, or pulled out. Pistols and SMGs, whose magazine is modelled into the body, show one in the hand the same way. The instance is held at the hand's grip with its insertion axis along the way the hand points, placed each rendered frame on the hand as it is drawn, and once the well catches it, it slides along the well in the gun's orientation, as it sits seated. Where the model's top is comes from the wells table (`spare_top_m`, from `tools/sights/extract_wells.py`; see [tools/sights/README.md](../../../tools/sights/README.md)). A seated magazine hides the instance, and the gun shows its own part again ([`WeaponComponentBase::Enable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-Enable)).

A round without such a model is the gun's own round part (`render::is_round_part`), shown again in the hand that took it and moved there by the late update of the gun's parts at the same pose, then hidden if the round is dropped with the gun's magazine still out (for a rocket, while the launcher is empty). That is the UPU-210's cartridge (its spare magazine prop is the empty cartridge), the launchers' rockets and the Hydra's pod, and a magazine gun with no spare magazine model, or with the pouches' models off (`weapons.pouches.models.enabled`). While such a gun still holds its magazine, its part is that magazine, so the fresh one shows nothing in the hand until the old one is out. A gun with neither a spare magazine model nor a magazine part (the Balloon Head pistol, the Automat-12) shows nothing in the hand.

### Knocking the old magazine out

A pouch magazine in the hand can knock the gun's own magazine out of the well, as a tactical reload is done in the films (`reload/knock.rs` for the test and the impulse, `reload/knocking.rs` for the input tick). While the well still holds a magazine, the fresh one is only held: it neither catches the well nor floats in, and a push at the occupied well does nothing (`InsertInputs::occupied`). Striking the inserted magazine with it, on its base or on its side near the well, faster than `knock.min_speed_mps` (1 m/s, in the debug UI's Weapons > Reload and gun action) knocks it out. A hand bringing a magazine to the gun moves at a few tenths of a metre per second and a deliberate strike at one and a half to three, so a gentle touch does nothing; the palms knock a loose object from the same speed ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)).

The pouch magazine in the hand has no body in the physics world, so the strike is a test between the held magazine's tip and the inserted magazine rather than a contact between two bodies. The inserted magazine is a capsule down the well's axis, `KNOCK.radius_m` (35 mm) from it, from `KNOCK.lip_m` (15 mm) inside the mouth to the magazine's base: for a magazine part, its length from the wells table (`part_length_m`) below the mouth; for a magazine modelled into the gun, whose mouth is its base, the mouth alone. The test runs in the well's moving frame, so the gun's own motion does not count, and it takes the line between two input ticks, so a fast strike does not pass through between them. The tip has to come into the capsule from outside, closing on its surface at the threshold speed: a tip already against the magazine, resting or sliding along it, never knocks. Only a magazine in a wells-table well is knocked out; a revolver's cylinder, and a well at the wrist fallback, are ejected or pulled.

A strike queues the old magazine's drop through the same serial request as an eject (`DetachKind::Knock`), with the eject's round accounting: with the magazines in the world on, it leaves as a physical magazine carrying its own magazine-owned rounds, none under unlimited ammunition, and with them off its rounds stay counted in the weapon for the next load to return to the reserve. A live chamber stays on the weapon. Nothing is loaded and the reserve is not touched: the fresh magazine stays in the hand and seats as any pouch magazine does once the well is empty, so the reserve pays for it alone when it seats. A drop the engine refuses (the world store full) leaves the old magazine in, with its rounds. `Event::MagazineKnock`, a full-strength thud, plays on both hands at the strike.

The knocked magazine's flight is the physics world's. The drop gives the prop the gun's velocity, as an eject does, and once the drop's result returns the strike adds an impulse at the struck point: the impulse a hand-driven body gives the point along the strike, with a restitution of `KNOCK.restitution` (0.3), so the point's speed along the strike changes by 1.3 times the tip's speed relative to the gun. The magazine's mass and inertia are its body's own, so a base strike sends it out along the well and a strike on the side near the well turns it as it goes; the change is capped at `KNOCK.max_knock_mps` (6 m/s) and `KNOCK.max_spin_radps`. The impulse waits up to `KNOCK.impulse_ticks` input ticks for the prop's body to be readable. A prop the world store does not retain (the magazines in the world off) is not the mod's to move, so it flies with the gun's velocity alone.

The fresh magazine is drawn in the hand while the old one is still in the gun, since it is an instance of the gun's spare magazine model rather than the gun's own part (see "Load"); the grab view draws the held tip (see below). With `zones` on, the grab view draws the knock zone on the held gun as a red axis with a ring at the top and a heavier ring at the base, the held tip as a dot that turns green while it moves fast enough to knock, and the zone flashing white at a knock ([grab-view.md](grab-view.md)).

The ways the old magazine leaves are the knock, the eject (button or flick), the pull by hand (see "Physical magazine removal" and "The magazine's zone"), and the belt reload, which drops it by itself as the gun is pushed onto the pouch, since there is no free hand to knock it with. All of them use the same drop and the same round accounting. A seat never drops a magazine. The pull needs a free hand, so it is not offered to the hand holding a fresh magazine; the eject still works then. A magazine picked up from the world is still offered only while the gun's magazine is out, so that a magazine lying near the player stays a loose object for the physical hands while the gun has one.

## The belt reload

Pushing a gun's well down onto the pouch reloads it in one hand (`belt.rs`), for every weapon reloaded by hand, whether or not a hand is free. The mouth has to enter the pouch moving down faster than `RELOAD.belt.min_push_speed_mps`, with the gun upright enough that its insertion axis leans no more than `RELOAD.belt.max_tilt_deg` from straight up, and then go `RELOAD.belt.depth_m` into it. An entry that misses any of these does nothing until the mouth leaves the pouch, and the belt waits `RELOAD.belt.after_draw_s` after a draw, so drawing from the hip holster beside the pouch never reloads. A magazine still in the gun drops as the fresh one goes in, so no eject is needed first (see "Knocking the old magazine out"). A belt reload works the action as well. The gun cannot fire from the push until the mouth leaves the pouch. A shotgun takes one shell per push.

The current wrist hint is limited to the dominant hand's eligible belt reload. The reload owner captures the insertion, extraction, draw-delay, well, and pouch facts after its steps, then reads the current gun's final ammunition state after weapon finish. A same-tick offer reaches the wrist only while the final gun claims still match and that physical hand is ready. The hint describes the push motion before it is performed; the existing speed, tilt, depth, and leave-before-retry checks still determine whether a push reloads. Pouch display models and native reload-button permission do not establish belt eligibility.

## The chamber

The game has no separate chamber inventory; its loaded-round count covers the available native rounds. The reload owner partitions that existing count into magazine-owned and chamber-owned rounds (`reload/ammo.rs`). While the input observation adopts an active partition, that partition owns chamber readiness. Persistent weapon records mirror it for action handling and later replacement seeding; queued shot events still drive lock-back and diagnostics but do not recompute that readiness. Weapons without an adopted partition retain the native-count fallback. Feeding moves one magazine round into a chamber without increasing the total. Retained extraction transfers only magazine-owned rounds and leaves a live chamber on the weapon. That chamber can fire once with the magazine absent; the shot callback consumes its ownership immediately, including when the native burst debit is deferred. No separate cartridge actor or additional ammunition is created.

The last shot empties the chamber, and reloading a dry gun leaves it empty until the action is worked. A tactical insertion preserves the chamber and returns only replaced magazine-owned rounds to reserve. Native full reload uses a temporary magazine-only count for its reserve calculation, then restores chamber ownership once. An unretained removed magazine remains in the native refund accounting but cannot feed or fire while absent. There is no slide-release button; the belt reload chambers by itself.

The action is worked with the hand without a gun. With `action.physical` on (the default), the action is a part that follows the hand through its travel (see "The physical action"). Off, it is a gesture:

- A slide or a charging handle: a grip press at the handle (a grip target while the chamber waits), a pull back along the gun's axis to `RELOAD.action.pull_fraction` of its travel, and a release or a push back home.
- A bolt: the same, but it has to be pushed back home.
- A pump: the hand already on the foregrip slides back and forward along the barrel.

The handle's place and travel come from the wells table; a weapon whose handle the table does not place is never held for its chamber.

A bolt-action rifle and a pump-action shotgun empty their chamber after every shot, and the game's own cycling is suppressed (see "The bolt and the pump"). A worked action loads the chamber when the magazine is in and holds a round. With the action a gesture, while a pistol's chamber is empty its slide is drawn back, and while it is worked it follows the hand: the game moves a slide back along its own axis after each shot and all the way while the magazine is empty ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength)), and the late update of the gun's parts adds the rest (`render.rs`, through `hands/parts.rs`).

A gun held by an empty chamber, an absent magazine without a live chamber, or the belt cannot fire: the input tick keeps its trigger from the game and plays `Event::DryFire` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)) at the pull's press edge, and the ammunition check clears the gun's shot as well against a buffered one (`empty.rs`). With the dual-wield split a held pistol's trigger is kept from the split, so the other pistol still fires.

## Working the action by hand

The slide, the charging handle, the bolt, and the pump are worked by hand only with `manual_action` on (debug UI, Weapons > Reload and gun action, "Work the action by hand"), which is off by default. Off, every plan's action is none (`ReloadPlan::with_manual_action`): a seated magazine chambers its first round, as a launcher's does, nothing waits on the action, no slide catches at the rear, and no handle is offered. The bolt rifle runs its update with its bolt-action setting cleared, as it does with the action worked by hand, so the game never plays its bolt act, and its bolt lock ([`SniperWeapon::m_HasFiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_HasFiredThisInput)) is cleared once the trigger falls to the weapon's hold threshold ([`WeaponTuning::m_trigger_sensitivity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_trigger_sensitivity)), so it fires once per pull. The pump-action shotgun keeps the game's own pump after every shot. The magazine is still loaded by hand, and launchers and revolvers are unchanged. With it on, everything in "The chamber", "The bolt and the pump", and "The physical action" applies.

## The bolt and the pump

The sniper rifle keeps its own bolt lock ([`SniperWeapon::m_HasFiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##field-m_HasFiredThisInput)), set by each shot, which blocks fire until the bolt animation runs. The rifle's update runs with its bolt-action setting cleared, so the game never queues the bolt act, and the lock keeps the rifle from firing until the player works the bolt; the worked bolt clears the lock. A reload by hand does not raise the reloading state that would clear the lock, so a rifle reloaded after running dry still needs its bolt worked.

The pump-action shotgun's pump is queued on every shot by its do-act component ([`DoActWeaponComponent::Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::DoActWeaponComponent##func-Fire_Start)), on the holder and on the gun's own skeleton. The component's fire start is skipped for the local player, and nothing in the game stops the next shot, so the chamber does. The worked pump plays the gun's pump clip ([`WeaponData::DoAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-DoAct)).

## The physical action

With `action.physical` on, each gun's action is a part on its travel (`travel.rs`, worked in `action.rs`): a place from home to the rear as a share of its travel, and for a bolt a turn up as a share of its lift. Each gun keeps its own, remembered with its chamber across weapon changes.

- Taking it. The handle is `GrabVolume::WeaponAction` at the part's current position, within `RELOAD.action.grab_radius_m` (0.105 m): a closed hand feeling along the gun lands a few centimetres off the handle, and the 2 November 2026 session's misses were 13 and 23 mm outside the 0.07 m it had, after which the hand closed on the foregrip or the stock. While the gun waits on its action (its chamber empty and its magazine in with rounds), a hand within that reach that holds nothing takes the handle first, over the foregrip, the gun's grip, and the inserted magazine (`chamber::prefer_waiting_action`). The free hand can take a supported slide or bolt even when the gun is ready and closed. The pump follows its held foregrip in the same ready state. A charging handle remains available only for an empty chamber or an action out of battery. Revolvers, launchers, break actions, and unknown handle placements do not gain ready-rack support. The volume uses `regions::action_volume` ([physical-guns.md, "Grabbing a gun anywhere"](physical-guns.md#grabbing-a-gun-anywhere)). Insertion, extraction, and a pending magazine eject exclude a new action claim. An existing action grip remains exclusive against magazine, brace, and transfer targets.
- Following the hand. Held, the part follows the hand along the gun's axis from wherever along it the hand took hold, between home and the rear: it never jumps to the hand, and the hand can pull no further than the travel. A hand that strays more than `RELOAD.action.release_distance_m` off the axis, or lets go of its grip (of the foregrip, for the pump), lets go of the part.
- Let go. A slide or a charging handle runs home on its spring, over `RELOAD.action.spring_s` from the rear. A pump stays where it is let go, and settles home once forward of `RELOAD.action.return_fraction`. A bolt stays where it is.
- The bolt. A bolt is turned up about its axis (`RELOAD.action.bolt_lift_deg`, the handle `RELOAD.action.bolt_radius_m` from the axis, on the side the table places it) before it slides; it counts as up past `RELOAD.action.lift_fraction` of the turn. It cannot be turned down until it is home, and a bolt not turned down is out of battery.
- The rack. Reaching `RELOAD.action.pull_fraction` extracts the chambered round and plays `Event::Rack` on the working hand. A ready chamber with a magazine in discards exactly one native loaded round at this point. Holding the action at the rear, losing tracking, or reacquiring it does not discard another round. An empty chamber discards nothing. Coming home past `RELOAD.action.return_fraction`, and turning a bolt down, cycles the action and marks one remaining loaded round ready. An empty or absent magazine cannot feed. The bolt rifle's lock clears, and `Event::Chambered` plays when a round is ready. A short pull before extraction preserves the round and does not cycle. The gun's own pump or bolt clip is not played over physical travel.
- The slide's catch. The shot that empties a pistol's magazine leaves its slide caught at the rear, as does a weapon first seen with an empty magazine, and a slide pulled to the rear and let go over a magazine that is in and empty catches there too. A caught slide stays back through an eject and a fresh magazine; taking it frees it from the catch, and letting go runs it home, which chambers from the new magazine (a rack). With no magazine in, the slide does not catch. The game holds a pistol's slide back on its own while the magazine is empty, so a slide that the travel has home is drawn home against that hold. There is no slide-release button: every button of the gun hand is taken on foot, so the slide is released by a rack, and the belt reload closes the action by itself.
- Out of battery. A gun whose part is back past `RELOAD.action.return_fraction`, or whose bolt is turned up, does not fire, whatever its chamber.

Physical extraction writes through [`equipment::weapon::WeaponBase::SetMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetMagazineAmmo), so the native HUD follows the loaded count. It does not call [`equipment::weapon::WeaponBase::ConsumeAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ConsumeAmmo), which also advances firing burst state and can consume reserve ammunition. The existing consume detour belongs to safe-grenade activation; reload adds no competing hook. It also does not call [`equipment::weapon::WeaponBase::Reload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Reload), whose refill can replenish the loaded count from reserve. A physical rack never refunds ammunition or replenishes the gun. There is no recoverable ejected-round prop. Current ammo is read after insertion and belt work rather than from the earlier input snapshot.

The part is drawn where the travel has it:

- A separate part (the pistols' slides, and the U-SV 45's `ejection_port`): the late update of the gun's parts moves it back along its own axis by its share of the game's own travel for it ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_RecoilMaxLength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_RecoilMaxLength)), less what the game holds it back by on an empty magazine ([`BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent::m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BarrelRecoilWeaponComponent##field-m_MaxRecoilOnEmptyAmmo)), so the game's kick after a shot still shows. The U-SV 45's part is a cover beside the bore, so it slides with the bolt's travel but does not turn.
- A bone of the gun's skeleton (the U-Rys 96's pump, [`WeaponData::PUMP_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData), and the CS111 Archangel's and the Final Argument's bolt, [`WeaponData::SLIDER_BONE_HASH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData)): a post-call detour on the gun's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)) moves the bone's model-space joint back along the model's +Z by its share of the table's travel and turns it about the model's Z by the bolt's lift, over the pose the gun's clips gave it (`bones.rs`). The update runs on the simulation's workers for every character's guns; anything but the reloaded weapons' guns is turned away before a lock. A part at home is left to the clips.
- Nothing (the SMGs' and rifles' charging handles, part of the body mesh): the travel, its stops, and its rack act, and nothing on the gun moves.

While the pump is back, the foregrip moves back along the barrel with it (`foregrip::set_pump_slide`), so the drawn off-hand rides the pump. With the hand bodies on, the off-hand on the foregrip is drawn at its body, joined to the gun body where it took the foregrip, so the drawn hand stays at the brace while the pump moves.

The log has, at info level, `reload: the action cycled` with the action, the gun hand, and whether a round was chambered; `reload: the last shot locked the slide back`; `reload: the slide caught at the rear on an empty magazine`; and `reload: took the slide off its catch`. At debug level, `reload: took the action's handle`. The debug UI's Status page shows `LEFT ACTION` and `RIGHT ACTION` under `WEAPONS IN HAND`: the action and where its part is (`HOME`, `BACK 40%`, `UP 100%, BACK 30%`, or `LOCKED BACK`), `HELD` while a hand holds it, the chamber, and the actions worked. The scenario signals are `actions.worked`, `actions.locked_back`, `actions.held`, and `actions.travel` (`testing/scenarios/physical_actions.toml`).

## Each hand's gun

Every gun in a hand is reloaded on its own, whichever hand holds it and whether the game wields it or it is armed in its hand ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The per-hand tick"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-per-hand-tick)): its plan, its well, its magazine, its chamber, and its action are its own weapon's. The shots of each weapon are counted against it, each hand's gun is held from firing on its own weapon's state, and the game's own reload is stopped while either weapon is reloaded by hand. A tap of the reload button ejects the driest of the two guns, and a second tap the other. The pouch is placed only for a hand holding nothing, so with a gun in each hand no round is taken by hand: a flick of a hand ejects its gun, and the belt push loads it.

A single pistol ([physical-weapons.md, "Separate pistols"](physical-weapons.md#separate-pistols)) is a weapon of its own with one gun, and is reloaded as any other: its magazine is its own, half the tuned size rounded up, which the reload reads as its capacity, and its reserve is the character's. The game's own reload fills it to the tuned size, so a load through the game's reload is brought down to the half at once, the rounds over it going back to the reserve. The game's pair, while the pistols are not made separate, is one weapon of one magazine with a gun in each hand: each hand's eject drops that gun's magazine prop and takes the pair's magazine out, and a load fills the pair.

A weapon is remembered by its item in the loadout table when the table holds it, and otherwise by its object (`records.rs`). Item-keyed chamber and action values can survive replacement of the native object. The pre-reset boundary discards object-address-only records and releases the ammunition ledger's native owners outside its locks. A replacement seeds its ammunition partition from its current native total and any surviving item values. A gun's chamber and action are kept by the gun's place in its weapon rather than by the hand, so a weapon handed to the other hand keeps them.

## Haptics

Each step plays a cue of the weapons category ([haptics.md](haptics.md)): the eject on the gun hand (`Event::Eject`), a round taken from the pouch (`Event::PouchTake`), an empty pouch (`Event::PouchEmpty`), a round caught by the well and a seat on both hands (`Event::WellCatch`, `Event::MagazineSeat`), a belt seat on the gun hand (`Event::BeltSeat`), a full pull of the action on the working hand (`Event::Rack`), the physical action's part meeting a stop (home, a bolt turned all the way up or down) on the working hand, or on the gun hand for a slide run home by its spring or caught at the rear (`Event::ActionDetent`), and a chambered round on the gun hand (`Event::Chambered`). A seat that leaves the gun able to fire with nothing left to work (a launcher, a revolver, a gun whose handle the table does not place, or a tactical reload with the chamber still loaded) plays `Event::Chambered` on the gun hand `RELOAD.ready_cue_delay_s` after the seat's bump, so the bump alone means the action still waits. The dry fire is `Event::DryFire`, played by the weapon handling's trigger refusal ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)) with the reason the reload holds the gun (`weapons::reload::fire_hold`), once per pull.

## The readout

The Weapons > Reload and gun action live status opens with the `LEFT ACTION`, `RIGHT ACTION`, and `MAGAZINES` status rows ([status.md](../status.md)), then shows each reloaded weapon's name hash, its load and action, whether its well came from the table, the magazine and the reserve (the pair's once), each gun hand's chamber, belt push, fire hold (with its reason), and turning speed, the round in hand and its distance from the mouth, whether a gun hand's physical action has racked (the rows have how far back, a bolt's turn, held, and locked back) or the gesture's work, the recent reload events, and the counters (ejects, knocks, seats, belt reloads, slides locked back, empty pouch grabs, and the game's reload starts refused).

4. Strike the side of the magazine near the well: it leaves spinning. Pick it up: it holds the rounds that were left in it.
5. Strike while swinging the rifle toward the hand, then swing the rifle and hand together without a strike: only the first knocks. Note the speeds that knock and the ones that do not, against `knock.min_speed_mps`, and whether 1 m/s is too eager or too stiff.
6. Repeat with a pistol and an SMG, whose zone is a ball at the bottom of the grip, and check that the revolver's cylinder still needs an eject.
7. Turn the magazines in the world off: the knocked magazine drops with the gun's velocity alone.
8. With unlimited ammunition on, knock a magazine out: `MAGAZINES` lists it with no rounds.

## In the headset: the magazine in the hand

1. With a rifle drawn and its magazine still in, take a magazine from the pouch: the fresh magazine shows in the hand, top ahead of the fist, and the old one stays drawn in the gun. Knock the old one out: it flies out while the fresh one stays in the hand. Seat the fresh one: the one in the hand goes, and the gun shows its magazine.
2. Draw a pistol, then an SMG, and take a magazine from the pouch with the magazine in and with it ejected: a magazine shows in the hand each time, sitting square in the fist rather than offset from it. Note any weapon whose magazine sits off the hand.
3. Look at the belt during all of this: the pouch's magazine is still drawn, and stays drawn after an eject.
4. Eject a rifle's magazine and at once take a fresh one from the pouch, before the game respawns the gun's spare magazine: the magazine still shows in the hand. Drop it and take another during the same gap: it shows again.
5. Take a cartridge for the UPU-210: the full cartridge shows in the hand (the gun's own part), as before.
6. With the debug UI's Weapons > Inventory and body slots open, check that the readout lists the magazine in the hand while one is held, and the log has a line naming each held gun without a spare magazine model.

## In the headset: the driest-first eject

1. Hold a pistol in each hand (separate pistols), fire the left one down to a few rounds, and tap the reload button: the left magazine drops, whichever hand is dominant. Tap again: the right one drops.
2. With both guns equally full, tap once: the dominant hand's magazine drops. Double tap quickly: both drop, the second without waiting for the first to land.
3. With one gun in one hand and its magazine already out, tap: nothing drops, and a held press still holsters.
