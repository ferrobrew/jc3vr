# Swim locomotion

How the game moves and turns a swimming character. The swim family is a separate locomotion pipeline that never touches the on-foot orientation machinery ([`NStateTask_LocoUtil::EvaluateCharacterOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterOrientation), the target-face-dir blackboard, `GetAimMoveAngle`), and its body yaw turns in act-sized steps rather than continuously.

## The state tasks

Swimming runs on an input and a movement state task per stratum: [`NStateTask_InputSurfaceSwimTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::NStateTask_InputSurfaceSwimTask##func-Update) and [`NStateTask_MovementSurfaceSwimTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::NStateTask_MovementSurfaceSwimTask##func-Update) at the surface, [`NStateTask_InputUnderWaterSwimTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::NStateTask_InputUnderWaterSwimTask##func-Update) and [`NStateTask_MovementUnderWaterSwimTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::NStateTask_MovementUnderWaterSwimTask##func-Update) underwater. The movement tasks delegate to the cores [`UpdateSurfaceMovement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::swim##func-UpdateSurfaceMovement) (the player only, since the surface task branches on player or NPC), [`UpdateSurfaceMovementNPC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::swim##func-UpdateSurfaceMovementNPC), and [`UpdateUnderwaterMovement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::swim##func-UpdateUnderwaterMovement).

## Swim speed

The stick sets only the direction, normalized: both cores take the horizontal magnitude of the animation's raw root velocity ([`AnimationController::GetRawRootVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetRawRootVelocity)) as the speed, so a swimmer goes as fast as the playing clip moves its root. With the stick at rest, the surface core keeps that speed along the measured world velocity, so the clip's own slowing is the glide.

The input task turns the stick's magnitude into acts. At or above [`CharacterSwimSettings::m_SurfaceSwimFullDeflection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::CharacterSwimSettings##field-m_SurfaceSwimFullDeflection), a swimmer not already starting or crawling gets the forward start act (`ACT_SURFACE_SWIM_START_0`), whose clip accelerates into the crawl; a full stick reaches about 4 m/s in about a second and a half. Below it, the task queues the crawl act (`ACT_SURFACE_SWIM_CRAWL`) until the stick has stayed under a small fraction of full deflection (in the field's def) for [`m_SurfaceSwimNoInputToStopTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::CharacterSwimSettings##field-m_SurfaceSwimNoInputToStopTime), then the idle act. A partial stick therefore queues only the crawl act, and a recording with the stick held between a third and two thirds for several seconds stays at a few centimetres per second, which suggests the state machine takes the crawl only through the start act (unverified: the transitions are in the shipped state machine, and the threshold's value is in a `.bin` resource, not the binary).

The surface core scales its velocity by its agreement with the move direction (the dot product, floored at zero), adds buoyancy, the waves' push, and the transition speed, and writes it to the proxy's [`CharacterInput::wantedVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-wantedVelocity) in the frame of the orientation it then passes to `SetOrientation` ([the capsule offset](#the-capsule-offset)). The proxy's [`GetTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-GetTransform) reads that orientation back at once, and [`Character::SetWantedWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetWantedWorldVelocity) converts a world velocity with it.

## Turn dispatch

The input tasks measure the signed XZ angle from the body's forward to a desired direction with [`ControllerUtility::GetDeltaAngleFromOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::controller_utility::ControllerUtility##func-GetDeltaAngleFromOrientation). The desired direction is the camera-relative move direction (the stick through `GameCameraManager::GetInputMatrix`) while moving, the direction to the aim target for an armed idle at the surface, and the direction to the grapple or explosive target while using either. The angle picks an act, not a turn rate: below [`SWIM_TURN_ACT_MIN_ANGLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::SWIM_TURN_ACT_MIN_ANGLE), the forward start act; from there up to [`SWIM_TURN_ACT_MAX_ANGLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::SWIM_TURN_ACT_MAX_ANGLE), a 120-degree turn clip (`ACT_SURFACE_SWIM_START_120R/L` while moving, `ACT_SURFACE_IDLE_QUICKTURN_120R/L` for the armed idle, `ACT_UW_SWIM_IDLE_TURN_120R/L` underwater). A latched turn act writes the angle to the blackboard's float key [`ObjectBlackboard::SWIM_TURN_ANGLE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::SWIM_TURN_ANGLE_ID) and stores the current forward and the desired direction in the swim action parameters (`m_AnimatedTurnBaseDir`, `m_AnimatedTurnTargetDir`).

## Orientation

The movement cores own the heading. `UpdateSurfaceMovement` takes one of two exclusive paths each update:

1. The animated turn. While the angle-correction animation segment plays, the heading is [`SlerpAroundAxis`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics##func-SlerpAroundAxis)`(m_AnimatedTurnBaseDir, m_AnimatedTurnTargetDir, t)`, with `t` the fraction of the turn taken from the segment's local time, so the latched turn plays out across the clip window and stops. This path skips the rest.
2. The continuous rotation. Otherwise [`DoRotate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics##func-DoRotate) turns the forward toward a desired direction by at most [`CharacterSwimSettings::m_SurfaceSwimFaceDirSlerpSpeed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::CharacterSwimSettings##field-m_SurfaceSwimFaceDirSlerpSpeed) times [`SWIM_TURN_RATE_SCALE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::SWIM_TURN_RATE_SCALE) times `dt` degrees per update (measured at about 69°/s, since the setting is in a `.bin` resource). The desired direction is the world move direction from `TransformInputDirToWorldDir` while moving, the aim target while aiming or planting an explosive (at the flat [`SWIM_AIM_TURN_RATE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::swim::SWIM_AIM_TURN_RATE)), the grapple target while reeling, or, with the stick neutral, the current forward, so a swimmer with no input never rotates.

The forward is blended with the water-surface plane for roll (`UpdateSurfacePlane`, not yet located) and assembled with [`Matrix4::CreateOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/types::math::Matrix4##func-CreateOrientation).

`UpdateUnderwaterMovement` has the same two paths, with three differences. It builds the move direction inline instead of through `TransformInputDirToWorldDir`, rotating the local `(forward, 0, -lateral)` by the camera's full input matrix, so the camera's pitch is baked in and looking up or down while pushing forward ascends or descends. That direction stays three-dimensional, and `DoRotate`'s output is both the facing and, scaled, the velocity, so the swimmer moves where it faces. Its rate is a fixed `SWIM_TURN_RATE_SCALE` times `dt` degrees per update, slower than the surface rate.

`DoRotate` has three call sites: the surface and underwater rotations and the velocity slerp in [`ProcessMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics##func-ProcessMotion). It scales its step by the remaining angle over a fixed threshold angle (on its def), capped at one, times the rate, and does not clamp the step to the remaining angle, so a rate above the threshold per call overshoots and one above twice the threshold diverges. [`ControllerUtility::TransformInputDirToWorldDir`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::controller_utility::ControllerUtility##func-TransformInputDirToWorldDir) is a general input helper with fifteen call sites (locomotion target direction, jump, melee, grapple hang and reel-in, fall steering, aim target updates, a state-machine condition, and AI steering override), two of them swim.

## The capsule offset

Before the orientation reaches the physics body, the core composes it with a swimming capsule offset chosen per frame, all three built in the [`SwimActionParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::swim_action_params::SwimActionParams) constructor (the idle threshold and exact angles are on the fields):

| Pose | Offset | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Surface, wanted horizontal speed at or below the idle threshold | [`m_SurfaceIdleSwimmingCapsuleOffset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::swim_action_params::SwimActionParams##field-m_SurfaceIdleSwimmingCapsuleOffset) | identity |
| Surface, moving | [`m_SurfaceCrawlSwimmingCapsuleOffset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::swim_action_params::SwimActionParams##field-m_SurfaceCrawlSwimmingCapsuleOffset) | a pitch about X of exactly a quarter turn |
| Underwater | [`m_UwSwimmingCapsuleOffset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::swim_action_params::SwimActionParams##field-m_UwSwimmingCapsuleOffset) | a steep pitch about X, less than a quarter turn, and a small downward translation |

It orthonormalizes the result, copies the matching inverse to `m_CurrentSwimmingCapsuleOffsetInv`, and calls [`PfxCharacterInstance::SetOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-SetOrientation), which sets the body's rotation and zeroes its angular velocity. The matrix `SetOrientation` receives is therefore the capsule frame, not the facing frame: for the crawl and underwater poses it is pitched steeply (the crawl exactly at gimbal lock for a yaw, pitch, and roll decomposition), and a heading is recovered from it only through `m_CurrentSwimmingCapsuleOffsetInv`.
