# Weapon firing and the weapon's user

The aim point, the shot direction, and which gun of several fires are in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md), the aim state in [aim-state.md](aim-state.md), and the ammunition check and the reload in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md).

## The weapon's user

Firing keys off the weapon's user, [`WeaponBase::m_User`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_User), and the two flags derived from it, [`m_IsUserLocalPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserLocalPlayer) and [`m_IsUserPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserPlayer). No step of the fire path reads the weapon's state ([`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)), the user's selected slot, or the user's wielded weapon: an updated holstered weapon with a user fires as that user's, with heat, the AI's gunshot event, bullet ownership and player flag, muzzle effect, sound, recoil, and rumble intact. The character's own inputs are the user's state bits (read by the ammunition check), the player's aim mode (the recoil and scatter scaling), the player aim control's weapon target (the bullets' lead), and the user's heat and AI entity; the ammunition check's reloads act on the user's wielded weapon. Besides the fire path, the user is read by [`IsCharacterUsingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-IsCharacterUsingWeapon), by `SetAimModeBase` (the grapple-hang hint), and by `StopUsingBase` (the mounted-gun teardown).

[`WeaponBase::StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing) registers the weapon for its pre-simulation update; sets the user and the two flags (the local one from [`Character::IsLocalPlayerControlled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsLocalPlayerControlled)); for a local user adds the weapon's camera hint, [`m_CameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_CameraHash) (or [`m_MountedCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedCameraHash) while mounted), unless already active; records the use statistic; and seeds [`m_AimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimTargetPosition) from the user's aim target ([`Character::m_AimTargetPositionWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AimTargetPositionWeapons)) or a point ahead. For a player user it then leaves precision aim when the weapon lacks [`m_SupportsPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SupportsPrecisionAim), and passes the player's mode to the weapon's [`SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetAimMode). It never changes the state. The equip virtual calls it, as does the inventory's add for weapons in use while holstered ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md#the-inventorys-add)). The weapon's per-tick update from its user ([`UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase)) checks no state either: it stores the aim point, updates the next gun with the inputs, and updates every gun's attached effects.

The stop-using virtual ([`WeaponBase::StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing), base [`StopUsingBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StopUsingBase)) stops fire on every gun if firing, stops the components, clears every gun's burst and three of its effects, and for a local user records the statistic, removes the use hint by its hash, and returns the weapon to normal aim, which removes its precision hints. It tears down a mounted gun (out of the heavy slot, a valid weapon selected), clears the user, its flags, and `m_InputBufferTimer`, and keeps the pre-simulation update while effects are active. A weapon wielding or dual-wielding outside the heavy slot gets [`E_WS_HOLSTERED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_HOLSTERED) written straight into its state, bypassing [`SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState)'s physics-world and visibility changes; it pokes each gun's object through its virtual table instead.

## The gate

The per-gun fire update, [`WeaponBase::UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel), does nothing unless the weapon has a live user (its control block's use count is non-zero), [`m_LieAboutUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_LieAboutUser) is set, or the weapon is in the [`E_WS_TURRET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_TURRET) state. Past that, placement rules go by slot, not state: the wall block applies to the dual-wield, two-handed, and two-handed-special slots, and the aim-angle check skips the hand grenade, the planted explosive, and the beacon.

## Trigger, interval, and the forced shots

With the primary input and the weapon's tuning ([`m_Tuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Tuning)):

- [`m_IsTriggerPulled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsTriggerPulled) is the input past the first of the tuning's [`m_trigger_sensitivity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_trigger_sensitivity) values, or past the lower second once pulled. [`m_IsFeatheringTrigger`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFeatheringTrigger) is any input above zero. A pull resets the burst and the predicted reload's idle time; a release clears [`m_FiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_FiredThisInput).
- The semi-automatic rule (a weapon without [`m_fully_automatic`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fully_automatic) fires once per pull) applies only while `m_IsUserLocalPlayer` is set; for any other user a held trigger keeps firing.
- [`m_ForceFireTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireTimer), [`m_ForceFireCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireCount), and [`m_InputBufferTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InputBufferTimer) force a shot request while positive, and so does a gun with burst shots left ([`WeaponData::m_BurstShotsLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BurstShotsLeft)). [`m_IsFiringCancelled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFiringCancelled) blocks until release.
- The wall block: [`m_IsWeaponPenetratingWallIfInAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsWeaponPenetratingWallIfInAim) without [`m_IsLeanOverFirePossible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsLeanOverFirePossible) blocks the shot and sets `m_InputBufferTimer` to [`WeaponBase::INPUT_BUFFER_TIME`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase::INPUT_BUFFER_TIME), so the shot fires if the block clears in time. The flags are written only by [`WallPenetrationWeaponComponent::UpdateWeapon_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WallPenetrationWeaponComponent##func-UpdateWeapon_Serial) for weapons a player holds, from a box collided from the holder's animated shoulders or hips toward the barrel, and are as fresh as that serial update. A wall hit also clears [`m_AimingWeaponIsPossible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimingWeaponIsPossible) unless the component allows aiming while penetrating.
- The aim-angle check, [`IsTargetWithinAimAngle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-IsTargetWithinAimAngle), compares the barrel with the direction to `m_AimTargetPosition` outside barrel fire-direction mode; failure clears and buffers the shot. It is bypassed by `m_LieAboutUser`, by the user's `ShouldIgnoreFiringAngle`, and by a per-weapon `m_IgnoreAimAngleRestriction` flag; the last two, and the weapon's fire-angle field, are unverified.
- Then come the ammunition check ([`CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered), [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#the-ammunition-check)), the fire-pattern update, a fire-delay timer that blocks while positive, and a spin-up time (the current and the required input time for fire); the delay and spin-up fields are unverified.
- A shot fires when the request stands and [`m_TimeSinceLastFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_TimeSinceLastFire) has reached the tuning's [`m_fire_rate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fire_rate) interval ([`m_fire_rate_level_2`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fire_rate_level_2) with the fire-rate upgrade, when the holder is the player), calling [`Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Start) on the gun being updated. With no request while firing, [`Fire_Stop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Stop) runs on every gun.
- [`m_ForceFireProjectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireProjectile) fires every gun once and clears the shot.
- The secondary input starts and stops a secondary fire through two further virtuals (names unverified); the inventory's fan-out always passes zero for it.

All of this state is the weapon's, only the burst per gun ([aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md#which-gun-fires)).

## What a shot does

[`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) runs once per shot, from the projectile override of each weapon class:

- It consumes a round when the weapon is locally owned, and registers the weapon for its pre-simulation update ([`m_RequiresPreSimUpdates`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RequiresPreSimUpdates)).
- It resets the fire timers and applies the recoil, scaled for the first shot of a pull and, when the user's controller is the player in precision aim ([`Player::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimMode)), by the precision factor. Scatter and overheat accumulate.
- It plays the gun's muzzle flash and shot sound ([`WeaponData::PlayFireEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-PlayFireEffect)), the local variant by `m_IsUserLocalPlayer`.
- For the local player it logs the shot's statistics, and the vehicle-weapon action events when a vehicle holds the weapon.
- It sets [`m_DeferredPhysicalRecoil`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredPhysicalRecoil) when [`m_PhysicalRecoilStrength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PhysicalRecoilStrength) is non-zero, and [`m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast) on the first shot of a pull.
- It runs the rumble ([`RumbleFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-RumbleFire)) and the camera shake, both for the local player only, and advances the gun's fire-point cursor and [`m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire).
- It adds heat (the wanted level) to the user through [`Character::AddHeatPoints`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-AddHeatPoints), with the firing entry of the heat manager's tuning ([`HeatManager::GetHeatPointData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/heat_manager::HeatManager##func-GetHeatPointData)), unless [`m_NoHeatOnFiring`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NoHeatOnFiring) is set, the user's faction is hostile to the default faction, or the weapon is the planted explosive. In a restricted area, a user in a vehicle a player drives (or drove recently) also heats the local player.

## The gunshot the AI hears

The weapon's pre-simulation update ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePreSim)), with `m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast` set and `m_NoHeatOnFiring` clear, clears the flag, takes the muzzle of the gun that fired last ([`m_LastWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_LastWeaponToFire)), and queues an [`EAwareness_WeaponFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ai::ai::AwarenessEvent##variant-EAwareness_WeaponFire) event there through [`BroadcastAwarenessEvent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ai::ai::BroadcastAwarenessEvent), with the user's AI entity ([`Character::GetAiEntityHandle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetAiEntityHandle)) as the source. While `m_NoHeatOnFiring` is set the flag stays pending. The queue is a fixed-capacity buffer with an atomic counter, safe from the parallel passes; overflow is dropped.

The fire path's only other AI-facing output is the heat (whether stealth reads more is unverified); bullet impacts broadcast their own event (not bound).

## Bullets

[`BulletWeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BulletWeaponBase##func-Fire_Projectile) builds the shot matrix, takes the holder's details ([`WeaponBase::GetHolderInformation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetHolderInformation), from [`m_Holder`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Holder)), runs `Fire_ProjectileBase`, and spawns the bullet:

- The bullet's owner is `m_User`, falling back to `m_Holder`, and its damage-source state comes from that owner when it is a character ([`Character::GetCurrentDamageSourceStateInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetCurrentDamageSourceStateInfo)).
- The bullet's player flag, and its tracer's, is `m_IsUserPlayer`; the first bullet of a shot also takes `m_IsUserLocalPlayer`.
- For a local user it sets the bullet's target point from the aim point along the shot direction, led by the velocity of the player aim control's weapon target ([`PlayerAimControl::m_Target`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_Target)) when there is one, or far out along the shot when there is none. That is the player's one weapon target, whichever weapon fires. How the bullet uses the point is unverified.

The holder's details are its faction, its network peer, whether it is a player, and the bodies the bullets ignore: a character holder names itself, and a vehicle holder names the vehicle and its driver. A bullet's ray filter skips those bodies until its first penetration, so a character's bullets pass through it. Without a user or holder, bullets are unowned, and without a holder they ignore no bodies (from the code, unverified in play).

## The shooting permission

[`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsShooting) is [`m_DoesAnimationAllowShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowShooting) with [`SB_DISABLE_SHOOT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_SHOOT) clear. It gates only the inventory's fan-out ([`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel) and its serial counterpart [`UpdateWeapons_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Serial)), which zeroes the primary input ([`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs)) while it is false, and the grenade input unless [`m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades) is set and [`SB_DISABLE_USE_GRENADES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_USE_GRENADES) is clear. Feathering comes from the same input, so a blocked weapon cannot raise either. No weapon reads the permission (of the inventory permissions only the reload one is read inside, by the ammunition check's empty-magazine branch), so a weapon updated directly bypasses it.

The fan-out ticks the mounted heavy-slot gun alone in the mounted-gun state; otherwise, with a boost wingsuit in use (the character has one, [`Character::HasBoostWingsuit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasBoostWingsuit), and flies the wingsuit), the wingsuit's guns, each with its own fire input, with the grenade input zeroed and the wielded weapon not updated; otherwise the wielded weapon, with the wingsuit's guns stopped; then the selected grenade. For the player, a riding state ([`Character::IsInRidingInVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInRidingInVehicleState), which covers the seats) skips the wielded weapon unless the ride is a motorcycle, and skips the grenade in any riding state.

[`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPermissions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPermissions) recomputes the permissions each tick, trying its rules in order: while pre-reeling or reeling in without the `shoot_while_reeling_in` upgrade, shooting is cleared; otherwise while the grappling hook has a live wire ([`GrapplingHook::m_ActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveWire)), every permission is cleared; otherwise while hanging from or reeled in to a grapple, only the pickup permission is cleared. No rule reads the vehicle, wingsuit, parachute, or fall states. It also clears the grenade permission and two others while the wielded weapon's precision hint is active. Each permission is then ANDed with the inverse of its disable bit, and the disable bits, per-frame requests a state task sets during the animation update, are cleared.

The setter is the `NSetInventoryPermissions` state task. Its `Flags` property maps bit by bit to the disable bits ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_TranslatePermissionsFlagToStateBit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_TranslatePermissionsFlagToStateBit)), and [`NSetInventoryPermissions_SetPermissions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_SetPermissions) ORs them into the animated model's current state-bit words, either for the whole state ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_NStateScope_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_NStateScope_Update), every update) or within a named clip segment ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_NSegmentScope_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_NSegmentScope_Update), with a `Time` and a segment name besides the flags).

In Rico's base graph (`rico_base.afsmb`, decoded as in [animation-graph.md](../character/animation-graph.md)), just under half the states carry a permission task with the shoot flag set:

- vehicle entry, exit, door, hatch, stunt-position, pull-out, and explosive-on-vehicle states, for the whole state or within `DISABLE_WEAPON_ACTION_SEG` segments;
- death, dying, ragdoll, get-up, crash, and the hit-reaction flights and stumbles;
- the grapple fire and tether fire states on foot, in the air, attached, upside down, swimming, and in the running strafes (some within `SEG_NO_WEAPON_HANDLING`), and the grapple's reel to a vehicle, jump away, release, and pre-reel states (some within `SEG_NO_SHOOT`);
- free fall, fast fall, the boost wingsuit's rolls, evades, transitions, and crash recoveries, and the wingsuit's flinches and exits;
- the parachute opening (`SEG_NO_SHOOT`), underwater swimming and dives, and the mounted-gun entry, exit, and pickup states;
- grenade throws (`SEG_NO_WEAPON_FIRE` on foot, the whole state while hanging or upside down), melee (`SEG_NO_ATTACK`, and the stunt melee's whole state), and the weapon pickups (`NO_SHOOT_SEGMENT` on `S_IDLE_PICKUP`, `S_MOVE_FWD_PICKUP`, and `S_MOVE_BWD_PICKUP`);
- `S_LAND_FAST_FALL`, `S_STUNT_BACKFLIP_JUMP_EXIT_VEHICLE_DETACHED`, and a few hang transitions.

No draw, holster, weapon-switch, or reload state of the base graph carries one; those block firing, for every weapon the character uses, through the state bits the ammunition check reads (`SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON`, `SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON`, `SB_IS_RELOADING`, and `SB_IS_NO_FIRING`). The upper-body and weapon partial graphs were not examined for permission tasks and may carry more.

### The permission by state

With the upper-body graph (the partial sync and additive graphs carry no shoot request) and the code rules, the permission holds in the seated idles (`S_IDLE_DRIVER_VEHICLE` and `S_IDLE_PASSENGER_VEHICLE`) and the seat-to-seat moves (though in a car nothing wielded is updated: the fan-out skips the wielded weapon when riding and every seat state unequips it); in the wingsuit glide (`S_JC_WINGSUIT_IDLE`) and most openings, while `S_JC_WINGSUIT_TO_FALL` and most landings withhold it; in `S_FALLING`; in `S_JC_PARACHUTE_IDLE` and its upper-body idle, while the upper-body grapple fire, tether, cancel, and grenade throw states withhold it for the whole state; and in the grapple's attached, hang, and upside-down idles and rotations. Most vehicle entries and exits, the door and bay transitions, the stunt-position moves, pulling an occupant out, and planting or triggering explosives on a vehicle withhold it, as do the other states listed above.

## Aim mode and camera hints

Each weapon keeps its own aim mode, [`WeaponBase::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimMode). [`WeaponBase::SetAimModeBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimMode) plays the aim-in or aim-out effect on every gun when the mode changes, stores it, and for a local user adds the precision hint on entering precision aim ([`m_PrecisionCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PrecisionCameraHash), or [`m_ReeledHangPrecisionCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReeledHangPrecisionCameraHash) while hanging from the grapple) and removes both on leaving it. The sniper rifle ([`SniperWeapon::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-SetAimMode)) and the mortar override it. The player's mode reaches only the selected weapon while the player uses it ([aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md#precision-aim-and-sniper-zoom)); any other weapon the player uses keeps the mode it got at `StartUsing`, though its scatter and recoil follow the player's mode.

Camera hints are global, keyed by hash, so weapons sharing a hash share one hint: the second `StartUsing` finds it active and adds nothing, and the first `StopUsingBase` of either removes it while the other still uses it, as does the normal-aim return inside it for a shared precision hint. A `StartUsing` of a weapon without precision support drops the player out of precision aim, forwarding normal aim to the selected weapon and removing its hint. Otherwise two weapons in use share no fire or aim state.
