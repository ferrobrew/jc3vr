# Character root motion and the collision proxy

The pyxis defs are mainly [`Character`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character) and the [`input::locomotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::locomotion), [`physics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics), and [`game_world`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world) modules.

## Where the world transform is authoritative

[`Character::m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) and [`Character::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT1) are authoritative; `GetWorldMatrix` and `GetWorldMatrixRef` return `&m_WorldMatrixT1`. The render-facing `m_GraphicsWorldT0`/`T1` (`CTransform`) are derived from them (`SyncGraphicsMatricesToWorld` → `CTransform::FromMatrix4`), and the frame lerps T0 to T1 by `dtf`. The direct writers are `CCharacter::WriteWorldMatrix` (full matrix), `WriteWorldMatrixTranslation` (translation only), and [`Character::SetOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetOrientation) (rotation only, below). A direct write is a teleport: it bypasses the collision solve.

## The displacement path

[`NStateTask_LocoUtil::EvaluateCharacterDisplacement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterDisplacement) is the per-tick movement producer, a pure velocity function: from the character, its new transform, and flags it fills `wanted_velocity_ws` and `wanted_velocity_ls`, never the root. Its callers are [`NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask##func-Update) (on foot), `NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask`, and `NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask`.

It selects one source and never sums. When the animation is not mid-blend, the marker [`ObjectBlackboard::APPLY_TARGET_DIR_INSTANTLY_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::APPLY_TARGET_DIR_INSTANTLY_ID) is not set, and the character is outside the special segments, it reads [`AnimationController::GetRawRootVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetRawRootVelocity), rotates it into world space, writes the wanted velocity, and returns. Otherwise it takes the code-driven path, which builds a direction from the blackboard's [`ObjectBlackboard::MOVE_DIR_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::MOVE_DIR_ID) (with the previous direction at [`PREVIOUS_TARGET_DIR_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::PREVIOUS_TARGET_DIR_ID) and the marker) and rotates it out; the `m_CodeDriven*` flags enable it (`m_CodeDrivenDisplacement`, `m_AllowCodeDrivenDisplacementUntilTolerance`, `m_EnableCodeDrivenDisplacementDuringBlend`, and `m_AngleCorrectionEnabled` / `m_MoveActionParams.m_AngleCorrectionRequestedDir`).

The wanted velocity goes to the Havok character proxy, [`PfxCharacterInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance) (`m_Avatar` is the `CAvatar`, and `m_PendingProxyState` / `m_DefaultProxyState` select the capsule or quadruped shape), held in [`Character::m_PfxCharacter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_PfxCharacter). The step solves the proxy (the capsule) against collision, and `m_WorldMatrixT1` is written from its solved position.

## The locomotion task runs at idle and writes the proxy input directly

[`NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask##func-Update) runs every on-foot tick, idle included: the only callers of [`NStateTask_LocoUtil::EvaluateCharacterOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterOrientation) are this task, [`NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask##func-Update), and [`NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask##func-Update), and a call counter on it advances at idle, while the move and aim tasks' counters stop. At idle it still runs [`EvaluateCharacterSpeed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterSpeed) (about 0), `EvaluateCharacterOrientation`, and `EvaluateCharacterDisplacement`.

At its tail it writes the proxy's one input, [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_Input`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_Input), a [`CharacterInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput) (`CPfxCharacterInstance::CCharacterInput`): [`CharacterInput::wantedVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-wantedVelocity), a `CVector3f` in metres per second in the character's local frame, the normalized local move direction times the speed; nothing is written while attached to a vehicle. The proxy reads it after the task.

The speed is the animation's: `EvaluateCharacterSpeed` is the horizontal magnitude of the clip's raw root velocity, so the starts' ramps, the stops' decay, and in-step speed changes reach the proxy. The input tasks' target speed, [`ObjectBlackboard::SPEED_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::SPEED_ID), does not set it: the input tasks branch on it (a value that is not positive routes them into the stop acts), and the movement task reads it for the player only to gate its angle correction. [`NStateTask_InputLocoSetTargetSpeedsTask::SetupTargetSpeeds`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_InputLocoSetTargetSpeedsTask##func-SetupTargetSpeeds) writes it from the movement settings by stick magnitude and state (walk, non-combat walk, run, or run backpedal), or zero when the stick is at or below the walk threshold, when [`Character::ShouldPreventFall`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ShouldPreventFall) reports a drop ahead taller than the settings' tolerance, or when the predicted wall distance on the blackboard is inside the settings' tolerance.

[`Character::GetWantedWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWantedWorldVelocity) rotates the stored value into world space through `m_WorldMatrixT1`; [`Character::SetWantedWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetWantedWorldVelocity) converts a world velocity into the local frame through the inverse of the proxy's transform and stores it.

The task also sets [`CharacterInput::gameControledVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-gameControledVelocity) while the proxy is supported on stairs ([`PfxCharacterInstance::m_IsSupportedOnStairs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_IsSupportedOnStairs)) and the blackboard carries a slope direction, and clears it otherwise. Set, the Havok proxy update rotates `wantedVelocity` by the proxy's rotation; clear, it passes the vector unchanged.

The function the IDB labels `CCharacterInput::SetWantedVelocity` is `Character::SetOrientation`: it writes a quaternion's rotation into `m_WorldMatrixT1`, keeping the translation, and stores the quaternion in [`Character::m_RotationGoal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_RotationGoal), bypassing the proxy. The ragdoll get-up, in-air grapple-fire, grappling-hang, stunting, jump, grapple-yank, and upright states orient through it; how they feed `wantedVelocity` is not established. The IDB's `CCharacter::WriteWorldMatrixOrientation` writes an attachment proxy's rigid body instead, and the orientation write appears inlined into `SetOrientation`; a second `SetOrientation(CQuaternion)` in the IDB also has an unrelated body.

### The turning acts

Besides the starts and stops, the input tasks turn the body with acts chosen by the angle to the move direction or the aim:

- [`QueueFlickStick`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueFlickStick) queues a straight or third-turn flick in place of the stops when a stop is called for inside the start clip's `FLICK_STICK_TURN_VALID_SEG` segment, and writes the residual angle correction as [`QueueStarts`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueStarts) does.
- [`QueueAimFlickStick`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueAimFlickStick), from the aim flick-stick task, queues a straight, quarter-turn, or half-turn aiming flick, with no angle correction.
- [`QueueAimRelativeOnSpotActions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueAimRelativeOnSpotActions) queues a quarter-turn aim-focus rotation for a standing player with a heavy weapon whose aim is well off the body's forward, and `ACT_TO_IDLE` otherwise, with no angle correction.
- [`QueueStuntTurns`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueStuntTurns) queues a straight or third-turn stunt turn, or `ACT_TO_IDLE` for a small angle, and writes the residual angle correction. The idle, stops, flick-stick, and rotate-on-spot tasks call it when the state machine would not take `ACT_STUNT_TRANSITION`, only while [`Character::ShouldApplyStuntModification`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ShouldApplyStuntModification) holds (the character is attached to a vehicle).

The game registers no act for the mortar's rotate-on-spot states, only its aim and fire acts.

## Capsule dimensions and runtime resize

The capsule is an `hknpShape` per proxy state. [`PfxCharacterInstance::SetProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-SetProxyState) stores the state in [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_ProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_ProxyState), fetches the shared character settings through [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_Owner`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_Owner) (its `GetSharedPfxCharacterSettings` virtual), takes the per-state shape from the settings' shape table (pre-built shapes keyed by `EProxyState`), swaps it onto the live body with [`hknpWorld::setBodyShape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyShape)`(world, bodyId, shape)`, and copies the state's per-shape byte into [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_CharacterRBListener`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_CharacterRBListener). [`PfxCharacterInstance::GetCharacterRigidBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-GetCharacterRigidBodyId) returns the body id. [`PfxCharacterInstance::BuildDefaultCharacterContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-BuildDefaultCharacterContext) builds the Havok character-state machine (on-ground, in-air, and jumping) but not the capsule dimensions. The engine swaps state on the sim thread (`m_PendingProxyState` / `m_DefaultProxyState`) on swimming, vehicle, and ragdoll transitions among others.

## The proxy's support and the fall

The proxy's support is [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_GroundInformation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_GroundInformation), a Havok [`hknpCharacterSurfaceInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpCharacterSurfaceInfo): a supported state (`UNSUPPORTED`, `SLIDING`, or `SUPPORTED`), the surface's normal and velocity, and whether it is dynamic. Beside it is the body it stands on, [`m_GroundBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_GroundBodyId), and the last valid one.

The proxy's update, [`PfxCharacterInstance::UpdateContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-UpdateContext), finds the support afresh every tick through [`CheckSupportCustom`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-CheckSupportCustom), which writes the surface information and ground body from the character rigid body's supporting contacts (an inactive body is left unsupported). It then derives the inherited surface velocity, runs the Havok character context on the support and the proxy input, and sets the body's velocity to the context's output through [`hknpCharacterRigidBody::setLinearVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpCharacterRigidBody##func-setLinearVelocity), which keeps the change of velocity over the step as the body's acceleration.

### When the support is found and when it is read

The character's passes sit in the game world's priority queues ([`Character::UpdatePassPrePhysics_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassPrePhysics_Serial)), each priority run to completion. In the pre-simulation queue the state-machine passes come before the pre-physics pass, which runs the pre-physics state tasks (the on-foot movement task among them) and then the integrating-physics update that calls the proxy's update. [`PhysicsSystem::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-Update) follows the whole queue, firing the pre-simulation callbacks ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md#pre-simulation-callbacks)) and stepping, and the post-simulation queue runs the post-physics pass. So in one tick:

1. the state machines evaluate their conditions on the support the previous tick's proxy update found;
2. the proxy's update finds this tick's support and sets the proxy's velocity;
3. the pre-simulation callbacks run, then the step;
4. the post-physics pass and the next tick's state machines read the support found in 2.

A support or velocity a pre-simulation callback writes is what the step and every reader see until the next proxy update; such a velocity replaces the context output, and the surface velocity is not added again that step. The physics-update wrapper ([havok-bodies.md, "The physics step"](../physics/havok-bodies.md#the-physics-step)) is mislabelled `IsStillAlive` in the disassembler.

### Who reads it

[`PfxCharacterInstance::IsSupported`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-IsSupported) is the supported-state test, called by the ground-contact condition (`CSuperGroundContactCondition`, exactly that call), the reel landing condition (`CReelLandCondition`), the fall bookkeeping (`CCharacter::UpdateFalling`), the ground raycasts (`CCharacter::DoGroundInfoRaycasts`), [`Character::IsInAir`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInAir), the script VM's ground query (`CCharacterModule::GetGroundInfo`), and the aim blend states' samplers. [`PfxCharacterInstance::IsGroundBodyValid`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-IsGroundBodyValid) (mislabelled `CPfxCharacterInstance::GetSurfaceNormal`) checks that the ground body names a static or dynamic body ([`hknpBody::IS_STATIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody), [`IS_DYNAMIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody)).

The character's ground contact is a valid ground body, a supported or sliding proxy, and no ragdoll that owns the character's displacement. With it, the ground raycasts skip the height-over-ground cast, clear the fall distance and the time until landing, and look up the ground body's owner to tell a vehicle underfoot; the fall distance condition (`CFallDistanceCondition`) reads zero; and `CCharacter::UpdateFalling` accumulates the fall time and the jump-or-fall time only in the falling and jumping states without it.

The fall conditions are ground contact, fall distance, fall time, and fall transition (`CFallTransitionCondition`, on the proxy's vertical speed, the fall distance, the time until landing, and the jump-or-fall time); unverified. In Rico's base graph ([animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md)) the on-foot states (idle, moves, stops) leave for `S_FALLING` on an `ACT_UPDATE` transition needing `C_IS_SUPPORTED` false, `C_ABOVE_FALL_HEIGHT` true, and no vehicle attachment, so ground contact never starts a fall from them.

The engine's own way of holding a positioned character supported, unverified, is `CPfxCharacterInstance::SetAttachedSupport` (from `UpdateContextAttached`): supported, level, with no ground body. The functions named `CPfxCharacterInstance::UpdateContextAttached` and `CPfxCharacterInstance::SetLinearVelocity` are something else, as is the one named `hknpCharacterRigidBody::setLinearVelocity`, a hash-map lookup.

## Raycast and scene-query API: `CPhysicsSystem::CastRay` and related functions

[`PlayerAimControl::UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) calls the general scene query directly, [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay), backed by Havok [`hknpWorld::castRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-castRay):

```
bool CPhysicsSystem::CastRay(CPhysicsSystem*,
                             const CRay3&        ray,        // { CVector3f start; CVector3f direction; } WORLD space, direction normalized
                             float               minFraction,// 0.0 in the aim call
                             float               maxDistance,// world-space length along direction
                             CCastRayInfoBase*   outHit,     // nullptr = boolean-only
                             CRaycastFilter*     filter,     // nullptr = default filter
                             unsigned int        flags,      // collision-filter-info word, 0 in the aim call
                             int                 layer);     // PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER in the aim call
```

The aim call's layer is [`PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER).

- Filter. The `CRaycastFilter` constructor ([`RaycastFilter::Construct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RaycastFilter##func-Construct)) is `CRaycastFilter(ignoreMaterialMask, forceMaterialMask, IPfxInstance* ignoreInstance, const hknpWorld*)`. The masks are material pass flags ([`MaterialPassFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::MaterialPassFlags)), not layers: ignore-mask materials are never hits and force-mask ones always are. `ignoreInstance` excludes one instance, such as the player's own proxy. Materials carry per-pass "holey" flags (foliage a bullet passes through, glass, and similar). The aim path uses the subclass `CIgnoreMaterialFlagFilter`, whose [`SetIgnoreBulletPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreBulletPass) / [`SetIgnoreRayPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreRayPass) / [`SetIgnoreCameraPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreCameraPass) each set or clear one pass bit in the ignore mask, wrapped by `CPlayerAimControl` in `CPlayerAimRaycastFilterCacheFirstHoleyHitAndMinDistanceHit`, which records both the first holey hit and the nearest solid hit.
- Result. `CCastRayInfo : CCastRayInfoBase` ([`CastRayInfoBase::Reset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfoBase##func-Reset)) carries the hit position, normal, and fraction, the hit body and instance, [`CastRayInfo::GetHitMaterial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfo##func-GetHitMaterial) → `EGameMaterialId`, and [`CastRayInfoBase::DidHitBulletPassMaterial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfoBase##func-DidHitBulletPassMaterial). `GetHitNameHash` is [`RagdollProxyPhantom::GetHitNameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RagdollProxyPhantom##func-GetHitNameHash), which takes a `CCastRayInfoBase` and returns the name hash of the ragdoll part hit, or 0 off the ragdoll's proxy phantom.
- Thread and phase. Sim-phase queries against the live world: `UpdateDirectAim` runs in the aim update on the sim thread ([`PlayerAimControl::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdatePreSim) / [`UpdateAllTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateAllTargets)); the engine does not call them during the solve or from a render worker.

The simpler `CPhysicsSystem` variants, all in world space:

- [`CastRaySimple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRaySimple)`(const CVector3f& start, const CVector3f& end, unsigned int flags)` takes no hit info and no filter, hardcodes `QUERY_LAYER`, and returns true when the segment is blocked. Unfiltered, it misses the player's capsule but can hit the player's ragdoll and hit-proxy bodies, which the player aim's filter ([`PlayerAimRaycastFilter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastFilter)) rejects along with every other physics instance of the objects it ignores.
- [`CastRayTerrain`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRayTerrain) (terrain only) and [`CastRayWaterSurface`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRayWaterSurface) (the water plane).
- Shape casts: [`CastSphere`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastSphere)`(const CVector3f& start, const CVector3f& end, float radius, float, hknpCollisionQueryCollector&, unsigned int, float)`, [`CastShape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastShape) (an arbitrary `hknpShape*` with a rotation), and the free function [`SweptSphereCast`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics##func-SweptSphereCast), which fills a `CCastResult`. The sphere cast's engine consumer `CMagnetWeaponComponent::CastSphereAgainstStatic` is not located: the IDB function with that name has a different body.

## Teleport writers and teleport signals

Relocation goes through the direct world-matrix writers, the `CTeleport` object ([`Teleport::SetTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::Teleport##func-SetTransform), `GetTransform`/`NeedsUpdate`, event-driven), the game world's teleport functions, and the animation warp tasks (`NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask`, `NStateTask_MovementHeightWarpTask`)., all bypassing collision.

- Fast travel and mission warps. [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayer) ([`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##func-TeleportPlayerImpl)) queues a deferred request that the teleport state machine in [`UpdateTeleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world##func-UpdateTeleport) applies over the following ticks [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstant`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayerInstant) ([`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstantImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##func-TeleportPlayerInstantImpl)) moves synchronously: it re-bases the character through `CGameObject::InitTransform`, the virtual at [`Character::GAME_OBJECT_INIT_TRANSFORM_SLOT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character::GAME_OBJECT_INIT_TRANSFORM_SLOT) of the `CGameObject` subobject at [`Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET), calls [`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState), sets [`AbilityFlags::m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AbilityFlags##flag-m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled) in [`Character::m_AbilityFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AbilityFlags), and re-bases the camera through [`GameCameraManager::ResetCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-ResetCamera), bracketed by `NEvent::CPostEvent::PostMsg("game_teleporting_initiated" / "…_completed")`.
- Scripted teleport objects: [`Teleport::Teleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::Teleport##func-Teleport), which issues `GameWorld::TeleportPlayer` to the object's transform, `UpdateTeleport`, and [`NStateTask_InputVehicleExitTask_TeleportUpwards`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/vehicle::vehicle##func-NStateTask_InputVehicleExitTask_TeleportUpwards).
- Vehicle: [`Vehicle::TeleportVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-TeleportVehicle); the seated character moves with the vehicle through the attach.

Two fields do not detect teleports. `m_NumFramesSinceTeleport` is incremented each tick by `CCharacter::DebugVerifyCharacter`, which runs unconditionally, and no writer resets it (an apparent reset is a local-variable fallback inside a warning-log call), so it counts frames since spawn. `m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled` controls leaving stunt positions automatically, and [`Character::SetStuntAutoExitEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetStuntAutoExitEnabled) writes it too.

The verified signals:

- [`GameWorld::IsTeleporting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-IsTeleporting) reports a deferred `TeleportPlayer` still being serviced; fast travel, mission warps, and `CTeleport` objects all enter through it or `TeleportPlayerInstant`.
- The `game_teleporting_initiated` / `…_completed` events bracket `TeleportPlayerInstant`, a string-keyed choke point for the scripted warps.
- A position discontinuity, a per-tick translation delta of `m_WorldMatrixT1` well above wingsuit speed times the tick length, catches any writer, including the vehicle teleport and the warp tasks, which do not go through the game world.

## Seat pose: `m_AttachBone` and `m_AttachOffset` on the character

In a vehicle the character is attached: `CCharacter::m_Attachable` (`CAttachable`), `m_attachType` (`CCharacter::AttachType`, not `NONE` while attached), and `m_attachedObject` hold the parent binding, set by the [`Character::AttachTo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##vfunc-AttachTo) virtual. The in-vehicle predicates are in [state-signals.md](state-signals.md#the-state-machine-hash-predicates-vehicle-riding-reeled-in-hang) and [its vehicle class section](state-signals.md#vehicle-presence-and-class).

The vehicle's `CSeat` stores no pose: it is interaction-graph metadata only (`m_GraphNode`, `m_DoorSlot`, `m_WindowPartInstance`, and `m_PlayerUsable`). The character defines the pose with `CCharacter::m_AttachBone` (`NBone::ESafeBoneIndex`, a safe bone or socket on the vehicle model) and `CCharacter::m_AttachOffset` (`CMatrix4f`, the character's transform relative to it): character world = vehicle.SafeBoneWorld(`m_AttachBone`) · `m_AttachOffset`. The Y-aligned attach entry, [`Character::SetYAlignedAttachTo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetYAlignedAttachTo), computes a yaw-only alignment from the target frame and calls `AttachTo`. `AttachToWithCurrentOffset`, which preserves the live offset, is not located: the IDB function with that name has a different body.

The whole skeleton rides the vehicle, so the head and eye bones give the seated eye position. The in-vehicle camera is a third-person chase rig (`SGenericVehicleCamera`: `m_CameraPosition`, `m_RotationPoint`, and `m_LookAtPoint` relative to the vehicle body, plus spring, lag, and FOV tuning) with no seat-eye anchor.
