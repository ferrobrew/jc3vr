# Physical weapons

A weapon behaves as one physical object: it is held in whichever hand took it, moves between hands, is picked up by the hand that touches it, and goes onto the body and comes off it as the same object. The loadout table says where every weapon is ([two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md)) and the game's slots follow it; the game keeps owning the weapon objects, the reserves, and the selected weapon's HUD, and the mod owns where each gun is and which hand fires it. The body sockets are [weapon-sockets.md](weapon-sockets.md), the gun as a body joined to the hand bodies [physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md), the foregrip, weight, and recoil [weapon-handling.md](weapon-handling.md), the reload [reload.md](reload.md), and grenades and C4 [grenades.md](grenades.md). The engine side is [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md), [item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md), and [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../../engine/character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md).

Everything here acts only while the bindings inject, under the controller scheme. The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/hold/`, tuned by `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/hold.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). The one setting is `controllers.weapons.hold.grip_to_hold` (on); the debug UI's Weapons > Hold page and the Status page's `Weapons in hand` section show each hand's weapon, how it came there, and the takes in reach.

## Either hand

A weapon rides the hand the loadout table holds it in ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The live table"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-live-table)). The table changes at once on the hands' own moves. A weapon the game wields on its own (the weapon wheel, a switch, an automatic draw) lands in the acting hand when that hand is empty, otherwise in the other, and is put away again if both hands are full; a pickup or take leaves the grabbing hand expecting its weapon for `loadout.expect_s` so another weapon cannot jump in first. A weapon the game puts away on its own leaves the table's hand for the socket the game docks its slot on.

## The owned attach bones

A gun is placed at the bone its attach-bone table ([`WeaponData::m_lBoneAttachementPerWeaponState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_lBoneAttachementPerWeaponState)) names for the weapon's state. The game rewrites the tables during every pose-finalization pass, so the mod writes the holding hand's bones at three points: before each placement ([`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)), after the end-of-pass rewrite ([`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose)), and at the weapon's own update before it fires. The write is idempotent, so the three agree, and the weapon's state is never changed. The detours (`hold/mod.rs`) run on worker threads for every character, so they check the table's published hands before taking any lock.

A single gun on the other hand's bone moves to its counterpart; the game's pair keeps its first gun in the holding hand and its second in the other, mirrored when held left (`hold::layout::placed_bone`). A gun whose hand changed skips one frame of render interpolation ([`WeaponBase::SkipRenderInterpolationForOneFrame`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SkipRenderInterpolationForOneFrame)) so a handover does not streak. Everything that derives a gun's hand from its bone follows unchanged.

## The right hand's grip

The skeleton relates its left and right bones with every local axis flipped rather than mirrored, so the right hand's own canonical grip holds a gun backwards. The right hand therefore takes the left hand's grip mirrored across the body's lateral axis whenever its own is more than `grip.auto_turn_deg` away from it (`hands::grip::pick_right_grip` in `crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/hands/grip.rs`, [hands-and-aiming.md, "The rigid grip"](hands-and-aiming.md#the-rigid-grip)). The Status page's `Right hand grip` row shows the choice.

## Firing

Every gun in a hand fires from that hand's own trigger: the wielded weapon, whose update the inventory's fan-out runs with its hand's fire input, and each gun armed beside it, which the mod updates ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The per-hand tick"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-per-hand-tick)). In a vehicle the holding hand's vehicle-weapon action fires it, and in the driver's seat the triggers stay the pedals ([bindings.md](bindings.md#vehicles)). A gun does not fire while its hand carries it by a part other than the grip, a dock's slide or a reload act runs, the reload holds it, or the shooting permission is withheld. Held guns act in every mode but UI mode (`weapons::capabilities`); a hand that carries or supports a gun reserves its trigger without firing.

### The shooting permission

Every held-gun fire path takes the game's permission through `weapons::permission::shooting_allowed`: [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsShooting), or a waiver. The game withholds it in free and fast fall, the wingsuit's rolls and evades, and the parachute's opening ([weapon-firing.md, "The permission by state"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-firing.md#the-permission-by-state)), states that pose the game's own arms; a gun posed by the controller has no such reason, so the gate waives it in the wingsuit, under a parachute, and falling. It never waives during the game's grapple wind-up, reel, or live wire (keeping the shoot-while-reeling upgrade's rule), except while the mod's own snatch line or grapple lock holds the other hand. A withheld press keeps the trigger reserved, so it never falls through to the game's action for it.

## Handing a gun over

While the other hand is free, a grab volume (`GrabVolume::Handle`, `hold.handle_radius_m`) sits on the held gun's pistol grip, placed from the last native placement carried onto the wrist as it is now (`hold::world::grip_on_current_wrist`). A grip there moves the gun to the free hand at once. A hand can also brace or carry a long gun by any other part ([physical-guns.md, "Grabbing a gun anywhere"](physical-guns.md#grabbing-a-gun-anywhere)).

## Picking a weapon up into the hand

A grab of a weapon lying in the world ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)) takes it straight into the grabbing hand through the table (`hold::pickup::take_by_table`), and the game's equip never runs, so the other hand keeps what it holds. The input tick first checks that the reached body still belongs to the weapon, which proves it alive, and that the weapon can be taken. The pickup virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup)) makes the player its holder and takes its bodies out of the world, and its stored rounds go to the reserve ([`Inventory::AddAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddAmmo)). The gun slides into the hand over `hold.snap_s` from where it lay.

Under the controller scheme the hands are the only way to pick a weapon up (`hold::button_pickup`): the pickup panel is cleared in place of [`Player::UpdateWeaponHUD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateWeaponHUD)'s readout, and the use-item input reads zero for the call that would queue the pickup act, so the use action still enters vehicles. A refused grab falls back to the use-item click. The game's own pickup (the use-item animation, or a swimming character's) is routed into the same take (`hold::use_item`): the detour of [`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem) queues the weapon for a free hand.

## Taking a weapon from a source

Every take goes through one path (`hold::source`), whatever the source: lying in the world, held out by a placement rule (a rack or locker), one gun of a rack's pistol pair, or a gun in a living character's hands or holster. The source decides how the weapon is given up; the delivery decides how it reaches the hand, and is the only difference between a hand's grab (`source::take`, at once) and the [grapple snatch](../grapple/snatch.md) (`source::free`, given up into the world for the line to pull in). Every source runs the same preflight before giving anything up (`pickup::preflight`), so a take the hand could not finish leaves the weapon where it was.

A gun on a character and a weapon on a rack have no physics body, so `hold::world` offers them as segments along the gun between its grips ([`WeaponBase::GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition)), each free hand offered the nearest point (`GrabVolume::WorldWeapon`). Racks are reached within `hold.take_radius_m` and characters within the wider `hold.steal_radius_m`, since the character moves while the hand closes.

## Taking a gun from a character

The inventory's end-of-pass detour notes each character within `hold.steal.notice_range_m` that wields or visibly holsters a gun of the pistol, two-handed, special, or heavy slot (`engine::takeable_gun_slot`). Within reach the gun wins over the character's capsule ([character-grabs.md](character-grabs.md#reaching-a-character)). A grip there (`hold::steal`) locks the character alive, rechecks the take, drops the gun from its inventory ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop), then [`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon)), staggers it toward the player without knocking it down ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)), and takes the gun into the hand. The character then does what the game does with an unarmed character ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md, "A character that loses its gun"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#a-character-that-loses-its-gun)).

## Taking a weapon off a rack

`hold::rack` walks the player's valid interaction entries as a swimming character's pickup does ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md, "Racks"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#racks)) and offers each weapon in the rack state, or lying in the world, that no character holds. A grip finds the weapon again among the entries and takes it after [`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart), which takes it out of the rack state and tells the rack. A rack with unlimited stock gives a copy instead (`hold::rack::copy`): the pickup start asks the inventory for a copy, which the rack-member give fence (`inventory::gives`) stages where the rack's weapon hangs without the give's equip, ammunition, or replacement drop; the rack's own weapon is hidden until it restocks.

The game does not offer a rack weapon of a kind the player's slot already holds ([`InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory::Evaluate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory##func-Evaluate)). Each hand holds its own weapon of the dual-wield slot, so a detour lets that slot's weapons pass for the local player (`hold::rack::offer`). Out of reach, the snatch takes from the same sources ([snatch.md, "Targets"](../grapple/snatch.md#targets)).

### Paired rack pistols

A dual-wield weapon on a rack is two visible guns sharing one object and one magazine. Each gun is its own grab target (`RackMember`), and a grab takes only its gun (`hold::rack::paired`). It qualifies only while the native object has exactly two gun records, read from the object rather than inferred from the rack's model.

A finite pair keeps the original object at the placement rule and stages one extra object through [`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon) with the source's spawn tag and an empty magazine, through the same give fence, at most once per observation (`hold.rack.paired.copy_wait_ms`). On the first accepted take the copy gets the taken gun's share of the actual magazine and the original keeps the rest, so no ammunition is created; a rejected take restores the original. The original is then a single pistol at the remaining gun's place, and the last take runs its `PickupStart` once, which consumes the placement rule. While neither gun is claimed, the second is drawn `hold.rack.paired.stack_m` up from the first, since the rule places both guns on one transform. The snatch claims the aimed gun the same way (`paired::claim`). A half-taken source keeps a weak reference and its single designation, so re-observing it never makes another pair.

## The sockets

Every weapon on the body sits on a socket of the loadout table's (each hip and each shoulder). An empty hand draws the weapon on a socket, a hand holding a weapon docks it on a free socket that fits, and a release near a socket draws the weapon onto it or swaps it, all without the game's acts ([weapon-sockets.md](weapon-sockets.md)). While neither hand holds a weapon, either hand may draw a long gun off either shoulder (`volumes::reserve_long_guns`).

## Letting go

With `grip_to_hold` on, a gun is held only while its grip is (`hold::dock::ReleaseWatch`). A gun taken by a grab is watched from the tick it arrives with the grip held; one that arrived some other way (the wheel, the game's draw) is watched only from a fresh grip press, so a hand that held a vehicle control while the game drew a gun does not drop it. Opening a watched grip (`loadout::grab::release`) docks the gun on a free socket in reach, sends it onto a socket whose attraction it is within ([weapon-sockets.md, "Attraction"](weapon-sockets.md#attraction)), passes it to the other hand if that hand braces it ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)), or lets it fall; the game's pair cannot fall, so it docks or stays. With `grip_to_hold` off, a gun stays until a socket grab docks it.

A stand-down or pause leaves every hold as it was; on the first tick back (`intent::grip_holds_now`) a grip that opened meanwhile sends the gun back to a socket, falling only when none takes it.

The release copies the gun's world point before disarming it and asks the body's one release estimate for its motion ([physics-substrate.md, "Release velocities"](../body/physics-substrate.md#release-velocities)), the same estimate every thrown item takes, with the hand's history starting when this weapon entered it. It runs `OnDrop`, then sets the motion on the native gun body ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)) after checking that the body is dynamic and still the weapon's, and clears [`WeaponBase::m_DeferredDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredDrop) so a queued drop cannot overwrite it.

## Separate pistols

The game's pistols are one weapon with two guns ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md, "The dual-wield weapon"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#the-dual-wield-weapon)), so neither could be dropped or held apart. Each pistol is made a weapon of its own, a single pistol (`weapons::dual_wield::single`), and an item of the table like any other: two pistols are one in each hand, each on its own hip. A single pistol is the dual-wield object kept to its first gun:

- its second gun is hidden after every show the game makes (the detour of [`WeaponBase::SetVisibleBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetVisibleBase)), and its next gun ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)) is held on the first, so it fires only what shows;
- its magazine holds half the tuned size, rounded up, sharing the reserve, and a magazine over the half is brought down at the next tick, the excess returning to the reserve;
- it stays one pistol when dropped and picked up again; pistols never merge back.

A pair the game hands over is split: the slot keeps it as a single pistol and a twin is spawned at the player ([`SpawnSystem::SpawnTagIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##func-SpawnTagIndex)), placed on the free hip with the pair's magazine dealt between them. Until the twin comes the pair stays the game's. On the left hip a single pistol borrows its second gun's attach offset ([`WeaponData::m_AttachOffset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_AttachOffset)) for the placement (`inventory::carry::borrows_second_offset`). The save sidecar records which pistols are single ([two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md#saves-resets-and-loads)); a twin that never returns after a reset turns the slot's pistol back into the game's pair, so no pistol is lost.

## Aiming down the sights

The gun aim already shoots along each gun's sight line ([hands-and-aiming.md, "Gun aim"](hands-and-aiming.md#gun-aim)); `weapons::ads` lines a long gun (two-handed and special slots) raised to the eye up with it so the player can look down the sights, tuned by `handling.toml`'s `ads` section. It is a stage of the hand composition after the two-handed pose and weight and before recoil, so the drawn gun, IK, sight line, and scope lens all see the moved gun, which moves rigidly with both hands riding it. Nothing turns the gun toward a target. Three steps fade in as their distance closes: the stock turns the gun about its front hand so the butt meets the shoulder pocket; the sights turn it about a point near the front sight so the line passes through the aiming eye, gated by the head looking along the gun; and a scope's cheek weld shifts it across its axis instead. The front sight stays where the hands hold it, so the shot lands where the sights point. The Status page's `Down the sights` row shows it.

## Guns against walls

A gun with a body stops at walls along its length ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)). A gun without one (a parked hand body, or a heavy weapon with its own body in the world) would pass through, so `weapons::collide` pushes held long guns out of walls with a ray probe from the grip to `collide.tip_margin_m` past the muzzle with the player aim's filter, cast along the gun's pose before any push. The hit becomes a plane kept for `collide.keep_s`, so grazing an edge does not bounce. The gun first turns about the grip away from the plane (up to `collide.max_turn_deg`, so it slides along a slanted wall), then retracts along itself (up to `collide.max_retract_m`), with both hands riding it. The stage runs after the sights and recoil, and the composition restores a physical gun hand afterwards (`stages::keep_physical`). The Status page's `Gun against walls` row shows it.
