# Integrated runtime testing

The replacement paths run under the existing enabled controller, full-body, weapon-handling, and camera settings. Existing saved switches remain effective. New tracking recordings use lossless chunked version 5.

## Runtime coverage

- Ordinary on-foot play uses the grounded rig ([rig.md](rig.md)). The pelvis solver separates a lean from a step, and a committed step drives the game's own character movement while the player owns the root. The game's animation keeps the hips, the pelvis, and the legs, and the procedural torso poses the spine on them. The body-follow switch (`steps.enabled`) disables the step drive. Tracked head and interaction-hand targets remain authoritative.
- Under the controller scheme with the head tracked, the learned body estimator runs on the game's device in every mode and publishes its estimate ([body-estimator.md](body-estimator.md#in-the-game)); on the ground the grounded rig's legs follow it and its chest is pulled toward it ([rig.md](rig.md#the-yaw)). `controllers.body.estimator.enabled`, the `Run the body estimator` switch in the debug UI's Body fit feature, turns it off, and the yaw is then the head's rules alone. The weights must be set up first (`scripts/egoposer_weights.sh`).
- Free parachute-handle targets use native resting joint observations captured during the game's existing sync. Held steering keeps its grip-derived behavior. Missing or expired native observations temporarily withhold the free target instead of substituting shoulder geometry.
- Authored camera following admits exact takeoff, landing, recovery, floor-pickup, and hanging states, including the verified pre-inversion to attached upside-down grapple sequence. The numbered native channel is applied relative to entry before tracked head motion. Native clip time and contribution observations detect same-state restarts and outgoing blend tails. Pending zero-weight handoffs retain entry across repeated unchanged finalized samples. Unsupported exits return to the normal tracked view. Completion eligibility must cover the evaluated camera channel; game acceptance remains unperformed.
- Magazine grip-and-pull, button ejection, flick ejection, and belt replacement execute detachment in the character's native serial post-physics pass. The callback revalidates the current weapon and spare identity. No extra magazine latch button is required. Magazine and chamber ownership partition the existing native loaded total: extraction leaves a live chamber on the weapon, and magazine-out firing can consume it once. Policy deactivation, pair splitting, and pouch/belt capacity checks share that accounting.
- A grenade draw takes the engine's own inert grenade from the weapon's pool without consuming ammunition, so the item the hand holds, stows, transfers, and drops is native from the start. Only a deliberate tracked throw arms it, after it has left the hand; an incidental release puts it into the simulation still inert.
- Qualifying finite and unlimited paired racks expose their two native gun models as independent source members. Taking one does not adopt its neighbour. Finite staging requests one empty copy and divides the source's actual ammunition on the first accepted take. The original notifies its placement rule on the final pickup. See [paired-rack-weapons.md](../input/paired-rack-weapons.md). An external same-tag mission give racing the callback remains ambiguous.
- Supported ready guns expose their manual slide, bolt, or pump action without requiring an empty chamber. Rear-stop ejection and completed feeding conserve loaded rounds. An open gun grip during a held action remains pending until the action ends; regrip or an ownership change cancels it.
- The shipped Massos static grenade shelf models provide safe-item sources at copied native instance transforms. Source reads discover already-loaded game-owned locations as well as streamed collections. Collection identity, model resource validation, and retirement bound source lifetime. Other unrecognized decoration does not become a source.
- Vehicle canopy/stunt defaults use physical right A for either dominance choice. Land handbrake defaults to physical left A, and explicit user bindings remain effective. Native seat eligibility remains contextual.
- Human collision hulls narrow horizontal bounds while retaining height, center, user data, and state-local contact policy; the bottom cone is scaled with the width so that it keeps its slope and still steps up kerbs ([human-hull-restoration.md](human-hull-restoration.md#the-narrowed-shape)). The debug physical-hands controls expose the runtime switch. Each native pre-simulation pass discovers live human owners and distinct shared settings, including existing NPCs. Construction hooks cover future settings.

## Collider restoration

Disable and shutdown request restoration through the native pre-simulation phase. Native convex distance queries and bounded compressed-terrain leaf traversal check each original shape before any body changes. Equivalent shallow floor contact can remain; new wall or ceiling penetration blocks restoration. Unsupported geometry, incomplete traversal, invalid query output, or an exhausted budget defers restoration and keeps normal gameplay and hooks running. An eject waits only a bounded number of attempts for restoration, then leaves the hulls as they are with every shape reference kept. No teleport or forced expansion is used. See [human-hull-restoration.md](human-hull-restoration.md).

After successful restoration, shutdown validates the copied preparation generation before teardown. A rejected body write rolls back all attempted changes. A rejected rollback refuses unload. An active native callback after hook removal keeps the image mapped. Unsupported or large terrain can still defer ejection. A process restart is not counted as successful live restoration.

## Test order

1. Load an ordinary on-foot scene and confirm the Body fit readout advances. Walk physically, bend, crouch, turn, and reach with one hand: a bend stays a lean, and a walk commits a step that plays the game's walk. Test slopes, stairs, edges, and moving platforms. Toggle body follow off and verify that steps stop.
2. With the weights staged, check the Body fit readout's estimator line reads `running` within a couple of seconds of the first controller frame, and that the log has one `body estimator: running on the game's device` line naming the weights and the 16-bit precision. Standing still, glance left and right and back: the green floor arrow (the estimate's pelvis) swings part of the way, the magenta one (the rig's legs) barely moves, and the chest follows the glance. Turn the whole body a quarter turn and hold it: the legs follow within about half a second in one smooth movement, with no discrete turn and no game turn-in-place stutter, and settle onto the green arrow. Repeat seated. Walk with the stick and look about: the legs behave as before (the head's moving turn). Recenter, snap turn, and smooth turn while watching the arrows: neither arrow jumps relative to the body. Cover a controller with a hand until it loses tracking: the arrow keeps a plausible heading. Enter a vehicle, the parachute, the wingsuit, and the water: the green arrows keep drawing and the magenta ones disappear, and nothing about the body changes from before. Switch to the gamepad scheme: the arrows disappear and the readout shows no estimate. If the legs wander, jitter, or turn on glances, turn `Run the body estimator` off and report the readout's estimate and say values with the moment. Rename the weights file away while the estimator is on: the readout shows the error naming the path, and the log has it once.
3. Rico's head on the neck pivot: stand still on foot after the neutral measurement, with `Body rig` visualisation on, and look level, then down at the chest, then turn the head well to each side and hold it while the body follows the turn. The neck pivot sprite should sit on Rico's neck in the mirror and in his shadow, his head should neither trail behind the view nor sit above it, and turning the head should not swing the camera into his head or shoulders (the 10 November take's clipping at a head yaw). A level look should feel about 2 cm taller and 4 cm further back over the body than before. Walk, crouch a little, and lean over a table: the chest stays out of the lower view. Toggle `Rico's head on the neck pivot` (beside `Steady view on foot`) to compare; turning it off restores the old camera and head target.
4. Repeat takeoff, landing, recovery, floor pickup, and hanging. Verify that head motion remains independent and that entering a sequence a second time still follows it.
5. Open the canopy, acquire both resting handles, steer, release, and reacquire. Compare the target markers with visible native rest.
6. Extract a loaded magazine by grip and pull, abort a partial pull, reinsert it, and verify chamber and magazine ammunition. Repeat button, flick, and belt operations, including weapon changes while an operation is pending. Rack a ready slide, bolt, and pump repeatedly. Check round ejection and feeding, partial pulls, release during an action, regrip cancellation, and ordinary drop from a pump foregrip at home.
7. Draw safe grenades into both hands at zero stock, transfer and stow them, drop one without throwing, recover it, and deliberately throw the other. Test the recorded Massos shelf, leave its streaming range, and return. Confirm no stock refund or deduction and no fuse on the inert items.
8. At qualifying finite and unlimited paired racks, take each member in either order with the opposite hand occupied and the holsters full. Confirm the other member stays at the source and total ammunition remains conserved. Leave and return after the first take, then repeat across a reset.
9. Inject with existing NPCs nearby, then stream in more NPCs. Exercise narrowed collision in standing, crouching, swimming, and wingsuit states. Request restoration on clear flat ground and beside an obstruction. A deferred request must leave gameplay responsive and must not partially restore bodies. Move clear, retry, eject, and reinject.
10. Capture full-rate tracking with joints while exercising these paths. Compare stored/uncompressed bytes, queue/drop counters, shutdown recovery, and that `tracking-synth dump` reads the file back. No real-capture compression performance result is established yet.

Compiler checks and pure tests do not establish the native callback schedule, model factories, live physics response, or headset comfort. The runtime paths are connected so these behaviors can be tested together.
