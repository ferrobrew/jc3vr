# Vehicle doors by hand

An empty hand opens a vehicle's doors: a grip on a closed door takes hold, a pull outward undoes the latch, and the door swings on its hinge with the hand. A released side door swings on, damped, a lid stays where it is left, and a door shoved shut latches. Hoods, trunks, and hatches work the same way, and a hard pull tears an open door off. It is always on under the controller scheme and builds on the pickup and the hand bodies ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)); the grapple snatch opens doors from range through the same unlatch ([snatch.md](../grapple/snatch.md#targets)).

The code is `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/doors/`, with the grip and tear handling in `physical_hands/pickup/` and the seated path in `physical_hands/seated.rs`; tuning is `crates/jc3vr-physical/tuning/doors.toml`. The engine side is [vehicle-doors.md](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md).

## Finding a door

The scan (`doors/scan.rs`) covers the vehicles the game's target system keeps near a free palm plus those each hand's reach rays hit, since the target system misses some parked cars. A door's handle comes from the door handle table (`tools/door_handles/door_handles.json`, embedded by `doors/table.rs`), keyed by the part's name hash ([`Part::m_name`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_name)) so one entry serves every variant of a model; [`tools/door_handles/README.md`](../../../tools/door_handles/README.md) has how it is built, from the entry animations ([vehicle-doors.md, "Entering through a door"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#entering-through-a-door)). A door the table lacks (DLC vehicles) takes a handle from its part's box (`DoorBox::handle`), opposite a hinge edge chosen by the kind of door, because a door part's origin is the vehicle's rather than its hinge.

A palm near a handle is offered that door, marked green ([grip.md](grip.md#markers)). The pickup's reach rays also test every vehicle hit first, so a player standing on a car can open its door: a hit on a loosened door part ([`VehiclePartGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject)) is an open door, and a hit on the vehicle's body tests each door's box (`doors::acquisition::panel`, [vehicle-doors.md, "The loose door"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#the-loose-door)) and takes a closed door where it was reached.

Acquisition needs a real ray hit inside the door's bounds. Window glass never acquires a door, and rays pass panes the glass feature has hidden, so an empty window supplies none; the known aircraft canopies are probed on their surface instead. A door wins over the climb's vehicle hold, since a hand closing on a door most likely means to open it. A body hit that reaches no door takes nothing, because a vehicle is never carried (`bodies::classify`).

## The latch

A grip on a closed door holds the hand on the grab point, carried with the vehicle as a climbing hand is held on a wall ([hand-climbing.md](hand-climbing.md#gripping)). The latch gives once the hand has pulled `grip.unlatch_pull_m` outward from where it took hold, so the reach across the gap never counts and pushing in or sliding along never opens it.

When it gives, the hand plays `Event::Unlatch` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)) and `doors::engine::unlatch` loosens the door the game's way without its motor: [`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) keeps the vehicle's door controller running, its opening request is withdrawn at once so no motor turns the hinge, and [`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart) loosens the part onto its hinge. The hand then takes the door's own body with the grab point in the palm: an offset in the palm's frame would swing off the door's arc with every turn of the wrist. The grip lets go when the grip opens, the hands stand down, the vehicle is gone, the door breaks off, or the opened door's body cannot be read within `grip.body_wait_s`.

## Seated doors and palm pressure

While seated, the seated path acts only on the occupied vehicle's doors, through contact and reach rays rather than the hand bodies; door grips use the same latch, and other pickups, grabs, and climbing stay off. An ungripped palm pressing a closed door's inner panel outward also unlatches it after `grip.unlatch_pull_m`, measured in the vehicle frame from first contact so penetration and the vehicle's motion do not count. A briefly missed contact keeps its progress for `pressure.contact_grace_s` only while a short ray still proves the same solid panel, and the probes stop at hidden glass, so the grace never bridges an empty window. Striking glass belongs to the [glass feature](breaking-glass.md).

## Swinging and shutting

The hand holds the open door through a joint as any loose object ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#holding-through-a-joint)), and the hinge constraint leaves only the swing. The game leaves a loose door on a frictionless hinge and merges it shut when it swings within [`DoorInfo::LATCH_ANGLE_DEG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_ANGLE_DEG) of shut faster than [`DoorInfo::LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S), which any rebound off the stop or the player's capsule reaches ([vehicle-doors.md, "The latch"](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#the-latch)). So every door the hands or snatch opened is registered (`doors/hinge/mod.rs`) and marked kept open ([`DoorInfo::FLAG_KEEP_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)) at its largest angle, which skips the game's latch without asking the motor to reopen it, and the mod decides each tick instead. A held door never latches. A free side door is damped relative to the chassis, so a door thrown open does not bounce back. A free lid above `hinge.strut_fraction` of its travel is held against its weight like a gas strut, and an aircraft canopy that settles open holds its pose relative to the chassis. A free door that swings shut fast or rests shut for `hinge.settle_s` latches through the game's merge ([`PartInstance::MergePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-MergePart), then [`Vehicle::OnFinishedClosingDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OnFinishedClosingDoor)), which destroys the door's body and so ends any hold. A door the game opens or closes itself leaves the registry with its latch restored, and every door gets it back when the hands stand down.

## Tearing an open door

The native hinge has no force-break threshold, so the mod judges tear gestures (`doors/tearing/mod.rs`). A grip must first settle, and gestures are measured from there in the vehicle frame, so a heavy door hanging off the hand still settles and the opening pull cannot tear it. Then a yank, a hard pull held for a moment, or a sustained push at the open limit tears it. Lag along the swing the hinge leaves free does not count, since that is a heavy door catching up, and reaching the open stop needs fresh settling, so one continuous opening movement never tears.

Before the tear, the pickup checks that the vehicle and part still match, that the part is detachable and unprotected, and that the vehicle is outside its parallel update. It then calls [`PartInstance::DamagePart_ToBreakOff`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-DamagePart_ToBreakOff) with player attribution and forced damage, which bypasses the healthy-part restriction but not invulnerability. The detached door becomes an ordinary prop in the same hand.

## Threading and readouts

Doors are found, gripped, and unlatched from the palms' tick on the game thread outside the physics step, as the door controller's own update runs, and every read of a vehicle's doors first checks that the vehicle is alive. The debug UI's Characters and doors feature has the `Vehicle doors` row, the door each hand reaches, tear counts, and pressure state; the log has the grips, unlatches, and tears under `physical hands: doors:`.
