# The IK body

While Rico stands on foot, his lower body is posed from the learned body estimate rather than the game's idle clip: the pelvis goes where forward kinematics on the estimate puts the player's, the feet stay planted on the ground through HumanIK and move only by explicit steps, and the root follows the floor point under the pelvis. In the air the pelvis hangs under the head with the legs relaxed toward a light air pose. On the walk the clip keeps the body, with a share of the IK pose turning its pelvis. Everything else keeps what it had: the game's clips, the procedural torso, the arms, and the head.

The pure parts are in the `body-rig` crate (`fk`, `contact`, `ik_weight`), and the game-facing parts in `payload/src/controllers/body/ik_body/`. The settings are `controllers.body.ik`, and the feature is on by default. The design it implements, and the pitfalls of the procedural legs removed on 2 November that it is built to avoid, are in the 14 November session's feedback (`docs/sessions/2026-11-14_02-05/feedback/body.md`, "The IK-first on-foot body: a design").

## The FK pelvis

The estimator ([body-estimator.md](body-estimator.md)) gives the global rotation of each of the 22 SMPL joints in the cockpit frame, but no translation: the network places the body by aligning its head joint with the tracked head. The pelvis is therefore found by forward kinematics down from the head (`body_rig::fk`). On an SMPL skeleton whose offsets are the neutral template's, scaled so the chain from the pelvis to the head is as long as the rig's spine from the waist pivot to the neck pivot and the legs stand the ankles at their height under hips at the waist pivot's height, the pelvis is the tracked neck pivot less the chain under the estimated spine and neck rotations. The hips hang from the pelvis, the knees from the hips, and the ankles from the knees under the estimated hip and knee rotations. A player who bends over gets a pelvis behind and below the head; one who crouches gets one lower under it.

The root's frame half advances it once per rendered frame in the rig's room frame, before the grounded rig (`ik_body::fk`), and publishes the body relative to the neck pivot. A critically damped spring with a smoothing time of `fk.smooth_s` (0.12 s) follows each point, which takes out the estimate's jitter between windows. When the estimate goes stale (older than half a second) the points ease over `fk.fallback_blend_s` (0.3 s) to a fallback, the grounded rig's waist pivot with the legs hanging straight, so nothing snaps; the published source reads `estimate`, `blending`, or `fallback`. The IK takes the standing body only while the source is the estimate.

## Seeding the pose

The procedural body's solve gate ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md)) writes Rico's pose before HumanIK solves. The IK body works there in two halves (`ik_body::gate`, `ik_body::effectors`).

Before the torso is solved, the FK body is carried into Rico's model space: the player's offsets from the neck pivot turned from the room frame into the model's by the view's rotation against the character's, scaled by Rico's spine over the player's for the pelvis and the hips and by Rico's legs over the player's for the knees and the ankles, and hung from the neck pivot the torso's solve reaches for. The hand-over works with positions, not rotations, so no bone's rest frame has to be known: the clip's pelvis frame is read from its upper legs and its first spine bone, the FK pelvis's from its hips and first spine joint, and the clip's hips are turned by the difference between the two and moved onto the FK pelvis, carrying the whole posture with them (`ik_body::seed`). Where a planted foot would be further from its hip than the leg reaches, the pelvis comes down until it is in reach, at most `max_pelvis_drop_m` (0.2 m). The torso's anchored solve then runs on this seeded posture rather than the clip's, so the spine bends from the player's pelvis toward the player's neck and the clip's forward lean is gone with its hips.

After the pose is written, HumanIK's targets hold it through the solve. The hips get a position target at the seeded hips with a translation reach of `hips_reach` and a zero rotation offset with a rotation reach of `hips_rotation_reach` and a resistance of `hips_resist` (all 1). [`HumanIK::UpdateEffectorsFromTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-UpdateEffectorsFromTargets) applies a rotation target as an offset onto the pose as it stands before the solve, which is the seeded pose, so a zero offset holds the seeded pelvis's orientation. Each foot with a contact gets an ankle target and a toe target ahead of it along the foot's heading, with a reach of `foot_reach` (1) and a pull of `foot_pull` (0), so reaching a foot moves only its leg; each knee gets a weak hint along the estimate's thigh with a reach of `knee_hint_reach` (0.2). The effectors are found from Rico's bones through [`HumanIK::GetEffectorIdFromBoneIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-GetEffectorIdFromBoneIndex).

The game's locomotion IK task ([`NLocoIKTask_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/animation::arm_aim_ik##func-NLocoIKTask_Update)) queues hip and foot targets every on-foot tick before the gate. A pass holds one target per effector, and a later queue overwrites the earlier one in place ([`HumanIK::AddEffectorTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-AddEffectorTargetPosition)), so each IK target replaces the game's: at full weight it is the IK's, and at a partial weight it is the game's own target (or the bone where the game queued none) mixed toward the IK's, with the reach, pull, rotation reach, and resistance mixed the same way and the game's rotation offset scaled by what is left. The targets are queued without the blend-out flag, so on the first tick the IK stops queuing one it is dropped rather than left steering while its reach decays, and the game's task takes the effector back with its next target.

## Feet

Each foot is planted, swinging, or unsupported (`body_rig::contact`). A planted foot is a pose in its support's frame with the support's identity, so it stays where it is however the pelvis moves; a swinging foot has a planned landing, an arc `contact.arc_m` (7 cm) high at its middle, and a duration; an unsupported foot is left to the clip and the game's foot IK. An unsupported foot is planted where the clip has it, on the ground under it, which is how the contacts are acquired when the IK takes the standing body and after a step that found no ground.

A planted foot is released into a step only by a rule, evaluated in priority order:

- out of reach: the FK hip is further from the ankle than `contact.reach_share` (1.02) of the leg's straight length;
- balance: the centre of mass, `contact.com_neck_share` (0.3) of the way from the pelvis to the neck, is further than `contact.com_margin_m` (5 cm) outside the support polygon of both feet's heels and toes;
- the root carried: something other than the player (the root's travel the FK pelvis did not make) has moved the root more than `contact.root_tolerance_m` (0.2 m) since the last landing;
- twist: the pelvis's heading is further than `contact.twist_deg` (40 degrees) from the feet's, and has stayed so for `contact.twist_hold_s` (0.3 s), so a glance that turns the hips a little moves no foot;
- tidy: once the body is at rest, a foot further than `contact.tidy_m` (15 cm) or `contact.tidy_deg` (25 degrees) off its stance spot for `contact.tidy_s` (1 s).

One foot swings at a time, and when both want to step the one that did not step last goes. A twist to the left opens with the left foot. A step after any rule but the tidy is followed at once by the other foot if it is left off its stance spot by half the tidy's thresholds, so a body turn is two steps. A step takes `contact.step_s` (0.3 s) and lands at its stance spot under the FK pelvis, `stance.width_m` across from its partner and turned out by `stance.toe_out_deg` (the square stance's values), led along the pelvis's travel by `contact.lead` (0.5) of the swing. The landing's ground is probed: the gate does not cast, so each foot's probe is posted to a mailbox and cast from the player aim control's post-simulation update, where the head probe casts (`ik_body::probe`), and the answer reaches the contact model a tick or two later, well within the swing. A step whose answer has not come by its landing waits up to `contact.landing_wait_s` (0.2 s). A probe that finds no ground within 0.9 m below leaves the foot unsupported, never planted.

When the movement is a walk rather than a step, the contacts are dropped and the legs handed to the game's walk: both feet out of reach at once while the pelvis moves faster than `contact.walk_speed_mps` (0.8 m/s), a step longer than `contact.max_step_m` (0.6 m), or the planted foot's hip carried more than `contact.outrun_m` (0.1 m) past its reach while the other swings. The IK then stays on the walk until Rico has stood still for `resume_s` (0.4 s) with the grounded rig's step drive at rest.

## Moving supports

A probe names the body it lands on as the foot's support (`ik_body::support`). A static body is the world, whose frame is the identity. A body that moves (a vehicle's deck, a keyframed platform, a loose crate) is its own support, named by its id in the Havok world and the lifetime stamp the hand climbing keeps for it ([hand-climbing.md](../input/hand-climbing.md#moving-bodies)), so a freed body whose id is reused is a different support and the foot on it is lost. The gate does not read the physics world, so the probe's update snapshots each moving support the gate has asked about in the last two seconds, its transform from the body's slot and its centre of mass and velocities from its motion, the same reads the climb's moving grips make. The gate carries the snapshot forward to its own moment by the body's linear velocity and its spin about its centre of mass, at most a tenth of a second; a snapshot older than a quarter of a second, or a body that has changed hands, stands for no support, and its planted foot is unsupported (`a foot's support is gone`).

A planted foot is held in its support's frame and rebuilt in the world each gate, so a deck carries it, and a swing is held in the frame of the support it left, so a step taken on a moving deck lands on the deck. The body standing on the support is carried too, so the rules read the body's own travel against it (`body_rig::contact::SupportFrame::carry`): the pelvis's velocity, which the walk's speed and a step's lead read, is its velocity on the deck, not the deck's, and the root's drift for the root rule is the root's travel the pelvis did not make once the deck's motion has been taken out of both. The feet's targets are queued in Rico's model space at the gate, which the render interpolates like the rest of the pose, so nothing is rebuilt at render time.

## The root

While the IK owns the standing body (its pelvis position's weight past one half), the grounded rig's drive follows the floor point under the FK pelvis rather than committing steps after the neck ([rig.md](rig.md#steps)): the rig's step phase reads `following`, the gap is the pelvis's to that point, and the drive closes it at the gap over `step.follow_response_s` (0.12 s), capped at the drive's top speed, with no walk on the move stick. A bend moves the FK pelvis little in the room, so it makes no root motion; a physical step moves it, the root follows, and the feet step for themselves. The ledger, the cockpit shift, the push-back, and the recenter's snap are as before. The step commit, the lean, the settle, and the idle drift take the root back whenever the IK does not own it: without an estimate, in gamepad-only play, on the walk, and during the blend.

## Per state

The state comes from the procedural body's hand-over case and the body's animation state, and each state asks for a weight on each channel (`body_rig::ik_weight`): the pelvis's position, the pelvis's orientation, the spine, the planted feet, and the airborne legs. The weights move toward the state's at a constant rate, so every hand-over takes `blend_s` (0.25 s) whichever way it goes, and a partial weight mixes local rotations rather than model-space positions, so no segment shortens mid-blend.

| State | When | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| idle | on foot, standing still, in one of the standing idles, with a fresh estimate | all but the airborne legs at 1 |
| walk | on foot with the stick or the game's walk moving Rico, or after a hand-over to the walk | the pelvis's orientation and the spine at `walk_weight` (0.2) |
| clip | on foot outside the standing idles (a landing, a turn, a weapon's act), an authored motion, a hit reaction, or a fall the torso has handed back | none |
| airborne | a jump, or a fall while the torso is still the player's, with `airborne` on | the pelvis, the spine, and the airborne legs at 1 |
| unchanged | every other case, and whenever the IK stands down | none |

On the walk the clip's hips turn a fifth of the way to the FK pelvis's orientation (yaw, pitch, and roll) about their own position, and the torso's solve on them carries the spine; the clip's feet keep the game's foot IK. The jump's takeoff is the clip's until the hand-over case reads a jump, which it does once the body leaves the ground, and the airborne body blends in from there. A landing reads as on foot in a landing state, so it is the clip's, and the contacts are acquired afresh where the feet stand once the state returns to a standing idle. In the air each ankle is drawn `air_relax` (half) of the way from the estimate's ankle toward a light air pose under its hip (the knee a little bent and forward), with a reach of `air_foot_reach` (0.6).

The IK stands down, and says why in the readout and the log, when it is switched off, the procedural torso is off (nothing would write the seeded pose), under the gamepad scheme (the estimator does not run, so gamepad-only play is unchanged), while the firm ragdoll owns the legs on the grapple line or under the canopy ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md)), without a fresh estimate, and in every case the table leaves to the game: seats, the parachute, the wingsuit, swimming, traversals, ragdolls, get-ups, and hand climbing. A teleport or a recenter drops the contacts; they are acquired again on the next standing tick.

While the IK owns the standing body the square stance yields the feet to it by the feet's weight, and stays as the standing body's feet whenever the IK does not have them ([procedural-body.md](procedural-body.md#the-stance)). The act filter keeps the game's turn-in-place acts dropped while the IK owns the standing body, so a body turn is the twist rule's turn steps rather than a clip's turn ([locomotion.md](locomotion.md#the-turning-animations)).

## Settings and readout

The debug UI's Body fit settings hold the switches `IK body standing on foot` (`enabled`) and `IK body in the air` (`airborne`), both on, and an `IK body` section with the walk's share, the blend, the resume time, the step's duration and lift, the release thresholds, the longest step and the walk's speed, the pelvis's drop, and HumanIK's reach, resistance, and pull for the hips, the feet, and the knees, the air pose's share, and the pelvis's smoothing. The Body fit readout shows the state and why the IK stands down, where the pelvis comes from, each channel's weight and how many targets the gate queued, each foot's state, the planted count, the steps and their rate over the last minute, the hand-overs and the missed probes, the last step's foot and reason, how far the seeded hips stand from the clip's, how far the pelvis was lowered, and whether the feet stand on the world or a moving body, how often they have come to stand on one, and how many moving supports are tracked.

## Logs and the recording

At info level, under the `ik body` source, the log reports each change of state (`takes the standing body`, `takes the airborne body`, `blends onto the walk`, `stands down`, with the reason and the case), each step (`a foot steps`, with the foot, the rule, the step's length, and the estimate's weight), each hand-over to the walk (with the reason and the pelvis's speed), each landing without ground and each lost support, each change of the feet's support between the world and a moving body (`the feet's support changes`), and the contacts dropped at a teleport or a recenter. Every ten seconds of a standing spell it logs `ik body: standing` with each foot's state, the planted count, the spell's steps and rate, the feet's weight, the hips' move from the clip's, the pelvis's drop, the targets queued, the estimate's weight, whether the feet stand on a moving body, and the pelvis's speed on its support, and each spell ends with `ik body: the standing body ends`, its length, steps, rate, hand-overs, and largest drop. Landings and acquisitions are at debug level.

The report lines are noted in the recording as diagnostic events, and the rig's sample names the drive's phase, which reads `following` on every frame the IK owns the root, so a recording carries the IK's transitions and steps with no change to the format. The final pose the recording samples shows where the solve put the feet against the steps' landings.

## Unverified

Nothing here has run in the game yet. How HumanIK answers a held pelvis and two planted feet (the controlled stand) is the first unknown: whether the zero rotation offset with full resistance holds the pelvis while the feet reach, whether the torso written before the solve stays where the anchored solve put it, and whether the knee hints keep the knees forward. The gains are in the debug UI for that reason. The FK skeleton's proportions, Rico's ankle height over the ground (read from his reference pose's ankle over its toe, plus a generic toe joint height), the effectors the bone lookup finds for the hips and the knees, and the thresholds are first estimates, and the offline replay of a recording with the contact model, which the design names as the first stage, has not been run.

## Headset checks

1. Standing still, the body mirror on: the readout reads `idle`, both feet `planted`, and eight targets queued. Rico's pelvis sits under the player's, his feet stay put on the ground, and his torso is not pulled about. If the hips or the torso fight, note the log's `hips_moved_m` and try `Hips resist` and `Hips turn reach` at lower values, then `Feet pull` above zero.
2. Bending over to look down and crouching: no foot steps, the pelvis goes back and down, the knees bend forward. If the feet float or sink, report the readout's pelvis drop; if the knees bend backward or sideways, raise `Knee hints`.
3. Glancing about and turning the hips a little: no foot steps. Turning the body and holding it: two turn steps, the left foot first for a turn to the left, and no game turn-in-place clip.
4. A physical step aside, forward, and back: one or two steps, feet alternating, landing square under the pelvis; the root follows without a slide. Record the steps per minute standing (the target is a few a minute).
5. Walking off physically and with the stick: the readout reads `walk` with a hand-over or `walking`, the game's walk plays, and stopping brings the feet back in planted with no snap.
6. Steps and slopes: steps up and down a kerb land on its height; a step over a ledge leaves the foot unsupported (`a step's landing has no ground` in the log) rather than planted in the air.
7. A jump and a fall: the takeoff and the landing are the clip's, the pelvis hangs under the head in the air, the legs relax, and the standing body comes back after the landing.
8. Standing on a parked truck's bed or a boat's deck: the readout reads `a moving body` and the log `the feet's support changes`. Have it driven off: the feet stay planted on the deck with no step and no hand-over while Rico stands still, a physical step lands on the deck, and the standing log's `pelvis_speed_mps` stays near zero. If the feet lag the deck or step on every turn, note the speed and whether `a foot's support is gone` appears.
9. The grapple line and the canopy keep the firm ragdoll's legs; seats, swimming, the wingsuit, and the parachute are unchanged; gamepad-only play is unchanged.
