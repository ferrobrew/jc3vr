# Taking hold of characters

An empty hand can take hold of a character. A grip on a standing character throws them into their ragdoll, and the hand holds the ragdoll by the bone nearest the palm, to be dragged and thrown; while it holds, the game's get-up is held off, and let go they get up the game's own way. A body on the floor, knocked down or dead, is gripped by the limb nearest the palm. A character seated in a vehicle is reached through an open door, where a yank is the game's own hijack, or through a window the hands brought down ([breaking-glass.md](breaking-glass.md)), where a yank pulls them out as a ragdoll. A character the game protects is not taken at all. The grapple's snatch swings a held body on its line ([grapple-snatch.md](grapple-snatch.md#swinging-on-the-line)).

The code is `crates/jc3vr-physical/src/controllers/physical_hands/characters/` and the grips in `physical_hands/pickup/grips.rs`, on top of the pickup and holding ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#picking-things-up)) and the doors ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md)); the engine side is in [melee-and-damage.md](../../engine/gameplay/melee-and-damage.md#the-hit-reaction), [ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md), and [vehicle-doors.md](../../engine/gameplay/vehicle-doors.md#entering-through-a-door). The tuning is the `characters` section of `crates/jc3vr-physical/tuning/characters.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). It is always on under the controller scheme.

## Reaching a character

The pickup's reach rays find characters as well as objects: a hit on a character carries the character and the body hit. A gun in the character's hands or holstered on its body is the weapons' own target and wins over the character whenever it is within its reach ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md#taking-a-gun-from-a-characters-hands), [a holstered gun](physical-weapons.md#a-holstered-gun)). What a hit is depends on the character:

- A character the game protects is never a target ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#protected-characters), `physics::characters::protection` in the body crate): one a mission marks as critical ([`m_IsMissionCriticalCharacter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_IsMissionCriticalCharacter)), one a scripted sequence drives ([`m_InSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_InSequence)), and the player. The grip's notes say why.
- A character on foot, hit on its proxy's capsule ([`PfxCharacterInstance::GetCharacterRigidBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-GetCharacterRigidBodyId)), is thrown into their ragdoll by the grip, or held on to ([below](#holding-on-and-yanking)).
- A character that ragdolls ([`Character::IsRagdolling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsRagdolling)), fallen or dead, is taken by a body of its ragdoll ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#the-ragdoll-instance)): the body the ray hit when it is one of the ragdoll's dynamic bodies, otherwise the one whose centre is nearest the hit, since the ray may meet the capsule or the hit proxy instead (`characters::ragdoll`).
- A dead character still in its death animation is not taken, nor a hit on a body of a standing character's ragdoll, which its animation drives.
- A character attached to a vehicle ([`Character::IsAttachedToVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsAttachedToVehicle)) is a target only when one of that vehicle's doors stands open at least `characters.hijack_min_open_deg` ([`PfxBreakable::GetHingeAngleAboveMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetHingeAngleAboveMin)) with its box's centre within `characters.hijack_door_reach_m` of the palm, or when the hand came in through a window of that vehicle the glass brought down ([through a broken window](#through-a-broken-window)). Intact glass is never a way in.

Whether the player's faction is hostile to the character ([`GetRelation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/faction##func-GetRelation)) is judged and logged; it decides nothing. The grip, the yank, the blows, and the shove treat every character alike ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#hits)).

## Holding a ragdoll

A ragdoll weighs as a whole, but the hand holds one body, and the rest hangs from it by the ragdoll's constraints. The joint between the hand's body and the bone carries the bone alone (`pickup::tick::joined_mass_kg`): the drive asks for the bone's momentum and weight within the hand's strength, and the rest of the body's weight reaches the joint through the ragdoll's constraints over the step, so the body hangs limp from the point gripped. A drive that asked for the whole body's momentum through the bone would overshoot several times over each step until the bone passed the tear distance. The hold still records the whole ragdoll's mass (`characters::ragdoll::Ragdoll::mass_kg`) for whether the hands lift it and how it is thrown, with the hand's strength scaled by `characters.ragdoll_carry_strength`. The softer pull, the point joints of a two-handed hold, and the damping a held ragdoll gets within itself (at `characters.held_ragdoll_damping_per_s`) are in [physical-hands.md, "Holding through a joint"](physical-hands.md#holding-through-a-joint).

A character dragged, swung, and thrown this way can be hurt by what its ragdoll hits, as far as the game's ragdoll impacts harm a ragdolling character; the topple itself does no damage.

## Throwing a character into their ragdoll

A grip on a standing character's capsule throws them into their ragdoll as it takes hold (`characters::topples`), when the character is alive and its state does not keep it out of its ragdoll ([`SB_SKIP_HITREACTION_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_SKIP_HITREACTION_RAGDOLL)). The throw is the character's own hit reaction ([`Character::HitReaction`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HitReaction)) from the grab point, a blow without damage of `characters.topple_strength` times the hand blows' knockdown, calibrated against the character's own knockdown threshold, so it falls just past the knockdown rather than flying. It goes along the hand's motion when the hand moves at least `characters.shove_min_speed_mps`, otherwise level toward the hand (`characters::topple_direction`). Because it is the game's own knockdown, the game's ragdoll, AI, and get-up handle it as any knockdown.

The hand then waits for the ragdoll as after a yank: it stays at the grab point, following the falling body, and takes the bone nearest the palm as soon as its reach finds one (`a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll`). The hit reaction returns without ragdolling in some states the topple cannot see (an ally holding her gun), so a character still standing `characters.ragdoll_retry_after_s` into the wait has the ragdoll act queued directly, once: the hit reaction's own ragdoll act where the state takes it, else the full ragdoll (`physics::characters::retry_ragdoll`, logged as `the topple left the character standing; retried the ragdoll`). Letting go leaves the ragdoll to the game: once it comes to rest, or its own timer runs out, the get-up stands the character up and its AI carries on ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#out-of-the-ragdoll)). A dead character stays down.

## Holding the get-up off

The get-up does not wait for the hand: the graph stands a character up on its ragdolling timers, the total time ragdolling and the time settled ([ragdoll.md](../../engine/character/ragdoll.md#out-of-the-ragdoll)), and a held body counts as settled once its limbs move together, which the damping hastens. Left alone, it ends a two-handed hold after a few seconds, the ragdoll's bodies going back under the character's control and both joints ending.

The timers sit in the character's control parameters ([`RagdollingControllerParams`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::RagdollingControllerParams)). So on each pickup tick while a hand holds a character by its ragdoll, once per character however many hands hold it, the hold writes the total ragdolling time below zero and the settled time to zero (`characters::get_up`, `pickup::held_ragdolls`). Below zero is the value the ragdolling state task keeps while the ragdoll's owner moves it, which the timer condition never holds on; a settled time of zero keeps the settled condition from holding. The game's update between two pickup ticks advances them by one frame at most. Let go, and the timers run again from there. The game's cancel input keeps only the player's own ragdoll, so the hold does not press it.

Each hold of a ragdoll that ends is logged with what the character does next: `physical hands: characters: the game's get-up ended a hand's hold of a ragdoll` at warn level when it is getting up ([`SB_IS_GETTING_UP_FROM_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_GETTING_UP_FROM_RAGDOLL)) or already stands, and `a hand's hold of a ragdoll ended` otherwise, both with the time held, whether the hold wrote the timers, and the loss. The first write of a hold logs `holding a held character's ragdolling timers off its get-up`. A get-up warning with `timers_held=true` means the graph stood the character up by a path that reads neither timer.

## Holding on and yanking

For a character whose state keeps it on its feet, a grip on its capsule holds the hand at the grab point, carried with the character's own frame, as a latched door holds it ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-latch)). Pulling the controller `characters.yank_m` away from the grab point, in any direction, is a yank, and ends the grip.

A yank on a character on foot pushes them along the yank through their own hit reaction, as a blow without damage of `characters.yank_strength` times the hand blows' knockdown (`hand_bodies.hits.knockdown_impulse_ns`), calibrated as a blow is. The default reaches past a knockdown, so the character falls as a ragdoll pushed along the yank unless its thresholds or state keep it standing. After a yank the hand stays at the grab point for up to `characters.regrip_s` of game time, so slow motion stretches it with the fall, and takes hold of the first body of that character's ragdoll its reach finds; if none comes within reach in time it lets go.

Letting go of a character on foot without a yank, with the hand moving at least `characters.shove_min_speed_mps`, shoves them along the hand's motion: a blow without damage of an arm of `hand_bodies.hits.blow_mass_kg` at the hand's speed.

The reactions are queued from the palms' tick and dealt on the next frame tick, where the blows are dealt ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md#threading)); the character is found again from the body the hand held, so a character freed since is never touched.

A yank on a seated character, through a door still open beside the hand, has the next input tick click the vehicle-entry action ([`ENTER_VEHICLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-ENTER_VEHICLE)), as the use button does on foot: the game takes the player in by the entry nearest the player, and an occupied seat is hijacked with the game's own animation. A yank after the door has swung shut only lets go.

A mounted gun's gunner is not hijacked, since the vehicle-entry action would seat the player elsewhere and leave the gunner. A yank on a character who mans a mounted gun, in a vehicle's gunner seat or on a standalone tripod ([`Character::IsInMountedGunState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInMountedGunState)), queues the full ragdoll ([`ACT_FULL_RAGDOLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable)) once the seat's state accepts it (`physics::characters::gunner_out`), since the hit reaction never ragdolls a character attached to a vehicle and the standing path's ragdoll never takes a tripod's gunner off the gun. The gunner falls out where they sit, and the hand takes hold of the ragdoll as after any yank. A protected gunner, or one whose seat takes no full ragdoll, stays, and the hand lets go; the log says which. Stepping into the emptied seat is in [vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md#the-gunner-seat).

## Through a broken window

A window the hands have shattered is hidden, not removed: its glass shapes still answer ray queries, and the physical hands' casts step past a pane the glass recorded as brought down ([breaking-glass.md](breaking-glass.md#bullets-through-a-pane-brought-down)). The glass does not collide with the hands either way, so whether a hand came in through a broken window is judged by casting along its way in (`characters::pane`): from the eyes to the palm, and on from the palm to the point the reach found on the occupant (`physical_hands::query::crosses_hidden_pane`). A leg that passes a brought-down pane of the occupant's vehicle before anything solid is a way in. The casts run only once the glass has brought a pane down, and only for an enclosed occupant with no open door beside the hand. So, standing on the hood with the windshield shattered, a hand reaching down through it reaches the driver, and one reaching through the roof does not. A grip taken this way keeps the seat for as long as the character keeps it, and the readout says `the character sits behind a window the glass brought down`.

A yank on an occupant reached through a broken window does not hijack. It takes them out of the seat as a ragdoll by the full ragdoll, queued once the seat's state accepts it, as for a gunner (`characters::unseat`, `physics::characters::seat_out`), and the hand takes hold of the ragdoll to drag through the opening: `physical hands: characters: a hand pulled an occupant out of the seat through a broken window`. An occupant the game protects, or whose seat takes no full ragdoll, stays, and the hand lets go: `an occupant reached through a broken window stays in the seat`.

A punch through a broken window is treated as a punch on a motorcycle rider. The game's hit reaction never ragdolls a character attached to a vehicle, so a knockdown on a rider, or on an enclosed occupant whose hand came in through a window of that vehicle the glass brought down (judged by the cast from the eyes to the hand at the contact), queues the full ragdoll in its place (`physics::blows::exposed_seat`): `hand bodies: a punch through a broken window knocked an occupant out of the seat`, and the blow's line carries `rider_reaction_queued`.

## Getting in

With a door open, the use button enters the vehicle as it always does, by the entry nearest the player, so the side whose door the hand opened is the side the player gets in by. A hand still holding that door lets go when the player sits down, since the hands stand down in a vehicle.

## When the grip lets go

A grip on a character lets go with the grip, with a yank (unless it waits for the ragdoll), when the hand stands down, and when the character's capsule no longer belongs to the character. A hold on a ragdoll's body lets go as any hold does.

## Readouts and logs

The debug UI's Characters and doors feature opens with the `Characters` status row (under `Hands in the world`), which counts the grips, the characters thrown down by a topple or a yank, the knockdowns of the yanks and shoves, the ragdolls dragged, and the occupants pulled out; beneath it, the readout says when a hand reaches a seated or standing character and behind which door.

The log has, at info level under `physical hands: characters:`, `a hand took hold of a character` with whether they are seated, `a hand's grip threw a character into their ragdoll`, `a hand yanked a character`, `a hand threw a character` with the share of a knockdown and the reaction impulse, `a hand took hold of a ragdoll` and `a hand took hold of a fallen character's ragdoll` with the body, the masses, and whether it is joined, `a hand's grip on a character could not be placed`, and `no ragdoll came within the hand's reach after a yank`; and under `physical hands: hijack:`, `a hand pulled an occupant out through the open door` and `the door beside the hand closed before the yank`. Each carries the hostility where it applies.
