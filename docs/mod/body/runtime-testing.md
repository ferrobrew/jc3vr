# Integrated runtime testing

The replacement paths run under the existing enabled controller, full-body, weapon-handling, and camera settings. Existing saved switches remain effective. New tracking recordings use lossless chunked version 4 by default; an explicitly saved compression-off setting still selects raw version 4.

## Runtime coverage

- Ordinary on-foot full-body updates use the constrained temporal fit at the body solve gate. The root movement executor consumes the same committed result once. Native support observations and bounded per-foot terrain casts enter contact transitions and final foot realization. The body-follow switch still disables inferred root movement. Tracked head and interaction-hand targets remain authoritative. Missing support observations leave inferred feet released. See [terrain-gait.md](terrain-gait.md) for support transport and contact limits.
- Free parachute-handle targets use native resting joint observations captured during the game's existing sync. Held steering keeps its grip-derived behavior. Missing or expired native observations temporarily withhold the free target instead of substituting shoulder geometry.
- Authored camera following admits exact takeoff, landing, recovery, floor-pickup, and hanging states, including the verified pre-inversion to attached upside-down grapple sequence. The numbered native channel is applied relative to entry before tracked head motion. Native clip time and contribution observations detect same-state restarts and outgoing blend tails. Pending zero-weight handoffs retain entry across repeated unchanged finalized samples. Unsupported exits return to the normal tracked view. Completion eligibility must cover the evaluated camera channel; game acceptance remains unperformed.
- Magazine grip-and-pull, button ejection, flick ejection, and belt replacement execute detachment in the character's native serial post-physics pass. The callback revalidates the current weapon and spare identity. No extra magazine latch button is required. Magazine and chamber ownership partition the existing native loaded total: extraction leaves a live chamber on the weapon, and magazine-out firing can consume it once. Policy deactivation, pair splitting, and pouch/belt capacity checks share that accounting.
- A grenade draw creates an inert model item without consuming ammunition. Each hand can hold a separate grenade. Hip/chest stow, hand transfer, and inert world drop retain its identity. Deliberate throwing release creates and launches the native explosive; incidental release leaves an inert item.
- Qualifying finite and unlimited paired racks expose their two native gun models as independent source members. Taking one does not adopt its neighbour. Finite staging requests one empty copy and divides the source's actual ammunition on the first accepted take. The original notifies its placement rule on the final pickup. See [paired-rack-weapons.md](../input/paired-rack-weapons.md). An external same-tag mission give racing the callback remains ambiguous.
- Supported ready guns expose their manual slide, bolt, or pump action without requiring an empty chamber. Rear-stop ejection and completed feeding conserve loaded rounds. An open gun grip during a held action remains pending until the action ends; regrip or an ownership change cancels it.
- The shipped Massos static grenade shelf models provide safe-item sources at copied native instance transforms. Source reads discover already-loaded game-owned locations as well as streamed collections. Collection identity, model resource validation, and retirement bound source lifetime. Other unrecognized decoration does not become a source.
- Vehicle canopy/stunt defaults use physical right A for either dominance choice. Land handbrake defaults to physical left A, and explicit user bindings remain effective. Native seat eligibility remains contextual.
- Human collision hulls narrow horizontal bounds while retaining height, center, user data, and state-local contact policy. The debug physical-hands controls expose the runtime switch. Each native pre-simulation pass discovers live human owners and distinct shared settings, including existing NPCs. Construction hooks cover future settings.

## Collider restoration

Disable and shutdown request restoration through the native pre-simulation phase. Native convex distance queries and bounded compressed-terrain leaf traversal check each original shape before any body changes. Equivalent shallow floor contact can remain; new wall or ceiling penetration blocks restoration. Unsupported geometry, incomplete traversal, invalid query output, or an exhausted budget defers restoration and keeps normal gameplay and hooks running. No teleport or forced expansion is used. See [human-hull-restoration.md](human-hull-restoration.md).

After successful restoration, shutdown validates the copied preparation generation before teardown. A rejected body write rolls back all attempted changes. A rejected rollback refuses unload. An active native callback after hook removal keeps the image mapped. Unsupported or large terrain can still defer ejection. A process restart is not counted as successful live restoration.

## Test order

1. Load an ordinary on-foot scene and confirm the constrained-fit status advances. Walk physically, bend, crouch, turn, and reach with one hand. Test slopes, stairs, edges, moving platforms, and support loss. Confirm a released foot can step and plant again after the support changes. Toggle body follow off and verify that inferred root movement stops.
2. Repeat takeoff, landing, recovery, floor pickup, and hanging. Verify that head motion remains independent and that entering a sequence a second time still follows it.
3. Open the canopy, acquire both resting handles, steer, release, and reacquire. Compare the target markers with visible native rest.
4. Extract a loaded magazine by grip and pull, abort a partial pull, reinsert it, and verify chamber and magazine ammunition. Repeat button, flick, and belt operations, including weapon changes while an operation is pending. Rack a ready slide, bolt, and pump repeatedly. Check round ejection and feeding, partial pulls, release during an action, regrip cancellation, and ordinary drop from a pump foregrip at home.
5. Draw safe grenades into both hands at zero stock, transfer and stow them, drop one without throwing, recover it, and deliberately throw the other. Test the recorded Massos shelf, leave its streaming range, and return. Confirm no stock refund or deduction and no fuse on the inert items.
6. At qualifying finite and unlimited paired racks, take each member in either order with the opposite hand occupied and the holsters full. Confirm the other member stays at the source and total ammunition remains conserved. Leave and return after the first take, then repeat across a reset.
7. Inject with existing NPCs nearby, then stream in more NPCs. Exercise narrowed collision in standing, crouching, swimming, and wingsuit states. Request restoration on clear flat ground and beside an obstruction. A deferred request must leave gameplay responsive and must not partially restore bodies. Move clear, retry, eject, and reinject.
8. Capture full-rate tracking with joints while exercising these paths. Compare stored/uncompressed bytes, queue/drop counters, shutdown recovery, and replay. No real-capture compression performance result is established yet.

Compiler checks and pure tests do not establish the native callback schedule, model factories, live physics response, or headset comfort. The runtime paths are connected so these behaviors can be tested together.
