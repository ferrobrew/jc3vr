# Enclosed vehicles (issue #15)

Open. Some vehicles, such as the CS Baltdjur armoured truck and the tanks, have no windows. The game's third-person camera does not need them, but the mod's camera is at the driver's eyes, inside the mesh, with no view of the road, obstacles, or enemies. The mod has no enclosed-vehicle handling yet. This page holds the engine findings the candidate approaches rest on.

## The vehicle camera path

[`Character::IsInDrivingVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInDrivingVehicleState) is true when the player's animation state is one of the driver-vehicle states (idle, forward to reverse, reverse to forward, and reverse) or when [`StateBitFlag::SB_IS_RIDING_MOTORBIKE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_RIDING_MOTORBIKE) is set in [`UserVM::m_CurrentStateBitFlags1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::UserVM##field-m_CurrentStateBitFlags1).

[`GameCameraManager::PushRenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-PushRenderContext), which pushes the gameplay camera's world transform to the engine camera, branches on it. On foot it applies the jitter filter; while driving it skips the filter (unless `s_use_jitter_filter_when_driving` is set) and passes the vehicle camera modifier's matrix straight to `CCameraManager::InitTransform` ([rendering.md §2.8](../engine/rendering/rendering.md#28-vehicle-camera-path)).

That modifier, [`GenericVehicleModifier`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::generic_vehicle_modifier::GenericVehicleModifier), serves every generic vehicle in one of three [`VehicleCameraState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::generic_vehicle_modifier::VehicleCameraState)s (driving, plane, and helicopter). It is a third-person follow camera: a position, a look-at offset, and a follow distance relative to the vehicle's matrix, with springs for lag, velocity-based offsets, collision, and a free-look stick.

## Telling an enclosed vehicle apart

The engine has no enclosed flag; the interior is mesh geometry, not metadata. [`VehicleType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleType) is too coarse (a convertible and an armoured truck are both `ECAR`), and its field on the vehicle is not mapped. [`Vehicle::m_VehicleTypeNameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleTypeNameHash), the hash of the vehicle's type name, is bound and identifies the model, so a curated list of enclosed models is the simplest test. A ray probe from the driver's head, or a search of the model for window materials near the seat, would avoid the list at a per-frame cost.

## Rendering from another camera

A render pass can draw from a camera other than the render camera: [`RenderPass::ExternalCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_pass::RenderPass##field-ExternalCamera) is copied into [`RenderPass::ExternalRenderCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_pass::RenderPass##field-ExternalRenderCamera) by `SaveRenderFrameData`, and the pass then sets that camera up and gives it to [`RenderPass::SetRenderContextCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_pass::RenderPass##func-SetRenderContextCamera) in place of the render camera. That is per pass, not a scene render. The reflection passes render from other viewpoints, but only depth, normals, and gloss for the reflections, not a colour image. A full exterior view means another scene render over the passes from [`RP_Z_OCCLUDERS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_Z_OCCLUDERS) to [`RP_POSTEFFECTS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderPassId##variant-RP_POSTEFFECTS) ([`RenderEngine::DrawRenderPassRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::render_engine::RenderEngine##func-DrawRenderPassRange)), with the same per-dispatch state handling as a stereo eye ([rendering.md §13](../engine/rendering/rendering.md#13-to-dispatch-one-correct-stereo-eye--checklist)).

## Candidate approaches

- A floating exterior camera: for an enclosed vehicle, place the head-tracked view at a fixed point behind and above the vehicle's matrix rather than at the driver's eyes, with a faded or eased transition and a short debounce on entry and exit. It costs no extra render. Letting the game's own follow camera through instead would lose head tracking.
- An exterior camera's image on a quad in the cabin, or filling the forward view, from an extra scene render at reduced resolution. It keeps the player in the seat, at the cost of a third view's render beside the two eyes.
