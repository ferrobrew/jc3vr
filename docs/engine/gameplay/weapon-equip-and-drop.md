# Weapon equip, pickup, and drop

The use-input pickup is in [item-pickup.md](item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon), the magazine and the reserve in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md), the attach bones in [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md), the weapon's user in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-weapons-user), and what keeps a weapon alive, the spawn reset, the gives, and the save in [weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md).

## One object for its whole life

A weapon is one [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase) in the world, held, or holstered. The inventory holds one weapon per slot ([`Inventory::m_Weapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Weapons)), and the weapon's state ([`WeaponBase::m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)) says where it is. The magazine ([`m_MagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MagazineAmmo)) travels with the object; the reserve belongs to the character.

The state setter ([`WeaponBase::SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState)) keeps the weapon's bodies in the physics world ([`WeaponBase::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-AddToWorld)) only in the dropped, mounted, and turret states, and wakes them for dropped. Every other state takes them out ([`RemoveFromWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-RemoveFromWorld)), as does a turret becoming dropped.

Each gun has its own body ([`WeaponData::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_PfxInstance)). Out of the world it keeps its id, its shape ([`hknpBody::m_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_shape)), and its motion, mass included. Putting it back ([`WeaponData::AddToWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-AddToWorld)) keeps or renews its collision system group, sets its layer for the state, makes it dynamic, and places it at the gun's world matrix ([`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)): the body's frame is the gun's model frame.

## The dual-wield weapon

A weapon of the dual-wield slot ([`E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD)) is one object with two guns, one per pistol ([`WeaponBase::m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData)). The attach-bone table puts the first gun in the right hand when wielding or dual-wielding (right hip holstered), and the second in the left hand only when dual-wielding (left hip otherwise) ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)). Everything else is shared: the state, the magazine, the fire interval and trigger state, the reload, the holder, and the drop. How the two guns take turns firing is in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md#which-gun-fires).

The equip dual-wields the slot for the player and for a character whose blackboard holds [`WeaponBase::DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##const-DUAL_WIELDER_BLACKBOARD_VALUE), and wields it for others. Wielding, it is one pistol:

- the visibility's base implementation ([`WeaponBase::SetVisibleBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetVisibleBase)) hides its guns past the first whenever it shows the weapon. A gun shows through its model instance's status bits ([`ModelInstance::m_Flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##field-m_Flags)), and a model part such as a slide shows only while its own gun's model does ([`WeaponComponentBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponComponentBase##vfunc-SetVisible));
- the tail of the shot keeps the next gun ([`WeaponBase::m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire)) on the first, and entering the wielding state resets it there.

The same base implementation hides the extra guns in every state for a non-player character without the blackboard value. A player's holstered or dual-wielded pair shows both guns. Nothing applies the rule between calls, so a state change keeps the old visibility until the next call; the animation's state tasks set the wielding state without one ([weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md#the-attach-bone-table-is-rewritten-every-pass)).

In the world it is one pistol: the drop virtual hides every gun past the first, as for any weapon of several guns, and `AddToWorld` adds only the first gun's body. The player picks it up as a pair again. Whether the player's animation accepts a wielding pair outside the states whose tasks set it is unverified.

## Completing a pickup

[`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem) takes the character's game-object subobject ([`Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##const-GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET)), ignoring other activators, and:

1. unequips the wielded weapon;
2. puts this weapon into the inventory with the equipping mode ([below](#the-inventorys-add));
3. selects the weapon's slot, adds its stored ammunition ([`m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo)) to the reserve, and plays the pickup vocal;
4. clears the stored ammunition.

It does not refill the magazine and plays no act. On land, [`PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart) first puts the weapon in the picking-up state, bodies out of the world, and the pickup track message calls `PickupItem` later. A swimming character's pickup drops the slot's occupant and calls it directly on a still-dropped weapon, then queues `ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON_PARTIAL` (see [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)).

Two kinds of weapon it does not add, though both lose their stored ammunition to it. A hand grenade is left to `PickupStart`, which adds its stored rounds to the reserve and despawns it whatever its infinite-pickup setting (it hides, leaves the physics world, goes to the spawn system's despawn, and sends its picked-up event), so a racked grenade never hands out a copy. A weapon with [`m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup) set gives the character a copy from `PickupStart`, spawned from the weapon's spawn tag ([Racks](#racks)).

## The inventory's add

Every way into a slot ends in [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon). It reads the weapon's slot ([`m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)); an occupant is dropped when the inventory's per-slot drop flag is set and otherwise despawned (a mounted gun is only released), so a slot emptied beforehand with `RemoveWeapon` ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#into-and-out-of-a-slot-without-a-drop)) loses nothing. It then stores the weapon and runs its pickup virtual, and with an equipping mode ([`EquipMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::EquipMode)) unequips the wielded weapon and equips the new one. The hand-grenade, planted-explosive, and beacon weapons are started using at once ([`StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing)): they are in use while holstered.

The pickup virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup)) alone takes a weapon into a character's keeping without a slot: it sets the holder, holsters it (bodies out of the world), disables its pickup interaction and hint, and for the locally controlled character sets the avatar ready for combat and shows the weapon in its slot's arm of the weapon selection ([`HUDUI::OnPickupWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-OnPickupWeapon)). [`HUDUI::InitWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-InitWeapons) rebuilds the selection from the slots.

The add has three callers:

- [`Character::PutWeaponIntoInventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PutWeaponIntoInventory), a direct call, used by the completed pickup (so every pickup from the ground, racks, and characters), a mounted gun's `StartUsing`, the weapon's track-message handling, and the supply drop's broken cargo.
- [`Character::PickUpWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-PickUpWeapon), which fills the magazine to the tuned size ([`WeaponBase::GetMagazineSize`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetMagazineSize)), adds the weapon, and with [`E_EQUIP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::EquipMode##variant-E_EQUIP) selects the slot ([`Character::SelectWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SelectWeapon)) for the dual-wield, two-handed, special, heavy, and FOW slots. The respawn hand-over and the spawn-and-equip callback call it ([the give](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-give)).
- The character's looting of its children (`CCharacter::LootChildren`).

## Equip and unequip

The equip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip)) shows the weapon, runs its components' equip, selects its slot, starts using it, puts the player's avatar into combat, and sets the state to wielding, or to dual-wielding for the player's dual-wield slot. It queues no act (the draw animation comes from the character's draw and the slot select), so a weapon equipped through it alone is in the hands at once.

The unequip virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip)) cancels the holder's reload, holsters, and stops using the weapon, with no act. A mounted gun keeps its state, and a heavy weapon is instead dropped by the inventory's drop of the heavy slot, whatever it holds, so a heavy weapon outside the slots is neither dropped nor holstered.

Both virtuals have base implementations ([`OnEquipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnEquipBase) and [`OnUnequipBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-OnUnequipBase)); the mortar, the rocket launcher, the planted explosive, and the fire-leech launcher override the equip, and the sun gun overrides the unequip. Neither checks the inventory's draw and holster permissions ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing) and [`GetAnimationAllowsHolstering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsHolstering)).

## Who equips and unequips

Every equip and unequip the game makes runs these virtuals:

- Equip: the inventory's add with an equipping mode; the draw clip's equip track message ([`WeaponBase::HandleTrackMessage`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-HandleTrackMessage)); the `NWeaponEquipTask` state task ([`NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponEquipTask_OnEnter)), which with nothing wielded switches to a weapon with ammunition or, instant, equips the first valid of the two-handed, dual-wield, and special slots, and which no state of Rico's graphs carries; the exit of the weapon-state task ([`NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NSetWeaponStateTask_OnExit)), which restores a wielding or dual-wielding state through the equip; and the deferred equip.
- Unequip: the `NWeaponUnequipTask` state task ([below](#vehicles-and-traversal)); the character's neutral state ([`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState)); the holster clip's unequip track message; the inventory's add when it equips another weapon; the pickup; the respawn hand-over ([`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned)); the despawn ([`WeaponBase::Despawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Despawn)); the drop virtual; and the deferred unequip.

`ForceNeutralState` drops the heavy weapon, stops using the planted explosive, unequips the wielded weapon, and cancels a primed grenade. It runs on every revive and respawn, at the start of every teleport ([`UpdateTeleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world##func-UpdateTeleport) and [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstant`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayerInstant)), on the vehicle exit's upward teleport, from the god mode and the walk-on-water mode, when a scripted sequence takes the character ([`Character::SetupForSequence`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetupForSequence)), when the character is enabled, from the network, from the AI's mission-critical teleport, and from the player's ragdoll constraint fix-up.

Every automatic unequip and the inventory's weapon tick start from the wielded weapon, [`Character::GetWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWieldingWeapon) ([weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-selected-slot)), so a weapon wielded in an unselected slot is invisible to them.

## Deferred operations

A weapon can queue an equip, unequip, start-using, stop-using, or drop for its next pre-simulation update, setting a flag ([`m_DeferredEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquip), [`m_DeferredUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUnequip), [`m_DeferredStartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStartUsing), [`m_DeferredStopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStopUsing), or [`m_DeferredDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredDrop), with the character in [`m_DeferredEquiper`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquiper) or [`m_DeferredUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUser)) and registering for the update ([`m_RequiresPreSimUpdates`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RequiresPreSimUpdates)). The requesters are few:

- [`EquipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-EquipDeferred): only the AI's equip action, so the game never defers an equip of the player's weapon;
- [`UnequipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UnequipDeferred): the AI's unequip and switch actions, the non-player branch of `NWeaponUnequipTask`, and the graph script's `SpawnWeapons` node, which is the only one to defer the player's unequip;
- [`StartUsingDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsingDeferred): only the mounted-weapon state task's entry (its stop-using counterpart is unverified);
- [`DropDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DropDeferred): only the inventory's drop.

The pre-simulation update ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePreSim)) runs, slot or not, the per-gun update ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)), the components', the deferred recoil impulse, and the deferred weapon-fire event ([weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-gunshot-the-ai-hears)), then the queued operations in this order: the drop runs the drop virtual; the unequip runs the unequip virtual and skips the guns' render interpolation; the equip runs the equip virtual with the deferred equipper and ends the update; a start-using and a stop-using queued together cancel each other; a start-using alone, for a weapon without a user, unequips the deferred user's wielded weapon and then starts using; and a stop-using alone runs the [`StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing) virtual on a weapon that has a user. With a user, it keeps the guns' animation at full detail while the user's [`m_LodFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_LodFlags) are clear, and otherwise drops it beyond a middle distance from the camera.

## Dropping

The inventory's drop of a slot ([`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon)) clears the slot, stops using the weapon when it is wielding or dual-wielding, and defers the drop; with its second argument set it then selects another weapon when the dropped slot was selected. The drop virtual ([`WeaponBase::OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop)), which the deferred drop runs, unequips a weapon still wielded by a user (leaving the player's precision aim), sets the dropped state, adds the bodies to the world and wakes them, gives them a velocity, clears the holder, enables the pickup interaction again, and hides every gun past the first. The velocity depends on the state once unequipped:

- holstered, with a character holder: the holder's velocity plus its forward direction scaled by the slot (forward for the two-handed slot, backward for the special slot, nothing for the others);
- still wielding or dual-wielding (a heavy weapon, which stopping does not holster): the first gun's measured velocity and angular velocity ([`WeaponData::m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) and `m_MeasuredAngularVelocity`), set on its body.

Since the inventory's drop stops using the weapon first, a held weapon other than a heavy one leaves with the holder's motion, not the hand's.

A swimming character's pickup calls the drop virtual directly, dropping the occupant with the slot still holding it. The deferral reads the state when the inventory's drop runs and skips an already-dropped weapon, which is so dropped once even if picked up in between.

## Racks

A weapon on a rack comes from a [`WeaponPlacementRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule). Once its weapon has spawned, the rule hands it its infinite-pickup setting ([`WeaponBase::m_InfinitePickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InfinitePickup)) and a weak reference to the rule ([`WeaponBase::m_SpawnedFromRule`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SpawnedFromRule)), which stays after it leaves the spot, and, for a rack, puts it in the rack state ([`E_WS_WEAPON_RACK`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WEAPON_RACK)), which keeps no body in the physics world and takes the gun's world matrix from the rule. A rule that places its weapon invisible hides it through the visibility virtual ([`WeaponBase::SetVisible`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetVisible)). A rack that does not use the ordinary pickup disables the weapon's own pickup interaction and offers it through an interaction-graph node that stands for the rule.

The player's interaction user proxy ([`Character::m_InteractionUserProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_InteractionUserProxy)) lists the nearby interaction-graph entries the player could take ([`InteractionUserProxy::m_ValidEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##field-m_ValidEntries)). The weapon an entry offers is its graph's context's parent object when that is a weapon ([`InteractionContext::m_Parent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionContext##field-m_Parent)), and otherwise, when the entry's node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex)) stands for a weapon placement rule, the weapon the rule spawned ([`WeaponPlacementRule::GetSpawnedObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/weapon_placement_rule::WeaponPlacementRule##vfunc-GetSpawnedObject)). A swimming character's pickup walks the entries this way, as does the player's weapon readout (unverified).

An entry is valid only while its graph's conditions pass: the proxy works through the entries it is validating ([`InteractionUserProxy::m_PotentialEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##field-m_PotentialEntries)) in [`ProcessPotentialEntries`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionUserProxy##func-ProcessPotentialEntries), flagged meanwhile by `m_IsValidatingEntry`. The pickup condition [`InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphParentWeaponNotInInventory##func-Evaluate) finds the weapon the first entry offers in the same two ways and fails when the character's inventory holds a weapon in that weapon's slot ([`WeaponBase::m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot)) of the same kind ([`WeaponBase::m_WeaponId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeaponId)). So a character is not offered a second weapon of a kind it carries, while another kind in the same slot is offered and replaces the occupant. Which graphs use the condition is inferred from behaviour (a rack's remaining weapon left the valid entries while one of its kind was carried, and returned when it was dropped), not read from graph data.

On land, the interaction's execution starts the pickup (`PickupStart`) and the pickup animation's track message completes it on the same weapon, found from the interaction in the same two ways (unverified). Starting a rack weapon's pickup puts it in the picking-up state and sends the rule's picked-up event. A rack with unlimited stock hands out copies: starting the pickup of its infinite-pickup weapon asks the inventory for a new weapon of the same spawn tag, with a magazine of the tuned size, equipped on arrival and dropping the weapon it replaces ([`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon), [the give](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-give)); the copy spawns a few frames later, and the rack's own weapon stays where it is.

## The drop's callers

[`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon) is called by the drop of every slot ([`Character::DropAllWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-DropAllWeapons), from the script's drop-all event and the player's reset to the default loadout), the destruction and death ([`Character::OnDestroyed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-OnDestroyed): its best weapons through [`Inventory::DropBestWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropBestWeapon), and its hand grenade when a random draw falls below [`m_DropGrenadeChance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_DropGrenadeChance), for a character other than the local player; the avatar's death handling drops nothing), the slot select, the neutral state, the `NDropWeaponStateTask` state task, the inventory's add replacing an occupant with the drop-old flag, the inventory's weapon handling (a heavy weapon on the holster or cancel input), the unequip of a heavy weapon and the sun gun's unequip, the single-use rocket launchers after their shot, and the network. The player's update also drops an empty Bavarium power-core weapon (unverified).

## Vehicles and traversal

Which weapons the inventory ticks in vehicles and the boost wingsuit is in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-shooting-permission). Rico's base graph puts weapons away through state tasks:

- `NWeaponUnequipTask` ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter)) acts on the wielded weapon alone. Its instant variant unequips at once (another character's weapon through the deferred unequip) on entering every vehicle entry, seat, and seat change, the motorcycle, pogo stick, and cow rides, the mounted-gun pickups and seats, the wingsuit openings, boosts, rolls, evades, and flinches, free fall and fast fall, the underwater dives, the hit-reaction flights, the ragdoll, the get-up, dying, and the guitar-case exit. Its non-instant variant queues the holster act on the mounted-gun and cow-ride entries, the crawl, and the surface-swim starts. Both return the player to normal aim and reset the avatar's mental-state timer.
- `NDropWeaponStateTask` ([`NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) drops the heavy slot's weapon in any state, holstered included, on every grapple and tether fire, grenade throw, fall, free fall and fast fall, parachute and wingsuit opening, reel-in to a fall, vehicle entry, surface swim, crawl, and hit-reaction flight and stumble.
- `NStopUsingWeaponStateTask` ([`NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)) stops using the selected slot's weapon in the ragdoll, crash, dying, drowning, and get-up states.
- `NWeaponHideInVehicleTask` ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter)), two per seat state, turns a configured slot's holstered weapon holstered-invisible when the vehicle sets [`Vehicle::m_HideCharacterWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_HideCharacterWeapons), and its exit ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon_animation##func-NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnExit)) shows it again.

The vehicle exit's upward teleport also forces the neutral state. The parachute has no holster in code (only the auto-aim tuning and statistics read it), and the grapple hang affects only the inventory permissions and the precision camera hint (unverified); holsters there come from the graph. The wingsuit's guns are placed from the holder's bones and the flight direction ([`WeaponData::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)). Nothing in these paths moves a weapon held outside the slots.

## A character that loses its gun

The game takes a gun from a living character only by dropping it: death drops the best weapon, and a state task can drop a configured slot. There is no disarm, and a hit reaction below the knockdown threshold staggers without a ragdoll ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#the-hit-reaction)). A character left empty-handed gets the ordinary handling:

- the inventory's drop with its select flag selects another weapon when the dropped slot was selected;
- the weapon-equip state task, with nothing wielded, switches to the first weapon that still has ammunition ([`Inventory::TryToSwitchToValidWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-TryToSwitchToValidWeapon));
- the AI's perception keeps a sense of weapons lying in the world whose pickup interaction is live, sorted by distance, and the behaviour trees can reserve one for a character to fetch (unverified, unbound).
