# The two-handed inventory

The local player's two hands are independent. Each holds its own weapon or object, items pass between the hands, weapons dock on the body's sockets and are drawn from them, and the player carries what the body holds rather than what the game's slots allow. Nothing docks, draws, or switches by itself: the player does everything with their own hands. The table acts under the controller scheme; the [grapple snatch](grapple-snatch.md) is built on it, the sockets' volumes, draw, dock, and swap are in [weapon-sockets.md](weapon-sockets.md), and the attach bones, letting go, and the release motion are in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md).

The loadout table is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/loadout/`, a pure core tested without the game. The game's side is `controllers/weapons/inventory/`, the per-hand tick and the armed weapons are `weapons/hold/{tick,armed}/`, and the pistols are `weapons/dual_wield/`. The tuning is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/inventory.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)); the inventory has no settings of its own.

## Why the game's inventory is replaced

The game wields exactly one weapon by construction ([engine: weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md), [engine: item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md)): `Character::GetWieldingWeapon` returns the selected slot's weapon, and nearly every system reads that one weapon. Workarounds that keep the model collide: a take with the free hand goes through the game's pickup, whose equip takes the other hand's weapon away.

So the mod owns weapon handling for the local player. The game keeps three jobs: owning the weapon objects, simulating them (projectiles, effects, damage), and holding the ammunition reserves. Every other reader of the wielded weapon is replaced, patched to work per weapon, or fed the primary hand's weapon (the AI's threat rating, Rico's lines, the button hints, the store).

## The model

One table holds every item the player has, each in exactly one place: the left hand, the right hand, a socket, or the world near the player. Items are weapons, the props a hand carries, and grenades and C4 charges while a hand has them. Both hands are the same type: a gun in either hand has its own trigger, aim, recoil, reload, and ammunition counter.

The primary hand is the dominant hand when it holds a weapon, otherwise the other hand when it does, otherwise none. It is derived, never stored, and its weapon is the one the game wields. The grapple is a device on the wrist, not an item, so the grapple hand is a hand like the other: a hand holding anything is not pinned to the line while hanging (`hands::tiers`), and a gun in the grapple hand takes its trigger (the retract moves to the stick click).

There are four sockets (`grab::socket::Socket`): each hip and each shoulder. The shoulders are where the game docks its long guns across the back, never mirrored for handedness ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose)): the two-handed and heavy slots on [`BACK_ATTACH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-BACK_ATTACH) on the right, the special slot on `BACK_ATTACH_2` on the left. The size classes are pistol, submachine gun, long gun, and heavy; a hip takes a submachine gun or smaller, a shoulder takes everything.

The types carry the invariants: an item cannot be copied and has exactly one container, each hand and socket holds at most one, a socket's item is made only through a fit check, and no two live items share an object. A prop or a charge let go of leaves the table, since only a weapon or a safe grenade can lie in its world. Where a weapon goes when the player does not choose (a give with full hands, a lost weapon's return, a respawn, a restore) is the socket the game docks its slot on, then the snuggest socket that fits, the dominant side first (`BodyLayout::preference`).

## The live table

The table is the one source of truth for the player's items. Each transition applies whole and returns its changes with a cause for the log, or is refused with a typed error and leaves the table as it was (`loadout::transition`): the player's takes, docks, draws, hand-overs, and let-gos; the game's gives, removals, and drops; and the weapons the mod spawns to stand for one the player has (a split pistol's twin, a restored weapon). Hooks and hands send one event per thing that happened (`loadout::event`); a detour decides nothing.

The live table (`loadout::live`) sits behind one lock and is the only writer of where items are. It tracks the pistol, two-handed, special, and heavy slots; the grenade, planted explosive, beacon, and sun gun slots stay the game's. Detours folding events under the lock make no engine call and take no other lock. After every fold that can move an item it publishes each hand's weapon object (compared by the placement detours before any lock), what each hand holds (`live::held_kind`), and every item's place.

The table leads the hands. A weapon the game wields lands in the hand a take left expecting it, or for the game's own draw (the wheel, a switch) the dominant hand when empty, otherwise the other; with both hands full it is put away again (`loadout::live::expect`). The table starts from the slots on its first input tick, as a load does. Hand moves are sent at once by the calls that make them; the input tick catches the game's own moves (a weapon the game holstered or dropped docks on its slot's socket or is let go) and what became of the weapons nothing keeps ([The weapons in the world](#the-weapons-in-the-world)).

What a hand holds from every source (the table's item, a wielded or armed gun, a grenade, a charge, the detonator, the reload's round) is one record per hand (`loadout::contents`), published at the end of the weapon handling's tick and sampled between ticks through `weapons::contents::now`. Every target of the tick (sockets, pouches, placed charges, guns in the world, the hand-over, the foregrip) offers itself only to a free hand. The reload alone treats a hand holding its own round as free.

## The slots as a projection

The projection (`loadout::projection`) says what each tracked slot holds: one weapon of that slot from the body, taken from the primary hand, then the other hand, then the slot's own socket, then the other sockets. Only a weapon bound to a live object goes in, and the primary hand's weapon is selected. The heavy slot stays empty ([Heavy weapons](#heavy-weapons)).

The reconcile (`weapons::inventory::reconcile`) makes the game match, each input tick and at once after a hand move:

- A weapon leaving its slot stays alive in the carry set and goes out through [`Inventory::RemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RemoveWeapon); one coming in goes through [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon) without an equip. A slot weapon the table does not have (a mission's give before a hand takes it) is left, since the game's word on ownership is final.
- The primary hand's weapon is wielded: the old one is unequipped and the new equipped ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip), then `OnEquip`) with no act, after aborting any draw, holster, or switch clip ([`EventIdSymbolTable::ACT_ABORT_WEAPON_PARTIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable)), whose messages would otherwise equip by slot. It waits while [`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing) or `GetAnimationAllowsHolstering` refuses.
- Every other weapon in a hand is armed there, and an armed weapon that left its hand or is now wielded is disarmed.
- Before the game stores its save, and before a mission or challenge caches the slots, the slots are given the store projection; before the game's own pickup fills a taken slot, the slot's weapon leaves it and stays on its socket.

## The per-hand tick

Every gun in a hand fires from its own trigger at its own aim point (`weapons::hold::tick`). The game's fire path keys the heat, the gunshot the AI hears, the bullets' owner, the flash, the recoil, the spread, and the rumble on the weapon's user rather than the selected slot, so a gun the game does not wield fires as the player's from the holstered state ([engine: weapon-firing.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-firing.md)).

A weapon in a hand that the game does not wield is armed (`weapons::hold::armed`): it stays holstered, so the inventory, HUD, and saves see it as holstered, but its holstered attach-bone entries name the hand's bone, its holstered scale ([`WeaponData::m_HolsteredScaleValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_HolsteredScaleValue)) is one, and the player uses it ([`WeaponBase::StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing)). Disarming restores all three.

- After [`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel) and `_Serial`, the mod updates each armed weapon through its [`UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel) and `_Serial` virtuals with its hand's input, checking the shooting permission itself since the game gates only its own fan-out. The wielded weapon stays on the game's fan-out with its hand's fire input substituted, since a second update would advance its timers twice.
- Each gun's ammunition check would ask the character to reload or cancel the reload of the wielded weapon; inside the local player's checks both requests are refused (`weapons::empty`), so one hand's gun never reloads or interrupts the other's. The same module suppresses the automatic switch away from an empty weapon.
- Each armed gun takes the wielded weapon's aim mode, and the camera's use hint, which two guns of one type share, is restored each tick while either is in use.

The true reload acts on each hand's gun with its record keyed by the table's item, so a weapon keeps its chamber across a hand-over and the spawn reset ([reload.md](reload.md#each-hands-gun)); one reserve per ammunition type is shared. The mod's per-hand counters replace the game's weapon readout ([hud.md](../hud.md)). The body never takes the combat stance: the weapon-wielded condition reads false for the player's base layer, and the arms are the mod's IK ([body/procedural-body.md](../body/procedural-body.md)).

## The gun in either hand

The subsystems that act on a held gun key it by the hand, not by the wielded weapon (`weapons::engine::in_hands`).

- The weight (`weapons::heft`), the recoil (`weapons::recoil`), the wall collision (`weapons::collide`), and the grip regions (`weapons::regions`) have per-hand state, so a rifle in the off-hand beside a pistol sags, kicks, and slides into the grip as a rifle held in one hand.
- The sights (`weapons::ads`) and the scope (`weapons::scope`) solve each long gun, but the aiming eye and the lens are single, so they act on the gun raised to the eye, picked each frame by how far each gun's pose has taken hold of the eye.
- The foregrip and the hand-over (`weapons::foregrip`, `weapons::hold::hand_over`) need the other hand, so they act on the gun held while the other hand is free (`weapons::engine::lone_gun`).

Per-hand state belongs to the hand: a gun handed over or drawn again starts at rest. The gun bodies joined to the hands are in [physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md).

## The grabs against the table

Every grab at the body is one decision against the table's snapshot (`loadout::grab`). An empty hand's grab of an occupied socket draws it; the reconcile wields it when the hand is the primary and arms it otherwise. A hand holding a weapon docks it on a free socket that fits by opening its grip over the socket or by a grab there, and opening over a taken socket swaps the two. Opening the grip anywhere else lets the weapon go into the world, except the game's pistol pair, one object of two guns that cannot lie in the world, which goes onto a free socket or stays in the hand. A socket that refuses the held weapon is marked for that hand (`loadout::grab::refusing`). A draw or dock the animation refuses does nothing, rather than leave the table and the game disagreeing. With `controllers.weapons.hold.grip_to_hold` off, a gun stays in the hand until a grab of a socket docks it. Nothing selects a slot or asks the game to holster.

A pistol is an item like any other. A pair the game hands over is split into two single pistols while the table runs (`weapons::dual_wield::single`): the slot keeps one and the twin is spawned onto the other hip; the split itself is in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md).

## Props and explosives in the table

Each input tick the table is fed what the holding subsystems have in each hand (`loadout::live::HeldOthers`): the physical hands' loose object, a primed or caught grenade, and the C4 throw's charge. The feed is per tick rather than per change because the throws move on the simulation's threads, so a grenade primed and thrown between ticks never enters the table. A grip on a door or a character is the world's ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)). A hand holding any of these draws nothing, takes nothing, and is not offered the other hand's gun. Under the gamepad scheme nothing enters the table.

A grenade with its pin in is a safe item (`loadout::safe_items`, moved by `weapons::grenade::safe`): bound to an item lease rather than an object, its inert actor owned by the grenade controller and its place by the table. It can be in a hand, on a hip, or lying in the world. Each move is checked against the caller's copied view and the item's movement revision, so a stale move is refused whole; a reset lets only retirements through. A grenade whose pin is out leaves the table ([grenades.md](grenades.md)).

## The takes

Every take of a weapon from the world lands in the table and then a hand, and the game's equip never runs, so a take never touches the other hand (`loadout::take`). The grabbing hand takes when free; the game's use-item pickup, which no hand grabs, takes into a free hand, the dominant first, and with both full leaves the weapon where it lies. A take through the table (`weapons::hold::pickup::take_by_table`) skips the game's pickup ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup)), credits the weapon's stored rounds as it would ([`Inventory::AddAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddAmmo)), and has the carry set keep it.

The sources are the ground, racks and lockers (`weapons::hold::rack`, [paired-rack-weapons.md](paired-rack-weapons.md)), characters' hands (`weapons::hold::steal`), and the use-item pickup (`weapons::hold::use_item`), whose completion ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem)) can run on any thread handling track messages, so it only queues the weapon for the next input tick. The game's own pickup still runs while the table is stopped and for the embedded power core, whose pickup spawns and equips another weapon.

The game's gives ([engine: weapon-gives-and-restores.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-gives-and-restores.md#the-give)) write the respawn table in [`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon), and the spawned weapon arrives through [`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned), which would equip it. `weapons::inventory::gives` records each give, queues the arrival, and hands it over on the next input tick with the slot's occupant moved onto the body and [`Inventory::m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon) cleared for the call, so it arrives unequipped and the table places it by the give rule. Gives during a reset or while a mission holds the weapons are left to the game.

The ammunition auto-pickup's check reads the slots alone ([engine: item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon)); its detour on [`Inventory::HasWeaponWithAmmoType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-HasWeaponWithAmmoType) (`weapons::inventory::ammo`) also answers yes for any weapon the table has on the body, from a lock-free mask published each input tick.

## Traversal

Traversal leaves the hands and the body as they are (`weapons::inventory::traversal`). The base layer's unequip, hide-in-vehicle, stop-use, and heavy-drop tasks ([engine: animation-graph.md](../../engine/character/animation-graph.md#the-base-layers-weapon-tasks)) are refused on entry for the local player while the table runs, the drop only while the heavy slot holds no wielded weapon; the hide task's exit puts back only what its entry hid. A mounted gun's use unequips the wielded weapon through its own start of use, which the table takes for a holster, so the player's weapon docks while the hands man the gun. [`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState) unequips the wielded weapon; its detour tells the table (`live::neutral_forced`) so the weapon stays in its hand and is wielded again.

The inventory's weapon update skips the wielded weapon in every seat but the motorcycle's and while the boost wingsuit flies. There the reconcile wields nothing, without waiting for the holster or draw permission, and every gun in a hand is armed and fired by the per-hand tick. What the hands may do in each control mode is `weapons::capabilities::Capabilities`; a hand holding anything takes no new vehicle control or handle ([vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md), [steering-wheel.md](steering-wheel.md), [flight.md](flight.md)).

## Heavy weapons

A heavy weapon is kept beyond the slots and never wielded from the heavy slot, because its unequip drops whatever that slot holds, and the drop task, the forced neutral state, and the holster input all drop through the slot ([engine: weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#dropping)). With the slot empty none of them reaches it. In a hand it is armed and fired by the per-hand tick; the primary's heavy weapon is not wielded, so the game's consumers follow the other hand's weapon. It fires straight from the reserve, gets a heavy gun body in the hand ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)), and docks on either shoulder.

## The weapons in the world

The inventory keeps a weak reference to each weapon it has seen ([`WeaponBase::m_WeakThis`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_WeakThis), `weapons::inventory::world`), and each input tick asks what became of each of the table's weapons that no slot, carry set, or hand keeps: one whose [`m_Holder`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Holder) is another character is theirs and leaves the table, one whose object is gone leaves, and any other lies in the world.

The game despawns a dropped weapon by distance from the player, but never one with a strong reference held elsewhere. So a weapon the player let go of is pinned by a strong reference (`weapons::inventory::pins`, the tuning's `pins`) while near, with hysteresis between pinning and release, the nearest few only, since a pinned weapon keeps its resources loaded. Past the forget distance it is left to the game and leaves the table. Weapons the player never touched are never pinned. At the spawn system's pre-reset event every reference is released.

A weapon let go of stays where it falls; retrieving it is the grapple snatch's job. It returns to the body only when lost (`weapons::inventory::lost`, the tuning's `lost`): sunk far below the sea's level (no terrain query is bound), or let go of at speed (the wingsuit, a vehicle, a fall) and now far behind. A distance rather than a time, since the speed makes the distance come quickly. A lost weapon returns to the first free socket that fits, or to the player's feet.

### Markers on the weapons let go of

Each weapon the table has in the world carries a marker: the game's own icon for it with the grab hint's halo, upright above it (`hud::lost_weapons`). The game's own pickup hints mark only the nearest useful weapon per slot while that slot runs low ([engine: ui-rendering.md](../../engine/rendering/ui-rendering.md#weapon-icons)), so they cannot find a weapon thrown in a fight. It fades in past arm's length, only the nearest few are drawn, and it lasts as long as the table keeps the weapon.

## The weapons beyond the slots

The game's inventory holds one weapon per slot ([`Inventory::m_Weapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_Weapons)), but what keeps a weapon alive is any strong reference, so a reference of the mod's keeps a weapon beyond the slots out of the despawn, and weapons move into and out of slots without being dropped. Every weapon on the body that no slot holds is in the carry set (`weapons::inventory::carry`): holstered, held by the player, and unseen by the HUD, the wheel, and the saves.

Each socket is one of the game's dock bones with an offset in the character's frame (`inventory::sockets`), so a carried weapon sits beside the slot's weapon on the same bone; a pistol on a hip sits in the holster. It is placed after each [`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms) by naming the socket's bone in its holstered attach entries, shown and hidden with the inventory's own weapons ([`Inventory::UpdateWeaponVisibility`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponVisibility)), and has each pose-finalization pass ended as the slots' weapons do, or it would be drawn a tick ahead of the body.

## The spawn reset

The game takes the player's weapons away and gives them back at three caches, replaces them at every spawn reset, and saves only the slots ([engine: weapon-gives-and-restores.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-gives-and-restores.md), [engine: weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md#the-spawn-reset-and-the-respawn-table)). The table snapshots and re-keys itself at each (`loadout::reset`, `weapons::inventory::{reset,missions,challenges,persist,restore,sidecar}`). The game's word on ownership is final: a removal empties the hands too, and the hands come back empty from a death.

Every spawn reset (a death, fast travel, a mission or challenge start, a load) replaces each slot weapon with a new object of the same type. The detour on [`Inventory::HandleEvent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-HandleEvent) runs for every character on whatever thread resets the spawn system, so it checks for the local player before any lock. At the pre-reset event the carried weapons are recorded, all references released, and every slot weapon set waiting for its slot's new object. The first input tick after the post-reset event, once no slot's spawn is pending or `loadout.settle_wait_s` passes, re-binds each waiting item to its slot's new object, so it keeps its identity and place; weapons in the world leave.

The reset's kind, read from the seams before it (health is already restored), decides what the hands keep: a respawn ([`Player::ReInit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-ReInit) since the last reset) empties them onto the body; a load (the save's load or [`GameStateFrontend::ResetSystems`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameStateFrontend##func-ResetSystems)) restarts the table from the slots; anything else is a travel and the hands keep what they hold.

The first input tick with the player then respawns the carried weapons, from the load's sidecar or the reset's own record, by type at the player ([`SpawnSystem::SpawnTagIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##func-SpawnTagIndex)). The callback only queues the weapon; the next input tick adopts it through one admission of the table's restore (`inventory::restore::adoption`) with its magazine set and onto its recorded socket.

## The caches

At each cache the slots are given the store projection first, and the table snapshots the weapons on the body with their places, or restores them. A give-back is folded once no slot spawn is pending, re-binds the snapshot's items by slot and type, takes everything out before putting anything back (so two swapped weapons swap back), and leaves the hands empty.

- The mission checkpoint ([`MissionManager::CacheWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::MissionManager##func-CacheWeapons), replayed by `RestoreCachedWeapons`): the snapshot stands for every restart from it.
- A mission's graph-script cache ([`NodeWeaponCache::CacheAllWeaponSlots`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graph_script::NodeWeaponCache##func-CacheAllWeaponSlots), given back by `RestoreWeapons`): the table takes every weapon on the body and stashes the carried ones (hidden, ungrabbable, still carried) until the give-back.
- A challenge ([`ROMManager::StartROM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMManager##func-StartROM), [`ROMObject::PostInit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/mission::ROMObject##func-PostInit), given back by `ROMManager::AbortROM` or `ROMObject::RestoreWeaponsAndAmmo`): the first snapshot is kept through retries, and when the challenge took a slot weapon the table takes and stashes everything.

## Saves, resets, and loads

Each [`StorePlayerSaveData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/save##func-StorePlayerSaveData) writes the save sidecar, `inventory.toml` in the per-user configuration directory beside `settings.toml` ([settings.md](../settings.md)), from one writer thread that keeps only the newest snapshot and replaces the file atomically (`inventory::sidecar`, format in `inventory::persist`). It holds a format version, the carried weapons, and a fingerprint of what the store writes: each slot's type and every reserve. After [`LoadPlayerSaveData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/save##func-LoadPlayerSaveData), the fingerprint is compared with the respawn table ([`Inventory::m_WeaponToSpawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawn)) and reserves; on a match the carried weapons come back, otherwise nothing extra does. The mod cannot tell which save a load reads, so the fingerprint is a compatibility check, not an identity.

## The scheme switch

Switching away from the controller scheme, or turning the bindings or controllers off, stops the table (`weapons::inventory::stand_down`); a pause (the debug panel, lost tracking) keeps it. The stop projects the table into the slots, disarms the hands, retires the safe grenades, and keeps the weapons beyond the slots stashed in the carry set. When the table runs again it starts from the slots and each kept weapon returns to its socket. Gamepad play meanwhile is the game's one-weapon model.

## The debug UI

The inventory view (Weapons > Inventory and body slots, `crates/jc3vr-ui/src/ui/controllers/inventory/`) reads only publications: the table's readout (`inventory::peek_status`) and the game's slots captured on the input tick (`weapons::inventory::game_view`). It shows each hand and socket, the weapons in the world, the latest transitions and refusals, and each game slot beside what the table projects into it, marking a mismatch (except while a spawn is pending, a mounted gun is manned, or the wielded weapon is skipped). A one-frame mismatch can be the publications a tick apart. The log records every transition with its cause.

Each hand's and socket's dropdown gives any shipped hand weapon that fits (`weapons::inventory::restore::give`) by the restore's path: spawned by its unique tag ([`SpawnSystem::GetUniqueTag`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##func-GetUniqueTag)) and adopted onto the socket. A give that does not arrive in time withdraws its request ([`SpawnSystem::CancelSpawn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/spawn_system::SpawnSystem##func-CancelSpawn)) so a late spawn leaves no stray weapon. The grenade row sets the stock, and the C4 row can remove every placed charge.

The `Docked guns` overlay (`controllers.overlay.docked_guns`, `weapons::inventory::docked_view`) draws a ring and a barrel line at each docked gun's last placement, green while its model is shown and red while hidden, so a docked gun that renders nothing says whether the game hides it or the placement misplaces it.
