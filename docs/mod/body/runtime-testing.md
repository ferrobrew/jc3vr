# Integrated runtime testing

Compiler checks and pure tests do not establish the native callback schedule, the model factories, the live physics response, or headset comfort. This page is the order in which the paths that need a live game are exercised together in one session. Each path's behaviour is in its own doc; the steps below say what to look for.

## Paths under test

- On-foot play with the grounded rig and the procedural torso ([rig.md](rig.md)); the body-follow switch (`steps.enabled`) disables the step drive.
- The learned body estimator under the controller scheme with the head tracked ([body-estimator.md](body-estimator.md#in-the-game)), with the weights set up by `scripts/egoposer_weights.sh`.
- The parachute handles' free targets from the game's resting handle props ([canopy-target-contract.md](../input/canopy-target-contract.md)). A missing or expired observation withholds the free target rather than substituting shoulder geometry.
- The hand reload's magazine pull, button and flick ejection, and belt replacement, which detach in the character's native serial post-physics pass after revalidating the weapon and the spare; magazine and chamber share the native loaded total ([reload.md](../input/reload.md)). The manual slide, bolt, and pump actions on a ready gun conserve loaded rounds.
- Safe grenades drawn from the weapon's pool without consuming ammunition, armed only by a deliberate tracked throw, and the grenade shelf sources ([grenades.md](../input/grenades.md)).
- Paired racks whose two gun models are independent sources ([paired-rack-weapons.md](../input/paired-rack-weapons.md)).
- The narrowed human collision hulls, their step-up, and their restoration at disable and eject ([human-hull-restoration.md](human-hull-restoration.md)).

## Test order

1. Load an ordinary on-foot scene and confirm the Body fit readout advances. Walk physically, bend, crouch, turn, and reach with one hand: a bend stays a lean, and a walk commits a step that plays the game's walk. Test slopes, stairs, edges, and moving platforms. Toggle body follow off and verify that steps stop.
2. With the weights staged, check that the Body fit readout's estimator line reads `running` within a couple of seconds of the first controller frame, and that the log has one `body estimator: running on the game's device` line naming the weights and the 16-bit precision. Standing still, glance left and right and back: the green floor arrow (the estimate's pelvis) swings part of the way, the magenta one (the rig's legs) barely moves, and the chest follows the glance. Turn the whole body a quarter turn and hold it: the legs follow in one smooth movement and settle onto the green arrow. Repeat seated. Recenter, snap turn, and smooth turn while watching the arrows: neither jumps relative to the body. Cover a controller until it loses tracking: the arrow keeps a plausible heading. In a vehicle, the parachute, the wingsuit, and the water the green arrows keep drawing and the magenta ones disappear. Under the gamepad scheme the arrows disappear and the readout shows no estimate. Rename the weights file away while the estimator is on: the payload stops with a message naming the path.
3. Rico's head on the neck pivot: stand still on foot with the `Body rig` visualisation on, look level, then down at the chest, then turn the head well to each side and hold it while the body follows the turn. The neck pivot sprite sits on Rico's neck in the mirror and in his shadow, his head neither trails behind the view nor sits above it, and turning the head does not swing the camera into his head or shoulders. Walk, crouch a little, and lean over a table: the chest stays out of the lower view.
4. Repeat takeoff, landing, recovery, floor pickup, and hanging. Head motion stays independent, and entering a sequence a second time still follows it.
5. Open the canopy, take both resting handles, steer, release, and take them again. Compare the target markers with the handles' visible rest.
6. Pull a loaded magazine by hand, abort a partial pull, reinsert it, and verify the chamber and magazine ammunition. Repeat the button, flick, and belt operations, including weapon changes while an operation is pending. Rack a ready slide, bolt, and pump repeatedly: check round ejection and feeding, partial pulls, release during an action, regrip cancellation, and an ordinary drop from a pump foregrip at home.
7. Draw safe grenades into both hands at zero stock, transfer and stow them, drop one without throwing, recover it, and deliberately throw the other. Use a grenade shelf, leave its streaming range, and return. Confirm no stock refund or deduction and no fuse on the inert items.
8. At finite and unlimited paired racks, take each member in either order with the opposite hand occupied and the holsters full. The other member stays at the source and the total ammunition is conserved. Leave and return after the first take, then repeat across a reset.
9. Inject with NPCs nearby, then stream in more. Exercise the narrowed hulls standing, crouching, swimming, and in the wingsuit. Request restoration on clear flat ground and beside an obstruction; a deferred request leaves gameplay responsive and restores no body partially. Move clear, retry, eject, and reinject.
10. Record full-rate tracking with joints while exercising these paths. Compare the stored and uncompressed bytes and the queue and drop counters, check the shutdown recovery, and check that `tracking-synth dump` reads the file back.
