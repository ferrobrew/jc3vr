# Weapon firing and the weapon's user

What a weapon needs in order to fire, what a shot does, and which of those steps read the weapon's own state and which read the character using it. The aim point and the shot direction are in [aim-pipeline.md](aim-pipeline.md), the aim flags and the fire gating that precedes the weapon in [aim-state.md](aim-state.md), and the ammunition and the reload in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md).

## The short version

Everything a weapon does when it fires keys off its user, [`WeaponBase::m_User`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_User), and the two flags derived from it, [`m_IsUserLocalPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserLocalPlayer) and [`m_IsUserPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsUserPlayer). No step of the fire path reads the weapon's state ([`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State)), the user's selected slot, or the user's wielded weapon. A holstered weapon that has a user and is updated fires as that user's, with the heat, the AI's gunshot event, the bullets' ownership and player flag, the muzzle effect, the sound, the recoil, and the rumble all intact.

The state that is the character's rather than the weapon's is the user's state bits (read by the ammunition check), the player's aim mode (read by the recoil and scatter scaling), the player aim control's weapon target (the bullets' lead), and the user's heat and AI entity. The reloads the ammunition check requests act on the user's wielded weapon, whichever weapon makes the request.

The [`StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing) that sets the user is separate from the equip: the equip virtual calls it, but it does not change the weapon's state. [`StopUsingBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StopUsingBase) clears it.

## The gate

The per-gun fire update, [`WeaponBase::UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel), does nothing at all unless the weapon has a live user (its control block's use count is non-zero), [`m_LieAboutUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_LieAboutUser) is set, or the weapon is in the [`E_WS_TURRET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_TURRET) state. Past that, the only rules that depend on where the weapon sits are by slot, not by state: the wall block applies to the dual-wield, two-handed, and two-handed-special slots, and the aim-angle check skips the hand grenade, the planted explosive, and the beacon.

## Trigger, interval, and the forced shots

With the primary input and the weapon's tuning ([`m_Tuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Tuning)):

- [`m_IsTriggerPulled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsTriggerPulled) is the input past the first of the tuning's [`m_trigger_sensitivity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_trigger_sensitivity) values, or past the second once pulled. [`m_IsFeatheringTrigger`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFeatheringTrigger) is any input above zero. A pull resets the burst and the time without input that the predicted reload reads; a release clears [`m_FiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_FiredThisInput).
- The semi-automatic rule (a weapon without [`m_fully_automatic`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fully_automatic) fires once per pull) applies only while `m_IsUserLocalPlayer` is set. For any other user a held trigger keeps firing.
- [`m_ForceFireTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireTimer), [`m_ForceFireCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireCount), and [`m_InputBufferTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InputBufferTimer) force a shot request while positive, and a gun with burst shots left ([`WeaponData::m_BurstShotsLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BurstShotsLeft)) forces the shot. [`m_IsFiringCancelled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFiringCancelled) blocks until release.
- The wall block, with its buffered retry, is described in [aim-state.md](aim-state.md#fire-gating). Its flags are the weapon's own, written by its wall-penetration component's serial update, so they are only as fresh as the weapon's serial update.
- The aim-angle check, [`IsTargetWithinAimAngle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-IsTargetWithinAimAngle), compares the barrel with the direction to [`m_AimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimTargetPosition) outside barrel fire-direction mode, and a failure clears and buffers the shot. It is bypassed by `m_LieAboutUser`, by the user's `ShouldIgnoreFiringAngle`, and by a per-weapon `m_IgnoreAimAngleRestriction` flag; the last two, and the weapon's fire-angle field, are unverified.
- Then come the ammunition check ([`CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggered), described in [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#the-ammunition-check)), the fire-pattern update, a fire-delay timer that blocks while positive, and a spin-up time (the current and the required input time for fire). The delay and spin-up fields are unverified.
- A shot fires when the request stands and [`m_TimeSinceLastFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_TimeSinceLastFire) has reached the tuning's [`m_fire_rate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fire_rate) interval ([`m_fire_rate_level_2`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponTuning##field-m_fire_rate_level_2) with the fire-rate upgrade, when the holder is the player). It calls [`Fire_Start`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Start) on the gun being updated. With no request while firing, [`Fire_Stop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Stop) runs on every gun.
- [`m_ForceFireProjectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireProjectile) fires every gun once and clears the shot.
- The secondary input starts and stops a secondary fire through two further virtuals (the secondary fire start and stop; their names are unverified). The inventory's fan-out always passes zero for it.

All of this state is the weapon's own: trigger, semi-automatic latch, cancel, buffer, interval, scatter, recoil, overheat, aim target, and aim mode, with the burst kept per gun.

## What a shot does

[`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) runs once per shot, from the projectile override of each weapon class:

- It consumes a round when the weapon is locally owned, and registers the weapon for its pre-simulation update ([`m_RequiresPreSimUpdates`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RequiresPreSimUpdates)).
- It resets the fire timers and applies the recoil, scaled for the first shot of a pull and, when the user's controller is the player in precision aim ([`Player::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimMode)), by the precision factor. Scatter and overheat accumulate.
- It plays the gun's muzzle flash and shot sound ([`WeaponData::PlayFireEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-PlayFireEffect)), with the local-player variant chosen by `m_IsUserLocalPlayer`.
- For the local player it logs the shot's statistics, and the vehicle-weapon action events when a vehicle holds the weapon.
- It sets [`m_DeferredPhysicalRecoil`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredPhysicalRecoil) when [`m_PhysicalRecoilStrength`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PhysicalRecoilStrength) is non-zero, and [`m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast) on the first shot of a pull.
- It runs the rumble ([`RumbleFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-RumbleFire)) and the camera shake, both for the local player only, and advances the gun's fire-point cursor and [`m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire).
- It adds heat (the wanted level) to the user through [`Character::AddHeatPoints`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-AddHeatPoints), with the firing entry of the heat manager's tuning ([`HeatManager::GetHeatPointData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/heat_manager::HeatManager##func-GetHeatPointData)), unless [`m_NoHeatOnFiring`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NoHeatOnFiring) is set, the user's faction is hostile to the default faction, or the weapon is the planted explosive. In a restricted area, a user in a vehicle that a player drives (or drove recently) also heats the local player.

## The gunshot the AI hears

The shot's awareness event is deferred to the weapon's pre-simulation update, [`WeaponBase::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePreSim). With `m_DeferredAwarenessBroadcast` set and `m_NoHeatOnFiring` clear, it clears the flag, takes the muzzle of the gun that fired last ([`m_LastWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_LastWeaponToFire)), and queues an [`EAwareness_WeaponFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ai::ai::AwarenessEvent##variant-EAwareness_WeaponFire) event there through [`BroadcastAwarenessEvent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ai::ai::BroadcastAwarenessEvent), with the user's AI entity ([`Character::GetAiEntityHandle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetAiEntityHandle)) as the source. While `m_NoHeatOnFiring` is set the flag stays pending. The queue is a fixed-capacity buffer appended to through an atomic counter, so the call is safe from the parallel passes, and an event past its capacity is dropped. Each shot registers the weapon for the pre-simulation update, so the event goes out on the next one.

The fire path has no other AI-facing output than this event and the heat; whether stealth reads anything else is unverified. Bullet impacts broadcast their own AI event, from the bullet (its impact broadcast is not bound).

## Bullets

[`BulletWeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BulletWeaponBase##func-Fire_Projectile) builds the shot matrix, takes the holder's details ([`WeaponBase::GetHolderInformation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetHolderInformation)), among them the physics objects its projectiles ignore, which come from [`m_Holder`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Holder), runs `Fire_ProjectileBase`, and spawns the bullet:

- The bullet's owner is `m_User`, falling back to `m_Holder` when there is no user, and its damage-source state comes from that owner when it is a character ([`Character::GetCurrentDamageSourceStateInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetCurrentDamageSourceStateInfo)).
- The bullet's player flag, and its tracer's, is `m_IsUserPlayer`; the first bullet of a shot also takes `m_IsUserLocalPlayer`.
- For a local user it sets the bullet's target point from the aim point along the shot direction, led by the velocity of the player aim control's weapon target ([`PlayerAimControl::m_Target`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_Target)) when there is one, or far out along the shot when there is none. That target is the player's single weapon target, whichever weapon fires. How the bullet uses the point is unverified.

A weapon with neither a user nor a holder fires unowned bullets, and a weapon without a holder gives its bullets no bodies to ignore; both follow from the code and are unverified in play.

## The shooting permission

[`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsShooting) is [`m_DoesAnimationAllowShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowShooting) with [`SB_DISABLE_SHOOT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_SHOOT) clear. It gates only the inventory's fan-out, [`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel), which zeroes the primary input ([`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs)) while it is false, and the grenade input unless [`m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades) is set and [`SB_DISABLE_USE_GRENADES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_USE_GRENADES) is clear. Nothing inside a weapon reads it; of the inventory permissions only the reload permission is read inside, by the ammunition check's empty-magazine branch. A weapon updated directly bypasses the shooting permission.

The fan-out ticks only the mounted heavy-slot gun, the boost wingsuit's guns, or the wielded weapon, then the selected grenade. For the player, a riding state ([`Character::IsInRidingInVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInRidingInVehicleState)) skips the wielded weapon unless the ride is a motorcycle, and skips the grenade in any riding state.

[`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPermissions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPermissions) recomputes the permissions each tick. Shooting is cleared while pre-reeling or reeling in without the shoot-while-reeling upgrade, and every permission while the grappling hook holds an object. Each permission is then ANDed with the inverse of its disable bit, the permission bytes are written, and the disable bits are cleared. The disable bits are per-frame requests: a state task sets them during the animation update, and this function latches and clears them.

The setter is the `NSetInventoryPermissions` state task. Its `Flags` property maps bit by bit to the disable bits ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_TranslatePermissionsFlagToStateBit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_TranslatePermissionsFlagToStateBit)), and [`NSetInventoryPermissions_SetPermissions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_SetPermissions) ORs them into the animated model's current state-bit words. It has two scopes: the whole state ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_NStateScope_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_NStateScope_Update), every update) and a named clip segment ([`NSetInventoryPermissions_NSegmentScope_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::NSetInventoryPermissions_NSegmentScope_Update), with a `Time` and a segment name besides the flags).

In Rico's base graph (`rico_base.afsmb`, decoded as in [animation-graph.md](../character/animation-graph.md)), just under half the states carry a permission task with the shoot flag set:

- vehicle entry, exit, door, hatch, stunt-position, pull-out, and explosive-on-vehicle states, for the whole state or within `DISABLE_WEAPON_ACTION_SEG` segments;
- death, dying, ragdoll, get-up, crash, and the hit-reaction flights and stumbles;
- the grapple fire and tether fire states on foot, in the air, attached, upside down, swimming, and in the running strafes (some within `SEG_NO_WEAPON_HANDLING`);
- the grapple's reel to a vehicle, jump away, release, and pre-reel states (some within `SEG_NO_SHOOT`);
- free fall, fast fall, the boost wingsuit's rolls, evades, transitions, and crash recoveries, and the wingsuit's flinches and exits;
- the parachute opening (`SEG_NO_SHOOT`);
- underwater swimming and dives;
- the mounted-gun entry, exit, and pickup states;
- grenade throws (`SEG_NO_WEAPON_FIRE` on foot, the whole state while hanging or upside down), melee (`SEG_NO_ATTACK`, and the stunt melee's whole state), and the weapon pickups (`NO_SHOOT_SEGMENT` on `S_IDLE_PICKUP`, `S_MOVE_FWD_PICKUP`, and `S_MOVE_BWD_PICKUP`);
- `S_LAND_FAST_FALL`, `S_STUNT_BACKFLIP_JUMP_EXIT_VEHICLE_DETACHED`, and a few hang transitions.

No draw, holster, weapon-switch, or reload state of the base graph carries one. Those block firing through the character's state bits that the ammunition check reads instead (`SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON`, `SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON`, `SB_IS_RELOADING`, and `SB_IS_NO_FIRING`; see [weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#the-ammunition-check)), and these apply to every weapon the character uses. The upper-body and weapon partial graphs were not examined for permission tasks and may carry more.

## Aim mode and camera hints

Each weapon keeps its own aim mode, [`WeaponBase::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimMode); the player has one, [`Player::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimMode).

- `StartUsing` registers the weapon for its pre-simulation update; sets the user and the two user flags (the local one from [`Character::IsLocalPlayerControlled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsLocalPlayerControlled)); for a local user adds the weapon's camera hint, [`m_CameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_CameraHash) (or [`m_MountedCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedCameraHash) while mounted), when that hint is not already active; records the use statistic; and seeds `m_AimTargetPosition` from the user's aim target ([`Character::m_AimTargetPositionWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AimTargetPositionWeapons)) or a point ahead. For a player user it then leaves precision aim when the weapon lacks [`m_SupportsPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SupportsPrecisionAim), and passes the player's mode to the weapon's [`SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetAimMode). It never changes `m_State`.
- [`WeaponBase::SetAimModeBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetAimModeBase) plays the aim-in or aim-out effect on every gun when the mode changes, stores it, and for a local user adds the precision hint on entering precision aim ([`m_PrecisionCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_PrecisionCameraHash), or [`m_ReeledHangPrecisionCameraHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ReeledHangPrecisionCameraHash) while hanging from the grapple) and removes both on leaving it. The sniper rifle ([`SniperWeapon::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-SetAimMode)) and the mortar override it.
- [`Player::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-SetAimMode) refuses precision aim while falling without a parachute or with none of the aimable slots filled. On a change it records the time, adds or removes the player's own precision hint, sets the aim control's aim type, and forwards the mode only to the selected weapon ([`Character::GetSelectedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetSelectedWeapon)), and only while the player is that weapon's user ([`WeaponBase::IsCharacterUsingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-IsCharacterUsingWeapon)); otherwise it draws the selected weapon when that weapon has a precision hint. It skips the forward while planting. Any other weapon the player uses keeps the mode it got at `StartUsing`, while its scatter and recoil scaling still follow the player's mode.
- `StopUsingBase` stops fire on every gun if firing, stops the components, clears every gun's burst and three of its effects, and for a local user records the statistic, removes the use hint by its hash, and returns the weapon to normal aim, which removes its precision hints. It tears down a mounted gun (removing the heavy slot and selecting a valid weapon), clears the user, keeps the pre-simulation update registered while effects are active, and clears the user flags and `m_InputBufferTimer`. A weapon wielding or dual-wielding outside the heavy slot gets [`E_WS_HOLSTERED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_HOLSTERED) written to `m_State` directly, not through [`SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState), so the state's physics-world and visibility changes do not run; it pokes each gun's object through its virtual table instead.
- `UpdateInventoryPermissions` clears the grenade permission and two others while the wielded weapon's precision hint is active.

Camera hints are global and keyed by their hash. Two users of weapons that share a hint hash share one hint: the second `StartUsing` finds it active and adds nothing, and the first `StopUsingBase` of either removes it while the other still uses it, as does the normal-aim return inside it for a shared precision hint. Weapons with distinct hashes add hints side by side. A `StartUsing` of a weapon that does not support precision aim drops the player out of precision aim, which forwards normal aim to the selected weapon and removes its precision hint. Apart from these, two weapons in use at once share no fire or aim state.

## The deferred operations

A weapon can queue an operation for its next pre-simulation update, recorded in [`m_DeferredUser`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUser), [`m_DeferredEquiper`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquiper), and the deferred flags that follow them. Each request registers the weapon for the update. `UpdatePreSim` runs, for a weapon with a user and no slot as for any other, the per-gun pre-simulation ([`WeaponData::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdatePreSim)), the components', the deferred recoil impulse, the awareness event, and then the queued operations in this order:

1. [`m_DeferredDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredDrop): [`OnDrop`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnDrop).
2. [`m_DeferredUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredUnequip): [`OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip), with the render interpolation skipped.
3. [`m_DeferredEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredEquip): [`OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip) with the deferred equipper, which ends the update.
4. [`m_DeferredStartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStartUsing) with [`m_DeferredStopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_DeferredStopUsing): both cleared, with the deferred user.
5. A start-using alone: skipped when the weapon already has a user; otherwise the deferred user's wielded weapon is unequipped and `StartUsing` runs.
6. A stop-using alone: the [`StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing) virtual, when there is a user.

The queuers are few. [`StartUsingDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsingDeferred) comes only from the mounted-weapon state task's entry, [`EquipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-EquipDeferred) only from the AI's equip, [`DropDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DropDeferred) only from [`Inventory::DropWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-DropWeapon), and [`UnequipDeferred`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UnequipDeferred) from the AI's unequip and switch, the graph script's `SpawnWeapons` node, and the non-player branch of the unequip state task. No player path queues an equip or a start-using of a weapon outside the wield, except the mounted-gun task.

While the weapon has a user, its pre-simulation update keeps the guns' animation at full detail while the user's [`m_LodFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_LodFlags) are clear, and drops it beyond a middle distance from the camera otherwise.

## Other readers of the user

Besides the fire path above, the weapon's user is read by `IsCharacterUsingWeapon`, by `SetAimModeBase` (the grapple-hang hint), and by `StopUsingBase` (the mounted-gun teardown). The user is required both to fire at all and to be the bullets' owner.
