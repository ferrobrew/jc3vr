# Item pickup and loose bodies

What the player can pick up, how the game picks up a weapon lying in the world, and what a loose object's body is to the Havok world: its motion, its owner, how its velocity is set and an impulse moves it, and how bodies are joined. Hitting and pushing a body is in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md), and the physics step and collision filter in [havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md).

## What lies in the world

A weapon in the world (dropped, broken out of a supply drop's cargo, or placed) is a [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase), a physics game object of its own rather than a rigid object: it has its own body, and a query that hits it reports the weapon. Its [`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State) is [`E_WS_DROPPED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_DROPPED) while it lies loose and [`E_WS_PICKING_UP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_PICKING_UP) while being picked up, and the run-time type test tells it apart ([`WeaponBase::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-TYPE_ID) through [`GameObject::IsType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-IsType), as in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#hit-objects-and-run-time-types)). It stays until the spawn system's despawn ranking, by distance from the player and the view rather than time, removes it, which never happens while anything holds a strong reference to it ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md)).

A loose prop (a crate, a barrel, a gas canister, debris) is a [`RigidObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject) ([`TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##func-TYPE_ID)), a physics game object with one rigid body that may be static, fixed, keyframed, or dynamic. Characters, vehicles, and weapons are not rigid objects.

A collectible (`CCollectibleObject`) is a plain game object with no physics instance, collected through its own collect event, which scripts and interactions send, so a physics query never reports one. The other use interactables (tombs, tapes, and the like) are interaction graphs entered through the use input, not bodies either.

## Picking up a weapon

Ammunition needs no input. Each weapon in the world carries a pickup interaction, whose activation, when the player comes within it, asks the weapon ([`WeaponBase::ShouldAutoPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ShouldAutoPickup)), which itself collects what the game collects unasked:

- a power-core weapon gives nothing;
- a hand grenade answers yes while the activator's grenade reserve is below its cap, and the interaction starts its pickup, which adds its rounds to the reserve and despawns it;
- any other weapon with stored rounds ([`m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo)) gives them to the reserve ([`Inventory::AddAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddAmmo), up to the cap) when the activator holds a weapon of the same ammunition type ([`Inventory::HasWeaponWithAmmoType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-HasWeaponWithAmmoType)) and neither gun nor character has unlimited ammunition. The HUD shows an ammunition pickup, the picked-up-ammunition event is sent, the weapon keeps what did not fit, and the answer is no, so the weapon stays.

`HasWeaponWithAmmoType` reads only the eight slots' [`m_AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AmmunitionType), and this credit is its one caller. The weapon's pickup check separately refuses the use-input pickup when the slot already holds the same weapon and the reserve is full, reading the slot only (unverified).

The weapon itself takes the use input. The player's weapon input task sets [`E_INPUT_USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_USE_ITEM) on a click of [`USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-USE_ITEM), and [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling) acts on it while the character may pick up ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPickup): the animation's permission, [`m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup), with [`SB_DISABLE_PICKUP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_PICKUP) clear). On land it queues the full-body pickup act (`ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON`); the pickup interaction's execution starts the pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart), which takes the weapon out of the physics world), and the weapon's pickup track message completes it ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem), detailed with the drop in [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md#completing-a-pickup)). A swimming character picks up the first weapon among its interaction proxy's valid entries at once and plays the partial pickup act instead.

The HUD's weapon pickup panel shows the weapon on offer and the ammunition it would bring, the weapon it would replace, and the use-item glyph. [`Player::UpdateWeaponHUD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateWeaponHUD), from the player's post-physics update each frame, takes the weapon the first valid interaction entry offers, as the swimming pickup does, and offers nothing while `USE_ITEM` is disabled in the player's action map or the animation forbids a pickup. It remembers the last offer ([`m_CurrentWeaponPickupId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_CurrentWeaponPickupId)) and tells the HUD only on a change: a new weapon or ammunition shows the panel ([`HUDUI::OnNewValidWeaponPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-OnNewValidWeaponPickup)), and no offer clears it ([`HUDUI::ClearValidWeaponPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-ClearValidWeaponPickup)). The game, not the input, chooses the weapon; which entry wins with several weapons in range, and how the interaction's execution follows the act, are unverified.

## A body in the Havok world

The Havok world ([`hknpWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld)) keeps its bodies in one array by body id ([`m_bodies`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodies)), up to the highest id allocated ([`m_peakBodyIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_peakBodyIndex)). A body ([`hknpBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody)) is moved by a motion ([`m_motionId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_motionId) into [`hknpWorld::m_motions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_motions)), and only a dynamic body ([`IS_DYNAMIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::IS_DYNAMIC) in [`m_flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_flags)) is moved by velocities and impulses. A destroyed body's id is freed, its slot's motion id invalid ([`INVALID_MOTION_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::INVALID_MOTION_ID)) until a new body takes the id, so a body id alone does not name an object across frames.

The motion ([`hknpMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion)) holds the centre of mass and the orientation of its frame, the inverse inertia and inverse mass as Havok half floats ([`m_inverseInertia`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion##field-m_inverseInertia); a zero inverse mass is infinite mass), the linear velocity in world space, and the angular velocity in the motion's frame. That frame is the body's principal frame: the inverse inertia is one value per axis, and the world-space inverse inertia is that diagonal turned by the motion's orientation. Bodies attached to one another share one motion, and with it one centre of mass and set of mass properties. Positions are Havok space, the game-space position less [`PhysicsSystem::m_AccumulatedShift`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##field-m_AccumulatedShift) ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#pushing-a-body)).

A body's owner is one of its properties. The body manager keeps per-property buffers by body id ([`hknpWorld::m_bodyProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodyProperties), looked up by key with [`hknpBodyPropertyMap::getWithDefault`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBodyPropertyMap##func-getWithDefault)), a body having a property when its bit is set in the buffer's occupancy. The engine's instance property ([`BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld::BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE)) holds the body's [`PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance), whose owner ([`m_GameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance##field-m_GameObject)) is the physics game object a ray query reports for the body, as [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) reads it.

## Setting a body's velocity

[`hknpWorld::setBodyVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyVelocity) (labelled a getter by the disassembler, though it writes both velocities) sets a dynamic body's linear and angular velocity, both in world space, and wakes it; [`setBodyLinearVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyLinearVelocity) and [`setBodyAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyAngularVelocity) set one each. Like [`applyBodyImpulseAt`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-applyBodyImpulseAt), they leave a body that is not dynamic alone. The impulse at a point is a rigid-body solver's arithmetic: the linear velocity gains the impulse times the inverse mass, and the angular velocity gains the impulse's moment about the centre of mass, turned into the motion's frame and scaled per axis by the inverse inertia, so its effect can be predicted from the motion alone.

The setters change the motion directly, not deferred, so they belong outside the physics step, where the engine's own impulses and queries run; that no step overlaps the player's aim update is unverified ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#threading)).

## Joined bodies

A Havok constraint joins a body to another, and the step resolves it with the body's contacts, removing whatever velocity the constraint forbids. The game joins bodies three ways:

- A placed [`Constraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint) (`CConstraint`, [`TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint##func-TYPE_ID)) is a game object that names two objects by object id and joins their bodies once both exist. Its data picks the joint (fixed, deformable fixed, ball and socket, stiff spring, hinge, limited hinge with angular limits and friction, ragdoll, wheel, or prismatic), optionally driven by a position, spring-damper, or velocity motor and optionally breakable past a force. Any object with a body can be joined; which shipped props have one (gates, hatches, signs, and the like), and that they are rigid objects, is unverified.
- A vehicle's parts ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)) carry constraints from the physics prototype: a part attached with one (`CPartInstance::AttachPartWithConstraint`, unbound) hangs from its parent, a loosened door or hood swings on its hinge, and the constraint breaks when the part breaks off (`CPartInstance::BreakPartConstraint`, unbound). A merged part has no body of its own, so a query on it reports the vehicle; a loosened one has its own body, owned by a vehicle-part object ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-loose-door)).
- The grappling hook's wire, the magnet weapon's gravity field, and a ragdoll's joints make constraints at run time.

The collision filter keeps joined bodies apart ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md#collision-filtering)); `hknpWorld::setBodyCollisionFilterInfo` (unbound) changes a body's filter word on the live world.
