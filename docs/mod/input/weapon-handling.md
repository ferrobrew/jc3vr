# Weapon handling

The parts of the controller weapon handling that sit between the hands and the weapons: the pouches on the body, the foregrip, a held gun's weight, recoil, the action-runs tier, the fire input with empty hands, the empty weapon, and how grenades meet the hands. The weapons as objects in either hand are [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md) and [physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md), the loadout [two-handed-inventory.md](two-handed-inventory.md), the reload [reload.md](reload.md), and what a grip takes [grip.md](grip.md). The code is `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/`, tuned by `hold.toml` and `handling.toml` in `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). Everything here acts only while the bindings inject, under the controller scheme.

The weapon handling runs on the input tick in two halves around the binding tables: the first reads the wielded weapons and offers their grip targets ([grip.md, "Targets"](grip.md#targets)), the grip model picks what each hand takes, and the second acts on the grabs and holds the fire inputs back during a weapon action.

## Pouches

The pouches ride Rico's skeleton, the body the player sees, so the player reaches where they look ([rig.md, "The body's volumes on Rico's skeleton"](../body/rig.md#the-bodys-volumes-on-ricos-skeleton)); their offsets are `volumes.pouches` in the hold tuning. The grenade and C4 pouches sit on the chest's front, the grenade on the dominant hand's side and the C4 nearer the midline on the other, so they never change sides with a handover. The magazine pouch sits on the belt's front, on the midline with one gun or none, so the free hand and the gun's push onto it ([reload.md, "The belt reload"](reload.md#the-belt-reload)) both reach it without crossing the body, and with a gun in each hand one on each gun's side.

On foot the hip joints the belt hangs from are low-passed over about half a stride (`volumes::StrideFilter`), because the gait bobs the pelvis and sprinting grabs missed. Looking down bends Rico's chest forward while the player's barely moves, so the chest pouches are turned back toward upright by the look's share while the spine is near upright, which leaves them on the chest in a skydive. A pouch exists only while it has something to give. The models are [grenades.md](grenades.md#the-pouches-models).

## The foregrip

A long gun held in one hand while the other is free has a foregrip ([two-handed-inventory.md, "The gun in either hand"](two-handed-inventory.md#the-gun-in-either-hand)) at the weapon's own grip point for the other hand ([`WeaponBase::GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition)), or a fixed distance along the barrel without one (`weapons::foregrip`); a hand on any other part of a physical gun braces it there ([physical-guns.md, "Grabbing a gun anywhere"](physical-guns.md#grabbing-a-gun-anywhere)).

While held, the holding hand turns about its grip by the shortest rotation that lays the foregrip on the line to the support hand, keeping the holding hand's roll, and the support hand moves onto the foregrip. The pose is applied to the cockpit-frame hands every hand subsystem starts from, and the engine's own off-hand IK stays suppressed ([hands-and-aiming.md, "The secondary hand IK"](hands-and-aiming.md#the-secondary-hand-ik)). The turn stops at `foregrip.max_turn_deg`, so a hand crossing behind cannot spin the gun toward the player, and fades out below `foregrip.min_span_m`. The hold ends when the support grip opens, the hand strays past `foregrip.release_distance_m` (measured with the holding hand fully turned, so only what the turn and a slide cannot absorb counts), a physical gun's brace breaks ([physical-guns.md, "The seat and the joints"](physical-guns.md#the-seat-and-the-joints)), the weapon leaves the hands, or a hand loses tracking.

## Weight

The class comes from [`WeaponBase::m_Class`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Class): pistols, revolvers, and SMGs are sidearms; assault rifles, shotguns, and sniper rifles are rifles; launchers, miniguns, mortars, and the special weapons are heavy (`controllers::weapons::heft`, tuned per class under `heft`). Held in one hand, a long gun is awkward, so the player braces it: its barrel sags more the further the hand reaches from the head, it sways in pitch and yaw on two incommensurate waves, and it follows on a damped rotational spring so it trails a fast turn. A one-handed shot kicks harder, and a detour on [`WeaponBase::GetCurrentScatterValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetCurrentScatterValue) widens the player's scatter. Braced in the foregrip, a long gun still follows the hands on a stiffer critically damped spring and sags a little, turning the support hand with it, so the gun drives both hands; the two weights blend as the grip engages.

The weight is applied after the foregrip pose and before recoil, so everything that reads the hands carries it and a shot goes where the drooping gun points. While a reload round is in the other hand, or the support hand is in the foregrip's volume, the gun freezes its turn without lag or sway, so the well and foregrip hold still for the hand closing on them.

## Recoil

A detour on [`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) reads the change in the weapon's per-tick recoil ([`m_RecoilPitch`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilPitch), [`m_RecoilYaw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilYaw)) across each of the player's shots and kicks a per-hand damped spring about the grip: a pitch, a yaw about the handle, and a shift back along the barrel, scaled by `controllers.weapons.recoil.kick_scale` (the pause menu's `RECOIL STRENGTH`). The offset is applied after the weight, so the aim follows the rendered gun: a player who holds still sees it climb, and one who pulls against it stays on target. The foregrip softens the kick, and the game's scatter stays on top.

While the guns aim by hand, a replacing detour on [`WeaponCameraModifier::ProcessCameraContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::weapon_camera_modifier::WeaponCameraModifier##func-ProcessCameraContext) writes the camera without the boom's recoil turn or the snap-aim assist, because the boom's turn persists into the camera's yaw and pitch, and the camera belongs to the headset.

## The action-runs tier

A reload, draw, or holster ([`SB_IS_RELOADING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_RELOADING), [`SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_DRAWING_WEAPON), [`SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_IS_HOLSTERING_WEAPON)) puts the weapon's hand in the action-runs tier ([hands-and-aiming.md, "Hand tiers"](hands-and-aiming.md#hand-tiers)): the game plays the action while the hand stays on its controller, and the fire inputs are dropped from the tables' output so a trigger pull does not fire during it.

## Fire with empty hands

The game draws the selected weapon when the fire or precision-aim input arrives with nothing wielded ([`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)). Under the controller scheme weapons come from sockets, the wheel, or a slot select, so for that call the player's fire and precision-aim entries of [`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs) read zero whenever they would draw, and are put back for the rest of the tick (`controllers::weapons::unarmed_fire`). For the same reason, a grenade or charge in a hand holds the fire input back unless the other hand holds a gun.

## The empty weapon

Pulling the trigger of an empty weapon that cannot reload makes the game switch weapons, which would take a gun out of the hand that drew it ([weapon-selection.md, "The switch away from an empty weapon"](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-selection.md#the-switch-away-from-an-empty-weapon)). A detour on [`WeaponBase::CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-CheckIfReloadShouldBeTriggeredBase) marks the check of the player's weapon, and a detour on [`Inventory::TryToSwitchToValidWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-TryToSwitchToValidWeapon) reports no valid weapon inside a marked check (`controllers::weapons::empty`). The game then plays its out-of-ammunition notice and the hand pulses (`Event::DryFire`); manual and scripted switches are unaffected.

## Grenades, C4, and melee

Grenades and C4 come from their pouches into a hand and leave it by opening the fingers; nothing throws them from a button, and the game's camera-aimed planting mode is bypassed. While one hand holds a gun only the other may take an explosive. The rest is [grenades.md](grenades.md).

The game's melee is on no binding, because it lunges the body toward its target and plays its own attack, taking the body from the player. An empty palm meets the world ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)) but passes through characters.

## Unverified

Unchecked in the headset: the recoil's signs and tuning; the weight tuning and whether a support hand carried with a lagging braced gun reads as holding it; the pouches on players of other sizes; and that zeroing the fire inputs has no side effect on the delayed holster and switch.
