# The weapon aim and fire pipeline

The aim state and its readers are in [aim-state.md](aim-state.md), the fire gating and the weapon's user in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md), the grapple's targeting in [grapple-pipeline.md](grapple-pipeline.md), and the grip and hand attachment in [weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md](../character/weapon-attachment-and-arm-ik.md).

## The short version

Everything the player fires is aimed at one point. [`PlayerAimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl) casts one ray from the camera transform and stores one target per purpose, not per weapon or hand. Each frame [`CPlayer::UpdatePostCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/character::player##func-Player_UpdatePostCamera) stamps the weapon slot's point onto the character as [`Character::m_AimTargetPositionWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AimTargetPositionWeapons), which fans out unchanged to every weapon the character uses. Each weapon keeps its own copy as [`WeaponBase::m_AimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AimTargetPosition) and builds its shot from its own muzzle toward it.

## The aim target: `PlayerAimControl`

One `PlayerAimControl` hangs off the player ([`Player::m_AimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimControl)). It caches targets indexed by [`TargetType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType), one slot per purpose: [`TARGET_WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_WEAPONS), [`TARGET_STICKY_AIM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_STICKY_AIM), [`TARGET_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_GRAPPLE), [`TARGET_MELEE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_MELEE), [`TARGET_GRENADE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_GRENADE), and [`TARGET_SNAP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_SNAP). Every per-slot array on it is [`NOF_TARGET_TYPES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-NOF_TARGET_TYPES) long: [`m_Target`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_Target), [`m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos), [`m_DirectPositions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_DirectPositions), [`m_MaxAimDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_MaxAimDistance), and so on. The one weapon aim position is `m_AimPos[TARGET_WEAPONS]`, returned by [`GetAverageWeaponTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetAverageWeaponTargetPosition) despite its name; the grapple's is [`GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetActiveGrappleTargetPosition), from the same raycast and its own fitness scoring.

The per-frame update, [`PlayerAimControl::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdatePostSim), runs in order [`UpdateInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateInput) (stick input and dead zones), [`UpdateRange`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateRange), [`UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) (the raycast), [`UpdateAllTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateAllTargets) (target selection and aim assist), `UpdateLastValidGrappleTarget`, and [`UpdateCrosshairLook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateCrosshairLook).

### The raycast (`UpdateDirectAim`)

`UpdateDirectAim` fetches one matrix from [`GetAdjustedCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetAdjustedCameraMatrix), which reads [`GameCameraManager::GetCameraMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetCameraMatrix), or [`GetAlternateAimMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetAlternateAimMatrix) when the alternate-aim transform is active, and adds for the current weapon ([`GetCurrentWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetCurrentWeapon) with `TARGET_WEAPONS`) a small per-weapon-type ballistic pitch (`FOV × weapon tuning`). The direction is the matrix's normalised `-Z` axis and the origin its translation moved a short fixed distance along it. When the character's vehicle is the rocket boat ([`Vehicle::IsRocketBoat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-IsRocketBoat)), it first raises the translation to at least a water height, queried through [`PhysicsSystem_s_WaterQueryProxy`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem_s_WaterQueryProxy), plus a constant. [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) runs the ray, and [`CheckDirectTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-CheckDirectTargets) writes the hit into `m_DirectPositions[TARGET_WEAPONS]` and `m_AimPos[TARGET_WEAPONS]`.

It computes the origin inline rather than calling [`GetRaycastStartPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetRaycastStartPosition)(the same offset on the unadjusted `GetCameraMatrix`, used by `GetAimDistance` and the grapple reticle's blend factors). It does call [`GetRaycastMinDistanceStartPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetRaycastMinDistanceStartPosition)(a point blended from the wielded weapon's barrel and the head) only to divide its distance from the ray origin by the ray length ([`m_RayCastDistance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_RayCastDistance)) and store it as `m_MinDistanceHitFractionLimit` on the raycast filter (`CPlayerAimRaycastFilterCacheFirstHoleyHitAndMinDistanceHit`), which records a hit below that fraction in `m_HasHitMinDistance` and `m_MinDistanceHitFraction`; the filter's hit logic is not traced.

### The camera getters

The camera getters split by phase:

- [`GetInputMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-GetInputMatrix) reads [`m_NextRenderContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_context::CameraControlContext##field-m_NextRenderContext), the render-phase context, for movement-direction mapping.
- `GetCameraMatrix` reads [`m_NextCameraContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_context::CameraControlContext##field-m_NextCameraContext), rewritten by the sim-phase camera update from the internal camera, for every sim-side aim consumer: `PlayerAimControl` (the raycast, `GetAdjustedCameraMatrix`, and the target visibility casts), the weapon aim-target queries, and the melee and grapple tasks.
- `GetAlternateAimMatrix` reads `m_NextRenderContext.`[`m_AlternateAimTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::camera_context::CameraContext##field-m_AlternateAimTransform).

### Body-turn reactions to the aim direction

[`NStateTask_LocoUtil::GetAimMoveAngle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-GetAimMoveAngle) measures the XZ angle from the move direction to the player's aim target, and the aim-relative act dispatchers (`QueueAimRelativeOnSpotActions`, `QueueRotateOnSpots`, `QueueStopTurns`, and related helpers) queue turn acts from it. The family also passes `track_face_dir` to the orientation executor with a blackboard face direction from the same aim reference, whose continuous yaw turns the body independently of the acts. Both follow `GetCameraMatrix`.

### Aim assist (`UpdateAllTargets`)

Assist acts on the aim target, upstream of any weapon. `UpdateAllTargets` scores candidates with per-purpose fitness functions (`SetTargetWeaponsFitness`, `SetTargetSnapFitness`, `SetTargetStickyFitness`, and others) driven by angle to the camera forward and distance, picks `m_Target[TARGET_WEAPONS]`, and the sticky and snap machinery nudges `m_AimPos[TARGET_WEAPONS]` toward it. Separately, the character-state task [`NAutoAimToTarget_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/aim::aim##func-NAutoAimToTarget_Update) rotates the aim and camera direction toward a locked target. A point written downstream, into `m_AimTargetPositionWeapons` or a weapon's `m_AimTargetPosition`, receives no assist.

The candidates are the [`TargetSystem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetSystem)'s three sets of [`Target`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::Target)s: characters, vehicles, and other objects. The wielded weapon's aim-assist tuning picks which sets a pass walks, through the system's include flags. The weapon, grenade, and snap purposes share [`GetFitnessScore`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-GetFitnessScore), which scores a target inside a cone around the adjusted camera forward, higher being better. Melee uses [`SetTargetMeleeFitness`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-SetTargetMeleeFitness) along the move input or body forward, lower being better, over characters in melee reach; `UpdatePostSim` clears the melee slot at its start, so the melee input task (`NStateTask_InputMeleeTask`, in the earlier state-machine pass) reads the previous tick's slot. The melee attack needs [`Character::CanUseMelee`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CanUseMelee) and the hammer ability ([`AbilitiesHandler::IsAbilityEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::AbilitiesHandler##func-IsAbilityEnabled)); the rest of melee is in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md).

## From aim control to the character: `CPlayer::UpdatePostCamera`

Each frame after the camera update, `CPlayer::UpdatePostCamera`:

1. runs `PlayerAimControl::UpdatePostSim`;
2. reads `m_AimPos[TARGET_WEAPONS]` into a local `weapon_target_pos` and copies it into [`ControlParameters::m_AimTargetWeapons`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::control_parameters::ControlParameters##field-m_AimTargetWeapons) of the character's control parameters ([`Character::m_ControlParameters`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_ControlParameters)), the copy the animation side sees;
3. [`Character::UpdateWeaponAiming`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateWeaponAiming)`(character, &weapon_target_pos)`, a setter, writes the point into `m_AimTargetPositionWeapons` (a `CVector3f`) and raises the one-frame flag [`m_AimTargetPositionWeaponWasSetThisFrame`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AimTargetPositionWeaponWasSetThisFrame).
4. [`Character::UpdateGrenadeAiming`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateGrenadeAiming) writes the grenade point, `m_AimPos[TARGET_GRENADE]`, into [`m_AimTargetPositionGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AimTargetPositionGrenade) directly;
5. for a driver, calls [`Vehicle::SetAimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-SetAimTargetPosition) ([below](#vehicle-turret-mounted-wingsuit-and-mech-weapons)).

If nothing set the weapon target this frame, `Character::UpdateWeapons_Serial` falls back to a point a fixed distance along the character's body forward (`m_WorldMatrixT1` forward). AI and remote players reach the field through `CSniperAimer` and the AI action `AiSetAimTarget`. `m_AimTargetPositionWeapons` is the narrowest choke point for the player's weapon aim.

## The fan-out to weapons: `Inventory`

[`Character::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel) passes `m_AimTargetPositionWeapons` and `m_AimTargetPositionGrenade` to [`Inventory::Update_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-Update_Parallel), which forwards them to [`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel), which hands the weapon point to each weapon it ticks (the mounted heavy-slot gun, each wingsuit gun in `m_Wingsuit->m_WingsuitWeapons[]`, or the wielded weapon; which in what state is in [weapon-firing.md](weapon-firing.md#the-shooting-permission)) and the grenade point to the selected grenade, through the [`WeaponBase::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel) virtual (base [`UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_ParallelBase)), `(dt, aim_position, inputs)`. The weapon validates and stores it in `m_AimTargetPosition` (the inlined `SetWeaponAimTargetPosition`).

A dual-wield weapon ([`E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD)) is one `WeaponBase` with two guns ([`WeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData) entries) ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md#the-dual-wield-weapon)), so both barrels fire toward the one point. There is no per-hand or per-fire-point target: `GetShotMatrix` reads the one `m_AimTargetPosition` for every fire point of the weapon.

## Fire direction, end to end (`GetShotMatrix`)

A shot's launch transform is [`WeaponBase::GetShotMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetShotMatrix), called from [`WeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-Fire_Projectile) (base [`Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase)), [`BulletWeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BulletWeaponBase##func-Fire_Projectile), and their siblings. Small arms have no hitscan path: bullets are fast projectiles, and `UpdateDirectAim`'s ray serves only the aim target and the reticle. [`BulletWeaponBase::GetTuning`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::BulletWeaponBase##func-GetTuning) decides explosive and water behaviour, and [`NWeaponUtil::Trajectory_GetAngleToHitPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::NWeaponUtil##func-Trajectory_GetAngleToHitPosition) is called only from `BulletWeaponBase::Fire_Projectile`; thrown grenades use another solver ([below](#grenades)).

- The origin is the [`GetFireFromPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-GetFireFromPosition) virtual (base [`GetFireFromPositionBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetFireFromPositionBase)): the gun's world matrix composed with the current fire point of [`WeaponData::m_InternalFirePositions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_InternalFirePositions), per weapon and barrel. Grips come from the separate [`GetGripPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetGripPosition).
- The aim point is `m_AimTargetPosition`, or for a remote player one from the network component.
- The direction is [`Matrix4::CreateOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/types::math::Matrix4##func-CreateOrientation)`(from = muzzle, at = aim point, up)`.
- If the aim point is very close to the muzzle (the distances are in the def), the weapon is in [`E_PROJECTILE_FIRE_DIRECTION_BARREL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ProjectileFireDirectionMode##variant-E_PROJECTILE_FIRE_DIRECTION_BARREL) mode ([`m_ProjectileFireDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ProjectileFireDirection), overridden by [`m_ProjectileTempFireDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ProjectileTempFireDirection), set per instance by `SetProjectileFireDirectionMode` and `ResetProjectileFireDirectionMode`), or `force_barrel_direction` is set, it fires along the weapon's forward. [`m_ProjectileOffsetFromAimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ProjectileOffsetFromAimPos) applies a spread ring, and scatter (`RotationYawPitchRoll` from tuning) is layered on unless suppressed.

## Which gun fires

A weapon of several guns fires one per shot, never two in a tick. The inventory calls its per-tick update once, with one fire input (on foot `FIRE_RIGHT`; there is no fire-left action, [input.md](input.md)), and the base update reads [`m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire), the current gun's index in [`m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData) (a [`WeaponDataArray`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponDataArray) of `WeaponData` pointers spanning [`m_Begin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponDataArray##field-m_Begin) to [`m_End`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponDataArray##field-m_End)), once before its per-gun loop. Only that gun gets the fire-input update ([`UpdateWeapon_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Parallel)), which starts at most one shot on it ([`Fire_StartBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_StartBase)), so whatever writes the index first picks the gun that may fire. The serial pass ([`UpdateFromUserPostSim_SerialBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_SerialBase)) walks the same gun only, and its per-gun update ([`UpdateWeapon_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateWeapon_Serial)) runs the components' serial updates and never fires.

The inlined tail of `Fire_ProjectileBase` (`MarkNextWeaponComponentForFire`) resets the current fire point's [`InternalFireInformation::m_TimeSinceLastFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::InternalFireInformation##field-m_TimeSinceLastFire), advances the gun's fire-point cursor [`WeaponData::m_InternalFirePositionCurrentIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_InternalFirePositionCurrentIndex) modulo its fire points, records the gun in [`m_LastWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_LastWeaponToFire), and advances `m_NextWeaponToFire` modulo the gun count, except that a dual-wield weapon in [`E_WS_WIELDING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WIELDING) resets it to the first gun. A held trigger fires the guns in turn, one per interval. A bullet weapon spawns its bullets after the advance, from [`GetLastFireFromPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetLastFireFromPosition) with the firing gun passed explicitly. Whether any one-handed weapon fires more than one projectile per shot is not checked. The function IDA labels `CWeaponBase::MarkNextWeaponComponentForFire` is [`Vehicle::IsRocketBoat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-IsRocketBoat), a const predicate comparing [`Vehicle::m_VehicleTypeNameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleTypeNameHash) with `HashString("v1706_boat_na_rocketboat")`, called by `Fire_Projectile` only for a vehicle holder to choose the rocket boat's `CPlayerActionObserver::OnAction` actions.

The rest of the per-shot bookkeeping runs once per `Fire_Projectile` call, keyed off the weapon and the `weapon_data` gun passed in: [`ConsumeAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-ConsumeAmmo), the decrements of [`m_ForceFireCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireCount) and [`m_ProjectileOffsetFireCount`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ProjectileOffsetFireCount), the recoil and scatter accumulators ([`m_CurrentScatterValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_CurrentScatterValue), [`m_CurrentRecoilPitchValueToRecover`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_CurrentRecoilPitchValueToRecover), [`m_RecoilYaw`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilYaw), [`m_RecoilPitch`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_RecoilPitch)), `PlayFireEffect`, `RumbleFire`, `CameraShakeFire`, `AddHeatPoints`, and `Fire_Projectile_Log_Statistics`. The gun index and cursor choose only the muzzle and which fire point's timer resets. The muzzle comes from the gun at `m_NextWeaponToFire`, but the flash plays on the `weapon_data` gun the inventory's dispatch chose, so when the two differ they come from different barrels.

The fire state is the weapon's: the trigger reading ([`m_IsTriggerPulled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsTriggerPulled)), the semi-automatic flag ([`m_FiredThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_FiredThisInput)), the cancel ([`m_IsFiringCancelled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFiringCancelled)), the buffer ([`m_InputBufferTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_InputBufferTimer)), the interval ([`m_TimeSinceLastFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_TimeSinceLastFire)), [`m_IsFiring`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsFiring), the aim target and mode, the magazine, recoil, scatter, overheat, and heat, and a stop ([`Fire_StopBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_StopBase)) applies to every gun. Per gun are only the burst counter ([`WeaponData::m_BurstShotsLeft`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BurstShotsLeft)), which forces fire on its gun, the tracer counters ([`m_TracerCounter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_TracerCounter)), the cursor, and the effects, so the guns share one fire rate.

## The reticles

The HUD movie (`hud.gfx`) draws two reticle groups, `MCI_weapon_reticle_group` and `MCI_grapple_reticle_group`, under `MCI_safe_area_center.MCI_reticles`, which also holds the separate remote-explosive indicators. [`HUDUI::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-Update) refreshes both every frame it does not hide the HUD.

[`HUDUI::UpdateWeaponReticle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-UpdateWeaponReticle) invokes the movie's `UpdateWeaponReticle(show, look, size, shape)` every call. `show` is false only in the HUD's plant-explosive mode. `look` is the weapon slot's crosshair look from `UpdateCrosshairLook`: the movie tints the reticle red over a hostile target and green over an allied one, and dims it out of range. `size` is the selected weapon's reticle size (its virtual `GetReticleSize`, drawn as the spread of the crosshair's arms) and `shape` its reticle shape; the function substitutes no reticle while the camera is turned to a look-at, and its own shapes in the wingsuit, while falling, while ragdolling, while grappled ([`GrapplingHook::ShouldShowAttachedUI`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-ShouldShowAttachedUI)), and in a vehicle, from the vehicle weapon the character uses. The movie sets the group's `visible` from `show`, shows only the current shape's clip, and moves its arms by the size. The group is never positioned: it sits at screen centre, where the camera aims.

[`HUDUI::UpdateGrappleReticle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-UpdateGrappleReticle) projects the grapple slot's aim point to screen ([grapple-pipeline.md](grapple-pipeline.md#the-grapple-reticle)), the frame's first world-to-screen call (the later ones are the wire-attachment and grip-radius samples). The movie places the group's base clip at that point, hides it when the look is out of range, and shows the attached-wire clip and a ghost of the base while a wire is attached. It writes only the child clips' visibility, never the group's.

Hit feedback is not part of either group: the movie plays `MCI_safe_area_center.MCI_inflict_damage` when the player deals damage.

## Grenades

The grenade slot's point, `m_AimPos[TARGET_GRENADE]`, reaches the selected grenade weapon as its `m_AimTargetPosition`, which `CThrownGrenadeWeapon::Fire_Start` copies into `m_GrenadeTargetPosition` (with a special case when grappling).

The throw is `CThrownGrenadeWeapon::Fire_Projectile`, which runs on the throw animation's release event (inferred from `CThrownGrenadeWeapon::HandleTrackMessage` and the fire acts, unverified end to end): it places the primed grenade at the weapon's world matrix (the hand's animated position that frame) and calls [`Grenade::PropelToward`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-PropelToward) with the target point and the character's velocity. That calls [`Explosive::PropelToward`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PropelToward), which stores the relative velocity in [`m_RelativeVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_RelativeVelocity) and raises [`m_DeferredPropelTowards`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredPropelTowards), calls the virtual `VelocityToHitTarget` ([`Grenade::VelocityToHitTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-VelocityToHitTarget), wrapping [`Explosive::VelocityToHitTarget`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-VelocityToHitTarget)) from the grenade's position to the target, stores the result in [`m_DeferredInitialVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DeferredInitialVelocity) for the physics body's later update, and resets the time since throw.

`Explosive::VelocityToHitTarget` clamps the target to `m_Range` and picks a flight time from the grenade weapon's arc tuning: `m_t_flat`, `m_t_up`, and `m_t_down`, blended by distance over `m_max_distance_for_blend` and by the elevation angle against `m_angle_up_max` and `m_angle_down_max`, with a height-based time for roof targets. It solves the launch velocity with [`NWeaponUtil::Trajectory_GetVelocityToHitPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::NWeaponUtil##func-Trajectory_GetVelocityToHitPosition) (`(start, end, time, gravity_factor)`, with the physics world's gravity). `Grenade::VelocityToHitTarget` adds the relative velocity unless the grenade has a homing target, and `Grenade::PropelToward` records the target in [`Grenade::m_InitialLandingPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_InitialLandingPos) and starts the homing search when the homing upgrade is enabled. The chain's only input is a target point; the solver picks the flight time, so a launch velocity cannot be requested directly.

The throw animation drives the weapon through two track-message requests, serviced by the weapon's update from its user ([`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel)), on the player's post-camera pass right after the aim update:

- The prime request, [`m_PrimeGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PrimeGrenade), is serviced after the base weapon update by [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::PrimeGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-PrimeGrenade) (`CreatePrimed<CGrenade>`), which consumes a grenade and spawns it live at the weapon's world matrix into [`m_PrimedGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##field-m_PrimedGrenade); the spawn can fail when the ammunition pool is full. The fuse ([`Explosive::m_DetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_DetonationTimer)) runs from the spawn, so a grenade held past its fuse explodes in hand; the quick release hides this. The post-simulation update counts it down only while [`Grenade::m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##field-m_ShouldTickDetonationTimer) is set, as the constructor sets it; the beacon weapon clears it, so a beacon's timer never runs. A primed object is checked against [`Grenade::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Grenade##func-TYPE_ID) before use as a grenade. While primed, the weapon's attachment update ([`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)) sets the grenade's transform ([`Explosive::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_WorldMatrixT1)) to the weapon's world matrix.
- The fire request, [`WeaponBase::m_ForceFireProjectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ForceFireProjectile), makes the base `UpdateWeapon_Parallel` call `Fire_Projectile` for each gun whatever the fire input. The base update runs before the prime, so a fire and prime requested together release nothing and then prime.

The primed grenade gets its physics body ([`Explosive::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_PfxInstance)) on its first post-simulation update, one update after the spawn, and `Explosive::PropelToward` and [`Explosive::SetPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetPhysicsTransform) do nothing before then. Both queue their effect for the body's next update, the transform first, and a later `SetPhysicsTransform` replaces an earlier one. [`ThrownGrenadeWeapon::CancelPrimedGrenade`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ThrownGrenadeWeapon##func-CancelPrimedGrenade) removes a primed grenade back to the inventory (the disassembler puts that name on an unrelated hash-table clear). The grenade permission ([`Inventory::m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowGrenades)) gates only the throw input; neither request checks it, nor the ammunition ([`WeaponBase::GetTotalAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-GetTotalAmmo)).

### The burning fuse

A thrown explosive shows and sounds its fuse through its trail: [`Explosive::PlayTrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PlayTrailEffect) plays the trail effect ([`m_TrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_TrailEffect)) and sound ([`m_TrailSound`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_TrailSound)) that the weapon gave the explosive at its spawn, at its current transform, and looks up the sound's `detonation_timer` parameter ([`m_TrailSoundDetonationParameterIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_TrailSoundDetonationParameterIndex)). From then on, every post-simulation update ([`Explosive::UpdatePostSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdatePostSim)), right after it ticks the fuse, sets that parameter to the share of the fuse burnt (zero at the start, one at detonation) and moves the effect and sound to the explosive's transform. What they do with it is uninspected data.

`PropelToward` starts the trail, so a grenade primed in Rico's hand ticks its fuse but shows and sounds nothing until the throw. The C4 weapon's parachute drop starts its charge's trail itself ([planted-explosives.md](planted-explosives.md)). A second call while a trail plays restarts it: the new effect takes the instance and softly stops a looping effect in it, and the new sound stops the old one. Playing an effect, the call belongs on the game thread outside the effect system's simulation and render updates. The grenade has no other fuse cue: no light, emissive, or HUD marker is driven from the timer, and priming plays no sound. Outside the explosive's own update, the only reader of a primed grenade's timer is `CThrownGrenadeWeapon::GetPrimedGrenadeData` (not bound), which nothing calls.

## Vehicle, turret, mounted, wingsuit, and mech weapons

Vehicle built-in weapons: [`Vehicle::SetAimTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-SetAimTargetPosition) writes [`Vehicle::m_AimingTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_AimingTargetPosition) and sets [`m_AIVehicleWeaponAimTimeout`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_AIVehicleWeaponAimTimeout) to its driver-seat or other-seat value; `CPlayer::UpdatePostCamera` calls it only from the driver seat ([`m_LocalPlayerSeat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_LocalPlayerSeat)). `CVehicle::UpdatePostCamera_Weapons_Parallel` passes the point to every built-in weapon that has a user through `UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`, substituting the position of `m_AimingTargetObject` when a lock-on target is set. For AI drivers it decays to a point ahead when the timeout lapses.

Turrets: [`TurretWeaponComponent::UpdateTurretAiming`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::TurretWeaponComponent##func-UpdateTurretAiming) rotates the turret toward the weapon's aim target at a limited rate, so the barrel lags. Mounted guns (in [`E_SLOT_HEAVY`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_HEAVY)) and wingsuit guns receive the character's shared point in the inventory fan-out.

The mech is a vehicle; its gravity gun is the second built-in weapon, with a `CMagnetWeaponComponent`, and its upper body turns with [`Vehicle::GetWeaponTurretCurrentYawAngle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-GetWeaponTurretCurrentYawAngle). The player aims it by the vehicle aim point like any built-in weapon; the AI path [`AIMechFireGravityGun`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/ai::ai##func-AIMechFireGravityGun) was read, and the player path is inferred from the built-in weapon loop (unverified).

## Precision aim and sniper zoom

When the weapon input [`Action::PRECISION_AIM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-PRECISION_AIM) is present, [`Character::CanPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CanPrecisionAim) holds (the `precision_aim` upgrade, or a weapon with a precision camera hash), and the animation allows it, the inventory toggles [`Player::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-SetAimMode). [`Player::UpdateAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateAimMode) returns to [`AIM_NORMAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::AimMode##variant-AIM_NORMAL) once precision aim is impossible. `SetAimMode`:

- refuses [`AIM_PRECISION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::AimMode##variant-AIM_PRECISION) while falling or freefalling outside a parachute, or with no weapon in the dual-wield, [`E_SLOT_TWO_HANDED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_TWO_HANDED), [`E_SLOT_TWO_HANDED_SPECIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_TWO_HANDED_SPECIAL), or [`E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE) slots;
- on a change, writes [`Player::m_AimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimMode) and a timestamp ([`m_AimModeChangeTime`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimModeChangeTime));
- in `AIM_PRECISION` adds the camera hint [`GameCameraManager::PRECISION_AIM_HINT_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager::PRECISION_AIM_HINT_ID) through [`GameCameraManager::AddHint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-AddHint), and removes it in `AIM_NORMAL`; any zoom is that camera state's data;
- sets [`PlayerAimControl::m_AimType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimType) to [`ENormal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::AimType##variant-ENormal) (assist off) in `AIM_PRECISION` unless the weapon enables auto-aim in precision mode ([`m_IsAutoAimInPrecisionAimEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_IsAutoAimInPrecisionAimEnabled)), and to [`EAuto`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::AimType##variant-EAuto) in `AIM_NORMAL`, subject to the aim-assist setting;
- forwards the mode to the weapon's virtual [`WeaponBase::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-SetAimMode) only for the selected weapon ([`Character::GetSelectedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetSelectedWeapon)) and only while the player is its user ([`WeaponBase::IsCharacterUsingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-IsCharacterUsingWeapon)), skipping it while planting; otherwise it draws the selected weapon when that weapon has a precision hint.

[`SniperWeapon::SetAimMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::SniperWeapon##func-SetAimMode) shows the HUD sniper overlay ([`HUDUI::ShowSniperOverlay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-ShowSniperOverlay)) and hides the player character (`CCharacter::SetVisible`) in `AIM_PRECISION`, and reverses both in `AIM_NORMAL` ([`HUDUI::HideSniperOverlay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-HideSniperOverlay)). A bolt-action rifle can leave precision aim to cycle ([weapon-ammo-and-reload.md](weapon-ammo-and-reload.md#sniper-bolt-and-shotgun-pump)).

`AIM_PRECISION` has three requesters: the inventory's weapon handling on the precision-aim input, only for a weapon with [`m_SupportsPrecisionAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_SupportsPrecisionAim); `CSniperWeapon::UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`, returning after a bolt cycle; and `CRPGBloodhoundWeapon::Fire_Projectile`, on every shot of the guided rocket launcher. All other callers pass `AIM_NORMAL`. A scoped rifle is a weapon of class [`E_WEAPON_CLASS_SNIPER`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponClass##variant-E_WEAPON_CLASS_SNIPER) ([`WeaponBase::m_Class`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_Class)), a `CSniperWeapon`. There is no second scene view for a scope: the zoom is the main camera's hint, and the only render-to-texture class is the Scaleform UI's.
