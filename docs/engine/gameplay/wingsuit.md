# The wingsuit

How the wingsuit opens, stalls, evades, and lands, and how the canopy opens from a jump or a fall. The canopy's own flight is in [parachute-locomotion.md](parachute-locomotion.md). The air physics of both is [`NAirMovement_UpdateAirPhysics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::parachute##func-NAirMovement_UpdateAirPhysics); the transitions below come from the decoded base graph (`rico_base`, `tools/anim_graph`, [animation-graph.md](../character/animation-graph.md)).

## Opening from the air

The wingsuit and the parachute open through two acts, `ACT_TRIGGER_WINGSUIT` and `ACT_TRIGGER_PARACHUTE`; which input action raises each is not traced and is unverified. The jumps (`S_JUMP_0` and its turned variants, the stunt jumps, and the train jumps), the falls (`S_FALLING`, `S_FAST_FALLING`, `S_FALL_TO_FREEFALL`), the reel-in's drops into a fall, and the falls out of the parachute and the suit take `ACT_TRIGGER_WINGSUIT` to `S_JC_WINGSUIT_OPEN_FROM_FALL`, and `S_FREE_FALLING` takes it to `S_JC_WINGSUIT_OPEN_FROM_FREEFALL`. `ACT_TRIGGER_PARACHUTE` goes to the matching parachute openings from the same states (the openings out of a grapple reel are in [parachute-locomotion.md](parachute-locomotion.md#the-openings-out-of-a-reel)). A character that walks off a ledge can therefore open either while the game still plays its jump, which runs until its clip finishes and only then hands over to `S_FALLING`.

The jumps and `S_FALLING` gate both acts on the suit or canopy being enabled and on two conditions:

| Condition | Class | Shipped parameters |
|---|---|---|
| `C_JUMP_OR_FALL_TO_IN_AIR_SPEED` | `CVerticalSpeedCondition` | a vertical speed below -0.1 m/s: descending |
| `C_JUMP_OR_FALL_TO_IN_AIR_HEIGHT` | `CFallDistanceCondition` | a fall distance of at least 4 m |

`CFallDistanceCondition` reads zero while the character has ground contact, and otherwise a distance from the ground probe results (to the ground below, or fallen so far: unverified). A press while rising from a jump, or too near the ground, opens nothing, and the act is not held over, so a later press is needed. `S_FAST_FALLING` and `S_FREE_FALLING` check only that the suit or canopy is enabled.

The boost wingsuit also takes off from the ground on `ACT_BOOST_FROM_GROUND`, taken from the idle, the moves, and the turns on foot (gated on the suit being enabled and the boost suit owned) to the wind-up `S_JC_WINGSUIT_BOOST_TRANSITION_FROM_GROUND_INTO`, then the launch `S_JC_WINGSUIT_BOOST_TRANSITION_FROM_GROUND`. The movement task does not run in the wind-up, so nothing turns the character there, and the launch leaves along its facing. The act takes a hold of about a second of the use button on foot; which action the game reads for it is unverified.

## The stall and flip

The wingsuit's blend state ([`WingsuitBlendState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::WingsuitBlendState)) blends the flight samplers each frame and alone decides whether the character stalls ([`UpdateSamplers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::WingsuitBlendState##func-UpdateSamplers)); no state bit, act, or sub-state of the wingsuit task is behind the stall. It measures the forward airspeed (the measured velocity along the forward axis) and enters the stall when that falls below [`WingsuitBlendState_ToStallSpeed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::WingsuitBlendState_ToStallSpeed) with the nose raised past [`WingsuitBlendState_StallNoseUp`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::WingsuitBlendState_StallNoseUp), skipping the nose test while a stall clip already has weight, and leaves once the airspeed recovers past a higher threshold. The decision is a local flag, set by one instruction ([`WingsuitBlendState_StallFlagSet`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::WingsuitBlendState_StallFlagSet)) and cleared on every other path.

While stalling, inside the stall clip's selection segment, the blend state ramps up one of two clips: the flip, a full loop that recovers from the stall, when the nose is raised further, and the plain stall otherwise. It sets the character's in-flip flag while the flip plays, and scales every other sampler down by the stall's weight, so the stall replaces the flight blend. The stall's turn segment is read in four places: the air physics skips part of its steering while it runs, the air brake and the close act are allowed only outside it ([the landing](#the-landing)), and the boost reads the in-flip flag (unverified).

Neither clip moves the physics body: the air physics clamps the body's pitch and orients it from the steering, so the loop is the clip's own rotation of the pose. No velocity damping is keyed to the stall; the speed is already lost to the ordinary drag, lift, and gravity. Without the stall, a character that keeps its nose raised loses lift and drops, and the body is pulled toward its velocity (the exact gating of that pull is unverified).

## The evade

The evade is a barrel roll with one act, [`ACT_BOOST_WINGSUIT_BARREL_ROLL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable), and no state bit or task sub-state of its own. The air physics starts it, reading the action only in its boost-suit part, for a character with the boost wingsuit ([`Character::HasBoostWingsuit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasBoostWingsuit)) in a state that flies it (this gate is unverified). When [`WINGSUIT_EVADE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-WINGSUIT_EVADE) fires, it tests a character value against a threshold, believed to be the boost's charge (unverified); short of it, the HUD shows the boost's failure unless an enemy missile is locked on, and nothing else happens. Otherwise, with the move stick out of its rest, it queues the act straight on the animated model ([`AnimatedModel::QueueAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimatedModel##func-QueueAct)), not through [`Character::QueueAct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-QueueAct), so nothing watching the character's queue sees it, while the model's queue, to which the character's forwards, sees every act. The game binds the action to the weaponized wingsuit's evade chord ([input.md](input.md)).

The directions are not separate acts but segments of the one clip (`SEG_IN_BARREL_ROLL_EVADE_MOVEMENT` and its `_LEFT`, `_RIGHT`, `_UP`, and `_DOWN` variants, then `SEG_IN_BARREL_ROLL_RECOVERY_MOVEMENT`). The clip rolls the pose a full turn. Inside the evade-movement segment, the air physics builds the body's orientation from a matrix the character holds and the velocity's heading, rather than from the steering alone; that the matrix is the animated reference bone, so the clip carries the body sideways with the velocity alignment off, is unverified.

## The landing

The wingsuit lands only through its close act: a character still gliding when it meets the ground ends in the crash ragdoll.

The wingsuit task (`NStateTask_MovementWingSuitTask::Update`, not yet located) queues `ACT_CLOSE_WINGSUIT` on every tick that [`CANCEL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-CANCEL) is pressed, outside the stall's turn segment, unless the grapple has an active wire or [`RETRACT_GRAPPLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-RETRACT_GRAPPLE) is pressed. `CANCEL` held is also the air brake, so a held brake asks to close the suit every tick. Every wingsuit state that takes the act (the glide, `S_JC_WINGSUIT_IDLE`, the openings, the grapple states, and the boosted takeoffs) gates it, outside the state's no-in-air-transition segment, on:

| Condition | Class | Shipped parameters |
|---|---|---|
| `C_WINGSUIT_CANCEL_FLIGHT_SPEED` | `CSpeedCondition` | the speed at or below the condition's limit |
| `C_WINGSUIT_CANCEL_LAND_HEIGHT` | `CHeightOverGroundCondition` | the height over the ground below the condition's limit |
| `C_HAS_WINGSUIT_CANCEL_UPGRADE` | `CUpgradePurchasedCondition` | the `upgrade_wingsuit_cancel` gear mod |

Slow and low enough, the act goes to `S_JC_WINGSUIT_TO_LAND_SLIDE`, then `S_JC_WINGSUIT_TO_LAND_SLIDE_ONGROUND`, and back to the idle on foot. Slow enough but higher, it goes to `S_JC_WINGSUIT_TO_FALL_SLOW`, an ordinary fall that lands as any fall does (`S_LAND_IDLE_*`, or `S_LAND_HARD` from high enough). Faster, nothing fires and the suit stays open.

`CSpeedCondition` compares the measured world speed, the magnitude stored after [`Character::m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) (not bound), so the sink counts as well as the glide; its flag picks the side of the limit. `CHeightOverGroundCondition` reads, by its flag, the first (body) rule system's [`AnimationRuleSystem::m_HeightOverGround`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::AnimationRuleSystem##field-m_HeightOverGround) (over the ground or the water surface, whichever is nearer) or `m_HeightOverGroundExcludingWater`. [`Character::DoGroundInfoRaycasts`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-DoGroundInfoRaycasts) zeroes both while the proxy is supported or sliding, and otherwise [`Character::CalculateHeightOverGround`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CalculateHeightOverGround) casts for them.

The air brake runs in the air physics while `CANCEL` is pressed in the wingsuit, outside the stall's turn segment, without `RETRACT_GRAPPLE`, and with the `upgrade_wingsuit_air_brake` gear mod bought and enabled. It multiplies the drag, and while the body descends multiplies the lift by its share of the descent. The steering's sideways input weakens both, the lift by its own factor and the drag by a capped share of the input (the unbound statics `s_max_airbrake_drag`, `s_max_airbrake_lift`, and their sideways-input factors), so a brake held while turning slows less. A suit that slows into a stall cannot brake or land until the stall ends.

The boost (Bavarium) wingsuit, `C_PLAYER_HAS_BOOST_WINGSUIT`, also lands from its brake state, `S_JC_WINGSUIT_BOOST_BREAK`, entered on `ACT_BOOST_BREAK` from the glide. While the brake is held (`C_WINGSUIT_BREAK_IS_HELD`) over ground whose normal is near vertical (`C_SURFACE_NORMAL_HORIZONTAL_FOR_BREAK_LANDING`), it goes to `S_JC_BOOST_WINGSUIT_BREAK_TO_LAND` near the ground (`C_BOOST_WINGSUIT_BREAK_LAND_HEIGHT`) and slow enough (`C_BOOST_WINGSUIT_BREAK_TO_LAND_FLIGHT_SPEED`), to `S_JC_BOOST_CRASH_RECOVERY` nearer the ground (`C_BOOST_WINGSUIT_CRASH_RECOVERY_LAND_HEIGHT`) under a higher speed limit (`C_BOOST_WINGSUIT_CRASH_RECOVERY_FLIGHT_SPEED`), and to `S_JC_BOOST_HIGH_VELOCITY_CRASH_RECOVERY` at that height at any speed. Each recovers onto the feet rather than ragdolling, and the plain close act also lands the boost suit.

Every wingsuit state also takes `ACT_CRASH_LAND` to `S_CRASH_LAND_RAGDOLL`. No code names the act, and what queues it on contact is not found (unverified).

The landing's parameters and gear mod names are the shipped graph's condition definitions. `CSpeedCondition`'s evaluator and the heights `CHeightOverGroundCondition` reads, with their writes, are checked against the IDB; the task's close act, the air brake's gates and multipliers, and `CHeightOverGroundCondition`'s evaluator are unverified.
