# Character root motion and the collision proxy

The character's world matrix and its writers, the displacement and proxy-input path, the proxy capsule and its support, scene queries, teleports, and the vehicle seat pose. The pyxis defs are mainly [`Character`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character) and the [`input::locomotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/input::locomotion), [`physics`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics), and [`game_world`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world) modules. Related: [havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md) (the physics step and collision filtering), [state-signals.md](state-signals.md) (the per-tick state signals), and [aim-pipeline.md](../gameplay/aim-pipeline.md) (the aim query).

## Where the world transform is authoritative

[`Character::m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) and [`Character::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WorldMatrixT1) are the authoritative pair; `GetWorldMatrix` and `GetWorldMatrixRef` return `&m_WorldMatrixT1`, which the aim IK reads. The render-facing `m_GraphicsWorldT0`/`T1` (`CTransform`) are derived from them (`SyncGraphicsMatricesToWorld` → `CTransform::FromMatrix4`), and the frame lerps T0 to T1 by `dtf`. The direct writers are `CCharacter::WriteWorldMatrix` (full matrix), `WriteWorldMatrixTranslation` (translation only), and [`Character::SetOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetOrientation) (rotation only, below). A direct write is a teleport: it bypasses the collision solve.

## The displacement path

[`NStateTask_LocoUtil::EvaluateCharacterDisplacement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterDisplacement) is the per-tick movement producer and a pure velocity function: given the character, its new transform, and flags, it fills `wanted_velocity_ws` and `wanted_velocity_ls` and does not write the root. Its callers are [`NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask##func-Update) (on foot), `NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask`, and `NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask`.

It selects one source and never sums. When the animation is not mid-blend, the marker [`ObjectBlackboard::APPLY_TARGET_DIR_INSTANTLY_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::APPLY_TARGET_DIR_INSTANTLY_ID) is not set, and the character is outside the special segments, it reads [`AnimationController::GetRawRootVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetRawRootVelocity), rotates it into world space, writes the wanted velocity, and returns. Otherwise it takes the code-driven path, which builds a direction from the blackboard's [`ObjectBlackboard::MOVE_DIR_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::MOVE_DIR_ID) (with the previous direction at [`PREVIOUS_TARGET_DIR_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::PREVIOUS_TARGET_DIR_ID) and the marker) and rotates it out; the `m_CodeDriven*` flags enable it (`m_CodeDrivenDisplacement`, `m_AllowCodeDrivenDisplacementUntilTolerance`, `m_EnableCodeDrivenDisplacementDuringBlend`, and `m_AngleCorrectionEnabled` / `m_MoveActionParams.m_AngleCorrectionRequestedDir`). While the stick is deflected, the walk cycle's root velocity already carries the walking speed.

The wanted velocity goes to the Havok character proxy, [`PfxCharacterInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance) (`m_Avatar` is the `CAvatar`, and `m_PendingProxyState` / `m_DefaultProxyState` select the capsule or quadruped shape), held in [`Character::m_PfxCharacter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_PfxCharacter). The physics step solves the proxy against collision (stairs, slopes, and walls), `m_WorldMatrixT1` is written from the solved proxy position, and the capsule follows because the proxy is the capsule.

## The locomotion task runs at idle and writes the proxy input directly

[`NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementLocomotionTask##func-Update) runs every on-foot sim tick, standing idle included. The only callers of [`NStateTask_LocoUtil::EvaluateCharacterOrientation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterOrientation) are this task, [`NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_MovementStuntingTask##func-Update), and [`NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask##func-Update), and a call counter on it advances on every on-foot tick including idle, which is neither stunting nor warping; the narrower move and aim tasks' counters stop at idle. At idle the task still runs [`EvaluateCharacterSpeed`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-EvaluateCharacterSpeed) (about 0), `EvaluateCharacterOrientation`, and `EvaluateCharacterDisplacement`.

At its tail it writes the proxy's one input, [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_Input`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_Input), a [`CharacterInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput) (`CPfxCharacterInstance::CCharacterInput`): [`CharacterInput::wantedVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-wantedVelocity), a `CVector3f` in metres per second in the character's local frame, set to the normalized local move direction times the speed. It returns without writing while the character is attached to a vehicle. The proxy reads `wantedVelocity` after the task, so a velocity added there afterwards is outside the displacement evaluator.

The speed is the animation's: `EvaluateCharacterSpeed` is the horizontal magnitude of the clip's raw root velocity, so the starts' ramps, the stops' decay, the turning starts' swings, and a run's speed changing within each step all reach the proxy. The input tasks' target speed, [`ObjectBlackboard::SPEED_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::SPEED_ID), does not set it: the input tasks branch on it (a value that is not positive routes them into the stop acts), and the movement task reads it for the player only to gate its angle correction. [`NStateTask_InputLocoSetTargetSpeedsTask::SetupTargetSpeeds`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_InputLocoSetTargetSpeedsTask##func-SetupTargetSpeeds) writes it from the movement settings by stick magnitude and state (walk, non-combat walk, run, or run backpedal speed), and writes zero when the stick is at or below the walk threshold, when [`Character::ShouldPreventFall`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ShouldPreventFall) reports a drop ahead taller than the settings' tolerance, or when the predicted wall distance on the blackboard is inside the settings' tolerance.

[`Character::GetWantedWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWantedWorldVelocity) rotates the stored value into world space through `m_WorldMatrixT1`; [`Character::SetWantedWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetWantedWorldVelocity) converts a world velocity into the local frame through the inverse of the proxy's transform and stores it.

The task also sets [`CharacterInput::gameControledVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CharacterInput##field-gameControledVelocity) while the proxy is supported on stairs ([`PfxCharacterInstance::m_IsSupportedOnStairs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_IsSupportedOnStairs)) and the blackboard carries a slope direction, and clears it otherwise. The flag changes how the Havok proxy update reads the vector, not which vector: set, the update rotates `wantedVelocity` by the proxy's rotation; clear, it hands the vector to the character state as it stands. Nothing re-derives the velocity from the flag.

There is one proxy-input channel. The function the IDB labels `CCharacterInput::SetWantedVelocity` is `Character::SetOrientation`: it writes a quaternion's rotation into `m_WorldMatrixT1`, keeping the translation, and stores the quaternion in [`Character::m_RotationGoal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_RotationGoal), bypassing the proxy. The proxy-driven states that call it (ragdoll get-up, in-air grapple-fire, grappling-hang, stunting, jump, grapple-yank, and upright) set their orientation through it; how they feed `wantedVelocity` is not established. The IDB's `CCharacter::WriteWorldMatrixOrientation` writes an attachment proxy's rigid body instead, and the orientation write appears inlined into `SetOrientation`; a second `SetOrientation(CQuaternion)` in the IDB also has an unrelated body.

### The turning acts

The input tasks turn the body with acts chosen by the angle from the body's forward to the move direction or the aim. Besides the starts and stops:

- [`QueueFlickStick`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueFlickStick) queues a straight or third-turn flick in place of the stops when a stop is called for inside the start clip's `FLICK_STICK_TURN_VALID_SEG` segment, and writes the residual angle correction as [`QueueStarts`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueStarts) does.
- [`QueueAimFlickStick`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueAimFlickStick), from the aim flick-stick task, queues a straight, quarter-turn, or half-turn aiming flick, with no angle correction.
- [`QueueAimRelativeOnSpotActions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueAimRelativeOnSpotActions) queues a quarter-turn aim-focus rotation for a standing player with a heavy weapon whose aim is well off the body's forward, and `ACT_TO_IDLE` otherwise, with no angle correction.
- [`QueueStuntTurns`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::locomotion::NStateTask_LocoUtil##func-QueueStuntTurns) queues a straight or third-turn stunt turn, or `ACT_TO_IDLE` for a small angle, and writes the residual angle correction. The idle, stops, flick-stick, and rotate-on-spot input tasks call it on their stunt branch when the state machine would not take `ACT_STUNT_TRANSITION`, and only while [`Character::ShouldApplyStuntModification`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ShouldApplyStuntModification) holds (the character is attached to a vehicle).

The game registers no act for the mortar's rotate-on-spot states, only its aim and fire acts.

## Capsule dimensions and runtime resize

The proxy's capsule is a Havok `hknpShape` selected per proxy state. [`PfxCharacterInstance::SetProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-SetProxyState) stores the state in [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_ProxyState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_ProxyState), fetches the shared character settings through [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_Owner`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_Owner) (its `GetSharedPfxCharacterSettings` virtual), takes the per-state shape from the settings' shape table (pre-built shapes keyed by `EProxyState`), swaps it onto the live body with [`hknpWorld::setBodyShape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyShape)`(world, bodyId, shape)`, and copies the state's per-shape byte into [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_CharacterRBListener`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_CharacterRBListener). [`PfxCharacterInstance::GetCharacterRigidBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-GetCharacterRigidBodyId) returns the body id. [`PfxCharacterInstance::BuildDefaultCharacterContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-BuildDefaultCharacterContext) builds the Havok character-state machine (on-ground, in-air, and jumping) but not the capsule dimensions. The engine swaps proxy state itself, through `m_PendingProxyState` / `m_DefaultProxyState` on the sim thread, on swimming, vehicle, and ragdoll transitions among others.

## The proxy's support and the fall

The proxy's support is [`PfxCharacterInstance::m_GroundInformation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_GroundInformation), a Havok [`hknpCharacterSurfaceInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpCharacterSurfaceInfo): a supported state (`UNSUPPORTED`, `SLIDING`, or `SUPPORTED`), the surface's normal and velocity, and whether it is dynamic. Beside it the proxy keeps the body it stands on, [`m_GroundBodyId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##field-m_GroundBodyId), and the last valid one.

The proxy's update, [`PfxCharacterInstance::UpdateContext`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-UpdateContext), finds the support afresh every tick through [`CheckSupportCustom`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-CheckSupportCustom), which asks the Havok character rigid body for its supporting contacts and writes the surface information and the ground body, or clears the ground body without support; a proxy whose body is not active is left unsupported. It then derives the inherited surface velocity, runs the Havok character context on the support and the proxy input, and sets the body's velocity to the context's output through [`hknpCharacterRigidBody::setLinearVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpCharacterRigidBody##func-setLinearVelocity), which keeps the change of velocity over the step as the body's acceleration.

### When the support is found and when it is read

The character's update passes sit in the game world's update queues by priority ([`Character::UpdatePassPrePhysics_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassPrePhysics_Serial) has the details), each priority run to completion. In the pre-simulation queue the state-machine passes come before the pre-physics pass, which runs the pre-physics state tasks (the on-foot movement task among them) and then the integrating-physics update that calls the proxy's update. [`PhysicsSystem::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-Update) follows the whole queue, firing the pre-simulation callbacks ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md#pre-simulation-callbacks)) and stepping, and the post-simulation queue runs the post-physics pass. So in one tick:

1. the state machines evaluate their conditions on the support the previous tick's proxy update found;
2. the proxy's update finds this tick's support and sets the proxy's velocity;
3. the pre-simulation callbacks run, then the step;
4. the post-physics pass and the next tick's state machines read the support found in 2.

A support or velocity written by a pre-simulation callback is the one the step and every reader until the next proxy update see. A later velocity write replaces the context output, and the surface velocity written beside it is not added again during that step; the next proxy update refreshes support before consuming surface velocity again. The physics-update wrapper is mislabelled `IsStillAlive` in the disassembler; it calls `PhysicsSystem::Update`, dispatches queued collisions, and processes damage events.

### Who reads it

[`PfxCharacterInstance::IsSupported`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-IsSupported) is the supported-state test. Its callers are the ground-contact condition (`CSuperGroundContactCondition`, exactly that call), the reel landing condition (`CReelLandCondition`), the fall bookkeeping (`CCharacter::UpdateFalling`), the ground raycasts (`CCharacter::DoGroundInfoRaycasts`), [`Character::IsInAir`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInAir), the script VM's ground query (`CCharacterModule::GetGroundInfo`), and the aim blend states' samplers. [`PfxCharacterInstance::IsGroundBodyValid`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxCharacterInstance##func-IsGroundBodyValid) (mislabelled `CPfxCharacterInstance::GetSurfaceNormal`) checks that the ground body names a static or dynamic body ([`hknpBody::IS_STATIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody), [`IS_DYNAMIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody)).

The character's ground contact is a valid ground body, a supported or sliding proxy, and no ragdoll that owns the character's displacement. With it, the ground raycasts skip the height-over-ground cast, clear the fall distance and the time until landing, and look up the ground body's owner to tell a vehicle underfoot; the fall distance condition (`CFallDistanceCondition`) reads zero; and `CCharacter::UpdateFalling` accumulates the fall time and the jump-or-fall time only in the falling and jumping states without it.

The fall conditions are the ground-contact, fall distance, and fall time conditions and the fall transition condition (`CFallTransitionCondition`, on the proxy's vertical speed, the fall distance, the time until landing, and the jump-or-fall time); unverified. In Rico's base graph ([animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md)) the on-foot states (the idle, the moves, and the stops among them) leave for `S_FALLING` on an `ACT_UPDATE` transition needing `C_IS_SUPPORTED` false, `C_ABOVE_FALL_HEIGHT` true, and no vehicle attachment, so a proxy with ground contact never starts a fall from them.

The engine's own way of holding a positioned character supported, unverified, is `CPfxCharacterInstance::SetAttachedSupport` (from `UpdateContextAttached`): supported, level, with no ground body. The functions named `CPfxCharacterInstance::UpdateContextAttached` and `CPfxCharacterInstance::SetLinearVelocity` are something else, as is the one named `hknpCharacterRigidBody::setLinearVelocity`, a hash-map lookup.

## Raycast and scene-query API: `CPhysicsSystem::CastRay` and related functions

[`PlayerAimControl::UpdateDirectAim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateDirectAim) calls the general scene query on the [`PhysicsSystem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem) singleton directly, [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay), backed by Havok [`hknpWorld::castRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-castRay):

```
bool CPhysicsSystem::CastRay(CPhysicsSystem*,
                             const CRay3&        ray,        // { CVector3f start; CVector3f direction; } WORLD space, direction normalized
                             float               minFraction,// 0.0 in the aim call
                             float               maxDistance,// world-space length along direction
                             CCastRayInfoBase*   outHit,     // nullptr = boolean-only
                             CRaycastFilter*     filter,     // nullptr = default filter
                             unsigned int        flags,      // collision-filter-info word, 0 in the aim call
                             int                 layer);     // PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER in the aim call
```

The layer is [`PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem::QUERY_LAYER) in the aim call.

- Filter. The `CRaycastFilter` constructor ([`RaycastFilter::Construct`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RaycastFilter##func-Construct)) is `CRaycastFilter(ignoreMaterialMask, forceMaterialMask, IPfxInstance* ignoreInstance, const hknpWorld*)`. The masks are material pass flags ([`MaterialPassFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::MaterialPassFlags)), not layers: materials whose pass flags are in the ignore mask are not hits, and those in the force mask always are. `ignoreInstance` excludes one instance, such as the player's own proxy. Materials carry per-pass "holey" flags (foliage a bullet passes through, glass, and similar). The aim path uses the subclass `CIgnoreMaterialFlagFilter`, whose [`SetIgnoreBulletPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreBulletPass) / [`SetIgnoreRayPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreRayPass) / [`SetIgnoreCameraPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::IgnoreMaterialFlagFilter##func-SetIgnoreCameraPass) each set or clear one pass bit in the ignore mask, wrapped by `CPlayerAimControl` in `CPlayerAimRaycastFilterCacheFirstHoleyHitAndMinDistanceHit`, which records both the first holey hit and the nearest solid hit.
- Result. `CCastRayInfo : CCastRayInfoBase` ([`CastRayInfoBase::Reset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfoBase##func-Reset)) carries the hit position, normal, and fraction, the hit body and instance, [`CastRayInfo::GetHitMaterial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfo##func-GetHitMaterial) → `EGameMaterialId`, and [`CastRayInfoBase::DidHitBulletPassMaterial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::CastRayInfoBase##func-DidHitBulletPassMaterial). `GetHitNameHash` is [`RagdollProxyPhantom::GetHitNameHash`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RagdollProxyPhantom##func-GetHitNameHash), which takes a `CCastRayInfoBase` and returns the name hash of the ragdoll part hit, or 0 off the ragdoll's proxy phantom.
- Thread and phase. These are sim-phase queries against the live world. `UpdateDirectAim` runs in the aim update on the sim thread ([`PlayerAimControl::UpdatePreSim`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdatePreSim) / [`UpdateAllTargets`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##func-UpdateAllTargets)); the engine does not call them during the solve or from a render worker.

The simpler `CPhysicsSystem` variants, all in world space:

- [`CastRaySimple`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRaySimple)`(const CVector3f& start, const CVector3f& end, unsigned int flags)` takes no hit info and no filter, hardcodes `QUERY_LAYER`, and returns true when the segment is blocked. Without a filter, a segment through the player misses the proxy capsule but can hit the player's own ragdoll and hit-proxy bodies, which the player aim's filter ([`PlayerAimRaycastFilter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimRaycastFilter)) rejects along with every other physics instance of the objects it ignores.
- [`CastRayTerrain`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRayTerrain) (terrain only) and [`CastRayWaterSurface`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRayWaterSurface) (the water plane).
- Shape casts: [`CastSphere`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastSphere)`(const CVector3f& start, const CVector3f& end, float radius, float, hknpCollisionQueryCollector&, unsigned int, float)`, [`CastShape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastShape) (an arbitrary `hknpShape*` with a rotation), and the free function [`SweptSphereCast`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/physics##func-SweptSphereCast), which fills a `CCastResult`. The sphere cast's engine consumer `CMagnetWeaponComponent::CastSphereAgainstStatic` is not located: the IDB function with that name has a different body.

## Teleport writers and teleport signals

Discrete relocation goes through the direct world-matrix writers above, the `CTeleport` object ([`Teleport::SetTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::Teleport##func-SetTransform), `GetTransform`/`NeedsUpdate`, event-driven), the game world's teleport functions, and the animation warp tasks (`NPhysicalAnchorWarpTask`, `NStateTask_MovementHeightWarpTask`). All bypass collision and move the character instantaneously.

- Fast travel and mission warps. [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayer`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayer) ([`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##func-TeleportPlayerImpl)) queues a deferred request that the teleport state machine in [`UpdateTeleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/game_world##func-UpdateTeleport) applies over the following ticks, never touching the character directly. [`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstant`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-TeleportPlayerInstant) ([`GameWorld::TeleportPlayerInstantImpl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##func-TeleportPlayerInstantImpl)) moves synchronously: it re-bases the character through `CGameObject::InitTransform`, the virtual at [`Character::GAME_OBJECT_INIT_TRANSFORM_SLOT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character::GAME_OBJECT_INIT_TRANSFORM_SLOT) of the `CGameObject` subobject at [`Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character::GAME_OBJECT_BASE_OFFSET), calls [`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState), sets [`AbilityFlags::m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AbilityFlags##flag-m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled) in [`Character::m_AbilityFlags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_AbilityFlags), and re-bases the camera through [`GameCameraManager::ResetCamera`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-ResetCamera), bracketed by `NEvent::CPostEvent::PostMsg("game_teleporting_initiated" / "…_completed")`.
- Scripted teleport objects: [`Teleport::Teleport`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::Teleport##func-Teleport), which issues `GameWorld::TeleportPlayer` to the object's transform, `UpdateTeleport`, and [`NStateTask_InputVehicleExitTask_TeleportUpwards`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/vehicle::vehicle##func-NStateTask_InputVehicleExitTask_TeleportUpwards).
- Vehicle: [`Vehicle::TeleportVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-TeleportVehicle); the seated character moves with the vehicle through the attach.
- Orientation and warps: `Character::SetOrientation` and the animation warp tasks.

Two candidates do not detect teleports. `m_NumFramesSinceTeleport` is incremented each tick by `CCharacter::DebugVerifyCharacter`, which runs unconditionally, and no writer resets it (an apparent reset is a local-variable fallback inside a warning-log call), so it counts frames since spawn. The `m_IsStuntAutoExitEnabled` bit controls whether the character leaves stunt positions automatically, and [`Character::SetStuntAutoExitEnabled`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetStuntAutoExitEnabled) writes it too.

The verified signals:

- [`GameWorld::IsTeleporting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game_world::GameWorld##vfunc-IsTeleporting) reports whether a deferred `TeleportPlayer` request is still being serviced. Fast travel, mission warps, and the `CTeleport` objects all enter through `TeleportPlayer` or `TeleportPlayerInstant`.
- The `game_teleporting_initiated` / `…_completed` events bracket `TeleportPlayerInstant`, a string-keyed choke point for the scripted warps.
- A position discontinuity, a per-tick translation delta of `m_WorldMatrixT1` well above wingsuit speed times the tick length, catches any writer, including the vehicle teleport and the warp tasks, which do not go through the game world.

## Seat pose: `m_AttachBone` and `m_AttachOffset` on the character

In a vehicle the character is attached: `CCharacter::m_Attachable` (`CAttachable`), `m_attachType` (`CCharacter::AttachType`, not `NONE` while attached), and `m_attachedObject` hold the parent binding, set by the [`Character::AttachTo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##vfunc-AttachTo) virtual. Its transform is then parented to the seat rather than driven by the proxy. The in-vehicle predicates are in [state-signals.md](state-signals.md#the-state-machine-hash-predicates-vehicle-riding-reeled-in-hang) and [its vehicle class section](state-signals.md#vehicle-presence-and-class).

The vehicle's `CSeat` stores no pose: it is interaction-graph metadata only (`m_GraphNode`, `m_DoorSlot`, `m_WindowPartInstance`, and `m_PlayerUsable`). The character defines the pose with `CCharacter::m_AttachBone` (`NBone::ESafeBoneIndex`, a safe bone or socket on the vehicle model) and `CCharacter::m_AttachOffset` (`CMatrix4f`, the character's transform relative to it): character world = vehicle.SafeBoneWorld(`m_AttachBone`) · `m_AttachOffset`. The Y-aligned attach entry, [`Character::SetYAlignedAttachTo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetYAlignedAttachTo), computes a yaw-only alignment from the target frame and calls `AttachTo`. `AttachToWithCurrentOffset`, which preserves the live offset, is not located: the IDB function with that name has a different body.

The whole skeleton rides the vehicle through the attach, so the character's head and eye bones give the seated eye position; no vehicle-side seat transform exists. The in-vehicle camera is a third-person chase rig (`SGenericVehicleCamera`: `m_CameraPosition`, `m_RotationPoint`, and `m_LookAtPoint` relative to the vehicle body, plus spring, lag, and FOV tuning) with no seat-eye anchor.
