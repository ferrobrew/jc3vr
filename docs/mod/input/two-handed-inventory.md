# The two-handed inventory

The local player's two hands are independent. Each holds its own weapon or object, items pass between the hands, weapons dock on the body's sockets and are drawn from them, and the player carries what the body holds rather than what the game's slots allow. Nothing docks, draws, or switches by itself: the player does everything with their own hands, even where that adds friction. The table described here acts under the controller scheme; the [grapple snatch](grapple-snatch.md) is built on top of it, and the hand-facing mechanics (the attach bones, the sockets' volumes, the draw and dock, the swap, letting go, and the release motion) are in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md).

The code is the loadout table in `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/loadout/` and the game's side of it in `crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/inventory/`, with the per-hand tick and the armed weapons in `weapons/hold/{tick,armed}/` and the pistols in `weapons/dual_wield/`.

## Why the game's inventory is replaced

The game wields exactly one weapon by construction ([engine: weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md), [engine: item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md)). Its slots are a fixed count, `Character::GetWieldingWeapon` returns the selected slot's weapon, and nearly every consumer reads that one weapon: the fire tick, the reload paths, the track messages, the animation graph, aim, the HUD, the AI's threat rating, the camera, the vehicles' and traversal's holstering, the saves, and the missions. Workarounds that keep that model (a second weapon fired beside the wielded one, a carry set beside the slots, takes that reshuffle the slots after the game's pickup) each collide with the others: a take with the free hand goes through the game's pickup, whose equip takes the other hand's weapon away, and a weapon beside the wielded one lacks what the wielded one has.

So the mod owns weapon handling for the local player. The game keeps three jobs: owning the weapon objects (their spawn, despawn, and references), simulating the weapons themselves (projectiles, effects, damage), and holding the ammunition reserves. Everything else that reads the wielded weapon is replaced, patched to work per weapon, or fed with the primary hand's weapon.

## The model

One table holds every item the player has, each in exactly one place: the left hand, the right hand, a socket of the body, or the world near the player. Items are weapons, the loose props a hand carries, and grenades and C4 charges while the table has them. Both hands are the same type: a gun in either hand has its own trigger, aim, recoil, reload, and ammunition counter.

The primary hand is the dominant hand when it holds a weapon, otherwise the other hand when it does, otherwise none. It follows from the hands and is never stored or set on its own. The primary hand's weapon is the one the game wields, for the consumers the mod feeds rather than patches.

The grapple hand is a hand like the other; the grapple is a device on the wrist, not an item. A hand holding anything is not pinned to the line while hanging from it (`hands::tiers`), since that would move its item without the player. A gun in the grapple hand takes its trigger (the retract moves to the stick click), a grenade takes it only for a press that can pull its pin, and a prop or a charge leaves it to the tethers.

### The places and their invariants

There are four sockets (`grab::socket::Socket`): each hip and each shoulder. The shoulders are where the game docks its long guns across the back, never mirrored for handedness ([`WeaponBase::UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose)): the two-handed and heavy slots' weapons on [`BACK_ATTACH`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-BACK_ATTACH), on the right, and the special slot's on [`BACK_ATTACH_2`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-BACK_ATTACH_2), on the left. So the right shoulder is the two-handed and heavy slots' own socket and the left shoulder the special slot's, whatever the dominant hand. The size classes are, smallest first, the pistol, the submachine gun, the long gun, and the heavy weapon; a hip takes a submachine gun or smaller, and a shoulder takes everything. Which sockets the body has is the loadout's `BodyLayout`, which the core can shrink for tests; the live table has all four.

The table's types carry the invariants. An item is a value that cannot be copied, owned by exactly one container. Each hand and socket holds at most one. A socket's item can only be made through a check that it fits. No two live items share an object. A prop or a charge let go of leaves the table, since only a weapon or a safe grenade can lie in the table's world.

Where a weapon goes when the player does not choose (a give with the hands full, a lost weapon's return, a respawn, a restore) is the socket the game docks its slot on, then the snuggest socket that fits it, the dominant side first (`BodyLayout::preference`).

A weapon a hand lets go of stays where it falls, kept from the despawn while the player is near, and returns to the body only when it is lost rather than dropped. A weapon on the body that no slot holds is kept alive by the mod's carry set. Both are in [inventory-world-and-carry.md](inventory-world-and-carry.md).

## The architecture

The table is the one source of truth for the player's items, and nothing else holds any of that state.

- The core is pure: the table, its places, and its transitions, tested without the game.
- The hooks are thin adapters. A detour decides nothing: it turns what the game did (a pickup, a give, a removal, a store, a cache) into an event for the table, or reads the table's published snapshot. One reconcile per tick makes the game match the table: the slots, the wielded weapon, the armed weapons, and their placement and visibility.
- The readouts are one: the debug UI's inventory view ([inventory-debug-ui.md](inventory-debug-ui.md)) shows the whole table beside the game's slots, and the log records every transition with its cause, so a bug is either the table being wrong or the reconcile not applying it.

What each hand does to the object it holds (the armed weapon's bones and user, the slide between a socket and the hand, the grip watch, the pickup's joint, the throw's stage, which hand expects the next wielded weapon) is per-hand runtime state beside the table, not an item's place.

## The core

An item is a weapon (its spawn tag's hash, its slot, and its size class), a prop, a grenade, or a C4 charge. Its identity is the table's own, assigned when it enters and never reused in a session. Its binding is a live object address; a slot weapon waiting for its new object across a spawn reset, or a restored weapon waiting for its spawn; or, for a safe grenade, an item lease that is not an object address ([Props and explosives in the table](#props-and-explosives-in-the-table)).

Each transition applies whole and returns its changes (an item entered, moved, left, was bound to a new object, or lost its object), each with its cause for the log, or is refused with a typed error and leaves the table as it was (`loadout::transition`):

- the player's moves: a take into an empty hand (each source bringing only its kinds, and a weapon lying in the world keeping its identity when taken again), a dock on a free socket that fits, a draw into an empty hand, a hand-over to an empty other hand, and a let-go, recorded as at an ordinary pace or at speed;
- a loss of a weapon lying in the world ([Letting go](inventory-world-and-carry.md#letting-go));
- the game's moves: a give, to an empty hand (the dominant first), else a free socket that fits it, else the player's feet; a removal of one item, or of every weapon on the body, the hands included, but not those lying in the world, since a mission takes what the player holds; and a drop, after which a weapon lies in the world as one the player dropped and anything else leaves;
- a weapon the mod spawned to stand for one the player has (a sidecar weapon, a split pair's twin), onto its recorded socket when free and fitting, otherwise by the preference, and a restored item's object spawned.

Hooks and hands send one event per thing that happened (`loadout::event`); the fold applies each whole or refuses it, and for a store or a cache returns the slots and the sidecar record the game should be given before it goes on.

The projection (`loadout::projection`) says what each weapon slot holds: one weapon of that slot from the body, taken from the primary hand, then the other hand, then the socket the game docks the slot on, then the other sockets, the oldest first among equals. Only a weapon bound to a live object goes into a slot, never one in the world, and the primary hand's weapon is selected. The heavy slot stays empty, in play and for a store or a cache alike ([Heavy weapons](#heavy-weapons)). For a store or a cache the sidecar record lists every weapon on the body with its place and its slot, or none for one beyond the slots. The consumables' slots stay the game's.

## The live table

The live table is the one `Loadout` of the local player's items (`loadout::live`), behind one lock, and the only writer of where the items are. It takes the weapons of the slots the inventory tracks (the pistols, the two-handed, the special, and the heavy weapons); the grenade, the planted explosive, the beacon, and the sun gun stay the game's. The detours that fold events under the lock make no engine call and take no other lock but the published hands' and places' leaf locks.

It publishes each hand's weapon as one object address per hand, which the placement detours compare before any lock, what each hand holds of any kind (`live::held_kind`), and the place of every item on the body (`loadout::live::published`), after every fold that can move an item: at the end of the input tick, and at once for the moves the hands make between ticks. Every gun in a hand rides its hand's attach bones ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md)), and the carry set places each weapon beyond the slots where the table has it.

The table leads the hands. A weapon the game wields lands in a hand of the table's (`loadout::live::expect`): the hand a take left expecting it, for up to `loadout.expect_s`, or for the game's own draw (the wheel, a switch) the dominant hand when it is empty, otherwise the other. With both hands full it is put away again. A move the table already shows sends nothing.

What feeds it:

- On its first input tick it starts from the slots, as a load does.
- The hand moves are sent at once by the calls that make them (a draw, a dock, a take, a hand-over, a let-go). The input tick sees the game's own moves: a weapon the table has in a hand that the game no longer wields and that is neither armed there nor carried (the game holstered or dropped it) docks on its slot's socket, or is let go of when it left the slots; then the wielded weapon lands in a hand when the table has it in none.
- The props and primed explosives the holding subsystems have in the hands ([Props and explosives in the table](#props-and-explosives-in-the-table)).
- What became of each of the table's weapons that no slot, the carry set, or a hand keeps ([The weapons in the world](inventory-world-and-carry.md#the-weapons-in-the-world)): one lying in the world that the table has on the body is a game's drop, one another character holds or whose object is gone is a removal, and one the pins forget leaves.
- The spawn reset, the store, the caches and their give-backs, and the weapons the sidecar restores or a split pair adds ([inventory-persistence.md](inventory-persistence.md)).

What a hand holds from every source, not only the table (the table's item, a wielded or armed gun, a grenade, the C4 throw's charge, the detonator, the reload's round, the gun it braces, and its fire input), is one record per hand (`loadout::contents`), published once at the end of the weapon handling's tick and sampled at the call by readers between ticks (`weapons::contents::now`). A hand is free when it holds none of those things (the brace and the fire input are not held), and every target of the tick (the sockets, the pouches, the placed charges, the guns in the world, the hand-over, and the foregrip) offers itself only to a free hand. The reload alone treats a hand holding its own round as free, since it carries that round on.

## The slots as a projection

The game's slots are made to match the table each input tick (`weapons::inventory::reconcile`), after the table's feed, and at once after the hands move a weapon:

- Each tracked slot holds the weapon the projection puts there. A weapon that leaves its slot stays alive in the carry set and goes out through [`Inventory::RemoveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-RemoveWeapon), unequipped first when wielded; one that comes in goes in through [`Inventory::AddWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddWeapon) without an equip. A weapon in a slot that the table does not have on the body (a mission's give before a hand takes it) is left there, since the game's word on ownership is final.
- The primary hand's weapon is wielded. Moving the primary unequips the old weapon and equips the new one ([`WeaponBase::OnUnequip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnUnequip), then `OnEquip`) with no act, which is what the draw clip's own equip message does, after aborting a draw, holster, or switch clip still playing ([`EventIdSymbolTable::ACT_ABORT_WEAPON_PARTIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::symbol_table::EventIdSymbolTable)), whose messages equip and unequip by slot when they fire. It waits while the inventory's permissions refuse a draw or a holster ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsDrawing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsDrawing), [`GetAnimationAllowsHolstering`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsHolstering)). With no weapon in the hands, a weapon of the table's the game wields is unequipped; one the table does not have (a grenade, the planted explosive, a mounted gun) is left to the game.
- Every other weapon a hand holds is armed there ([The per-hand tick](#the-per-hand-tick)), and an armed weapon that left its hand, or that the game now wields, is disarmed. When the primary moves to a hand whose weapon is armed, it is disarmed and wielded in the same hand.
- A slot's weapon on a socket other than its slot's own is placed there by the mod, as a carried weapon is ([The weapons beyond the slots](inventory-world-and-carry.md#the-weapons-beyond-the-slots)).
- Before the game stores its save, and before a mission or a challenge caches the slots, the tracked slots are given the store projection; in play they already hold it, so this catches a move the hands made since the last pass.
- Before the game's own pickup puts a weapon into a taken slot, the slot's weapon leaves the slot and stays on its socket.

## The per-hand tick

Every gun in a hand fires from that hand's own trigger and aims at that hand's own point (`weapons::hold::tick`). The game's fire path keys everything on the weapon's user rather than its state or the selected slot: the heat, the gunshot event the AI hears, the bullets' owner and the player's bullet flag, the muzzle flash and sound, the recoil, the spread, the rumble, and the camera shake all follow the user, and the holder gives the bullets the bodies they ignore. So a gun the game does not wield fires as the player's from the holstered state ([engine: weapon-firing.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-firing.md)).

A weapon the table holds in a hand that the game does not wield is armed there (`weapons::hold::armed`). It stays holstered, so the game's inventory, selection, HUD, and saves see it as holstered and its bodies stay out of the world. Its guns' holstered attach-bone entries name the hand's bone, its holstered scale ([`WeaponData::m_HolsteredScaleValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_HolsteredScaleValue)) is one, and the player uses it ([`WeaponBase::StartUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-StartUsing)); disarming restores the entries and the scale and stops using it ([`WeaponBase::StopUsing`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-StopUsing)). Its holder stays the player. The rigid grip, the gun aim, the per-shot seam, the finger poses, and the render-time late update treat it as held.

- After the inventory's own weapon updates ([`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Parallel) and `_Serial`), the mod updates each armed weapon through its [`UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Parallel) and `_Serial` virtuals with its hand's fire input and aim point. The shooting permissions ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsShooting`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsShooting)) gate only the input the game's own fan-out passes down, so the mod checks them for its own, with the airborne waiver of [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md), as it does the true reload's hold on the gun.
- The wielded weapon is still updated by the game's fan-out, but with its hand's fire input in place of the game's own (substituted in the gun aim's per-weapon detour), so both hands fire the same way. Updating it a second time from the mod would advance its timers twice, and the fan-out's choice of what to update (the mounted gun, the boost wingsuit's guns, the grenade) stays the game's. The game's pistol pair, while the table does not run, fires from the game's own input, which alternates its guns.
- Each gun's ammunition check asks the character to reload the wielded weapon when its own magazine runs low, and a gun that does not reload by magazine cancels the character's reload every tick. Inside the local player's checks both requests are refused (`weapons::empty`, on the detours of [`Character::StartReloadingWieldingWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-StartReloadingWieldingWeapon) and [`Character::CancelReload`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-CancelReload)), so one hand's gun never reloads or interrupts the other's. The reload input's own start still reaches the game. The same module suppresses the game's automatic switch away from an empty weapon.
- The game forwards the player's aim mode to the selected weapon alone, so each armed gun takes the wielded weapon's aim mode, which is the player's; under the controller scheme, which refuses precision aim, that is normal aim.
- A weapon's use hint is keyed by hash in the camera's hint table, added at its start of use only when absent and removed at its stop only when present ([`GameCameraManager::HasHint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/camera::game_camera_manager::GameCameraManager##func-HasHint)), so two guns of one type share one reference and the first to stop takes it away. The next input tick puts the hint back while a gun still in use carries it.

The true reload acts on each hand's gun at once, each with its own magazine, chamber, and action, and its record keyed by the table's item, so a weapon keeps its chamber across the spawn reset's re-key and a hand-over ([reload.md](reload.md#each-hands-gun)). One reserve per ammunition type is shared by both hands. The mod's ammunition counter stands above every hand holding a gun, and the game's weapon readout, which shows the primary alone, is not drawn ([hud.md](../hud.md)).

The body never takes the combat stance: the animation graph's weapon-wielded condition reads false for the local player's base layer, so the locomotion keeps its relaxed families whatever the hands hold, and the arms are the mod's IK ([body/procedural-body.md](../body/procedural-body.md)). The game has no per-hand hold layer; its hand poses are one clip per weapon class covering both hands, so the fingers take the grip poses ([grip-poses.md](grip-poses.md)).

## The gun in either hand

The subsystems that act on a gun in a hand key it by the hand, not by the game's wielded weapon. Each input tick reads the gun in each hand (`weapons::engine::in_hands`): the wielded weapon in the hand its first gun rides, and an armed weapon in its own. A long gun armed in a hand is a long gun by its slot, since it stays holstered, and each hand's long gun is measured on its own wrist.

- The weight (`weapons::heft`): each hand's gun has its own weight, spring, and freeze, so a rifle in the off-hand beside a pistol sags, trails, and kicks as a rifle held in one hand. A round carried to the reload freezes the gun in the other hand, and a hand closing on a foregrip freezes the gun it closes on.
- The recoil (`weapons::recoil`): each hand has its own spring, kicked by its own gun's shots, and the one-handed kick scale is that gun's weight.
- The wall collision (`weapons::collide`): each hand's long gun has its own probe, wall, and push.
- The sights (`weapons::ads`) and the scope (`weapons::scope`): each hand's long gun is solved for the eye and each scoped gun's lens placed, but the aiming eye and the drawn lens are single, so they act on the gun raised to the eye (`ads::raised`, `scope::chosen`), picked each frame by how far each gun's pose takes hold of the eye and shoulder rather than by which gun the game wields. Each gun's hold fades in as it comes up, so the pick passes from one gun to the other without state of its own.
- The regions (`weapons::regions`): a lone hand always holds its gun by the grip, so a long gun drawn by its barrel or picked up by its stock slides into the hand by its grip, wielded or armed. Each hand has its own slide.
- The foregrip, a touch along the gun, and the hand-over (`weapons::foregrip`, `weapons::hold::hand_over`) take the other hand, so they act on the gun held in one hand while the other is free (`weapons::engine::lone_gun`). With a gun in each hand neither hand is free, so there is at most one such gun and one foregrip hold. Keying by the free hand rather than the wielded weapon keeps the rule through the reconcile's passing state, where a gun is armed in the only hand holding one until the next reconcile wields it.

The per-hand state is the hand's, not the item's: a gun handed over or drawn again starts at rest in its new hand, and a hand that takes another gun drops the last one's spring. The gun bodies joined to the hand bodies are in [physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md).

## The grabs against the table

Every grab at the body is one decision against the table's snapshot (`loadout::grab`):

- An empty hand's grab of a socket that holds an item draws it: the table takes the draw at once, the reconcile wields the weapon when the hand is the primary and arms it otherwise, and the gun slides off the socket into the hand. Both hands draw, dock, and let go at once, each with its own slide and grip watch.
- A hand holding a weapon docks it on a free socket that fits it, by opening its grip over the socket ([grip.md](grip.md)) or by a grab there; the table takes the dock when the slide ends. Opening the grip over a taken socket the weapon fits swaps the two, the docked weapon falling clear. Opening it within a free socket's attraction sends the gun travelling onto it. The volumes, the attraction, and the swap are in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md).
- Opening the grip anywhere else lets the weapon go into the world with the hand's motion. The game's pistol pair, one object of two guns that cannot lie in the world, goes onto the first free socket the body prefers for it instead, or stays in the hand with none free.
- A socket that does not take the held weapon (taken without a swap, or too small) is marked for that hand in the refusal colour, so the hand knows before it opens (`loadout::grab::refusing`).
- A draw refused by the animation and a dock of the wielded weapon refused by it do nothing, rather than leave the table and the game disagreeing until the next tick.

Nothing selects a slot, clicks the slot-select actions, or asks the game to holster: the table takes the move and the reconcile does the rest. With `controllers.weapons.hold.grip_to_hold` off, a gun stays in the hand until a grab of a socket docks it. While the table does not run, no socket volumes are placed and the game's own selection draws and puts away its one weapon.

## The pistols as plain items

A pistol is an item like any other weapon. A pair the game hands over is split into two single pistols while the table runs (`weapons::dual_wield::single`): the slot keeps one, and the twin is spawned and restored onto the hip the slot's pistol does not take, or the socket the table prefers when that hip is taken. A pair split while in a hand keeps a pistol in each hand when the other hand is empty. The game docks the pistol slot's weapon on the right hip; on the left hip the mod places it. A pistol on a hip sits in the hip's holster with no offset, unless the slot holds the game's pair, whose two pistols take both holsters. The split, the half magazines, and the respawn of a lone pistol's twin are in [physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md).

## Props and explosives in the table

Everything a hand holds is in the table. Each input tick the table is fed what the holding subsystems have in each hand (`loadout::live::HeldOthers`): the loose object the physical hands carry, a primed or caught grenade, and the C4 throw's primed charge. A hand that took one takes it, and a hand whose prop is let go of, lost, or torn away, or whose grenade or charge was thrown or pressed on, lets it go, which takes it out of the table; a grenade that went off in the hand is removed as gone. The feed is per tick rather than per change because the throws' stages move on the weapons' updates on the simulation's threads, so a grenade primed and thrown between two ticks never enters the table. A prop both hands hold is the item of the hand that took it first. A grip on something hinged, a door, or a character is the world's, held by the physical hands alone ([physical-hands.md](physical-hands.md)).

A grenade with its pin in is a safe item (`loadout::safe_items`, moved through `live::admit_safe_item` and its siblings by `weapons::grenade::safe`): it is bound to an item lease rather than an object address, the grenade controller owns its inert actor, and the table owns its place. It can be in a hand, holstered on a hip, or lying in the world as dropped. Each move is checked against the caller's copied view and the item's movement revision, so a stale or occupied move is refused whole; a reset refuses admissions and moves but lets a retirement through, so the actor's owner can clean up. A grenade whose pin is out is a live grenade the table no longer owns ([grenades.md](grenades.md)).

What a hand holds is then one condition (`live::held_kind`): a hand holding a prop, a grenade, or a charge draws nothing off a socket, takes nothing from the pouches or a source, is not offered the other hand's gun, and is not pinned to the grapple line. Under the gamepad scheme the hands hold no props and nothing enters the table.

## The takes

Every take of a weapon from the world lands in the table and then in a hand, and the game's equip never runs, so a take never touches the other hand. Where it goes is one decision (`loadout::take`): the grabbing hand when it is free, or for the game's use-item pickup, which no hand grabs, a free hand, the dominant first. A hand holding something takes nothing, and with both hands full the use-item pickup takes nothing and leaves the weapon where it lies: the player chooses the hand by freeing one.

A take through the table (`weapons::hold::pickup::take_by_table`) makes the weapon the player's without the game's pickup ([`WeaponBase::OnPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnPickup)), credits its stored rounds to the reserve as the game's pickup does ([`Inventory::AddAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddAmmo)), and has the carry set keep it. The reconcile then wields it when its hand is the primary and arms it otherwise. A weapon whose slot is taken goes into the table beside the slot's weapon, and the projection decides which of them the slot holds. The game's pickup line is not played.

The sources ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md) has how a hand reaches each):

- the ground: the physical hands' direct pickup and the weapon handling's take, the same item again for a weapon the table has lying in the world;
- racks and lockers (`weapons::hold::rack`): a rack's weapon after the game's pickup start, and a locker's weapon, offered lying in the world; a rack with unlimited stock gives a copy made inside the rack-member fence, staged at the rack and taken as the rack's own weapon would be (`weapons::hold::rack::copy`, [paired-rack-weapons.md](paired-rack-weapons.md));
- the characters' hands (`weapons::hold::steal`), the hand and the route checked before the character lets go, so a refused take leaves the gun in its hands;
- the game's use-item pickup (`weapons::hold::use_item`): its completion ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem)) may run wherever the character's track messages are handled, so the detour keeps the weapon alive and queues it, and the next input tick takes it into a free hand or puts it back with both hands full. A swimming character's pickup drops the weapon in the picked weapon's slot first ([`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling)), so while the player swims and presses use, the slot's weapon is taken out of the slot before the game's handling runs and stays where the table has it.

The game's own pickup takes the weapon, equipping it and putting the wielded weapon away, only where the table cannot hold it in a hand of its own: while the table does not run, and for the embedded power core, whose pickup spawns and equips another weapon.

### The game's gives

The game's gives take one chain ([engine: weapon-gives-and-restores.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-gives-and-restores.md#the-give)): [`Inventory::GivePlayerWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GivePlayerWeapon) writes the slot's respawn-table entry, and the spawned weapon arrives through [`Inventory::WeaponSpawned`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-WeaponSpawned), which would equip it and replace the slot's occupant. The give's detour (`weapons::inventory::gives`) records each give to the local player's tracked slots while the table places gives (it runs, no reset is under way, and no mission holds the player's weapons). The arrival's detour, which runs wherever the spawn system calls back, keeps a matching weapon and queues it; the next input tick's game stage hands it over on the game thread, with the slot's occupant first taken out onto the body and the respawn table's equipped slot ([`Inventory::m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_WeaponToSpawnEquippedWeapon)) turned to none for the call, so the weapon arrives in its slot unequipped. The table then places it by the give rule. A give made inside a rack-member request is staged at its rack instead. A give the reset owns, or one made while a mission holds the player's weapons, is left to the game and bound later by the reset's completion or the give-back ([inventory-persistence.md](inventory-persistence.md)). 

### Ammunition pickups

The ammunition auto-pickup credits a weapon lying in the world's stored rounds while the player's inventory holds a weapon of its ammunition type ([engine: item-pickup.md](../../engine/gameplay/item-pickup.md#picking-up-a-weapon)), and its check reads the slots alone. The check's detour (`weapons::inventory::ammo`, on [`Inventory::HasWeaponWithAmmoType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-HasWeaponWithAmmoType)) also answers yes when a weapon the table has in a hand or on a socket is of that type; its one caller credits the rounds itself. The types are published as a mask at the end of each input tick and read without a lock, since the credit runs wherever the pickup interaction activates.

A locker refills through the player's `ply.ammo.givemax` event, answered with [`Inventory::GiveMaxAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GiveMaxAmmo), which sets every reserve to its cap directly. What makes a locker send the event is in the game's data, not its code, so every rise of the local player's reserve is logged at info level, naming the setter, the refill, or neither.

## Traversal

Traversal leaves the hands and the body as they are (`weapons::inventory::traversal`). The base layer's weapon tasks run on entering their states ([engine: animation-graph.md](../../engine/character/animation-graph.md#the-base-layers-weapon-tasks) lists which states own each): the unequip task ([`NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::NWeaponUnequipTask_OnEnter)), the hide-in-vehicle task ([`NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::NWeaponHideInVehicleTask_OnEnter)), the stop-use task ([`NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::NStopUsingWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)), and the heavy-weapon drop ([`NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::NDropWeaponStateTask_OnEnter)). Each entry is refused for the local player while the table runs, the drop's only while the heavy slot holds no weapon the game wields. The hide task's exit still runs, and puts back only what its entry hid. Every unequip task is refused, the mounted-gun entries included: a mounted gun's use unequips the wielded weapon through its own start of use, which the table takes for a holster, so the player's weapon docks while the hands man the gun.

The forced neutral state ([`Character::ForceNeutralState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-ForceNeutralState), on a teleport's start, a revive, a cutscene's setup, and the vehicle exit's upward teleport) unequips the wielded weapon. Its detour tells the table which weapon it took (`live::neutral_forced`), and the next input tick keeps that weapon in its hand rather than taking the unequip for a holster; the reconcile then wields it again, or arms it while the animation refuses a draw.

The inventory's weapon update skips the wielded weapon in every seat but the motorcycle's, and while the boost wingsuit flies ([`Character::HasBoostWingsuit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-HasBoostWingsuit)). There the reconcile wields nothing: it unequips the wielded weapon without waiting for the holster permission, since the game's own task would have, and every gun in a hand is armed and fired by the per-hand tick. A draw there does not wait for the draw permission either, which a car's seats withhold. The motorcycle's seat wields the gun and keeps the permission. Off the seat, the primary's weapon is wielded again.

The held guns act in every control mode but the UI (`weapons::capabilities::Capabilities`): they fire, are let go, handed over, braced, docked, and reloaded in seats, while swimming, and in the wingsuit as on the ground. The pouches stay in reach in seats but not while swimming or in the wingsuit, and nothing in the world (characters' hands, racks, lockers) can be taken from a seat, the water, or the wingsuit. A hand holding anything of the table's takes no new vehicle control, cockpit button, mounted gun's handle, or parachute handle, and keeps one it already holds ([vehicle-weapons.md](vehicle-weapons.md), [steering-wheel.md](steering-wheel.md)). In the wingsuit the arms steer and gesture whatever the hands hold ([flight.md](flight.md), [wingsuit-gestures.md](wingsuit-gestures.md)).

## Heavy weapons

A heavy weapon is kept beyond the slots like every other weapon beyond them, and is never wielded from the heavy slot. Its unequip drops whatever the heavy slot holds rather than holstering it, and the base layer's drop task, the forced neutral state, and the holster input's heavy drop all drop it through the slot ([engine: weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#dropping)). With the slot empty none of them reaches it, and the drop task is refused besides. The save stores the heavy slot's type as none and the graph script's give-back skips the heavy slot, so the store and the caches keep it out of the slot as well and record it where it survives, beyond the slots.

- A take of a heavy weapon goes through the table from every source a gun does, and a heavy give goes by the give rule.
- In a hand it is armed and fired by the per-hand tick. The primary's heavy weapon is not wielded, since no slot holds it, so the game's wielded-weapon consumers follow the other hand's weapon when it has one, and none otherwise.
- It has no magazine limit and fires straight from the reserve, as in the game. An armed weapon that does have one and whose reload is the game's is refilled through the game's round move ([reload.md](reload.md#armed-weapons-left-to-the-game)).
- It gets a gun body of the heavy class in the hand, since the carry set holsters it and so takes its own body out of the world ([physical-guns.md](physical-guns.md)).
- It docks on either shoulder.
- A heavy weapon of the table's found in the heavy slot (the game's own pickup while the table was stopped, a restore) is taken out into the carry set. One the game wields from there is left to the game, since its unequip would drop it, and is the one heavy weapon the drop task still drops.

It stays in its hand through a grapple, a throw, a glide, a swim, a hand climb, and a vehicle's entry, since none of the game's traversal paths moves a weapon beyond the slots. A spent single-use launcher, which the game drops through the heavy slot, stays where the table has it until the player lets go; that the launcher's drop goes through the slot alone is unverified.

## Caches, resets, saves, and the scheme switch

The game takes the player's weapons away and gives them back at the mission checkpoints, the missions' graph-script caches, and the challenges, replaces them at every spawn reset, and saves only the slots. The table snapshots and restores itself at each, and a sidecar file carries the weapons beyond the slots across a save and a load. Switching to the gamepad scheme projects the table back into the slots and keeps the weapons beyond them stashed until it runs again. All of this is in [inventory-persistence.md](inventory-persistence.md).

## Tuning and settings

The inventory has no settings of its own. The pins, the lost-weapon rules, the sockets' offsets, the docked guns' view, the restore's and the gives' waits, and the table's waits are the `pins`, `lost`, `sockets`, `docked_view`, `restore`, and `loadout` sections of `crates/jc3vr-weapons/tuning/inventory.toml` ([tuning.md](../tuning.md)). The hands' settings are `controllers.weapons.hold` and the sockets' `controllers.weapons.holsters` ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md)).
