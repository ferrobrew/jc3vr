# The weapon-agnostic body

On foot under the controllers, Rico's body plays its unarmed animation whatever he holds, and the controllers alone place the arms and the guns. The game's weapon body animation breaks the match with the player's body (dual SMGs put him in a foot-forward stance, and a two-handed gun keeps his left hand on the weapon), so every weapon's body animation is left out rather than tuned per weapon. The engine side is in [animation-layers.md](../../engine/character/animation-layers.md) and [animation-graph.md, "The on-foot idle"](../../engine/character/animation-graph.md#the-on-foot-idle). The code is `controllers::body::unarmed`, with the detours in `hooks::character::modifiers`, `layers`, and `wielded`.

It applies under the controller scheme with the headset driving the view, in gameplay, while the hand tiers classify the character as on foot or in a reload, draw, or holster. The decision is made at the entry of the local player's pose-finalization pass and used by the next tick's modifier pass and pose job, so it lands a tick late; a decision older than a quarter of a second counts as not applying.

Four routes carry the weapon into the body, each cut separately:

- Clip variants. The detour on [`Character::SetCharacterModifiers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-SetCharacterModifiers) lets the weapon's modifiers reach only the upper-body action layer ([`AnimationLayer::GLOBAL_PARTIAL`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationLayer##variant-GLOBAL_PARTIAL)), so a reload or draw lasts as long as it does natively while every other layer plays unarmed clips.
- Partial and additive layers. A detour on [`Blender::UpdatePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::Blender##func-UpdatePose) leaves the upper-body action, synced partial, and additive layers out of the pose by copying the reference pose through, as the pose job does for a layer with no clips. The layers keep running, so reload events, unequips, and fire blocks still happen. The blenders come from [`AnimationController::m_BodyParts`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##field-m_BodyParts) and are published as atomics with a quarter-second expiry, because the pose job runs on worker threads and a freed blender's address must not be honoured for long.
- The off-hand grip. The off-hand grip's weight key is zeroed for both hands at the entry of [`Character::UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass), so the mod's foregrip grab ([weapon-handling.md](../input/weapon-handling.md#the-foregrip)) is the only thing that puts a hand on a two-handed weapon. Independently of the unarmed body, the same switch-off applies at that pass's entry for every hand the controllers drive (recorded per thread when their targets are queued): the weapon off-hand IK task runs inside the pass after its entry ([animation-layers.md](../../engine/character/animation-layers.md#state-tasks-inside-the-pose-finalization-pass)), so zeroing earlier would be overwritten.
- Combat families. With a weapon wielded, `C_WEAPON_WIELDED` switches the base layer's locomotion and turns to combat families whose default clips are a rifle's ([animation-graph.md](../../engine/character/animation-graph.md)). A detour on [`WeaponWieldedCondition::Evaluate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon_animation::WeaponWieldedCondition##func-Evaluate) makes the any-slot condition read false for the local player on every rule system but the upper-body actions'. The character is the machine's [`StateMachineInstance::m_UserData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::StateMachineInstance##field-m_UserData), and the single-slot condition (the planted explosive's) is left alone.

The aim families and the aim and arm IK tasks are already held off by the aim-state pin ([hands-and-aiming.md](../input/hands-and-aiming.md#the-aim-state-pin-and-the-mental-state-cuts)). The debug UI's Body/tracking > Animation and IK live status shows whether it applies and each route's state.

Unverified: that every node a weapon variant replaces has an unarmed clip, and the unarmed root motion suits play with a weapon out; that the body-part index is the layer index for Rico; that the reload, draw, holster, and switch end correctly with the action layer masked and still block firing; that dual pistols stay in both hands without the weapon clips' clear-left-hand segments; that heavy weapons read acceptably (the minigun's idle has no unarmed variant); and that no state outside the upper-body actions needs `C_WEAPON_WIELDED` true to leave.
