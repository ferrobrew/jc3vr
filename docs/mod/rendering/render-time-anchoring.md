# Render-time anchoring

The simulation ticks at 30 Hz, and the engine draws each object between its last two ticks' cached matrices by the frame's interpolation fraction. The headset's view, the hands drawn by the late update (`crates/jc3vr-body/src/controllers/hands/late/`), and the upper body the presentation layer re-poses ([presentation-layer.md](presentation-layer.md)) are placed from the latest tracking at render time instead. Anything the mod places from a tick's data and draws relative to the player must therefore be anchored to a render-time pose, or it is drawn where the player was up to a tick ago: a few centimetres at walking pace, about a metre a tick in a fast car.

## The rule

- An overlay primitive (`jc3vr_view::controllers::overlay::primitive::Anchor`) placed in the cockpit frame is anchored to the head (`Anchor::Head`, an offset in world axes from the render camera's eye point) or to a hand (`Anchor::Hand`, an offset in the hand's pose as drawn, `late::drawn_hands`). `Anchor::Ticked` is for a point measured once a tick on a moving body, such as the grapple's aim point from a vehicle, interpolated by the frame's fraction as the camera is. `Anchor::World` is for points that lie in the world: a grapple target, a hit point, a pickup on the ground.
- A model the mod holds in a hand is re-pushed at render time from the hand's render-time pose or correction, after the engine's own render update has pushed its interpolated matrix.
- A model the mod places on the body keeps its interpolation: both of its cached matrices are written on the tick, so the engine draws it between them with the body it rides.

## The eye point

The published head (`headpose::query`) follows Rico's head joint and the HMD's travel, and the camera hangs its eye point off it by the arm from the head joint to Rico's eye midpoint, ten to fifteen centimetres forward and up. `jc3vr_view::hooks::camera::eye_points` is the one place that computes it. `CockpitTransform::current` maps the cockpit head (the HMD's eye midpoint) to the current eye point, so the hands, aim rays, grab volumes, vehicle controls, and cockpit-frame markers land where the player sees them through the rendered eyes; composed from the published head instead, they would sit that arm behind and below. The late update composes the hands on both sides of the head pose pair from that side's eye point and interpolates them attached to the camera, so a hand rides the camera across the frame. While the camera is not placed from the head pose, the head anchors resolve against the camera and the hands stay composed from the eye point. The head-through-geometry probe and the recorder use the same eye point.

## Moving-frame sampling

Wrist references and render-time controller poses interpolate in the character's local frame and relative to their head transport pair, then take the same interpolated character transform as the skeleton, which keeps the arc of a turn where interpolating world positions would cut across it. The root transport supplies platform movement, so these consumers add no platform velocity of their own. The gun mesh interpolates the same way before the hand correction.

The physical hand's render offset is sampled from the current body after the simulation and frozen before the render hands are composed. Its reference is the palm the last step drove the body toward (`physics::state::acting_palms` in the physical crate, including the physical gun's support and weight target), led by the palm's velocity over the step's lead (`Target::led`). Against the palm without that lead, a free body would read as a hand ahead of its controller by up to a third of a metre in a sprint, and the late update would carry the drawn hand off the end of the arm. A raw controller reference would cancel or duplicate the support and weight. A failed body read uses the latest bounded residual, and the sampler never drives or places a body.

Gun parts follow the ownership of the held weapon, including one retained in a hand while the native state is holstered in a vehicle. A part interpolates its local transform relative to its gun and is composed beneath the gun's rendered parent, so the slide, bolt, and magazine keep their relative travel; a part placed in a reload hand follows that hand, and a weapon on a body socket gets no hand correction.

The collider view ([hand-views.md](../input/hand-views.md#the-collider-view)) refreshes its retained bodies every rendered frame and draws the current collision pose, after the engine's render update, so it compares that frame's gun mesh with the current body of the held gun. A gap there is a timing or pose observation, not the hull's fidelity, and must not be hidden by moving current collision geometry onto a delayed mesh.

## Where it applies

- Head anchors: the grip markers in the cockpit frame (the body's sockets and pouches, the vehicle's controls, the parachute's handles; [grip.md](../input/grip.md#markers)), the grab view's reach rays ([hand-views.md](../input/hand-views.md#the-grab-view)), and the body mirror's figure. The debug panel ([hud.md](../hud.md#where-the-debug-panel-lives)) is composed on eye 0 through the render-time head, or from the holding hand's aim pose.
- Hand anchors: markers on a held gun, the grab view's palm markers and line ends, the wingsuit guide, the grenade fuse ring, and the debug panel's pointer.
- World anchors: markers on what lies in the world, and the grab view's door handles, candidates, and refusals.
- Models in a hand: the held gun and its parts, re-pushed by the late update ([`WeaponBase::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateRender) and the parts' render update, detoured); and the primed grenade, a caught grenade, and the primed C4 charge (`crates/jc3vr-weapons/src/controllers/weapons/in_hand.rs`), re-pushed at the drawn hand's grip through post-call detours on [`Explosive::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-UpdateRender) and [`PlantedExplosive::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-UpdateRender). The throws still place each on the tick. A registration not refreshed each tick lapses after a little more than a tick.
- Models on the body: the weapons the VR inventory carries beyond the slots, placed on their sockets on the tick through the weapon's own placement. A weapon enters the carry set with its render interpolation skipped, so it does not streak from the slot's bone to its socket, and only [`WeaponBase::UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose), which only the slots receive, clears that skip; left set, the weapon would be drawn a tick ahead of the body. So the carry set ends each pass as the game does for the slots (`inventory::carry::end_pass`, from the detour of [`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose)), clearing [`m_SkipRenderInterpolation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_SkipRenderInterpolation). A slot's weapon, a snap between a dock and the hand, and the draw and holster slides interpolate with the body. The pouches' models ([grenades.md](../input/grenades.md#the-pouches-models)) have no cached matrices, so each is placed on the tick in the character's model space and pushed once per rendered frame interpolated with the character's world transform.

In the headset: guns carried on the body stay on their sockets at speed and in a fall; held explosives stay in the hand; markers stay on the wheel, the gun, and the body while driving; and the drawn hand covers the real hand's place standing, crouched, seated, swimming, and under the parachute, with no hand floating behind and below it.
