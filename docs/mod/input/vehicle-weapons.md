# Weapons in vehicles

Out of the driver's seat the hands fire the weapons they hold, or the mounted gun they man. The driver's built-in vehicle weapons are in [controllers.md, "Vehicles, mounted guns, and flight"](controllers.md#vehicles-mounted-guns-and-flight). This covers shooting from a passenger or stunt seat by the hand's aim, and manning a mounted gun with both hands on its handles. The code is in `payload/src/controllers/vehicles/`: `passenger.rs`, with its call in the aim update (`controllers::aim`), and `mounted.rs`, `mounted_tick.rs`, `mounted_grip.rs`, and `mounted_recoil.rs`, with the shot's call in the per-shot seam (`controllers::weapons::fire`).

## Passenger and stunt seats

In a passenger seat, and on a stunt position, the weapon in the hands is the player's wielded weapon, so it already aims and fires by the hands as on foot: each gun aims along its holding hand's sight line through the per-weapon update, the character-level aim point follows the gun hand, and the trigger fires the passenger's fire action, as does the hand's upper face button, its vehicle weapon action, which a gun in the hand takes over in every seat ([controllers-and-roomscale.md, "Land vehicles"](controllers-and-roomscale.md#land-vehicles-car-motorcycle-in-the-last-rows), [controllers.md, "Aiming"](controllers.md#aiming)). In the driver's seat the triggers are the pedals, so a gun there fires from its hand's upper face button alone. The seated body did not follow. Its upper body aims through the vehicle aim blend ([`VehicleAimBlendState::UpdateSamplers`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::VehicleAimBlendState##func-UpdateSamplers)), which turns the torso and the gun arm toward the aim control's weapon point ([`PlayerAimControl::m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos), its weapon slot), and the game sets that point from the gaze. A passenger looking ahead while pointing out of the side window twisted toward the windscreen, and the shot left the drawn gun's muzzle at an angle to it.

A gun in a hand in a seat other than the motorcycle's is not the wielded weapon: the inventory's weapon update skips the wielded weapon there ([engine: weapon-equip-and-drop.md](../../engine/gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md)), so the reconcile wields nothing in such a seat, and every gun in a hand, the primary's included, is armed in its hand and fired by the mod's per-hand tick ([two-handed-inventory.md, "Traversal"](two-handed-inventory.md#traversal)). The same holds in the boost wingsuit, whose guns the game updates in the wielded weapon's place. Leaving the seat wields the primary's again. The aim, the seat's body turn below, and the sight line are each hand's as on foot.

With `vehicles.passenger_body_by_hand` (on), after the aim control's post-simulation update, in a vehicle seat other than the driver's and off a mounted gun (in a seat by the seat grapple's test, `grapple::seat::rides_in_seat`: Rico has a vehicle and is in one of its riding states, [`Character::IsInRidingInVehicleState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsInRidingInVehicleState)), while a gun hand aims, the weapon slot of that point is set to the gun hand's aim point: the acting hand's, or the other hand's when only it holds a gun. The game copies the slot into the control parameters and the character's weapon point right after, in the post-camera update, so the blend, the weapon point, and everything that reads them follow the hand, and the body leans and points the gun where the hand does, as it does toward the gaze for the gamepad. The point is the same one the gun is handed, so the drawn gun and the shot agree. With no gun in the hands, or with the option off, the game's point stands. Off, the gun still aims by the hand; only the body follows the gaze.

## Mounted guns

A mounted gun, standalone or in a vehicle's gunner seat, aims by its handles as a gun on a pivot does ([controllers.md, "Vehicles, mounted guns, and flight"](controllers.md#vehicles-mounted-guns-and-flight)): when the hands take the gun, a reference is taken from their midpoint, the lever from the handles to the pivot, and the barrel, and from then on the barrel points from the hands' midpoint through the pivot. That part is unchanged. The barrel reaches the gun through its mount's motors, which the mounted aim drives toward that aim point, so the runtime keeps them running while the hands hold the handles and disables them only while a hand holds the gun's own body, which swings it on its joints. Disabled for the handles too, they would leave nothing to turn the gun, as the tripod minigun barely moving under the hands on the 14 November take showed. A gun the hands grip by its own body, its holder, or a handle on its holder's vehicle is driven without the use prompt: the runtime gives it a temporary shooter, fires it on the trigger, and tears it off on a sustained pull. Under the controllers a standalone mounted gun's use prompt is hidden from the moment the gun is seen, not only while the hands hold it, so it never offers the native use, a state that is hard to leave, on the approach; a vehicle's gun keeps its prompt, since the gunner seat needs the native use, and the gamepad scheme keeps every prompt (`mounted::runtime::hide_standalone_prompts`). The use prompt is refused while the gun is selected, and a native use the player already entered is stopped and taken over, keeping the hands the game placed on the handles. The runtime derives the aim from the hands after that stop, so the gun does not fire along a frozen barrel. The tear frees the gun from its mount: the weapon's own detach drops it but removes only the constraint that attaches it, and leaves the mount's horizontal and vertical hinges enabled (the game's own tear equips the gun in the same message, out of the physics world, so the hinges never act), which kept a gun torn off a tripod on it on the 14 November take. The runtime takes the attach constraint and the hinges before the detach, since the drop clears the weapon's references to them, and disables each afterwards ([`WeaponBase::m_ConstraintAttach`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_ConstraintAttach), `mounted::native::free_from_mount`); the tear's log line counts them. The gun then goes into the hand through the table, which holsters a heavy weapon the pickup leaves dropped ([physical-weapons.md](physical-weapons.md)), or, refused, falls where it is with its pickup enabled ([`WeaponBase::PickupEnable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupEnable)), which the drop skips off a mount. The game's use-item action is no fallback: it picks up the nearest pickup rather than the torn gun, and on the 14 November take its slot swap took a dead soldier's rifle and ejected the player's own weapons.

### Tearing a gun off

A sustained pull on a gun the hands hold (0.3 m past the handle's reach, at the hand's full strength, for 0.4 s; `control::Tear`) tears it off its mount (`runtime::tear_off`). The gun's own detach ([`WeaponBase::DetachMountedGun`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-DetachMountedGun)) stops its use and drops it. The hand then takes it through the loadout table, or, for a heavy gun the table cannot take from the dropped state, through the game's own use-item pickup, which carries it in the heavy slot as the game carries a torn-off tripod minigun.

A gun on its own mount comes off only where the game lets it ([`WeaponBase::m_MountedCanBeDetached`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedCanBeDetached)). The mounted weapons' entity property files set it through `mounted_can_be_detached`, and only the standalone tripod minigun (`w201_minigun_mount`) has it. That the property is what fills the field was read from the data, not traced in the code.

A vehicle's gun (the armoured jeep's M2HM `w206`, the vehicle minigun `w201_minigun`, and the Uzhas cannon `w205`, none of which have the flag) comes off the way the vehicle's explosion lets it go (`mounted::vehicle_hold`). The vehicle keeps a reference to each gun it carries ([`Vehicle::m_MountedWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_MountedWeapon)) and places it on its mount part every update ([`Vehicle::UpdatePostParts_WeaponsAttachment_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-UpdatePostParts_WeaponsAttachment_Parallel)); the detach alone does nothing to it. When the vehicle explodes it drops each gun and then clears its entry. The tear does the same for the one gun after the detach, while the vehicle is outside its parallel update phases ([`Vehicle::m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter)), so the vehicle stops placing the gun and it comes away. The vehicle's mount parts stay on the vehicle, empty.

From the gunner seat, the same pull on the handles asks for the game's own exit from the seat (a click of the exit action, asked once more after 1.5 s while the player is still seated). Once the player has left the seat, the gun, which the exit has stopped using, is torn off into the hand that pulled, as above. The tear is given up if the player is still seated after 4 s.

### The gunner seat

A vehicle's empty gunner seat is entered by stepping into it (`vehicles/mounted/gunner_seat.rs`): a player whose head stands behind an unmanned vehicle gun's handles, within 40 cm of the point 35 cm behind their midpoint on the level and not below them, for 0.4 s, while the game offers the gunner seat from where the player is ([`NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask::HasAvailableGunnerDestination`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask##func-HasAvailableGunnerDestination)), is lowered into the seat: the vehicle-entry action is held, as the game takes a held entry past its hold time for the gunner seat ([`NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::NStateTask_InputVehicleEnterTask##func-Update)), until the player mans the gun or 1.5 s pass. A gun is unmanned when it has no user, so a gunner pulled out ([character-grabs.md](character-grabs.md#getting-in)) or shot frees it. After a hold that seats nothing, the head leaves the spot before another starts.

A gun's start of use in the mounted state fades a local player user out ([`Character::FadeCharacterInOut`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-FadeCharacterInOut)) and holds them transparent for as long as they man it, for the game's turret camera; in the headset the view is at the head, so in the gunner seat on the 14 November take nothing of Rico was drawn, not even his free hands. Under the controllers the mounted tick fades him back in as soon as he mans a gun while held faded out ([`m_FadingIn`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_FadingIn) clear), and logs it (`vehicles::mounted_tick::keep_gunner_visible`).

In the seat the gun is left to the seat's native use: the runtime neither stops it nor refuses its start, since the native use is what holds the player in the seat until they leave it with the exit action. The handles aim the gun as on any gun the player mans (below), through the game's own mounted aim. A vehicle gun turns on two powered hinges of its vehicle part, which the weapon's pre-simulation update drives toward a desired yaw and pitch: from the look input when the weapon turns from the stick for a player ([`WeaponBase::m_MountedInputIsControlledByWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_MountedInputIsControlledByWeapon)), and from the aim point otherwise ([`WeaponBase::UpdateMountedInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateMountedInput) decides). A gun that turns from the stick ignored the hands: on the 14 November take a jeep's turret held straight ahead under the gripping hands. While the hands hold the gun's handles and their aim point is fresh, the look-input path is refused (`vehicles::turret`), so the hinges turn toward the hands' point at the gun's own turn speeds and within its limits; a gamepad turns the turret as before. The log says once whether the gun turned from the stick, and the debug UI counts the ticks the hands aimed it. Off the seat, the runtime's grip-driven gun stays as it was.

### Taking the handles

A hand holding anything of the loadout table's takes no handle ([two-handed-inventory.md, "Traversal"](two-handed-inventory.md#traversal)): the player docks or lets go first.


With `vehicles.mounted.grip_handles` (on), each hand takes its own handle like a physical control. A handle within reach ranks over every other grip target ([grip.md](grip.md)), so a hand closing on a handle takes it rather than the gun's body, its holder, or the vehicle part the gun turns on beside it, which the nearest grab point picked by a few millimetres at the session of 10 November (a loose hold on a part the vehicle's own hinge drives, which no pull could turn). A grip press with the hand's grip within 12 cm (`GRAB_RADIUS_M` in `controllers::vehicles::mounted_grip`) of its handle takes it (the handle is a grab volume, `GrabVolume::MountedHandle`, so the press is latched as a grab and does nothing else), and only releasing the grip lets go: a holding hand that drifts off its handle keeps aiming from where it is, however far it goes. Only a holding hand is drawn on its handle, blending on and off over the anchored tier's blend time, its wrist target and its drawn hand moved onto the handle with the controller's orientation kept (`vehicles::state::apply`), and only the holding hands aim: two hands aim from their midpoint, one from itself, and a change of hands takes a new reference, so taking or letting go of a handle never jumps the gun. With both hands off, the gun keeps the direction they last gave it, held in the body frame, so a gunner can let go to throw a grenade or look around and find the gun where they left it. The hands' finger pose on the handles follows their hold. A hand holding a standalone gun's handles through the runtime, without the native mounted state, is drawn on its handle the same way: there the tiers leave the hands free and give no anchor weight, so the grip's own blend draws the hand (`mounted_hand_weight`), where on the 14 November take the hands floated at the controllers beside a tripod minigun they held (the tiers read `free`). A hand on a handle also leaves the physical hands' palms (`physical_hands::free_hands`, through `vehicles::mounted_holds`), so its hand body neither stays at the controller fighting the gun it holds nor draws the hand there. Off, both hands sit on the handles and aim the gun whenever the character mans it, as before, and not off the native mounted state. Before, a hand could not leave the gun: the anchored tier held both hands on the handles for as long as the character manned it.

The off-seat handle pose declares matching finger grips at the same blend weight as the wrists. Grapple-owned hands declare neither pose. Virtual handle control requires a current handle hold and a closed grip; releasing it ends that hand's aiming, firing, and tearing input. Native-use takeover remains separate and can retain the hands with open grip buttons until ownership is lost.

### Recoil in the handles

Each shot of the mounted gun the hands aim kicks a damped spring by 2 cm back along the barrel and 8 mm down (`KICK_BACK_M` and `KICK_DIP_M` in `controllers::vehicles::mounted_recoil`), as the breech jumps back and the muzzle climbs about the pivot. The spring's offset moves the handles the hands are drawn on, so the drawn hands ride the kick, and each shot plays `Event::MountedKick` ([haptics.md](haptics.md)) on the hands holding the handles. The spring is stiff, so one shot settles within a tenth of a second, and its kick is limited to three shots' worth, so a minigun holds the handles back and shakes them rather than driving them off. The aim is left alone: the game's own gun takes its recoil about its pivot. The shots are seen by the per-shot seam on [`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase), which before let a mounted gun's shot through untouched, since it rides no hand; the game's own fire rumble still reaches the hands through the continuous rumble ([controllers.md, "Recoil, haptics, and the grapple hand"](controllers.md#recoil-haptics-and-the-grapple-hand)).

## Settings, logs, and readouts

The switches are `controllers.vehicles.passenger_body_by_hand`, `vehicles.mounted.grip_handles`, and `vehicles.mounted.aim_by_hands`, which the handle recoil follows, in the debug UI's Vehicles feature under Movement/vehicles. Taking a handle logs `the hand took the mounted gun's handle`, with `manned` saying whether the character mans the gun in the native mounted state, and plays `Event::HandleGrab` on that hand, and letting go logs `the hand let go of the mounted gun's handle` with its reason (`grip released`, or `gone` when the hand loses tracking or leaves the gun), at info level. The VEHICLES section's MOUNTED GUN row shows which hands are on the handles (`HANDS OFF, GUN HELD` with neither), the count of grabs and releases, and the count of kicks; off a mounted gun it reads `WAITING, NOT ON A MOUNTED GUN`. The row heads the Vehicles feature's live status, which adds the gun's pivot, barrel, and handles and the hands' aim. The recorder's signals are `vehicles.mounted_hands_on`, `vehicles.mounted_grips`, and `vehicles.mounted_kicks`, and `testing/scenarios/mounted_gun.toml` is the template. For the passenger: The aim update logs `the seated body follows the gun hand's aim` when it starts and `the seated body stops following the gun hand's aim` when it stops, at info level. The debug UI's Status page has a PASSENGER AIM row in its VEHICLES section: `FOLLOWING THE HAND` with the number of starts while it acts, `WAITING, NO GUN HAND AIMING` in a passenger seat with no gun aimed, and `WAITING, NOT A PASSENGER` elsewhere. The recorder's signals are `vehicles.passenger_aiming` and `vehicles.passenger_aim_starts`, and `testing/scenarios/passenger_aim.toml` is the template.

## Unverified

- That the vehicle's gun comes cleanly off its mount once its entry is cleared: it is dropped as the explosion drops it, but on a whole vehicle whose mount parts still stand.
- That the exit action leaves the gunner seat, and where the exit leaves the player beside the mount.
- That nothing, such as an AI gunner, tries to man a gunner seat whose gun is gone; the vehicle's own code skips the cleared entry, as it does after an explosion.
- That the use-item pickup takes a vehicle's dropped gun into the heavy slot.

- That the game offers the gunner seat (`HasAvailableGunnerDestination`) from a stunt position on the vehicle's roof, and that a held entry from there takes the gunner seat rather than another.
- The gunner's spot behind the handles against the seats' entry points, and the dwell against a player walking over the hatch.
- That refusing the look-input path turns the vehicle's turret toward the handles' aim point (`mounted gun: the hands aim a manned vehicle gun in place of the look input`), and that the aim point is far enough from the mount's pivot, about a third of a metre, for the update to aim at it.

- That the vehicle aim blend turns the seated body toward the hand's point without a fight with the passenger's lean-out animation, and how far round it turns before the game's blend runs out of samplers.
- Whether a stunt position runs the same blend; on it the gun aims by the hand either way.
- Whether the game refuses a passenger's shot that points into the vehicle; if it does, the hand's point decides that, rather than the gaze.
- That the per-shot seam sees every mounted gun's shot, the turrets' included, and the kick's size and the spring's feel against the guns' rates of fire.
- That the grip press on a mounted gun is free for the handle grab in every gunner seat.

## Headset checklist

1. In a passenger seat of a car with a pistol drawn, look ahead and point the pistol out of the side window: Rico's torso and arm turn toward the side, the shots go where the hand points, and the log has `the seated body follows the gun hand's aim`. The PASSENGER AIM status row reads `FOLLOWING THE HAND`.
2. Point ahead, behind, and up: the body follows as far as the game's blend goes, with no snapping when the hand crosses the vehicle's axis.
3. With `vehicles.passenger_body_by_hand` off: the body turns toward the gaze again, and the shots still go where the hand points.
4. On a stunt position on a car's roof: the gun aims and fires by the hand, from its trigger and from its hand's B.
5. Driving, and on a mounted gun: the row reads `NOT A PASSENGER`, and nothing changes.
6. In a passenger seat with a pistol drawn, aim and fire through a few seconds: the body keeps following the hand throughout, with no `stops following` in the log while seated.
6. Man a standalone mounted gun: the hands stay free until each grip closes near its handle, then the hand is drawn on the handle with a click and the MOUNTED GUN status row reads `BOTH HANDS ON`. Swing the hands: the gun follows with no jump at the grab.
7. Let go with one hand: the gun keeps aiming by the other. Let go with both: the gun stays where it was, the row reads `HANDS OFF, GUN HELD`, and taking the handles again carries on from there without a jump.
8. Fire: each shot kicks the drawn hands back and a little down and pulses the holding hands; a long burst holds them back and shakes them without drifting away; the aim does not wander from the kick.
9. With `vehicles.mounted.grip_handles` off, the hands sit on the handles as soon as the gun is manned, as before.
10. Walk up to a standalone tripod minigun without pressing use and close each grip on its handle: `the hand took the mounted gun's handle` with `manned=false`, and each drawn hand slides onto its handle over a moment, the gun visibly in the hands while the controllers stay where they are. Turn a wrist: the drawn hand turns with it on the handle. Open one hand: that hand blends back to its controller.
11. Holding the handles, swing a hand well off its handle with the grip closed: it keeps the handle and keeps aiming from where it is; only opening the hand lets go, and the log's `the hand let go of the mounted gun's handle` names the reason `grip released`.
12. On an armoured jeep's roof, grip its turret gunner by the collar and yank back: `a hand pulled a mounted gun's gunner out of the seat`, the gunner falls limp out of the hatch, and the hand holds the body; the player is not seated. Carry the body off the roof and let go.
13. With an armoured jeep's turret gunner pulled out or shot, stand on the roof behind the gun's handles for half a second: `gunner seat: the player stands at an empty gunner seat; entering it`, and the player is lowered into the seat; then `the player mans the gun from its gunner seat`. Walking across the hatch without stopping enters nothing.
14. In the gunner seat, take the handles and swing them: the turret turns and the gun follows; fire. Let go: the hands are free and the player stays in the seat. Leave with the exit action.
15. Standing on the armoured jeep's roof outside the seat, close each hand on a turret gun handle: the grip press logs the handle as taken, the gun body as lost to `a mounted gun's handle is in reach`, and swinging the hands turns the turret and the gun.
16. Outside the armoured jeep, take the turret gun's handles and pull back hard for half a second: `the pull tore the gun off` with `from_vehicle=true`, the gun comes away in the hands, and it ends carried as a heavy weapon (or the log shows the use-item pickup). The turret mount stays on the jeep, empty, and the jeep drives on.
17. In the jeep's gunner seat, take the handles and pull back hard: `tore at the gun from its gunner seat; leaving the seat`, the player leaves the seat, and then the gun is torn off into the hands, as above.
