# The parachute's steering and opening

The game's parachute turns toward where the camera looks, and an opening out of a grapple reel rolls Rico into the harness. In VR the camera is the headset and the view hangs from Rico's head, so the first would steer the canopy with the player's head and the second would roll the view through his body. The engine side is [parachute-locomotion.md](../../engine/gameplay/parachute-locomotion.md). The steering changes are `crates/jc3vr-body/src/hooks/input/parachute.rs`, tuned by the `parachute` section of `crates/jc3vr-body/tuning/input.toml`; the opening skip is `crates/jc3vr-body/src/hooks/animation/canopy.rs`. All of it applies to the local player while the head is decoupled (always, under the VR source), and NPC parachutes are untouched. The debug UI's Air feature counts the look-steer suppressions, the line-turn ticks, and the skipped openings.

## The look steer

[`GetParachuteSteeringValues`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::GetParachuteSteeringValues) mixes the camera input matrix into the canopy's steering, so turning the head past the look-steer dead zone would turn the parachute with no stick input. The jump and fall suppression in `hooks::input::locomotion` does not reach it: the parachute is a separate movement core that never calls `UpdateFallSteering`. A post-call detour subtracts the exact look-steer amount from `steering_values_out`, leaving the stick and the velocity alignment, and zeroes `look_steer_out`, which `UpdateParachutePhysics` otherwise feeds into the yaw and pitch springs.

## The line turn

In the game the look steer is also what turns the canopy toward a grapple line: the slingshot pulls only along the canopy's facing and its own turn toward the line is off ([engine: "The slingshot"](../../engine/gameplay/parachute-locomotion.md#the-slingshot)). So while the hook reels in under the canopy ([`GHS_REELING_IN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHookState##variant-GHS_REELING_IN)), `line_turn` puts back the yaw look steer a camera looking along the line ([`GrapplingHook::GetReelingInDirection`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##func-GetReelingInDirection)) would give, shaped by the game's own look-steer settings. The game's springs then turn the canopy smoothly, and the pull grows as it comes round. The pitch half is left out, and like the game's block it yields to a stick steering across.

## The opening's turn toward the velocity

The steering ends by springing the wanted yaw toward the horizontal velocity's heading (`m_RotateCharYawToVelocity`). An opening out of a fall is shielded from it by its transition state; one out of a reel is not, and the reel's speed falls through zero and overshoots backward as the canopy opens, which reads as a half-turn heading error and spins Rico (a strong hypothesis rather than a proof). So `yaw_alignment_held` calls the steering with that spring's speed at zero for a moment after an opening (detected as a gap in the steering's calls), while the horizontal speed is low, and while the velocity points well away from the facing.

## Skipping the opening out of a reel

A canopy opened during a reel plays `S_JC_PARACHUTE_OPEN_FROM_REEL_IN_LONG` or `_SHORT` ([engine: "The openings out of a reel"](../../engine/gameplay/parachute-locomotion.md#the-openings-out-of-a-reel)). The mod removes the roll rather than moving the camera around it. The body machine still enters the opening, so its state tasks run (the canopy object's own opening act among them), and the local animation controller's update (the `AnimationController::UpdateAnimations` detour) moves it on to `S_JC_PARACHUTE_IDLE` through [`AnimationRuleSystem::SetStateById`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::AnimationRuleSystem##func-SetStateById) before any frame of the opening's clip is evaluated. A detour on [`StateMachineInstance::SetStateInternal`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::rule_system::StateMachineInstance##func-SetStateInternal) catches the transitions that call it; the act transition that opens the canopy does not, which is why the animation update checks too. The idle then flies from the reel's speed with the ordinary parachute physics, without the opening's animation-driven lift. Each skip is logged at info level with the opening's name. An opening from a plain fall plays as the game authored it.
