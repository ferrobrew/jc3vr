# Planted explosives (C4)

How the C4 weapon places charges and detonates them: the weapon and its charges, the planting mode, the drop under the parachute, the collision that makes a charge stick, and the detonate input. The grenade's side is in [aim-pipeline.md, "Grenades"](aim-pipeline.md#grenades), the slot selection in [weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md), and the default buttons in [input.md](input.md#the-default-gamepad-keymap).

The native functions below that are not linked are not bound in pyxis; they are verified in the IDB and named here without numbers.

## The weapon and its charges

The C4 weapon ([`PlantedExplosiveWeapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon)) sits in the inventory's [`E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::WeaponSlot##variant-E_SLOT_PLANTED_EXPLOSIVE) slot. It owns the charges the character has placed: a live list, which the HUD's charge hints and the detonation read, and a cap on its length, [`MaxActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-MaxActiveExplosives), which picks one of three tuned values by the player's explosives-capacity upgrades. [`NumActiveExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-NumActiveExplosives) is the list's length, and [`CanPlantExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-CanPlantExplosive) compares the two. Placing a charge consumes no inventory stock: the cap is the only limit, and detonating the charges frees it.

The inventory runs the weapon's serial update ([`UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateFromUserPostSim_Serial)) every frame, whatever the character wields ([`Inventory::UpdateWeapons_Serial`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeapons_Serial)), so the detonation works with a gun in the hands. The inventory's attachment pass places the weapon every frame too ([`UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)), and with a primed charge it puts the charge's in-hand model on the character's right-hand bone.

A charge ([`PlantedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive)) is an explosive with two models: the in-hand model, shown while it is primed and placed at [`m_InactiveModelWorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##field-m_InactiveModelWorldMatrixT1), and the live model at the explosive's own transform, which [`Activate`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-Activate) shows in its place. Like a grenade, it gets its physics body on its first post-simulation update after it spawns ([`Explosive::m_PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##field-m_PfxInstance)).

## Planting

Planting needs the weapon equipped. Selecting the explosives slot ([weapon-selection.md](weapon-selection.md#the-decision)) equips it, which puts it in its planting mode ([`m_IsInPlantingMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_IsInPlantingMode)): the camera aims a placement ghost at the surface ahead, and an animation track message primes a charge. With the planted-explosives ability, the weapon input task throws a grenade only on a `THROW_GRENADE` released within the hold threshold.

- Priming ([`PrimeExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-PrimeExplosive)) puts the weapon in its [`E_WS_WIELDING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WIELDING) state, hides it, spawns a charge from the ammunition pool at the weapon's shot matrix, and stores it in [`m_PrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_PrimedExplosive). It checks neither the planting mode nor the cap.
- The plant input ([`E_INPUT_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES)), which the weapon input task sets while planting on a `PLANT_EXPLOSIVE` released within the hold threshold, reaches the weapon's fire start. `CPlantedExplosiveWeapon::Fire_Start` does nothing at the cap, while the character is already throwing or re-priming a charge, and outside the planting mode; a target out of range shows the HUD's out-of-range notice, and with no primed charge the weapon is holstered. Otherwise `CPlantedExplosive::PlaceExplosiveAtPosition` casts a short ray at the aimed surface, puts the charge on it oriented by the surface normal, and attaches it to the object hit, and the weapon queues a throw act picked by the target's height. The charge is already in place when the act plays.
- The act's track message calls [`Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile): it removes a charge stuck to a vehicle whose bavarium shield is up, activates the charge, adds heat, runs the base weapon's fire ([`WeaponBase::Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase)), adds the charge to the live list (which ignores a charge beyond the cap), and moves it from [`m_PrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_PrimedExplosive) to [`m_LastPlantedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_LastPlantedExplosive). A track message with no primed charge left queues the re-prime act, or leaves the planting mode at the cap.
- Leaving the planting mode (the weapon's `StopUsing`) removes a primed charge ([`DespawnPrimedExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-DespawnPrimedExplosive)). Outside the planting mode the serial update neither primes nor removes a charge.

The inventory's planting permission ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPlantingExplosives)) is recomputed every frame: off without the planted-explosives ability, while a heavy weapon is wielded, while a camera hint of the wielded weapon is active, while the grapple is busy, and while the [`SB_DISABLE_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_PLANT_EXPLOSIVES) state bit is set.

### The drop under the parachute

Under a deployed parachute ([`Character::IsUsingParachute`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsUsingParachute)) there is no surface to place on. [`Fire_Projectile`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##func-Fire_Projectile) then moves the charge to the right-hand bone ([`Explosive::SetPhysicsTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-SetPhysicsTransform)), drops it into the simulation ([`PlantedExplosive::DropExplosive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-DropExplosive), which makes its body dynamic and keeps it from colliding with the thrower), gives it the character's [`m_MeasuredWorldVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_MeasuredWorldVelocity) with a small extra downward speed ([`PlantedExplosive::SetVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::PlantedExplosive##func-SetVelocity)), and plays its trail ([`Explosive::PlayTrailEffect`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::explosive::Explosive##func-PlayTrailEffect)).

### Sticking

The charge keeps the base explosive's collision handler, which the grenade overrides. On a collision it moves the charge to the contact point, oriented by the contact normal, and attaches it to the object it hit (to a vehicle's part when it hit a vehicle), or plays its stick effect against the world. So a dropped charge sticks where it lands, as a placed one does.

## Detonation

The detonation reads the character's detonate input, [`E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_DETONATE_EXPLOSIVES) in [`Character::m_WeaponInputs`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_WeaponInputs). The player's weapon input task writes it every frame, planting or not. Under the gamepad rules ([`EPCUseGamepad`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/settings_manager::SettingType##variant-EPCUseGamepad)) it is set once `DETONATE_EXPLOSIVE` has been held past the hold threshold, which is why the gamepad's RB throws a grenade on a tap and detonates on a hold; under the keyboard rules a press of `DETONATE_EXPLOSIVE_TAP` sets it too.

The weapon's serial update reads the input directly from its holder, not from the input struct it is passed. While the input is positive, the inventory allows triggering explosives ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsTriggeringExplosives): off without the planted-explosives ability, with a heavy weapon wielded, while the grapple is busy, and while [`SB_DISABLE_TRIGGER_EXPLOSIVES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_TRIGGER_EXPLOSIVES) is set), the character is not already detonating ([`Character::IsDetonatingExplosives`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsDetonatingExplosives)), and the weapon has not already acted on this input ([`m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::PlantedExplosiveWeapon##field-m_AlreadyDetonatedOnThisInput)), it queues the upper-body detonate act when the character can take it, or cancels a reload when it cannot. A queued act sets the latch and adds heat when charges are live; the latch clears once the input is zero again, so a held input detonates once. The act's track message schedules for detonation every live charge that is outside its safety range (`CPlantedExplosive::ScheduleForDetonation`, which skips a charge for which `CPlantedExplosive::IsOutsideSafetyRange` fails; how that range is measured is unverified) and tells the HUD. A charge too close stays placed. The detonator's model is visible only while the act plays.

The detonation does not depend on the selected slot, the planting mode, or the charges' count: with no charges live the act still plays.
