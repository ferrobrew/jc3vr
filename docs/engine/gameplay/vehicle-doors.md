# Vehicle doors

How a vehicle's doors, hood, trunk, and hatches are kept, opened, held open, latched shut, and broken off, and how a character enters through one. A door is a part of the vehicle's breakable body ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)): closed, it is merged into the body; open, it hangs on a hinge as a body of its own. The vehicle's other parts loosen and break off through the same calls.

## The door controller

Each vehicle's door controller ([`Vehicle::m_VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_VehicleDoorController), a [`VehicleDoorController`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController)) keeps a `std::map` from the door slot ([`DoorSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorSlot): the four side doors, the centre doors, a turret door, the trunk, the hood, a rear door, a cargo bay door, a folding wing, and a missile hatch) to a vector of door entries ([`DoorInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo)), since a slot can hold several parts that open together. The map is the standard library's red-black tree: the controller holds its head node ([`m_DoorMapHead`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleDoorController##field-m_DoorMapHead)), whose parent link is the root and to which every leaf links back, and each node ([`DoorMapNode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorMapNode)) carries its slot, the vector's bounds, and a flag set on the head only.

A door entry names its part instance ([`m_PartInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PartInstance)), its state and the state it is asked to move to ([`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_State), [`m_PendingState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PendingState), each a [`DoorState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState)), the hinge's range as its powered controller reports it, a smallest angle for a door held open, the last hinge angle the latch measured, and flags. A pending [`DOOR_CLOSED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_CLOSED) means nothing is pending, and a door whose part broke off is [`DOOR_MISSING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_MISSING).

## Opening and closing

[`Vehicle::OpenDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OpenDoor) asks every door of a slot to open: it puts the vehicle's body in the physics world if it is not, puts the vehicle in the update, and sets each entry's pending state to [`DOOR_OPENING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPENING) and clears its flags, or, asked to keep the door open, sets [`DoorInfo::FLAG_KEEP_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo) and a smallest angle a little below the hinge's largest. [`Vehicle::CloseDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-CloseDoor) asks for a close the same way. The callers are the character's entry and exit animations (through track messages), the AI, scripts, the turret, the cargo bay, and the EMP state.

The controller acts on a request in the vehicle's post-simulation update (`CVehicle::UpdatePostSim_Doors`, through `CVehicleDoorController::UpdateDoors`), on the game thread after the physics step. For an opening, a part that has not broken off is loosened ([`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-EnablePart) with [`EPER_PartOperated`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartEventReason##variant-EPER_PartOperated), keeping the constraint). For either request, `CVehicleDoorController::StartDoorAnimation` converts the hinge to a powered one on first use (`CPfxBreakable::ConvertHingeToPowered`, with the door's default motor values), enables its motor at the speed that covers the hinge's range in the door's open or close duration, and clears the pending state.

While opening (`UpdateOpeningDoorInternal`), the motor's target angle advances each update. Once the door is within a small angle of the hinge's largest, or has touched another body for a while, it is open: the motor is disabled unless the door is kept open, the hinge's smallest angle is set where the door is, and the vehicle is told. While closing (`UpdateClosingDoorInternal`), the door's collision is off, the target angle comes down, and at the hinge's smallest angle the part is merged back (`CPartInstance::MergePart`) and the door is closed.

## The latch

An open door whose motor is off swings freely, pushed by collisions and the vehicle's motion; the game gives a loose door no friction or spring, whether a finished motor opening or a direct `EnablePart` loosened it. Each update, for a free door that hangs by its constraint ([`PartInstance::IsConstraintActive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-IsConstraintActive)) and has a door object, the controller compares its hinge angle above the smallest ([`PfxBreakable::GetHingeAngleAboveMin`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetHingeAngleAboveMin), the absolute current angle less the smaller absolute limit) with the last update's ([`DoorInfo::m_PreviousAngle_Deg`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_PreviousAngle_Deg)). A door within [`LATCH_ANGLE_DEG`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_ANGLE_DEG) of shut that swings shut faster than [`LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-LATCH_SPEED_DEG_S) (statics of the update) is merged by [`PartInstance::MergePart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-MergePart) and reported closed, and [`Vehicle::OnFinishedClosingDoor`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##func-OnFinishedClosingDoor) queues the slot's door-closed effect: a door swung shut latches, one that drifts shut stays loose. The speed is low enough that a door rebounding off its open stop, or a lid falling under its own weight, reaches it. The latch acts on the part's constraint and door object, whatever the entry's state says.

A kept-open door skips the latch. While it is open and touching nothing, it is asked to open again (the motor turns it back) once the hinge's largest angle exceeds its angle above the smallest plus its kept angle ([`m_KeepOpenMinAngle_Rad`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##field-m_KeepOpenMinAngle_Rad)) by more than [`KEEP_OPEN_REOPEN_RAD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorInfo##const-KEEP_OPEN_REOPEN_RAD); a kept angle at the hinge's largest never meets that, so such a door swings freely and never latches. An opening request clears the flags, so the next entry or exit animation puts the door back under the latch.

## The loose door

Loosening a merged part with its constraint kept (`PfxBreakable::EnablePart`) runs at once on the calling thread. The part's owner spawns its physical part, for a vehicle `CVehicle::SpawnPhysicalPart`: a vehicle-part body (`CPfxVehiclePart`, a [`PfxRigidBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxRigidBody)) from the part's world-collision body, a separate raycast body, and the rigid object that owns and draws it, a [`VehiclePartGameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject), which finds its vehicle through [`GetVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::VehiclePartGameObject##func-GetVehicle). `SpawnPhysicalPart` retains the object in the vehicle's part collection, stores its weak reference in the matching `DoorInfo`, and sets its `m_State` to [`DOOR_OPEN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::DoorState##variant-DOOR_OPEN), the same updates as `CVehicleDoorController::SetDoorObject`, before `EnablePart` returns; the body and raycast body exist only if creation succeeded. Then the prototype's constraint is cloned to hang the part from its parent, the loosened event fires, and the part disables the merged world-collision and raycast shapes for itself and its children. A part already hanging by its constraint is left alone, and without the constraint kept the part breaks off instead. A loose part's body is [`PartInstance::GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-GetPfxVehiclePartIfAny), null while merged.

The loosened event does not publish the door object: the vehicle's handler processes damage effects and operated turret hinges. The next post-simulation update starts a motor only for a pending request, so without one an open hinged door takes the free-door latch path through the published weak reference, or the keep-open path when its flag is set.

A closed door has no body of its own, so a ray that hits it reports the vehicle. Its place comes from its part: the model's box in the part's own space, carried by the part's root-space transform and the vehicle's transform ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-live-parts)). A door part's parent space offset is the identity, so its own space is the vehicle's and its origin says nothing about its hinge; the hinge is the part's constraint, whose point the deform points carry as the door's `..._CON` entry ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md#the-parts-resource)). [`PfxBreakable::GetConstraintInfoFromPrototype`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##func-GetConstraintInfoFromPrototype) reads the authored limited hinge's frame, axis, and signed angular limits by part-name hash, refusing absent parts, absent constraints, and other constraint kinds.

## Breaking off a door

[`PartInstance::DamagePart_ToBreakOff`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-DamagePart_ToBreakOff) applies enough neutral-flag damage to pass below zero health, and leaves negative health unchanged. Ordinary damage admission checks the part's invulnerability, player-only vulnerability, and damage-flag exclusions; forced damage bypasses that and the healthy-body restriction ([`Part::m_prevent_breaking_while_alive`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_prevent_breaking_while_alive)) in [`PartInstance::IsAllowedToBreakOffNow`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##func-IsAllowedToBreakOffNow), but [`Part::m_should_break`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_should_break) and the non-root requirement still apply. The final instant break refuses [`PartInstance::m_Invulnerable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PartInstance##field-m_Invulnerable) on its own, and queues its non-thread-safe operation while [`PfxBreakable::m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::PfxBreakable##field-m_InParallelUpdatePhaseCounter) is positive. Vehicle damage admission suppresses part damage while [`Vehicle::m_Invulnerable`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_Invulnerable) or [`Vehicle::m_InJesusMode`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::Vehicle##field-m_InJesusMode) is set, which a direct call to a part helper skips.

A part that disappears when broken ([`Part::m_disappears_when_broken`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_disappears_when_broken)), or has no authored world-collision shape ([`Part::m_has_world_collision_shape`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::parts::Part##field-m_has_world_collision_shape)) outside the native special behaviour, is removed. Otherwise an active hinge is broken and the loosened body kept. The broken-off notification marks the part broken for good and notifies listeners; the vehicle marks matching door entries missing and drops its retained part object, and the native material and collision-group changes still happen. `EnablePart` without the constraint bypasses the damage admission and the authored breakability, so it is not equivalent to damage.

## Entering through a door

A seated character is attached to the vehicle through the interaction graph ([`Character::IsAttachedToVehicle`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-IsAttachedToVehicle), [`GetVehiclePtr`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetVehiclePtr)); each seat ([`VehicleSeat`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/vehicle::vehicle::VehicleSeat)) is a node of the graph, and its occupant is the node's occupant. The player enters with [`ENTER_VEHICLE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-ENTER_VEHICLE) on foot, and the entry's animation opens and closes the seat's door through the controller. The animation set has hijack acts and states (`ACT_GET_HIJACK`, `ACT_EXIT_VEHICLE_HIJACK`, `S_HIJACK_STRUGGLE_FAIL`), and the graph has conditions on a door's existence and closure and on a vehicle being locked against hijacking. Which entry the action picks, and that it hijacks an occupied seat, is the game's behaviour on the gamepad, unverified in the code.

### The interaction graph in the entity

A vehicle's [`InteractionGraph`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph) is built from its entity property file: a `CInteractionContext` object holds a `CInteractionGraph` object whose children, each with its own `_object_id`, are the nodes and entries, read by `CInteractionGraph::SetEntryProperties`, `SetNodeProperties`, `SetPointNodeProperties`, and `SetDeformablePointNodeProperties`. The properties that matter:

- The graph's `modifier`, an animation modifier such as `MOD_LOW` or `MOD_HIGH` its users take; an entry or node can carry its own, as the rear seats' NPC entries do.
- An entry's `state` (`S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE`, `S_ENTER_PASSENGER_VEHICLE`, and the no-door, hijack, unlock, and stunt variants), its act, its `user_type` (the player, NPCs, or anyone), and its `alignment_bone`, the bone the warp aligns (`Reference` on every vehicle entry).
- An entry's `entry_node`, the object id of the node it leads to, which becomes its [`m_EntryNodeIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraphEntry##field-m_EntryNodeIndex).
- An entry's identifier (`m_Identifier`, whose property name is not known): on a vehicle, the door slot of the seat it leads to (the driver's names the front left slot, the front passenger's the front right).
- A point node standing in for a deformable point node (`m_TempPretendImADeformablePoint`) and its deform point's name. Every seat node is one: the seat's place is not the node's own `world` matrix but the vehicle's deform point of that name (`driver`, `passenger1`, and so on, in `<model>_vehicle_deform.vmodc`), whose world transform, deformation and all (`CVehicle::GetDeformableTransform_WorldTransform`), is the node's.

### Entry clips and the open message

The entry's state and modifier pick an animation node: the player's `S_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE` with `MOD_LOW` plays `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE_MOD_LOW`, and without a modifier `ANIM_ENTER_DRIVER_VEHICLE`. The node names its clip, segments, and track messages, one of which opens the door at the moment the hand is on the handle: `open_door` and `quick_open_door` open the slot of the character's current entry (its identifier), and `open_door_with_identifier` the slot its argument names, so a clip can open another seat's door. The character's message handler calls `Vehicle::OpenDoor` (or `CVehicle::QuickOpenDoor`) with the slot. The standard car entries open their door partway into the clip; the NPC entry clips open it at their first frame, before any hand reaches it.

### The entry warp

An entry clip plays with a warp (`NInteractionGraphEntryTasks::NWarpedMotion`) so it ends in the seat whatever the vehicle. On entering the state, `OnEnterSetupMotionWarping` takes the entry's alignment bone as the anchor and its node ([`InteractionGraph::GetNodeByIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/interaction::InteractionGraph##func-GetNodeByIndex) of the entry node) as the destination, and sets the goal, in the graph's space, by one of three modes the state's warp properties choose: the best point on the node (`CInteractionGraph::GetBestPointOnNodeLS`, for a deformable point node the deform point's full transform), the closest point, or the node's centre with the character's current orientation. `NMotionWarpUtility` extracts the anchor's motion from the clip's current time to its end (less the track's early finish time, or to the end of a named extraction segment) and spreads the correction that lands the anchor on the goal over the clip's warp segments: `SEG_WARP_HORIZONTAL`, `SEG_WARP_VERTICAL`, and `SEG_WARP_ORIENTATION` for the horizontal position, height, and heading, or one `SEG_WARP` for all three. Outside its segment, each part of the motion is the clip's own.

On the standard car entry clips, the horizontal and heading segments end at the open message and the vertical one starts after it. When the door opens, the character is therefore placed along and across the vehicle by the seat, less the clip's motion still to come, at the clip's own height above the ground it started on. The clips are shared by a class of vehicles, one per modifier, so the hand meets the door where the class's reference vehicle had its handle, not necessarily where this vehicle's is modelled.

## Unverified

- Which object a ray query reports for a loose door (the part object that owns its body, or the vehicle), and whether it hits the part's world-collision body or its raycast body.
- That the part's root-space transform follows a loose door's swing.
- How each vehicle class's hatches and folding wings hinge, and whether any opening slides rather than hinges.
- How the message handler picks the slot for `open_door`, `quick_open_door`, and `open_door_with_identifier`.
- Which goal mode each entry state's warp uses; one that keeps the character's orientation would not turn it to the seat's heading.
- The early finish times of the entry clips' tracks, which would end the extracted motion before the clip does.
