# The weapon in the hand and the arm aim IK

The weapon-to-hand attachment chain (the props system), the `SECONDARY` hand pass that puts hands on grips, and the two shipped per-arm aim IK systems. The pyxis defs are mainly [`Character`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character) and the [`equipment::weapon`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/equipment::weapon) and [`animation::arm_aim_ik`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/animation::arm_aim_ik) modules. Related: [humanik.md](humanik.md) (the solver, the effector ids, and the pass order), [skeleton.md](skeleton.md) (the Joint API), [aim-pipeline.md](../gameplay/aim-pipeline.md) (the aim point and fire direction), and [grapple-pipeline.md](../gameplay/grapple-pipeline.md) (grapple targeting).

## Weapon-to-hand attachment (the props system)

### The weapon model follows a hand safe bone, not `UpdatePropEffects`

Despite its name, [`Character::UpdatePropEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePropEffects) does not place the wielded weapon. It does three things: a `SetJoint` on the [`SafeBoneIndex::HEAD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-HEAD) safe bone, [`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentEffects), and `UpdateBalloonHead`.

The weapon's placement is a bone attachment. Each gun carries, per weapon state, an `NBone::ESafeBoneIndex` naming the bone it rides: [`WeaponBase::GetCurrentBoneAttachement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetCurrentBoneAttachement)`(weaponIndex)` returns [`WeaponData::m_lBoneAttachementPerWeaponState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_lBoneAttachementPerWeaponState)`[m_State]`, [`WeaponBase::ChangeBoneAttachement`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ChangeBoneAttachement)`(bone, weaponIndex)` rebinds it, and `CWeaponBase::IsLeftHanded` is `bone == ATTACH_HAND_LEFT`. The relevant safe bones are [`ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT), [`ATTACH_HAND_LEFT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_LEFT), [`ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT2`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT2), [`ATTACH_HAND_LEFT2`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_LEFT2), [`RIGHT_HAND`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-RIGHT_HAND), and [`LEFT_HAND`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-LEFT_HAND), bound with their name hashes in [`SafeBoneIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex) (`NBone::ESafeBoneIndex`).

The chain is weapon world ← hand attach bone ← character world. Each hand has two dedicated attach points: the primary one (`ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT`, `ATTACH_HAND_LEFT`), which a gun held in that hand rides, and the second one (`ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT2`, `ATTACH_HAND_LEFT2`), the support hand's grip point, which the `SECONDARY` hand pass brings to a two-handed weapon's grip ("The secondary hand pass" below). A gun can ride a second attach point too when its table names one.

### The gun's pose in the hand is animated

For a gun in a hand state, [`WeaponData::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms) sets [`m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1) straight from [`Character::GetWorldMatrixForBone`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetWorldMatrixForBone) for the bone the gun's table names (the bone's model-space matrix in the current pose times the character's world matrix), with no offset matrix in between. Only a holster or back bone is composed with the gun's own attach offset, and only when the weapon's attach tuning asks for it. So the gun's pose relative to the wrist is exactly the attach bone's.

The attach bones are ordinary skeleton bones, posed by the clips over the reference pose ([animation-layers.md, "The pose job"](animation-layers.md#the-pose-job)). A clip that keys an attach bone therefore turns or moves the gun in the hand, and two clips that key it differently sweep the gun between their poses over a blend. Which shipped clips key the attach bones is unverified: only the hand-pose clip's binding was read, and it drives the fingers alone ([animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md), "Clips, body parts, and the hand poses"). The rest pose relative to the wrist comes from the bind pose ([`AnimationController::GetBindPoseBoneMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-GetBindPoseBoneMatrix), the skeleton resource's model-space matrix, unchanged by animation). A [`AnimationController::SetJoint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::AnimationController##func-SetJoint) on an attach bone before a placement moves the gun and everything derived from its cached transform.

### Where the weapon's world transform is computed per frame

[`WeaponBase::GetTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-GetTransform) returns [`WeaponData::GetWorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##func-GetWorldMatrixT1): the gun's cached [`WeaponData::m_WorldMatrixT1`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT1), scaled by [`WeaponData::m_HolsteredScaleValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_HolsteredScaleValue) when the weapon's state is [`WeaponState::E_WS_HOLSTERED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_HOLSTERED). The attachment placement refreshes that cache each frame from the attach bone, the weapon being a child model instance of it. Sim-side consumers, such as the `GetIPfxInstanceClosestTo` physics query and the fire origin, read `T1`.

The render transform is not `T1`. The virtual [`WeaponBase::UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdateRender) (mislabelled `CWeaponData::UpdateRender` in the IDB) runs in the render phase over [`WeaponBase::m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData), with the per-data body inlined: it lerps [`m_WorldMatrixT0`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_WorldMatrixT0) to `m_WorldMatrixT1` by the render context's `dtf`, applies the holstered scale, and pushes the result to [`WeaponData::m_BaseModelInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_BaseModelInstance) with [`ModelInstance::UpdateMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/graphics_engine::model_instance::ModelInstance##func-UpdateMatrix) (a plain matrix store). It also updates the skinning of the weapon's own animated model with the same `dtf`. The hand mesh interpolates separately, through the character's [`MakeSkinningPalette`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::pose_producer::PoseProducer##func-MakeSkinningPalette)`(dtf)` blend of the `T0`/`T1` poses ([20-animation-judder.md](../../issues/20-animation-judder.md)).

### A gun's separate model parts

A gun can draw parts of itself (a magazine, a pistol's slide, a minigun's barrel) as separate models, each a [`ModelAttachementWeaponComponent`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent) or one of the barrel recoil and barrel spin components derived from it, which share its render update. A part keeps its own pair of cached world matrices ([`m_WorldMatrix`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_WorldMatrix) and [`m_WorldMatrixSaved`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_WorldMatrixSaved)). Its placement ([`UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms)), run from the weapon's, shifts the current matrix into the saved one on an advancing placement and sets the current one to the part's [`m_Offset`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_Offset) composed with the gun's `GetWorldMatrixT1`. Its render update ([`UpdateRender`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-UpdateRender)), called for each component after the guns' matrices are pushed, interpolates the two and pushes the result to the part's model instance. The part therefore follows the gun's sim-rate transform, not its render push: changing only the gun's pushed matrix leaves the parts where the simulation put them.

An animation task (`NWeaponModelAttachementSetBoneTask`) can put a part on one of the holder's bones instead ([`AttachToCharacterBone`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-AttachToCharacterBone), setting [`m_CharacterBone`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##field-m_CharacterBone)), as a reload does with the magazine. The component's end-of-pass hook ([`UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::ModelAttachementWeaponComponent##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose)) resets the bone, so the render update cannot tell which bone the part was last placed on. The IDB mislabels the render update `NInteractionGraphExitTasks::NAnimationDrivenOnlyMotion::Update`, and puts its name on a function that reads a gun's fields instead.

### What derives from the attach bone and what derives from the weapon cache

The weapon model, the muzzle effects, and the weapon's physics proxy all derive from the hand attach bone, so a `SetJoint` on it in the post-finalize phase moves them together. `T1` is downstream of the bone and nothing on the hand side reads it, so changing `T1` alone (or the return of `GetWorldMatrixT1`) moves the rendered weapon and muzzle away from the hand.

### Dual-wield: one weapon object, per-gun attach bones

A dual-wield slot (`E_SLOT_DUAL_WIELD`) is one `CWeaponBase` holding one `CWeaponData` per gun in [`m_lWeaponData`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_lWeaponData), not two weapon instances. Each gun has its own attach-bone table, indexed by `weapon_index`; `ChangeBoneAttachement` rebinds one gun's bones independently, writing the slot of every weapon state ([`WeaponState::E_WS_NUM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_NUM) slots). Placement is independent per gun, and the per-barrel aim-target write ([aim-pipeline.md](../gameplay/aim-pipeline.md)) gives each gun its own target. State is shared: the pair has one `m_State`, one aim flag, and one reload, equip, and holster animation. The guns are posed independently only in the free-aim states (`E_WS_WIELDING`, `E_WS_DUAL_WIELDING`); outside them one two-gun animation drives both hands.

### The attach-bone table is rewritten every pass

A gun's table is not set once at equip time. [`Character::UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdatePassFinalizePose_Parallel) runs, in order: the pass's state tasks; [`Inventory::UpdateAttachmentTransforms`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateAttachmentTransforms), which places every slot weapon's guns on the bones their tables name for the weapon's state (run again after the `MAIN` solve, from [`Character::UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass), and from the prop-transform update); and near the end [`Inventory::UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateInventoryPostFinalizePose). That runs each weapon's [`WeaponBase::UpdatePostFinalizePose`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-UpdatePostFinalizePose) (mislabelled `CWeaponBase::CalculateAttachOffsets` in the IDB), which rewrites every gun's entries for the wielding, dual-wielding, and holstered states from [`m_EquipSlot`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_EquipSlot). A bone written into a table between passes places the gun only if written before the placement, and lasts only until the end of that pass.

For the dual-wield slot the rewrite puts gun 0 on [`ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT) when wielding or dual-wielding and on [`RIGHT_HOLSTER`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-RIGHT_HOLSTER) when holstered, and gun 1 on [`ATTACH_HAND_LEFT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_LEFT) when dual-wielding and on [`LEFT_HOLSTER`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-LEFT_HOLSTER) otherwise. The wielding state ([`E_WS_WIELDING`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_WIELDING)) is the slot's one-pistol state, gun 1 waiting on the left thigh holster, and the engine special-cases it: [`WeaponBase::SetState`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetState) resets [`m_NextWeaponToFire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_NextWeaponToFire) to gun 0 on entering it, the tail of [`Fire_ProjectileBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-Fire_ProjectileBase) keeps the index at gun 0, `CWeaponData::UpdateAttachedEffects` skips gun 1's fire-effect restart, and [`WeaponBase::SetVisibleBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SetVisibleBase) hides every gun after the first whenever it is called, which the state tasks below do not do when they set the state ([weapon-equip-and-drop.md, "The dual-wield weapon"](../gameplay/weapon-equip-and-drop.md#the-dual-wield-weapon)). The player's draw ([`WeaponBase::OnEquip`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##vfunc-OnEquip)) always sets the dual-wielding state; a non-player without a particular blackboard value gets the wielding state, the single pistol NPCs carry.

Within a pass, state tasks move single guns between hand and holster while the weapon stays wielded. `NSetWeaponBoneTask` rebinds one gun to a named bone inside an animation segment; the motorbike's dual-wield draw, holster, and switch states use it to draw and holster one pistol at a time. `NSetWeaponPreDefinedBoneTask` has three modes: `clear_left_hand` parks gun 1 on its holster and sets the wielding state, `clear_right_hand` parks gun 0 on the right holster (moving the weapon to the left hand), and `restore_default` puts gun 0 back in the right hand; outside its segment the task (and its exit) sets the dual-wielding state back. In Rico's graphs ([animation-graph.md](animation-graph.md)) `clear_left_hand` runs across the base graph's on-foot locomotion, jumps, grapple fire and reel states, and motorbike aim states, most instances gated on the `CLEAR_LEFT_HAND` segment; `clear_right_hand` in its grenade throws, explosive planting and detonation, and some grapple-attach and landing states; and `restore_default` in every reload state of the upper-body graph except the left-handed reloads and the mortar's. Both tasks call [`WeaponBase::SkipRenderInterpolationForOneFrame`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-SkipRenderInterpolationForOneFrame) for the gun they move, setting its [`m_SkipRenderInterpolation`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponData##field-m_SkipRenderInterpolation) so the next placement copies the new world matrix into `m_WorldMatrixT0` too and the render does not blend across the move; the end-of-pass rewrite clears the flag. None of these task functions are bound in pyxis except where linked.

`CWeaponBase::IsWieldedInLeftHand` (gun 0's current bone is `ATTACH_HAND_LEFT`) is read by an animation-graph condition and by the vertical-aim blend samplers, so a table that puts gun 0 in the left hand while the graph evaluates can change the states the graph picks.

### Muzzle-flash and shell effects and fire logic (aim-pipeline interface point)

The wielded weapon's effects (muzzle flash, fire particle, casings, and sounds) live in the `CAttachedEffectContainer`. [`AttachedEffectContainer::UpdateAttachedEffects`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::attached_effects::AttachedEffectContainer##func-UpdateAttachedEffects) updates each instance through [`AttachedInstance::UpdateTransform`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::attached_effects::AttachedInstance##func-UpdateTransform), which resolves its transform through a virtual resolver reading a bone or socket on the weapon model (the muzzle bone) relative to the weapon's cached `T1`, copies the transform block ([`EffectTransformInfo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::attached_effects::EffectTransformInfo)), applies its transform modes, and derives a per-frame velocity (the position delta over the fixed tick length). The muzzle flash therefore follows the hand attach bone, and also a change to `T1` alone. The shot origin is the muzzle and the shot direction the weapon's stored aim point, so moving the weapon moves the origin but not the direction ([aim-pipeline.md, "Fire direction, end to end"](../gameplay/aim-pipeline.md#fire-direction-end-to-end-getshotmatrix)).

## The secondary hand pass

[`Character::UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-UpdateSecondaryHandIKPass) runs after the `MAIN` solve ([humanik.md, "Per-frame lifecycle"](humanik.md#per-frame-lifecycle)) and places wrists from blackboard keys written by state tasks in the finalize range ([animation-layers.md](animation-layers.md#state-tasks-inside-the-pose-finalization-pass)). It turns each world-space key into a model-space position target on the `SECONDARY` pass for that arm.

The weapon off-hand IK task ([`NWeaponOffhandIKTask_Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/module/animation::arm_aim_ik##func-NWeaponOffhandIKTask_Update)) puts a hand on a two-handed weapon's grip. Unless [`StateBitFlag::SB_DISABLE_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK) is set or its segment is not playing on the base layer, it marks the hand with [`StateBitFlag::SB_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK_LEFT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK_LEFT) or [`StateBitFlag::SB_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_WEAPON_OFFHAND_IK_RIGHT) and writes [`ObjectBlackboard::OFFHAND_IK_HAND_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::OFFHAND_IK_HAND_ID) and [`ObjectBlackboard::OFFHAND_IK_WEIGHT_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::OFFHAND_IK_WEIGHT_ID); the pass reads them and moves that wrist so the hand's second attach bone lands on the weapon's grip point. The base layer's rifle variants also pose the off-hand on the foregrip in the clips themselves.

The parachute handle IK task ([`NParachuteHandleIKTask::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NParachuteHandleIKTask##func-Update)) reads each handle link bone of the parachute's rig, offsets it by the character's hand-attach offset, and writes it to the wrist's `SECONDARY` position key ([`ObjectBlackboard::SECONDARY_HAND_POSITION_WS_LEFT_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::SECONDARY_HAND_POSITION_WS_LEFT_ID) and its right twin) with the blend weight in the wrist's weight key ([`ObjectBlackboard::SECONDARY_HAND_WEIGHT_LEFT_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::SECONDARY_HAND_WEIGHT_LEFT_ID) and its right twin). Unlike the weapon and grapple keys, these are written with [`PERSISTENCE_SESSION`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::PersistenceType##variant-PERSISTENCE_SESSION) rather than per-frame persistence: they stay on the blackboard between ticks while the task runs, and [`NParachuteHandleIKTask::OnExit`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NParachuteHandleIKTask##func-OnExit) removes all four. The parachute object's bone sync ([`NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::parachute::NParachuteObjectControl_SyncBones)) is a second, independent path between the hands and the handles that goes through neither the blackboard nor HumanIK ([parachute-locomotion.md, "The handle props"](../gameplay/parachute-locomotion.md#the-handle-props)). No parachute system queues a `MAIN` target on a wrist or gates the HumanIK solve.

## Per-arm aim IK

The game ships two per-arm aim IK systems, the gun arm and the grapple reel-in arm; there is no generic left-arm aim IK. Both are player-only state tasks (the weapon-aim state and the grapple reel-in state; the state-task tables that reference them are not modelled in the defs), both are gated on the blackboard aim-IK weight [`ObjectBlackboard::AIM_IK_WEIGHT_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/blackboard::ObjectBlackboard::AIM_IK_WEIGHT_ID), and both queue `CHumanIK` rotation targets on `PASS_MAIN` at [`SolveStep::UPPER_BODY`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::SolveStep##variant-UPPER_BODY) during the finalize pass's state tasks, before the `HasTargets(PASS_MAIN)` gate. How targets from several suppliers share the solve is in [humanik.md, "Target entry lifetime and the `is_valid` flag"](humanik.md#target-entry-lifetime-and-the-is_valid-flag). The aim-state conditions that activate aim IK (`NAimIKTask::Update`) are in [aim-state.md, "Arm and look IK"](../gameplay/aim-state.md#arm-and-look-ik).

### Right-arm aim IK (the gun arm): three effectors, including the head

[`RightArmAimIK::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIK##func-Update) runs only when `CCharacter::IsPlayer` holds. It reads the aim weight, brings the aim target into model space with `inverse(m_WorldMatrixT1)`, and calls [`RightArmAimIK::UpdateAimEffector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIK##func-UpdateAimEffector) on [`Character::m_HIK`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##field-m_HIK) for each of three effectors. Its instance properties ([`RightArmAimIKInstanceProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIKInstanceProperties)) are a pole vector and three [`AimEffectorProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::AimEffectorProperties) entries (`NRightArmAimIK::SAimEffectorProperties`), one per `EAimEffectorTypes` value in order, `AET_RIGHT_ARM`, `AET_RIGHT_HAND`, and `AET_HEAD`, each holding the bone index, the bone position, the gain, and the derived aim direction and rotation axis. The gains are [`RightArmAimIK_ArmGain`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIK_ArmGain), [`RightArmAimIK_HandGain`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIK_HandGain), and [`RightArmAimIK_HeadGain`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::RightArmAimIK_HeadGain).

`UpdateAimEffector` computes the current-to-target rotation (axis and `acos` angle), calls [`HumanIK::AddEffectorTargetRotationVector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-AddEffectorTargetRotationVector) with `SolveStep::UPPER_BODY` and [`Pass::MAIN`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::Pass##variant-MAIN), and writes [`HumanIK::m_TargetReachR`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##field-m_TargetReachR)`[effector]`. Because it drives [`Effector::HEAD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::Effector##variant-HEAD), the game turns the head toward the reticle whenever the player aims, and any other head target on the same solve competes with it.

### Reel-in aim IK (the grapple arm): one arm effector and the chest (grapple-pipeline interface point)

[`ReelInAimIK::Update`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::ReelInAimIK##func-Update) drives one arm effector ([`ReelInAimIKInstanceProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::ReelInAimIKInstanceProperties) has one `m_AimEffectors` entry) through [`ReelInAimIK::UpdateAimEffector`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::ReelInAimIK##func-UpdateAimEffector), plus a chest-end rotation, from two sources.

The arm target is the live [`PlayerAimControl::m_AimPos`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::aim::PlayerAimControl##field-m_AimPos) entry for the weapon slot, [`TargetType::TARGET_WEAPONS`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/aim::target::TargetType##variant-TARGET_WEAPONS), not the grapple slot, read through [`Player::m_AimControl`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_AimControl) and brought into character-local space. `UpdateAimEffector` takes the direction from the [`SafeBoneIndex::RIGHT_ARM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-RIGHT_ARM) bone to that point and adds a rotation on the `RIGHT_ARM` effector (`CHumanIK::AddEffectorTargetRotation`) that turns the axis of [`SafeBoneIndex::ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-ATTACH_HAND_RIGHT) onto it. The target is the current aim point, not the hook's position or the target latched when the hook fired.

The chest target comes from the hook. When [`Character::GetGrapplingHook`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::Character##func-GetGrapplingHook)'s [`GrapplingHook::m_ActiveWire`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_ActiveWire) is non-null, the function brings [`GrapplingHook::m_CachedReelTargetPosition`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::grappling_hook::GrapplingHook##field-m_CachedReelTargetPosition) into character-local space and measures the signed horizontal angle, around the [`SafeBoneIndex::SPINE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::SafeBoneIndex##variant-SPINE) bone, between the arm target and that position. Past a threshold constant it scales the excess to a rotation of up to [`ReelInAimIK::MAX_CHEST_ROTATION_DEGREES`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::ReelInAimIK::MAX_CHEST_ROTATION_DEGREES), weights it by one minus the reel segment's local time, and adds a chest-end rotation ([`HumanIK::GetChestEndEffectorId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::HumanIK##func-GetChestEndEffectorId)) with `AddEffectorTargetRotation`; [`AimIKUtil::GetIKWeightValue`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::arm_aim_ik::AimIKUtil##func-GetIKWeightValue) gives the reel-in weight envelope. The decompiler does not resolve every argument of that call, so how the angle and weight reach it is inferred. The field name `m_CachedReelTargetPosition` has medium confidence: it comes from a `CGrapplingHook` layout that matches at the neighbouring mapped fields.

Where the hook fires and attaches is grapple targeting ([grapple-pipeline.md](../gameplay/grapple-pipeline.md)); the reel-in IK poses the arm and chest toward that result.

### Wrist targets and the animated grip

A position target on [`Effector::LEFT_WRIST`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::Effector##variant-LEFT_WRIST) or [`Effector::RIGHT_WRIST`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/animation::ik::Effector##variant-RIGHT_WRIST) ([humanik.md, "Effector-id model"](humanik.md#effector-id-model)) places a wrist on either pass, but the weapon's animation also poses the hand, so a wrist target that disagrees with the animated grip pulls the wrist off the pose the animation and the attach bone assume. How a wrist effector behaves on an arm the animation holds in a two-handed grip is not verified.
