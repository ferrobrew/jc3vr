# Item pickup and loose bodies

What lies loose in the world, how a weapon is picked up, and what a loose body is to the Havok world. Hitting and pushing a body is in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md), and the physics step and collision filter in [havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md).

## What lies in the world

A weapon in the world (dropped, broken out of a supply drop's cargo, or placed) is a [`WeaponBase`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase), a physics game object with its own body, not a rigid object, so a query that hits it reports the weapon. Its [`m_State`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_State) is [`E_WS_DROPPED`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_DROPPED) while it lies loose and [`E_WS_PICKING_UP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponState##variant-E_WS_PICKING_UP) while being picked up, and the run-time type test tells it apart ([`WeaponBase::TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-TYPE_ID) through [`GameObject::IsType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/game::GameObject##vfunc-IsType), as in [melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#hit-objects-and-run-time-types)). The despawn ranking removes it by distance from the player and the view, not time, and never while anything holds a strong reference to it ([weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md](weapon-ownership-and-despawn.md)).

A loose prop (a crate, a barrel, a gas canister, debris) is a [`RigidObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject) ([`TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::RigidObject##func-TYPE_ID)), a physics game object with one rigid body that may be static, fixed, keyframed, or dynamic. Characters, vehicles, and weapons are not rigid objects.

A collectible (`CCollectibleObject`) has no physics instance, so no query reports one; scripts and interactions send its collect event. The other use interactables (tombs, tapes, and the like) are interaction graphs entered through the use input.

## Picking up a weapon

Ammunition needs no input: a world weapon's pickup interaction, activated as the player comes within it, asks [`WeaponBase::ShouldAutoPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-ShouldAutoPickup), which collects it itself:

- a power-core weapon gives nothing;
- a hand grenade answers yes while the activator's grenade reserve is below its cap, and the interaction starts its pickup, which adds its rounds to the reserve and despawns it;
- any other weapon with stored rounds ([`m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_StoredNonMagazineAmmo)) gives them to the reserve ([`Inventory::AddAmmo`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-AddAmmo), up to the cap) when the activator holds a weapon of the same ammunition type ([`Inventory::HasWeaponWithAmmoType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-HasWeaponWithAmmoType)) and neither gun nor character has unlimited ammunition. The HUD shows the pickup, the picked-up-ammunition event is sent, and the weapon keeps the rest and stays (the answer is no).

`HasWeaponWithAmmoType` reads only the eight slots' [`m_AmmunitionType`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##field-m_AmmunitionType), and this credit is its one caller. The weapon's pickup check separately refuses the use-input pickup when the slot already holds the same weapon and the reserve is full, reading the slot only (unverified).

The weapon itself takes the use input. The player's weapon input task sets [`E_INPUT_USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::character::WeaponInput##variant-E_INPUT_USE_ITEM) on a click of [`USE_ITEM`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/input::input_action_map::Action##variant-USE_ITEM), and [`Inventory::UpdateWeaponHandling`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-UpdateWeaponHandling) acts on it while the character may pick up ([`Inventory::GetAnimationAllowsPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##func-GetAnimationAllowsPickup): the animation's permission, [`m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::inventory::Inventory##field-m_DoesAnimationAllowPickup), with [`SB_DISABLE_PICKUP`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/state::StateBitFlag##variant-SB_DISABLE_PICKUP) clear). On land it queues the full-body pickup act (`ACT_PICKUP_WEAPON`); the pickup interaction's execution starts the pickup ([`WeaponBase::PickupStart`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupStart), which takes the weapon out of the physics world), and the weapon's pickup track message completes it ([`WeaponBase::PickupItem`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/equipment::weapon::WeaponBase##func-PickupItem), detailed with the drop in [weapon-equip-and-drop.md](weapon-equip-and-drop.md#completing-a-pickup)). A swimming character picks up the first weapon among its interaction proxy's valid entries at once and plays the partial pickup act instead.

The HUD's pickup panel shows the weapon on offer, its ammunition, the weapon it would replace, and the use-item glyph. [`Player::UpdateWeaponHUD`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##func-UpdateWeaponHUD), each frame from the post-physics update, offers the weapon of the first valid interaction entry, the one a swimming pickup takes, and offers nothing while `USE_ITEM` is disabled in the player's action map or the animation forbids a pickup. It remembers the last offer ([`m_CurrentWeaponPickupId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/character::player::Player##field-m_CurrentWeaponPickupId)) and tells the HUD only on a change: a new weapon or ammunition shows the panel ([`HUDUI::OnNewValidWeaponPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-OnNewValidWeaponPickup)), and no offer clears it ([`HUDUI::ClearValidWeaponPickup`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/ui::hud_ui::HUDUI##func-ClearValidWeaponPickup)). Which entry wins with several weapons in range, and how the interaction's execution follows the act, are unverified.

## A body in the Havok world

The Havok world ([`hknpWorld`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld)) keeps its bodies in one array by body id ([`m_bodies`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodies)), up to the highest id allocated ([`m_peakBodyIndex`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_peakBodyIndex)). A body ([`hknpBody`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody)) is moved by a motion ([`m_motionId`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_motionId) into [`hknpWorld::m_motions`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_motions)), and only a dynamic body ([`IS_DYNAMIC`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::IS_DYNAMIC) in [`m_flags`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody##field-m_flags)) is moved by velocities and impulses. A destroyed body's id is freed, its motion id invalid ([`INVALID_MOTION_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBody::INVALID_MOTION_ID)) until a new body reuses it, so an id alone does not name an object across frames.

The motion ([`hknpMotion`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion)) holds the centre of mass and the orientation of its frame, the inverse inertia and inverse mass as Havok half floats ([`m_inverseInertia`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpMotion##field-m_inverseInertia); a zero inverse mass is infinite mass), the linear velocity in world space, and the angular velocity in the motion's frame. That frame is the body's principal frame: the inverse inertia is one value per axis, and the world-space inverse inertia is that diagonal turned by the motion's orientation. Bodies attached to one another share one motion, and with it one centre of mass and set of mass properties. Positions are Havok space, the game-space position less [`PhysicsSystem::m_AccumulatedShift`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##field-m_AccumulatedShift) ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#pushing-a-body)).

A body's owner is one of its properties. The body manager keeps per-property buffers by body id ([`hknpWorld::m_bodyProperties`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##field-m_bodyProperties), looked up by key with [`hknpBodyPropertyMap::getWithDefault`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpBodyPropertyMap##func-getWithDefault)), a body having a property when its bit is set in the buffer's occupancy. The engine's instance property ([`BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld::BODY_PROPERTY_PFX_INSTANCE)) holds the body's [`PfxInstance`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance), whose owner ([`m_GameObject`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PfxInstance##field-m_GameObject)) is the physics game object a ray query reports for the body, as [`PhysicsSystem::CastRay`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::PhysicsSystem##func-CastRay) reads it.

## Setting a body's velocity

[`hknpWorld::setBodyVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyVelocity) (labelled a getter by the disassembler, though it writes both velocities) sets a dynamic body's linear and angular velocity, both in world space, and wakes it; [`setBodyLinearVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyLinearVelocity) and [`setBodyAngularVelocity`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-setBodyAngularVelocity) set one each. Like [`applyBodyImpulseAt`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::hknpWorld##func-applyBodyImpulseAt), they leave a body that is not dynamic alone. An impulse at a point adds the impulse times the inverse mass to the linear velocity, and its moment about the centre of mass, turned into the motion's frame and scaled per axis by the inverse inertia, to the angular velocity, so the motion alone predicts its effect.

The setters change the motion directly, not deferred, so they belong outside the physics step, where the engine's own impulses and queries run; that no step overlaps the player's aim update is unverified ([melee-and-damage.md](melee-and-damage.md#threading)).

## Joined bodies

A Havok constraint joins two bodies, and the step resolves it with the contacts, removing whatever velocity it forbids. The game joins bodies three ways:

- A placed [`Constraint`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint) (`CConstraint`, [`TYPE_ID`](https://ferrobrew.github.io/pyxis/#/JustCause3_Steam_20206564/item/physics::Constraint##func-TYPE_ID)) is a game object that names two objects by object id and joins their bodies once both exist. Its data picks the joint (fixed, deformable fixed, ball and socket, stiff spring, hinge, limited hinge with angular limits and friction, ragdoll, wheel, or prismatic), optionally driven by a position, spring-damper, or velocity motor and optionally breakable past a force. Any object with a body can be joined; which shipped props have one (gates, hatches, signs, and the like), and that they are rigid objects, is unverified.
- A vehicle's parts ([vehicle-parts.md](vehicle-parts.md)) carry constraints from the physics prototype: a part attached with one (`CPartInstance::AttachPartWithConstraint`, unbound) hangs from its parent, a loosened door or hood swings on its hinge, and the constraint breaks when the part breaks off (`CPartInstance::BreakPartConstraint`, unbound). A merged part has no body of its own, so a query on it reports the vehicle; a loosened one has its own body, owned by a vehicle-part object ([vehicle-doors.md](vehicle-doors.md#the-loose-door)).
- The grappling hook's wire, the magnet weapon's gravity field, and a ragdoll's joints make constraints at run time.

The collision filter keeps joined bodies apart ([havok-bodies.md](../physics/havok-bodies.md#collision-filtering)); `hknpWorld::setBodyCollisionFilterInfo` (unbound) changes a body's filter word on the live world.
